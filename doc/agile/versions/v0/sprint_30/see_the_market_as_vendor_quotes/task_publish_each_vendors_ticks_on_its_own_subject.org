* Acceptance

- The ingest publishes each accepted tick once on the vendor's live subject, with the same conflation as the party path.
- The party subjects still carry their ticks, so no consumer breaks while it moves.
- A test pins the vendor subject a tick is published on and that it is published once.

* Plan

1. Build the subject in =tick_subjects.hpp=: =feed_tick_subject()= names the
   provider, the feed, the instrument type, the series token and the
   coordinate token. The coordinate token is the coordinate fields that hold a
   value, as =name=value= in the schema row's order, or =_= for a datum with
   none.
2. Give the plan one delivery for each source and datum: the feed subject and
   the targets for each route. The loop caches it as it cached the targets, so
   no tick builds a subject.
3. In =announce()=, publish each tick once on the feed subject after the age
   gate, beside the copies for each party. The meter, the stats and the CRM
   bridge stay for each route.
4. Pin the subject with unit tests, and show in a loop test that a tick of two
   consumers arrives once on the feed and once on each party.

A tick from a source that no route names is still dropped before it is stored,
so it reaches no subject. The quotes view of the system tenant needs those
ticks too; that is a change to the drop, and it belongs to a later task.

* Result

- The ingest publishes each accepted tick once on
  =market_tick.<provider>.<feed>.<instrument_type>.<series_token>.<coordinate_token>=,
  built by =feed_tick_subject()=, after the age gate. The copies for each party
  are still published, so every consumer keeps working.
- The plan holds one delivery for each source and datum: the feed subject and the
  targets for each route. The loop caches it, so no tick builds a subject. The
  meter, the stats and the CRM bridge are unchanged.
- Tests pin the subject of an IR swap pillar and of an FX spot, show that one
  wildcard covers a series and nothing else, and show that a tick of two routed
  parties arrives once on the feed and once on each party. The market data API
  suite (1131 cases), the market data service suite (314 cases), the shell
  stream tests and the portfolio projection tests pass.
- The branch also fixes two build breaks on main: =booking.ts= did not fill the
  basket option batches, and a portfolio projection test held an unused helper.
