* Goal

After the marketdata service was restarted the newest synthetic tick of AUD/USD stopped advancing, and nothing in the product said so. Find the cause: whether the feed does not restart with the service, whether the ingest loop lost its subscription, or whether something else. Say what a person sees and does, and feed the answer into the journey.
