#+brief: The base date and frequency of an ORE inflation curve's seasonality adjustment.
#+entity_singular: inflation_seasonality
#+entity_plural: inflation_seasonalities
#+entity_title: Inflation Seasonality
#+created: 2026-11-02
#+updated: 2026-11-02

ORE's =seasonalityType= requires =BaseDate=, =Frequency= and =Factors=
together. The inflation curve held them as =has_seasonality= and two nullable
columns, which could say a curve has a seasonality with no base date. A row of
this part is the seasonality: it exists only where the curve states one, so its
presence replaces the flag, and its base date and frequency are required.

The factors stay rows of =inflation_seasonality_factor=, keyed by the same
curve entry. The export refuses factors with no seasonality and a seasonality
with no factors, as the schema does.

A curve entry holds at most one row of this part, so the generated table adds
the unique index on the live rows of =(tenant_id, curve_definition_id)=.
