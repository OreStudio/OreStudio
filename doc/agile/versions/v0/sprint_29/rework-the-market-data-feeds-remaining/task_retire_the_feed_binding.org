* Result

The route is the only consumer record. The ingest builds one consumer from
each route, and the feed binding is gone from the model, the code, the tables,
the screen, the permissions and the widening guard. The database is recreated
from the create scripts: no migration, no compatibility view and no legacy row.

** What changed

- The ingest reads the routes only. The binding read, the =consumer_of= copy
  and the binding change event are gone.
- The synthetic controller no longer writes a binding when a feed starts, and
  it no longer carries the bearer token that existed only for that write.
- The binding model, its two tables, the notify trigger, the event mapping,
  the operations activity read, the service, the repository, the handler, the
  history provider, the shell commands, the client methods and the web list
  and record pages are deleted.
- The three =marketdata::feed_bindings= permissions are gone, and the guard
  exception with them.
- The ER diagrams, the component diagrams and the operations protocol are
  regenerated.

** Verification

- The marketdata api, core, client and service libraries and executables build,
  as do the shell and the synthetic service.
- The web typechecks, formats, builds and passes 3,206 tests across its
  workspaces.
- The guard reports 13 widenings, all listed. The codegen suite passes 1,589
  tests and the component drift check finds no drift.
- The database recreates, the pgTAP suites pass, and the marketdata api, core,
  client and service, shell and web suites pass in ctest.

** Inherited failures

Four ctest suites fail on code this branch does not change: =ores.ore.core=
(7 failures on the ORE parameter data), =ores.reporting.core= and
=ores.compute.api= and =ores.compute.core= (a SIGSEGV in
=domain_result_tests.cpp=). The branch touches none of those trees, so they
came in from main. They need their own fix.
