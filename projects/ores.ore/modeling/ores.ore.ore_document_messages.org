#+created: 2026-10-26
#+updated: 2026-10-26

The ORE component writes a document and answers with the files a browser
saves: each is a name and the text under it. The type is stated once here
because every export answers with it, and a second statement of the same
struct in a second protocol would be two types with one name: the two
protocols cannot be included in one unit, and a reader of one cannot be sure
it is the other.

This model has no operation of its own. It states the shared payload the
exports carry, which is the whole of what a caller reads it for.
