* Goal

A person opens the curve evolution screen and chooses the curve or surface from a list of the series their party can see, instead of typing its asset class, scope, instrument type, quote type and fields.

* Acceptance

- A server operation returns the typed identity of each series the caller's party can see: asset class, scope, instrument type, quote type and the other identity fields, with the owning party.
- The BFF routes that list for a session, and a session sees no series of a party it cannot read.
- The screen offers the list as a picker, filled in from the chosen row, and still lets the person type an identity.
- An empty list says so in words and does not read as an error.

* Decisions

* Risks

- *An empty picker with no error.* Market series that provisioning seeds have no identity rows until
  =marketdata backfill-identity= runs for the party, so a fresh Acme environment lists nothing for a
  party. The picker says the list is empty and why a list can be empty. The fix is the capture
  =provisioning_projects_seeded_series_identities=.
- *A cut list read as a complete one.* The list holds at most 5000 series. The picker must show the
  =truncated= flag and say to narrow by party.

* Out of scope

- Searching or filtering the list beyond what a short list needs.
- A series with no declared shape stays a refused read.
- Changing which party a person works for.
