* Goal

The administrator can account for every open session: who is signed in, what the session did, who is failing to get in, where the session came from, and they can end a session that cannot be explained.

* Acceptance

- iam.v1.auth_events.list reads the tenant's authentication events, filtered by account, period and event.
- iam.v1.sessions.statistics reads the session statistics from the continuous aggregates.
- Logout ends the caller's session row and records a logout event, and an administrator can end another account's session in the tenant.
- An address resolves to a country over the wire, and the session read carries where each session came from.
- The Audit: sign-ins screen reads all of it over the BFF.
