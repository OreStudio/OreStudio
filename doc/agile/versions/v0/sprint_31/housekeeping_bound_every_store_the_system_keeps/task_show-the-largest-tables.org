* Result

The housekeeping screen can now answer what fills the disk. Its foot opens a page
of the largest relations, each with its size, its share of the database, and the
store the catalogue calls it — or a stated "not named", which is the answer for
most of the head on this environment. A pie of the largest five, ten or twenty
folds the rest of the database into one slice, and the table shows ten, twenty-five
or a hundred rows.

The first read on this environment is the argument for the screen: the largest
relation is bounded, and the next three largest are named by nothing at all.

The read is live, so it states what is large at the instant it is asked. What grew
is the capacity panel's question, and the footnote says so.
