This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The review of PR #4686 found that the surface's tick is tested only through hand-built markets, so
four things it does are unproven: that it names a member the run has not published, that two ticks
against an unchanged curve emit identical quotes, that a spot feed's level reads back from the board
as the process's own value, and that the round trip goes *through* the board rather than around it.

=fx_vol_expiry_markets= was extracted for exactly this: it takes the three values and the periods and
answers one market per expiry, so a test can publish curves and a spot to a board, read them back
through the same views the feed reads, and assert both the messages and the markets without a wire.

Two layout questions ride with it, recorded as opportunities and not defects because nobody has
measured them: =run_market::drive= finds a series' drivers in a =std::map= per tick, and a board
member holds its points in a heap vector. Both are candidates for a flat arena once a run with a
thousand feeds is measured.

* Why

Because a test that guarantees its own precondition proves nothing about the code: the round trip as
written passes one market to the emitter and the same one to the calibration, so the board, the
period addition and the two-curve resolution are all bypassed. The gate messages are the whole point
of the two gates, and neither is asserted anywhere.
