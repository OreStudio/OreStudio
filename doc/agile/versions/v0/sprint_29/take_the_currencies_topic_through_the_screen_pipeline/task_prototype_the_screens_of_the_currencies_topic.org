* Notes

Decided while drawing, for the standard and the build:

- The Import steps are a page at their own address, not a dialog: a table
  kind has four steps and no target to choose. RS-ORE-05 and RS-ORE-07 say
  so now.
- Day basis and ORE currency type are selects over lists the server does not
  hold yet; the build adds the two classifications.
- A reader sees Export and not Import, as RS-ORE-02 says.
- Two script faults the render found before the screenshots: the seed import
  event dated after the user's edits, and a chart that took the first and
  last entry by position instead of by date.

From the journeys' judgement, for this task:

- The pair's create dialog is the shared record dialog with two groups, the
  pair and its convention, and a derived pair code; the two writes behind it
  are the server's gap, [[id:56C6A5CA-1A0D-435A-9AC5-1263CBB29485][Write a currency pair and its convention together, and check the pair code]], and the dialog must say what a failed second write leaves.
- The currency's Day basis and ORE currency type are free text today; the
  prototype decides their kinds. The numbers are text inputs.
- The currencies list shows codes where the page shows names; the prototype
  draws the names.
- The currencies list header draws the standard Import and Export pair of the
  record screen standard's Import and Export section, between Refresh and
  Add: the Export dialog that names the document and its count, and the
  Import dialog of five steps on the server's preview. Both are the shared
  =OreExchange= the build adds; the prototype draws every state of both at an
  address.
