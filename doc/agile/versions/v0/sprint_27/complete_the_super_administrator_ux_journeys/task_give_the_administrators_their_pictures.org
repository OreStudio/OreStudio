* Notes

The images and the pattern to follow:

- The source images are =/home/marco/Pictures/super_admin_avatar.png= and
  =/home/marco/Pictures/tenant_admin_avatar.png=; vendor them into the
  repository beside =external/acme/Acme-corp.png=.
- The pattern: =projects/ores.sql/populate/acme/acme_party_logo_populate.sql=
  seeds a system-tenant template row in =ores_assets_images_tbl=, keyed by
  name, and =attach_photos= copies it per tenant and attaches it.
- The step: =attach_photos_step= in
  =projects/ores.iam/core/include/ores.iam.core/messaging/tenant_provisioning_handler.hpp=,
  with the =acme_demo= arguments in
  =projects/ores.sql/populate/iam/iam_seed_profiles_populate.sql= (position
  50: =party_logo= and the =parties= datasets).
- The staff photos make the same trip: =generate_sql.py= under
  =projects/ores.seeder/datasets/acme_corporation/= generates
  =acme_staff_photos_populate.sql= from =external/facestudio/faces= and
  =photo_assignments.json=.
- The tenant and its administrator are created before the steps run, so
  =attach_photos= can name the ACME administrator; the super
  administrator's account is created at first run in the system tenant
  and the step runs in the run's own tenant.
