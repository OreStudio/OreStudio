* Acceptance

- The dashboard lists the providers and counts the feeds that should run
- The feed tree has a paged table with state, asset class and search filters, and start or stop by selection
- The old list screens, their cards, addresses and words are removed

* Plan

The shape is the [[id:17E6CB2C-E1C9-4911-81AC-591D88AC5B89][Browse the feed tree prototype]].

- =marketdata/synthetic/= holds the screen, the feed pages and their panels. =SyntheticScreen= has the two tabs through =useTabs=, inside the synthetic record store.
- The dashboard lists the providers with what each simulates and a banner for the feeds that should run. The feed tree has the providers and their folders, and a table that pages with the standard pager and filters by state, asset class and words, all in the address.
- Rows are picked, or every row the filter matches, and one Start or Stop sends the filter and the ids. =selection.ts= is the pure model of a pick.
- The overview, the tree, the two lists, the folder page, their cards, addresses, redirects and words are removed.
