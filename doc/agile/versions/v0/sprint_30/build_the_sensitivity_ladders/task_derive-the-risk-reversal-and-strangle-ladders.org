* Acceptance

- The derivation states the change of basis it applies, cites the smile identity on FX Volatility Surface, and names the surface points it reads.
- The strangle ladder reads the butterfly style the pair is stored with, and refuses a pair whose style it cannot convert rather than assuming one.
- The derived figure, its inputs and the run it came from are all stored, so the figure can be recomputed without re-running the engine.
- A test proves the derived risk reversal equals the difference of the two surface-point deltas on a worked example.
