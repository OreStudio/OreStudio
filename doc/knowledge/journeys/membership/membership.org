#+title: Membership journeys
#+description: Which parties a person works in, and who they report to.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:user_journey:security:
#+created: 2026-09-23
#+updated: 2026-09-23

An account /works in/ a party. The association decides which parties the account
may act for: at sign-in an account that works in more than one party chooses one,
and it can switch later. The reporting line is the other half of the same idea —
it records who answers to whom inside the organisation.

Both journeys are about a person's place in the organisation rather than about
their identity. The rules are strict in one direction: an account with no party
is refused at sign-in.
