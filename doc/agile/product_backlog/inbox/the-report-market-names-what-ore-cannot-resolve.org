This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

A smokehouse run prices two of its three trades. The forward prices at 80,815.55
GBP; the option prices at zero, which is correct, since its premium is its value
on the valuation date. The swap records no pricing:

#+begin_example
2nd leg: OvernightIndexedCouponPricer: missing GBP-SONIAON Actual/365 (Fixed)
fixing for fixing date November 24th, 2026.
#+end_example

* Why

The run carries no index fixing, and there are two reasons, each on its own side
of the line.

The first was the routes. A subscription step wrote one route per provider with
the asset class stated as a constant =fx=, so a party subscribed to
=simulated-fixings= reached the FX fixings and not the interest rate ones, which
are the ones an overnight coupon reads. The step now reads what each provider's
catalogue states: the asset class is the authority of every address the provider
maps, so a provider that serves interest rates gets an =ir= route as well as an
=fx= one. That change is in and the routes are right.

The second is upstream of the run. No fixing series reaches the tenant at all,
and the fixing source says why:

#+begin_example
The simulated-fixings fixing series of party <id> quote against EUR and NZD at
once, so no one base cancels; it is left out.
#+end_example

A fixing series is derived from the party's spot series, and the inference of
which currency a fixing quotes against cancels one base against the other. When
the series in scope carry several bases, as a party routed to every vendor's
spots does, nothing cancels and every fixing is dropped. So a party holding a
rich spot book gets no fixings, and every overnight coupon in the run is refused.

That is the defect to fix next, and it is a data-model question rather than a
configuration one: either the fixing is keyed by the pair it belongs to rather
than inferred from the party's whole spot book, or the inference is narrowed to
the series the trade's index names.
