* Goal

The Qt FX rate editor offers a simple mode next to the raw component table: sliders for the global trend drift, the global volatility and the jump event frequency, which build the Gaussian mixture, and for the mean reverting engine a κ slider on a log scale with the half-life read out and a σ slider. A quant who does not think in μ, σ and w gets a feed that behaves as they describe. The web mixture tab has only the raw table.

* Acceptance

- The mixture tab offers Simple and Advanced, and the simple controls write the same components the table holds.
- A mixture the simple controls cannot express is shown in Advanced only, with a note that says so.
- The mean reverting engine gets its own simple controls: κ by half-life and σ.
- The preview and the save work the same from either mode.
