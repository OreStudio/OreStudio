* Goal

A term index of a currency that has an overnight curve gets its curve and
fixings from that curve. Term SOFR and IBR are the compounded overnight
forward over the term. EYTIBOR, WIBOR and the generic indices of KRW and
other currencies are the overnight forward plus a stated spread. Each rule is
data beside the curve configs, and task 3's list reads it.

* Plan

The rule is data, and the row it belongs on is the synthetic curve config,
because that config is the only thing that states an index rate. Reading the
pipeline settled this:

- =ir_curve_feed= is the sole publisher of =market_member_kind::index_rate=
  on the run's board, and it publishes the rate of its own config's pillar
  whose end tenor equals the index tenor.
- =index_fixing_feed= writes a fixing from whatever the board holds as that
  index's rate, so a fixing needs only a board rate.
- The marketdata bootstrap (=curve_republish_service=) publishes discount
  factors, never rates, so it cannot serve this.

Five indices need it, over three existing overnight configs:

| Term index | Currency | Overnight index | Rule |
|------------|----------|-----------------+------|
| SOFR 1M | USD | SOFR | compounded over the term |
| IBR 3M | COP | IBR | compounded over the term |
| EYTIBOR 3M | JPY | TONA | overnight plus a stated spread |
| GENERIC 3M | KRW | KOFR | overnight plus a stated spread |
| WIBOR 6M | PLN | POLONIA | overnight plus a stated spread |

1. *The rule on the config.* =ir_curve_generation_config= gains
   =rate_source= (=process= | =compounded_overnight= |
   =overnight_plus_spread=), =derived_spread_basis_points= and
   =derived_spread_source=. A derived config states no process parameters:
   its rate comes from its currency's overnight config, which is the one
   config of that currency that funds (a =FUNDING= role and no tenor), so no
   new reference is needed. Checks: a =process= config states no spread; a
   spread config states both the points and their source.
2. *The derived rate.* =ir_curve_feed::index_rate()= answers a derived
   config from the board's overnight curve instead of its own pillars:
   =compounded= is the simple forward over the term,
   =(P(t)/P(t+T) - 1) / year_fraction(t, t+T)= — the OIS compounded rate of a
   daily overnight curve — and =spread= is the overnight rate plus the
   stated points. Both read the primitives already there:
   =curve_instrument_pricer::fra_rate= and
   =day_count_calculator::year_fraction=. Nothing compounds or adds a spread
   in the tree today, so this is the new arithmetic.
3. *The five rows.* One =ir_curve_generation_config= per derived index,
   seeded against the currency's overnight config, with the spread of the
   two spread indices stated and sourced.

Deferred to a follow-up task if it costs more than a slice: publishing a
*projection curve* for a derived index. The acceptance of this task asks
for the fixing addresses to answer and for the compounded equality, and
both follow from the board rate alone.

Test: a unit test proves the compounded rate equals the forward implied by
two discount factors of the overnight curve and that the spread form adds
its points; a fresh provision shows the five fixing addresses answering.
