* Goal

ores.web shows ORE Studio's documents, knowledge pages, sprints, stories, tasks
and test scenarios, and searches them, through a document service compass
provides, and agents answer from the same service. ORE Studio parses, stores
and indexes no document: it runs compass's service in its fleet, gates it in
ores.http by the caller's role, and builds the pages that show it.

The service does not exist yet. Its design is a proposal handed to compass on
2026-11-22, not yet published in the compass repository; this story's tasks
are planned against it and wait for compass to settle and release the
service.

It takes over what /Search the knowledge base from ores.web/ set out to do,
which built the pipeline inside ORE Studio and is being removed by
[[id:A436C077-AEB1-4010-9587-67269252CC4B][Decommission ores.orgmode and the knowledge base pipeline]].

* Acceptance

To be written with the tasks. What the story has to cover, as known today:

- compass's document service runs in ORE Studio's fleet, in development from
  the checkout and in production from the release's documents.
- ores.http forwards document requests to it for a signed-in caller, and picks
  the view the caller's role may see; no caller reaches development documents
  it should not.
- ores.web searches and shows the documents through it: knowledge pages, and
  the agile browser and the scenario runner, which read through =@ores/org=
  today.
- =@ores/org= is gone, and recording a scenario run, which writes into the
  scenario's org document today, has a replacement agreed with compass or is
  retired on purpose.
- The assistant and the MCP agent answer from the same service, limited to
  knowledge documents.
