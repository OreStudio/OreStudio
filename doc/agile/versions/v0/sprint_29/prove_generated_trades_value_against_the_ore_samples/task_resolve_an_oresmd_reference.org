* Goal

A builder can look up the conventions an oresmd identifier needs, and the exporter and importer can project an identifier to ORE and back under a market configuration, both in the marketdata API. The names follow the market data requirements pages: resolution is a requirement mapped to an identifier, which this task does not do.

* Acceptance

- =convention_lookup= answers by identifier: an index's convention, the swap convention that uses it, a CDS convention, an equity or commodity underlying; an unknown address is a stated error. An in-memory implementation serves tests, the generator and the shell.
- =ore_projection= writes an identifier as its ORE name, and reads an ORE name back where the codecs can, under one market configuration: fixed for indices through =ore_index_codec=, configuration-bound for curves.
- =reference_curve_names= and =reference_curve_names_of= are replaced by =ore_projection= and deleted, with the bond export unchanged in the recorded trades.

* Result

Two pieces in =ores.marketdata.api=, named as the market data requirements
pages name them, after the owner asked that they follow those pages:

- =convention_lookup= finds the conventions an oresmd identifier's index is
  defined and used by: the defining Ibor or overnight index convention by the
  family address (the tenor dropped), and the swap and OIS conventions that
  use the index by the identifier itself. One match answers; none, or more
  than one, is an error naming the identifier and why. It is not called a
  resolver: the pages reserve resolution for mapping a requirement to an
  identifier.
- =ore_projection= is the vendor projection to ORE, one way as the pages
  define it: an index by its ORE index name, a quote by its ORE key, a curve
  series by the name the run's market configuration gives it. It replaces the
  function the exporter threaded to name a bond's reference curve;
  =reference_curve_names_of= is =ore_projection_of=. A column that is not an
  identifier, or a curve the run does not name, is written as stored, so the
  export is unchanged; the recorded trades do not move.

Acceptance: the second and third criteria are met, the second amended to
one direction as the pages define the projection (reading an ORE name back is
the importer's, in
[[id:639CEE8C-0586-4EEF-8F12-C450F956B00F][Store trade market references as oresmd addresses]]).
The first is met for index definitions and swap and OIS conventions; the CDS
convention and the equity and commodity underlyings move to
[[id:B19AE99F-0E8F-4701-9E9C-4987D5C5E4DA][Generate every trade type through its builder]],
where their families' builders need them, since no seeded CDS convention
states an address yet.
