One contributor's quote of a pillar: the market series it is read from, and the
weight the contributor carries in the calibration. A pillar reads one series per
contributor, so a broker's quotes and the derived source's quotes of the same point
are two sources of one pillar, and a missing observation from one of them is not a
refusal by itself. See
[[id:7AE57098-08D4-4D4A-83D6-918FE520B68E][A pillar names the market series it reads]].

=market_series_id= is a soft reference to an =fx_option= quote series in
=ores.marketdata=, named by the caller from the series the party holds.
=contributor_code= names the provider whose series it is, as the series' own
=provider_code= does, so a reader sees the contributor without a cross-component
read.
