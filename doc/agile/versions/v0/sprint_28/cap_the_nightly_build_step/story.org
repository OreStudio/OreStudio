* Decisions

- The cap is 320 minutes, the same value =continuous-linux.yml= uses, so the
  two workflows explain the same arithmetic in one place rather than two.
- The cap is on the step, not the job. The job limit is already 360 minutes
  and cannot be raised on a hosted runner, so the only way to keep room for
  the cache steps is to stop the build early.
- The gcc job's runner shutdown is left alone. It is infrastructure, it
  happened at 3h13m, and no timeout change affects it.

* Out of scope

- Diagnosing why the runner shut down. That is GitHub's, and the same
  signature has hit Linux jobs repeatedly this month.
