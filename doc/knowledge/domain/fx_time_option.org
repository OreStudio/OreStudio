#+title: A time option forward settles inside a window at one agreed rate
#+description: A forward whose delivery date the client picks inside a window; the holder must still complete the whole amount.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

A time option forward commits the client to exchange currencies at a pre-agreed rate, at some point between two pre-agreed dates. The word /option/ refers only to that flexibility. The client must still complete the whole amount by the final date.

* Detail

The market also calls it a window forward. A drawdown cannot be earlier than the start date. The flexibility carries a premium over a forward to one date in the window, which comes from the forward points and grows with the length of the window.
