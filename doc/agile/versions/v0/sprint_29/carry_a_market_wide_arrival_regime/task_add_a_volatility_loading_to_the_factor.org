* Goal


Deferred on 2026-10-27. The market speed on tau (#3894) already moves a busy
market's prices further per wall second, in proportion to its pace, because a
tick steps its price by the simulated time it covers. What this task would add
is a larger move per unit of market time, a rougher tick as well as a sooner
one, and that needs a volatility sensitivity column on both generation-config
kinds and a centring rule; it waits until that is wanted.
The shared factor moves price variance, not only the tick clock: a feed's returns
widen with the market and quieten with it, by a loading the feed carries, while a
run's long-run sigma stays the one its configuration states.

* Acceptance

- =i_stochastic_process= gains =set_volatility_multiplier(double)=, the sibling of
  the arrival process's =set_baseline_multiplier=, and a process that cannot honour
  it refuses rather than ignoring it.
- =market_link= gains =volatility_sensitivity=, and both generation-config kinds
  gain the column: schema, migration and codegen, as one change.
- The multiplier is =(sensitivity_vol * A_centred)^gamma= with =gamma = 1/2= by
  default, so variance is proportional to activity; the exponent is a stated
  default, not a hidden one.
- =A_centred= subtracts the heat's stationary mean, keeps the long-run mean at one,
  and is floored above zero so a calm market cannot freeze a price.
- Over a long run with the heat on, the realized sigma equals the configured
  sigma; a pinned active market raises it by the loading, and calm lowers it.
- A feed with =sensitivity_vol = 0= behaves exactly as it does today.
- The Arrivals tab edits the loading beside the sensitivity, with the same audit.

* Plan

1. Schema column on both config kinds, migration and codegen, and the link's
   loading with its docstring.
2. The setter and its override in every process that can scale its own law.
3. The centring, with the floor, as a function beside =rate_multiplier()=.
4. The three tests: calibration, response to a pinned state, and the zero-loading
   no-op.
5. The UI field, and the BFF read that backs it.
