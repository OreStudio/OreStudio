The task's acceptance carries what this capture asks.

* What

Two checks prove that the trade representation tests catch the defects they
claim to catch. A property-based roundtrip (RapidCheck, which works with Catch2)
generates many instruments of each trade type, writes them as ORE XML, reads them
back and requires equality, so it reaches values neither the ORE samples nor the
generator state. Mutation testing (Mull, built on clang) makes small changes to
the projection readers and writers and lists each change no test notices. Mull
runs outside the normal build, as an occasional job.

* Why

The validation story that proposed them closed into the coverage story, which
owns how a trade is represented. These two improve test quality rather than
coverage, and would be lost without a record.
