* Plan

The horizon is one decision in four places: the read's default, the hours a
range covers, the presets the browser offers, and the state the page opens on.
All four move together, and the range nobody states answers the day.

The last hour stays offered even though the sampler writes hourly. It holds at
most one mark, and the page already states that a horizon holds no measurement
or only one, so the choice tells the truth rather than showing a line through a
single point.

* Result

the last week, and the month is gone from the read, the client and the screen.
