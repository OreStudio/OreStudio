The values an IR feed's engine parameters take in the simple view, and back.

The simple view asks four questions anyone can answer: where the rate starts,
where it settles in the long run, how long it takes to come half way back,
and how far it moves in a year. The advanced view is the engine's own
parameters: κ, θ, σ and the initial rate, or the seven of the Two-Factor
Gaussian engine. Each engine reads σ in its own unit, and Black-Karasinski
reads θ as a logarithm, so a screen asks here for the numbers the engine
should read. The conversion is the service's, beside the engines it describes.
Nothing is stored.
