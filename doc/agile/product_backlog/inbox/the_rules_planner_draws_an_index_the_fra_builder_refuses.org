* What

On a freshly recreated database provisioned with acme_demo at main 0d2e6758d7,
the generate_trades step writes fourteen of its sixteen configs. The two
interest rate configs, ACME ACCOUK rates and ACME ACCOUS rates, are not written:

  warn: not written: ACME ACCOUK rates for ACME Corporation UK plc: The
  ForwardRateAgreement builder refuses the drawn index: a forward rate
  agreement needs an Ibor index

The other classes are written; the population holds 808 trades where the mix
states 1100, and the 308 missing ones are those two configs.

* Why

The rates planner draws a forward rate agreement over an overnight index for
some slots, and the builder refuses it because a forward rate agreement's index
must be an IBOR by definition. Either the planner should draw an IBOR index for
that product, or the builder should accept what the planner draws; the
definition settles it in the planner's favour, so the planner is the side to fix.

Until it is fixed, a fresh provision loses a third of the rate trades, and any
measurement over the rate book is over two thirds of it. Found while proving the
population fix in #4828 on a recreated database.
