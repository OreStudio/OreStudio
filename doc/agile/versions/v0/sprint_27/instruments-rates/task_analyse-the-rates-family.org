the catalogue files fourteen trade types under =product_type= =swap=, and

decision.

* Result

The analysis is delivered as =design.org= beside this task, with its
entity-relationship diagram in =design_er.puml= and =design_er.png=. No
code changed.

- *The family.* The boundary is the refdata =interest_rates= asset class:
  nine codes over nine tables. =RiskParticipationAgreement= is a credit
  product and =InflationSwap= an inflation product, though the catalogue
  files both under =product_type= =swap=; three further codes are
  unrouted.
- *The findings.* Twenty, each with its evidence. The schema defects are
  the missing header, the surrogate-keyed legs, the text-typed option
  block, and the one table with no coverage. The boundary defects are
  larger: the import never writes the legs it builds, a cross-currency
  swap's type is overwritten to =Swap=, seven columns that exist are
  never written, direction is lost on every leg, and three products have
  no reverse mapper.
- *The proposal.* A family header =rate_instruments= with the family's
  coverage check and its delete cascade; product fact tables for the
  products that own fields; the legs re-keyed to the trade with a
  direction and amount and rate children; and the rates family writing
  to the shared option, schedule and strike shapes the bond pilot built.
  No security level, because a rates trade has no issuer.
- *The staging.* Eight units, each ending in a verifiable state, the
  routing repairs first and the mapping repair as a unit of its own
  rather than a follow-up.

Implementation does not start on this task. The story carries the
decisions and the next unit is the routing repairs.
