* Goal

Our 79 skills describe our process well but never tell an agent how to think or how to pick an approach. Two external skill sets fill that gap: pstack brings a rigor spine of principles, playbooks and verification harnesses; the Matt Pocock skills bring alignment depth through interviewing, wayfinding and prototyping. Both carry mental models that clash with ours and with each other. This story integrates the parts that earn their place into one catalogue with one vocabulary, namespaced under a compass- prefix, with every imported skill attributed to its upstream source so we can track it as it changes.

* Acceptance

- Every skill in the catalogue carries the compass- prefix, and the naming conventions document records the amended prefix and verb registers.
- The catalogue is layered into principles, methods and process actions, and the layering is documented with the reasoning behind it.
- Every imported skill records its upstream repository, path, commit and divergence, and a manifest lists them all.
- Terminology is consistent across all skills: one word per concept, recorded in the naming conventions document.
- No imported skill duplicates an existing one; overlaps are folded into a single skill and the loser is retired through skill-delete.
- The deployed bundle builds and every skill loads.
