* Summary

A convention family is an entity model that declares =#+convention_family:=.
This facet renders, for each such entity that opts in, one translation unit
holding that family's two functions: the apply of one staged payload and the
read of its live rows as payloads. The unit decodes and serialises the
family's own type and no other, so no translation unit of the conventions
service instantiates the serialisation of two families. That is the budget the
=check_translation_unit_budget= gate holds.

The facet is opt-in per entity, with
=:ores.cpp.convention-family.enabled: true= in the model's property drawer. The
family facts the template reads are the registry's, so the unit agrees with
the registry header and the dispatch by construction.

* Mustache incantations

The archetype reads one context, =convention_unit=, which the generator builds
only for this facet's template. It carries the family's =key=, =entity=,
=domain_type= and the =is_party=, =is_by_id= and =is_world= flags of its write
mode.
