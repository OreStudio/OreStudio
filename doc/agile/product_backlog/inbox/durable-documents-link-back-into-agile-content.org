This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The link between durable documents and agile content runs one way in the rule
and two ways in the tree. About 180 id links run from =doc/knowledge/=,
=doc/recipes/=, =doc/meta/= and =doc/llm/= into stories, tasks, captures and
plans. The bulk is the journey documents' =* Prototype= sections, which link the
task that built each prototype; the rest is spread thinly across recipes, hubs
and knowledge pages that name the work that produced them.

* Why

A durable document that links a task pins itself to a sprint. When the task
closes and the sprint recedes, the link leads to a record of work rather than to
knowledge, and a reader who follows it learns what somebody did rather than what
is true. The rule is already encoded and already measured: =compass lint= counts
these links and reports them as an advisory, so the number is visible and nothing
fails. Making it fail without first clearing the backlog would block every
unrelated change, which is why it is advisory today.

The questions this capture has to settle before it becomes a task: whether the
prototype links are worth keeping in some other form — a prototype branch or a
prototype page is itself durable, so the record may move rather than disappear —
and whether the advisory becomes a gate in the same change or a later one.

* References

- The rule as the lint encodes it: the durable directories are =doc/llm/=,
  =doc/meta/=, =doc/knowledge/= and =doc/recipes/=, and the agile types are
  story, task, sprint, design, capture, version, plan and retrospective.
  =investigation= is deliberately not among them, so a durable page may cite the
  survey it answers.
- The measurement is the line =compass lint= prints: at the time of writing, 180
  links across 89 files.
