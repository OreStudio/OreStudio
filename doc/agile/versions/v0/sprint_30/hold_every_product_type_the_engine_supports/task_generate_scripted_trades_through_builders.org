* Result

The scripted family is generated through builders, and the engine builds every
type it books.

- The scripted builders need no convention: a script reads its variables as the
  trade states them, so convention_lookup answers them with no change.
- scripted_trade_builder takes a library script, its inputs as the store holds
  them and the trade's typed variables, and refuses a trade that leaves out an
  input or states it in another type or shape. The double digital option, the
  performance option and the autocallable each have a builder of their own.
- The planner reads the library's inputs into the scope, draws values and calls
  the builders. construct_scripted_batch, its row helpers and script_of are
  deleted, and the four types leave the list awaiting builders.
- Autocallable_01 is booked: the projection reads and writes its element, and a
  class member that holds a ScheduleData is an event.
- The scripted entries leave the known lists. The test recipe names its
  equities as ORE's Products market holds their curves and volatilities, which
  clears 23 equity-curve errors across the equity and scripted types. The
  engine then reached three trade errors, which are fixed: the Ascot states one
  exercise date, a convertible draws a share in the bond's currency, and the
  autocallable fixes from a year after the anchor. EquitySwap's EquityLeg has
  no engine configuration in the Products example and is listed as such.
- The read of a class member asked every member for a child, which threw on a
  scalar and emptied the double digital option's import. It now asks the
  binding whether the member holds a schedule.
- The corpus roundtrip reads every ORE sample of ScriptedTrade,
  DoubleDigitalOption and PerformanceOption_01 with no known loss. ORE ships no
  XML sample of Autocallable_01, so the projection test round-trips one.

Verified: trading api 4640 cases, trading core 252, ORE core 545 and synthetic
145 with the ORE engine, all passing.
