## Finding

No test-only public API. Every hand-written header that declares a symbol
has a production consumer: the handlers are registered by registrar.cpp,
the repositories are called by the services, and the option, parser and
client headers are called by their applications. None is reached only from
a test, so no symbol is dead by B06's test.

The two rows with no consumer at all declare an empty namespace and
nothing else:

  ores.compute.core/ores.compute.hpp
  ores.compute.service/ores.compute.service.hpp

They declare no symbol, so B06 has nothing to classify. They are the
component's banner headers, one of 42 such headers across the repo, and
nothing includes them. The core overview calls the first an aggregate
include, which it is not: it carries no includes. The overview line is
corrected rather than the header made to aggregate, because no caller
asked for the aggregate.
