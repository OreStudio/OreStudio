* Acceptance

- Approved iam.reporting_line requests not yet applied are found at start and on every change, in every tenant not terminated.
- The line is written through set_reporting_line with the account version as precondition; a stale version leaves the request approved and applied-later, and says so.
- The loop check runs again at apply time, because the tree may have changed since the proposal.
- The history of the account shows the proposer, the decider and the request.
