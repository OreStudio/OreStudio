* What

Holiday calendar administration describes a governance layer: a holiday proposed by an external source waits for authorisation unless the source is trusted. Today every written exception takes effect when the dates are rebuilt. Model a pending state or a workflow over imports.

* Why

A wrong external holiday moves settlement dates the moment it lands.
