This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Seven requests of the marketdata operations model carry a field called =identity=:
=list_series_identities_request=, =get_series_slice_request=, =get_series_evolution_request=,
=get_curve_snapshot_request=, =key_manual_point_request=, =clear_manual_point_request= and
=get_series_ticks_request=. The type of the field is =resolve_series_identity_request=. The
field holds the typed facts that tell one series from another: the asset class, the scope, the
instrument type, the quote type, the remaining fields and an optional party. It is not a key
and not an id. The name does not say any of this. Rename the field to =series_identity= in all
seven requests in one change, and add one glossary entry that says what a series identity is.

* Why

On 24 October a reader of the history call asked what =identity= is. The call had taken a
=series_id= until that day, and the change to =identity= made the question easy to ask. Alone,
the word could mean the identity of the caller, the session or a tenant, and the system has
all three. A series identity is a separate idea from the =series_id= UUID. The UUID changes on
a database recreate. The identity does not. A caller who reads =identity= next to =series_id=
cannot tell which one is the stable name.

The rename has a cost. It changes the generated C++ and TypeScript protocol of seven requests
and every client of them, with no old field kept. It should happen in one change, because a
rename of one request would make the reads differ from each other. The same word is also in
the names =resolve_series_identity_request= and =ores_marketdata_market_series_identity_tbl=.
Those names already say series, so they stay.
