#+title: A dealer hedges an outright forward with a spot trade and an FX swap
#+description: Why an outright is a spot trade plus an FX swap, and why forward points sit in a separate book.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

An outright forward is equivalent to a spot trade plus an FX swap. Dealers construct and hedge outrights this way, because the spot trade carries the exchange-rate risk and the swap carries the forward points.

* Detail

In practical dealing the forward book is separate from the spot book, and forward points are not quoted at the spot desk. The forward desk often belongs to the money market department.

IFRS 9 recognises the same split. Each forward has a spot element and a forward element, and an entity can designate only the spot element as the hedging instrument.
