identifier, so the two are not confused.

* Result

The knowledge notes call the firm's own identifier the trade id. Six
uses changed: the blotter's column and the mini-blotter header in
=trade_blotter.org=, the identity row of =positions.org=, the filter
example and the property list in =dynamic_books.org=, and the property
list in =books_and_portfolios.org=. The originating system's deal ID in
=trade_blotter.org= is now the OS trade identifier: it is a scheme in
=trade_identifier=, not the trade id. No durable document outside the
agile records still says deal id; the agile records are history and
keep their words.
