* Result

The IR shapes are declared as data beside the classification rules. An IBOR
fixing requires =ccy=, =index= and =index_name= and forbids =unit_ccy=; a swap
index fixing requires =ccy=, =index= and =tenor=; a yield series requires
=ccy=, =instrument_type= and =quote_type=; none carries a unit currency. The
identity writer refuses a new IBOR fixing that misses one of those fields or
carries a unit currency, naming the series, the family and the field
(=an_ibor_fixing_that_misses_a_required_field_is_refused_by_name=,
=an_ibor_fixing_that_carries_a_unit_currency_is_refused_by_name=). The
classification rules hold one =index_fixing= rule. On a freshly provisioned
database all 51 IBOR fixing series hold to their shape. The task was handed to
clever dijkstra; the work reached main through the other tasks of this story, so
it closes on evidence.
