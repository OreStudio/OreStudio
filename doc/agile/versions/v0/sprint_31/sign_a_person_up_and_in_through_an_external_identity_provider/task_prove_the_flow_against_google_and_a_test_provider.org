* Goal

Prove the flow twice. In the gate and the suites, against a provider the fleet runs and the tests control, so no build needs Google's credentials or the network. By hand, against Google itself, from the sign-in screen, with the evidence recorded on this task. Write the recipe a person follows to create the Google client — the project, the consent screen, the test users and the redirect address — because those steps need a Google account and only a person can take them.

* Acceptance

- The suites and the gate sign in through a provider the fleet runs, so they pass with no Google credentials and no network.
- A recorded manual sign-in to Google creates the account under the policy and leaves a session that names its method, with the evidence on this task.
- The recipe states every step a person takes to create the Google client, including the loopback redirect address a development deployment needs and the test users a consent screen in testing mode requires.
- The flow serves a second provider with no code change, and the record shows it.
