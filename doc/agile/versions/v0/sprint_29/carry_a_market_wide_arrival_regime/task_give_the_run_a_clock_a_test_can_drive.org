* Goal

Everything that reads or waits on time in the arrival path does it through one clock interface. Production uses the steady clock; a test advances a manual clock and asserts exact event times without sleeping.

* Acceptance

- The interface offers now() and an interruptible wait_until(t).
- A test of the manual clock wakes a waiter exactly when time is advanced past its deadline, and a stop wakes it at once.
- The clock maps its time to wall time, so a tick can be stamped from it.

* Plan

=service/run_clock.hpp= holds the interface and both clocks. The feeds
stamp a tick inside their body, in fx_spot_feed and ir_curve_feed, so
taking the stamp from the clock changes the body signature that
tick_clock, tick_scheduler and both feeds share. The scheduler task
rewrites that signature and deletes tick_clock, so the stamp moves there,
to [[id:5AEEAC8B-5435-46FD-B9C4-250F634682D3][Schedule every feed on the market clock]],
and the signature changes once.

- =i_run_clock= has now(), wall(t), wait_until(deadline) and interrupt().
  A wait says whether it reached its deadline or was interrupted.
- An interrupt with no waiter is kept for the next wait, so a command
  posted just before the loop waits still wakes it.
- =steady_run_clock= maps the steady clock to wall time once, at
  construction, so a stamp does not jump when the system clock moves.
- =manual_run_clock= moves only on advance(), and await_waiter() lets a
  test advance past a thread that is already waiting.

* Result

=service/run_clock.hpp= gives the run one source of time. A test on the
manual clock wakes a waiter exactly at its deadline, one step after a step
that leaves it short, and an interrupt wakes it at once without moving time.
An interrupt sent before a wait is kept for that wait and only that one.
The steady clock waits for real, gives way to an interrupt, and stamps with
the wall time. The scheduler moves onto the clock, and the ticks take their
stamps from it, in
[[id:5AEEAC8B-5435-46FD-B9C4-250F634682D3][Schedule every feed on the market clock]].
