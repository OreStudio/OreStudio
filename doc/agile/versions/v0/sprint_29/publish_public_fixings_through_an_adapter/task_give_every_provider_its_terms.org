* Acceptance

- The provider has the terms URL, the credit text, whether storage is allowed, a credential reference, and the accepted terms version with who accepted it and when.
- A change of terms makes a new version and clears the acceptance.
- Values stored under earlier terms stay. When new terms withdraw permission to store, the system administrator can delete that provider's fixings, and the deletion records who did it.
- The adapter contract refuses a demand for a provider whose terms are not accepted, and a test shows it.
