* Acceptance

- The rows and columns follow the order the shape declares and the cells hold the snapshot values.
- A hole is an empty cell, never a value of zero.
- The colour scale can span the whole surface or each column, and a column or surface of equal values has one colour.
- A shape with one axis, or more than two, is reported as not drawn.

* Result

=market/surfaceView.ts= turns a snapshot with two axes into a surface: the rows, the columns and the cells, with each cell's text, number and shade. =slicesOf= gives one line for each row as runs that a hole ends.

Acceptance met, with 12 tests in =surfaceView.test.ts=:
- The rows and columns follow the declared order, and the cells hold the snapshot values.
- A hole, and a value that is no number, is an empty cell with no number and no shade.
- The shade can span the whole surface or each column. A group of equal values, a column with one value, and a flat surface sit in the middle. A column of only holes has no shade.
- A shape with one axis, or more than two, is refused as =axes=, and a surface with no node as =empty=.

The module is pure. It draws nothing and adds no server call.
