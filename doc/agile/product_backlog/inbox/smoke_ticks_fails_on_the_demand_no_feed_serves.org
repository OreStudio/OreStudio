* What

=compass smoke ticks= provisions a tenant, waits for the first stored tick and
then fails on the adapters: on a freshly recreated database it reported
=bloomberg-a= accepted 66 and rejected 20 of the 86 ids demanded, after 30 ticks
had arrived and the tenant's 10 subscriptions and 10 routes had been written.
The synthetic service announces 362 ids for that provider, 296 of which it does
not know, so the demo profile asks for market data no feed in this build makes.
An environment therefore cannot pass its own smoke test.

* Why

The smoke is the check an environment is signed off with, and a check that
cannot go green stops being read: the next real regression in tick delivery
arrives as one more line among the rejected ids. Either the profile provisions
only the instruments the feeds cover, or the smoke separates "no feed makes this
id" from "the service dropped this tick" and fails on the second alone.
