* What

On a fresh database at main =4e6e99f2e1=, two pgTAP suites fail.
=acme_fx_volatility_configs_test.sql= fails 3 of 9: the ACME entities read
54 surfaces where the test wants 22, and nine entity and pair rows it does
not list. =refdata_fx_vol_surface_blueprints_publish_test.sql= fails 2 of
15: the derived source quotes 35 pairs where the test wants 10. It then
stops on a duplicate key in =market_series_party_id_oresmd_uri_provider_code_uniq_idx=,
because the series its fixture inserts already exists. Update the counts and
the fixture pair to the seed as it now stands.

* Why

The seed now surfaces every pair the ladder carries, out to ten years, and
the two tests still state the counts from before. A red pgTAP suite hides a
real failure among the stale ones.
