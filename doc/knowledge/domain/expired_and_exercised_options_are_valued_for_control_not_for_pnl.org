#+title: Expired and exercised options are valued for control, not for P&L
#+description: After expiry an option leaves P&L, but it is still valued to catch wrong decisions; controls check each cut.
#+type: knowledge
#+audience: user
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:expiry:option:valuation:pnl:
#+created: 2026-11-22
#+updated: 2026-11-22

* Summary

An expired option is not valued and an exercised one is not valued either,
because its exercise trade carries the value; valuing both would count it twice.
For control, both are still valued: an expired option to catch one that was in
the money, an exercised one as a forward at its strike, so that the exercise
trade can be checked against it. Control reports after each cut check that the
right action was taken, the right trades were generated and the right spot was
used.

* Detail

** Valuation after expiry

- *Expired options* are force-valued for control, in case one was expired in
  error while in the money.
- *Exercised options* are valued as if they were a forward at the strike, on
  the fixed date. The value of the exercise trade less the value of the
  exercised option should be zero; a difference shows a wrong exercise.
- *Rolling through an expiry.* Rolling the portfolio forward removes expired
  and exercised options, or values them at zero, and generates their exercise
  trades on forward dates.

** In P&L attribution

Expiries and exercises are trade activities, attributed after new trades,
amendments and cancellations and before fixings; an exercise pairs an amended
option with a new FX or cash trade, which net together but each look like a
break alone. The activity components are in [[id:B50355E0-F016-4CA1-9932-404DAF83EA7A][P&L Attribution]], and
the fixings that follow expiries are in
[[id:D4CA27E5-8194-470B-A422-B77C53545560][A fixing replaces a projected rate with an observed one]].

** Controls

| Report or control | Purpose |
|-------------------+---------|
| Post-cut checks | After each cut: the right action, the right settlement and fixing trades, the right spot, every option left aside signed off, and the right non-deliverable fixing dates |
| Expiries by cut, expected actions | The options of every cut and the action each should take |
| Expiry report | Options expiring over a horizon, in the money beyond a threshold |
| Control explains | Exercise trades that do not match the option's value before exercise; options that should have been exercised and were not |
| Exceptions | Fixing-determined trades without a fixing |
| Non-deliverable warnings | Holiday conflicts on expiry, delivery and fixing dates |

The fixing controls are in
[[id:B5CA0D8F-D9C5-4F09-ABD7-7ED6F0FEF1A5][Fixing management authorises every fixing that live trades require]].

today; see [[id:3976695F-F526-4C2A-80ED-89B54854CF6D][Trade Blotter]].
