* Acceptance

- Every process announces its start and its graceful stop, and telemetry stores the events so the roster tells stopped from lost and counts restarts.
- A service that starts again raises a service restarted notification to the super administrators.
- A service that shuts down gracefully logs out and leaves no open session behind.
- The system-health home shows a services section that summarises the roster and links to the services screen.
