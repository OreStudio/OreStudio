* Acceptance

- =flows=, =stresstest_flows= and =todaysmarketcalibration_cashflows= each have a typed row and a reader.
- A flow row keeps its trade, leg, cashflow number and pay date, and the reader refuses a row that omits one of them.
- A stressed flow states base, scenario and difference for each figure, and the reader refuses a row that states fewer than three.
- The tests read the checked-in sample of each family.

* Plan

Three families, three typed rows, three readers, because their meaning is the
relation between columns rather than a bag of figures. Each reader asks for the
columns that make the row a row — trade, leg, cashflow number and pay date — and
refuses a row that omits one. A figure the engine states as its null string
stays empty, so a flow with no fixing is not a flow with a zero fixing.

Two cases the corpus does not ship are stated as fixtures beside the suite: a
flow row without its pay date, and a stressed flow that states a base without a
scenario. The reader's rules came from the corpus first: the volatility figures
are stated with a base and a scenario and no difference, so a difference is
optional and only the base and the scenario are required.

- The corpus states the floor and cap volatilities with a base and a scenario
  and no difference, so a stressed figure requires two parts and carries a third

  every checked-in flow states one. The reader refuses it rather than guessing a
  date.
- The three families are read by three functions rather than one generic one
  because the row types differ: a flow, a stressed flow and a calibration flow
  share almost no columns.

* Result

Three typed readers, one for each child-row family, and 42 assertions over 7
cases. A flow keeps its trade, leg, cashflow number, pay date, flow type and
currency, and carries each figure as an optional, so a null fixing is absent
rather than zero. A stressed flow carries its base, scenario and difference per
figure, with the difference optional because the corpus omits it for the
volatilities. A calibration flow keeps the market object, pillar and rate helper
that made the flow.

The suite reads the checked-in sample of each family and three synthetic
fixtures for what the corpus does not show.
