* Goal

Give =feed_binding= a product-kind discriminator (or equivalent), and
make every consumer of =get_feed_bindings_request= filter by the kind
it actually cares about, so binding an IR curve feed never contaminates
FX-only screens (and vice versa).

Root-caused while manually testing [[id:48D6933D-05F4-4B34-B796-FE1B4A2A6444][Extend SyntheticBindingDialog to
cover IR curve configs]] (scenario step "Confirm no regression to the
FX-only flow" failed):

- =ores_marketdata_feed_bindings_tbl= / =feed_binding=
  (=projects/ores.marketdata/api/include/ores.marketdata.api/domain/
  feed_binding.hpp=) carries only =ore_key= and =source_name= -- no
  field distinguishing FX from IR (or anything else).
- =FxSpotGridWindow::reload()=
  (=projects/ores.qt/mktdata/src/FxSpotGridWindow.cpp=) fetches *all*
  enabled bindings via =get_feed_bindings_request{offset=0,
  limit=1000}= with no kind filter, and =buildRows()= renders every
  one as a currency-pair row via =pair_from_ore_key()=.
- Before this story, only FX spot configs could ever be bound, so this
  happened to work. Now that =SyntheticBindingDialog= can bind IR
  curve configs too, an IR binding (e.g. ore_key ="USD/LIBOR-3M"=)
  shows up in the FX Spot grid, misparsed as if it were a currency
  pair.
- =CrmCrossRatesMatrixMdiWindow= and any other consumer of the same
  request are likely exposed to the same issue -- worth auditing all
  callers of =get_feed_bindings_request=, not just FxSpotGridWindow.

* Acceptance

- =feed_binding= carries a way to identify what kind of series it
  binds (FX spot / IR curve / ...), populated at bind time.

  feed no longer produces a row in the FX Spot grid.
- Every other consumer of =get_feed_bindings_request= (CRM matrix,
  etc.) is audited and filters by the kind it expects.
- No regression to the existing FX-only binding/display flow.
