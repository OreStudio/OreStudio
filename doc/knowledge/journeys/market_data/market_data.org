A curve or a surface is a composite object the desk is responsible for: it is
built from what the market sends, corrected by hand where the market is wrong
or thin, approved by a second pair of eyes, and published so that pricing and
reporting can use it. This group holds the journeys that author those objects,
watch and clear the market data they are built from, build and diagnose them,
adjust them by hand, and govern their publication.

Market observations and published curves are two different things. An
observation is what the market sends and nobody signs it off; a published
curve is what the system uses, and it exists only once a person has published
it. Nothing a feed writes is used until then.
