* Goal

The public overnight rate of as many currencies as their publishers allow is stored as fixings in the system tenant, with its history. The system reads each rate the way it reads the public FX rates through Frankfurter: a supplier, an adapter, a session with its terms accepted at setup, and durable fixings. [[id:619CE2BF-C972-47CF-8900-AC2742AC2CD9][Calibrate the synthetic IR feeds from the public rates]] then starts each synthetic overnight-index feed from its currency's latest public rate.

* Acceptance

- Each usable public overnight rate has its own adapter, a supplier in the catalogue, a session and a feed. Its terms are accepted at setup, as the terms of the FX sources are.
- A fresh environment stores the history of each usable rate as fixings, with no person acting.
- SOFR, €STR, SONIA and CORRA are stored first, because the survey found them usable.
- Each of the 28 currencies has a route and terms read, and a recorded answer: an adapter, or the reason it has none.
- The fixings are the input of [[id:619CE2BF-C972-47CF-8900-AC2742AC2CD9][Calibrate the synthetic IR feeds from the public rates]] in [[id:BE2FF8F5-3AC6-4B7C-B1BB-4EA480DE2C29][Build the IR curves of every supported currency from public data — the remaining work]].

* Decisions

- One adapter for each publisher. Each publisher has its own API, its own format and its own terms, so an adapter that reads many publishers does too much work. Two publishers share an adapter only when their API and format are the same, as the Frankfurter providers are.
- The plumbing is shared and the fetching is not. A library holds the announce, the demand, the backfill and the publish; an adapter supplies its fetcher, its parser and its settings. See [[id:F4BBEF34-D84C-4AE6-A3AA-DC47C3EE06EE][Public sources publish daily fixings through an adapter]].
- Each publisher is read directly, not through an aggregator such as FRED, because the survey read the terms of each publisher and not of the aggregators.
- Each adapter task ends with fixings stored in a fresh environment: the adapter, the catalogue mapping, the supplier, the session and the feed land together.
- One session per supplier; the supplier and the publisher coincide for the direct APIs.
- The service is end_of_day for every source.
- Restructured on 2026-11-09. The story now reads overnight rates only.
