* Acceptance

- The cap/floor strikes are rows of instrument_strikes and its schedules are rows of instrument_schedules and instrument_schedule_dates, written by the import and read by the export.
- The swaption's option data is rows of instrument_options and its four children, with premium_amount, premium_currency, premium_pay_date, exercise_prices and settlement_fixing_date typed rather than text, and instrument_option_exercise_fees.start_date typed as a date.
- The rates header's Delete cascade names the shared children, so a header delete closes them.
- instrument_schedules.owner_role's check lists the rates family's owner roles beside trs_funding, repo, ascot_swap, option and trs.
- The children's registration sits in the root that owns them, not in registrar_bond.cpp.
- The ORE round trip keeps a cap/floor's strikes and schedules and a swaption's option block.

* Plan

1. *The shapes.* The bond pilot's =instrument_strike=, =instrument_schedule=,
   =instrument_schedule_date=, =instrument_option= and its four keyed children
   are the target. Read them first and reuse their column sets rather than
   widening them; the family writes to them, it does not extend them. The one
   exception is =instrument_schedules.owner_role=, whose check lists
   =trs_funding=, =repo=, =ascot_swap=, =option= and =trs= today: the rates
   owner roles join that list when the first rates schedule lands.
2. *The models.* Give the cap/floor and the swaption the children they write,
   and retype the option block's text columns: =premium_amount=,
   =premium_currency=, =premium_pay_date=, =exercise_prices=,
   =settlement_fixing_date= and =instrument_option_exercise_fees.start_date=.
3. *The cascade and the children list.* Add the shared children to
   =ores.trading.rate_instruments=' =* Delete cascade= and to the trade-type
   catalogue's =* Batch / ** Children=, so a header delete closes them and the
   export carries them.
4. *The registration root.* Move the shared children's registration out of
   =registrar_bond.cpp= into the root that owns them, so the bond family stops
   being the reason a rates child is registered.
5. *The mapper.* Write the children on import and read them on export, beside
   the leg collections the family already carries.
6. *Order.* Take one product end to end first — the cap/floor, which has the
   fewest own fields once the dates moved — with a build and the round trip
   between products.
