* Acceptance

- The as-of read names the series the party's recipes quote from, rather than
  every series the party sees, and a series that holds no expiry or strike is
  not read at all.
- The step publishes the same surfaces it published before the read changed.
- The read's cost is stated as rows read before and after, for one party of the
  demo tenant, with the command that counted them.

* Plan

=read_party_quotes= today reads =market_observation_repository::read_as_of_all=
and keeps the rows whose datum carries an expiry and a strike. The series a
party's surfaces quote from are already known by then: =read_party_recipes=
returns the recipes, and each recipe's =sources= name a =market_series_id=. So
the read can be asked for those ids, the way =read_party_recipes= already asks
for its blueprints, pillars and sources by id list.

What to keep an eye on: a surface whose source series is missing is a surface
that waits, and the wait is decided by =plan_party_surfaces= from the series
the party holds, not from the quotes. Narrowing the quote read must not turn a
series the party holds but that has not ticked into a series the party does not
hold.
