* Result

The equity family states its rows. The planner builds the header row each of the
seventeen codes routes to, the worst-of basket's constituents and periods, the
position's option underlyings and the accumulator's range bounds, and the writer
persists what the plan carries. The writer's equity arm is gone: it was the
longest of the six, and it carried three stamping idioms of its own.

The population is unchanged: 17 headers across nine tables, 2 basket
constituents, 4 swap periods, 4 range bounds and 127 trades, which is what a
fresh tenant held before the flip.

One thing the flip made explicit that the bag had hidden: a worst-of basket
swap's header row names no underlying. Its names are its constituents, so the
test now reads the constituents when the header states none, instead of
asserting that every header names a single name.

The plan's bag narrowed to two: bond, whose security still travels in it, and
credit, which has not flipped.
