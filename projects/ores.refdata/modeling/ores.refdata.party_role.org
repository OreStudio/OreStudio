A party role is the part a party plays in a transaction or a relationship, such as the guarantor or the beneficiary. The scheme can be extended with custom roles. Each row is one value of an FpML coding scheme: a code, unique within
its coding scheme, with the source that defines it and a description. The rows
arrive through DQ from the FpML codelists.
