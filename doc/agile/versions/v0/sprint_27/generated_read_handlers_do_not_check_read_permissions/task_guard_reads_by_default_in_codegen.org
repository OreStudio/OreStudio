* Acceptance

- =protocol_operations= guards a read unless its subject is in the model's
  =:open_reads:=; a subject that is not one of the resource's reads fails the
  generation.
- =:guard_reads:= is removed; the role and permission models open exactly the
  allow-listed subjects.
- Every component's handlers and history registrars are regenerated, and the
  diff is the permission checks alone.
- The codegen tests state the new rule, including a junction and a misspelt
  open subject.

* Result

=protocol_operations= guards every read with its resource's read code. A
model opens a read only by naming its subject in =:open_reads:=, and a subject
that is not one of its reads fails the generation. =:guard_reads:= is gone
from the seven models that set it; the role model opens =iam.v1.roles.list=
and =iam.v1.roles.get=, and the permission model =iam.v1.permissions.list=.
History registrars always pass the entity's read code.

Every component's handlers and history registrars were regenerated; the diff
is the permission checks alone (a script filtered every changed line against
the check's shape). Codegen tests: 1161 passed, including an open read, a
misspelt open subject and a junction.
