#+title: The trade entity's column list exceeds MSVC's template depth
#+description: windows-msvc-release-ninja fails C1202 at trade_entity.hpp:95, but not because the trade entity is wide: it fails in ores.trading.core unity unit 55, where it is reached after 56 other entity headers and 1,179 bindings, and compiles in unit 54, reached after 91. The cost is what the unity unit has accumulated, and binding counts alone do not predict it.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The nightly of 2026-11-19 (run 44485188155, head =e866b76f3e=) failed in
=windows-msvc-release-ninja= with =fatal error C1202: recursive type or
function dependency context too complex= at =trade_entity.hpp:95=, the
entity's =columns()= chain.

The trade entity's width is not the cause. At 19 bindings it is not the
widest entity, and MSVC printed only 2 levels of template context, so
the instantiation in hand was shallow. The failure is in
=ores.trading.core= unity unit 55 (=trade_identifier_mapper.cpp= to
=trade_repository.cpp=), which reaches =trade_entity.hpp= at position
139 of 165 headers, after 56 other entity headers and 1,179 bindings.
Unit 54 also includes it, after 91 bindings, and compiles. =swap_leg=
is in units 52 and 53, not unit 55.

A binding budget per unit does not explain it either: unit 53 holds
1,299 bindings and unit 44 holds 1,271, more than unit 55's 1,199, and
both compile. Mappers, repositories and other templates the sources
instantiate are not counted, and only MSVC can say what it counts.

The fix waits on a measurement. compass's story Keep MSVC within its
template limits runs MSVC probes that rebuild unit 55's shape from
synthetic entities, to find whether the column lists alone reproduce
it. If they do not, unit 55 itself is compiled with MSVC and its
sources bisected. Options once the cost is known: break unit 55 with
=cmake/unity_exclusions.cmake= or a smaller unity batch for
=ores.trading.core=, or change how the column list nests.

* Why

The failure is on MSVC release alone; the Linux and Windows clang legs
compile the same tree. No MSVC Windows artefact can be built while it
stands. The earlier reading of this capture, that the trade entity is
too wide, would have sent the fix to the wrong place.
