* Acceptance

- A job of the service drops the whole chunks older than the retention period when the database has no retention policy, and it does nothing when the policy exists.
- A test inserts ticks into old chunks and checks that the job drops them and keeps the newer ones.
- The job logs how many chunks it dropped, and the ingest status shows the age of the oldest tick.
