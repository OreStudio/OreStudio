The adapter is specialised: it reads the Bank of England statistical database alone, and it supplies only its fetcher and its settings to the shared half of [[id:0DB85F8F-4C7D-4428-B514-AF69CE8E5D86][Separate the public source adapter's plumbing from its fetching]].

* Acceptance

- The adapter reads the Bank of England statistical database alone, and it does not copy the announce, the demand, the backfill or the publish code.
- The Bank of England statistical database is a supplier in the catalogue, its SONIA series maps to the SONIA item of [[id:0D8B2280-F6E1-4198-8372-6712D1E8DFD8][Catalogue the overnight rate items]], and a session and a feed of it are seeded in the system tenant.
- The session's terms are accepted at setup, as the terms of the FX sources are. A session whose terms are not accepted is skipped and reported, and provisioning does not fail.
- A fresh environment stores the SONIA history as fixings within minutes of provisioning, and the log names the source and the count it stored.
- Each fixing is stored as published, with the credit that the terms ask for.
- The depth of the history is measured and written into [[id:3F9D6EAE-9FB8-4A0D-9BAF-1EA3EA93AB4F][the calibration sources note]].
- Unit tests parse a recorded response of the Bank of England statistical database, and a malformed response gives an error, not a fixing.

* Plan

- =boe_fetcher= implements =fetcher=: adapter =boe_iadb=, scheme =boe_iadb=, field =value=. A source's key is a series code, =IUDSOIA= for SONIA, checked as capitals and digits before it enters a URL.
- A read is one GET of =_iadb-fromshowcolumns.asp= with =csv.x=yes= over the window, whose dates the database names as =29/Oct/2026=. The answer's header must be =DATE,<code>=, and each row is a date such as =29 Oct 2026= and the value in percent, stored as its decimal.
- Probed on 2026-11-10: a window with no business day answers its header alone, which holds no fixing; an unknown series answers a 302 to the database's error page, which is an error.
- The seeds add the =boe= supplier, the =boe_iadb= adapter, the =boe-database= session, and the =boe-sonia= feed, the Bank's default, scoped to the SONIA item. The system party gains a subscription to =boe= and an =interest_rates= route to the feed.
- The Bank's terms allow SONIA for our own calibration and curves; a direct licence is needed to redistribute it to clients. Only the system tenant routes to =boe-sonia=: a provisioned tenant has no route to it.

* Result

SONIA is stored as fixings in the system tenant, read from the Bank of England's statistical database by a specialised fetcher that the official adapter hosts beside Frankfurter, the New York Fed and the ECB. A fresh environment accepted the Bank's terms at setup and stored 276 fixings, the last 400 days, with the log line "Sent 276 fixing(s) of 'boe-sonia'". The values are the decimals of the published percents, such as =0.039824= for 3.9824, and the provider =boe= carries the SONIA credit line of the Open Government Licence. Only the system tenant routes to the feed, as the Bank's terms need a direct licence to pass SONIA to clients. The calibration sources note records the 7,542 rates the database serves since 1997-01-02.

Unit tests parse a recorded answer, Windows line ends and a header alone, refuse seven malformed answers, check the URL of a read, treat the database's redirect as an error, and refuse a key that is not a series code.
