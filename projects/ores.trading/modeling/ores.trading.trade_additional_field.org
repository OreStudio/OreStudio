One field a trade carries in a document's own vocabulary: ORE's
=AdditionalFields= is open content, any element name with any content,
and it is where a document states what ORE Studio does not model. The row
keeps the element's name and its text, so nothing is decoded and both
round trip. The order is the document's order, and the ordinal preserves
it.

The row is keyed by the trade and the ordinal, and references the
[[id:4304A441-E532-45FB-837A-378F13693CAE][trade anchor]] with a database foreign key. It copies the anchor's party,
pinned to the anchor, because row-level security needs the party on
every row. It replaces the envelope's additional field table, which the
ORE import moves off in its own task.
