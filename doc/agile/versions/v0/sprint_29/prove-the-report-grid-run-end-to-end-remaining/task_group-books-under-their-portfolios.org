* Result

The book level states each portfolio above the books it holds.

A book that names a portfolio carries the portfolio's name, and a portfolio row
sits above the books under it, with its trades, NPV and notional summed from
them. Books sit under their portfolio, ordered largest first; a book in no
portfolio stands on its own. The grouping is the one the counterparty level
already uses, now shared: one helper, two parents.

On run =c2a86671= the level draws *Liquidity Buffer Portfolio* at
=33,709,129.46= HKD over eight trades, above *Liquidity Buffer Bonds* with the
same eight trades: the two agree, which is the point of stating a group at all.

A portfolio row does not lead anywhere, because no screen reads a portfolio
record; the book under it does.
