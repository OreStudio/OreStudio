#+description: The bond planner draws its product by the slot's position and the book by the slot's position too, so with an even number of products against two books a product's parity fixes its book: every even-indexed product lands in one book, every odd-indexed one in the other, and no security is ever shared across books. Measured while writing the security naming test: eighty slots over two books produced no security held by both. Two cycles of even length align. Either the product cycle takes a stride coprime with the book count, or the book is drawn from something other than the slot's index.
