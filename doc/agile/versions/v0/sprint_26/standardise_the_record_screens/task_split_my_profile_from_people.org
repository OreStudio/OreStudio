* Result

My profile holds only the signed-in person's own account, in tabs: details,
contact and the access they hold. Managing other people moved to People: the
shared record list over the accounts, paged, searched (username, name, email)
and sorted (username, name) on the server, and a page per person with the
standard header and details, contact, roles and sign-ins as tabs. Both pages
draw the same identity and contact forms from =access/PersonForms.tsx=. People
has a Home card and a menu entry for a person who may read accounts.

Along the way the tab bar became a shared part (=ui/Tabs.tsx=) and the record
list takes a source, so any record kind lists the same way. Checked live: 59
people page fifteen at a time, a search for "admin" finds the tenant
administrator, and the person and profile tabs draw.
