#+title: Parties journeys
#+description: A party is the tenant's own legal entity and a counterparty is the entity it trades with: both are described by identifiers and contacts, and by the countries, currencies, books and units they are connected to.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:user_journey:refdata:

#+updated: 2026-10-07

A party is the tenant's own legal entity. A counterparty is the entity the
tenant trades with. The model holds both, and a junction says which parties may
trade with which counterparties. Each is described the same way: a name, the
identifiers that name it in the world, the people to contact, and the
memberships that connect it to countries, currencies and business units. A
counterparty carries more: the netting agreements, the sets under them, and the
credit support that governs them.

The two journeys are separate because the goals are separate. A person brings a
counterparty on board once, and a person keeps a party's own record current.
Both are tenant-administrator work.
