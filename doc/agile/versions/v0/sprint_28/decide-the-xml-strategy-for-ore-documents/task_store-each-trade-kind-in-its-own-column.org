** Problem

The schema's trade element holds one instrument kind out of about 190 element
names (=struct trade= has 194 lines between its braces, measured at the
current =main=). The generator turns that choice into one inline member per name, so
=struct trade= in =xml/generated/portfolio.hpp= is 346,624 bytes. Every new
trade zeroes all of it. Some names share a type: four members are a
=swap_data=. The zero fill is 33 percent of the portfolio read test.

The problem is in the binding record only. The domain types are separate and
the projections copy out of this record.

** Constraint

No variant. Our approach is data-oriented: plain structs, columns, indices. A
variant hides a tag and a union behind a type and gives each trade the size of
its largest kind.

** Design

Store each kind as a column.

- =trade= keeps =present=, =id=, =trade_type=, =envelope=, =trade_actions=, a
  kind tag and an index into the column for that kind.
- =portfolio= owns one container per kind type, not per element name. The four
  =swap_data= names share one column, and the tag tells them apart.
- The reader sees the element name, finds its tag in the table, appends to the
  column and stores the index.
- The writer reads the tag, follows the index and writes the element under the
  name the tag gives.

This is the same idiom as =indexed_nested=: a member is an index into a
container the parent owns. The new part is that the column belongs to the
portfolio and not to the trade, and that one trade uses at most one of them.

** Open questions

1. Where the column lives. A trade nested in a component or a derivative has a
   different parent from a top-level one. The column may need to live in the
   document root, or in each parent that holds a trade list.
2. A new field kind or a reuse of =indexed_nested=. The existing kind assumes
   the arena sits in the same record as the index.
3. How the generator detects the choice. It records =CHOICE_CHILDREN= already,
   but only to drop the required flag.
4. What the projections read. They walk =trade= members by name today and must
   walk the tag instead.
5. Whether the deque from #3579 is still needed for trades once the record is
   small.

** Steps

1. Answer the open questions by reading the generator, the engine and the
   projections. Write the answers here.
2. Add the column and the tag to the generator, behind the choice detection.
3. Teach the engine to append to a column and the writer to follow a tag.
4. Move the projections to the tag.
5. Measure with callgrind and record the benchmark.
