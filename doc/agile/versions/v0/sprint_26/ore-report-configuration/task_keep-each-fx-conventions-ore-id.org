* Acceptance

- Every FX convention in the corpus is stored under its own id, including the 600 or more that do not end in -FX-CONVENTIONS.
- A currency pair can hold more than one FX convention, as FX-BOE-USD-CAD and USD-CAD-FX do in one file.
- ores_refdata_validate_convention_id_fn resolves an FX convention by its stored id, and the conventions round trip asserts the id instead of documenting its loss.
