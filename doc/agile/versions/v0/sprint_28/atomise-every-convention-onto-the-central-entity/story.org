* Goal

Tenor basis swap is rebuilt on the central convention row and the composite parent-and-parts pattern, with the family get and put generated. Each remaining convention follows in its own PR, and no convention type or row holds the whole convention.

* Acceptance

- Tenor basis swap is a central convention row plus tenor_basis_swap_pay_receive and tenor_basis_swap_long_short child entities, with the parent version bumped on any part write.
- The generic put writes the central row and the family put writes the central row and the parts in one transaction, and each family get mirrors its put.
- The nine conventions of the cluster and the other kinds each convert in their own PR, with no aggregate type or nested member left.
- The Windows translation unit pressure is gone, because no unit reflects a wide entity type.
