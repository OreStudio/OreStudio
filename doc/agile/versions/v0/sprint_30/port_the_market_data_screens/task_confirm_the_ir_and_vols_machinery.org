* Result

The machinery exists and is the machinery the service already runs:
=curve_bootstrap_engine= for the discount factors, =day_count_calculator= for the
year fractions, =forward_rate_calculator= for the implied forwards,
=resolve_end_date= for the maturity dates, and =surface_grid_of= with the
calibration service for the vol grid and its slices. The zero rate is
=-ln(DF)/τ= over two values those functions already return.

What the operations must decide, and state once, is: that =zero_rate= is
continuously compounded, that =implied_forward= is the rate the segment ending
at the pillar implies, that =shift= is curve less market, and where the
staleness threshold lives. None of the four is quant work.

The investigation is =investigation_ir_and_vols_machinery.org= in this story.
