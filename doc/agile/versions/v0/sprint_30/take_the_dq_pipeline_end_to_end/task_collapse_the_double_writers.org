* Acceptance

- No table is written by both a direct seed and a DQ publish.
- The surviving writer is the one that owns the data, and the reason is stated.
- A check fails when a table gains a second writer.
- The eleven system-only tables are classified, not left undecided.

* Plan

1. Take the fourteen two-writer tables from the inventory.
2. Keep DQ for the eight refdata datasets and =iso.countries=; keep the direct
   seed only for the system party and the WRLD business centre.
3. Delete the other side, and adjust the dataset or the seed accordingly.
4. Classify the eleven tables that have no tenant route.
