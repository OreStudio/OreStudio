* Goal

A person can see, from clickable prototypes, how conventions are managed. Each prototype answers to a user journey that is written first, to the journey conventions of the project. The journeys build on the five that exist and do not repeat them. Each gap a journey finds on the server is recorded.

* Decisions

- The management of conventions is four journeys, not one: find a convention by its terms, name a convention set, retire a convention and decide a convention change. The owner agreed the catalogue on 2026-10-19.
- Naming a set is the authoring UX that the story "Name the convention sets a report chooses" defers.
- The approver role exists. The missing piece is an approval kind for a convention, which is filed as a capture.
- The four prototypes share one stylesheet and one script by copy, and the inventory check keeps the copies equal.
