This page documents a test scenario verifying [[id:5057B9A6-66F0-4E4E-967F-DF915809DF3E][Merge the trades and structures screens]] in [[id:67C1C30A-5CF6-408A-B73C-99A79A01B19F][Trade Blotter — the remaining work]]. It walks the one screen the Trading area offers: the trades and the structures read together, by the portfolio tree or by counterparty. Record the outcome by hand in each =*** Result=.

* Before you start

- The fleet is up on this checkout and the database holds tenant
  =acme_corporation= provisioned from =acme_demo= with its trade population.
  Recreate and provision only if it is stale:
  =compass db recreate -y -k=, then
  =compass shell -f projects/ores.shell/scripts/library/provisioning/how_do_i_provision_the_system_with_acme_corporation_holding_group.ores=.
- What the screen shows is one party's holdings. Signed in as =diane.kay= you
  see the structures and trades of =ACME Corporation UK plc=. The database holds
  the other legal entities' holdings too; they belong to parties this session
  does not work for, and they are not a defect.
- A structure's legs are placed where its trades are booked. The population books
  most structures into one book each; one structure whose legs sit in two books
  is the case Step 5 needs, and it says so on its row when the data has one.

* Steps

** Log in as diane.kay

- Open http://127.0.0.1:20802/.
- Username =diane.kay@acme_corporation=, password =Secure-Password-123=, then
  *Sign in*.
- Passes when the home page greets *Welcome, Diane Kay* and the status bar names
  tenant *Acme Corporation* and party *ACME Corporation UK plc*.

** Reach the blotter from the area

- Choose *Trading* in the menu.
- Passes when the area page offers one card, *Trade blotter*.
- Open it. Passes when the address is =/trading/blotter= and the crumb reads
  *Home › Trading › Trade blotter*.
- Also open =/trading/trades= and =/trading/structures= in the address bar: each
  passes when it lands on =/trading/blotter=, because those two lists are the
  screen's rows now.

*** Result

| Field  | Value |
|--------+-------|
| Status | PASS |

** Read the portfolio tree's counts

- The rail on the left is headed *Read by* and offers *Portfolio tree*.
- Passes when the rail's first node is the party, *ACME Corporation UK plc*, with
  a number on the same line, and each portfolio and book under it carries its own
  number.
- Read the biggest portfolio's number, open it, and read the list's pager. Passes
  when the pager's total equals the number the node stated.
- Passes when a book with nothing booked to it is not drawn, and when the numbers
  add up: a portfolio's figure is the rows placed anywhere below it.

*** Result

| Field  | Value |
|--------+-------|
| Status | PASS |

** Read the rows and their columns

- The columns are *Identifier*, *What*, *Book*, *Counterparty*, *Date*, *State*;
  *Structure* is not drawn yet.
- Passes when a structure's row states its reference (=PKG-…= or =DYN-…=), its
  kind, the book or books its legs sit in, a counterparty name, a date and a
  state.
- Passes when a trade's row states its own reference (=POP-…=) and its own book,
  counterparty, date and state, and when the *Book* and *Counterparty* cells are
  links.
- Passes when no row is a leg of a structure: a structure's legs are inside it,
  and its row stands in their place.

** Narrow to a book and a portfolio

- Choose a book on the rail. Passes when every row's *Book* is that book, and the
  pager's total equals the number the node stated.
- Choose the portfolio above it. Passes when the list holds the rows of every
  book below that portfolio, and its total equals the portfolio's number.
- Passes when a structure whose legs span books is **not** under either book but
  under the portfolio that holds them both, and its row carries the chip
  *spans books* and names both books. If the data holds no such structure, record
  that here and pass the step on the chip's absence being explainable.

** Flatten the list to the legs

- Tick *Flat* beside *Group trades*.
- Passes when the *Structure* column appears, the pager's total grows to every
  trade of the party, and each leg states the structure that holds it.
- Passes when a trade no structure holds shows a dash in *Structure*, and when
  unticking *Flat* brings back the top rows with the column gone.

*** Result

** Read the rows by counterparty

- Choose *Counterparty* in *Read by*. Passes when the rail becomes the
  counterparties the rows face, each with its own number, and the tree is gone.
- Choose a counterparty. Passes when every row's *Counterparty* is that one, and
  the pager's total equals the node's number.
- Choose *Portfolio tree* again. Passes when the tree is drawn at once, not the
  counterparties and not a wait.
- Choose a counterparty again, then the tree: the same, both ways.

** Open what a row names

- From the top rows, click a structure's identifier. Passes when the structure's
  page opens at =/trading/structures/<reference>=, titled by its kind, with its
  tree beside it and its legs under *Members*.
- Go back, tick *Flat*, and click a trade's identifier. Passes when the trade's
  page opens at =/trading/trades/<id>= with its *Details* tab.
- From either page, the crumb leads back to the blotter.

** Group trades from the blotter

- Click *Group trades*. Passes when the grouping screen opens, at
  =/trading/structures/group=.
- Pick two trades of one counterparty and group them. Passes when the new
  structure is offered back to the blotter: return to =/trading/blotter= and it
  is one row holding those two trades as legs.
- If the picker offers no pair sharing a counterparty, record it: that is the
  population's shape, not the screen's.
