* What

The calendars are generated from QuantLib. Its lists of lunar holidays are hard-coded and stop at a year. Closed days fall from 19 in 2026 to 1 in 2035 for China.SSE, from 15 to 4 for Taiwan, and from 11 to 5 for Singapore and Turkey. Extend those lists, from an official source, as data on our side.

* Why

A long-dated value date in those currencies can fall on a holiday. ORE prices with the same lists, so the two stay consistent until we extend them.
