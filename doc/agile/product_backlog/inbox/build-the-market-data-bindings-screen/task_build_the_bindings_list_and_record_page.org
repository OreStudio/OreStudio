* Goal

The prototype is the design. The list has the key first, the kind, the series title as a link, the enabled flag, the status and the last tick, with search, the kind filter, the pager and the four states. The record page has the access mark, the status, the Details tab with the feed link and the series with its oresmd URL and Inspect, and the Last changed line. A vendor binding is read only and shows its one notice.
