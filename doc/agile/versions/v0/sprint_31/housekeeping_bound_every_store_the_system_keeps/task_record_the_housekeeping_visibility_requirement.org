* Result

The requirement is recorded in three places, so that it survives whichever of
them a later reader reaches first.

- The story's acceptance now states that every housekeeping run is visible in the
  system as a scheduled task, with when it last ran, what it freed and whether it
  is enabled, so a person can tell what housekeeping is running and tune it.
- The task
  [[id:8F23F8FE-1951-441C-A866-27EE12733832][Show housekeeping work as
  scheduled tasks]] states how a sweep registers as a scheduler job, appears in
  the screens a person already uses, and is enabled, disabled or retuned from
  there.
- The model now states that each firing is recorded as a scheduler job instance
  and that the housekeeping jobs appear in the scheduler's screens beside every
  other job.

The distinction that made a separate task necessary: a screen listing policies
and their sizes shows what a store is allowed to keep, and says nothing about
whether housekeeping is running, when it last ran or what it freed. The
visibility of runs belongs with the scheduler, which already records job
definitions and their instances, rather than a console of its own.
