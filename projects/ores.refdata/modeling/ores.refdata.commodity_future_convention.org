#+created: 2026-09-29
#+updated: 2026-11-02

Describes how ORE builds a commodity future: when it expires, which contract
months it lists, how its expiry anchors to a day of the month or a weekday, and
the schedule of the options written on it. Corresponds to the
<CommodityFuture> element in ORE conventions.xml.

The contract terms state the listed contract itself, and ORE requires their
frequency and calendar, so every convention fills that part once. The other
four blocks are optional in ORE, and each is a part a convention fills at most
once: the expiry anchor, the option expiry, the off-peak power index and the
averaging data. Each holds one invariant: its fields mean something only when
the convention states that block.

The lists the element holds stay text columns, as they were on the flat row:
the valid contract months, the prohibited expiries and the two continuation
mappings hold comma-separated values.

The family's central row is the shared [[id:9477A085-0822-485F-BDF7-3C8F4A306E93][convention]], which names this kind
with =kind_code= =COMMODITY_FUTURE= and holds the ORE document id. The central
row is shared by every kind, so it holds nothing this kind alone states: those
fields are in this family's parts.
