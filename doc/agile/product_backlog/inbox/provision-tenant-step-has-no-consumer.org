* What the logs said

The step was published and nobody was listening, and the engine said so at the
time:

    [ERROR] [ores.workflow.service.workflow_engine] Step command could not be
    published to a stream, so no service was asked to act:
    workflow=f4e325d7-3c10-4ae0-ba43-cc3a55274d0c step=gleif.lei_entities.large
    subject=refdata.v1.ops.publish_legal_entities_from_dq
    error=JetStream publish failed: No responders available for request

The subject is new: it arrived with the legal entity work (75e227d3cf, merged as
PR #4074), and the running refdata service was a binary built before it, so it
had no handler and did not answer. Nothing was wrong with the workflow's routing.

Two defects follow, and neither is the stale binary:

1. The engine *knows* the step reached no one. It logs an error and yet leaves
   the step `in_progress`, so the parent waits its full 20 minute timeout and the
   tenant stays in `bootstrapping`. A step that could not be published should
   fail at once, with the reason, and the parent should fail with it.
2. The tenant is left in a state nothing can finish. `bootstrap setup` refuses
   because the system is no longer in bootstrap mode, and the workflow's own retry
   publishes to the same subject and waits again.
