* Result

The tenors area now opens on a resolve screen. A person picks a convention, a
tenor, a calendar and a horizon, and reads the anchor date, the date before the
roll, the adjusted date, the roll rule and the calendar. Nothing on the page
writes. The journey that authors the rules hangs off it as a workflow, so
authoring is a deliberate step from reading rather than the price of arriving.

The page asks =refdata.v1.ops.resolve_tenor=, which had no route until now, so
the by-code operation the coverage table promised is reachable from the browser
for the first time. The two resolution routes share one reply and one helper.

Proved against the running service, not by proxy. The same question through the
resolve route and the preview route answers identically: 3M under
RATES_SPOT_FORWARD on Argentina from a horizon of 2026-03-16 and a spot of
2026-03-18 gives an anchor of 2026-03-18, a date before the roll of 2026-06-18
and an adjusted date of 2026-06-18, ModifiedFollowing. A rule asked without its
spot date refuses =missing_spot_date= with empty dates and a 200, which is why
the page now asks for one. An unknown tenor refuses =tenor_not_found= the same
way.

Two defects surfaced in review and one while proving it. The page could not
answer any spot-anchored convention because it sent no spot date. Its default
horizon came from the UTC clock. And the calendar pick list named a catalogue
resolution cannot use, which also broke the journey's own test step.

Web: 146 files and 1643 tests pass, the BFF suite is 66 files and 786 tests,
typecheck and prettier are clean, and the served bundle carries the new route.
