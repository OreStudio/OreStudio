* Acceptance

- Every wave reaches every party of the tenant with a random share, and the shares add up to the wave.
- The Usage tab draws a pie of the succeeded and failed results of the last 24 hours, for a tenant and for the operator.

* Plan

An earlier soak drew one party for each wave, so 8 waves gave an uneven spread, and the share on the Usage tab is a lifetime figure that the old ACMCOR work dominated. The owner asked for a realistic split: the UK arm biggest, then the regions, then the holding company.

- Each wave is one shell session that adds a batch for each party and dispatches that party's share, so every wave reaches every party.
- The share of a party follows its weight, nudged by 20% at random for each wave. The default weights for Acme are UK 5, US 3, Hong Kong 2 and the holding company 0.5. =--party-weight CODE=WEIGHT= overrides one.
- A wave smaller than the number of parties picks its parties by weight, with a job each.
- The Usage tab draws a second pie of the results that finished in the last 24 hours, green for a success and red for a failure. The group pie and the outcomes pie sit side by side with captions. The same panel serves the operator and a tenant.
- The owner asked for the existing jobs to be trashed. The compute tables turn a delete into a logical close, so the current rows of batches, workunits and results were closed and the ledger was emptied. The history stays.
