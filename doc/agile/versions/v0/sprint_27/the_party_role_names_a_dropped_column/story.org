* Decisions

- *Fix the model, not the generated files.* Both generated files say
  =DO NOT EDIT MANUALLY= and are reproduced from the model on every
  regeneration, so editing them would only hide the defect until the next run.
- *Remove the index rather than repoint it.* It existed to find a party role by
  the counterparty, and the counterparty now lives on the trade anchor. The
  generated create script already indexes =(tenant_id, trade_id, role)= and
  =(tenant_id)=.

* Out of scope

- A permanent gate for this defect class. The scratch scan is named in the
  task's Notes; promoting it into =local_checks.py= is its own change.
