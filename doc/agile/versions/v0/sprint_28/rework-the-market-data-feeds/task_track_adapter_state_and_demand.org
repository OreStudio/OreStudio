* Acceptance

- A missed heartbeat makes an adapter stale and a returning heartbeat triggers a new demand set.
- The demand set is the union of the mapped external ids of the parties routed to the adapter, including the inputs of composites.
- The reply lists accepted and rejected ids with reasons, and the status read shows them.

* Plan

(Implementation strategy. Written when work starts; key decisions
are distilled into the parent story's =* Decisions= at close, but the
plan itself stays — it is the historical record of what we did.)

1. Keep the five states and their changes in a registry that reads no clock and no network, so each change can be tested.
2. Compute the demand set of each adapter in a pure function of the catalogue: the union of the mapped vendor ids of the provider over the parties routed to the source, refusing a party with no right as not_entitled.
3. Put the NATS side in a supervisor: it answers the announce, takes the heartbeats, makes an adapter stale on a missed heartbeat, and sends the demand set when an adapter is ready, has recovered or when the catalogue changes.
4. Add a status read that lists each adapter with its state and the ids of its last reply.
5. Test the registry, the calculator and the subject helpers without NATS.

A party states no requirements yet, so a party needs every item of its provider that a mapping reaches. The calculator takes a policy for requirements and for the inputs of a composite, so the composite task only has to supply it.

* Result

The service tracks each adapter as announced, ready, serving, stale or rejected. =adapter_registry= holds the five states and the changes the messages cause: an announce checks the provider and the universe against the catalogue, a missed heartbeat makes an adapter stale after 15 seconds, and a returning heartbeat makes it ready and asks for its demand again. =compute_demand= computes the demand set of each adapter as the union of the mapped vendor ids of the provider over the parties routed to its source, refuses a routed party with no right as not_entitled, and accepts a policy for requirements and the inputs of a composite. =adapter_supervisor= answers the announce, takes the heartbeats, and sends the full demand set when an adapter is ready, has recovered, or the catalogue changes. A status read lists each adapter with its state and the ids of its last reply, under the permission to read market data sources. Thirty-five service test cases and ten subject cases pass. The build of the service, its tests and the core tests passes.

Left for later tasks:
- The supervisor, the status read and the NATS exchange were not run against a live NATS server or a database, because the worktree =.env= is at version 32.
- A route is not refused when its source is not ready. The registry knows the state, and the route trigger does not read it yet.
- A party states no requirements, so it needs every mapped item of its provider. The composite task supplies the policy.
- The demand is not recorded as intervals. The metering task does that.
