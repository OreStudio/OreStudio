* Result

=market/SurfacePanel.tsx= draws the surface of a snapshot with two axes, below the "now" table on the evolution screen. It reads the snapshot the screen already holds, so it adds no server call.

Acceptance met, with 8 tests in =SurfacePanel.test.tsx=:
- The heatmap has the first axis down the side and the second across the top, in the declared order. A cell is shaded with the theme's accent in a share that grows with its value.
- A hole is a dash with no colour, and it breaks a slice line. A lone point before a hole is drawn as a dot.
- A choice of colour scale offers the whole surface or each column.
- A shape with one axis draws nothing, and a shape with more than two axes says it is not drawn.
- The words exist in English, French and Portuguese, and the language parity test passes. The note says a slice is not always a smile.

The slice lines use their own SVG and not =LineChart=, because =LineChart= has no way to draw a hole. The web package is rebuilt so the BFF serves the panel.

Not checked: no one has used the panel in a browser yet, and the colour scale choice is tested only as the options it offers, not as a click that changes the colours.
