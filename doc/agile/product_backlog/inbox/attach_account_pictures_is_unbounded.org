This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

=attach_account_pictures_request= reads the tenant's accounts with a limit of
10,000 and attaches one picture to each account that names one: one
=ensure_image= and one attach per account. Its work follows the account count,
and every account past the ceiling is dropped without a word.

Give the request a page of accounts it names, or retire it and let the
provisioning step do the work one page at a time.

* Why

The story [[id:A4D0E305-CEF6-4684-ABBA-0D09657479F1][Keep workflow steps alive while they work, and off iam]] rules
that no request handler does work that grows with the data. This is the one
request on the provisioning path that still does, and it is the shape that
failed =set_staff_passwords= before it was paged.
