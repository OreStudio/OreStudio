* Goal

Rework the credit trade types and the risk participation agreement to the instrument table rule: a family header holding exactly its codes, product fact tables keyed by the trade, shared children with role codes, and securities keyed by their reference datum.

* Acceptance

- The family header's trade_type check lists exactly the family's codes, and each product fact table checks its own.
- Every instrument row is keyed by its trade and pinned to the anchor's trade type and party by a composite foreign key.
- The family's trade types route through the trade-type catalogue, and the two importer-less codes leave the routing.
- The credit round trip and the risk participation agreement round trip are lossless, and a money value is stored and exported as the digits the document wrote.
- The credit family's dates are mapped into the blotter's common-date view, and the family's tables have a generated eventing integration test.

* Decisions

The remainder is carried in [[id:6A48161C-53AE-4F60-A7C4-8649C3653414][Prove the credit round trip over the corpus]].

Distilled from =design.org=, which carries the argument and the evidence.

- *The family header holds the reference entity and the two dates.* The six
  products share nothing else, and a header column must hold for every code.
- *Credit reuses the swap leg tables,* told apart by a nullable =leg_group=
  column. The legs are keyed by the trade, so a second leg table would repeat
  the first.
- *The two codes with no importer leave the routing.* The catalogue rule is
  that a type with no writer has no table.
- *The agreement's facts get a table of their own.* Its participation rate
  and protection fee are required by the schema and belong to no other
  product.
- *Every money column is an exact decimal, and a continuous quantity is a
  binary float.* A float column stores an artifact of a money value. The
  recovery rate, the tranche points and the participation rate are continuous
  quantities, and each column agrees with its domain type.
