* Summary

The curve-fit prototype argues what the trader's or quant's "Check a curve's
fit" screen shows when a candidate is judged against the quotes it was built
from. The candidate sits at the top, the raw quotes over it, the residuals and
forwards in the middle, and the calendar and roll logic at the foot, so a person
reads the market, then the fit, then the dates it settles on.

It is throwaway: not production code, reading no service, every value mock. It
exists because the [[id:3AC1CC5B-3366-428A-A604-C5921CBEE2C8][journey]] settles
what the check reads but not how it is shown. The group accepted the argument: a
curve is checked against the quotes it came from — residuals, forward smoothness
and roll logic on one screen — and a quote it does not join is a gap, not a zero.

Open the [[file:check-a-curves-fit/][fit check]].

* Detail

** The question

The journey's Goal is to know whether a candidate still stands on the quotes it
was built from and whether its forwards and dates make sense; the person reads
and diagnoses and does not publish. It leaves open how those readings share one
screen: the candidate and the quotes are two separate reads, keyed differently —
the quote by an =oresmd_uri=, the candidate by a =point_id= — so the screen must
place them side by side and say what each distance means. An unjoined quote read
as a zero would invent a market the person never had; a forward step read as a
confirmed fault would claim a check that never ran. The page is written against
both: an unmatched quote is a gap, and a forward finding is marked as one no
operation serves.

** The variants

Switch with the *Show* buttons in the bottom bar, or =?variant=. The variant is
the presentation, which the journey records as the central design question.

| Variant | Name      | The structural argument |
|---------+-----------+-------------------------|
| =a=     | Residuals | The quotes over the candidate, the pillar residuals, the residual plot, then the forwards. Reads a distance at each pillar. |
| =b=     | Overlay   | The candidate against the raw quotes on one chart, with the forwards below. Reads the shape, and a quote standing off the line is seen at once; it carries no residual table and no residual plot. |
| =c=     | Combined  | The overlay and the residual plot above the quotes and residual tables, the tables side by side. Both readings of one fit, and the most work for the least certainty; it carries no forward view. |

The variants disagree about the reading, not colour: A reads a distance, B a
shape, C both. Every variant carries the candidate header, the calendar and roll
panel and the inert read controls, so they differ only in the middle.

** The states

Switch with the *Market* buttons, or =?state= — the mock market the screen reads against.

| State   | What it shows |
|---------+---------------|
| =fit=   | Nine pillars joined, every residual within 2 bp, no forward step, and each tenor resolving on its pillar. The sound candidate. |
| =large= | The 7Y candidate moves to 3.7830 against a quote of 3.8480, so that residual reads *too large* in the table and red in the plot, and the 5Y-7Y forward carries a step of −9.2 bp. |
| =gap=   | The 7Y candidate is absent, so that quote reads *not joined*, its residual reads *unmatched*, and the overlay chart breaks the candidate line and tags the 7Y quote as not joined. |

The residual threshold is the argument in one cell. The page draws 2 bp as the
line between *within* and *too large*. In =gap= the reading is *unmatched* and
never zero: an absent candidate point is not a candidate of zero.

** Driving it

The bottom bar switches the variant and the market, and the page answers to
=?variant= and =?state=, so any combination is a URL. *Read candidate* and
*Recompute candidate* neither read nor compute: each rewrites the strip at the
top with the missing subject it names, and changes nothing else.

** What is fake

Everything. The USD OIS candidate, its nine pillars, the quotes, the forwards,
the roll convention and the resolved dates are constants in the page. There is
no server, no session and no build step, and nothing is published or stored. The
residual, the relative residual and the reading are computed on the page from
the two mock lists, because no diagnostics operation serves a residual; the
forward step is hard-coded and labelled unchecked; the dates are mock and
labelled derived. The oresmd URL editor's plumbing — the iframe to
=../oresmd-url-editor/index.html= — is carried but unreachable.

** Where the code lives

=doc/prototypes/market_data/check-a-curves-fit/=. One file, =index.html=,
holding its markup, styles and script, with a plotting helper =PC= in the same
file, and no framework and no build step; there is no =prototype.css= and no
=prototype.js=. The site copies the directory as it stands through
=ores-deploy-web-app= and adds nothing to it.

** The verdict

The group accepted the argument rather than one variant: a curve is checked
against the quotes it came from, and the residuals, the forward smoothness and
the roll logic belong on one screen in that order. It accepted the readings the
page insists on — an unjoined quote is a gap not a zero, a forward step is drawn
and marked unchecked, and a resolved date is labelled as derived because no
point stores it.

What stayed on the page as the record: the three variants, kept because the
argument must hold whether the person reads a distance or a shape; the three
states beside them; the residual plot the page computes itself; and the inert
read controls. No Gemini review was raised.

** What it does not settle

The diagnostics payload. The screen derives the residual from the two reads it
already has. Whether a residual arrives as its own subject or as a per-pillar
field on the compute reply, and whether the engine's own distance is the one to
show, is open.

The health checker and the join. A checker that flags a forward step exists and
nothing calls it; the quote and the candidate cannot be read together, so the
page matches them on the coordinate. Whether a forward finding is a subject or a
findings field, and whether an overlay read returns both together, is open.

The as-of read, the tolerance and surfaces. The candidate is read at now, so the
page cannot show it as built; the 2 bp line between a sound residual and a large
one is mock; the screen is for curves only, so a surface fit is not checked here.
