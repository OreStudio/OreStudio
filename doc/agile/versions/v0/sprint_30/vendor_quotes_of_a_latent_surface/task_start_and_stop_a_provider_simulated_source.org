* Goal

An operator starts and stops a provider's simulated source from the Provider tab of the synthetic screen. Today a simulated source runs whenever the catalogue serves its provider, and no screen controls it. A vol surface feed runs only while a source holds it, so its feed page shows the state and offers no Start or Stop. This control is the one place to start and stop it.

A vol surface reads three upstream values on every tick: its pair's spot and the two currencies' discounting curves. Nothing in its config names them, so its page cannot show them, and the spot is picked by the pair code alone in two places that can disagree. The config names the spot it reads as the pair the market series is keyed by, an =oresmd= URI and a provider, and the feed's Calibration tab links each upstream.

* Acceptance

- The Provider tab shows each simulated source of the provider, with its state: running or stopped.
- A person who holds the feeds run permission starts and stops a source from the tab.
- A stopped source detaches from its shared surfaces; a surface no source holds stops, and its vol feed reads as stopped.
- A start or stop goes through the BFF, with a test of the route and of the refusal without the permission.
- A vol surface config holds =spot_oresmd_uri= and =spot_provider_code=; the seed sets them from the surface's own collection, and a write that names no spot feed is refused.
- The board read and the latent block's anchor both resolve the spot from that pair, so a surface reads and correlates with one spot feed.
- A running vol feed is never called not running on its Calibration tab.
- The Calibration tab of a vol feed links its spot feed and its two discounting curves, each with its run state, rather than copying their cards.

* Plan

Two parts, each in slices that end in a check.

*Part A: the vol surface names its spot.*

1. *Schema.* =spot_oresmd_uri= and =spot_provider_code= on the vol surface
   feed entity, through its model and codegen. The data that seeds the vol
   feeds sets them from the surface's own collection: its pair's spot URI
   and the collection's provider. The insert refuses a pair that names no
   current spot feed of that provider.
2. *Service.* The spot feed writes its board member under its provider and
   pair, and the surface reads the member its config names. The latent
   block's anchor resolves the spot feed by the same pair, so the read and
   the correlation name one feed.
3. *Screens.* The BFF writes the two fields. The Surface tab shows the spot
   as its URI and provider. The Calibration tab of a vol feed links the spot
   feed and the two discounting curves, each with its run state.

*Part B: a provider's source control.* The UX was agreed with the owner on
2026-11-22:

- A *source strip* above the feed table: the source's name, its state, how
  many feeds it serves and how many vol surfaces it holds, and one button,
  Stop source or Start source. The header's buttons become Start feeds and
  Stop feeds, so the two levers never share a word.
- *Stored stop.* A stopped source stays stopped until someone starts it, a
  service restart included. The catalogue has no enabled flag, so the
  synthetic service stores the stopped sources itself.
- *Confirm with the effect.* Stop source opens a dialog that names what
  stops: the source's served feeds and the vol surfaces it holds, where a
  surface another source still holds keeps running. Feeds started by hand
  keep running. Start needs no confirmation.

4. *Storage.* A synthetic entity for a stopped source, by its catalogue
   name, with who stopped it and when.
5. *Service.* Stop and start verbs for one source. A stop stops the source's
   adapter and records it; a start removes the record and starts the
   adapter. The adapter fleet skips a stopped source at start up. A status
   read answers each source's state, its served feed count and its held
   surfaces.
6. *BFF.* Routes for the read and the two verbs, under the feeds run
   permission, for a system session only, with route tests and the
   refusals.
7. *Web.* The strip, the renamed buttons and the dialog, in English, French
   and Portuguese, with tests.

* Notes

** Why the spot was never named (analysis, 2026-11-22)

- *Deferred on evidence.* [[id:4825DEE0-D3E4-4E38-B46F-0C5EA838F1E0][The investigation of the forward a surface is struck against]] measured the quoted vols unchanged, to 1e-12 at all 15 points, when the spot moves from 1.0 to 1.30 with the discounts held. It recorded "The spot, later": not on the critical path. The board then carried the spot as one member per pair, =spot:<PAIR>=, with nothing in any config.
- *The hidden dependency was seen and accepted.* [[id:DDF8585F-75EE-4190-B20B-8A913DC1F883][The board task]] calls visibility "the load-bearing one": a feed that reads through the board has a dependency nothing states, which hides the graph.
- *The curves were fixed when they failed loudly.* Curves from another collection sharing a member made the surface refuse every tick, so curves were scoped by collection. A spot from the wrong feed fails silently, because the vols ignore its level.
- *Two pickers can disagree.* Every pair now has two spot feeds, in the Bloomberg and the Uniform Volatility Demo collections. The board read (=fx_vol_surface_feed.cpp=) takes whichever ticked last; the block anchor (=application.cpp=) takes the last row of the pair. The block's spot correlations can therefore tie a Bloomberg surface to the demo spot.
- *A URI alone does not name a provider's spot, by design.* Both EUR/USD spot feeds publish =oresmd://fx/EUR?type=quote&instrument=fx_spot&quote=rate&ccy=USD=. The grammar names what is observed ([[id:C3E053CA-0D4B-480B-9119-E11530160EC1][oresmd]]); a market series is keyed by the URI, the provider and the party (=market_series.hpp=), so two providers that quote one item keep two series. The pair of URI and provider is the existing address of one provider's spot, and putting the provider in the URI would break one datum, one URI.
- *The Calibration tab said "not running" for a running vol feed.* It took the run state from the presence of a start, and a surface starts from no published value. Fixed in =b56208ccb9=.

- The task comes from [[id:D9A61A85-F8E6-446C-B5D4-D5A1855DAAA5][Configure a vol surface feed on the synthetic screen]]. That task refuses a hand Start of a vol feed, because a run of its own is a second market beside the one the vendors quote.
- No control of a simulated source exists today. The BFF has only =GET /api/marketdata/adapters=, and the Serving tag on the Provider tab only reads it.
- Each adapter holds a shared surface under the name =<source_name>.<provider_code>= (=simulator_offers.hpp=). The surface stops when the last hold detaches (=shared_surfaces.hpp=).
