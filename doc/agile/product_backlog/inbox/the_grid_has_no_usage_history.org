* What

The grid read answers with the newest stored grid sample alone. The poller
writes a sample every interval, and no read returns more than the last one,
so no screen can show the grid's usage over time: a batch's progress, a
node's busy periods, or a trend in outcomes.

The operations group states this on the screen rather than building it. Its
grid journey records the gap as "No history", and the story puts the charts
over the stored telemetry aggregates out of scope, leaving them with the
capture Expose the telemetry stats surface.

* Why

An operator watching a batch wants to know whether the grid is speeding up
or falling behind, and one sample cannot say. The data is already stored:
grid samples are a TimescaleDB hypertable with a thirty-day retention, so a
read over a time range is the whole of the missing piece, and the poller
already writes more than anything reads.
