* Goal

The shell says one thing per act, and the provisioning the wizards did has no
remnant left in it. The command that brings an installation to life is named
for what it does; the generated duplicates of the porcelain provision verbs are
gone; and the synthetic organisation generator, whose only caller was a recipe,
went with its recipe.

* Acceptance

- The shell's provisioning surface is one command per act: =bootstrap setup=
  for the first run, =provision tenant= for every tenant, =provision party= for
  the party stage, and no second spelling of any of them.
- The verbs nothing calls are gone: =iam.v1.tenants.complete-provisioning=, the
  generated =tenant_provisioning= commands, and the =tenants
  complete-provisioning= porcelain command.
- The synthetic organisation generator and the recipe that drove it are gone,
  and what is left of the =synthetic= menu is the market simulator.
- The recipes and the scripts they tangle to call the commands that exist, and
  the journey documents and the manual describe the surface that exists.
- Every gate green and every affected suite passing.

* Plan

1. Rename the first-run command and move it into the bootstrap menu, where the
   verbs that answer the installation and give it an administrator already are.
2. Retire the generated =tenant_provisioning= unit and the completing verb it
   carries: the run's own completing step is what completes a tenant, and the
   standalone verb is dead weight with a defect of its own.
3. Delete the synthetic organisation generator and its recipe, and take its
   consumers -- the registrar, the shell command group, the component overview,
   the seeder note and the recipes -- with it.
4. Bring the documents up to date: the story's Status, acceptance and
   decisions, the three journey documents, the manual chapter, and the captures
   the deletions leave behind.
