* Result

Every screen a human role needs is reachable, and Acme exercises the system.

- The role seed holds the reads and writes the screens' cards and the journeys'
  actors name: Trading the synthetic and curve authoring, Operations the pricing
  engine and market-risk approval and subscriptions, Member the engine settings
  groups, with the earlier market data reads.
- The Acme profile entitles each party to its own provider and source, written
  by the subscribe step itself, so a recreate keeps the spread and every adapter
  accepts every demanded id.
- The trade population is entered by each party's own people, so a party user
  sees the party's requests. The group's treasurer carries the group's.
- The IR and FX catalogues are generated from the feeds, so a provider's
  catalogue is exactly what its feeds serve.

Open. The decision queue is empty for party personas because no party role holds
the permission to decide a trading operational authorisation; the tenant
administrator holds it and sees 52. The SuperAdmin screens stay with the platform
administrator, as their journeys say.
