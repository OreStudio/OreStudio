* What

Take the five open questions on [[id:D9D59BD1-CAC0-4B4A-B32D-A734A4D79AB0][Greek ladder reports]]
to the product owner and record the answers on that page. The questions are:

1. What is the .1 as a number: a tenth of a volatility point, ten basis
   points of rate, or a tenth of the level? The prose catalogue states the unit
   three different ways in three neighbouring paragraphs.
2. Which greeks does the family cover, and which tenors and expiries are the
   desk's buckets?
3. Which market data must the store hold before the vol ladders are honest, and
   is the FX vol surface a requirement for the first .1 report?
4. Is 1.8.17.0 the engine version the reports must run on, and may the store
   stop publishing 1.8.16.0-4-g3b62ba248?
5. Which of the family runs intraday on the live cut, and which runs only on
   the authorised cut?

* Why

The .1 family is prose only. [[id:D9D59BD1-CAC0-4B4A-B32D-A734A4D79AB0][Greek
ladder reports]] grounds it in the engine and finds that the engine has no .1
and no risk-reversal factor, so each of the five questions changes *what* gets
built rather than *how*. Answering them before seeding is cheaper than seeding
twice.

The first question blocks the rest. The shift size is a bare real in the engine,
and the same ".1" is a tenfold different report depending on whether the surface
is stored as a decimal or in volatility points.
