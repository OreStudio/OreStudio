* Result

A feed's law on the Market tab's Arrivals, Laws and Fit opens the feed's
Arrivals tab, and every feed name opens the feed's main page; Fit's name had
opened the Arrivals tab, and now opens the page as the others do.

At the user's request the feed's Arrivals tab follows the product's section
pattern: it reads the model, the sensitivity and the two Hawkes numbers as
facts, and an Edit button, offered to a caller who may write the feed, opens a
dialog that holds the fields, the running note with its restart, and Save. The
dialog lays the fields out with the product's =Field=, =Input= and =Select= in
a two-column grid, which fixes the misaligned boxes the tab had.

Evidence: web tests pass (227 in marketdata/synthetic and i18n), typecheck
clean, and the harness passes 15 of 15 live: it checks the tab has no field to
type in, edits a running feed's sensitivity through Edit and the dialog, sees the
restarted feed run on it, and saves the old value back. Screenshots of the tab
and of the dialog were checked.
