* Acceptance

- each element's members, including its option block, schedule and underlying blocks, are read into rows rather than skipped
- each of the two types names an instrument table and the catalogue routes it
- each type has a builder
- the generator books one trade of each type through its builder, and the recorded trades hold it
- the ORE engine test reads and builds each, with no error of the trade kind
- every fact the element states is a column or a row: no list, basket or schedule is held as text

* Plan

Both elements are flat, and the class-read path already reads every member
they state: scalar leaves, Underlying blocks, lists of values in a container
(=InitialPrices=, =KnockOutLevels=, =FixedTriggerLevels=), and dated and
derived schedules. They route to the scripted instrument table.

- =EuropeanOptionBarrier= states its underlyings in =OptionUnderlying= and
  =BarrierUnderlying=. The model says an element-named block's role is its
  element, so the role vocabulary gains =optionunderlying= and
  =barrierunderlying=, and the table check is regenerated from it.
- Builders: =european_option_barrier_builder=, European barrier style only, and
  =fx_worst_of_basket_swap_builder=, with dated period and pay schedules and a
  fixing schedule derived two days before each period.
- The FX worst-of pays in its floating index's currency, preferring the quote
  currency's index: its coupons depend on each pair's performance, a ratio.
- The generator books the European option barrier in the scripted family, on
  two of the tenant's equities, and the FX worst-of in the FX family.

* Result

EuropeanOptionBarrier and FxWorstOfBasketSwap are held in the scripted
instrument table. The underlyings are underlying rows under their own roles,
the level lists are parameter values, and the schedules are schedule rows,
including the derived fixing schedule. The round-trip test reads ORE's two
examples from its AMC portfolios and writes them back. Each type has a builder,
the catalogue routes both, and the generator books one trade of each. The ORE
engine builds the recorded trades with no error, and the recorded FX worst-of
trades state every path ORE's example states.

The builder does not state a European option barrier's Premiums block or an
American barrier with its schedule; the generated trades are European.
