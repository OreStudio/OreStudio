This page documents a test scenario verifying [[id:894F3A64-F564-44B2-A6F1-AB00FF30A6B9][Draw the nested collections of a curve entry]] in [[id:18A29061-FBAD-4C5A-90A1-F02039F791BF][Standardise the curve configuration's parameters on the one contract]]. It is filled in with the target screen and checklist of steps before testing starts; record the outcome by hand in =* Results=.

* Before you start

This is a readiness section for the person who prepares the environment, not for
the tester. By the time the tester opens this page, all of it must already be
true.

- The environment runs a **publish built from the branch under test**. The
  screens draw what the MESSAGE router reads, so a stale publish shows the old
  page with no warning: rebuild (=compass build=), then =compass services start=.
- The database is **freshly provisioned with Acme Corporation Holding Group**:
  =compass db status= for the state, =compass db recreate -y -k= only if stale,
  then
  =compass shell -f projects/ores.shell/scripts/library/provisioning/how_do_i_provision_the_system_with_acme_corporation_holding_group.ores=.
  The script must finish with no failed step; a tenant left in =bootstrapping=
  means the workflow published a step nothing answered, and the walk cannot
  start.
- The tenant =acme_corporation= is =active= and holds four curve configurations,
  one of them the UK one with seventeen yield curves:
  =compass sql -- -c "select status from ores_iam_tenants_tbl where code = 'acme_corporation' and valid_to = ores_utility_infinity_timestamp_fn();"=

* Steps

** Log in as the tenant administrator

- Open =http://localhost:21202/=.
- Type =tenant_admin@acme_corporation= and =Secure-Password-123=, then *Sign in*.
- Expect the dashboard: *Welcome, Tenant Admin*, working for *Acme Corporation
  Plc*, and the areas across the top (Home, Reference data, Organisation, Market
  data, Trading, Requests, Operations, Reporting, Development).
- If the shell asks *which party*, that is the next step, not a failure.

** Choose the party to work as

- If a list of parties is shown, choose *Acme Corporation Plc*.
- Expect the dashboard to settle with the same areas across the top.
- Expect no error notice. A refused party is a failure: the walk cannot continue.

** Open the UK curve configuration

- Click *Reporting*, then *Curve configurations*.
- Expect a list of four: Group, HK, UK and US curve configuration.
- Click *UK curve configuration*.
- Expect the page title *UK curve configuration*, the tabs *Details*, *Curves*,
  *Used by*, *History*, and the line under the title reading *Version 1 · 221 of
  238 parameters carry documentation*.

** Read the segments of a curve

- Click the *Curves* tab.
- Expect the rail on the left: *YieldCurves* holding 17 curves, and one line
  *18 sections hold no curve in this document*.
- Expect *GBP1D* to be selected, and the workbench on the right to show its
  header, *The curve*, then *Its parameters*.
- Scroll to the card titled *Its Segments*.
- Expect the card to say *1 row*, and the row to name its kind: *Simple*.
- Expect the row to list its leaves, each with a value or a default and a
  *Documentation* link: *Conventions* with =GBP-OIS-CONVENTIONS=, and
  *Projection curve* reading *Default: Not given. ORE applies its own rule.*
- A snag is a segment leaf that prints an ORE name such as =Conventions= or
  =RecordedAt= where a label belongs, or a leaf that belongs to another kind.

** Confirm the segment's quotes

- Still on the *Its Segments* card, below the leaves.
- Expect the line *12 quotes*.
- Expect twelve identifiers, in tenor order, all of the form
  =IR_SWAP/RATE/GBP/0D/1D/…=: 1M, 3M, 6M, 12M, 2Y, 3Y, 5Y, 7Y, 10Y, 15Y, 20Y,
  30Y.
- A snag is a quote listed under the wrong segment, a quote missing, or the list
  ordered by anything other than tenor.

** Confirm what the page does not draw

- At the foot of the workbench, under the parameters and the segments.
- Expect the line *Held as its own element:* with *Report · not stated* and
  *BootstrapConfig · not recorded here*.
- Expect *Segments* **not** to appear in that line: the page draws the segments as
  rows, so naming the block again would say it twice.

** Read the Details tab

- Click *Details*.
- Expect the name, the version, the party that holds the configuration, the count
  of curves, and the documentation count.
- Expect a sentence saying what the configuration decides (each curve's
  definition and bootstrap) and what it does not (the market a run reads, nor the
  trade data).

** Read the reports the configuration serves

- Click *Used by*.
- Expect a table of the reports bound to this configuration, each with its party
  (Audit Trail Summary, Bucketed DV01, Cashflows, Credit Curves, CVA/DVA/FV…
  under *ACME Corporation UK plc*).
- Expect the sentence that a change reaches the next run of each report, and that
  a run already produced keeps what it ran with.

** Read the history of the configuration

- Click *History*.
- Expect the newest version first, dated, with *Created* and its reason code
  (=system.external_data_import= for a freshly provisioned configuration).
- Expect a *Hide system activity* control.
- A snag is an empty history, or a timeline that shows no version at all: the
  curve configuration's history provider has to be registered for the page to
  draw it.

** Check the known snags

These are the defects already recorded. Meeting one is expected; the step is to
confirm the *current* behaviour and say whether it is what the note describes.

- *A segment leaf prints its ORE name.* =Conventions= draws as the word
  =Conventions= with no label and counts as a gap, because the contract holds no
  =Segments/\{Kind\}/Conventions= row: the leaf is read by the segment base class
  and the extractor loses a class's base when it is declared in a header and
  defined in a source (task 3D1208F2). Expected today: *1 without documentation*
  on the =GBP1D= header.
- *The entry's header counts only its own parameters.* The tag reads *10
  parameters · 1 without documentation* while the page reads *221 of 238*: the
  segment's leaves are counted on the page and not in the curve's tag.
- *The instant a row was recorded.* Fixed on this branch; worth confirming it is
  gone. A row reading =RecordedAt= with a timestamp is the old behaviour.
