* Goal

The site states a Content-Security-Policy on every page it serves, so a script
that reaches a page without being part of the bundle cannot run, and a page
cannot load from or talk to a host the policy does not name. The policy is the
BFF's, so it holds on any host the service runs on, not only behind the proxy.

* Acceptance

- Every response that carries a page states a =Content-Security-Policy= whose
  =default-src= is ='self'=, and which names no host outside the environment.
- The bundle runs under the policy with no =unsafe-inline= for scripts: the
  theme script =index.html= inlines is allowed by the hash the build writes,
  taken from the final page rather than from the source.
- =style-src= allows what the bundle uses, which today includes inline styles
  from the component library.
- =connect-src= reaches the BFF and the NATS monitor the shell reads, and
  nothing else.
- =object-src 'none'=, =base-uri 'none'= and =frame-ancestors 'none'= are
  stated. The proxy keeps =X-Frame-Options= until the policy is enforced, and
  the policy is stated as a header rather than a meta tag, where
  =frame-ancestors= and the report-only mode are ignored.
- The policy is stricter in a built site than under the development server,
  which needs inline scripts and a websocket, and no test relies on the
  development policy.
- A violation is visible: the policy is served in report-only mode for one
  release before it is enforced, with a =report-to= endpoint that accepts the
  reports, and the reports are read.

* Decisions

- *The BFF states the policy, not the proxy.* Caddy is one deployment of the
  site; a policy written in =build/scripts/expose-web.sh= would be absent from
  every other host and from the development server.
- *Hashes before nonces.* The page has one inline script and it is the same one
  on every load, so its hash is fixed at build time and needs no per-request
  plumbing. A nonce would make every response uncacheable.
- *Report-only first.* A policy written against a bundle that grows can break a
  screen nobody tested; one release of =Content-Security-Policy-Report-Only=
  with a report path settles the rules before they are enforced.

* Out of scope

- The CSP of the QA route, which already states =default-src 'none'; sandbox=.
- Subresource integrity for anything the site does not serve itself.
- A reporting collector; the first release may log the reports instead.
