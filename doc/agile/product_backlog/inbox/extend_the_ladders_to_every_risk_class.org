This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Build the same ladder report over the risk classes the .1 work does not reach:
swaption volatility, cap and floor volatility, yield volatility, equity spot and
volatility, credit spread, CDS volatility, base correlation, security spread,
commodity curve and volatility, dividend yield, correlation, bond future
volatility and intraday power.

Each is one sensitivity block of =SensitivityAnalysis= over a different kind of
market data. Rows 11 to 26 of the family table on
[[id:D9D59BD1-CAC0-4B4A-B32D-A734A4D79AB0][Greek ladder reports]] name the
block, the factor key and the bucket columns for each.

* Why

The family the product owner asked for reads as a volatility family, and the
engine is much wider: the sensitivity schema carries a block for each of these
risk classes, and the report the store builds for one of them is the report for
all of them, parameterised by the document it binds.

The work waits on market data, not on code. The ACME seed holds interest rate
swap curve quotes only, and the schema's volatility configuration tables —
swaption, cap and floor, CDS, equity, commodity, inflation cap and floor, yield —
exist with nothing to describe. So this capture is a queue behind
[[id:3D2A2BD4-4126-4E54-9046-A9E00966D898][Support volatility surfaces]] and
behind whatever brings credit and equity data in.
