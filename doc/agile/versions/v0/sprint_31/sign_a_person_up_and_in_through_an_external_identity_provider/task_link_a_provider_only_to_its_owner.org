* Goal

Make the link between a provider account and an ORE account safe. A person links a provider account after proving their ORE account with their password; a provider account never links to an ORE account silently because the two addresses match; a subject ORE has never seen registers instead of linking; and a person removes a link without losing their last way in. The refusals say which rule stopped them, and they are the same through the API and the screen.

* Acceptance

- Linking needs a proven session, and a provider account whose address matches an existing account still links nothing until that account proves its password.
- A registered account keeps a password or another way in, so removing its only link never locks the person out.
- An unknown subject registers, it does not attach to the account that happens to hold the same address.
- Each refusal is the same shape through the gateway and the screen, and the suites cover each one.
- Unlinking removes the link, records it as the person's act, and leaves the account's other credentials as they were.
