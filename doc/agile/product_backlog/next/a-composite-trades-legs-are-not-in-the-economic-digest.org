#+title: A composite trade's legs are not in the economic digest
#+description: The economic digest of a composite trade folds only the composite instrument row, which declares no economic field, so a change to a leg leaves the digest alone.
#+type: capture
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :capture:
#+created: 2026-10-11
#+updated: 2026-10-11

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The catalogue lists =composite_instrument= as the only component of the composite family, and its model declares every column =:economic: false=. The family's economics sit in =composite_leg=, which no reader in the digest shape reads.

Declare =composite_leg= economic, give it a reader by trade id, and list it under =composite_instrument= in =* Digest/Instrument components=. Then tighten the catalogue loader so that a listed component with no =:economic: true= column is refused.

* Why

A composite trade's digest is constant, so an amendment to any of its legs does not move the external version. The loader accepts the listing today because refusing it would change what the digest covers.
