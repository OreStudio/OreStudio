#+created: 2026-10-25
#+updated: 2026-10-25

One term of one convention kind, as a screen draws it: the entity and the column
its value travels under, and the leaf of ORE's conventions document that holds it,
named by its schema and its XPath. The XPath is the key of the term's entry in the
ORE parameter contract, which gives the term its label, help, description, default,
format and value set, so none of them is restated here or in a screen.

A kind's terms are what a convention screen shows, in the order the position
states. The first rows come from the mapper that reads and writes the conventions
document, so the column a value is stored in and the element ORE reads it from are
the ones the import and the export already use.
