* Acceptance

- The list and the count of observations of a series include the ticks of the tick table.
- The paging, the order and the filters give the same result on both tables, and a test states each.
- A tick has no id or source in the tick table, and the list says what it shows in their place.

* Plan

Abandoned on 2026-10-23. The goal asked the list of observations to return ticks, with the
paging, order and filters of both tables. A tick has no id and no source, and it has no
correction. A merged list gives it a made-up id, and the count over the tick table costs
more than a page is worth. The reads are separate instead. The task "Read the ticks of a
series with one history call" adds the tick read, and the list of observations stays a list
of observations. See [[id:10E479D2-990E-4627-95A2-1DA0FA5D68D5][A chart reads a series with one history call and one live subscription]].
