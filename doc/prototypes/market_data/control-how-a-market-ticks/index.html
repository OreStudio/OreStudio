Every live feed, ranked by how hard it follows the market. A row is read here; its clustering is edited in that feed's own editor, one row at a time, which is what A is for.

Global the raster is the only figure that shows what a market-wide factor means: rows densify together, and a burst in one row lifts the others.
Boundary it sets the market and reads the feeds; it edits no feed's clustering, so the two views never do the same job.

Both rasters are drawn from the same model the service computes, with a fixed seed: a shared heat every event feeds and that fades, each feed's intensity scaled by the market state and by its own sensitivity, and its own Hawkes excess on top. Local shows one row; global shows them all.
