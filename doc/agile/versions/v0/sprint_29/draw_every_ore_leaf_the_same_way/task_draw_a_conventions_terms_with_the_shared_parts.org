* Goal

Give a convention a record page whose terms follow the ORE element and leaf screen standard, with the words the owner chose on 2026-10-26: the one Simple and Advanced switch named for a screen of leaves, the documentation under each term from the contract, the value in force, the filters with the noun /terms/, and the kind card from the kind's data. * Acceptance

- A convention in the documents list opens its record page, which shows its kind and every term of its kind.
- Each term shows one label from the contract, or its ORE name with the undocumented note; the value in force; the words a closed list gives a value; and the documentation under it, in Simple or Advanced.
- The switch says *How much to show*, the section button says *Show all documentation*, the count names the terms, and no filter is drawn that the data cannot answer.
- The words come from the =oreParameter= namespace in every language, and the standard records the owner's decisions and the page's conformance line.

* Plan

1. Record the owner's four word decisions in the standard.
2. Serve =GET /api/conventions/:family/documents/:id=: the convention, its kind, and each term with its contract entry by schema and XPath and its value set by the entry's scope and name.
3. Move the value set reader from the pricing route into a shared module, unchanged, so both routes read the sets one way.
4. Give the shared switch the words of a screen of leaves, the documentation button an owner for its state, and the filters the kind's noun and an optional numbers filter.
5. Build the page from those parts, and open it from the documents list.

* Result

A convention now opens on a record page that draws its kind and its terms with the shared leaf parts and the words the owner chose.

- =GET /api/conventions/:family/documents/:id= answers the convention, its kind from the kind's row, and each term with its contract entry, by schema and XPath, and its value set. The value set reader moved unchanged into =ore-value-sets.ts=, which the pricing route now reads too; the pricing tests pass unchanged.
- A convention is named by its id within a party, and a session reads several parties' conventions, so the list opens a record with its party and the route picks that party's row; a read with no party is refused when more than one party holds the id.
- The page draws one label per term, the value in force, the words of a closed list's value, the shared documentation panel in Simple or Advanced, *Show all documentation*, the filter count in terms, and *How much to show* on the switch; *Changes the numbers* is not drawn, because the data cannot answer it.
- The standard records the owner's four word decisions and the page's conformance line; the shared filter takes the kind's noun, so product pricing's count says parameters.

On a fleet built from this branch and an Acme tenant provisioned from a recreated database, the families route served each kind's asset class and holder, and the record route answered =AED-EIBOR-3M-SWAP= for a named party with its eight terms documented and their sets drawn: frequencies from ORE's list of 18, business day conventions and day counters from their catalogues. Unnamed, it was refused, because four parties hold that id. A tenor basis swap answered with its parts laid over the central row and its long and short terms marked undocumented. The page was not driven in a browser. The local gate passes the web check; =prototypes= fails on a market data prototype main left unlisted. The currency pair and tenor lists answer no count, which this task did not change.
