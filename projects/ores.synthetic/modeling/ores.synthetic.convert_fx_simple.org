#+updated: 2026-10-12

The mixture an FX feed's engine reads for the simple view, and back.

The simple view asks how far the price drifts and how far it moves in an hour,
and how often it jumps; or, for the mean-reverting engine, how long the price
takes to come half way back. The advanced view is the mixture itself: a mean,
a deviation and a weight for each component. Every figure of the simple view is
per hour, so it does not depend on the feed's rate. The service turns the one
into the other, beside the engines it describes. Nothing is stored.
