* What

ORE's =BMABasisSwapConvention::fromXML= reads the lockout as
=OvernightLockouDays= (=OREData/ored/configuration/conventions.cpp:921= at ORE
=3d75a6908=), and its =toXML= writes =OvernightLockoutDays= (line 951). The
=ores.ore= mapper reads and writes =OvernightLockoutDays=
(=projects/ores.ore/core/src/domain/conventions_family_bma_basis_swap.cpp=).

So a conventions document ORE Studio exports carries a lockout that ORE does not
read, and the BMA basis swap runs with no lockout. A document written with the
parser's spelling imports without its lockout. Write the spelling ORE reads, read
both on import, or report the misspelling to ORE.

* Why

The lockout changes the numbers a BMA basis swap curve is built with, and the
loss is silent in both directions. Found building the convention kind terms, where
the term records the spelling ORE reads as a stated exception.
