* Result

The framework is specified as a regulator over an agent, and what the earlier
documents stipulated is now derived.

Scope follows from three results rather than from convention. The good regulator
theorem requires a regulator to model what it regulates and, through its
simplicity clause, forbids modelling anything else. Requisite variety bounds
what must be supplied to the residue the agent does not already absorb. The
black box covers the rest. The rule that falls out settles which targets the
framework models without appealing to whether a subject is a file, which is why
a branch is modelled and a compiler is not.

The seven classes are derived from the primitives of a control loop, so a
candidate eighth has a test rather than a preference. Declaration is the goal,
recording the sensor, execution the effector, loading the model the theorem
requires, citation and sequencing constrain choice and order, and convening
amplifies variety.

Terminology now agrees with what it borrows from. The session role is a System,
which is Beer's own term; the seven classes are regulatory functions. Neither
collides with the other senses the corpus carries, and where a word does carry
two referents the document says which is meant.

Upstream is classified rather than forked. All 52 pstack skills carry a
regulatory function and a disposition. Forking was rejected because it severs
the second loop: a copy stops tracking the moment upstream improves, and a stale
fork fails silently where a broken reference fails loudly.

Two things were added that the story did not ask for and the work showed were
needed. A writing standard for argumentative prose, since the register problem
recurred across every page. And two lint checks, since two rules the framework
states had nothing enforcing them, and each was found by fixing the same defect
twice.

Not done, and left to the story's other tasks. No skill is renamed, no
dispatcher is built, and the catalogue is not attenuated by level. No
agent-behaviour evaluation was run, so nothing here claims to change how agents
act; the specification records that under its own Limitations.

One measurement worth carrying forward: nine distinct defects traced to a single
mechanical rename, of which grep found four and reading found five. The last was
found by a reviewer.
