* Goal

One verb, =compass env status=, that gathers this checkout's environment in one
place and emits it as JSON on request. It reports the environment identity from
=.env=, the database's restore time with its age, schema version and drift
against HEAD, and every service unit with its state. =compass bearings= stops
composing the same block inline and reads the verb instead, so the
machine-readable contract and the human orientation come from one
implementation.

* Acceptance

- =compass env status= prints the environment block bearings prints today:
  preset, label, =.env= version, scope, database restore time with its age and
  drift, and the service counts.
- =compass env status --json= prints one JSON object carrying the identity, the
  database, and every service unit, and that object is the shape the plugin's
  =CONTRACT.md= names.
- A unit systemd reports as failed is counted and reported as failed, and is no
  longer folded into stopped.
- =compass bearings= prints the values it printed before for this checkout, and
  its environment section comes from the shared gather.
- The JSON output carries no colour codes and no terminal-only prose.
- Tests cover the unit classification including failed and missing, the JSON
  shape, and the human render.

* Plan

The work splits into one gather, two renderers, and a caller.

- =compass_services.py= gains one per-unit gather that classifies a unit and
  returns its detail. =cmd_status= and =gather_counts= both read it, so the
  table, the counts line, and the JSON cannot classify the same unit
  differently. The classification gains =failed= as a fifth state: systemd's
  =ActiveState= already reports it, and folding it into =stopped= hid a crashed
  unit behind a deliberate shutdown.
- A new =compass_env_status.py= composes the identity, the database, and the
  services into one dict. It owns no policy: =compass_db.database_info= and
  =schema_drift= answer for the database, and the service gather answers for the
  fleet.
- =compass env status= renders that dict as the human block, and =--json= prints
  it. The human render is the text bearings prints today, moved rather than
  rewritten, so orientation output does not change.
- =compass bearings= calls the renderer instead of composing the block inline.
  Its genesis-environment and missing-=.env= paths stay where they are, because
  they describe the absence of an environment rather than its state.

* Result

=compass env status= reports one checkout's environment as text, and
=--json= reports it as one object for the DSH environment plugin to read.
=compass bearings= prints its environment section from the same gather, so
orientation, the verb, and the plugin cannot disagree.

The service state vocabulary gained =failed=, and it is now the fifth state
rather than a shade of =stopped=. =compass services status= prints it in the
counts line and marks the row =failed=, and a unit the manager has never
heard of is =missing= on its own. The counts line reports the 24 service
units and nats-server on a line of its own, where before nats was folded
into the totals and the JSON counted without it.

Two smaller corrections came out of the work. =compass db status= and the
new verb now read their thresholds from the same constants, so a restore
past 24 hours and a connection budget past 85 percent cannot be a warning
in one place and a failure in another. And =gather= adopts the checkout's
systemd transport itself: without that a caller that did not go through
=compass services run= read every unit as =missing= rather than =stopped=,
which is the same symptom an earlier capture records for a sandboxed
=compass services status=.
