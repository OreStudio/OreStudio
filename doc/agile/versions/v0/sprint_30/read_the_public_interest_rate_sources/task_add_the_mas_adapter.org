* Goal

SORA is stored as fixings in the system tenant, with its history, read from the domestic interest rates page of the Monetary Authority of Singapore, which needs no key. The MAS API needs a Singapore Corppass login, so the adapter posts the page's download form back to it. The adapter is specialised: it reads the Monetary Authority of Singapore alone, and it supplies only its fetcher to the shared half of the official adapter.

* Acceptance

- The adapter reads the Monetary Authority of Singapore alone, and it does not copy the announce, the demand, the backfill or the publish code.
- The Monetary Authority of Singapore is a supplier in the catalogue, its series maps to the SGD-SORA item, and a session and a feed scoped to that item are seeded in the system tenant.
- A fresh environment stores the history as fixings within minutes of provisioning, and the log names the source and the count it stored.
- Each fixing is the decimal of the published percent, with the credit the notice of the MAS Terms of Use of Datasets, with the dataset and its date of access.
- The depth of the history is measured and written into the calibration sources note.
- Unit tests parse a recorded response, and a malformed response gives an error, not a fixing.

* Result

The adapter reads SORA from the domestic interest rates page of the Monetary
Authority of Singapore. The MAS API needs a Singapore Corppass login, so the
fetcher does what the page's Download button does: a GET of the page for its
ASP.NET hidden fields, then a POST of the form with a range of months and the
SORA column. The answer is CSV, which states a row's year and month only when
they change and repeats its header above each year. The page needs no key
and no cookie. The HTTP transport gained =post= for this; a transport that
only reads refuses it, so the test transports of every other fetcher are
unchanged.

The first fresh run failed on the header of the second year, because a first
read covers 400 days. The parser now reads past it, and a test pins it. On a
fresh environment the service then sent 275 fixings about 50 seconds after it
started. Each is the decimal of the published percent, with the credit the
MAS Open Data Licence asks for, naming the dataset and its source. The date
of access is not part of the credit, since a provider holds one credit for
every read. The route serves SORA from 2005-07-01; the calibration sources
note records it. The system and Acme provisioned to completion.
