This page documents a [[id:0820B7FD-147C-4832-AC25-C043D38D5B61][sprint]] (*Sprint 28*) of ORE Studio. It captures the sprint's mission, current status, and the stories that compose it. For the surrounding context — version goals, sprint order, and product identity — see [[id:E6FD30ED-963E-4705-B414-91BF471C23D0][Version 0]].

* Mission

Finish the work carried out of sprint 27 — the record screens, the remaining
journeys, and the rest of its remainders — before drawing anything new from the
product backlog.

Sprint 28 opens with 41 stories carried from sprint 27, across nine themes: the
record screens and the web UX standard, the journeys in ores.web, four-eyes
approvals, market-data feeds and bindings, security and tenancy, the grid and
telemetry, reporting and ACME data, trading-model integrity, and codegen and
data-quality debt. The sprint commits to finishing those stories rather than
adding scope. Sprint 27's retrospective asked for fewer stories in flight, so
nothing new is pulled from the product backlog this sprint.
