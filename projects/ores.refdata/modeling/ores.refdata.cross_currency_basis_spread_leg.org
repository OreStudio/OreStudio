The leg of a cross-currency basis swap that pays its index plus the basis
spread, as ORE's =SpreadIndex= with the =Spread*= fixing rules: the tenor, the
payment lag, whether the spread is included in the coupon, the lookback, the
fixing days, the rate cutoff, whether coupons are averaged, and whether the
observation is shifted. They are a part because each states how this one leg
builds its coupon; the flat leg beside it states its own. Each column keeps the
=spread_= prefix of ORE's element, for the reason the flat leg's columns do.
