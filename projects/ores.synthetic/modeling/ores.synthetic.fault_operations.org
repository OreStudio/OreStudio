A simulator author sets the faults of the adapter =ore-simulated= with one request. The request
replaces the whole plan, so a request with every field off turns every fault off. The faults are
off by default and a restart of the service turns them off.

Each fault goes through the adapter contract and is seen as a state of the adapter or as a drop
reason of a tick. A lost connection silences the heartbeat and the demand reply, and the service
makes the adapter stale. A delay beyond the age limit makes the service drop each tick as stale. A
repeated tick makes the service drop the repeat as out of order. An out of range value makes the
service drop each tick as out of range. A not entitled rejection makes the adapter reject every id
of a demand set as not_entitled.

Only a session of the system tenant that may run feeds sets the faults.

*** connection_lost
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: bool
:default: false
:END:

The adapter sends no heartbeat and answers no demand, as a connection that dropped.

*** tick_delay_ms
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::uint32_t
:default: 0
:END:

How many milliseconds older than the moment of the tick each vendor time is. A delay beyond the
age limit of the service makes the tick stale.

*** repeat_ticks
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: bool
:default: false
:END:

Each generator sends the tick before the new one again, with its older vendor time.

*** out_of_range
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: bool
:default: false
:END:

Each value is larger than the service accepts.

*** not_entitled
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: bool
:default: false
:END:

The adapter rejects every id of a demand set as not_entitled and runs no generator.
