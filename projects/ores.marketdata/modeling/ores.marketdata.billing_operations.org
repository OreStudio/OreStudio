#+title: ores.marketdata.billing_operations
#+description: Codegen operation model for the bills of the market data sources: the request for the bill of a period and the views of the source bill and the party charges.
#+type: ores.codegen.operation
#+component: marketdata
#+subcomponent: api
#+entity_singular: billing_operation
#+namespace: ores::marketdata::messaging
#+brief: The request for the bills of a period, and the bills.
#+filetags: :model:operation:marketdata:
#+created: 2026-10-23
#+updated: 2026-10-23

A bill is computed from the usage events of a period and the rate card of each source, so it is not stored.
The request names a period and may name one source. The answer holds the bill of each source, the charge of
each party for each component, and the part of the bill that no party bears. Every amount is a whole number
of millionths of the currency.

A session of the system tenant reads every bill. A session of any other tenant reads the charges of its own
tenant only.
