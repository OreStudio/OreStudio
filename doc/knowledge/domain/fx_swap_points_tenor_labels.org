#+title: Short-date swap points run between their own two value dates
#+description: Overnight, tom-next and spot-next are one-day swaps, so their points differ from forward points measured from spot.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

A swap's points are the difference between its far and near outrights. A spot-start swap therefore has the forward points of its tenor, and the two terms are often used for each other. The short dates differ, because each one is a swap between its own two value dates.

* Detail

| Tenor | Near date | Far date |
|-------+-----------+----------|
| Overnight (O/N) | Today | Tomorrow |
| Tom-next (T/N) | Tomorrow | Spot, for a T+2 pair |
| Spot-next (S/N) | Spot | One business day after spot |

For outright deals the same dates are named /value today/, /value tomorrow/ and /value spot-next/. Later tenors run from spot, so 1M is one month after spot.

Because each short date is a one-day swap, their points chain back from spot. [[id:85526DCE-31B2-4D46-BA3F-564876397BE2][Valuation spot date and overnight]] applies this to outrights before spot. A consumer that shows both columns must state which pair of dates a row uses.
