Describes how ORE builds the two legs of a tenor basis swap, where each leg
pays a different tenor off its own index and the two legs may differ in more
than the tenor. Corresponds to the <TenorBasisSwap> element in ORE
conventions.xml.

ORE lets a tenor basis swap be written two ways, and the corpus uses both. The
paying and receiving form names =PayIndex= and =ReceiveIndex= with an optional
frequency each, and adds the averaging and sub-periods fields. The long and
short form names =LongIndex= and =ShortIndex= with an optional payment tenor
each, and adds the spread flags. The corpus also writes =IncludeSpread= and
=SubPeriodsCouponType= alongside both forms, so each part declares them and the
shared central row carries neither. Apart from those two fields the two forms
share nothing, so the family carries one part for each form and a convention
fills exactly one.

The family's central row is the shared [[id:9477A085-0822-485F-BDF7-3C8F4A306E93][convention]], which names this kind
with =kind_code= =TENOR_BASIS_SWAP= and holds the ORE document id. Everything
kind-specific is in the two parts.
