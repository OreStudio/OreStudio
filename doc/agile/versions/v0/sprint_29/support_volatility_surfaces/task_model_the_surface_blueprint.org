* Goal

A party can record how one of its FX volatility surfaces is built: the pair, the
smile model, the interpolation across expiries, the output grid and the output
series, and the pillars the calibration reads, each naming one market series per
contributor and that contributor's weight. It is the surface's counterpart of the
IR curve bootstrap config and its pillars.

* Acceptance

- =fx_vol_surface_blueprint= in =ores.refdata=: party, name, base and quote
  currency, smile model (=ssvi=, =sabr=, =spline=), expiry interpolation, the output
  grid's expiries and strikes, and the output series, a soft reference to an
  =fx_vol_surface= =market_series= the party holds, chosen and not minted, as the
  IR config's output series is.
- =fx_vol_surface_pillar=: one expiry and strike label of a blueprint, =ATM=, a
  risk reversal or butterfly at a delta, or a call or put at a delta.
- =fx_vol_surface_pillar_source=: one contributor's market series for a pillar and
  the weight it carries, the series a soft reference to a quote series.
- What one row can show is checked on write: a pillar's strike label is ATM or a
  risk reversal, butterfly, call or put at a delta, a source's weight is positive,
  and a blueprint's pair is two different currencies. Whether each expiry holds the
  pillars its smile model needs spans rows, so it is checked before a calibration
  reads the blueprint, which is the calibration task's.
- Generated through codegen across SQL, domain, repository, protocol, shell and
  TypeScript, with the database round trip tested.

* Plan

Follow the IR curve bootstrap config and pillar: model the three entities in
=ores.refdata=, generate them, and add the recipe's checks. The calibration that
reads a blueprint, the surface reads and any screen are later tasks.

* Result

The three entities are modelled in =ores.refdata= and generated across SQL, domain,
repository, protocol, shell and TypeScript. The blueprint keeps its output series as
the sole natural key and its name unique per party. A pillar is keyed by its
blueprint, expiry and strike label, and a source by its pillar and series. The
checks on write hold the strike label grammar, a positive weight and two different
currencies. The tables, permissions, roles, handlers, event mappings and shell
commands are registered, and the list requests are regenerated.
