* Result

The credit family states its row. The planner builds the row each type routes to
and the writer persists it, which leaves the writer's dispatch with one bag arm:
the bond security's.

The population is unchanged: five rows, one for each of the five products the
seeded population draws, every one of them referencing a legal entity by LEI of
twenty characters, and 127 trades in total.

The family had no planner test, because it had no planner until the population
work gave it one; the flip adds the first. It asserts one row per slot, the type
that routes it, and the LEI reference, so the family that motivated the legal
entity model is now covered where it is drawn rather than only in a provisioning
run.
