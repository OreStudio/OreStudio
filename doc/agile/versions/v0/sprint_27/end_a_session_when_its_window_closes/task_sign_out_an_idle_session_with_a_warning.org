* Goal

Idle sign-out works where it is wanted and nowhere else. Input, not requests, counts as activity. A warning gives the person a chance to stay. An exempt role is never signed out for being idle.

* Acceptance

- Keyboard, pointer and touch input reset the idle timer; the polling and the heartbeat do not.
- A warning appears before the sign-out and one input cancels it.
- A person whose role is exempt is never signed out for being idle, even with the policy on.
- The sign-out says it happened because the session was idle.
