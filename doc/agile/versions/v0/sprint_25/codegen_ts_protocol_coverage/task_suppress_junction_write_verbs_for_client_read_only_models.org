* Result

A junction model declares =:client_read_only:= beside the repository's
=:read_only:=, and the loader derives =wire_write_enabled= from the two.
The five messaging templates and the derived TypeScript list branch on
that one flag; the repository keeps its own =:read_only:= guard, so the
two switches are independent.

=ores.refdata.calendar_date= declares =:client_read_only:=. Its client
save and delete are gone from the protocol, the service, the handler,
the sub-registrar and the TypeScript twin, and its materialisation
service still writes through the repository. =-tenor_convention_
resolution= keeps =:read_only:= and renders no write verb anywhere. Tests
pin both flags and their independence.
