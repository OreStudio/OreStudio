* Acceptance

- A kind declares its subject, its compute, its checks and its approval kinds in one place, and no step of the raise, the decide, the apply, the supersession or the notice branches on the kind.
- All three kinds publish through the one path, and the curve's published behaviour is unchanged, proved by a test against the behaviour the delivered tree has.
- An FX forward publish supersedes the set it replaces and writes its notice, as every kind does.
- The three publish implementations are deleted and the lifecycle exists once.
- The get_series_slice subject is served, and no declared subject is left unreachable.

* Decisions

- *A route names the class its feed serves, not the class its provider maps.* A
  provider may serve several classes through different feeds, and a feed's scopes
  are what the store enforces. The route's class is the catalogue's, kept to the
  feed's scope when the feed states one.
- *A smokehouse tenant is the end-to-end proof, not a demonstration tenant.* One
  party, three books, three trades and six report documents provision in 212 s and
  price in one run, where ACME takes a quarter of an hour. A gate can afford it.
