** (Known gap) No screen can book a draft

The completion's draft path, where a leg is taken live and raises its request,
cannot be reached from a screen today: =Book a trade= books live, so the legs a
tester can make are live ones. That path is covered by the service's own cases
(=a_draft_that_is_not_executed_raises_no_authorisation_request= and the
completion cases in =ores.trading.core.tests=), not by this scenario. A screen
that books a draft is the missing piece, and it is not in this branch.
