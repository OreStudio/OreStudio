This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

A multivariate Hawkes over the feeds: a kernel matrix whose entry (i, j) says
how much a tick in feed j raises feed i's intensity, and how fast that fades.
A burst in EUR/USD then lifts GBP/USD more than it lifts SOFR. Stability is a
spectral radius: the largest eigenvalue of the mean kernel must be below one.
The matrix is too large to write by hand for a hundred feeds, so it is built
from groups (asset class, currency, provider) with a within-group and a
between-group weight.

* Why

Gemini's review drew a cross-asset excitation matrix. Today every feed shares
one heat, which is the rank-one special case: a tick anywhere lifts every feed
by the same share. That makes the market move together but cannot make a
currency's pairs move together more than the rest.

Option 3 of the regime capture named this and left it for later; options 1 and
2 are now built. It is distinct from the bivariate activity and volatility
capture, which couples two coordinates of one market, and from cross-rate
correlation, which couples prices rather than arrival times.
