* Acceptance

- A lookup holds the draft reasons and the party responsible for each.
- The booking request carries the reason for a draft_capture.
- A leg of a new structure can name its structure.
- A draft report lists age and reason.

The third line needs a structure that does not exist yet when the leg is booked,
which is what [[id:F6A6BAA9-5F73-4D39-9FA7-04517576AD7E][Complete a structure in one unit]] builds. The
fourth is a report, and [[id:025EA107-987D-47B2-BE0D-BA250EE22994][Report the drafts and who is late]] carries it.

* Plan

Three pieces: the lookup, the trade's own column, and the booking's rule.

The lookup is a simple one, in the shape the trading component's other closed sets
take: a code, a description and the desk that owns it. The desk is what makes an
aged draft somebody's work rather than nobody's.

The reason lives on the trade, not on its state: a report lists drafts, and the
trade is the row it lists. The booking request carries it because only the
booking knows whether the trade it makes is a draft.

The rule is then a pair the service answers. A =draft_capture= that states no
reason is refused, because a draft nobody can explain is a deal nobody can pick
up. A booking that goes live and states a reason is refused too, because a
concluded deal waits on nothing.
