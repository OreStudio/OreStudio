#+title: The Acme provision leaves warnings that retry cannot fix
#+description: A full Acme provision on pauli left five steps with warnings: one is a retry gap, one is a missing fan-out of derived series, and two are gaps in the starting point's data.
#+type: capture
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :provisioning:market-data:workflow:
#+created: 2026-11-21
#+updated: 2026-11-21

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

A full Acme provision on pauli left 5 of 17 steps with warnings. The
retry logic and the service dependencies cover the wait for quotes, and
that part worked. They do not cover three of the five failures below.
Two are gaps in the data the starting point carries, so no amount of
waiting closes them.

The run was `bootstrap setup super_admin ... --profile acme_demo` from
the shell against the remote environment, on branch
=feature/package-the-pooler-for-pauli=, image tag =0.0.30-c5a68a85a9=,
against a freshly recreated database on pauli. The tenant ended
=active= with 6 parties, 98 accounts, 927 trades and 486,540 calendar
days.

The five steps: =attach_photos=, =publish_party_curves=,
=give_party_surface_blueprints=, =publish_party_surfaces=,
=generate_trades=.

** The curve deferral is decided once and never re-examined

Every party holds *20 of 35* curves. The 15 missing ones are the LIBOR
curves that discount off GBP-SONIA, USD-SOFR-FOMC, EUR-ESTR, JPY-TONA
and CHF-SARON.

The log shows the dependency arriving just after the step gave up:

#+begin_src text
18:01:52.67  Deferred bootstrap config ...: curve 'GBP-LIBOR-3M'
            discounts off 'GBP-SONIA', which has published nothing yet
18:01:55.33  Declared shape of series ...curve_id=GBP-SONIA: 1 axes,
            7 new values
18:01:55.94  Step answers the engine: failed: 60 party curve(s) were
            not published
#+end_src

All five discount curves are published for every party now, so the data
is there. The wait for quotes retries ("asking again in 10s"); the wait
for another *curve* does not. The deferral is computed once, and the
step reports failure on that same pass.

*Fix*: publish the deferred set in a loop while it shrinks, then report.
The step already promises that a rerun is safe, which is the same
contract.

** The derived series never reach a tenant party

=give_party_surface_blueprints= asked for 35 surfaces per party and got
none: every party holds *0 of 35*. This one is not a timing problem.

The resolver requires each party to hold its own =derived= fx_option
=rate_lnvol= series. Its own test states the rule:
=a party's surface blueprint reads its own delivered volatility quotes=
in =projects/ores.marketdata/core/tests/messaging_party_surface_blueprints_tests.cpp=.

What the database holds:

| Party                   | bloomberg | ice | tradeweb | derived |
|-------------------------+-----------+-----+----------+---------|
| Acme Corporation Plc    |        63 |  63 |       63 |       0 |
| ACME Corporation UK plc |        63 |  63 |       63 |       0 |
| ACME Corporation US Inc |        63 |  63 |       63 |       0 |
| ACME Corporation HK Ltd |        63 |  63 |       63 |       0 |
| System Party            |        23 |  14 |       14 |      35 |

The routes are there: each Acme party holds 7 =derived= routes to the
=derived-fx-vol= feed, service =end_of_day=. Nothing serves them:

#+begin_src text
Adapter 'composite-derived' of provider 'composite' announced: ready (0 served)
Dropping ticks for unknown source 'synthetic.bloomberg.vol.nzdusd.7cb9dd4b-...':
  the catalogue names no feed for it
#+end_src

A tick whose source the feed catalogue does not name is dropped, so no
series is created, so the blueprint waits for ever. Only the System
Party, whose quotes came with the system provision, holds derived
series.

*Fix*: find why the derivation names a source the catalogue does not
hold, and why the composite adapter serves nothing to a tenant party.
Until then, no retry in the step can succeed.

** The starting point names an image the seed does not hold

=attach_photos= attached 24 of 25 images. One was refused:

#+begin_src text
The picture was not attached to the account 'adrian.vance': Repository
error: Invalid image_id: 89b3bc34-78ad-5998-b935-23fb06a692da. Image
must exist.
#+end_src

*Fix*: either the staff bundle names a stale image id, or the image
catalogue is missing one row. Decide which, then make the other match.

** Two trade populations are drawn with conventions ORE cannot build

=generate_trades= wrote 14 of 16 populations, 927 trades in total. Two
were refused:

- A cross-currency swap drawn with PKR =KIBOR=: "no Ibor or overnight
  index convention defines it".
- A forward rate agreement drawn without an Ibor index: "a forward rate
  agreement needs an Ibor index".

Both refuse the same way on any host, so they are gaps in the draw, not
in the environment.

*Fix*: exclude those pairs from the draw, or seed the convention the
draw needs.

** A remote provision cannot publish its engine packages

Not one of the five warnings, but it stops the same recipe. The shell
uploads packages over HTTP and defaults to
=http://localhost:$ORES_HTTP_PORT= (20600 when the variable is unset),
so against a remote host every =compute publish-package= fails with
=connect: Connection refused=. The three packages published only after
passing =--http-base-url= through an ssh tunnel to the host's HTTP
server.

*Fix*: let the host profile carry the shell's HTTP base URL, the way it
already carries the NATS URL and TLS paths, so a remote provision works
without a hand-passed flag.

** The grid engine needs three facts no profile carries

Bringing the engine up on pauli took three hand-written facts and one
code fix, so a second host repeats the work:

- The profile carries no =ORES_COMPUTE_*= block. The deploy refuses
  without the tenant, the NATS URL, the subject prefix, the wire format
  and the TLS trio, and the profile generator writes none of them.
- The wrapper downloads its engine package over HTTP and is given a
  *path*, not a URL. Without =ORES_COMPUTE_HTTP_BASE_URL= it retries
  =only http:// URLs supported: /api/v1/storage/...= and every claimed
  job fails. Two reports were failed by this before the value was set;
  nine completed after it.
- The compute Quadlet unit always asked for =UserNS=keep-id=, which on a
  rootful host stops crun at =mount `sysfs` to `sys`=. Fixed in
  =docker/remote-run.sh=, which now asks for it only where podman is
  rootless — the same guard the runtime path already had.

* Why

The provision is the fastest way to exercise the market data path end to
end, so its warnings are the cheapest signal that a dependency or a
retry rule has a hole. The retry work closed the wait for quotes. These
five show what it does not yet close, and separate the two that no retry
can fix.
