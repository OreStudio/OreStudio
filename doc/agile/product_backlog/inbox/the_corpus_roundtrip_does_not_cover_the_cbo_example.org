#+title: The corpus roundtrip does not cover the CBO example
#+description: The corpus roundtrip walks ORE's Example_Trades. The Products examples hold one more trade, a collateralised bond obligation in Input_CBO, which no test reads and which needs its bond basket from reference data.

#+level: cross

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The corpus roundtrip test reads every portfolio under
=external/ore/examples/Products/Example_Trades=. =Input/portfolio.xml= holds
the same trades. =Input_CBO/portfolio.xml= holds one more trade, a =CBO=,
which no test reads. Its bond basket and tranches are in
=Input_CBO/reference_data.xml=, so the roundtrip must read reference data as
well as the portfolio.

* Why

The test's zero losses cover the 182 trades of =Example_Trades=, not every
ORE product example. A CBO the model drops or changes is not detected. The
PR review of #4468 found the gap.
