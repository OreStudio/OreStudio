* Goal

Two facts share one name in the store. The publisher is the party whose item a
value is. The producer is what made the value. =market_series.provider_code= holds
one of them, and the series key makes it the same one, so a derivation that no
publisher owns had to mint a provider called =derived=, and a simulated source had
to claim the identity of the administrator it stands in for.

This story splits the two. A series will name the publisher whose item it is and
the producer that made the value, with a kind that says whether the value was
observed, generated or computed. Every value will state a standing, internal or
external, so the model can hold a value no party may see without inventing a
party to own it. Every derivation will name the series it read.

When the story closes, the words on
[[id:DF2B616F-E112-4C57-A73B-69F85BE96EF4][Market Data Architecture]] are the
words of the store, and no provider names a mechanism.

* Acceptance

- A series names the publisher whose item it is and the producer that made the
  value, and the producer's kind says whether the value was observed, generated
  or computed.
- A real view and a simulated view of one item are two series for one party, and
  the ranking replaces one with the other; a test proves the replacement.
- Every value states a standing, internal or external. An internal value never
  becomes a series, no party can be entitled to it, and no bill meters it.
- No provider names a mechanism. =derived=, =composite= and =latent= are not
  publishers.
- Every derivation writes the series it read, the derived FX volatility quotes
  among them.
- The 194 series, 2,612 fixings and 2,280 vendor ids filed under =derived= are
  filed under the owner of their item, by

* Decisions

- *Three roles, three words.* An /administrator/ sets the benchmark or index an
  item belongs to. A /publisher/ owns the ids, the credit and the terms, and a
  series travels under its identity. A /producer/ makes the value. A value may
  have no administrator, and then nobody is attributed for it.
- *One word for each act.* A producer quotes a price or fixes a benchmark. The
  ingest records the observation and delivers the tick. We publish a set, with its
  approval, its candidate version, its supersession and its withdrawal. The
  broker's publish of a message on a subject is the broker's word.
- *Standing is a property of the value, not of a party.* Internal: consumed by
  the generators of the synthetic market alone, never a series, never entitled,
  never metered. External: may reach a party, by subscription or published set.
  The latent volatility surface is the one internal value today.
- *Three producer kinds.* Real, for a value observed from a live feed; simulated,
  for one generated by the simulator, whether it stands in for a vendor or a
  benchmark administrator; derived, for one we compute from other data, where
  =derivation_kind= names the computation.
- *The producer kind enters the series key.* A real and a simulated view of one
  item are different series for one party, so the ranking can replace one with the
  other. Without that, the two collide on the current key.
- *=latent= is a producer, not a publisher.* Its provider row is referenced by
  nothing: it holds no series, vendor instrument, mapping, feed, subscription or
  route (measured 2026-11-27 in =bright_faraday=). The word survives as the
  namespace token that [[id:90D85E90-E7EA-48D8-9245-313ECA3F9392][Synthetic feed
  naming scheme]] reserves for a market no vendor publishes.
- *=ore= is the producer of our own computation.* It is already a provider with
  3,648 series in the store, so the code =derived= retires rather than being
  replaced by a new name.
- *Position is not a producer.* =composite= names a rule that blends providers'
  series, and =demo= names a fictional publisher for exercises. Neither makes a
  value of its own, so neither belongs in the producer column.

- *The derived quotes serve one role, and it is internal.* They drive the
  simulated surface's generation. They are not a quote source for a party, they
  keep a stored series for their evolution, and no non-system party may read them.
- *The platform's own arithmetic is produced by =orestudio=.* A producer is not a
  publisher, so the mechanism-named =derived= retires and no item is re-filed to
  an owner it does not have.
- *A party with no system-level quotes authors its own surface.* The platform never
  manufactures one for a party. Generation needs a spot and two curves; without
  them there is nothing to quote, and the party calibrates and publishes its own.
