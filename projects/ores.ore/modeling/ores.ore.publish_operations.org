The workflow steps that publish the description of ORE that =ores.ore= owns from
its DQ datasets: the value domains of the ORE parameters and the parameters
themselves. Each is a trigger: the DQ publisher sends it and reads no reply, so
the step states a subject and no response, and its body is the DQ artefact the
server-side function knows how to expand.

The SQL function the step calls is derived from its subject, so the spelling of
the subject matters twice: once as the subject the registrar subscribes and once
as the function the handler calls.
