* Goal

A person who recreates the database, starts the services and provisions Acme has a working environment in a few minutes, with every service up and every curve, blueprint and surface the provisioning promises, on the first attempt.

* Acceptance

- Every service starts on a fresh database within its start timeout, and a service that depends on another starts once that one is ready.
- Provisioning Acme on a fresh database completes with no step ending with warnings for want of something an earlier step makes.
- The tenant setup's critical path is measured per step, and its longest steps are shortened, with the before and after recorded.
