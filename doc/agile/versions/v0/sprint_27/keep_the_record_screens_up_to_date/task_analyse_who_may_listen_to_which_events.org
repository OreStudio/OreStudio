* Goal

A session must hear only the changes of the tenant and the parties it may see. A published entity event names no tenant or party, in its subject or its payload, and ores.qt does not filter either. Analyse how events reach a session today, what each option costs, and recommend one: a tenant and party header from the generated registrar, the tenant in the subject, a NATS account or permission per tenant, or a check in the BFF before it relays. State what the options mean for a party-scoped reader.
