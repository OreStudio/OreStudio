This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Two SQL tests failed now and then in a full run of the suite and passed when run
alone. The CSA publish in =refdata_publish_from_dq_create.sql= read its staged
rows through a chain of common table expressions; it now stages them in a
temporary table and inserts in two statements. The thirteen atomised parts of
the conventions publish failed on a duplicate key when a dataset was published
again; each insert now ends in =on conflict do nothing=. The failures stopped
over nine full runs, but no run reproduced the failure on demand, so the cause
is not known.

* Why

A fix without a known cause can hide a real defect. The conflict fallback in
particular may hide a publish that writes the same part twice, which would be
a bug in the publish rather than in the test. Find the cause, reproduce it, and
keep or remove each change on that evidence.
