* What

Add a table of job titles. A job title is an entity that a person selects from a list. It is not free text.

The entity has a code, a name and a description. An account refers to a job title by key, in place of the =job_title= text column. The screens show the field as a list. The existing titles in the Acme account data become rows of the table.

* Why

Today =job_title= is a nullable free-text column on the accounts table. Free text gives spelling variants, so the same title appears in many forms. A report cannot group by title, and a screen cannot offer a list to choose from.
