* Goal

A running synthetic market follows its own path after release and drifts from the real rates; bringing it back is a decision, not a side effect. An operator's Re-anchor on the Calibration screen restarts a class's feeds from the levels the latest calibration stored, ignoring their last stored spots, and records who re-anchored, when, the reason, and each feed's level before and after. Nothing else re-anchors a feed.

* Acceptance

- Re-anchoring a class restarts each of its feeds from its calibrated level, and the feed's start record says it was re-anchored.
- The re-anchor is recorded with who, when, the reason, and each feed's level before and after, and shows in the feed's history.
- No restart, recalibration or approval moves a running feed's level; only a release or a re-anchor does.
