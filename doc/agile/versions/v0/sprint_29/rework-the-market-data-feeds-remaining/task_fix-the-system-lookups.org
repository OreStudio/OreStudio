* Result

Two defects found by live testing are fixed.

- The cut families and resolution rules are the system tenant's rows, but their
  repositories filtered to the caller's tenant, so every tenant saw an empty
  list and the edit dialog's selects were empty. The models now carry
  system_tenant_visible, and the regenerated repositories admit the system
  tenant's rows; verified live: the shell lists 5 families and 7 rules, and
  /api/reporting/cut-kind-choices answers all four arrays.
- The cut types screen derives the signed-or-not from the inbox approval kind,
  so a cut reader needs inbox::approval_kinds:read. The seed now grants it to
  Member, Trading, Operations, Support, Viewer, Finance and ReportRun; the
  running tenant's roles were granted and the types list answers for henry.
- Checks: the reporting service rebuilt and restarted; the codegen regenerated
  the two repositories; the live shell and BFF verified.
