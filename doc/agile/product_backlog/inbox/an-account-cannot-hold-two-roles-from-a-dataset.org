#+title: An account cannot hold two roles from a dataset
#+description: The account artefact carries one role per account, so a second role for one account has no published path and a populate script writes it.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The account artefact carries one role per account: =ores_dq_accounts_artefact_tbl=
has a single =role= column, and the IAM publish grants that one role. The ACME
demonstration needs one account to hold two -- =henry.jones= is a junior trader
and also takes the end of day cut, which is the Finance role's -- so
=acme_cut_take_roles_populate.sql= writes the second row straight into
=ores_iam_account_roles_tbl=.

That is the last table the collapse of the double writers left, and the only
place where a populate script and a publish write the same row of the same
tenant.

* Why

The publication-level rule gives every row one writer. Today the account's
first role arrives from the =acme.*.accounts= dataset and its second from a
populate script, so a tenant that publishes its accounts from a dataset cannot
express a second role at all.

Two designs would close it. The artefact could carry role assignments of its
own, which needs a second artefact -- the role junction is not published --
to hold one row per account and role. Or the publish could read a list of roles
from the one column, which puts a list in a field whose name says one role, and
would need the account screens to render it.
