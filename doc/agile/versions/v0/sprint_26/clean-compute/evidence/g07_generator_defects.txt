# G07: no generated artefact is refreshed while its generator has a known defect
# Run 2026-09-26 after the rebase onto origin/main.

## The generator's dry run, read before any refresh

$ python3 build/scripts/generate_component_puml.py --project ores.shell.compute --dry-run
  [WARN] No include/ directory found for ores.shell.compute

0/1 projects would change.

## Known generator defects and the artefacts they hold back

1. generate_component_puml.py resolves only projects/<name>/include/<name>/.
   A component whose sources live in a nested tree is not found, so its
   diagram cannot be refreshed. ores.shell.compute is one: its path is
   projects/ores.shell/compute/. The dry run above reports it and would
   change 0 projects. Its diagram is therefore hand-maintained, and the
   refresh is deferred until the resolver is fixed.

2. entity_meta_model_cpp_qt.org still describes the Qt presentation
   drawers. M09 removed the Qt presentation from the compute models, so
   refreshing through that facet would put back what M09 took out. The
   facet is left alone and the defect is recorded rather than papered over.

## What was refreshed, and why it was safe

Every other generated artefact was refreshed only after the defect that
touched it was fixed at source, with a case: G05 records the nine fixes
and the seventeen cases. The two defects above are the ones still open,
and no artefact behind either was refreshed in this branch.
