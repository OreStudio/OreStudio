* What

Hold EquityWorstOfBasketSwap on a table of its own with every term ORE's
=worstOfBasketSwapData2= states as a column, its basket members as underlying
rows, its eight schedules as schedule rows and its InitialPrices, KnockOutLevels
and FixedTriggerLevels as =instrument_value= rows, as FxWorstOfBasketSwap is
held. Retire its =equity_basket_constituent= and =basket_swap_period= rows.

* Why

The type is held on =equity_swap_instrument=, which keeps only part of the
element. The initial fixed pay date, the knock-in pay date, the floating
lookback and rate cutoff, the averaging and spread flags, the strike and five of
the eight schedules have no column or row, so an imported trade loses them on
the round trip. Two worst-of types also hold the same lists in two shapes.
