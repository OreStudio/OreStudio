* Result

The audit's findings are applied, and the three that do not hold are stated in
the investigation with their evidence.

- Eleven sets that stated text became the list or the switch ORE reads:
  =bounds=, =timingadjustment=, =bootstrap_mode=, =yieldcurvemodel=,
  =infmodeltype=, =floatspreadmapping=, =irvolatilitytype=,
  =regression_vargroupmode=, =heston_calibrationmethod=,
  =heston_processdiscretization= and =trainingsamples=; =globalmodel=,
  =usead= and =useindexcurveforindexcds= became boolean sets.
- Nine sets that stated text became numbers: =displacement=, =irreversion=,
  =infjyrealratereversion=, =bootstrap_mesherconcentration=,
  =regressionvariancecutoff= and the three smoothing settings.
- The bounds ORE enforces are stated: =beta= and 21 whole numbers stored in a
  QuantLib =Size= take a floor of 0, and the four seeds are left signed, as
  ORE reads them.
- =calibrationstrategy= gained =UnderlyingATM= and =UnderlyingDealStrike=, and
  =scheme= gained =GaussLobatto= with its description naming the two engine
  families the union serves.
- The period patterns accept a signed period, and the interpolation method set
  gained the deprecated =DefaultLogMixedLinearCubic= alias.
- =curve.business_day_convention= and =curve.day_counter= are catalogue sets
  again. The business day convention catalogue states the 26 spellings ORE's
  parser maps onto seven conventions, and the day counter registry already
  held ORE's spellings.

Not applied, and recorded with the evidence: =Model= and =Engine= (the
population holds fifteen or more builder names and the valid one depends on the
product type) and =Calibration= (five values in the IR builders, an unvalidated
string in a scripted trade).

Verified live: the catalogue views answer 26 and 71 values, the guard accepts
=F= and =Following= and refuses =XX=, and =ore_parameter_settings check=
answers OK over 266 drawn parameters. The nine C++ suites and the 639-test
pgTAP suite pass.
