* Goal

A CBO that states its structure inline is held in full: its bond basket, its
tranches, its fees and its payment schedule are rows and columns, so such a CBO
is read and written rather than refused.

* Acceptance

- the structure's scalars (day counter, payment convention, currency, senior
  and subordinated fees, equity kicker, fee day counter, reinvestment end date)
  are columns of a table keyed by the trade
- each tranche (name, notional, IC and OC ratios) is a row, in document order
- the payment schedule is instrument schedule rows
- each bond in the basket is held as a bond issue, as the bond family holds a
  security, and the basket names its members by issue
- the reader no longer refuses a CBO that states its structure, and a round-trip
  test reads ORE's example and writes it back
- the generator can book a CBO with a structure stated inline, and the ORE
  engine builds it with no error of the trade kind

* Plan

Decided with the user on 2026-11-19: each tranche's =LegData= is held as the
shared swap leg rows (=swap_leg= with its amounts and rates), keyed by the
tranche's ordinal, as every other leg in the system is held, rather than as a
coupon column, so a tranche of any leg kind is read.

- A =cbo_structure= table keyed by the trade holds the structure's scalars:
  day counter, payment convention, currency, reinvestment end date, senior and
  subordinated fees, fee day counter and equity kicker.
- A =cbo_tranche= row per tranche holds its name, notional and IC and OC ratios,
  in document order; its leg is swap leg rows under the tranche's ordinal.
- The payment =ScheduleData= is instrument schedule rows.
- Each =SubTrade= of the =BondBasketData= is a bond issue, as the bond family
  holds a security, and a =cbo_basket_member= row names the issue, the sub-trade
  id and the bond notional, in document order.
- The reader stops refusing an inline structure; the round trip reads a CBO
  that states ORE's reference structure inline and writes it back.
- The builder states the rows and the generator books a CBO with its structure
  inline.

* Result

A CBO that states its structure inline is read and written rather than refused.
The structure's scalars are a =cbo_structures= row; each tranche is a
=cbo_tranches= row in the structure's order, with its =FixedLegData= as swap leg
rows under the tranche's ordinal and the =tranche= leg group; the payment
schedule is instrument schedule rows; each basket bond is a
=cbo_basket_members= row naming its sub-trade id, security id, notional and the
counterparty its envelope states. The round trip reads a CBO that states its
structure inline and checks every row before and after a rebuild. The builder
states the rows and the generator books ORE's Pegasus structure inline; ORE
builds the CBO from it and stops at the basket bonds, whose reference data the
engine run does not hold, which the engine test classifies as market data.

A basket bond stated in full rather than by its security id, and a tranche
whose coupon is not =FixedLegData=, are still refused with a message; ORE's
examples state neither.
