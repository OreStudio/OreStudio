This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Two levels of work the simulator has not reached, and the shape that lets them land
without a redesign:

- *Two-way quotes.* Every published point becomes a bid and an ask around the mid it
  already publishes, so a mid consumer never changes.
- *A stochastic spread.* The spread is a positive mean-reverting process of its own --
  log-Ornstein-Uhlenbeck or CIR -- and one more member of the feed's block, so its shocks
  correlate with the mid, with volatility and with the regime by the loading it is given.
  It widens in the active regime when it is given a regime loading, and a contributor's
  width stays the contributor's.
- *Depth.* An order book is state with structural updates, not a scalar observation, so it
  needs its own datum and its own store (snapshots plus deltas) and an identity per
  instrument. It must not become a board member.

* Why

Because the two land differently, and only one of them is nearly free. Order flow is what
the arrival channel already models -- a Hawkes series is self-exciting order arrivals and
the heat is the market-wide burst -- and a spread is a block member of the latent market
whose driver already exists. A book is a new kind of data for the platform: the market
data path stores (instant, value) observations, and depth has structure.

The one thing to decide first is which of the two is primary for a given instrument. Where
there is depth, the top of the book is the spread and a spread model beside it would be a
second source of truth; where a contributor quotes without depth -- volatility surfaces,
curves -- the state process is the only source there is. That split is what the current
vendor model already implies.

The design statement, and what to keep true now so the work stays cheap, is
[[id:1F404EE8-215D-4E62-AB09-B2D34BE9C12F][The components of the market data generator]], section "Two-way prices and depth,
when they come". The story defers two-way pricing to a later level of work
([[id:95541791-E25C-44A5-9625-C8A1E44976D7][Support volatility surfaces — the remaining work]], out of scope).
