#+title: Trade sets, groups and links
#+description: The three ways trades relate: a set shares an attribute, a group is tied to an operation and has an identity, a link joins trades with a reason. The test, the catalogue and what each implies for the model.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:domain:finance:trading:trade_groups:
#+created: 2026-10-02
#+updated: 2026-10-02

* Summary

Trades relate to each other in three ways, and the words for them are
not synonyms.

- A *set* is an assortment of trades that share an attribute: all the
  trades in a book, for a counterparty, or in a netting set. No
  operation attaches to the set itself.
- A *group* is a set of trades associated with an operation: the
  trades a compression run ended and created, the trades a structure
  is authorised and cancelled as. A group has an identity of its own,
  and it is not a product.
- A *link* joins trades with a reason: a close-out answers a live
  trade, a hedge answers the trade it hedges. A link holds nothing; it
  exists only because the trades it joins exist.

Where a collection is something the business names and describes,
something with an identity is behind it, and that something is an
entity with a key. For a set it is the thing the members share — the
book, the netting set. For a group it is the group itself.

* Detail

** The vocabulary

The internal analysis notes propose mathematical terms to keep these
apart, and this page follows them.

| Term | What it is | Has an identity | An operation attaches | Example |
|------+------------+-----------------+-----------------------+---------|
| Set | Trades that share an attribute | Through the attribute's entity | No | The trades in a book |
| Group | Trades associated with an operation | Yes, its own | Yes | A compression run |
| Structure | A product implemented as a group | Yes, its own | Yes | A straddle |
| Link | A relation between trades | No | The link records one | A close-out |

A structure is the case where the group is also a product: it is
priced, authorised and confirmed as a whole. [[id:FC31FC9C-7A6E-4EFD-8A62-6DE4137F7160][Trade Structures and
Deal Composition]] covers it. Every other group is an operation's
record rather than a product.

** The test

#+begin_quote
Ask what the collection is for. If an operation attaches to the
collection as a whole — authorise it, cancel it, run it, undo it — it
is a group, and it gets an identity. If the members only share an
attribute, it is a set, and the identity belongs to the attribute's
entity. If it relates particular trades for a reason and contains
nothing, it is a link.
#+end_quote

The test follows from the rule [[id:BBD43BF2-9FEE-42B0-A667-9468AF759DC2][Data-Oriented Design]] states for
persisted data: relationships are keys, not ownership. A collection
the business names cannot be a by-product of the links between its
members, because nothing would then carry its name, its attributes or
the fact that it is empty. A structure with no legs yet is a structure
being booked; a netting set with no trades still has an agreement
behind it.

The converse matters as much. A link is not a degenerate group.
Recording a hedge as a group of two would give it an identity the
business never refers to, and every reader would have to look through
the group to reach the relation.

** Sets

A set's identity is the entity its members share. The set needs no
table of its own: membership is a key on the trade.

| Set | The entity behind it | A trade belongs to | Detail |
|-----+----------------------+--------------------+--------|
| Book | The book | Exactly one | [[id:130A3261-E788-4222-9BD7-371AD92982FB][Book]] |
| Counterparty | The counterparty | Exactly one | [[id:03C12558-69AC-4925-AAE1-85FFF75B10BA][Trade Classification]] |
| Netting set | The netting set, under its agreement | Exactly one | [[id:5F12A37F-9F26-4B49-BAAF-1BAA7B2BB94F][Netting sets]] |
| Portfolio | The portfolio | Through its book | [[id:5FC58A79-DBC9-46AF-9AAA-2C80B3A47850][Books and portfolios]] |

A trade moves between sets by changing the attribute, and that change
is versioned with the rest of the trade's booking.

** Groups

Groups come in two kinds.

A *standing group* exists before, during and after any one trade's
membership. Trades join and leave it over time, and the history of
membership is part of the record. The structure is the main case: a
trade sits in at most one structure at a time, and it can be linked to
a structure and unlinked from it later.

An *act* is a group that one operation creates at one moment. Its
members do not join or leave: the act records which trades took part
and in what role, and that record does not change afterwards.

| Group | Kind | What the operation does | Roles of the trades | Detail |
|-------+------+-------------------------+---------------------+--------|
| Structure | Standing | Prices, authorises and confirms the deal as a whole | leg, by role in the structure | [[id:FC31FC9C-7A6E-4EFD-8A62-6DE4137F7160][Trade Structures and Deal Composition]] |
| Compression | Act | Replaces offsetting trades with fewer trades of the same net risk | terminated, partially terminated, replacement | [[id:9BC498E0-91ED-4CA4-A7D7-7EF15684E799][Trade compression]] |
| Merge | Act | Combines several trades into one | contributor, result, bridge | [[id:BBD96309-7C59-4D94-BDA7-27EAB7A8612E][Trade merge]] |
| Allocation | Act | Splits a block trade across the accounts it was dealt for | block, allocation, bridge | [[id:73766E2D-D01B-4628-A1A0-78C9BBF709F6][Trade allocation]] |
| Funding roll | Act | Rolls a book's currency balances to the next day | generated | [[id:5CC47697-2655-4ECD-B04D-C6C08FF620D5][Tom/Next]] |
| Sweep run | Act | Centralises residual balances | generated | [[id:53841E4A-C5F3-4B7A-A269-45B8010C708A][Spot Sweep]] |
| Cleardown | Act | Resets book results at a period boundary | generated | [[id:DFC04627-FB0F-4FFF-973E-E3EC9E05AD87][Central remittance]] |
| Bulk book move | Act | Moves many trades to another book in one operation | moved | [[id:6CB4879F-3F4B-48CE-A01E-E1B9AEE866B0][Book moves]] |
| Import | Act | Brings trades, market data and configuration into a sandbox to inspect or rehearse | created | [[id:4AB0BC63-D73A-4FC3-B9AF-16C1BB90653F][Sandbox]] |

Every act has the same properties, whatever its kind:

1. /Identity./ The act has a key, so a trade can say which act created
   or ended it, and a report can list what one act did.
2. /Provenance./ The act records when it ran, who or which process ran
   it, and any notes. An act run by an outside provider also records
   the reference the provider gave it, such as a compression cycle id.
3. /Created together./ Every trade an act creates or ends, and the act
   itself, are written in one transaction or not at all; the
   [[id:2AAC92EB-2320-4385-999E-947E9E8FE5C5][trade link]] invariant of the same name applies.
4. /Explicit roles./ Each participating trade has a named role, and
   the kind of act states which roles it allows and how many trades
   may fill each.
5. /Undone as a whole./ The analysis notes treat a funding run as a
   /system event/: a unit of work that can be undone and redone as a
   whole, where the undo is P&L-neutral. An act is undone by a second
   act that reverses it, never by deleting the first.

** Links

With the groups taken out, the [[id:04F5EFF1-D4B5-4BD0-8CBC-4EF5DDF97921][link catalogue]] keeps the
relations that have no container: close-out, novation, roll,
misbooking correction, exercise, fixing, wash or back-to-back, book
move, hedge, linked cash and user-defined links. Most join two trades,
and each carries a type, a direction and named roles.

The catalogue lists the groups in a section of their own, with the
economic effect of each, so that every relation between trades is
found in one place whether it is a link or a group.

** What is neither

A [[id:886AAFB5-62F3-4AA3-B325-386665AFF926][position]] aggregates trades but has no identity: it is
derived from the trades in scope and computed rather than recorded. A
filter, a dynamic book or a blotter selection is a query over trades,
not a collection anyone refers to by key. None of them is stored.

** What follows for the model

- The entity behind a set is a table with a key, and membership is a
  key on the trade's booking.
- A standing group is a table with a key, and membership is a table of
  its own with its own history.
- An act is a table with a key, plus a participation table that names
  each trade and its role. The kinds share the participation shape and
  differ in their attributes, so each kind states its own attributes
  in a table of its own rather than in a list of optional columns.
- A trade created or ended by an act references the act, so a reader
  traces it back to its origin through the act. That satisfies the
  /back to origin/ invariant of [[id:2AAC92EB-2320-4385-999E-947E9E8FE5C5][trade links]].
