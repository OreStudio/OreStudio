This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

=core.py='s =_group_import= in the TypeScript domain projection takes the
second segment of a qualified name as the owning component, and for another
component it writes the import path =../../<owner>/domain/=. Add a unit test
with a field group from another component, in both the dotted and the =::=
form, and check the import path.

* Why

The review of [[https://github.com/OreStudio/OreStudio/pull/2496][PR #2496]] found this. Today the only check is the
=ores.web= typecheck in CI, which fails on a wrong path. The trading audit
group that imports =ores.dq.audit_record= covers the cross-component case.
No codegen test covers it, so the failure shows late and far from its
cause.
