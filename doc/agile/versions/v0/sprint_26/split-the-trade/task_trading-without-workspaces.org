* Acceptance

- No trading model, field group or profile it binds declares has_workspace_id or a workspace_id column; no trading table has the column.
- The ORE import, export and round trip behave as before.
- Build, ctest, pgTAP and the local check set pass.

* Plan

Section 13 of the trade shape design removed workspaces from trading, and
sandboxes and virtual books replaced them. The workspace column comes from
the generator, so the change is to the models and profiles.

- The =trading-instrument= and =trading-record= profiles, which only trading
  binds, set =has_workspace_id= false.
- =lifecycle_event= moves from =workspace-scoped-lookup=, which refdata
  shares, to =simple-lookup=, its twin without a workspace.
- =bond_issue= drops its own flag, and the four identity field groups drop
  their =workspace_id= column.
- =drop_trading_workspace_prose.py= rewrites the model prose that described
  the workspace and drops the workspace filter from the pasted queries of the
  wide trade and the swap leg.
- The rest of the system keeps its workspaces until
  [[id:8421A419-0311-4CC8-9384-D1097477813F][Remove workspaces from every component]].
