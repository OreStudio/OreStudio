The leg a cross-currency fix-float swap pays off an index, as ORE's =Index=
with its fixing rules: whether the index is resettable, whether the spread is
included in the coupon, the lookback, the fixing days, the rate cutoff, whether
coupons are averaged, and whether the observation is shifted. They are a part
because a convention that states the floating leg states that block or none of
it; the fixed leg beside it holds none of them.
