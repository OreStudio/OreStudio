#+title: Terminating a tenant leaves its usage intervals open
#+description: A tenant terminated while it holds market data subscriptions keeps its subscription and demand intervals open forever: marketdata can no longer write for it, so nothing closes them, and usage summed over open intervals over-counts the tenant.
#+type: capture
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :marketdata:billing:tenancy:
#+created: 2026-10-26
#+updated: 2026-10-26

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Close a tenant's open subscription and demand intervals when the tenant is
terminated, as part of the termination, while writes for it are still allowed.
After termination =ores_iam_validate_tenant_fn= refuses every write that names
the tenant, so marketdata cannot close them later, and the usage recorder now
leaves terminated tenants out of its plan altogether.

* Why

An interval stays open in the store with no end. Usage summed over open
intervals counts the terminated tenant as subscribed for as long as anyone asks,
which over-bills or misreports it. Before the hotfix for the 2026-10-26 outage
the recorder tried to close these intervals and the store refused every write,
which also stopped every other tenant's usage; the hotfix stopped that by
leaving terminated tenants out, which leaves their open intervals as they were.
Raised by the review of PR #3876.
