* Goal

Give the calibration the measures it can learn from: what the fixings behind a
start actually did, served beside the start and recorded for later policies.

* Acceptance

- A start reports the realised volatility over the window, daily and hourly,
  and the drift annualised from the same returns.
- It reports the daily range, the number of samples against the window, and
  the age of the fixing.
- It reports the jump count above three deviations and the dispersion against
  another accepted provider of the same pair, in basis points.
- The screen says which measure changed the calibration today (the realised
  volatility scales the mixture) and which are recorded for later.
- The measures are information: a missing measure never blocks a start, and a
  feed without a usable fixing reports that it has nothing to measure.

* Plan

- One pure function, =measure_window=, reads the window's business-day log
  returns in the feed's orientation: the daily spread, the drift times 252,
  the smallest and largest return, and the returns beyond three spreads. An
  inverted item negates its returns, so its drift changes sign and its range
  turns round.
- The resolver records the age of the fixing against the date it read, and
  reads each other provider's candidate on the same date for the dispersion
  in basis points of the level. A missing measure is left empty; nothing
  blocks a start.
- The measures ride on =feed_start=, so the running feeds, the calibrate read
  and the BFF carry them with no new call.
- The start notice says the volatility changed the calibration and lists the
  rest as recorded for a later policy.

* Result

A start reports what its fixings did: the realised volatility daily and
hourly, the annualised drift, the daily range, the samples against the
window, the age of the fixing, the jumps beyond three spreads and the
dispersion against each other provider of the pair in basis points. Only the
volatility changes the calibration, and the start notice says so; the rest
are recorded. A feed with no usable fixing says it has nothing to measure.
The measures are computed when a start resolves and are not stored apart
from the run's start.
