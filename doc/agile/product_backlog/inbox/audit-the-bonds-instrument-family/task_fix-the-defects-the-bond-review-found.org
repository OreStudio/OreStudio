* Acceptance

- The money columns that reach the persisted domain through a binary float are read with a decimal parse instead, or each is recorded with the evidence for why it cannot be.
- bond_forward.premium_amount is exact decimal in the model, the generated domain type and the column.
- instrument_option.premium_amount and instrument_option_exercise_price.price use numeric(38, 12), the scale the family's other money columns use.
- The money-column check flags a money column typed text, and its baselines are seeded rather than empty.
- The schedule parent and schedule-date child agree on the set of owner roles they admit.
- bond_repo.repo_index and bond_trs.funding_index generate an optional domain type, matching their nullable columns.
- The model that cites doc/knowledge/architecture/trading_bond_relational_model.org cites a page that exists.
