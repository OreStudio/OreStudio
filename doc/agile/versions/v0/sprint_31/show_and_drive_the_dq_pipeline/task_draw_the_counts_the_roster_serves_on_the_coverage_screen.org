* Notes

- *Four reads sent together do not all arrive.* On the provisioned environment
  the screen stayed on Loading and the browser reported "Cannot reach the
  server" for two reads the DQ service never logged. The service answered the
  two bundle reads and never saw the datasets or artefact-types requests, so the
  fault is between the browser and the service and not in either. Unauthenticated
  concurrency and keep-alive reuse both pass, so it needs an authenticated read
  to show itself. The reads are serialised as a mitigation with the reason in
  the code; the gateway's HTTP session layer is where the cause lives and its
  log is full of "Session error ... end of stream" for this client across the
  whole session, before this work as well as after it.
