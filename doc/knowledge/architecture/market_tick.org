* Summary

=market_tick= is the one tick type of every asset class. It is declared in
=ores.marketdata.api/messaging/operations_protocol.hpp= and travels on the
NATS subject =marketdata.v1.tick=. The same struct is published by a
producer and read by a consumer, so both ends of the pipeline share one
wire type.

* Detail

** The four fields

- =oresmd_uri= is the quote URI of the datum observed.
- =value= is the observed value as decimal text, so the producer's digits
  survive the trip.
- =observation_time= is when the value was observed.
- =source= is the producer, unique within a tenant. Its feed bindings name
  the consumers.

The tick carries no owner. The producer, the datum and the value are
everything on the wire, and a binding supplies the ownership. That is what
lets one tick land in the series of several parties.

The struct also carries =requires_session= as a compile time flag, and its
value is false. A client reads the flag rather than assuming that every
call carries a token.

** How the datum URI carries the asset identity

The URI is the only identity a tick carries besides its source. A URI such
as =oresmd://ir/USD?type=quote&instrument=ir_swap&quote=rate&fwd_start=0D&tenor=3M&term=5Y=
names the instrument type, the quote type and every coordinate field of the
datum. =oresmd_uri_codec::read= is strict, so a URI either names exactly
one datum or is refused. [[id:CE99CD2A-B31F-4306-865F-CBC519FBC1B8][The oresmd datum identity]] states the codec and
the schema row behind it.

** The republish subject

=market_tick_subject(tenant, party, ore_key)=, in
=ores.marketdata.api/domain/tick_subjects.hpp=, builds the per consumer
subject ="marketdata.v1.tick.<tenant>.<party>.<key>"=. The key is the
datum's canonical ORE key, lower cased with its slashes turned into dots.
The producer subject note states the other end of this traffic, which is
the subject a producer publishes on.
