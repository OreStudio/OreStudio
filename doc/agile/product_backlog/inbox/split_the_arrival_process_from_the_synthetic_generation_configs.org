* What

=fx_spot_generation_config= (20 members) and =ir_curve_generation_config= (21)
both carry =arrival_mode= with =branching_ratio=, =decay= and =sensitivity=
beside it. Read from outside the component, the three are the parameters of
one arrival mode: a discriminator followed by its columns, and the same block
in two types.

* Why

The re-inventory of the "Split the types that carry too many columns" story
lists both types over the twenty-member threshold. A verdict outside refdata is
a reading, so the synthetic owner confirms it or argues it down. If the reading
holds, the arrival process is one part that both configurations share, keyed by
the configuration.
