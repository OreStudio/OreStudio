* Acceptance

- A rate card holds components with the drivers fixed_per_period, per_instrument, per_party, per_licence and per_thousand_ticks.
- Rating gives the source bill for a period, and allocation gives each party a bill by the driver of each component, splitting a fixed fee equally among active subscribers.
- The bill of a simulated source is marked a shadow bill and nothing else differs.
