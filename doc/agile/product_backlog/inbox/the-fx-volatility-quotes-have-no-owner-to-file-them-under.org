This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The provider =derived= still states thirty-five FX volatility series and holds
2,275 =derived_fx_vol= vendor instruments: the ATM, risk reversal and butterfly
quotes of each pair, inferred from that pair's stored reference fixings. Retiring
the provider for the fixings left these where they were, because there is no
party to move them to.

Three candidates were weighed and each is false:

- =ore= imports the ORE example market data files, and those quote other pairs.
- =composite= is "the internal provider of the items that the service derives
  from the quote series of other providers", and these derive from fixings.
- Deleting them removes the volatility the synthetic FX option pricing reads.

* Why

The architecture already answers the question, and the store cannot hold the
answer: an internal value is consumed by the generators of the synthetic market
alone, never becomes a series, and no bill meters it. These quotes are exactly
that, and they are series today.

The model that can say it is the producer beside the publisher, with a standing
of internal or external, which
[[id:142FDA27-36E6-4C0D-95F2-6FAE223FC5E3][Split the publisher of a market data value from its producer]]
builds. Its acceptance already states that the 194 series, 2,612 fixings and
2,280 vendor ids filed under =derived= are re-filed by
[[id:0B9995FC-96D1-4785-AEBD-512397ECA7D5][Annotate a derived value instead of naming a derived provider]];
that task re-filed the fixings and recorded, in its Decisions, that these quotes
are the part it cannot finish without the standing.
