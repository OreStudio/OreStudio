* Goal

Model the two facts custody needs, in =ores.vault=, as codegen entities like
every other table: a secret a tenant keeps, sealed under a key version and bound
to the row it belongs to; and a consumer, which is the identity a token presents
and the public key the vault seals to. The secret's row holds the nonce, the
tag, the ciphertext and the version, and it never holds a plaintext. The
consumer's row holds a subject and one public key, so the vault finds a
recipient from a token and from nothing else. Both are tenant-scoped, and the
schema, the domain types, the protocol and the suites are generated from the
models.

* Acceptance

- The secret's row carries the nonce, the tag, the ciphertext, the key version and the owning consumer, and two tenants may use one name.
- The consumer's row carries the subject a token presents and its public key, and one subject has one key.
- Both are tenant-scoped under the isolation policy, and no generated wire surface carries a ciphertext or a value.
