* Goal

The Official rates screen is where a person runs the public sources. Three
interactions are planned on it and one new state: pausing a provider, starting a
backfill of a chosen range, and reading the adapter's health. None of them has a
precedent on this screen, and two of them rest on decisions the architecture has
not taken yet: pause is a fact about demand, and a backfill is a request the
adapter contract does not carry. So the design is settled on a prototype before
any control is built.

The prototype answers four questions:

1. What does a paused provider look like, and what happens to its fixings, its
   chart and its pair on the comparison while it is paused?
2. While a backfill of a chosen range runs, what does the person see: a
   progress, the fixing table filling day by day, or nothing until it ends?
3. How does a late fixing read differently from an adapter that is down, and how
   does a provider with no fixing yet read, without three colours meaning the
   same thing?
4. Does accepting new terms that withdraw permission to store show deleting the
   provider's fixings as a consequence, or as a command the person must
   remember?

Where the design space is open, the prototype carries two or three competing
answers beside each other rather than one guessed shape: the paused state and
the freshness line are the two places this is expected.

The provider is the object, over time: terms, freshness, fetching state and the
actions that follow from them on one row, and a page of the provider's fixings,
its window, who accepted what, and the trail of the acts done to it.

  its design note beside it, and the design note says what it argues and links
  the page, as every prototype in that index does.
- The row and the page answer the four questions in the goal, in the words a
  person uses, with mock provider data only.

  answers side by side, so the choice is made by looking, not by imagining.

  remain the two decision tasks of this story.

  no build and no session.

* Plan

- Build the page from the existing Official rates screen's vocabulary: the same
  provider table, the same fixings chart and pair comparison, the same tags and
  cards, so what the prototype shows is a shape this screen can take.
- Extend the row with terms state, freshness (last fixing date, received at,
  next expected publication) and the adapter's state, using the counts the
  adapter read already answers.
- Draw the paused state twice: a muted provider whose fixings stay and whose
  feeds keep their start, and a provider hidden from the list with a count
  saying how many are paused.
- Draw the backfill in progress twice: a range with a progress that names the
  days fetched, and a table that fills as the days arrive.
- Draw the late-fixing and adapter-down states once each, with the sentences that
  tell a person what to do.
- Take the next review as the design review; the two decision tasks take the
  open questions from it.

The page shipped with the row and the page, the three failure states, both
paused variants, both freshness lines, the compare panel, the withdrawn terms,
the provider page for any provider, the pause held in the address, and the
backfill at rest, running and done. Every state is an address.

* Result

The prototype is at =doc/prototypes/market_data/official-rates/=, with its design
note =official-rates.org= beside it and a row on the market data prototypes page.
It answers the four questions. The paused row stays, dimmed, and =?paused=hidden=
draws the row that leaves beside it. The backfill shows a progress and a done
state that names what the store skipped. The three failures read as three
badges and three sentences, and =?state=never= reaches the never-fixed provider
on its own. Withdrawing terms draws the deletion of the provider's fixings where
the person accepts the new version, not as a row action.

The design note states what is fake and what the two decision tasks still own.
