* Acceptance

- The Suppliers tab draws the tree: supplier → session → feed, with the adapter,
  environment and endpoint on the session and the publisher, service and scope
  on the feed.
- The Adapters tab is the read-only catalogue: version, services, schema,
  sessions.
- A session record has Overview, Feeds, Runtime and History; a feed record has
  Overview, Underlyings, Schedule, Routes and History.
- The two flows, new session and new feed, are drawn from the node they belong
  to.
- The screen uses the words publisher, supplier, adapter, session, service,
  feed and route, and no others for these rows.
