* Goal

A person on any feed page sees whether the feed runs and, when it does not, why and because of which node, and walks the graph up and down from it. They stop or start the node, or one vendor's view of a surface, with a reason, and read the node's own messages without opening a service log.

* Acceptance

- Every feed page has a Graph tab: the node's state with its reason and, for a starved node, a link to its cause; its upstreams and downstreams, each a link with its state; and a Stop or Start of the node with a reason.
- A latent surface's Graph tab lists its vendor views, each with its state and its own Stop or Start.
- Every feed page has a Messages tab: the node's telemetry log over the last day, read by its name.
- The graph read leaves out the latent provider's nodes for any caller but the system tenant.
- Live: from the AUD/USD surface's page, stopping the ICE view shows it stopped and the other views running, and its Messages tab shows the stop.

* Plan

One PR. It applies the screens part of the

   the node's state with its reason and a link to its cause, its upstreams and

   reason; a latent surface's downstreams are its vendor views, each stopped
   and started there.
2. *Messages tab.* The node's telemetry log over the last day, read by its
   name through the existing log route.
3. *Tenants.* The graph read leaves out the latent provider's nodes for any
   caller but the system tenant.

* Result

Every synthetic feed page has a Graph tab and a Messages tab. The Graph tab
shows the node's state with its reason, links the node that starved it, lists
what it reads and what reads it, each a link with its state, and stops or
starts the node with a reason; on a latent surface's page its readers are its
vendor views, each stopped and started on its own. The Messages tab reads the
node's telemetry log over the last day by its name. The graph read leaves out
the latent provider's nodes for any caller but the system tenant.

A tenant's live read was not exercised: the tenant sessions this environment
gives are refused by every synthetic route of =ores.http=, not by this one
alone; the filter is one call in the handler, and its function has its own
test.

The live graph showed the USD/ZAR surface failing for want of its ZAR curve on
the board; that is captured in the backlog as
[[id:3506D7BB-B130-4ACC-A1FA-720ED0BB0E1A][The USD/ZAR latent surface fails for want of its ZAR curve on the board]].
