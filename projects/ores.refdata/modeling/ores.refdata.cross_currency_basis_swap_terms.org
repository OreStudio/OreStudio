The terms a cross-currency basis swap states once for both legs, as ORE's
=SettlementDays=, =SettlementCalendar=, =RollConvention=, =EOM= and
=IsResettable=. It is a part because its fields hold one invariant: each
applies to the whole swap at once, so neither leg can hold it alone. The
settlement fields fix the settlement date, the roll convention and the end of
month rule roll the schedules of both legs, and the resettable flag resets the
notional of the swap. The flat and spread legs beside it hold none of them.
