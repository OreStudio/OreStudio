#+title: Register a stream for every workflow step subject
#+description: Step dispatch moved to JetStream and only one step subject got a stream. Every other component's step subject publishes into nothing and times out, so provisioning stalls after the IAM step and the tenant is left bootstrapping. Affects refdata, reporting, ore, compute, inbox and the DQ publish subjects.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Every workflow step subject needs a stream, and the service that serves the
subject needs a durable consumer on it. This was done, then generalised: a
service now declares its whole family of step commands in one `step_routes`
table, and `serve_step_commands` derives the stream coverage, the consumer
filters and the dispatch from that one list.

* Why

The capture described the stalls that a step subject with no stream caused. The
story [[id:6B5E348B-6B7A-46BF-AB56-33E4D5327AE4][Bring the service code onto the
NATS patterns]] closed it. Coverage landed first, one consumer per subject; the
work then replaced that with one consumer per family, so a service does not own
a server-side object per subject.
