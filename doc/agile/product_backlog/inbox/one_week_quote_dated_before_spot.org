* What

A provisioning run on 2026-11-13 refused every surface with

#+begin_src text
expiry 1W: the row dated 2026-11-16 is before the spot date 2026-11-17
#+end_src

The pair market refuses a quote whose date is before the spot date
(=fx_pair_market.cpp=), and the derived quotes date the ="1W"= expiry one day
short of the spot the run computes. All 40 surfaces of the four Acme parties
were refused, so =publish_party_surfaces= failed the run at step 13 of 15.

Either the derived quote dates roll on a calendar day where they must roll on
the pair's business days, or the expiry is dated from the wrong leg. Find which
and make the two agree on the spot date the forward set publishes.

* Why

It fails the whole surface step of every provisioning run made on a Friday, and
it hides behind a refusal that reads like market data being wrong rather than a
dating bug. Found on
[[id:C1048693-F5A5-40F2-9BCA-186D7E6B84B7][Run each step in the service that owns it]], whose own
change is not implicated: no marketdata or quant code changed on that branch.
The refusal is in =ores.analytics.quant=, merged as =47b59a61ee=.
