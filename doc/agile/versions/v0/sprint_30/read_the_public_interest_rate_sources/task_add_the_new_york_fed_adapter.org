* Goal

SOFR is stored as fixings in the system tenant, with its history, read from the New York Fed through the markets API, =markets.newyorkfed.org/api/rates/secured/sofr=, in JSON. The adapter is specialised: it reads the New York Fed alone, and it supplies only its fetcher and its settings to the shared half of [[id:0DB85F8F-4C7D-4428-B514-AF69CE8E5D86][Separate the public source adapter's plumbing from its fetching]].

* Acceptance

- The adapter reads the New York Fed alone, and it does not copy the announce, the demand, the backfill or the publish code.
- The New York Fed is a supplier in the catalogue, its SOFR series maps to the SOFR item of [[id:0D8B2280-F6E1-4198-8372-6712D1E8DFD8][Catalogue the overnight rate items]], and a session and a feed of it are seeded in the system tenant.
- The session's terms are accepted at setup, as the terms of the FX sources are. A session whose terms are not accepted is skipped and reported, and provisioning does not fail.
- A fresh environment stores the SOFR history as fixings within minutes of provisioning, and the log names the source and the count it stored.
- Each fixing is stored as published, with the credit that the terms ask for.
- The depth of the history is measured and written into [[id:3F9D6EAE-9FB8-4A0D-9BAF-1EA3EA93AB4F][the calibration sources note]].
- Unit tests parse a recorded response of the New York Fed, and a malformed response gives an error, not a fixing.

* Plan

- =nyfed_fetcher= implements =fetcher=: adapter =nyfed=, scheme =nyfed_rate_type=, field =percentRate=. A source's key is a rate type, and a table maps each type to its path under =/api/rates=; only =SOFR= is in it. A read is one GET of =search.json= over the window, which the API answers unpaged.
- The value is stored as a decimal, because the rates of the system are decimals and ORE reads a fixing as one. =decimal_of_percent= moves the point two places in the published text, so no digit changes and no binary rounding enters. The calibration sources note states the change of unit, as the New York Fed's terms ask for a label on modified data.
- The date, number and HTTP helpers move from the Frankfurter fetcher to =published_text=, which each fetcher of the story uses.
- The process hosts both fetchers, and a =--nyfed-url= option names the API.
- The seeds add the =nyfed= supplier and adapter artefacts, the =nyfed-markets= session, the =nyfed-sofr= feed scoped to the SOFR item, and the system party's subscription and route. The service registry hosts =nyfed-sofr:nyfed:nyfed:SOFR=.
- The terms of a provider that states them are accepted at setup by the bootstrap. A route whose provider's terms are not accepted is refused with =terms_not_accepted= in the demand, which =app_demand_calculator_tests= covers, and provisioning does not fail.

* Result

SOFR is stored as fixings in the system tenant, read from the New York Fed markets API by a specialised fetcher that the official adapter hosts beside Frankfurter. A fresh environment accepted the New York Fed's terms at setup and, minutes after provisioning, stored 272 fixings, the last 400 days, with the log line "Sent 272 fixing(s) of 'nyfed-sofr'". The values are the decimals of the published percents, such as =0.0389= for 3.89, and the provider =nyfed= carries the credit "Source: Federal Reserve Bank of New York". The calibration sources note records the change of unit and the 2,149 rates the route serves since 2018-04-02.

Unit tests parse a recorded response and an empty window, refuse four malformed answers, check the URL of a read, and check the decimal of a percent over ten texts.
