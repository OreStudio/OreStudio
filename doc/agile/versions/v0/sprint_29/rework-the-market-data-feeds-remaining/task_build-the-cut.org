* Result

The cut data model is built, reviewed and repaired.

- The cut kinds: the family, resolution-rule and kind lookups, the seven types
  (EOD, EOM, EOY, SOD, IPV, FLASH, LIVE), the two signed approval kinds and the
  per-kind retention. The kind's approval is a nullable FK to the inbox approval
  kinds; the signed-or-not is derived from it.
- The report's selection on the definition (cut_kind_code, cut_id) and the run's
  resolved cut (cut_id, cut_label, cut_kind_code, cut_timepoint).
- The cut entity: the body references, the gaps, the acts, the indicative mark
  and the retention copy; the state derived and stored nowhere; supersession
  computed in production.
- The resolution operation for EOD (with the month-end and year-end fallbacks),
  EOM, EOY, SOD, IPV, LIVE (minted at the run) and a pinned cut, serving the
  preview and the run. The report-instance trigger resolves and records the cut
  where the covered date, scope and centre are stated.
- The widened unique index (tenant, kind, party, scope, centre, timepoint,
  ordinal), and the cut's generic writes removed with client_read_only, so
  nothing bypasses the copy rule.
- The two-axis review (Standards and Spec) was run; every finding was closed:
  the permissions seeded and granted (the gate passes), the indicative mark on
  the cut and in the resolution, supersession in production, the permission
  inversion, the unique index, the copy bypass, the ER diagrams, the test
  conventions and coverage, the cut_book claim and the grammar.
- Checks: the handler-permission gate, lint, the codegen, the SQL validators,
  the ER diagram check, and the reporting core/api/service and scheduler suites
  (213 + 69 + 10 + 24 cases) passing on the rebased tree.

Left deliberately: a scheduled firing records no resolved cut (the covered
date, scope and centre are the run's and the schedule does not carry them); the
take raises no inbox approval request (the hand-take surface does not exist in
this component); the configuration_market_binding retirement is deferred because
the run document still builds its market data from those rows.
