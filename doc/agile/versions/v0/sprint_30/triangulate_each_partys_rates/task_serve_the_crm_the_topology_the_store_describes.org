* Goal

The CRM's drivers are hand-listed in refdata, and the identity the store holds is not consulted. Read the fixing series a party holds, group them by publisher into fixing sources, and hand the chosen source to the policy and then to topology_builder, so a party's topology follows the series it actually holds and the rank the providers carry. A series without ccy or unit_ccy is refused by name rather than skipped.

* Acceptance

- The CRM's engines are built from the party's fixing identities and the provider rank; crm_driver_pair is no longer the source of the drivers.
- A fixing series with no ccy or no unit_ccy is refused by name and the party it belongs to.
- The CRM suites pass, including the montage reads that exercise the engines.
