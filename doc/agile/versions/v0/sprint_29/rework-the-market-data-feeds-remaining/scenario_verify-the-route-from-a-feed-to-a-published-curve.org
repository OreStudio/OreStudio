This page documents a test scenario verifying [[id:BAF9151C-3A23-4A52-8012-8DD9E0F7FE85][Task: Route the synthetic feeds into the ingest]] in [[id:C2C442FA-8A95-46F6-84C3-64AC22DE05EE][Rework the market data feeds into a market data service with adapters — the remaining work]]. * Before you start

These are readiness conditions, performed before the tester opens the client.
The environment is already in this state; the commands are recorded so it can
be brought back to it.

- The Acme tenant is provisioned and the fleet is running: =compass services
  status= reports 25 running, none failed, and NATS running.
- The demo curve is populated by hand from
  =evidence/acme_ui_curve_seed.sql=: the ladder series
  =oresmd://ir/USD?type=series&instrument=ir_swap&quote=rate&fwd_start=0D&tenor=1D=
  with seven quotes (3M to 30Y), the output series
  =oresmd://ir/USD?type=series&instrument=discount&quote=rate&curve_id=USD-SOFR-UI=,
  the blueprint =USD-SOFR-UI= for ACME Corporation US Inc, and its seven
  spot-starting pillars, each naming the ladder series.
- The set is published through the real flow: =henry.jones= raised
  =01a18c8f-8f4b-7585-8b54-8f1c6f0e8a26=, =mark.reid= approved it, and
  =henry.jones= published at =2026-10-30 11:26:28Z=. The published set reads
  back with state =published= and seven points.
- The quotes are stamped at =2026-10-30 11:26Z=. The Check's default as-of is
  now, which is after them; an as-of before them finds no quotes.

* Steps

** Log in as the maker

- Open the app and sign in as =henry.jones@acme_corporation= with
  =Secure-Password-123=.
- Confirm the session's party is ACME Corporation US Inc (ACCOUS); the
  blueprint and the published set belong to it.

*** Result

** Open the curve blueprint

- Go to *Market data* and then *Curve blueprints*.
- Confirm the list shows =USD-SOFR-UI= with a published state line.
- Open it. The record header reads the name, the version and the role; the
  header offers Copy, Edit and Delete.

*** Result

** Read the blueprint's details

- Open the *Details* tab.
- Confirm the name =USD-SOFR-UI=, the output series URI ending
  =curve_id=USD-SOFR-UI= as a link, the currency =USD=, the index tenor
  =1D=, the role =Funding=, the interpolation method and the day count.
- Confirm the discount curve reads as an absence, not a nil UUID.

*** Result

** Read the pillars

- Open the *Pillars* tab.
- Confirm seven rungs in maturity order: a 3M deposit and swaps to 6M,
  12M, 2Y, 5Y, 10Y and 30Y.
- Confirm each row shows the market series it names, the ladder URI ending
  =fwd_start=0D&tenor=1D=, and its version.

*** Result

** Run the check

- Open the *Check* tab and press *Run the test*, leaving the as-of at its
  default.
- Confirm all six recipe checks read as passed: one pillar, maturity order,
  deposit from spot, split tenor, projection funding, funding not a
  projection.
- Confirm the test returns seven points with their discount factors and
  dates, and the forwards between them. Any server refusal must name the
  pillar and the series it could not read, not a nil-UUID record.

*** Result

** Read the published set

- Open the *Published sets* tab.
- Confirm the published set at =2026-10-30 11:26Z=, publisher
  =henry.jones=, seven points, and the state =Published=.

*** Result

** Open the published curves

- Go to *Market data* and then *Published curves*.
- Confirm the list shows =USD-SOFR-UI= for party =ACCOUS=, published, with
  its publisher and as-of.
- Open it and confirm the *Published points* tab draws the seven-point curve
  and the *History* tab carries the decision that approved the publish.

*** Result

** Read the request as the decider

- Sign out and in as =mark.reid@acme_corporation= with
  =Secure-Password-123=.
- Go to *Requests* and open the curve publish request raised by
  =henry.jones=.
- Confirm the request names its subject (the curve, the party, the as-of and
  the point count), reads as approved, and that the decision appears in its
  History.

*** Result

** Start a feed by hand

- Sign out and in as =henry.jones@acme_corporation= with
  =Secure-Password-123=.
- Go to *Market data* and then *Simulator*, find the USD SOFR IR feed and
  start it by hand.
- Confirm the feed reports running, and that its ticks reach the store: the
  sources board shows its adapter active, and the feed's status names the
  source it published through. Before this task's fix the feed published into
  a subject nothing consumed, while the log still looked healthy.

* Notes

- The demo curve is hand-applied evidence, not a committed seed. To rebuild
  the state: recreate and provision the environment, apply
  =evidence/acme_ui_curve_seed.sql= with the DDL user, then run the
  raise/decide/publish flow with =curve_publish_operations publish-curve= and
  =approval_operations decide-approval=, as the maker and the decider.
- Raising a publish and applying it are shell-only today; the UI shows the
  request, the decision and the published set, not the raise or the apply.
- The feed start is the task's second fix; its proof is that the ticks are
  stored, not only that the feed reports running.
