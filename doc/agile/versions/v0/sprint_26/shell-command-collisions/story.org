* Decisions

** One owner per menu, enforced at startup, not by registration order

cli::Menu holds its children in a vector and answers a name by first match, so
the order of the calls in repl.cpp decided which unit answered. The root is now
a shell_root_menu that claims a name once and refuses a second claim, and every
unit inserts through a seam the codegen template declares, so a component that
gains a unit joins the rule with no edit.

** A second unit extends a menu instead of registering its own

The generated entity unit owns the menu named for its entity. The hand-written
unit beside it contributes the verbs the model cannot express through
extend_menu, and the root applies those once every unit has registered, so the
order the units register in cannot decide whether an extension lands. Merging
rather than renaming keeps every verb at the address callers and recipes
already use.

** The name is read through Menu::Prompt()

cli::Command keeps a command's name protected, so the root cannot read it back
directly. Prompt() is the same string because every shell menu is constructed
from its name alone, and check_shell_menu_collisions.py rejects a construction
that passes a separate prompt, which is what holds the two together.


* Out of scope

- The twenty generated units in refdata and synthetic that carry their own
  components' pending model drift. They do not claim their names yet, and the
  gate covers them until their next regeneration brings the seam with it.


* Result

Merged as PR #2272, all five checks green. One owner per menu name is enforced
at startup by shell_root_menu and held earlier by check_shell_menu_collisions.py,
and a second unit contributes verbs to a menu it does not own rather than
registering a second one. The reported symptom is gone: the help inside accounts
listed five verbs and hid the seventeen the hand-written unit answered, and it
now lists all twenty-two.

The census the story carried was wrong in two places and was corrected before any
edit: four menus were registered twice rather than five, because the dq and
reporting collision went with the generator fix, and seven verbs were dead rather
than eight, because permissions get was counted and never existed.

Two further defects were forced by the guard and fixed: page-size was registered
twice in one file and answered by falling through on arity, and the provisioning
smoke recipe called the generated tenants add with the hand-written
five-argument form and an invalid tenant type.
