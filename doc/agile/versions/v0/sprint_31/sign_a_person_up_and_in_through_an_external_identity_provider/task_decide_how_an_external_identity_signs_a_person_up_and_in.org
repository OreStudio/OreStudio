* Goal

Decide how ORE Studio lets a person sign up and sign in with an external identity provider, and how the same identity layer answers a client of ORE's API. Cover which protocol and flow to use, against Google's OpenID Connect documentation; where the flow is served, in ores.http's realm or in the IAM service; how a provider's registration becomes data a tenant controls; how a first sign-in becomes an account under the deployment's existing registration policy, and how an existing account is linked without letting a second person take it; what a session and an auth event record about the method; and how ORE becomes an OAuth 2.1 authorization server whose consent page reuses the same sign-in, so an MCP client needs no second identity system. Weigh the options with their costs and end with a recommendation the product owner approves.

* Acceptance

- The options are written with their costs, against Google's OpenID Connect documentation and ORE's existing session, registration and agent-grant machinery.
- The recommendation states where the flow is served, what a provider's registration holds, the rule by which an account is linked, and what a session records about its method.
- The recommendation says how ORE's authorization server reuses the same sign-in and consent.
- The verification plan says how the flow is proven in the gate and the suites without Google's credentials, and how a real Google sign-in is recorded.
- The product owner approves the recommendation, and the story's Decisions carry it.
