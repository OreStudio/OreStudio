* Acceptance

- Every credit trade type the catalogue routes to credit_instrument books a trade.
- The two unrouted types are routed or named out of scope.
- The class share of the population matches its weight within tolerance, and every routed type appears at least once.
- The family exports as the ORE portfolio the engine reads, and reverse-maps without loss.
- The report that prices the family runs against the spread and hazard curves it needs, or the task states which pricing it cannot prove yet.
