The signup protocol messages.

The response is the door's answer. The policy behind it — the switch, the tenant
a registration lands in, and the party and role a new account receives — is
settled in
[[id:84706C3E-D48D-4A4B-967A-4AADB3CF44B2][registration and onboarding
defaults]], and the path the call takes is
[[id:504863AB-C45A-47E8-BB77-AADCB620A305][the entry journeys]]. The read that
tells a screen whether to offer the form at all is a separate subject,
=iam.v1.ops.registration_policy=.

 * system tenant by omission, which is the defect the survey found.

#+begin_src cpp :name comment
/**
 * @brief The state the account was created in: @c active or @c pending.
 *
 * An account is active when its tenant nominated a default party, so it
 * received an association and can sign in at once. It is pending when the
 * tenant nominated none: the account exists, but it waits for an
 * administrator to finish setting it up.
 */
#+end_src
