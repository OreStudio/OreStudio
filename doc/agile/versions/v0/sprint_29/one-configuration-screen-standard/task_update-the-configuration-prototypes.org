* Plan

** Why

The two configurations a report binds that carry the word "engine" were designed
at different times and in different words. The journey of record for the engine
run settings did not exist, and the pricing journeys still said "pricing
engines". A person reading either screen learned the wrong name for the other.
The owner decided the words and the lifecycle rule on 2026-10-24, and asked for
the journeys and prototypes first, so the standard can describe what has been
signed off rather than propose it.

** What was done

1. Read the ORE 1.8.17.0 source and the ORE Studio tree to establish the
   boundary and the linkages. The record is the investigation.
2. Wrote the journey for the engine run settings, with the section lifecycle
   and the related configurations.
3. Renamed the five pricing journeys, and added the disambiguation section and
   the cross-links.
4. Updated the two prototypes: the standard words, the "what this decides"
   sentence, the related configurations card, and the section lifecycle on the
   engine run settings.
5. Corrected facts the source contradicts: =buildFailedTrades= defaults to Yes
   in the ORE source, and the engine run settings have three sections, not five.
6. Updated the two design notes, the reporting group page and the two
   prototype counts.
