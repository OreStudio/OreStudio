* Goal

A quant composes an IR curve feed in ores.web in the words of the Qt IR curve editor, on the same list and page as an FX price feed. The screens are shared, so the next asset class states a feed kind and writes only its own tabs. This delivers most of the task Build the IR curve feed screen, which stays open for the Ticks tab.

* Acceptance

- The feed list, the feed page and the add, edit and delete dialogs are drawn by one component for every kind, and the FX price feed screens are unchanged.
- The IR curve feed has a list with run controls, a page with Details, Process, Curve template and History tabs, and a dialog in the words of the Qt editor.
- The Process tab saves the engine and the parameters together under one reason, and previews the curve shape and sample short-rate paths from the values on the screen.
- The curve template is edited as the parts of the feed, and the history shows the feed, its parameters and its entries as one stream.
