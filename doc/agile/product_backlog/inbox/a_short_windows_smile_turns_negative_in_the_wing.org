#+title: A short window's Gram-Charlier smile turns negative in the wing
#+description: The derived source drops an expiry when the broker butterfly cannot be solved because the Gram-Charlier smile is not positive at the search start. EUR/CZK at 1W and USD/TRY at 3M hit it on every pass, and a surface missing one expiry is refused whole, so both pairs are left out of the catalogue.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The derived source builds each expiry's smile as a Gram-Charlier expansion and reads the
five quotes off it. For two pairs one expiry never reads, on any day:

#+begin_src text
  The derived source left out EURCZK 1W: the broker butterfly: no sign change near the start; the smile is not positive there
  The derived source left out USDTRY 3M: the broker butterfly: the smile is not positive where the search starts
#+end_src

The series of both pairs hold every other expiry. The fixings behind them are ordinary ECB
days (EUR/CZK near 24.4, EUR/TRY near 55.7 in November 2026), so the input is not the fault.
The smile is: =hold_in_gram_charlier_region= keeps the skewness and kurtosis inside the region
where the density is positive, but that does not keep the implied-volatility smile positive out
to the 10-delta wing where the broker butterfly search starts. A short window carries the
largest skewness, because =over_days= scales it down with the square root of the days, which is
why EUR/CZK fails at 1W and nowhere longer.

A related symptom of the same short-window shape: EUR/TRY reads all five quotes at 1W, but the
calibrated surface breaks the butterfly condition there, so the publish gate holds it back on
every run ("EURTRY_SSVI: breaks 1 arbitrage condition(s): butterfly at 1W"). Provisioning still
completes, because a held-back surface is not a failure.

* Why

A surface whose expiry has no quotes is refused whole at publish, so provisioning fails for
every party that holds the pair. The horizon task left EUR/CZK and USD/TRY out of the catalogue
for that reason. Either fix brings them back:

- keep the smile positive across the strikes the quotes read, in the derivation itself; or
- calibrate the expiries a surface has and record the rest, which is the sizing story's task,
  so one missing expiry costs that expiry and not the surface.
