* Notes

The note's sections, which are also the outline of the work:

1. *What the factor is today.* One shared state and one shared heat, read by the
   arrival processes as a rate multiplier. The arrival work, and what it left
   open.
2. *Sampling against time.* Business time, integrated activity, and why a
   diffusion's variance follows elapsed time and not the number of observations.
   The processes that take the gap against the ones that step once per tick, and
   the inconsistency between them.
3. *The volatility loading.* The factor in the link, the loading per feed, the
   setters, and the normalisation that keeps calibration. Two meanings of
   volatility, and which one a test measures.
4. *The tails.* The Gaussian mixture, reweighting toward the wide components as
   activity rises, and why a diffusion scale cannot produce a panic's kurtosis.
5. *The control plane.* What a person sees and sets, and what it costs: the same
   switch, the same tab, one number per feed.
6. *Out of scope.* The bivariate Hawkes, its 2x2 kernel and the spectral-radius
   stability condition, captured rather than scoped.

* Result

The note is written at
[[id:9D778F72-8FF6-4F01-AF4B-696DC7BA855A][The market's volatility channel]], and
the arrival note links to it, so the pair reads both ways.

It settles the four things the story asked for. A rate change must change
sampling and not time, and the note names the fixed-step processes that get that
wrong today. The volatility loading is three seams that mirror what the arrival
work already built, with calibration — not plumbing — named as the cost. The
tails come from reweighting the FX mixture the feed already carries, not from a
new model. The control plane is the switch, the read and the tab that exist, plus
one number per feed.

It also states two traps the next tasks must not fall into: the heat's long-run
mean is not one, so it has to be centred in the volatility margin or calibration
drifts; and "volatility" means two different things — the process's sigma, and the
sigma a client computes from the ticks published — so every test has to say which
it measures.

The bivariate Hawkes is out of scope and stays captured: its 2x2 kernel replaces
the scalar stability test with a spectral radius, which is a model of its own.

The three tasks the note scopes are raised on the story, in its order: sampling
before time, the loading with its calibration, and the tails. The bivariate Hawkes
stays captured.
