* Acceptance

- reporting::report_errors and reporting::report_error_scopes are granted to the roles that own them, with the choice recorded and justified.
- The grant is visible in the live database and survives a provision.
- A caller holding the role can read a run's errors over the subject and a caller without it is refused.
