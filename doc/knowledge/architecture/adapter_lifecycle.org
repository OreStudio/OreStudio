* Summary

The service tracks each adapter in one of five states: =announced=, =ready=,
=serving=, =stale= and =rejected=. The messages of
[[id:5DFA094B-4F9F-4190-8251-8D4D0B77A5C7][An adapter and the service meet at four messages]] cause every change. The service needs no other knowledge
of how the adapter runs.

| State | Meaning | Entered by |
|-------+---------+------------|
| =announced= | The adapter has sent its provider and universe. | Announce |
| =ready= | The universe agrees with the catalogue and no demand is set. | Universe check |
| =serving= | The adapter holds a non-empty demand set. | An accepted demand reply |
| =stale= | The adapter missed its heartbeat. | Heartbeat timeout |
| =rejected= | The provider is unknown to the catalogue. | Universe check |

A =stale= adapter keeps its routes. The service shows the gap in the source's
screen and in quality counters. When the heartbeat returns, the service sends the
demand set again.

** What the adapter does inside these states

A live adapter manages its vendor session: connect, authenticate, resubscribe
and reconnect. The simulator starts and stops generators. Both are private to
the adapter. See [[id:5ABE694A-7F87-46A9-943E-B6734B3B6EF9][A live and a simulated source differ only inside the adapter]].
