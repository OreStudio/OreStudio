Data Sourcing and Generation Steps:

    1. START FROM THE ORE SAMPLES
       The shape comes from the ORE sample netting data: one master agreement
       with each bank, a netting set for each agreement, and a credit support
       annex for each collateralised set. The content does not: the samples name
       the banks and terms the ORE examples happen to use, and are published
       separately as sample data.

    2. CHOOSE A PANEL FOR EACH LEGAL ENTITY
       Each of the four legal entities faces the banks that deal with its
       jurisdiction: the US entity three US dealers, the UK entity five European
       and UK dealers, the Hong Kong entity three banks active in Hong Kong, and
       the holding company two banks for its treasury. Each bank is the legal
       entity the GLEIF counterparty set holds for it, named by LEI. An
       affiliate with a similar name is a different counterparty.

    3. DOCUMENT EACH RELATIONSHIP
       Every relationship is an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement. New York law governs
       the US entity and English law governs the others. The reference is the
       entity, the bank, the document and the year signed.

    4. GIVE EACH RELATIONSHIP A COLLATERAL REGIME
       Variation margin only, variation and initial margin, or uncollateralised.
       The largest relationships take initial margin. A relationship may hold a
       second, uncollateralised netting set for trades the credit support annex
       does not cover, such as commodity derivatives.

    5. SET REALISTIC CSA TERMS
       Each CSA is in the currency its entity margins in, on that currency's
       current overnight rate: USD-SOFR, GBP-SONIA or EUR-ESTER. No retired
       index is used. Variation margin has a zero threshold and a minimum
       transfer amount that depends on the currency; a combined variation and
       initial margin agreement takes a higher amount. Calls and postings are
       daily, the margin period of risk is ten days, and the eligible
       currencies are the three major currencies with the margin currency
       first.

    6. ADD THE INTRAGROUP AGREEMENTS
       The holding company hedges for each subsidiary, so it holds an agreement
       with each of them, uncollateralised. The UK and US entities also hold an
       agreement with each other for risk transfer between booking centres, with
       variation margin and no minimum transfer amount. The two entities to one
       intragroup agreement each hold it under the same reference.

    7. PUBLISH
       The datasets are members of the legal entity bundles. They publish in
       dependency order to the party the bundle names. The intragroup
       counterparties are the ACME legal entities themselves, published by the
       acme.lei_counterparties dataset.
