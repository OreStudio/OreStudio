- The local gate is green.

* Plan

Serve the sign-in records and the sessions at the BFF's addresses in a
hand-written unit, =sign_in_routes=: a page of the tenant's sign-in records and
one account's, a page of the tenant's sessions, the open ones and the caller's
own. A page is read as whole numbers within the BFF's limits. One account's
record is the caller's own by the self read when the identifier is the
token's subject, as the BFF compared it with the session's account. The
records and sessions are stated by =account_view=, camelCase, an absent time
read as null.

The password policy and change, sign-up and one account's sign-ins move to
[[id:A888280F-3BCE-49EC-94F2-69F57430B60F][Serve the password, sign-up and sign-in history routes]].
