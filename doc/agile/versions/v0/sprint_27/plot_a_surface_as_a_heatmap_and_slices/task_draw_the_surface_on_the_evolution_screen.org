* Acceptance

- The panel shows the heatmap with the first axis down the side and the second across the top.
- A hole is drawn as empty, and a hole breaks a slice line.
- The colour scale choice changes the colours.
- A shape with one axis keeps the existing chart; a shape with more than two axes says it is not drawn.
- All words exist in English, French and Portuguese.
