* Acceptance

- The operation returns asset class, scope, instrument type, quote type, the other identity fields and the owning party for each visible series.
- A caller of another party does not receive the series, and the operation checks a read permission.
- A test with two parties shows each party its own series only.

* Plan

Add one operation, =list_series_identities=, to the marketdata operations model. Its reply is a
list of =listed_series=: the series id, the typed identity the curve reads take with the owning
party stated, and the axes the series declares. A lister in =core/service= reads the series
the caller's context can see, turns each URI into its typed identity through the codec, leaves
out what a curve read cannot name, and reads every series' axes in one query. The handler checks
=marketdata::market_series:read= and the registrar subscribes it. Tests cover the round trip to
the resolver, party isolation under row-level security, the party filter, a fixing, and the axes.

* Result

A caller can now list the series its party can see, as the typed identities the curve reads take.
The operation is =marketdata.v1.ops.list_series_identities=. Each row carries the series id, the
identity with the owning party stated, and the axes the series declares. An identity listed under a
party names that party's series and no other's, so a picker cannot send a person to another party's
data. The handler checks =marketdata::market_series:read=.

*What the first attempt got wrong.* The lister first read =market_series= for visibility, and a
party then saw another party's series. The series table is isolated by tenant only. The identity
projection is the party-isolated table, which is why the resolver already behaved. The lister now
takes visibility from the projection and the rows from the series table. The isolation test failed
before the change and passes after it.

*What the tests also caught.* The new axes test wrote a point into =NZD/PLN=, a surface another
suite reads, and made that suite fail one run in several. The tests share one tenant and each file
gives every test its own currency pair. The axes test now reads =SGD/THB=. The derived point test
from the provenance task shared =NZD/CZK= with another test, and now reads =NZD/ZAR=. The four suites
passed together in eight consecutive runs.

Left for the BFF task: nothing in the browser calls this yet. The running stack still serves the old
marketdata binary until it is restarted.
