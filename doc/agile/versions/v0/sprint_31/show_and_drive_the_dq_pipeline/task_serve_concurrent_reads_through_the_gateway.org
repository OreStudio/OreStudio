* Notes

Found while building the coverage screen. Four reads were sent together and the
browser reported "Cannot reach the server" for two the DQ service never logged; the
service answered the other two. Unauthenticated concurrency and keep-alive reuse
both pass, so the fault needs an authenticated read to show itself, and the
gateway's log is full of "Session error ... end of stream" for that client across
the whole session, before this work as well as after it.

The reads are serialised in =readCoverage()= as a mitigation, with the reason in
the code, and that serialisation is what the last acceptance point removes.
