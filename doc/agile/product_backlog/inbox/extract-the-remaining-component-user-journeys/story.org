* Goal

Every component left after the extraction standard and the ores.refdata
pass has its user journeys documented under the
[[id:AFE1E3F9-F687-4CA7-AA6D-C9AAF727D00D][User Journey Extraction
Standard]], and every action its legacy Qt screens offered is covered by
a journey or recorded as dropped: analytics, the application shell,
compute, dq, iam, marketdata, reporting, scheduler, synthetic, trading,
workflow and workspace.

This story was split from
[[id:28844DEF-E9AD-45B9-A6E0-CFF51CA1A064][Extract the user journeys that
replace the ores.qt screens]] when that story was reworded to the
standard and the ores.refdata journeys it delivered and closed DONE at
the sprint 26 close on 2026-10-05; its thirteen remaining component tasks
moved here.
