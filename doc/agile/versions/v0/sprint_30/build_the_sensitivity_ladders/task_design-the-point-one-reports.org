* Plan

Read the engine first and the store second, because the .1 family is a name for
something the engine already does. Read the whole sensitivity schema rather than
the example that looks closest, because the family is as wide as the schema and
only the vocabulary comes from the example. Then measure what the =Delta= column
actually holds before designing anything that divides by a shift size, since a
Greek written as a quotient and a Greek written as a value change lead to
different reports.

The work is docs and tasks only: no code, no seeds, no definitions. The build is
sequenced so that each unit ends in a state a reviewer can check, and so that the
plumbing lands before the reports that depend on it:

1. [[id:8A951C62-4081-4CF1-9051-862533FA17B9][Bind a sensitivity
   configuration to a report definition]] — without this, every later run shifts
   nothing.
2. [[id:D168771B-BC57-46D7-93B5-F9FA10A4A4B7][Seed the sensitivity shift
   families]] — the content the binding carries.
3. [[id:FABF9F45-2B9B-4844-9200-DEFECB81CC69][Build the rates delta and gamma
   ladders]] — the first ladder, over data the store already holds.
4. [[id:AFA116AA-0FF8-4FA0-88B4-1DF2F26022F5][Build the par-rate DV01 and
   theta ladders]] — the two one-figure ladders off that same run.
5. [[id:3DD3449A-CF81-458D-BE3D-07951A1E0EC2][Build the .1 ATM and surface vol
   ladders]] — the vol pair, waiting on market data.
6. [[id:0C0F9826-403E-4C62-B03F-050888E9F88B][Derive the risk reversal and
   strangle ladders]] — derived from unit 5, not asked of the engine.
7. [[id:CC276A2C-4676-4DE3-8114-C496CAB0C082][Build the cross-gamma ladder]] —
   last, because it is the widest and the least load-bearing.

Units 1 to 4 need no new market data. Units 5 to 7 wait on

* Result

The design note is [[id:D9D59BD1-CAC0-4B4A-B32D-A734A4D79AB0][Greek ladder
reports]]. It states what a .1 report is in the engine's terms, the full ladder
family across every risk class and order, what each ladder needs, what the store
holds and lacks, the ordered report list, six decisions and five open questions.

Six findings decide the design, and each is cited on the note:

- The engine has no .1. It has a shift type, a shift size, a shift scheme and a
  bucket list, and a .1 report is a report bound to one of those sizes.
- The =Delta= column is the value change for the configured shift, not a
  derivative, and it is not divided by the shift size. This was measured rather
  than assumed.
- The risk reversal and the strangle are not engine factors. ORE shifts an
  ATM-only grid or a strike-by-strike surface, so the two ladders are a change
  of basis over the surface points.
- =crossgamma.csv= has no writer at 1.8.17.0, though the cross gammas are
  present in =sensitivity.csv= and =scenario.csv=.
- Theta is a risk factor key of its own, not a mode of a rates run.
- The store names the sensitivity document and switches the analytic on in nine
  risk report configurations, and no configuration binds a document, so a
  sensitivity run today shifts nothing.
