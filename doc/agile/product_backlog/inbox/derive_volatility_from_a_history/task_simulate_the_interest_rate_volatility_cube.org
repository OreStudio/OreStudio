* Goal

A latent cube for each currency, and the vendor views of it, so that a party has
quotes to calibrate from and the difference between two vendors is the noise each one
adds.

* Acceptance

- A latent cube per currency exists over expiry and term (the ATM matrix) and over strike (the smile), and its ATM cell at the as-of equals the derived volatility for that expiry and term.
- The cube evolves: each step moves its level and its smile parameters by a stated process, and the derived level is where it starts.
- Each contributor quotes the cube with noise of its own, and the difference between a contributor's quote and the latent cell is the noise the run recorded.
- The quotes travel as swaption and cap/floor series under each contributor's own ids, mapped to the =oresmd= items their keys name, as the FX surfaces' ids are mapped.
- The latent series is stored under the system provider =latent= and no tenant reads it.

* Notes

- The FX precedent is =fx_vol_surface_feed= with =fx_vol_contributor= and =fx_vol_view=: a latent surface, contributors with noise of their own, and an adapter per vendor. The clean contributor =demo= records the latent surface itself as market ticks, so a test compares a view against the truth.
- The seed of the cube is the derivation, not a vendor quote: this task is the first place the platform quotes an interest rate volatility it made itself.
