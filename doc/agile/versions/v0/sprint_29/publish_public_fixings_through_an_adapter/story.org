* Goal

A synthetic feed that starts takes the most recent rate that a central bank or public body published, and every publisher it can draw on is a market data provider like any other, with its terms accepted, its vendor ids mapped, and its fixings stored for good.

* Acceptance

- A durable tick class keeps the FX fixings with no age gate and no expiry, in a table of its own, and a backfill in order is stored.
- Every provider carries its terms, and no public provider is fetched until the system administrator accepts them.
- The public sources adapter speaks the adapter contract, fetches through Frankfurter one provider at a time, backfills the window on start and polls daily.
- The catalogue holds every daily Frankfurter provider whose terms are read and allow storage, so the main pairs are covered by several providers.
- A reader returns the latest fixing of an item on or before a date, and a window, each with its provider and time.
- A synthetic feed starts from the latest fixing of its best-ranked item, and the screen shows the provider and the time of the value.

* Decisions

- A public source is an adapter that publishes ticks, not a DQ dataset (2026-10-28). DQ is for canned data, a snapshot that stays fixed. A public source publishes every day, and a feed that starts again should take the newest value. DQ keeps the canned vintages.
- Each publisher is a provider of kind =reference=, with a source of kind =live=. Frankfurter is an access route, a fetcher, and not a provider (2026-10-28).
- The item is an FX fixing, =FX-SOURCE-CCY1-CCY2=, so the publisher is part of the identity and the durable rule, keyed by instrument type, leaves live spot alone (2026-10-28).
- One adapter serves every public source, with Frankfurter as its first fetcher (2026-10-28).
- The durable fixings are kept for good, in a table of their own (2026-10-28).
- The catalogue starts wide: every daily Frankfurter provider whose terms allow storage, so each main pair has several providers from the first day (2026-10-28).
- It supersedes the task [[id:74F33C8C-AF28-4A6E-812A-A2D443A77E40][Load published quotes from the public sources]], whose five new tables are not built. The design and its history are in [[id:F4BBEF34-D84C-4AE6-A3AA-DC47C3EE06EE][Public sources publish daily fixings through an adapter]].

* Out of scope

- The curve sources: the US Treasury par curve, SOFR, SONIA and €STR. They are later fetchers of the same adapter.
- The monthly, weekly and quarterly providers (the BIS, Japan Customs, the US Treasury reporting rate), until the class policy says how stale such a fixing may be.
- A Frankfurter provider with no terms page of its own, until its own site is read.
