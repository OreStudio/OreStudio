* Result

=refdata.v1.parties.list-of-tenant= is removed with its model, service,
handler, registrar, tests, shell command, recipe and script, because a request
that names a tenant breaks the protocol's tenancy rule. =GET /api/parties=
pages the session's own parties through =refdata.v1.parties.list= with the
server total and refuses system administration, whose tenant the party policy
lets read every tenant's parties. The *Parties* screen pages them with the
pager the roster now shares, and entering a tenant lands on it. The tenant's
screen in system administration reads no party and points inside. Both tenancy
journeys say where the parties are read.

Tests: two wire cases, three BFF route cases, two screen cases, and the tenant
screen's cases rewritten. Live, the parties of Acme read 2, 2 and 1 across
three pages with a total of 5, and system administration was refused.
