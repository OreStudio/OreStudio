#+title: Northwind Traders dataset generator
#+description: How the Northwind Traders demonstration tenant's populate SQL is generated, and where its source rows come from.

Northwind Traders is ORE Studio's second demonstration tenant. This directory
generates the SQL that registers and stages its data, under
=projects/ores.sql/populate/northwind/=.

* The source

The company is the classic Northwind Traders sample: a fictional specialty-food
importer and exporter. The rows that describe the company's people and its
trading partners are vendored under =source/=, extracted from the sample's
PostgreSQL dump. Six tables are kept:

| File | Rows | What it provides |
|------+------+------------------|
| =employees.csv= | 9 | Names, titles, addresses, phones, cities and the reporting line |
| =customers.csv= | 91 | The countries the company sells to |
| =suppliers.csv= | 29 | The countries the company buys from |
| =categories.csv= | 8 | Kept for reference; no dataset uses it |
| =region.csv= | 4 | Kept for reference; no dataset uses it |
| =territories.csv= | 53 | Kept for reference; no dataset uses it |

The binary columns (=photo=, =picture=) and the =photo_path= column are dropped
from the vendored tables. The photographs themselves are Northwind's own
templates, generated under =northwind_staff_photo:*= from the facestudio faces
the generator selects.

* What the source decides, and what it does not

The sample decides everything about the nine people: their names, job titles,
addresses, phone numbers, cities and who reports to whom. The countries the
company's customers and suppliers trade from become its =party_countries=.

It decides nothing else. The sample is a food wholesaler, so its product
categories do not describe an ORE book, and its customers are not the banks a
treasury faces. The business units, portfolios, books and tradeable
counterparties are therefore Northwind's own:

- Six business units, from the sample's reporting line: executive, trading,
  treasury, operations, and a London and a Seattle trading desk.
- Six portfolios and seven books, named for what ORE's trade population writes
  into them.
- Twenty-four GLEIF counterparties, the same set every deployment publishes.

The company's registered office is the London office its London staff work
from, because the sample states no company address.

* The legal entity

The company's LEI is synthetic, on the =9695= test prefix ACME uses, with
ISO 17442 mod-97 check digits computed by the generator. A fictional company
has no entry in the public register, and the party has to come from somewhere.

* The configuration documents and the reports

The pricing-engine, curve, today's-market, risk-config, engine-settings and
report-definition documents are the ones ACME Corporation already ships. A
document names no legal entity except in its title and description, so the
generator reads ACME's UK records, renames them and writes them under
=northwind.*= dataset codes. One source for those documents means a correction
to one of them reaches both tenants.

* Regenerating

#+begin_src sh
cd projects/ores.seeder/datasets/northwind_traders
python3 generate_sql.py
#+end_src

The generator rewrites every file in =projects/ores.sql/populate/northwind/=
and nothing else. Do not edit that directory by hand.

The seed profile that provisions the tenant — its steps, its arguments and the
demonstration password — is hand-written, in
=projects/ores.sql/populate/iam/iam_seed_profiles_populate.sql=. The recipe that
runs the tenant at a deployment is
=doc/recipes/shell/provisioning/northwind_traders_provision.org=.
