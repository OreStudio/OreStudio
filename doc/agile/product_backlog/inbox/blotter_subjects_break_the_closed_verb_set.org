* What

The blotter model in =projects/ores.trading/modeling/ores.trading.blotter.org= declares the subjects =trading.v1.blotter.rail= and =trading.v1.blotter.page=. The verbs =rail= and =page= are not in the closed set of eight, and they are not in the reserved ops namespace. Rename the two verbs to verbs in the closed set, or change the subject protocol to accept them.

* Why

Two tests in =test_check_subject_conformance.py= fail on main: =test_the_committed_tree_is_clean_against_its_baseline= and =test_no_declared_subject_violates_the_protocol=. The =codegen-tests= gate check is red for every branch until this is fixed. The subjects came from PR #4403 (merge b903acb430).
