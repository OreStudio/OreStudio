* Plan

** What a refdata row is made of, measured first

A census of the member types of the component's generated structs, over its 688
domain headers, is 24 distinct types. Twenty-two of them already have a carrier:
the strings, the integers, the truth values, the uuids, the instants, the dates,
the tenant ids and the decimals, and the optionals of each. So the component is
much closer to the move than dq was, and the census says exactly what is
missing:

- =double=, in three structs directly and in thirteen as an optional. That is
  what a rate or a weight is, and it had no carrier.
- The component's *field-group* structs -- =convention_identity=,
  =convention_payload= and the =tenor_basis_swap_*= parts -- which are members of
  other types and are rendered by the field-group templates.
- =ores::dq::domain::audit_record=, a field group another component owns, which
  is reached as a member of three refdata types.

There are no enums among them: a coded value in this component travels as its
code, a string, which the carrier already covers.

** The order that follows

A carrier first, because nothing else compiles without it -- and the doubles'
proof is the same one every other carrier carries, a byte comparison against the
reflector. Then a shape for the field groups, which is the same move the
protocol header got: state =wire_fields= where the struct is declared, from the
iteration that writes its members. Then the models opt in, one entity at a time,
and the suites say what the move changed.

* Notes

The doubles' carrier landed first, because the census put it at the root of
everything else: an integer and a truth value had been added for dq, a real had
not. The proof is the one every carrier carries -- the bytes are reflect-cpp's
for the same value -- and it held for =0.1=, for a negative and for =1e300=,
which is the answer to the question the shortest-round-trip spelling could have
made awkward.

Next: the field groups. =cpp_field_group.hpp.mustache= declares those structs and
states no shape for them, so a refdata type that holds one has nothing to
recurse into. That is the same change the protocol header got, and it is a
change to a template every component renders, so it wants the same care: a gate,
and a proof that a component that has not opted in renders the header it has.
