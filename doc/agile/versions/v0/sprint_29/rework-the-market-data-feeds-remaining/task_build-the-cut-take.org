* Acceptance

- The take flow assembles the body from the reads that exist, names each gap, and writes nothing until the take.
- The take writes the cut with the kind's retention and raises the approval request of the kind's approval kind, with the cut's detail keyed by the request.
- The cut record shows the acts and links the request.
- The screens and the tests are green, and a cut taken live is recorded and requests its authorisation.

* Plan

The work follows the platform's own order: the model, then the store, then the
operations, and the screens last.

1. The cut's approval detail is a codegen entity in the reporting component: the
   cut the request authorises, the counts of the body's parts as the take
   counted them, and the applied facts. Its `request_id` is the natural key, so
   a request authorises one body, and it is client read only, so the take writes
   it and the apply stamps it.
2. The assembly is a pure function over the facts the reads answer: a part is
   closed when a read answered it and a gap stands with the item, the reason and
   the remedy when it did not. That keeps the rule that a gap stops the press
   testable without a database.
3. The reads are the components' own operations, called with the caller's
   Authorization header: trading's list at the as-of, market data's published
   sets, compute's app versions, and this component's own configuration rows.
4. The take is one operation, `reporting.v1.ops.take_cut`, serving the preview
   and the press as the resolution serves its own two callers. It writes the cut
   through `plan_take` and raises the request in the same transaction as the
   body, with the detail row keyed by the request.
5. The record read answers the detail beside the gaps, so the page states who
   waits without reading an inbox it may not see.
6. The screens: the take flow with its rail, the list's take button gated on the
   permission, and the authorisation tab's subject panel.

* Notes

** The environment holds no published curve set

`ores_marketdata_curve_publishes_tbl` is empty, so the market data part of every
body stands as a gap and no hand take in this environment can reach the press
that raises a request. That is the design's own gap ("a series with no value at
or before the instant"), drawn with its remedy, and the flow states it. The
request path itself was proven by the integration suite against the same
database; the evidence file names the rows.

** The take's reads run as the person

The caller's Authorization header travels with the parts' reads, so a person who
may take a cut must be able to read what the body names: the engine version is
compute's read, and the subject a request names is the detail row's. Both are
granted where the take is, to the Finance role.

** The assembly states what the store does not keep, and does not gap on it

No read materialises a copy of a deal population, a fixed market data snapshot,
or the revision of the services that assembled the body. A body that refused
over each of them could never be taken, so each part says so beside itself
(`snapshot_note`) and the take records the parts it answered. A gap stands only
where a read answered "nothing there" — the design's own gap table.

* Result

The hand take and the authorisation it raises are built and verified as far as
the environment allows.

- The take: reporting.v1.ops.take_cut serves the assembly and the press, gated by
  reporting::cuts:take. The assembly reads the parts the store answers (trading's
  list at the as-of, the published sets, compute's app versions, the component's
  configuration versions) through the caller's session, and records every part it
  cannot fill as a cut_gap with item, reason and remedy.
- The take writes through plan_take (the kind's retention, destination and
  authorisation basis copied; ordinal, taker and instant set). A body with a gap
  is kept as an incomplete draft and raises nothing.
- The approval raise: where the kind names an approval kind, the request is raised
  in the same transaction, with a new reporting detail entity keyed by the request
  id (the cut, the counts of the parts, the applied facts empty). The record read
  answers the detail.
- The screens: the four-step take flow, the list's take button for the permission
  holder, the Authorisation tab's subject panel and request link.
- The seeds: the detail read permission, the taker's engine-version read, and the
  demo's Finance arm for henry.jones; applied live additively.
- Tests: 14 new C++ cases (237 cases/1321 assertions in the suite), BFF 1192,
  web 2389.
- Live: as henry.jones the assembly answered four of five parts and stood one gap
  (the environment holds no published curve set), the press wrote the incomplete
  draft with the kind's retention, and the record and history read back. The
  request path is proven against the same database by the suite (a waiting
  reporting.eod_cut_authorisation with its detail: deals 1284, sets 7, gaps 0).
- The branch was rebased on main after main's own synthetic repair landed; the
  two local synthetic repair commits were dropped.

Remaining: the decide and the apply (the inbox decides, the reporting side stamps
by reconciliation); the report's cut selection and the LIVE mint UI; and a
published curve set in the environment, without which a hand take cannot raise a
request live.

* Result

The take exists and it raises the authorisation it is for.

- The subject of a cut's approval request is a codegen entity in the reporting
  component: the cut, the counts of the body's parts, and the applied facts. It
  is client read only, its request id is its natural key, and its SQL is applied
  to the development database additively and carried in the seeds.
- The assembly answers five parts — the kind and its retention, the deal
  population, the published sets in use, the versions in force, and the engine
  and the code — and records every part it could not fill as a gap with the
  item, the reason and the remedy. The rule is a pure function, so the design's
  "a gap stops the press" is tested without a store.
- The take is one operation, `reporting.v1.ops.take_cut`, serving the flow's
  preview and its press. It writes the body through `plan_take` — the kind's
  retention, its destination and its authorisation basis copied, the take
  ordinal, the taker and the instant set — and, where the kind names an approval
  kind, raises the request in the same transaction with the cut's detail row
  keyed by it. A body with a gap is kept as an incomplete draft that raises
  nothing, and a kind that names no approval kind records that none was required.
- The record read answers the detail row beside the gaps, so the page states who
  waits without reading the inbox.
- The BFF serves the take on one route and the record's subject, and the web
  draws the four-step flow, the list's gated take button, and the authorisation
  tab's subject panel.
- The permission is `reporting::cuts:take`, checked at the handler; the deciders
  are the kind's own, `inbox::approvals:decide_finance` for the close.

Left to the next unit: the decide and the apply. The inbox decides, and the
reporting side stamps the cut's authorisation by reconciliation over the
request's state — the detail row's `applied_at` is the marker it will write.

Left open by the environment: a published curve set, without which no hand take
can raise a request here; and the run's cut selection, which is the report
journey's.
