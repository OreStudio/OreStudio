* Goal

The web UI has a Quotes screen, a Fixing series page that shows one item opened by its URI, and a Public rates screen that shows only the official publishers. Nothing lists the fixings. Add a system-level Fixings screen beside Quotes, for the super administrator, that lists the FX and IR fixing observations across every source: the index or pair, the source (TR20H, ECB, a curve's index), the publisher or generator, the latest value and its date, and how many days the series holds. A row opens the Fixing series page. The screen shows observations, which are system-level facts, and holds no authorisation; a party's fixings manager, which adopts observations for the party's trades, is a separate piece of work. See [[id:FA5C96A3-1F8E-4F9B-8973-40D92055D7DD][System-level fixing observations and party-level fixings]].

* Acceptance

- The super administrator sees every FX and IR fixing series the store holds, across every source, filterable by asset class, currency and source.
- The screen offers no authorisation or override.
- Each row shows the latest value, its date and the count of days, and opens the Fixing series page.
- A fixing series with no values shows as empty, not hidden.
