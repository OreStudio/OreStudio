* Decisions

- *The masters publish their own images.* Each dataset's master include
  publishes to the system tenant at the end, mirroring
  =populate/assets/system_avatars_populate.sql=. The country publish then
  reads a flag that is already live, whatever loads the dataset.
- *The masters are edited directly, not through codegen.* The flag
  archetype is retired (3ecbdb8b15) and the batch-execute master archetype
  is bound to no dataset model, so nothing regenerates either master today.
  The two bespoke =*_generate_metadata_sql.py= scripts still name the
  pre-rename =populate_flags.sql= output and emit the old metadata format,
  so they are stale and are deliberately left alone.

* Out of scope

- The stale =flags_generate_metadata_sql.py=, =crypto_generate_metadata_sql.py=
  and =ip2country_generate_metadata_sql.py= master output names. Fixing them
  is part of the dataset-pipeline migration, not this hotfix.
