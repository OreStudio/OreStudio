* Summary

The first round settled how a person chooses the vendors: the source rail.
Built, the screen had one row for each vendor's quote of each point. A vol
surface of 65 points quoted by four vendors became 260 rows, and the board of
UK plc held 4,373 rows, most of them a derived surface an hour old. This round
asks how the screen shows a series.

* Detail

** What was wrong with the built screen

A review of the built screen on 2026-11-19 found these, with the screenshots of
the live screen as the evidence:

- The points sort as text: 1M, 1W, 1Y, 2M. The term structure is lost.
- A surface's point is one text cell, "1M 10BF", so the smile and the term
  structure cannot be read.
- The by-series group mixes the vendors' rows, and its closed row shows the
  first point, not the newest, with no vendor.
- Opening a group adds its 65 rows to a page of 50.
- The order puts the stale derived surfaces first; the ticking spots are pages
  away, and finding "EUR USD" shows 260 option rows before the spot.
- Values have no unit: a vol is 0.0592, a risk reversal 0.0000002144968.
  The change has its own precision and no pips, bp or vol points.
- The comparison chart's x axis counts samples, not time, so vendors that tick
  at different rates do not line up; its low label is cut; two vendors at
  different levels flatten each other.
- The mid trusts every vendor: the demo feed's EUR/USD at 0.8668 against
  bloomberg's 1.1062 gives a mid of 0.986.
- The left column headers sit right of their data, and the layout toggle's first
  button is named "Layout By series".

** The rules every variant shares

- A vendor is a column, not a row. More vendors widen a row; they never
  multiply the rows.
- Points sort by tenor: 1W, 1M, 2M, 3M, 6M, 9M, 1Y, 18M, 2Y.
- Each quote type has its unit. FX spot shows the pair's decimals and changes
  in pips; rates show % and changes in bp; vols show vol points.
- A quote is measured against the median of three or more live vendors, or,
  with fewer, against the highest-ranked vendor by the providers' precedence,
  the rule the FX forward sets already use. A quote far from it (50 pips, 25
  bp, 0.4 vol points) is flagged, left out of the mid and left off the chart's
  axis. A stale vendor is dimmed and left out of the mid.
- The rail includes or excludes a vendor, with a tick box, instead of picking
  one. Leaving one out removes it from every row, mid and chart.
- The order is live first, with A–Z as the other choice.
- A spot's chart is the last five minutes on a time axis, as a level or as the
  distance to the mid.

** The variants

Switch with the bar at the foot, or =?variant=.

| Variant | Name                          | The idiom |
|---------+-------------------------------+-----------|
| =a=     | Series list and detail        | One row for each series. A spot row shows each vendor's value; a curve or surface row shows how much of it each vendor quotes, how fresh, and how many of its quotes are outliers, with the widest spread on any point. The picked series opens above the list in its own shape: a spot's all-quotes table and chart, a curve's tenor strip beside the curve drawn by tenor, a surface's tenor by strategy grid that shows the mid, one vendor or the spread as a heat map. |
| =b=     | Datum rows, series headings   | The closest to the built screen. Each point is one row with a column for each vendor, under a heading for its series. A curve opens as its tenor rows; a surface opens as its grid, not its 65 rows. The picked row opens the all-quotes panel above. |
| =c=     | Boards by asset class         | Tabs, each with the board its desk reads. FX spot is a board of pairs by vendor beside the picked pair's quotes. Rates puts every curve side by side, tenors down, for the mid or one vendor. FX vols is a surface list beside the picked surface's grid. |

** Comparing two sources of a surface

A surface's /Compare/ mode answers "where and how do two vendors' surfaces
differ", the question the single-source grid cannot. It follows how the
terminals and the consensus services do it, from memory and not checked: the
terminals overlay one source's smile and term structure on another's, and the
consensus services show a contributor's surface against the consensus as a
grid of differences.

- Two pickers, A and B, each a vendor or the consensus, with a swap. The
  consensus leaves out the vendor it is compared with, so a vendor is never
  measured against itself.
- A difference grid, tenor down: each cell is A − B in vol points, blue where A
  is above B and red where below, bold with ⚠ over 0.4. A line above it gives
  the mean ATM difference, the widest cell and the count over the limit.
- /Vol by delta/ (10P, 25P, ATM, 25C, 10C) or /As quoted/ (ATM, RR, BF). A
  risk reversal difference is a skew difference, not a level, so the default
  reads the surface by delta.
- The smile of the picked tenor and the term structure of a chosen measure,
  every source drawn, A and B strong, the consensus dashed.
- A picked cell lists every source's value of it and its distance to B.

The vols by delta must come from the server. The conversion depends on the
pair's delta convention (spot or forward delta, premium-adjusted or not, which
ATM) and on a fitted smile; the prototype stands in with the simple strangle
approximation, 25C = ATM + 25BF + 25RR/2, and labels the view as the server's.

=?pick=<series id>= opens a series, such as =spot-EURUSD=, =swap-USD-1D= or
=vol-EUR/USD=; =?tab== chooses variant c's tab; =?surface=compare= opens a surface in compare
mode.

** What is fake

Every value, time and age is mock, from a seeded random walk. The pairs,
curves and surfaces echo the board of UK plc on 2026-11-19: the spots of
bloomberg and demo, the swap curves of bloomberg and tradeweb, the 13 by 5 FX
vol surfaces of bloomberg, ice, tradeweb and the derived source. The demo
feed's wrong EUR/USD and USD/CHF are copied from the live board. The curve and
surface names ("USD swaps · SOFR") are what the screen would derive from the
series; the raw series name ("USD 0D 1D") stays as the subtitle.
