#+title: Read the outputs the samples do not ship
#+description: The file_type seed declares 55 output parameters. The checked-in samples ship the file of 17 of them, so the catalogue names 17 families with a parameter and 43 more by the engine's convention. The 38 parameters whose file no sample ships are: jacobi, jacobi_inverse and crossgamma (sensitivity and xvaSensitivity), productclass_mapping, calibration and its pca and meanReversion outputs, creditcurves, crif, marketObjects and riskFactors (portfolioDetails), the scenarioGeneration and simulation scenario dumps and cubes (scenariodata, cube, cptyCube, nettingSetCube, amcBuffer), timeaveragedexposure, dim_evolution and dim_regression, additionalResults, and sacva's net sensitivity file. Their columns live only in the writers, so reading them means deriving the shape from the C++ that writes it rather than from a sample. Extend the catalogue and the readers over these, and give the gate a way to say a family has no sample rather than skipping it silently.
