* Acceptance

- The build compiles past oresmd_uri_codec.cpp.
- The reader and the writer both spell the instrument and quote through oresmd_uri_codec.

* Plan

Commit =91612d3ea5= gave =oresmd_uri_codec= the two static members
=instrument_spelling= and =quote_spelling=, and replaced the two
=lower(ore_name(...))= call sites inside =spelling_of= with them. That function
is a free function in the file's anonymous namespace, so unqualified lookup
walks out of the class and finds nothing. Clang reports it as an unknown
identifier and suggests the qualified name, which is the error the build shows.

=read()= kept its own copy of the same expression, so the header's promise — that
the spelling a reader compares against is the spelling the writer produces — was
untrue. Route both readers through the same two members.

The fix is four qualified names in one file.

* Notes

The break came in with commit =91612d3ea5= ("Project the series identity into
joinable columns"). That commit was merged on a green-looking branch and the
build ran after the merge, so the error surfaced on =main= rather than on the
branch that wrote it. Nothing else in the commit is implicated: the projector
already called the two members through the qualified name.

=spelling_of= stays a free function. Making it a static member would also fix
the lookup, and it is only ever called from member functions, but it would move
four helpers and both overloads for no gain; qualification is the smaller change.
