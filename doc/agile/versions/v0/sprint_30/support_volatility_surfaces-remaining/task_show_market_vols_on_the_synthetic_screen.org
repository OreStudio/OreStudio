* Goal

A person who runs the system's market data can see the FX volatility quotes the system publishes, as they see its spots and IR quotes. The synthetic screen shows, for each pair, the latest ATM, risk reversal and butterfly quotes by expiry, and how each moved over a range. These are the vendor's raw quotes, not a surface: a surface is a party's derived data and shows on the Evolution page once it is published. Today the derived source is the only vol source, and it derives its quotes from the fixings. The FX volatility surface generator adds synthetic contributors later, and the same view shows them.

* Acceptance

- The synthetic screen lists each pair the derived source quotes, with its latest quotes by expiry and quote kind, the as-of they carry and the source.
- A person sees how a pair's quotes moved over a range they choose.
- A pair with no quote says so, rather than showing an empty grid.
- Once the generator exists, its contributors show in the same view, each named, beside the derived source.
- The view is in English, French and Portuguese, and tested.
