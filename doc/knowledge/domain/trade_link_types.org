#+title: Trade link types
#+description: The catalogue of trade link types: what each one connects, the role of each end, which process creates it, and whether it carries an economic effect.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:domain:finance:trading:trade_links:
#+created: 2026-10-02
#+updated: 2026-10-02

* Summary

A link type names the reason two trades are joined. The trade model
carries the types below; each row says what the link connects, the
role each end plays, what causes it, and which page owns the detail.
One property sorts them: the economic effect. A type with an effect is
a trade event, and a type without is an annotation. Adding a reason
means adding a row here, not a special case in code.

Some collections of trades used to be filed here and are not links:
structures, merges, compressions, allocations and process runs. Each
is a [[id:2AF58405-4F3D-4B74-AAD0-6A97E7DA215F][group]] with an identity of its own, and its trades
reference the group rather than each other. The last section lists
them and where they now live.

* Detail

** How to read the catalogue

[[id:2AAC92EB-2320-4385-999E-947E9E8FE5C5][Trade links]] states the
principle: a link with an economic effect is a trade event, and a link
with no economic effect is an annotation. The tables below follow that
split. The first table's links cut a version of the trades they join,
and some of them need a confirmation. The second table's links are
recorded, displayed and reported, and they change no term.

A link is directed. The /from/ end is the trade the link starts at and
the /to/ end the trade it reaches, and the roles column names both.

** Notes

/On novation./ Novation changes the parties rather than the terms.
Under the ISDA Master Agreement a party may not transfer its interest
without the prior written consent of the other party, and the 2005 ISDA
Novation Protocol sets a same-day consent process between the
transferor, the transferee and the remaining party. The substance is
that a novation is a new legal relationship, not an edit to an existing
one, and a system that models it as an edit loses the audit trail. Our
model treats it as a link between two trades, directed from the
transferred trade to the trade with the new counterparty. The firm's
own side is just as fixed: a trade that must move to a book owned by
another legal entity is closed out and rebooked in the new book, and
the two trades are linked (see [[id:07CEDE6A-21B7-4F87-9745-708FDCA8C362][Book lifecycle]]).

/On exercise./ Exercising an option ends it, or reduces it, and may
create a trade. A physically settled FX option exercised into an FX
forward or spot is the common case: the option is the /exercised/ end
and the new trade the /resulting/ end. A partial exercise reduces the
option's notional by amendment and links it to the trade generated for
the exercised amount. An option that settles in cash creates no trade;
its settlement is [[id:AAF5F9CB-D854-40A8-AEB5-50D998A9F0C0][linked cash]]. FpML calls the two ends the
originating and the resulting trade.

/On misbooking correction./ A correction cancels the erroneous trade
and books a new one, and the link runs from the correction back to the
original. It replaces the single =successor_trade_id= field: that field
named one kind of relation on one side of it, while the link names its
reason and both ends. [[id:8BC3A226-6DD8-49C8-959A-F1AAC97A3F97][Trade Versioning]] sets out the
cancel-and-rebook sequence.

/Netting has several senses./ Merging trades into one is an operation
with an identity, covered by [[id:BBD96309-7C59-4D94-BDA7-27EAB7A8612E][Trade merge]]. Netting
settlement amounts is a payment mechanic: it nets cash flows, not
trades, and it carries no link. Credit netting is the
[[id:5F12A37F-9F26-4B49-BAAF-1BAA7B2BB94F][netting set]].

/Split has two senses./ Allocating a block splits it into child trades,
an operation covered by [[id:73766E2D-D01B-4628-A1A0-78C9BBF709F6][Trade allocation]]. Separately, the
blotter's /Link Split/ breaks an existing link.

/A move within the boundary is not a link./ A trade that moves to a
book with the same legal entity, branch, regulatory book and accounting
classification keeps its identity: the move amends its booking and
creates no trade and no link. See [[id:6CB4879F-3F4B-48CE-A01E-E1B9AEE866B0][Book moves]].

/Restructure is not a link./ A restructure renegotiates the terms of
the same trade, so it is an amendment that cuts a new version; see
[[id:8BC3A226-6DD8-49C8-959A-F1AAC97A3F97][Trade Versioning]]. A roll is
a link only where it closes the trade and books a replacement.

/Expiry and downsizing are not links./ Expiry moves a trade to
=expired= and generates nothing; a partial close-out reduces the
notional of the same trade by amendment. Where expiry produces a
settlement, the resulting cash is [[id:AAF5F9CB-D854-40A8-AEB5-50D998A9F0C0][linked cash]]. Exercise
into a new trade is the exception, and has its own row.

** Groups, not links

These collections have an identity of their own: an operation attaches
to the set of trades as a whole, and the business refers to the
collection by name. Under [[id:2AF58405-4F3D-4B74-AAD0-6A97E7DA215F][trade sets, groups and links]]
each is a group. Its trades reference the group, and each trade's role
in it is recorded with the reference.

| Group | Economic effect | Roles of its trades | Detail |
|-------+-----------------+---------------------+--------|
| Structure | Yes; a change to a leg is a change to the deal | leg, by its role in the structure | [[id:FC31FC9C-7A6E-4EFD-8A62-6DE4137F7160][Trade Structures and Deal Composition]] |
| Merge | Yes; the contributors close and the result opens | contributor, result, bridge | [[id:BBD96309-7C59-4D94-BDA7-27EAB7A8612E][Trade merge]] |
| Compression | Yes; trades terminate or reduce | terminated, partially terminated, replacement | [[id:9BC498E0-91ED-4CA4-A7D7-7EF15684E799][Trade compression]] |
| Allocation | None for the firm's risk; the counterparty moves to the allocations | block, allocation, bridge | [[id:73766E2D-D01B-4628-A1A0-78C9BBF709F6][Trade allocation]] |
| Funding roll | None; a funding mechanic | generated | [[id:5CC47697-2655-4ECD-B04D-C6C08FF620D5][Tom/Next]] |
| Sweep run | None; a cash-management mechanic | generated | [[id:53841E4A-C5F3-4B7A-A269-45B8010C708A][Spot Sweep]] |
| Cleardown | None; a period-end reset | generated | [[id:DFC04627-FB0F-4FFF-973E-E3EC9E05AD87][Central remittance]] — see its cleardown section |
