* Goal

=postgres_listener_service::listen_loop= sleeps only when a poll returns no
notifications, so a backlog drains at the speed the socket can be read.

* Acceptance

- =postgres_listener_service_drains_a_burst_of_notifications= sends 20,000
  notifications in one statement and requires them drained within 10 seconds;
  it fails on the old loop.
- Every =[postgres_listener]= test passes.
- With the marketdata service running, a whole-corpus import leaves no
  listener backend in =ClientWrite= once the import ends.

* Plan

Diagnosis, from the evidence of 2026-10-02:

1. Two other environments' =DROP DATABASE= stalled on =ProcSignalBarrier=. The
   blocker was prime_origin's marketdata listener backend, in =ClientWrite=.
2. The =LISTEN ores_refdata_crm_enabled_derived_pairs= shown for that backend
   was only its last statement. After the 18:57 restart the service ran no CRM
   refresh at all, so the CRM channel was not the cause.
3. The service's journal held 566,867 =market_observation_event= lines from
   19:47 until it was stopped at 20:14, at a steady 10,000 to 26,000 a minute:
   the notifications of this environment's corpus import tests, one per row.
4. A test with a callback that only counts drained 20,000 notifications in
   43,463 ms, about 460 a second: the cap is the loop, not the work done per
   notification.
5. The loop paused 100 ms after every poll, whether or not the poll found
   anything, and a poll reads only what the socket holds at that moment.

Fix: pause only after a poll that found nothing.

* Notes

The =LISTEN ores_refdata_crm_enabled_derived_pairs= shown against the stuck
backend sent the first reading toward the CRM refresh, which also runs on the
listener thread. The journal ruled it out: no CRM refresh ran after the
restart. The column is the connection's last statement, not the channel it
was blocked on.

During the incident a check of =pg_stat_activity= with a bare =psql= printed
nothing because it had no password, and that silence was first read as "no
backend". =compass sql=, or =psql= with the environment's =.env= loaded, is the
check that works.
