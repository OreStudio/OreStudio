* Goal

Each child that a =* Delete cascade= section names gets a foreign key of its
own with =:on_parent_remove: cascade=, and the section is removed. The
generated delete rules then come from one source. Today 8 models name 111
child tables, and only 6 of those children declare a foreign key back.

* Notes

- Many of the 111 children are the instrument tables of the trading families,
  keyed by the instrument's trade id. Check how each child names its parent
  before writing its link.
- The bond instrument's =:cascade_orphan:= on its issue is the reverse shape:
  the child takes a shared parent when it is the last to name it. Keep it, and
  decide whether it becomes a fourth behaviour.
