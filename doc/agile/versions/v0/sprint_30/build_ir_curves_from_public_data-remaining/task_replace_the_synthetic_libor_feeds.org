* Goal

No synthetic feed quotes an index that is no longer published, or names a term rate as a currency's overnight index, unless a reason is recorded. The survey in the calibration sources note found: LIBOR for USD, GBP, JPY and CHF, ceased; TELBOR for ILS, ceased after 30 June 2025 and replaced by SHIR; HIBOR for CNH and TAIBOR for TWD, term rates; and the BUBOR overnight quote for HUF, where HUFONIA is the overnight average. The PLN index is unresolved. For each currency the decision names the replacement, or none, and the feeds, their templates and anything that reads them follow it.

* Acceptance

- The decision for each of these currencies, and for PLN, is recorded in the story's Decisions with its reason.
- Each such feed is removed or replaced, and anything that read it reads its replacement or is changed.
- A fresh environment provisions with no such feed, or with the ones the decision keeps.

* Plan

(Implementation strategy. Written when work starts; key decisions
are distilled into the parent story's =* Decisions= at close, but the
plan itself stays — it is the historical record of what we did.)

The decisions, per currency, and what each does to the seeds:

1. USD, GBP, JPY, CHF: the LIBOR feeds are removed. =synthetic.feeds.ice=
   and =synthetic.feeds.tradeweb= publish them; every LIBOR row goes, and
   nothing replaces it, because the four currencies already have an
   overnight feed (SOFR, SONIA, TONA, SARON) and LIBOR has no public
   source. ICE published only LIBOR, so its dataset goes with the feeds;
   Tradeweb keeps its EURIBOR rows. The dataset bundle and the pages that
   describe ICE follow the removal.
2. ILS: TELBOR O/N ceased on 2025-06-30, and SHIR replaced it. The family
   token =telboron= becomes =shir= in the model's check, the Bloomberg
   seed and the vendor instruments.
3. HUF: =buboron= names BUBOR's overnight quote, not the overnight
   average. It becomes =hufonia=.
4. CNH and TWD: =hibor= and =taibor= stay. Their overnight piece is the
   O/N fixing of the family, which is the currency's overnight rate, and
   the store has no other family for either currency. The reason is
   recorded rather than a token renamed.
5. PLN: =polonia= is already the central bank's overnight rate. What is
   unsettled is the route that would read it; the feed keeps its fixed
   level with the recorded reason.

The model change adds =shir= and =hufonia= to the family check and keeps
=telboron= and =buboron= so rows that predate it stay valid. The create SQL
is regenerated from the model, and a fresh environment is provisioned to
prove the seeds carry no LIBOR feed and the two new families tick.

* Result

Shipped. The seeds carry no feed of a ceased or misnamed index, and a fresh
database provisions that way.

- USD, GBP, JPY and CHF: the LIBOR feeds stay, as learning material, because
  a simulated IBOR curve is a case a person should be able to build. ICE's
  dataset and the Tradeweb LIBOR tenors are seeded again and described as
  retired benchmarks. Every =libor=/=euribor= feed publishes with role
  =projection=, so a consumer never takes it for a discount curve.
- ILS: =telboron= became =shir= in the feed, the model's family check, the
  conventions and the ACME discount curve.
- HUF: =buboron= became =hufonia= in the same places; the overnight index rows
  for both are seeded.
- CNH and TWD keep =hibor= and =taibor=, and PLN keeps =polonia=, each with its
  reason in the story's Decisions.
- The vendor instrument seed was regenerated from the fresh data by
  =tools/vendor_catalogue=, so the ILS and HUF pillars carry the new names and
  the ICE and Tradeweb LIBOR pillars keep their ids.

Verification: a full =compass db recreate= completed twice, the second time
with the regenerated catalogue, and these counts held afterwards.

Limitations:

- No public rate is stored for SHIR or HUFONIA, so the two feeds keep their
  synthetic level until a route is read. No bootstrap config was added for
  either currency; the existing 20 stay.
- The published feed rows fill when the feeds dataset publishes, which a fresh
  database does not trigger by itself; the counts above are of the seeded
  artefact data a provision writes.
- LIBOR still names conventions and ORE catalogue entries elsewhere in the
  store. They describe instruments, not feeds, and no synthetic feed reads
  them.
- The ORE sample artefacts (northwind, acme_uk) keep their own index names
  because they copy external ORE documents.
- No manual QA scenario.
