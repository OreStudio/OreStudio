* Goal

The run's latent market can be seen while it runs. A person reads which factors exist, how each series loads on them, and what each driver's value is now, beside the arrival factor the Market regime tab already shows.

This is the last unmet line of [[id:9331995E-79ED-40B3-923D-524975949FB5][Build the latent market of shared Brownian factors]]: the factors and loadings are set from the shell and drive the feeds, but nothing shows them.

* Acceptance

- The market regime read answers with the run's factors, each series' loadings, and each series' driver value at the time of the read.
- A series the run does not drive reads as undriven, not as a zero.
- A run with no block answers with empty lists rather than an error.
- The Market regime tab draws the factors, the loadings and the driver values, in the three shipped languages.
- The read is taken on the scheduler's own thread, like the regime's other reads.
