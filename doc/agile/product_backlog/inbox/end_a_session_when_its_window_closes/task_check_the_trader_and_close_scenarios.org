* Acceptance

- A trader's screen left open overnight is still signed in, with the screen as left.
- A closed window is signed out inside the grace period plus a poll, and IAM shows the end time.
- A reload, a brief sleep and a short network loss do not sign anybody out.
- Idle sign-out, turned on, signs out an ordinary member and not an exempt role.
