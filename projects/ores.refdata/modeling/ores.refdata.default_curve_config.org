The settings of one =DefaultCurve= entry of a =curveconfig.xml= that do not
depend on how the curve is built, one row per =curve_definition= in the
=DefaultCurves= section. That is only its currency: everything else belongs to a
configuration, a row of =default_curve_configuration=.
