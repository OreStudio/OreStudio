This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Eleven of the 43 ORE series types the shape table defines have no oresmd
representation. Three of them are blocked only on the field work, because the
grammar is already written down in
=doc/knowledge/domain/ore_market_data_catalogue.org=:

**CAPFLOOR**, 96,790 corpus lines across three shapes the catalogue names
dimension by dimension:

| Key | Lines | Catalogue reading |
|-----+-------+-------------------|
| =CAPFLOOR/RATE_LNVOL/CHF/5Y/6M/0/0/0.03= | 78,269 | dim3=CCY, dim4=maturity, dim5=float tenor, dim6/7=shift/strip flags, dim8=strike |
| =CAPFLOOR/PRICE/EUHICPXT/10Y/C/0.00= | 18,424 | the same shape as =ZC_INFLATIONCAPFLOOR/PRICE=: index, maturity, cap-or-floor, strike |
| =CAPFLOOR/SHIFT/EUR/6M= | 97 | shift for the displaced log-normal model, keyed by currency and tenor |

The first is the largest single wireable block left in the corpus, a sixth of
everything it carries. The two flag slots are =0= on 97% of lines and =1/1= on
the rest, and the catalogue says what they are, which the corpus alone did not.

**RATING**, =RATING/TRANSITION_PROBABILITY/SP/BBB/BB/1Y= -- provider,
from-rating, to-rating, tenor. 128 lines.

**BOND_OPTION**, =BOND_OPTION/RATE_LNVOL/EUR_GENERIC/1Y/10Y/ATM= -- dim3 is a
generic index name rather than a currency, dim4 the option expiry, dim5 the bond
tenor, dim6 the strike. 154 lines.

Each needs an oresmd model addition and, where its key is not keyed by a
currency, a decision about which identifier carries it: CAPFLOOR/PRICE and
BOND_OPTION are index-keyed, so neither fits the IR identifier's entity field.

* Why

This capture replaces an earlier one that said the option and capfloor grammars
were not derivable and needed ORE's own source. That was wrong: the grammar is
in this repository, in the catalogue page, and the gap was that the coverage
work never read it. The correction is worth keeping in the record because the
catalogue is the first place to look the next time a key looks underdetermined.

What the catalogue does not settle is narrower, and is captured separately: the
futures' qualifier depth, which is a contradiction in our own shape table rather
than a missing grammar.

* Resolution

RESOLVED by [[id:92415927-9D6F-4C3D-8C4C-6E282B73FA28][Rewrite the oresmd grammar as a hand-written codec checked against ORE]]. The typed datum models all 45 ORE instrument
types, =CAPFLOOR=, =RATING= and =BOND_OPTION= among them, and the ORE key codec
reads every form of each that ORE's catalogue holds. The corpus fixture holds
4,545 distinct keys of the three types, and the codec tests match each with
ORE's reading.
