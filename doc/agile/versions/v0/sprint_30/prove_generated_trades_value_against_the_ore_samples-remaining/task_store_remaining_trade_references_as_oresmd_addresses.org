* Result

Credit legs and the FX, settlement, bond, funding, repo and leg indexing columns store an oresmd address. The conversion helpers are in =index_reference.hpp=. The corpus roundtrip passes.

The bond stub index columns, commodity legs, scripted indices, barrier monitoring indices, composite FX indices and the equity swap floating index are not converted here. They move to the underlyings and entities task, apart from the stubs, which that task also takes.
