#+description: A freshly recreated database has no materialised calendar dates, so every tenor resolution refuses with calendar_not_materialised until somebody rebuilds a calendar. Verified 2026-10-18: after compass db recreate and the ACME provisioning script, ores_refdata_calendar_dates_tbl was empty across all 122 calendars, and every preview and resolve call against any calendar refused. One rebuild fixed it: POST /api/refdata/calendars/Argentina/rebuild with endYear 2027 wrote 1461 dates spanning 2024 to 2027 and the same call then resolved. The journey's test step and the new resolve screen both therefore refuse on a brand new environment, and the refusal is correct but reads as a broken screen. Provisioning should materialise at least the calendars the seed conventions roll on, or the rebuild should run as a provisioning step, or the screens should offer the rebuild where they report the refusal.
