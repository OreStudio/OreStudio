* What

A fourth arrival law beside periodic, Poisson and Hawkes: Engle and Russell's
ACD(1,1). The expected gap is =psi_i = omega + alpha * gap_(i-1) + beta * psi_(i-1)=,
and a gap is =psi_i= times a unit-mean draw. It is a value with visible state, as
the other laws are, drawn from the series' keyed stream, and it is stable while
=alpha + beta < 1=.

* Why

Gemini's review offered it in a model selector. Hawkes clusters arrivals by
exciting an intensity; ACD clusters them by making a short gap likely to follow
a short gap. Trade durations are the data ACD was written for, and they are
what a desk would calibrate a feed to. It costs one law, its validation, its
column on the feed's Arrivals tab, and its compensator for the fit tests.
