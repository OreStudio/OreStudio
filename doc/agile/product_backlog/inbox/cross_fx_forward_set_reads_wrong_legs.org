* What

On bright_faraday, provisioned on 2026-11-18, each Acme party published 39 FX
forward sets. ACME Corporation UK plc's EUR/GBP set has a spot of 0.703757, built
from a EUR/USD base leg of 1.10615 and a GBP/USD quote leg of 1.57178, both
observed at 23:21. The same party's EUR/USD and GBP/USD sets, published a few
seconds later, read 1.161183 and 1.316153. The other three parties publish
EUR/GBP at 0.8827 to 0.8854, which agrees with their USD sets. Find where a cross
set reads its legs, and why UK plc's legs come from another source than its
direct pairs.

* Why

A published forward set is the number a trader trusts, because somebody approved
it. A cross 20% away from the market is published and auto-approved with nothing
to say it is wrong, and two screens show two EUR/USD spots for one party at one
instant. A guess, not checked: the legs follow UK plc's feed routes, and one of
them is the demo feed, whose values the quotes view shows as nonsense.
