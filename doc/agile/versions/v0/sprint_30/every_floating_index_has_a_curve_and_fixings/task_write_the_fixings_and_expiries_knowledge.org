* Result

The fixings cluster and the expiries cluster are in the corpus, each with a
structure note, and both are listed in the knowledge index. The fixings cluster
separates the system-level fixing observation from the party-level fixing, lists
the supported fixing sources by option cut, and draws the fixing process as a
state and a sequence diagram; the synthetic generation of fixings has a note of
its own in the synthetic generation section of Market Data Architecture, and the
general pages only link to it. The intraday valuation of options expiring today
is recorded with its open questions.

The Zettelkasten page states how to create a knowledge page and the house style:
academic prose, citations with academic papers preferred, titles that read like
academic paper titles, UML diagrams, and data-source neutrality. CLAUDE.md, the
doc-add and academic review skills, the knowledge contract and the knowledge
scaffold point to it. Sixty-nine existing pages with sentence or question titles
are retitled and their links repaired; the content work they still need is
recorded in the retitling capture. A compass lint for titles is being built in
compass v0.0.13.
