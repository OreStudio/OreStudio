#+created: 2026-11-02

What an ibor fallback segment falls back from and to, as ORE's =IborIndex=,
=RfrCurve=, =RfrIndex= and =Spread=. They hold one invariant: the ibor index is
the RFR index on the RFR curve plus the spread. ORE requires the ibor index and
the RFR curve, so every ibor fallback segment has this part.

A segment holds at most one row of this part, so the generated table adds the
unique index on the live rows of =(tenant_id, curve_segment_id)=.
