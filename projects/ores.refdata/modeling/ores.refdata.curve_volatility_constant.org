#+brief: The one quote a Constant volatility kind names.
#+created: 2026-10-30
#+updated: 2026-10-30

ORE's =constantVolatilityConfig= states a single quote, and nothing else of its
own. That quote is the kind's own field: the four terms every kind may state and
the =priority= are on =curve_volatility_kind=, and a row of this part exists only
when the entry holds a kind of this type.
