* Acceptance

- Eight codegen models in projects/ores.inbox/modeling generate the tables, with
  primary keys, foreign keys to IAM accounts, permissions and the system-tenant
  lookups, and row-level security on every tenant table.
- A delivery's outcome is the enum domain::delivery_outcome, stored as text
  with a database foreign key to its closed lookup, and a failed delivery says
  why while no other outcome does.
- A preference is keyed by the account, the kind and the channel together.
- A recreate seeds the two channels, the three outcomes, the
  inbox.approval_waiting and inbox.approval_decided kinds, and the
  permissions.

* Plan

- Kinds and channels use the simple-lookup profile, held by the system tenant;
  every reference to them is a foreign key with =:use_system_tenant:=.
- The delivery outcome follows trading's closed enum pattern: a tenant-less,
  immutable lookup under the read-only-current-state profile, a hand-written
  C++ enum header, and an enforced database foreign key.
- Arguments and recipients are tenant-scoped junctions keyed by the
  notification and the name or account. Junctions carry no generated drop or
  row-level security, so both are hand-written, as other components do.
- The notification and the delivery use the uuid-identified-lookup profile;
  the preference is a simple-lookup with a three-part primary key.
- =link_id=, =audience_permission_code= and =failure_reason= are nullable,
  because each is absent in a real case: a link to a whole screen, a
  notification to named people, a delivery that did not fail.

* Result

Eight models generate the notification tables: kinds and channels as
system-tenant lookups, the delivery outcome as a closed tenant-less lookup
behind the C++ enum domain::delivery_outcome, the notification, its arguments
and recipients as junctions, the delivery, and the preference keyed by account,
kind and channel together. Every tenant table has row-level security; the
junctions' policies and drop scripts are hand-written, as other components'
are.

A recreate seeds the in_app and mail channels, the three outcomes, and the
inbox.approval_waiting and inbox.approval_decided kinds. The probe in
=evidence/inbox_notifications_probe.sql= shows the database refusing an unknown
kind, audience permission, channel and outcome, a failure without a reason and
a second preference for the same account, kind and channel, and accepting the
rest.
