* Acceptance

- The oresmd templates (=oresmd_*.mustache= and their literate =.org= sources) and their tests are deleted.
- The twelve oresmd quote-type models and =model.org= are deleted, with the =variant_order=, =parse_order= and =URI order= tables.
- =oresmd_resolver=, =market_data_requirement=, =to_curve_key= and =instrument_type::curve= are deleted, or a production consumer is named.
- =ores_series_key_shapes_populate.sql=, its table and =series_key_registry= are deleted, and =ores.ore= keeps only the file reader.
- =check_oresmd_ore_coverage.py= is deleted or rewritten against the new model.
- The codegen suite, the drift gate and the whole-tree build pass.

* Plan

Delete after the cut-over, so each deletion is proved by a green build.
