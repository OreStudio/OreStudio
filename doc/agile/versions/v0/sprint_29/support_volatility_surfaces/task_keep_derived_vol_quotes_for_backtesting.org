* Goal

A backtest can read the derived FX volatility quotes of any past day from the store. Today they are quotes: the age gate admits only the current day and the tick table keeps seven days, so history exists only as something to recompute. This task gives them a durable home, as market_fixing is for fixings, and fills it for the history the fixings cover.

* Acceptance

- Each day's derived ATM, RR and BF quotes are kept with no retention, keyed by series and date, with a correction stored as a new version.
- The derived source can write past days, so the history the stored fixings cover is filled once and kept.
- A reader returns the derived quotes of a series over a window, as the fixing reader does.
- Derivation of FX volatility quotes from historical fixings states the durable storage.

* Plan

The store already has a durable, versioned home for a series' values at a coordinate:
=market_observations=, a hypertable with no retention, whose corrections are kept as
versions by its soft-update trigger, under a =market_series= whose =derivation_kind=
says whether the series is observed or derived. Ticks, the seven-day table, are the
only short-lived store. So the derived quotes take that home rather than a new one.

1. Marketdata: a window reader of a series' observations, as the fixing reader is for
   fixings: an item, its provider, a party, an end day and a number of days, answered
   with each coordinate's value at each observation time.
2. The derived source: one =market_series= per pair under the provider =derived= with
   the derivation kind =FX_VOL_DERIVATION=, and the day's quotes written as its
   observations at the end of their day. On start it fills each past day the stored
   fixings cover and the store does not yet hold, each day derived only from the
   fixings up to it.
3. The derivation note states the durable storage.

* Result

A backtest can read the derived FX volatility quotes of any past day from the store.

- Marketdata reads the observations of a series over a window of days,
  =get_observation_window=, every point each day with its coordinate, as the
  fixing reader does for fixings, refusing a window above 3,660 days or one that
  holds more than the 5,000 observations a reply carries (PR 4415). The fixing
  reader's party scoping and ambiguity refusals are shared by both readers.
- The derived source keeps each day's quotes as the observations of one
  =fx_option= series per pair under its own provider, whose derivation kind is
  =FX_VOL_DERIVATION=. On start it reads the days each series holds and derives
  every other day the stored fixings cover, each from the returns on or before it;
  afterwards it writes each new day. History is kept for every pair of its
  universe, demanded or not. The market data client writes series and observations,
  and the synthetic service may write both.
- Derivation of FX volatility quotes from historical fixings states the durable
  storage.
- Tests: 17 reader test cases against the database, and 20 derived source test
  cases, among them a past day derived from its returns alone, a history kept once
  and then a day at a time, and no day the store holds written again.
