* Goal

A step that hits a transient transport failure tries again with backoff and jitter while its budget remains, and a refusal fails at once. Each attempt is logged with its number and its reason, and the policy lives in one helper rather than at each call site.

* Acceptance

- The helper tells a timeout and a no-responders failure apart from a refusal, and retries only the first two.
- The delay grows with jitter, so two steps that failed together do not return together.
- The attempts stop at the step's remaining budget, so the step does not outlive its own deadline.
- Every attempt is logged with its number and the reason the previous one failed.
- A refusal is reported at once, with the server's reason.
