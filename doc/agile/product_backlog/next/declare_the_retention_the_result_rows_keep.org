This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *next* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

The result rows hypertable keeps its rows indefinitely. The model declares no
=:hypertable_retention_interval:=, and this environment runs no Timescale
retention job on any table.

The task that asked for the table named "the security, indexes and retention the
market data ticks use". The ticks declare 7 days. A run's rows are the figures a
person compares across runs, so a short interval loses the history a comparison
needs, and an indefinite one grows with every run.

Decide the interval and state it on =ores.reporting.report_result_row= so codegen
applies the policy, or record that the rows are kept indefinitely and why.
