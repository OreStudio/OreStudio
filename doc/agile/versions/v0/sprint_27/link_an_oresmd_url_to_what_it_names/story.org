* Goal

An oresmd URL behaves like every other reference in the app. The link goes to the thing it names, a series to its page. A small icon button opens the URL in the dialog, which shows it as labelled values in the layout of the record screens when the screen cannot change it, and as the editor when it can.

* Acceptance

- A series URL and a quote URL are a link to the page of their series when the party has the series: the Evolution page for a series with an axis and the series page for one without. A link opens in a new tab and copies as a link. A fixing, a series the party does not have and a URL the codec refuses are plain text.
- A button beside the URL opens the dialog: Inspect for the read-only view and Edit where the screen stores the URL.
- The read-only view of the dialog shows the kind, the instrument, the quote, the path and the fields as labels and values in the layout of the record screens, with the codec's URL under them and Copy, and no inputs.
- A series with no axis, such as an FX spot rate, has a page that draws its values over time.
- The IR curve feed and the FX feed screens use the link and the button, and a tooltip on the label says the oresmd URI is the series the feed writes to.

* Decisions

- A click on an oresmd URL goes to the object it names. This is the least astonishing result: a link goes to a thing, a real address opens in a new tab and copies as a link, and the other references of the app work this way. Editing is a different intention, so it is a separate small button. Marco agreed on 2026-10-12.
- The button is the text Inspect, or Edit where a screen stores the URL. An icon was tried and not signed off, so the icon table is unchanged. Marco decided this on 2026-10-12.
- The read-only view of the dialog is laid out as the non-edit mode of the record screens: label and value pairs in a grid, and no inputs, so a URL is read the same way everywhere. Marco asked for this on 2026-10-12.
- The object of a series URL is its series. The object of a quote URL is the series of its datum, because a quote is one point of a series and the series has the page. The identity the page needs is the asset, the path as the scope, the instrument, the quote type and the identity fields of the row, which the series list already builds from the same URL. A fixing names no series, so it has no link.
- A series has a page whatever its shape. The Evolution page reads a composite object, a grid of instants by nodes, so it needs an axis. A series with one value at each instant, such as an FX spot rate, is a plain time series, and its page draws the observations with the line chart. The link goes to the Evolution page for a series with an axis and to the series page for one without, and is plain text only where the party has no such series. Marco asked for this on 2026-10-12, after the first cut hid the link for a spot rate.
- The oresmd URI of a feed is its target: the series the feed writes its values to. A tooltip on the label says so.
- A series the party does not have, a series with no axis on the Evolution page, a series with no observation and a URL the codec refuses are each said in words and never as a server error. Marco asked for this on 2026-10-12.
- The read-only view of the dialog was signed off by Marco on 2026-10-12.
- The link carries the URL in the address, as =uri=, and the Evolution page reads it through the server's parse. The link holds no part of the grammar.

* Out of scope

- A page for a quote or a fixing of its own.
- Writing a URL back from the feed screens. A feed's URL follows from its currencies and its curve.
- Changing the editor's grammar or the server's operations.
