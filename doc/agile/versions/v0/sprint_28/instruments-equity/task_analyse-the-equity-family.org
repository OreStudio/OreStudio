* Result

The analysis of the equity family is written and the story is promoted from
the product backlog into sprint 28.

=design.org= states the family, inventories its surfaces, records 33 findings
with the command behind each number, proposes the target model and stages the
rework into ten units. =design_er.puml= draws the proposal. The PNG is not
rendered yet; design.org names the command.

What the analysis found, in brief: the catalogue holds 32 codes of product
type equity, 19 are routed, 17 of those to an equity table, and 13 have no
instrument table at all. Nine routed models and one child table hold 218
columns, 17 to 26 per table, of which 32 have no writer and 4 more are
written and never read. The family has no header. Regime 2 scores 1 of 5 and
Regime 3 scores 1 of 5, both debt High.

Two follow-ups this task does not take. The design is not yet reviewed with
the data-model review skill, so its findings are unaccepted. =design_er.png=
is not rendered.
