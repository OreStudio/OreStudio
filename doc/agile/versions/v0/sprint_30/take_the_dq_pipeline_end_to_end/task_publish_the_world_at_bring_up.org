* Result

A database leaves bring-up with its platform and world catalogues in place.

- =ores_dq_bootstrap_publish_fn(text[])= publishes the bundles it is given into
  the system tenant, without the bus. It orders the union of their members by
  the dataset dependencies, skips the optional members and the terminal artefact
  types, and calls each member's publish function with the system tenant as the
  target. It refuses a bundle that has no members, and it warns when a member
  has no dataset, which is what a seed script's typo looks like.
- The function derives each publish function from the artefact type's subject,
  with one fallback: DQ's own datasets are written by
  =<component>_<artefact type>_publish_fn= and not by the subject's operation.
  Six of the seventy-one distinct subjects need the fallback, and the function
  fails loudly when neither name resolves.
- =populate.sql= calls it last, once every artefact row is staged, and its
  "reference data is NOT published here" block is gone.
- The three publish calls inside the seed scripts stay, and they are seeding
  dependencies and not publication policy: the WRLD business centre is validated
  against the coding schemes the ISO seed publishes, and the system party is
  validated against the business centres the FpML seed publishes. Both are
  published by the bring-up run as well, idempotently.
- Proof, on the development database inside a transaction that was rolled back:
  the three catalogues were emptied in the system tenant, the renamed bundle map
  was applied, and the bring-up wrote 61 calendars, 11 equity underlyings, 160
  currencies and 250 countries.
- The first full recreate failed, and the failure is the reason the function has
  two parts. The conventions publish refused EUR because its validator reads the
  currency catalogue, and no dependency edge declared it, so the conventions
  sorted before the currencies. The graph now carries the edge, and the publisher
  retries a dataset that fails once every other member has published, because a
  validator may read a catalogue that no edge names. A dataset that fails twice
  is raised with its own error.
- A second recreate passed end to end: the system tenant holds 61 current
  calendars, 250 countries, 170 currencies, 3,275 conventions and 213 business
  centres, and `compass db status` reports the schema in sync.

The tenant tier is untouched: a tenant's own rows are published when the tenant
is created, which is the next task. The level on the bundle row, and the target
it derives, remain with the publish guarantees.
