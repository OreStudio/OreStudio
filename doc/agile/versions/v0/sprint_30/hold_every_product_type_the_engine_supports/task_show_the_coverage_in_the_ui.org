* Goal

A person can open one page and see every product type with the form ORE reads it as, the table that holds it, whether a builder and a generator exist for it, and the unsupported types with their reasons, with the counts of declared, supported and unsupported.

* Acceptance

- the page lists every catalogue type with its form or forms, its instrument
  table, and whether a builder and a generator exist for it
- the page states each type's level, measured rather than claimed: held, built,
  generated, read by ORE, built by ORE, each from the tests that prove it
- the page lists the unsupported types, tagged, with the reason each is not
  carried, so the gap is visible rather than silent
- the counts of declared, supported and unsupported types are stated, and they
  reconcile
- the page is reachable from the catalogue's own screen, and its data comes from
  the served catalogue rather than a hand-kept list
