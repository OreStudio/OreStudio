* Goal

A person sees, in Security, which provider accounts are linked to theirs — the provider, the address it stated and when it was linked — links another after proving their password, and removes one. When removal would leave the account with no way in, the screen says so and refuses, and the person is told what to do instead.

* Acceptance

- Security lists each link with its provider, the address the provider stated and the date it was linked.
- Linking from the screen proves the ORE account with its password and shows the refusal when the provider account already belongs to someone else.
- Removing a link asks for confirmation, and a link that is the last way in is refused with what the person must do first.
- The screens are pinned by tests, and the strings are translated.
