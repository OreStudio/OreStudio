#+title: Provisioning asks for every account in one request, and drops what is past ten thousand
#+description: The password and picture steps read the tenant's accounts with limit 10,000 and work one by one over what comes back, so the request grows with the account count and silently truncates past the ceiling. Page it, or read a page at a time as the step advances.

* What

Two provisioning steps read the tenant's accounts with =limit = 10'000= in one
request: =set_staff_passwords= and the picture attach behind =attach_staff_photos=.
Each then works one by one over the answer, resetting a password or attaching a
picture per account.

Give the read a page the step advances through, and let the step carry how far it
has got, so a tenant with more than ten thousand accounts is not silently
truncated and a step is not one request.

* Why

The story [[id:A4D0E305-CEF6-4684-ABBA-0D09657479F1][Keep workflow steps alive while they work, and off iam]] rules
that no request handler does work that grows with the data, and the account read
is the one request on the provisioning path that still does. Today a tenant past
the ceiling loses the accounts beyond it without a word.
