* Acceptance

- convention_lookup answers what each bond builder asks.
- The bond planner draws values and calls the builders; its construct_* functions and *_terms structs are deleted.
- The bond entries leave the recorded trades' known lists, and the recorded files change in the same commit.
- The ORE engine test reads and builds every booked bond type.
- The roundtrip tests read every ORE sample trade of each booked bond type and write back every element ORE needs.

* Plan

- The export test joined a bond's issue but none of its other rows, while the
  export reader joins them all. So the recorded bonds were not what a real
  export writes: Bond, CallableBond and ConvertibleBond lacked their coupon
  leg, BondOption its strike, and BondFuture, BondTRS and ForwardBond were
  refused. The reader's assembly becomes a pure function over the rows,
  =bond_legs_of= for the issue's legs and one for the trade's rows, which the
  reader and the test both call.
- The test scope states a reference curve, as a tenant's generation config
  does; the Products example names =EUR-EURIBOR-6M=.
- One builder per booked bond type. A bond issue builder states the security
  and its coupon leg; each trade builder states the rows its product needs:
  the option block and strike of an option, the cash leg of a repo, the
  funding leg of a TRS, the swap leg of an Ascot, the identifier of a position.
- The =construct_*= functions, their terms and the plan's =bond_terms= are
  deleted.
