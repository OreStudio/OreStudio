* Acceptance

- A curve is chosen within a currency, and the curve beside it is the same currency's by default.
- Two currencies are compared only overnight against overnight, and the screen says what that shows.

* Decisions

- A curve is read within its currency. Two currencies are compared only
  overnight against overnight, because that pair says one thing, the rate
  differential; a term curve of one currency beside the overnight of another says
  nothing either curve means.
- The rules that say which curves may stand together live in one module apart
  from the page, so the page asks and never decides.

* Out of scope

- Comparing term curves across currencies, such as EURIBOR 6M against GBP
  LIBOR 6M, which mixes the differential with two panels' credit.
