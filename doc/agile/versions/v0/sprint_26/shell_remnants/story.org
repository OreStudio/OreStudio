* Result

Merged as PR #2281, with all five of its checks green. The four pieces of work
the ores.shell clean-standard pass recorded rather than closed are closed: eleven
hand-written trading instrument units retired behind generated ones, the session
listing's parse defect fixed at its source, the bootstrap smoke recipe's
precondition stated, the account-role duplication resolved per verb with the
service's own generator repointed, and the iam S01 exception reason rewritten to
describe what the registry enforces.

Two of the four are handled by recording rather than by a fix, and both say so
where the next reader will look: four trading units need a model written before
generation can supersede them, and a shell recipe cannot be idempotent while the
language has no conditional and no per-command tolerance.
