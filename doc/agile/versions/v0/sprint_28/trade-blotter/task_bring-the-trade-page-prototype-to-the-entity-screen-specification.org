** Book fields and structures in the list, 2026-10-21

A book in the prototypes now carries the columns of the book table: parent portfolio, owner unit, functional currency, ledger account, cost centre, status, regulatory type, purpose, ledger feed, sweepable, rates centre and sandbox. The code sets come from the seed files: purpose (9 codes), status (Active, Frozen, Closed), regulatory type (Trading, Banking) and ledger feed (None, Automatic, Manual).

- The purpose "Virtual" never existed. A virtual book is a book with a sandbox. In a sandbox the table requires no ledger account and no sweeping.
- Book a trade offers a book that is not Active as a disabled choice with its status, and shows the facts of the chosen book under the field. The book move and the ORE export offer open books only.
- The ORE import asks for the functional currency of the books it creates. The planner sets them Active and Trading; in a sandbox the purpose is Test and the ledger feed is None.
- Two books were added: EMEA-RATES-2025 (Closed) and EMEA-CMDTY (Frozen).
- Decision, 2026-10-21: the screen enforces the book status. The database does not refuse a trade in a Frozen or Closed book today, so the rule lives in the screens (Book a trade, book move, ORE import and export) until the model states it.

The trade list now shows structures and trades as rows of one level (=?show=trades|structures=). The real read model, trade_summaries, holds trades only. A mixed list needs either a read model that unions both, or two reads that the screen merges; a merged page then cannot be exact about paging and order. The request line shows both reads. This also needs a decision.
