* Goal

A provisioning run whose steps run side by side completes, however long one of
them takes. A service serves its workflow steps on a bounded set of workers,
which it chooses for the work its steps do, so a step that waits for many
minutes on another service no longer holds back the steps dispatched beside it
until the workflow fails them as unclaimed.

* Acceptance

- A service can serve its step commands on more than one worker; one, the
  default, keeps today's behaviour.
- The IAM service serves its provisioning steps on four workers.
- The workers wait for every running step when the service stops.
- The service token provider is safe to call from several threads.
- Unit tests prove that steps run at once, that a step waits for a free
  worker, and that the workers wait for running steps.
