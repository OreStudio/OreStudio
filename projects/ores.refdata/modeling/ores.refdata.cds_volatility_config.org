The settings of one =CDSVolatility= entry of a =curveconfig.xml=, one row per
=curve_definition= in the =CDSVolatilities= section: the expiries the surface
is quoted at, how its strikes are quoted, and its day counter and calendar.
Its =Terms= are rows of =cds_volatility_term=, and the surface it is built as,
when it names one, a row of =curve_volatility_config=.

The schema also allows a =PriceInfo= element, which no corpus document writes;
the mapper refuses an entry that has one rather than lose it.
