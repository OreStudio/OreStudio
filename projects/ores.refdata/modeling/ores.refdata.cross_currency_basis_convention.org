Describes how ORE builds both legs of a cross-currency basis swap: the two
indices, their tenors, their payment lags and their fixing and cutoff rules, and
whether the spread is included in a leg's coupons. Corresponds to the
<CrossCurrencyBasis> element in ORE conventions.xml.

The flat leg pays its index with no spread, and the spread leg pays its index
plus the basis. Each states its own index, tenor, payment lag and fixing rules.
The swap terms state what applies to both legs at once: the settlement days and
calendar, the roll convention, whether the schedules roll to the end of the
month, and whether the notional resets. ORE requires both indices, the
settlement days and the roll convention, so a convention fills all three parts.

The family's central row is the shared [[id:9477A085-0822-485F-BDF7-3C8F4A306E93][convention]], which names this kind
with =kind_code= =CROSS_CURRENCY_BASIS= and holds the ORE document id. The
central row is shared by every kind, so it holds nothing this kind alone
states: those fields are in this family's parts.
