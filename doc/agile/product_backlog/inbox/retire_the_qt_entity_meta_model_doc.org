This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Retire =projects/ores.codegen/modeling/entity_meta_model_cpp_qt.org=.

The Qt client is gone and its codegen facet with it: no =qt= templates remain
under =library/templates/=, and =ores.cpp.qt= appears in no graph, in no
projection and in none of the codegen source. The page therefore documents a
facet that cannot be admitted, and describes properties as live that nothing
reads -- it is the last place =:history_request_class:=,
=:history_response_class:= and =:history_message_type:= are still stated as the
way history is reached.

Retire it rather than correct it, unless the Qt client is coming back, in which
case the properties it names are the ones that would need re-deriving from the
generic history service.

* Why it matters now

Those three properties were removed from the entity scaffold in the same pass as
this capture, so nothing generates them any more. A doc that still presents them
as part of the model is how a dead property gets reintroduced: the scaffold was
the last generator of them, and this page is the last description.
