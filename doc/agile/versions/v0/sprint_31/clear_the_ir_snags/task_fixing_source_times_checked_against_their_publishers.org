* Goal

Check the seeded publication time, zone and calendar of each fixing source
whose time came from market convention against its primary source, and correct
the seed where they differ:

| Source | Seeded time and zone | Check against |
|--------+----------------------+---------------|
| =KFTC18= | 15:30 Asia/Seoul | Annex A, Seoul Money Brokerage Services |
| =TAIFX1= | 11:00 Asia/Taipei | Annex A, Taipei Forex Inc. |
| =SAEC= | 09:15 Asia/Shanghai | Annex A, CFETS |
| =FBIL= | 13:30 Asia/Kolkata | Annex A, Financial Benchmarks India |
| =PDSPESO= | 11:30 Asia/Manila | Annex A, Philippine Dealing System |
| =JISDOR= | 10:00 Asia/Jakarta | Annex A, Bank Indonesia |
| =PTAX= | 13:00 America/Sao_Paulo | Annex A, Banco Central do Brasil |
| =BOC= | 16:30 America/Toronto | Bank of Canada |
| =IMF= | 12:00 Europe/London | International Monetary Fund |
| =TCMB= | 15:30 Europe/Istanbul | Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye |

=PDSPESO= also takes the =WeekendsOnly= calendar, because the calendar dataset
holds no Philippine calendar.

** Why

The seeds are live reference data: a non-deliverable trade settles on its
source's fixing at its time, so a wrong time fixes the wrong market. The task
Hold the fixing sources and their cuts as reference data seeded these times from
market convention, and the review of #5052 asked for the check to be tracked.
