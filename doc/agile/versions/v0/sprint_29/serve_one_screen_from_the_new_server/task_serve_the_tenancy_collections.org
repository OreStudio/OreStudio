* Notes

- The four are the tenancy area's plain collections: the tenants, their statuses and

  the tenant setup flow, are the V increments and are not this task.
- The inventory had no rule for the area's generated routes, so the family states two:
  one for the reads and one for the writes, in the shape the reference data family uses.
