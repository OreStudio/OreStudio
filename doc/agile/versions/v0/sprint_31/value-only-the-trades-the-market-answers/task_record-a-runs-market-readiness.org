* Goal

A run knows, before the engine sees it, which trades its market can answer: the readiness of every address and of every trade is recorded against the run, the incomplete trades are left out, and a run with no complete trade never reaches the engine.

* Acceptance

- The run records, per address, whether the party's market answers it at the as-of, the series that answers it, and the cause when it does not.
- The run records, per trade, whether it is complete and the addresses and causes that make it incomplete.
- An incomplete trade does not travel to the engine, and the counts of booked, complete and excluded trades are recorded on the run.
- A run none of whose trades is complete fails in preprocessing, naming the causes, and never submits work to the grid.

* Plan

- Decide the set before anything is written. A new =resolve_market_readiness=
  step runs before =gather_trades=: it asks trading which trades the scope
  holds and what each one needs, asks market data which addresses it answers,
  records the answer, and hands the surviving trades to the export. Gathering
  the trades afterwards writes only what the engine will see, so no second
  export and no filtering of a blob are needed.
- A gap names a cause the read can state. The coverage function already tells a
  missing series from a series that holds no point at or before the as-of. The
  coverage repository keeps those apart and a new market data op carries the
  cause, so the record says =no_series= or =no_point= rather than "unanswered".
- Readiness is three records, at the three grains the design page states: the
  counts on the run, one row per trade, and one row per address. The counts went
  to a narrow table of their own rather than onto =report_instance=, because the
  run entity is read by every screen of a run and a widening it does not need
  would reach all of them.
- The decision is a pure function of the per-trade requirements and the
  coverage answer, so the rule is tested without a database, a NATS client or a
  workflow.
- A run with no complete trade fails in the readiness step, which is
  preprocessing, names the causes, and never reaches submission.

* Notes

- =market_series_handler.hpp= is generated from =ores.marketdata.market_series.org=,
  so the export's own loop is edited in the model and not in the header. Editing
  the header alone survives until the next =compass codegen regenerate=, which
  restores it.
- The coverage read is stricter than the export's inclusion rule on purpose. A
  series with no point at the date answers the export, which writes its history
  alone, and does not answer a run, which would value a trade off nothing.
- The rigging the codegen leaves to the author is three things, and the ratchet
  names all three: =rls_tenant_isolation= and =rls_party_isolation= under the
  model's =* SQL= flags, the permission codes the handlers check, and the
  history-provider registrars. A flag placed in the top-level =* Flags= section
  instead of the =* SQL= one generates nothing and says nothing.
- The run's readiness table first carried =report_definition_id= and the
  codegen's wide-type check refused it at sixteen fields. The run already names
  its definition and the record is one per run, so the column went.
- The branch was rebased onto main before its review, and the schema diagram
  conflicted because both sides added tables to it. The diagram is a codegen
  output, so it was regenerated from the merged models rather than merged by
  hand, and every gate was re-run on the new base.
- The trade grain states whether a trade is complete and how many of its
  addresses went unanswered. The addresses and their causes are the address
  grain; joining the two through the trade's own requirements, which trading
  stores per trade, names each gap without writing the cause twice.

* Result

A run decides what it may value before it writes anything.

- =resolve_market_readiness= is the first step of a run that executes its
  pre-processing. It resolves the scope's books, asks trading which trades they
  hold and what each names, asks market data which addresses it answers at the
  run's date, records the answer, and fails in preprocessing when no trade is
  complete.
- The coverage read names the series that answers an address and states
  =no_series= or =no_point= when none does, so a party buying data knows which
  one it is missing. The export keeps its own looser rule and the two are
  documented apart.
- Readiness is recorded at three grains: =report_run_market_readinesses= holds
  the counts and the as-of, =report_trade_market_readinesses= the complete flag
  and the gap count, and =report_address_market_readinesses= the series and the
  cause. All three carry tenant and party policies.
- =gather_trades= writes the trades the readiness step named, so an incomplete
  trade reaches neither storage nor the engine. The export takes the trades to
  write for that reason.
- The decision is =decide_market_readiness=, a pure function of the per-trade
  requirements and the coverage answer, with eight cases: a complete trade, an
  absent series, a series with no point, a trade that names nothing, one address
  two trades name, a trade named twice, and the two refusals of a message that
  does not match what was asked.

What the tree says: build green, the eight decision cases and the five
component suites green, the schema suite at 111 files and 1074 tests,
=compass-ng= at no new diagnostics, and the format gate clean. Every number is
in the measurements above with its commit.

Two things this task does not do. It records the addresses of an incomplete
trade at the address grain rather than repeating them per trade, so a report
that names a trade's gaps joins the run's record to the trade's requirements in
trading. And the phase a failure was produced in is still unstated: that is
[[id:E6B29704-7BEF-4C2A-B95F-32623AF49200][State the phase of every error a run records]].
