* Goal

The system can fit a volatility surface to its quotes. A pure engine beside the curve engine in =projects/ores.analytics/quant= takes the weighted ATM, risk reversal and butterfly quotes of each expiry, the forward to each expiry and the pair's conventions as inputs. It fits power-law SSVI, with gamma calibrated as QuantExt does, samples the fitted surface on an output grid, and checks the result for arbitrage. It reads no database and calls no service, so unit tests prove it. SSVI is the only smile model in this task. See [[id:53D14F31-EE32-493E-BBEB-140AA4906BE4][Constructing a volatility surface: from quotes to a published set]].

* Acceptance

- Quotes generated from known SSVI parameters are fitted back to those parameters within a tolerance the tests state.
- The engine returns the ATM total variance of each expiry, the skew, the curvature, the sampled grid and the error of each pillar.
- Each strike label resolves to a strike through the given forward and the delta and ATM conventions.
- A butterfly quoted as a broker strangle enters the fit as the price of the strangle at its own strikes, and gives a different surface from the same number read as a smile butterfly.
- Between expiries the engine interpolates in total variance.
- The result reports calendar spread arbitrage and butterfly arbitrage, using the SSVI conditions on the parameters, and tests show each violation detected.
- QuantExt's =testCalibratedSsvi= power-law case passes on its own smiles and bounds, and =testSmileBF= and =testBrokerBF= hold on the fitted smile.
- A pillar whose error exceeds the tolerance is reported by name.
- An expiry without the pillars SSVI needs is refused, and the error names the expiry and the missing pillars.

* Plan

Name the data shape first. =calibrate_ssvi= flattens the request into one =fit_state= of hot rows, with cold strings left in the request, and one wing buffer, so an objective evaluation needs no container of its own.

Variable projection. Given rho, eta and gamma the expiries are independent. A =nelder_mead<3>= searches the shared parameters, and =boost::math::tools::brent_find_minima= solves each log theta inside it.

=math::read_smile= reads any smile in a convention. =quote_smile= delegates to it, so the generator and the fit share one reading of a butterfly.

* Result

The engine fits power-law SSVI to weighted quotes and is in =service/ssvi_calibration_engine=. It fits gamma as QuantExt does, returns each expiry's theta, rho, eta, gamma, the error and strikes of each pillar and the sampled grid, and reports arbitrage. All acceptance items are met by unit tests.

Ported ground truth is QuantExt's =testCalibratedSsvi= power-law case on its 13 smiles and its bounds, its moneyness arbitrage grid, and =testSmileBF= and =testBrokerBF= at both expiries and both deltas of the fixture. Each slice of those two tests is fitted alone, because SSVI shares its curvature across expiries.

Not run are the database, service and smoke gates, because the change is a pure library that no service calls yet.
