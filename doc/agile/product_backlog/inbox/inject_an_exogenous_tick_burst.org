* What

A system verb, applied at the loop's now and recorded in the trace as its own
kind, that injects a burst from outside the market. Two forms, one verb:

- *A tick burst.* N ticks arrive at once, shared over the running feeds by their
  rates, or aimed at an asset class or one feed. Each is a real tick: the feed
  steps its price and publishes, the heat rises by the jump, and a Hawkes feed's
  own intensity rises by its excitation. The burst then propagates by the laws
  already in place, so the market's response is the system's own and not a
  scripted one.
- *A heat shock.* The heat alone rises by a stated amount, with no tick
  published. Every feed's pace rises by the heat's share and decays at the
  heat's rate: the market's impulse response, with no price moving.

The Market screen offers both beside the force switch, as the review drew them
(+25 and +100 ticks).

* Why

Gemini's review drew an *Inject exogenous tick burst* control with *+25 ticks*
and *+100 ticks shock*. Today a tester who wants to see a panic either pins the
market active, which holds a level rather than a spike, or waits for a burst to
happen. An injected burst is the one input that shows what the jump, the decay
and a Hawkes feed's branching ratio mean, and it is what a stress scenario
starts from: news arrives, then the market reacts.

The tick burst touches more than the loop: a feed's body must take an
injected tick as it takes a scheduled one, outside its law's draw, and the
price steps by a gap of its own. The heat shock is cheap, because the heat is
one number the loop already owns. Neither changes the loop gain, so both stay
inside the stability rule.
