#+title: Provisioning asks for a market data source nobody serves
#+description: The acme_demo profile's subscribe_market_data step issues marketdata.v1.market_data_sources.list and no service handles it, so every provision aborts at step 7 and the trade population never runs. The marketdata client now exposes list_feeds and the tree holds no source naming the old subject.

* What

Provisioning a tenant now aborts before its trade population runs:

#+begin_example
[7/5] subscribe_market_data: failed - NATS request failed: no service is
handling subject 'ores.dev.jolly_knuth.marketdata.v1.market_data_sources.list'
#+end_example

The =acme_demo= profile orders the step at position 60, ahead of
=generate_trades= at 70, so the run stops with a tenant whose parties, staff and
books exist and whose trades do not. Every profile that orders the step fails
the same way.

Point the step at the entity the marketdata service serves now, or drop the step
from the profiles until that entity exists again.

* Why

Found on 2026-10-31 while proving that a seeded trade raises no authorisation
request. The refactor that moved market data onto suppliers, sessions and feeds
renamed the client call -- =ores::marketdata::client::market_data_client= now
answers =list_feeds()=, and the synthetic service calls that -- but no source in
the tree names the old subject any more, so the requester is a compiled path the
sweep did not reach, or a step executor still carrying the old name.

The database refuses the local workaround: a =delete= of the step row as the
application role removes nothing, because the profile belongs to the system
tenant.
