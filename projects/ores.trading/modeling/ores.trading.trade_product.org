The product a trade was booked as, and the form it was booked in, so a screen
reopens the trade in that form. The product is a product type's code, or a
library script's name for a script-only product, as =describe_product= names it;
the form is =native=, the product's own element, or =scripted=, a =ScriptedTrade=
over a library script. Neither follows from the trade alone: a script such as
=VanillaBasketOption= is the scripted form of the equity, FX and commodity basket
options alike, so a =ScriptedTrade= over it does not say which was booked.

The row is its own type, not two columns of the [[id:4304A441-E532-45FB-837A-378F13693CAE][trade anchor]], because the
anchor is at its ceiling of fields. It is keyed by the trade, written by the
booking operation with the trade, and a trade booked any other way has none.
