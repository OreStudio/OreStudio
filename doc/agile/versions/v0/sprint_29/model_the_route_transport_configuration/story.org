* Naming and model

Two concepts, two names. A *driver* is the code that speaks an external
interface's language: Bloomberg's or LSEG's API, or, for Frankfurter, the code
inside the synthetic service. Nobody outside engineering needs the word. A
*connection* is a driver configured as one instance a person knows: Bloomberg
test, Bloomberg production, Frankfurter. It carries the endpoint, the expected
rhythm, the poll interval and the backfill window, and its secrets are encrypted
at rest and write-only on the wire.

A connection reaches *providers*: the ECB, the NY Fed, the publishers whose
values it delivers. A provider belongs to the connection that delivers it, so a
publisher reached two ways is two connections over one provider, and the item
keeps its identity with the connection as provenance.

The last piece is where the values land. A *destination* points the stream at
the party and the store it fills, so a connection may deliver to the system
party, or to a tenant, or to both. The prototype covers only the provider and
its data today; the connection and its destination are the two screens this
story adds.
