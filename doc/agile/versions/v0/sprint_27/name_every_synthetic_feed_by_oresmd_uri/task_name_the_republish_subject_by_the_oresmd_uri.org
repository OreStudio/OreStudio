* Goal

Every valid oresmd quote reaches its consumers. Today plan_tick writes the ORE key of the tick's datum and drops the tick when there is none, before it is stored or republished. The subject takes its key from the URI instead, encoded into safe subject tokens, and the shell stream command, the web tick buffer and the feed binding page follow.

* Acceptance

- A quote with an oresmd URI and no ORE key, such as one with the quote type hazard_rate, is stored and republished.
- The subject is built from the URI by one encoder with a round trip test, and no subject part holds a character NATS refuses.
- The shell stream command, the market tick and feed binding pages, and every subscriber use the new subject.
