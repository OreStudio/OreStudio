#+title: Let the heat turn the regime
#+description: Make the chain's transition rates depend on the heat, so a market that runs hot is likelier to turn active and stay there. Today the chain is exogenous: it turns on its own, and the heat only adds to the pace.
#+type: capture
#+level: cross

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Make the chain's two transition rates depend on the heat, for example
=wake = wake_0 * exp(k * C)=, so a market that runs hot is likelier to turn active
and slower to calm. Keep the mean pace at one over the long run, which now
depends on the heat's own mean. A third, panic state is its own capture; the
coupling here would apply to its transitions too.

* Why

Gemini's review drew heat thresholds that switch the state, and a third
*volatile burst* state. A threshold is the wrong mechanism, because a state that
flips at a number flaps around it. The need behind it is real: today the chain
is exogenous, so a burst of activity can never cause a calm market to turn,
and a panic cannot feed on itself through the regime.

It changes the market's model, so it needs the same care as the tails: a
stated rule, a test that the long-run pace still holds, and a note in the
market volatility channel page.
