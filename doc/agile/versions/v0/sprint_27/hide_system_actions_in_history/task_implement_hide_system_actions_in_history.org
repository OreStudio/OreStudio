* Goal

A history shows a toggle that hides or shows the entries made by service accounts. Tenant admin and super admin are people, so the toggle never hides them. The server classifies the actor, because the web directory only names the accounts the caller may read, and cannot tell a service from a person it may not see.

* Acceptance

- Every timeline entry carries the kind of its actor, set by the server for any caller who may read the timeline.
- Every history screen shows a toggle that hides or shows the entries made by service accounts, and the toggle is on by default.
- Tenant admin and super admin entries stay visible when the toggle hides services.
- The toggle is one shared part of the Timeline and the version history panel, not one copy per screen.

* Result

A history shows a "Hide system activity" toggle to a reader who may read accounts, when a service account wrote an entry. The Timeline and the version history panel share one toggle. The people directory tells a service from a person by the account type that the accounts read returns. A reader who may not read accounts is offered no toggle. Tenant admin and super admin are people, so the toggle never hides them.
