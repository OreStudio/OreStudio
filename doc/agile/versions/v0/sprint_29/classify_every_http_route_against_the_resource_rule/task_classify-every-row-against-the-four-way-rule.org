* Acceptance

- Every row in the inventory states a kind, and no row is left empty.
- Every route called a composed view states its name, its owning component, its computation and its cache policy.
- Every route called not-an-API names the field or behaviour that condemns it, so the decision can be argued with.
- The decisions survive a regeneration and the gate stays green.
