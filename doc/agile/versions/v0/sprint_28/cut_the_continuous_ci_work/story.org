* Decisions

- Continuous Linux carries neither coverage nor a package. Coverage adds
  =-fprofile-arcs -ftest-coverage= to every translation unit and then
  walks the tree with gcov, and the Nightly Build already runs it
  together with memcheck.
- The package is the deliverable of the nightly and tag runs. Nothing
  consumed the continuous artifact, so dropping it costs nothing.
- =CTest.cmake= gains a =package= flag that defaults to on, so only the
  continuous workflow changes and every other caller keeps packaging.

* Out of scope

- Further compile-time work. The tier change already puts the continuous
  clang jobs at about 2h50m against a six-hour limit, so unity builds, a
  faster linker and precompiled headers wait on a measurement that says
  they are needed.
- Splitting continuous into a build job and separate sharded test jobs.
