* Quote types

The class carries no currency, no tenor and no metric key: the entity is the
provider and the point is the pair of rating grades the migration runs between.
ORE writes the metric as the second segment, so the one metric is its own quote
type here -- the `metric` query key is one of the IR-only keys every other class
rejects.

| enum_name | ore_type | ore_constant | ore_metric | point_semantics | qualifier_shape | comment |
|-----------+----------+--------------+------------+-----------------+-----------------+---------|
| transition_probability | RATING | rating | TRANSITION_PROBABILITY | composite | no_ccy | RATING/TRANSITION_PROBABILITY/PROVIDER/FROM/TO, and the four-segment form the corpus also writes, which names the provider and no grades. |

* Enum brief

The `quote` query key for rating providers. Rating-only; only meaningful when
`type=quote`.

* Identifier brief

A fully-resolved oresmd identifier for a rating provider (asset_class=rating).

* Requirement brief

A logical, possibly-partial oresmd requirement for a rating provider.
