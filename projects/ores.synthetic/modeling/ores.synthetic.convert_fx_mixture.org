#+brief: FX mixture conversion between price-process engines.

#+created: 2026-10-11
#+updated: 2026-10-11

The values an FX feed's mixture takes when its engine changes.

The engines read the same three numbers of a component differently. The
geometric engine reads μ and σ as log-returns; the arithmetic engine reads
both in price units; the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck engine reads only the first
component, its weight as the reversion speed κ and its σ in price units. A
screen that changes a feed's engine asks here for the mixture the new engine
should read, so the conversion is the service's, beside the engines it
describes. Nothing is stored.
