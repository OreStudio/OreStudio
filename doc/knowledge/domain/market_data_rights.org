#+title: Display, non-display and derived rights are modelled on the subscription
#+description: The three usage rights vendors license separately, where the system records them, and how they decide cost and permission.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :marketdata:rights:licensing:subscription:
#+created: 2026-10-22
#+updated: 2026-10-22

* Summary

Vendors license data by the use made of it. The system models three rights on
each subscription: =display=, =non_display= and =derived=. A right decides what a
party may do and what the party costs. The system models them now, so a later
real vendor contract fits without a change of model.

* Detail

** The rights

| Right | Meaning | Example |
|-------+---------+---------|
| =display= | A person looks at the data on a screen | A trader's blotter |
| =non_display= | A system uses the data without display | A pricing or risk engine |
| =derived= | A party computes a new value from the data | A blended spot, a curve |

These follow the way vendors price access. A vendor licence for derived data
covers internal use only and is priced for each legal entity. See
[[id:1B8A3DB7-006A-4E14-AC11-57FA3741222D][A rate card on the source turns usage into a bill the platform shares]] for the sources.

** Where the rights are checked

- A demand checks that the subscription holds the right that the use needs. A
  pricing engine needs =non_display=.
- A composite needs =derived= on the subscription of every input provider. See
  [[id:41D84AD1-0058-4135-9B9D-BDE08BE8169A][A composite attributes its use to the providers of its inputs]].
- A screen read needs =display=.

** What the rights drive

Each right is a driver of a charge component in a rate card. See [[id:1B8A3DB7-006A-4E14-AC11-57FA3741222D][A rate card on the source turns usage into a bill the platform shares]].

** What is not modelled yet

Redistribution to another legal entity, and a limit on the number of display
users, are not modelled. A later change adds them as new rights or new limits.
