* Summary

The fixing process takes an observation from its source to the trades that
need it, across two levels. At system level, the source publishes the
observation and it is stored once for every party. At party level, each party
that holds trades on the source runs the process in its own fixings manager:
its operations authorise the observation after the publication time, against
the prevailing front-office rate; authorisation raises an event, and every
trade of that party that depends on the observation is fixed, with coupons,
settlements and state changes labelled with the event. Because the source is
agreed, an error lies in the observation or in its adoption, so the fixes an
authorisation produced can be undone and redone. The two levels are set out in
[[id:FA5C96A3-1F8E-4F9B-8973-40D92055D7DD][System-level fixing observations and party-level fixings]].

* Detail

** The process in outline

An observation has no status of its own: it is the source's value, corrected
only by a new version from the publisher. The status belongs to the party
fixing, a party's adoption of the observation, which passes through four
states. It is required once a trade of the party names the source and date,
dormant if the party keys a value before that date, unofficial once the source
publishes or the party keys a value, and official once the party's operations
authorise it. Authorisation and undo move it between the last two states, and
each move raises the event that fixes or unfixes the party's trades.

The interaction crosses the two levels. The market data store holds the
observation for every party; the party's fixings manager holds the party
fixing; the party's product services hold its trades. The fixings manager never
writes a trade; it raises the event, and each product service books or
reverses the actions its trades need.

** Subscribe and observe

Booking a trade that needs fixings subscribes the trade's party to its
sources, so a party's process concerns only the sources its live trades need.
Observations of sources with a producer arrive at system level whether or not
any party needs them, and the super administrator can view them across every
source. A party keys a value only for its own fixing: where its source has no
producer, or where a trader has agreed a value with a client as part of a sale.

** Authorise

A party's operations authorise each of the party's fixings, and only after the
source's publication time. The system compares each observation with the current
front-office rate and highlights large differences. An override keeps both the
published and the overridden values, and may apply to only some of the trades
subscribed to the observation. Front office may key a value but may not
authorise one, and may not change a value once it is authorised; this is a
four-eyes control. A trade that must carry a fixing source and has none is
reported, not silently skipped.

** Apply

Authorisation sends an event. Services find the affected trades, generate the
action each kind of trade needs and label every action with the event, so that
the event can later be reversed. The progress of a large application is
visible, some books or trades can be excluded, and ideally all affected trades
go live together after the P&L impact has been seen.

Generated trades cover the period from the last generation to now. Real
events run in settlement mode; hypothetical events, such as a time ladder that
rolls a portfolio forward, run in valuation mode and generate nothing durable.
A trade remembers the start of its settled generation, which is its first
unkeyed fixing.

A party's official fixings of a source form an unbroken prefix in time: there
cannot be an official fixing, then an unofficial one, then an official one. A
future fixing keyed in advance therefore stays dormant until every fixing
between now and its date is official.

Observations are stored once and never stamped on the trades; see
[[id:C6A619F3-B2B6-42D5-9C41-5269A84BC96C][Storage of fixing observations apart from trades]].

** Undo

The process can be undone as a whole, or for one time, book, currency or
trade. Undo sends an undo event and runs the same chain in reverse. A trade may
be amended freely before its fixing date; after it, it is amended as a fixed
trade or unfixed first.

** Checks at booking

A trade whose fixings generate trades, such as a swap's coupons, is checked at
booking: every fixing already applied must have its settlement trade. A fixing
activity is either a fix or an unfix, and a fixed non-deliverable forward must
have its fixing trade in the right state.

** A bounded context of its own

Fixing management is a bounded context in the sense of domain-driven design,
at party level: it owns a party's fixings and their authorisation, reads the
observations from the system-level store, and the products link to it rather
than embedding it. An event-sourced design, in which every fix and
unfix is an event, gives the undo and the audit for free.
