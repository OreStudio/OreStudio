#+description: Codegen operation model for reading what one party demands of each route, computed from the catalogue before any tick arrives.

#+brief: Reading the demand of one party.
#+filetags: :model:operation:marketdata:
#+created: 2026-11-22
#+updated: 2026-11-22

A planner decides what a party can trade from the market data the party is
configured to receive, not from what has arrived: a vol quote or a curve can take a
while to appear. The demand of a party follows from the catalogue alone: its routes,
its subscriptions, the providers' terms, the feeds' scopes and the mappings. So the
request needs no adapter to be ready, and it answers the same before the first tick
as after it.
