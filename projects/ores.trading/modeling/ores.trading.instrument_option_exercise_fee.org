One row per exercise fee an option block's list states, keyed to the
instrument and the entry's ordinal.

The schema states the amount as text with three attributes on the same
element, and the container keeps the number and the attributes apart.
The ordinal preserves the document's order.

The amount is the only required member, so it is the only column here
that cannot be null.
