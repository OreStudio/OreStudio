One refresh request serves the scheduler and the Fetch action. A job per feed carries the rhythm, and the adapter no longer polls on its own flags.

* Acceptance

- The request names the feed, the scope and a day or a range; the adapter answers it.
- One scheduler job per feed, seeded from the feed schedule.
- The adapter poll and backfill flags are gone.
- An end-to-end test shows the scheduler and the Fetch action sending the same request.

* Decisions

- The scheduler owns the rhythm; the screen owns the on-demand trigger.
- A real_time feed is demand driven and a range fetch does not apply to it.
- The request is on the adapter contract, not a private message.
