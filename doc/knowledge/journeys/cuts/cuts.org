#+title: Cuts journeys
#+description: Taking a cut, pricing a report from one, and rerunning and auditing one: the journeys that fix the body of a valuation at a timepoint and consume it.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:user_journey:cut:valuation:
#+created: 2026-10-30
#+updated: 2026-10-30

A cut fixes a body of data at one timepoint so that a valuation can be
reproduced. Three journeys cover a person's side of that:
[[id:E840111E-AA2F-4746-8D49-A1D73742925E][taking one]],
[[id:9123193E-352D-4F46-8174-7763FA0A44CD][pricing a report from one]], and
[[id:8BD41683-EFA1-467A-B170-BE7041A745D7][rerunning and auditing one]].
The domain is on [[id:B5214F3A-A72E-421E-82BC-79338B2E95CB][Cuts]], and the
model the screens imply is on
[[id:F6362451-D569-4609-97A3-F1C785AAAF02][The cut is the body a run prices
from]].

The three read as one answer. The take fixes a body and has it signed; the run
names the cut and prices from it; the audit reproduces a figure from it or says
why it cannot. A cut that nobody consumes is a day's paperwork, and a run that
names no cut is a number whose production no screen can account for, which is
what the [[id:FB8F701C-E56A-49A9-AA90-1B6BD0B02092][report binding decision]]
records when it says that a binding to a curve leaves the deal population open.

* Journeys

#+ATTR_HTML: :class hug-leading
| Journey | Actor | Gist |
|---------+-------+------|
| [[id:E840111E-AA2F-4746-8D49-A1D73742925E][Take a cut]] | finance, market-risk, trading-desk | Choose the kind and its timepoint, assemble the body, review what it holds against the previous cut, take it, and have a second person authorise it — or read the gaps that stop it |
| [[id:9123193E-352D-4F46-8174-7763FA0A44CD][Price a report from a cut]] | market-risk, tenant administrator | Name the cut a report prices from, read what it holds and who signed it, be refused a cut that is not consumable, run the report, and read the card that names the cut and says whether the run reproduced |
| [[id:8BD41683-EFA1-467A-B170-BE7041A745D7][Rerun and audit a cut]] | auditor, market-risk, engineer | The body, the two acts, the runs that rested on the cut, and a rerun compared with its original in one of three verdicts |

Two of the three write: the take writes the cut and raises its authorisation, and
a rerun writes a run. The audit writes nothing, and the cut it reads is immutable
once taken.

* What a cut is here

The cluster states it. The body holds the deals, the market data, the reference
data, and the engine and code versions; the cut records the taking identity, the
authorisation and the gaps as the acts around the body. The occasion a cut is
taken for classifies it, and the purpose — a valuation that can be reproduced —
fixes the body for every occasion, so a flash cut and an end of day cut hold the
same four inputs.

Two facts about our system shape all three journeys. No cut exists today: no
component, no table, no read and no screen. And where a cut would fix a body, the
repository holds a read that re-resolves: the curve snapshot read carries no
as-of, and the trade store's intervals are rewritten when a correction arrives.
The journeys therefore name operations that do not exist, and each says so in its
*What is missing* section rather than drawing a screen the server cannot answer.

* The states, in one place

A cut is drawn in one of six states, and the derivation is on
[[id:F6362451-D569-4609-97A3-F1C785AAAF02][the decision note]]. The three
journeys use the same words, and the same words appear on the screens.

#+ATTR_HTML: :class hug-leading
| State | The person's reading | The run's answer |
|-------+----------------------+------------------|
| Draft | The take began and no body is fixed yet | Nothing |
| Incomplete | The body could not be assembled, and the gaps say why | Nothing, and the gaps name the remedy |
| Taken | The body is fixed and nobody has signed it | Nothing, and the refusal names the request it waits on |
| Authorised | A second person signed the body | It prices from the cut |
| Refused | The signature was refused, with the reason | Nothing; the remedy is a new take |
| Superseded | A later cut of the same kind and scope replaced it | It prices from the cut, and says a later cut is in use |

* Who reaches the screens

#+ATTR_HTML: :class hug-leading
| Actor | What they read | What they change |
|-------+----------------+------------------|
| finance | The cuts of their party, the body's parts, the gaps, and the requests they must decide | Take a cut; authorise one they did not take |
| market-risk | The same rows, and the independent verification cut they take | Take a cut; authorise one they did not take; choose the cut a report prices from |
| trading-desk | The flash cut of the day and the runs that priced from it | Take a flash cut |
| auditor | Every cut, its acts, its runs and the comparison of a rerun | Nothing |
| engineer | A comparison that differs, and the versions on each side | Nothing; the remedy is a change to the engine, which is another journey |

The reads are =reporting::cuts:read= and =reporting::report_instances:read=, the
take is =reporting::cuts:take=, and the decision is the inbox part's permission,
which the seeded =finance= part names. The three names are proposals, and the
[[id:F6362451-D569-4609-97A3-F1C785AAAF02][decision note]] states why they take
the shape they do.

* Where the screens are

The area is Reporting, because a cut exists for the run that consumes it and a
person who asks what a result was computed from looks where the reports are. The
trail of the list is Home, Reporting, Cuts; the record adds the label; the take
flow is a workflow at =/reporting/cuts/take=, listed in the Workflows menu of the
list header as the [[id:339F0405-43AE-4049-B6B3-EB12E5A38CA4][Menu structure]]
requires.

The opposite placement is rejected rather than unconsidered. Market data holds the
prices and curves that change by the minute, and a cut changes once a day and is
not a price; Trades holds what the tenant holds and does with a trade, and a cut
is not a trade. The reference data the take reads — the named cut times — is a
list that changes by the month and sits in Reference data, which is the one area
for such a list.

No BFF route serves reporting and no reporting screen exists, so every surface
these journeys name is new. Each journey records that gap, and the prototype draws
what the screens would be.

* The prototype

The [[file:../../prototypes/cuts/the-cut/][the cut prototype]] draws all
three journeys, with [[file:../../prototypes/cuts/the-cut.org][its design note]]
beside it. It is one plain page: the cuts list, the cut's record, the take flow,
the picker that consumes a cut, the run that names one, and the named cut times
of Reference data. Every state the three journeys name is behind the query string.
