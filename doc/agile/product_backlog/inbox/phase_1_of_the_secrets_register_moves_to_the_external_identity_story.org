* What

Promote this under [[id:0D2294A4-873A-47FE-908A-60BC21F7AAA9][Sign a person up and in through an external identity provider]] when its token exchange exists: the gateway resolves a provider's client secret through the vault, and the provider's row names the vault entry instead of an environment variable.

* Why

The register's phase 1 is the only piece of the secrets work that has not landed, and it waits on something another story owns. A provider's registry row already carries =client_secret_ref=, which names a lookup rather than a value, and nothing reads it until the exchange asks for it.

Everything it will stand on is in place: the vault serves the write, the resolve and the sealed delivery; the gateway holds its own key pair and registers its public half; provisioning writes any consumer and any value the deployment states; and the boot order that lets a workload reach the vault with no token is written down and exercised by every service at start.
