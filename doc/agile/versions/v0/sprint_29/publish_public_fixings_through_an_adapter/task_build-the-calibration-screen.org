items a generator starts from, and understands the value it took and the

* Acceptance

- The class screen lists every feed of the asset class with its start, source
  and state, filters by all, needs attention and overridden, and opens a feed's
  record; it never selects a subset for calibration.
- The list is clean: a class switch, a one-line policy summary with a link,
  the counts, the filters and the feeds. It carries no policy form.
- A feed detail has two tabs, with a way back to the list: *Feed record*
  carries the start, the candidates, the measures and the exceptions; *Flow*
  explains how it resolved and links to the policy.
- The policy is its own class-level screen with the coverage and the
  calibration record, warning that a change affects every feed of the
  class.
- With no configuration the screens state automatic calibration and the
  defaults, and every feed resolves.
- The class screen optionally states the policy: window, inverse allowed,
  provider order and the fallback, and shows the coverage across every feed of
  the class.
- The feed page shows the start derived from that policy, the candidate list
  with each candidate's reason, and the measured volatility and its factor.
- A person pins an item or a window on one feed as an exception; the page says
  that the feed overrides the policy, and offers the way back to the policy.
- A calibration records each feed's start and the policy it used.
- The screens carry no vintage control or field, and a feed with no usable
  candidate shows the policy's fallback or refusal.

* Plan

- The resolver reports every candidate it read, in rank order, with its
  outcome and reason; the calibrate read carries the list.
- Three screens under =/market-data/calibration/{class}=: the list, a feed's
  record with the Feed record and Flow tabs, and the policy.
- The BFF saves a class's policy and brings its provider order to the list
  given, or returns the class to automatic; it pins one candidate on a feed
  or clears the feed's items.
- The feed's own window is edited on the FX feed form, which now keeps it
  through an engine change.
- A calibration is not stored. The calibrate read is the record: each feed's
  start and the policy it used, shown on the policy screen. Nothing reads a
  history yet, so none is kept.

* Result

The calibration screens are built. The list shows every feed of a class with
its start, source and state, behind a class switch, the policy in one line,
the coverage counts and three filters. A feed's record shows its start, the
candidates with each one's reason, the measures and its exceptions; a person
pins a candidate or clears the feed's items there, and the Flow tab explains
the path from the policy to the process. The policy screen states and edits
the window, the inverse rule, the provider order and the fallback, warns that
a change affects every feed, and shows the coverage and each feed's start;
only the system tenant writes it. With no policy the screens say the class
calibrates on the defaults. No screen names a vintage.
