A [[id:A7FCC423-1CA3-4345-BF5B-18D54207B00B][report]] page describes one report in
depth: what decision it feeds, who reads it, how often it runs, what it reads and
produces, the measures it carries, the ORE XML that configures it, the
parameters a definition of it states, and the test that exercises it. The page is
written from the report's definition and its configuration, never from one run of
it. A run is a report instance, and where a run stopped belongs on the task or
the investigation that owns it.

This folder holds the pages for individual reports, as =report_<slug>.org=. It
sits beside [[proj:doc/knowledge/workflows][=doc/knowledge/workflows/=]] for
the same reason: a report page is a durable statement about a report, and it
outlives the sprint that wrote it.

* What belongs here, and what does not

- A page here owns *one report* — its purpose, audience, frequency, inputs,
  outputs, measures, ORE configuration, parameters and test coverage.
- The *pipeline* that runs a report is not this folder's subject. The phases, the
  run configuration, the compute handoff and the substitution mechanism live in
  [[id:61EA2FC7-C112-44FB-8042-9BD7994B71E9][Report Processing]] under
  [[proj:doc/knowledge/architecture][=doc/knowledge/architecture/=]]. A report
  page links that cluster; it never restates it.
- The *business catalogue* of report families, their classification axes and
  their measures lives on
  [[file:../domain/risk_reporting.org][Risk reporting]]. A report page cites
  the axes and the measure definitions rather than copying them.
- A fact about one *run* — where it stopped, what it produced, what failed —
  belongs on the task or the investigation that owns that run.

* Reports catalogued but not yet given a page

Two catalogues name reports that have no page here yet. They are the migration
queue, and they do not agree with each other.

- The business catalogue on
  [[file:../domain/risk_reporting.org][Risk reporting]] describes 22 market risk
  reports, each with its purpose, its measures and its five classification axes.
- The seed =projects/ores.sql/populate/reporting/reporting_report_definitions_populate.sql=
  creates 31 operational report definitions, all of type =risk=.
- The two *report types* the seed registers are =risk= and =grid=
  (see [[file:../architecture/report_definition_and_schedule.org][Report definition and schedule]]).

Migrating a report out of a catalogue and into this folder is the point at which
it acquires a purpose, an audience and a worked ORE configuration in one place.
Until then, the catalogues above are the only description, and they disagree
about how many reports there are.

* Adding a report

#+begin_src sh :results verbatim
compass add report \
  --slug <slug> \
  --title "<title>" \
  --description "<one line>" \
  --report-code <code> \
  --tags "report,<topic>"
#+end_src

=--parent-dir= defaults to =doc/knowledge/reports=, so it can be omitted. The
file is =report_<slug>.org=, and =--report-code= defaults to the slug. Fill in
every section the scaffold leaves, then add a row to the table above. The full
contract is on
[[id:4498456E-71DE-4250-8080-727690FAEFE6][Document type: report]].
