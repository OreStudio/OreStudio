* Goal

The system administrator sets how the installation's market behaves while it
runs, without restarting the synthetic service: how often the regime turns,
how hard a panic spreads, and how fast the simulated world runs. Today the
regime rates, the heat's jump and decay, and the market speed are fixed in the
service at start.

* Acceptance

- The scheduler takes a command that sets the regime's two transition rates,
  the heat's jump and decay, and the market speed, applied at the clock's now
  and recorded in the trace.
- A set whose heat loop gain is one or more, or a speed that is not positive, is
  refused with the number named.
- A speed change reaches a curve's step when the curve next starts, and the tab
  says so.
- The Market regime tab offers the five numbers to a system session beside the
  switch, and shows them read-only to a tenant.

* Plan

- The clock gets =reconfigure=. It validates first, through the same check as
  its constructor, so a refusal changes nothing. It keeps the heat and the
  dwell's unit draw, and computes the calm multiplier again.
- The clock counts simulated seconds beside tau, each stretch at the speed in
  force. A tick's simulated seconds are the change in that count, so a speed
  change splits correctly across a gap.
- The scheduler's =configure= is a command like =pin=. It validates in the
  caller's thread, then applies on the loop and records a =configure= event.
- A curve keeps the speed it was built with as its step length. A spot feed
  takes the new speed at once.
- One NATS verb, =synthetic.v1.ops.configure_market=, system-only like the pin.
  It replies with the regime, as a pin does. A refusal is =success= false with
  the clock's message.
- Shell =synthetic regime set name=value ...=; BFF =PUT
  /api/synthetic/market-regime/settings=; a form on the tab.

* Result

The system tenant now sets the chain's two rates, the heat's jump and decay,
and the market speed while the service runs. The clock validates first and
then reconfigures from its now, keeping the heat and the dwell's unit draw. It
counts simulated seconds at the speed in force, so a spot feed takes a new
speed at its next tick. A curve keeps the step it started with.

The verb is =synthetic.v1.ops.configure_market=, and the pin's system-only
guard protects it. The shell has =synthetic regime set=, the BFF has =PUT
/api/synthetic/market-regime/settings=, and the tab has a form. A tenant sees
the five numbers read only.

Live check on prime_origin:
- A tenant admin was refused with forbidden.
- =jump=40= was refused: "the heat's loop gain, jump / decay, is 2".
- =speed=-1= was refused: "speed is -1".
- =wake_rate=0.01= was refused, because an active share of 0.75 leaves no
  positive calm multiplier at an active multiplier of 2.
- =speed=2880 wake_rate=0.003= applied, the trace showed one =configure=
  event, and the restore applied.

Tests: the quant suite and the synthetic api suite pass under ctest. The web
vitest (202) and the BFF regime tests (19) pass. The harness spec passes 9 of 9.
