* Goal

=:depends_on:= in =projects/modeling/service_registry.org= becomes a list,
and each service lists every service it calls, at start or at run time. The
systemd unit generator and the service runner both read the list. A check
compares each list with the subjects the service's code calls, so a new call
to another service cannot go undeclared.

* Acceptance

- =:depends_on:= takes a list, and codegen and =compass systemd= read it.
- The synthetic service lists the market data service among the others it
  calls.
- A check fails when a service calls a subject of a service its list does
  not name, and names both.
- The check's rule is data, as for the other checks compass-ng ports.
