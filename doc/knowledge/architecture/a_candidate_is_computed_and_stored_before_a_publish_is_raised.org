#+title: A candidate is computed and stored before a publish is raised
#+description: Every kind of derived market object is computed by an operation of its own into a stored, versioned candidate with its lineage and checks, so a decider approves numbers that exist.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :architecture:marketdata:publish:
#+created: 2026-11-11
#+updated: 2026-11-11

* Summary

A publish is a decision about numbers, so the numbers exist before anyone is asked. Each kind has a compute operation that computes a candidate for a subject at an as-of and stores it, with its lineage and its checks, as a version. The raise names that version, the decider reads it, and the apply publishes it. Nothing is computed at the raise or at the apply.

* Detail

** What a candidate is

The compute writes the candidate's points as observations of the subject's output series at the as-of, each with a lineage record naming the series it read and the version of the recipe that produced it ([[id:573F9165-C20B-463C-B0B3-F324AFE4E81F][Curve point provenance]]). The output series is the subject's own, named by its oresmd identity ([[id:C3E053CA-0D4B-480B-9119-E11530160EC1][oresmd: ORE Studio Market Data URI]]). The candidate is traceable and it is not authoritative: no report and no screen of published data reads it.

** One candidate per subject and as-of, versioned

A candidate is keyed by its subject and its as-of. Computing it again writes a new version and keeps the old one. A request names the version it was raised for, so a decision never covers numbers computed after it.

** The checks

The compute runs the kind's checks and stores their result with the version: for a curve, the bootstrap's checks; for a forward set, its inputs' states; for a surface, convergence, the pillar errors and the arbitrage checks. The decider reads them on the request, so a refusal can name what failed.

** Why before the raise

A decider approves what the trader saw. If the numbers were computed at the apply, the published set would be whatever the market held at that moment, and the approval would cover nothing in particular. If they were computed at the raise, a trader could not inspect them before asking.
