Whether a person is reached through one channel for one kind. The key is the
account, the kind and the channel together, so a person holds one row for each
pair they have chosen; no row means the kind's default.

The in-application copy is always kept, and a kind that is not
=mail_optional= ignores a choice to turn its mail off. The service applies both
rules when it delivers.
