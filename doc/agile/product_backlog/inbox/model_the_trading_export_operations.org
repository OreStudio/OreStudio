* What

Two trading verbs are not entity verbs: =trading.v1.trades.portfolio.export=
and =trading.v1.trades.export-to-storage=. Their request and response structs
are declared in a =cpp= block in =ores.trading.trade.org=, and codegen pastes
that block into the generated =trade_protocol.hpp=. Their handlers are
hand-written in =registrar_trades.cpp=. Write one operation model per verb,
in the shape that =ores.dq.publish_bundle= uses. The protocol and its
TypeScript twin then generate, and the handlers stay hand-written.

* Why

Items P04 and M06 of the Component Clean Standard want every operation
that is not an entity verb as an operation model, and no hand-written wire
type. These two verbs are the only gap for both items in ores.trading. The
PR 7 task recorded the fix as the next unit and did not take it.
