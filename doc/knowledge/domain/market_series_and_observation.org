#+title: Series and observation
#+description: What a market series catalogues against what an observation records, the soft update rule for a correction, and why observations carry no audit columns.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :marketdata:storage:series:observations:
#+created: 2026-10-06
#+updated: 2026-10-06

* Summary

Two tables hold the stored result of the pipeline. A =market_series=
catalogues what is being observed and is declared in
=ores.marketdata.api/domain/market_series.hpp=. A =market_observation=
records one observed value and is declared in
=ores.marketdata.api/domain/market_observation.hpp=.

* Detail

** What each one holds

=market_series= holds =oresmd_uri=, the series URI of the datum, plus
=series_subclass=, a code from reference data for the coarse taxonomy, and
the set of asset classes in =market_series_asset_classes=. A series is
identified by its oresmd URI together with its party, and it is unique per
party. The table keeps no decomposed copy of the key, so a reader that
wants a part of the key reads it through the codec.

=market_observation= holds =series_id=, =observation_datetime=, =oresmd_uri=
and =value=. The URI is the row's only identity. The ORE key is not stored,
because =ore_key_codec= writes it from the datum the URI names, so a row
cannot hold two identities that disagree.

** The soft update rule

=market_observations_tbl= is a TimescaleDB hypertable partitioned by
=observation_datetime= with 30 day chunks. A correction replaces the
previous value through the soft update trigger rather than by a delete and
an insert. GIST exclusion and DELETE rules are incompatible with
hypertables, so uniqueness is enforced by a partial unique index and by the
soft update and soft delete trigger pair.

** Why observations carry no audit columns

The observation type has no =version=, =modified_by=, =performed_by=,
=change_reason_code= or =change_commentary=. Tick level data volumes make
those columns impractical. =observation_datetime= is the financial valid
time, and =valid_from= and =valid_to= carry the transaction time.

** The producer kind stamp

=market_series.producer_kind= defaults to VENDOR. The ingest loop stamps it
from the binding the tick arrived under when it creates the series, and it
leaves an existing series alone. The column therefore records the producer
that created the row rather than the last writer to touch it.

** The derivation marker on the series

=market_series.derivation_kind= defaults to OBSERVED, and a derived series
carries a named mechanism such as IR_CURVE_BOOTSTRAP. It pairs with
=derivation_config_id= and =derivation_config_version=, which hold their
nil and zero sentinels when the kind is OBSERVED. This is a catalog level
marker, and it is a different thing from the per observation lineage row
stated in [[id:78B65BF0-C003-48F6-84E3-615A36D39266][Lineage and provenance]].
