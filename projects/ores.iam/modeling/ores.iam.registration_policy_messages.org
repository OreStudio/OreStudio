The registration policy protocol messages.

The door asks one question before it offers a form, and this read answers
it: whether the deployment accepts registrations, whether one also waits on
an approval step, the tenant the registration lands in, the party and role
the new account receives, and whether the result is usable at once. The
policy itself is settled in
[[id:84706C3E-D48D-4A4B-967A-4AADB3CF44B2][registration and onboarding
defaults]], and the interactions that read it are
[[id:504863AB-C45A-47E8-BB77-AADCB620A305][the entry journeys]].

The read needs no session, because the screen that shows the form is the one
a person who is not in the deployment yet is looking at. It is separate from
=iam.v1.bootstrap.status=: one answers whether the deployment has been set
up, the other what it offers a person who is not in it, and they change for
different reasons.

The address the request arrived at names the tenant. A client states the
hostname it was served at, and the service resolves the tenant from it,
falling back to the tenant flagged =is_registration_default= only when the
address names none.

/**
 * @brief The stable code a client branches on.
 *
 * Empty when the read answered. @c signup_disabled and
 * @c signup_requires_authorization mean the deployment refuses the form;
 * @c no_registration_destination means it accepts registrations but has not
 * said where they land. @c no_default_role means it has not said what a new
 * account may do. Each names a different thing for an administrator to fix,
 * so a screen states the reason rather than one closed door.
 */
