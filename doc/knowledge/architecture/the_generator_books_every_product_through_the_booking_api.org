* Summary

The synthetic trade generator books every product in every form it offers
through the booking API ([[id:3085E65E-413B-4E7A-B60A-5844A124C999][One booking API books a product in either form]]), drawing each product's terms against
its schema ([[id:290A1069-A1EE-4DD1-8D23-1C60F76F8949][Every product has a schema of its fields and its forms]]) by rules held as data per field: an underlying from
the scope, a level a stated distance from the underlying's price, a monthly
schedule to expiry. So the path a person books on is the path the generated
trades prove, and the ORE engine test proves ORE reads, builds and values every
product and form.

* Detail

** What it proves

- Every product books in every form: the generated population holds one trade
  of each product in each form.
- ORE reads and builds each, with no error the trade causes; the test's known
  list holds only market data and engine configuration lines.
- A product's two forms agree: the test values both and compares them, and a
  pair that differs is listed with its reason and a bound.

** Why levels are drawn from the price

A barrier or a strike drawn as a fixed literal is reached by some underlyings
and never by others. A level a stated distance from the underlying's price is
reached as the product intends, so a barrier script's barrier logic is
exercised.

** Where today's code differs

The generator books native trades through builders, then books scripted forms
by evaluating recipes over the native rows in =ores.synthetic.core=, and books
script-only products from a carrier type's rows with barriers set at levels no
underlying reaches.
