* Goal

The rates family has a header: =ores_trading_rate_instruments_tbl= carries the
family's coverage check, the fields the products share, the trade-activity key
and the delete cascade that owns the family's rows. The eight product tables
become fact tables keyed by the same trade, and the import and the export read
and write the header beside them.

* Acceptance

- =rate_instruments= exists with the family's coverage check over its codes,
  =start_date=, =maturity_date=, =description=, the trade-activity key, the
  audit spine, tenant isolation and a =* Delete cascade= that names the
  family's legs, leg children and fact tables.
- The eight product tables are fact tables: =trade_id= as a flat primary key,
  =trade_activity_id= flat, their own fields, no =instrument_identity=, no
  =party_id=, no =trade_type_code=, no shared dates, tenant isolation.
- The trade-type catalogue routes the family's codes to
  =rate_instruments=, so the header's check lists exactly them.
- The import writes the header row and the fact row, and the export reads both;
  =ores.ore.core.tests= passes at the new shape, including a delete that closes
  the family.

* Plan

The shape is the bond pilot's, so each step is a copy of something that already
works rather than a new mechanism.

1. *The header model.* =ores.trading.rate_instruments= mirrors
   =ores.trading.bond_instrument=: =:profile: trading-instrument=, the identity
   group =ores.trading.instrument_identity=, the audit group, =:routed_by:= the
   trade-type catalogue with =:routed_column: trade_type_code=,
   =:rls_tenant_isolation: true=, the party and trade-type indexes, and a
   =* Delete cascade= section naming =swap_legs=, the two leg children,
   =callable_swap_call_dates= and the eight fact tables, each with
   =:table:= and =:column: trade_id=.
2. *A fact table's shape.* =ores.trading.bond_option= is the template: flat
   =trade_id= as the primary key, flat =trade_activity_id=, the product's own
   fields, a =* Foreign keys= section for the two the facts keep, and
   =:rls_tenant_isolation: true=. Each of the eight models drops its identity
   group, its =party_id=, its =trade_type_code= and the shared dates, and keeps
   its own fields.
3. *The catalogue.* The nine rates codes move from their product tables to
   =rate_instruments=. That is what turns the header's check into the family's
   coverage check, and it is why the products must be converted in the same
   change: =instrument_table_for= starts answering =rate_instruments= for every
   rates code, so the export reads the header for the instrument and the fact
   table for the fields.
4. *The carrier and the mapper.* =swap_instrument_data= gains the header, so a
   rates instrument in memory is the header plus its leaf plus its children.
   The forward mappers fill the header's fields from the document, as they fill
   the leaf's today, and the reverse mappers take it. The import writes the
   header row before the fact row; the export service reads the header rows
   into the batch's =rate_instruments= array and the facts into theirs, and
   =rebuild_instrument= joins them by trade id, exactly as it joins the legs.
5. *Order of work.* Author the header and the catalogue entry first, then
   convert one product end to end - FRA, which has the fewest own fields - with
   a build and the ORE round trip between each conversion. Each conversion is
   verifiable on its own, and the family is never half-routed: a product's codes
   move to the catalogue's header row in the same change that converts it.

* Notes

- The story's acceptance says "each product fact table checks its own" trade
  type. The pilot this family follows does not do that: =bond_instruments=
  carries the family's whole check and the bond fact tables carry none, and they
  join the header by trade id. The catalogue can state one routing per trade
  type, so a fact table cannot hold a check of its own while the header holds
  the family's. This task follows the pilot and the design's entity table; the
  acceptance line is reconciled when the story closes.
- The staging unit also carries two clauses that are already done and are not
  part of this task: the cross-currency swap's type (its own task) and, in
  part, the seven unwritten columns. What remains here is the header, the field
  move and the routing.
- =description= and =trade_activity_id= are written by the handler and
  =stamp_ids= rather than by the mapper, so the field move touches them in the
  handler; =start_date= and =maturity_date= are the mapper's (19 and 14 uses,
  the family's most-used fields) and are the bulk of the mechanical work.
- The three unrouted codes (RateDigitalOption, SwaptionStraddle,
  ForwardVolatilityAgreement) have no table and are out of scope, as the design
  records.
