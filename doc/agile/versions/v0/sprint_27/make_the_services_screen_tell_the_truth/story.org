* Decisions

- The heartbeat is a publish on =telemetry.v1.ops.service_heartbeat=, which needs no session. The generated TypeScript protocol already carries the subject and its message, so the web service reuses it. Its first step proves the web service's NATS identity may publish on that subject.
- The overview needs no new backend. The roster read gives running, lost and missing; the logs read gives a total for any level and range, so the error count is a read with a page size of one.
- The overview lives on the Operations services screen, the screen that answers whether the installation is healthy. The system home shows the same figures in its health block, from one shared hook and one figures component, so the two cannot disagree.
- The home keeps its tenant health figures. Tenant health and installation health are different questions, and the home states both.
- The operations hub stays a plain list of cards.

* Out of scope

- A last-tick or uptime history for services. The services journey records it as a gap.
- Alerting when a service goes quiet.
