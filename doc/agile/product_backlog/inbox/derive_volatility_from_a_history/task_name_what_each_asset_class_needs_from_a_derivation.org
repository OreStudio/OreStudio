* Goal

One page states, for every asset class, what the platform needs to derive its
volatility: the series it holds, the transform, the coordinates a quote is named by, the
smile model and the object a published surface must be. It says which classes can be
served now, which wait on a history, and it is the list the engine is built against.

* Acceptance

- Every asset class is named: FX, interest rates, credit, equity, commodity and inflation, with its volatility kinds (FX options, swaptions, caps and floors, CDS, equity options, commodity options, inflation caps and floors).
- For each class the page names the series the platform holds or imports, and whether it is a free source, a generated series or nothing. The overnight fixings, the spot fixings and the generated curves are held; a swap-rate history, a CDS spread history and a commodity forward history are not, unless the page says where they are.
- For each class the page states the transform (a log return or a difference), the coordinates a quote is named by, the smile model and the shape of the surface a publish must write.
- The page states which classes the platform can serve today and gives the reason a class cannot be served: no history, no free source, or a datum the key grammar cannot name.
- The page is the list the engine is built against: a class it names servable is three parameters and a series, and a class it names unservable is out of scope with a reason.

* Notes

- The store already names every class the taxonomy holds (=refdata_asset_class_codes=): fx, interest_rates, credit, equity, commodity, inflation, bond and scripted. A class with no volatility kind needs no derivation, and the page says so instead of leaving it out.
- The datum schema and the key grammar cover the volatility of every class: =fx_option=, =swaption=, =capfloor=, =cds= with a spread quote, =equity_option=, =commodity_option=, =inflation_capfloor= and =correlation= (=schema.hpp=).
- The platform imports free histories for FX spots and overnight rates, and a generated series for every curve the simulator steps. A class whose quoted history exists only inside a paid feed cannot be derived without a fabricated history, and the page states that rather than inventing one.
