* Goal

A feed binding says that a party consumes one producer source. Today a binding is made by provisioning, a person can only read it through the operations journey, and the series it feeds and the last tick are not shown anywhere. This story designs how a person manages bindings: list them, say what each source writes and when it last ticked, bind and rebind a party to a source, enable and disable a binding, and start or stop the feed behind it. The journey covers two scenarios. Synthetic data is the one the product supports. Real data from a vendor is drawn in the journey and the prototype so the design holds it, and it is marked as not supported, so no screen offers to connect a real feed. The prototype comes first and is signed off before any screen is built. The snags that the oresmd link story left are tasks of this story.

* Decisions

- The journey extends the existing journey [[id:986CED57-341F-49DE-8F5D-F186771F10D8][Keep the market data feeds running]], which lists bindings and starts and stops feeds for the operations area. The new journey is about managing the bindings: what a binding connects, how it is made and changed, and what it feeds. The two journeys link to each other, and the new one does not repeat the old one's steps.
- Synthetic data is the supported scenario, and a person can follow it to the end. Real data from a vendor is drawn in the journey and the prototype and marked as not supported, so the design holds both and no screen offers to connect a real feed. Marco decided this on 2026-10-13.
- The prototype comes first and Marco signs it off before the screen is built, as for the other prototyped journeys.

* Out of scope

- Connecting a real vendor feed. The prototype and the journey show the case and say that it is not supported.
- A new producer of synthetic data. The story manages the bindings of the producers that exist.
