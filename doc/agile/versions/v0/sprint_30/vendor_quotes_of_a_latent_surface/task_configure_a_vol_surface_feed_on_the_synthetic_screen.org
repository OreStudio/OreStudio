* Goal

A person who runs the synthetic market sees and changes how each FX
volatility surface is generated, on the same screens as the FX spot and IR
curve feeds. The Provider tab of =/market-data/synthetic= lists a
provider's vol surface feeds beside its spot and curve feeds, and a vol
surface feed has its own page: its pair, ticks and arrival, the SSVI
start and dynamics, the correlations with spot, the expiry ladder and the
contributors that quote it.

Gamma, the SSVI power-law exponent, shows on the page as fixed at one half
and cannot be edited. The generator's arbitrage bounds hold only at that
value: with gamma at one half, condition 2 of Gatheral and Jacquier holds
for every rho and condition 4 reduces to eta at most the square root of
two, which is the bound the curvature driver keeps. Any other gamma makes
both conditions depend on theta and rho expiry by expiry, so an edited
gamma could make the generator publish an arbitrageable surface. See
[[id:5910304A-FA45-461E-B2A2-8F24FA798769][SVI Volatility Parameterisation]] and
[[id:BB2B7C4C-FD4E-4DCA-BF1D-00F929622B87][Synthetic market data generation: approach]].

* Acceptance

- The Provider tab lists a provider's vol surface feeds, grouped and counted
  as its FX spot and IR curve feeds are, and starts and stops them.
- A vol surface feed's page follows the FX and IR feed pages: details,
  calibration, its own tabs and history, with add, edit and revert.
- A person edits the SSVI start (skew and curvature), the skew and curvature
  dynamics, the three correlations, and each expiry's ATM vol and
  forward-variance process, and the screen refuses a value outside its
  bound before the save.
- Gamma shows as fixed at one half, with the reason, and no form edits it.
- The screens are in English, French and Portuguese, and tested.
