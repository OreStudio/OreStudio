#+description: After the trade model declared the counterparty and the trade type filterable and the trade type searchable, four needs of the trade list remain: a search by the trade's identifier, a filter by state and by book, filters on the enum columns, and by-trade reads for the child tables.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The story "Filter and search trades through the model" declared the counterparty and the trade type filterable, and the trade type searchable and sortable. Four needs of the trade list remain.

- *Search by identifier.* The firm's trade identifier is a row of =trade_identifier=, not a column of the trade, so the standard =search= member cannot read it. Decide whether the trade carries a display identifier of its own, or whether a search may read a child table.
- *State and book.* The state is a column of =trade_state= and the book of =trade_booking=. A list that filters by either needs a filterable column on the read it uses, or the standard scoped read =list_by_book_id= with the subtree scope.
- *The enum columns.* The booking nature, the entry channel and the counterparty scope are enums. No model declares an enum column filterable yet, so the generator's support for one is unproven.
- *Child tables.* The identifiers, the party roles, the portfolios, the additional fields and the instrument's parts have no filter at all. A trade page needs a read by trade id for each.

* Why

The trade screens read through the standard entity protocol and invent no operation. What they need beyond it is a change to the model.
