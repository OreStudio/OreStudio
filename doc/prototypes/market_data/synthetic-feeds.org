* Summary

The synthetic feeds, as one screen set after demand replaced start and stop.
The dashboard opens with the totals and then the providers. The Provider screen
chooses one provider, shows its tree and its feeds. A feed keeps its record
page and its process tab. The sources board and subscriptions are reached from
the same tree.

Every number counts one state: a feed serves when its provider's source serves
and is demanded at least one id. The old tree and the old Market Simulator are
gone with their prototypes.

* Screens

=screen=dashboard|provider|feed|sources|subscriptions=, with =provider=, =feed=
and =tab= for the detail.

- Dashboard. Five tiles, then the provider list. A demanded provider that does
  not serve is the only alert.
- Provider. A combo at the top, then a group-by control and a collection
  filter, then the feed grid grouped by a feed property: asset class by
  default, instrument or engine. No folder tree. The low folder levels were
  derived containers for asset class and instrument, and the grid can group by
  the same properties directly. Start and Stop are the operator test control.
- Feed. The record page and its tabs: Details, Price behaviour or Process,
  Ticks, History. The demand line says who makes it run.
- Sources. The operations board as it stands.
- Subscriptions. A party's rights and the source its route picks.

* States

A provider is serving or stopped. A feed is serving or stopped by the same
rule. Quiet and stale sources appear on the sources board. The mock covers
serving, stopped, no demand and stale.

* What is fake

Mock data. No service, no session, no build step. The numbers follow the live
provisioned tenant.
