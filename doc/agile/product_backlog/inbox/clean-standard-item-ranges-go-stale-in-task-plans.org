#+title: The clean-standard item ranges go stale in the component task plans
#+description: Every clean-* task plan enumerates the standard's section ranges, and the standard has grown twice since the tasks were scaffolded, so the plans name ranges that no longer cover the standard and the five items added later are invisible to the worker.
#+type: capture
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :codegen:clean_standard:docs:agile:
#+created: 2026-09-26
#+updated: 2026-09-26

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Each =task_clean_*.org= under a clean-* story carries a copy of the same
=* Plan= section, and that plan enumerates the standard's sections with
hard item ranges:

#+begin_src org
1. Baseline and inventory (B01 to B05). Record the baseline before any edit.
4. Generation (G01 to G06).
7. Structure and hygiene (H01 to H03).
8. Verification (V01 to V06), ending with the registry entry.
#+end_src

The [[id:7B477025-132E-4B20-80B4-D8C9420880B8][Component Clean Standard]]
has grown twice since those plans were written. The survey of the four
model-free infrastructure components added B06, G07, H04, V07 and V08, and
the =ores.security= pass added G08. The plan now stops short of the
standard by up to three items in two sections, and a worker who follows the
plan literally never runs H04, V07 or V08.

Of the 35 tasks, 22 are BACKLOG and one is STARTED, so the stale plan is
live instruction for 23 of them. The ranges are copied into every task
rather than generated, so no edit to the standard reaches them and nothing
keeps them in step.

* Why

Two review rounds already paid for this defect.

[[id:D042AF8C-F308-46E0-9646-B9BEA4FADC91][Bring ores.logging to the clean standard]]
was failed by independent verification: "The standard grew to 47 items on
main and the table had 42." B06, G07, H04, V07 and V08 were missing from
its plan, its notes and its evidence.

[[id:74AED0CF-573B-4179-8C75-A9572EE49B2B][Bring ores.compute to the clean standard]]
carried the same finding: "Five standard items (B06, G07, H04, V07, V08)
are absent from the Plan table, Notes and evidence." Both records now say
the table holds 47 items, which G08 has since made 48.

Both rounds fixed the task in front of them. Neither fixed the cause, which
is that the plan text is duplicated rather than derived from the standard.

Two ways out, in order of preference:

1. Remove the ranges from the plan and name the sections only. The sentence
   above the list already points at the standard, which is the one place
   that holds the ids, so the enumeration is redundant and will go stale
   again the next time the standard grows.
2. Keep the ranges and generate them, so that the standard is the single
   source and a check fails when the two disagree.

Either way the sweep covers the 23 live tasks in one mechanical change;
that is what makes it a capture rather than a fix on the component pass
that noticed it.
