* Notes

The discovery behind this table is written up in full at
[[file:analysis.org][Analysis: where ores.marketdata stands]], with the raw
surveys under =tmp/marketdata_analysis/=.

** What the environment would not let us do

=compass build= cannot run in this environment: its =systemd-run --user
--scope= wrapper fails with "Failed to connect to user scope bus". The
same targets build through =cmake --build --preset linux-clang-debug-make=
directly, and that is how V01 and V02 were measured. The two suites that
read the tables need =ORES_TEST_DB_USER= from =.env= and a live database.

** The shape of the work

Six PRs, in order. PR 1 fixes the models and the one generator defect at
source and regenerates nothing, because regeneration before those fixes
would not compile. PR 2 points the suite at the ORE corpus and fixes the
two parser defects it finds. PR 3 is the regeneration, the consumer
migration and the dead-code deletion. PRs 4 and 5 are the oresmd
native-identity cutover the abandoned sprint 25 story designed. PR 6 joins
the registry.

** What the corpus says about oresmd

ORE's shape table defines 43 series types. oresmd declares 29 of them, so
14 are unrepresented; measured against the committed corpus at
=external/ore/examples/=, those 14 are 48.8% of the key lines. The larger
problem is the other direction: 9 of the 29 declared types still cannot
express the keys ORE actually writes.

The shape table and the oresmd models contradicted each other, and the
shape table was right. =ores_series_key_shapes_populate.sql= states every
shape correctly -- it was corrected from the corpus -- and the oresmd
models were never brought into line. FRA and IMM_FRA were declared with an
index where ORE writes =ccy/start/length=, so all 2,273 of their corpus
lines projected nothing; MM's index-less form, 94% of its lines, was
rejected for want of an index the producer never wrote. All three now
project and round-trip.

What remains needs the identifier grammar extended rather than a shape
corrected: BASIS_SWAP and the two cross-currency swaps carry two tenors
and sometimes two currencies, ZERO carries a day count, the futures carry
a contract month and an exchange, CORRELATION carries two operands on a
strike surface, and CPR has no currency at all.

Two live defects fall out of the same walk. The IR 6-segment path discards
the settlement segment and rebuilds it as a hardcoded ="2D"=, so the 1,338
corpus keys carrying =0D= are stored as =2D= -- the row already differs
from the file. And the CSV tokeniser needs two commas, so a line with
exactly one aborts its whole file.

=projects/ores.codegen/scripts/check_oresmd_ore_coverage.py= makes the
coverage record permanent and rerunnable; =--corpus= adds the line counts.
It also enforces the test requirement: every modelled quote type carries a
projection test and a round-trip test, or a recorded reason. Seven of the
twenty-nine were one-sided when the check was written -- six with no round
trip and one with no projection -- which is how a projection that emits a
key its own inverse cannot read survives a green suite.
