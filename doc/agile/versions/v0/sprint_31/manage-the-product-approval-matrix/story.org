* Goal

A risk or reference data administrator reads and changes the product approval matrix from the web, at the party and at the book. The set a party permits and the set a book narrows are legible together, an attempt to widen a book past its party is refused in the person's own words, and the screen draws what the standard already draws rather than a shape of its own.

* Decisions

Settled on 2026-11-25, when the story was formed.

1. *The arc is the screen pipeline.* The four steps are the four tasks, in the
   order of [[id:61C6B0A5-03A5-4424-8EA3-EE7E587629FD][Take a topic through the screen pipeline]]: extract and judge
   the journeys, prototype, build, test and sign off. Each step ends in a
   document the owner signs off before the next begins.
2. *This is not a reference data topic.* The topics of
   [[id:C0D1A833-3E57-489A-A4D3-8C44266D3D6D][The screen pipeline topics]] are the Qt entities the web has yet to
   carry. The approval matrix is new: its sets and the rule between them
   arrived with [[id:F77263EF-6F2D-4129-BA60-CA6C2F2D6153][this story]]'s
   predecessor, so it takes the pipeline's steps without a row in that table.
3. *Two subjects, one reading.* The party's set is the maximum and the book's
   set narrows it, so the journeys must let a person see both at once or move
   between them without losing the comparison. Whether that is a tab of the
   party, a panel of the book, or a screen of its own is the journeys' to
   decide, and the prototype's job is to show the decision.
4. *The refusal is the screen's to state.* The database already refuses a book
   outside its party, with the party's set in the message. The screen must not
   let the attempt leave the page in the first place, and must read the
   database's refusal when it arrives.
