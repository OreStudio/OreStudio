#+title: Offer the trade vocabularies from their lookups in a trade editor
#+description: No screen edits a trade instrument, so the option type, position, settlement, exercise style and the other vocabularies the store now checks are offered nowhere. A trade editor should fill its choices from the lookup lists.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

When a screen edits a trade instrument, its option type, position, settlement,
exercise style, barrier type, averaging, price and return types, moment and
amortization fields offer their choices from the lookup tables the store checks
them against, read through the lookups' list operations.

* Why

The store now refuses a vocabulary value its lookup does not hold, on 43
trading columns. No screen edits a trade instrument today, so nothing offers
these choices, and a hand-typed value would be refused on save. The lookups are
already listable over the wire, for example =trading.v1.option_types.list=.
