* Acceptance

- A read by outcome returns the results with that outcome that the session may see.
- Each result in the answer leads to its workunit, and the job page of the workunit names its batch.

* Plan

The nats entity protocol defines the filter record, so the work is to declare the column
and carry the filter up.

- The result model declares =outcome= filterable. The generator adds =outcome= and
  =outcome_one_of= to the filter record and the repository condition.
- The wire-protocol read =readResultsByOutcome= sends the filter on the generated list, and
  the BFF serves it at =/api/operations/grid/jobs/results?outcome=N=, with the node hidden
  from a role that may not read it.
- The Jobs tab has an Outcome choice. When it is set, the tab lists the runs with that outcome
  in place of the tree, and the batch filters are off.
