* What

=currency_currency_groups= offers =list_by_currency_iso_code= and a filter on currency codes, so listing or counting one group's members pages through every membership in the tenant. Closing a group does not close its memberships. Add =list_by_currency_group_code= and a group filter, and refuse a group delete while members remain or close them with it.
