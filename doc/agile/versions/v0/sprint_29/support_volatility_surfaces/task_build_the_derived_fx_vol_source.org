* Goal

A derived source publishes, once a day, the ATM, risk reversal and butterfly
quotes of each catalogued FX pair it can infer from stored spot fixings, under
its own identity, as quotes of the same =fx_option= items a vendor would quote.
A surface generator can then start from them, and a vendor of the same items
ranked above it replaces it with no change downstream.

* Acceptance

- =ores.analytics.quant= computes the quotes natively: sample moments of daily
  log returns, their shrinkage toward authored priors, the Gram-Charlier smile
  of Backus, Foresi, Li and Wu held inside the region where it is a density, the
  term scaling of the moments, and the quotes of an FX option convention at the
  25 and 10 delta pillars, smile or broker butterfly, spot or forward delta,
  premium-adjusted or not, delta-neutral or forward ATM.
- The delta and ATM strike calculations reproduce QuantLib's black delta
  calculator tests; the broker butterfly reprices the broker strangle on the
  smile, as ORE's BF/RR surface test checks; the smile reproduces the paper's
  pillar coefficients.
- A derived adapter, hosted in the synthetic service under its own provider,
  announces its items, answers demand and publishes the day's quotes after the
  day's fixings, through the four adapter messages; its catalogue rows give the
  system party a subscription and a route.
- The derived quotes carry the derived provider's identity and no credit of the
  publishers whose fixings fed them.

* Plan

Two pull requests, each verified on its own.

1. The quant layer, pure and tested against QuantLib, ORE and the paper:
   the normal quantile and a Brent root; Garman-Kohlhagen deltas and strikes
   for the four delta types and the ATM types; the Gram-Charlier smile and the
   quotes of a convention, with the broker butterfly solved so the broker
   strangle reprices on the smile; return moments, shrinkage, the density
   region and the term scaling; and one entry point from a series of fixings
   and a set of expiries to the quotes.
2. The adapter: a derived source in the synthetic service that reads fixings
   through the market data reader and publishes the quotes; the provider, the
   feed, the vendor instruments mapped to the =fx_option= items, the scopes,
   the subscription and the route; the convention of each pair read from
   =fx_option_convention=.
