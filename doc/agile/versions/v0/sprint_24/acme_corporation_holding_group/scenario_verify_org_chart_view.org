** Open the Org Chart from the Admin menu

- With the User Accounts admin menu enabled (already logged in),
  select *User Accounts > Org Chart*.
- A new MDI subwindow titled "Org Chart" opens, showing a tree of
  cards fanning out from a single dark "Adrian Vance / Group Chief
  Executive Officer" card at the top.
- Confirms: the window has a working maximize button (double-click
  its title bar, or the maximize icon, expands it to fill the MDI
  area) — it behaves like a normal list window, not a fixed-size
  dialog.

*** Result

| Field  | Value |
|--------+-------|
| Status | PASS |

** Confirm every card shows photo, name, title, office

- Zoom/pan the chart (mouse wheel / click-drag) to inspect a few
  cards at different depths — e.g. Adrian Vance (root), one Country
  Head (e.g. Leanne Shaw), and one of their reports.
- Each card shows: a circular photo (not a blank/placeholder square),
  the person's full name, their job title (wrapped onto its own
  line(s), not cut off or overflowing the card), and an office/party
  label near the bottom of the card (e.g. "Acme Corporation UK plc"
  for UK staff, "Acme Corporation Plc" for Adrian Vance).
- Each of the three Country Head branches (UK/US/HK) renders in a
  visibly different pastel colour from the others and from the root's
  neutral dark card; every card within one branch shares that
  branch's colour.
