#+created: 2026-11-02
#+updated: 2026-11-02

ORE's =swaptionVolatility= is one =xs:all= with an optional =Dimension= and
every smile element optional, so the schema says only that the smile fields may
be absent. It cannot say that they are meaningless for an ATM surface: an ATM
surface has no smile, and a cube has one. That is the deficiency this part
corrects, as the FX smile part does for the FX entry. A row exists only where the
entry states a smile, and the export refuses a smile on an entry whose dimension
is not =Smile=.

The part holds =SmileOptionTenors=, =SmileSwapTenors= and =SmileSpreads=, each
a comma separated list in ORE's own text. =Dimension= stays on the entry,
because it is the entry's own setting and the smile only depends on it.

An entry holds at most one row of this part, so the generated table adds the
unique index on the live rows of =(tenant_id, swaption_volatility_config_id)=.
