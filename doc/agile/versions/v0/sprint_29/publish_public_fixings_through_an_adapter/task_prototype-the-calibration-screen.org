* Goal

person reads the ranked fixing items, the item and provider a generator will

fixed level.

* Acceptance

- Three variants of one screen sit on a static page under
  =doc/prototypes/market_data/calibrate-a-generator/=, switchable by =?variant=
  and by a bottom bar, with no build and no session.
- Each variant carries the ranked items with rank, item URI, provider, policy
  and window; the resolved start with the value, the fixing date, the time
  received, whether it was inverted and the provider's credit; the measured
  volatility and the scale it applies; and the fallback reason where none
  applies.
- It carries no vintage control, and it states that vintage is retired.
- The group index lists it with review none, and the design note sits beside
  the page.

* Plan

- Mock one synthetic EUR/USD config, five ranked items (ECB direct, IMF stale,
  ECB inverted, a provider whose terms are not accepted, an item with no
  fixing), and a market clock of 2026-10-31. The resolver mirrors the runner:
  rank order, =latest_on_or_before_clock= or =exact:<date>=, the window, the

  the fallback. Variant B is a ladder: one card per item with its own reason,

* Notes

The page is at
[[file:../../../../../prototypes/market_data/calibrate-a-generator/index.html][calibrate-a-generator]]. It carries three variants of the calibration screen,
switchable by =?variant= or the bottom bar:

- *A, resolution first* — the start card, then the ranked table, then the trail
  and the fixed level beside it.
- *B, ladder* — one card per item with its own reason, and the trail under it.
- *C, flow* — the items as chips into a resolver and the process, with an
  inspector and the last runs.

Every variant shows the level a run takes, the item and provider behind it,
the time received, the inversion, the daily and hourly spread, the factor
applied to the mixture and the fallback reason. There is no vintage control,
and the page states that vintage is retired.

The mock data is one synthetic EUR/USD generator, five ranked items and a
clock of 2026-10-31: ECB direct, IMF a week old, ECB inverted, BoC with terms
not accepted, and TCMB with no fixing. The resolver mirrors the runner's tests
and prints the whole state after each action.

The variants are judged against the question in
[[file:../../../../../prototypes/market_data/calibrate-a-generator.org][the design note]]. The decision taken here belongs in
[[id:2159FD0C-714C-45CF-BC8A-9ABFB98D3254][Build the calibration screen]] before it starts.
