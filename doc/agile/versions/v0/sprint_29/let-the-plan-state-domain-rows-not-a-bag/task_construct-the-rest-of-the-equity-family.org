* Goal

Every equity product is constructed by name. The barrier table's three codes, the digital table's digital and touch, the accumulator table's accumulator and target redemption forward, the swap table's plain and basket swap, and the single-code tables all state what they are instead of being assembled from whichever bag fields happen to be set.

* Acceptance

- Each of the sixteen routed equity codes has a named constructor and the trade type code is fixed by it.
- Two codes sharing a table cannot fill each other's columns; the closed vocabularies are refused at construction.
- The writer holds no per-product assembly for equity; it hands the constructor's product to the batch.
- A provisioning run books the equity family with the same seventeen rows as before, and the suites are green.
