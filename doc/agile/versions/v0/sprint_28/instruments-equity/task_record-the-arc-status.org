* Result

The design carries a Status section. It names the five merged units and E15,
with their PRs, and states what the data model now holds: a header keyed by
the trade with the family's common dates, a basket stored as rows, exact money
on every money column, and an identity keyed by the trade on all twelve
tables.

It also records why units 6 to 11 wait. The reason is the routing. The
generated trade_type_routing enum is consumed at runtime by the trade
component queries and the trade export service, so pointing the equity codes at
the header before the importer writes it would make both look for equity
instruments in an empty table. The re-pointing, the fact tables' duplicated
identity columns and the columns E7 and E8 name all move with the same mapper
migration, which the operator deferred to the xsdcpp removal.

Verification is the documentation check: all 39 components pass.
