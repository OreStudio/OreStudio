* Result

The prototype is a static page, doc/prototypes/testing/test-scenarios/. It draws the Tests tab with a queue of waiting and done scenarios, and a runner in three layouts: steps beside the detail, one step at a time, and the whole scenario as a document. Four cases switch from the bar: queue, running, reopened and failed step. Every layout puts the story and the task beside the steps. The design note states seven gaps. The main one is that nothing reads or writes a scenario, so the BFF must call a compass scenario verb. The reviewer accepted layout B, one step at a time, on 2026-10-10, and asked that the queue carry no heading of its own.
