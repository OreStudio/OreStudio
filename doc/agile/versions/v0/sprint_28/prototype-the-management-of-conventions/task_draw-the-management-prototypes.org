* Notes

** The entity screens come first

The owner asked for the plain entity screens of the conventions, with the other four prototypes as workflows layered on top, as the Workflows menu does for other entities. The fifth prototype, See the conventions a party uses, draws the list of kinds, the list of one kind and the record page. Find, Name a set and Retire are lines in its Workflows menus. Decide stays in the Requests area and has a Requests tab on the record page.

** UX rules applied

- One screen with a rail of steps. A record page has Details and History tabs, and the last-changed line.
- A list has a search, a sort, a pager that starts at 15 rows, and states for no records, no match and an error.
- A write that a second person decides says Propose. A decline needs a comment. A pending request can be withdrawn.
- An action a person may not take is not drawn. The access mark says Editable or Read only. No permission code appears on a screen.
- No native select. Keys are monospace, an empty value is a muted dash, a time is relative with the exact time on hover.
- Colour is for meaning only: green, amber and red are status, grey is inactive, violet is a classification.

** Limits

- The strings are in English only. The standard asks for French and Portuguese before a screen ships.
- Three of the 26 kinds carry mock documents. The other kinds in the set picker show no terms.
- The prototypes were checked in headless Chrome by screenshot and by a read of the console. No person has clicked through them.
- The server gaps that each page marks are the gaps in the four journeys.
