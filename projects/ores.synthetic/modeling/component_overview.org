* Summary

=ores.synthetic= generates synthetic market data. A generation config and
its sub-configs (FX spot, IR curve, market data) are persisted entities,
and the quant process parameter definitions supply the vocabulary those
configs instantiate. The =.api= part carries the domain types, the
generators, the feed implementations and the NATS protocol. The =.core=
part owns the repositories, the CRUD services, the NATS handlers and the
history providers. The =.service= part is the runnable host that wires
them and runs the feeds. The component is structured as a composite with
three sub-components and holds no code of its own.
