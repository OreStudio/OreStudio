#+title: A curve feed that serves an adapter publishes no display tick
#+description: The market regime raster draws a row for every running feed and reads each feed's ticks from its display subject. * What

An IR curve feed publishes one display tick for each pillar it prices, and skips
that publish for any pillar an adapter serves: the loop sends the adapter's own
tick and then continues to the next pillar. A curve whose pillars are all served
by an adapter therefore publishes nothing on its display subject at all, while
its ticks are stored and its adapter is served. An FX feed publishes its display
tick before the adapter branch and is not affected.

* Why

Screens that tail a feed's display subject show such a feed as silent while it
is running and storing ticks. The market regime raster gives every running feed
a row, and 20 of the 24 IR curve rows on a demo environment are empty for this
reason. The Ticks tab of a curve feed has the same gap, and has had it since the
tab was written, so the defect predates the raster.

The tick loop is in
=projects/ores.synthetic/api/src/feeds/ir_curve_feed.cpp=. The display subject
is =feed_display_subject_for(source_name)= in
=ores.marketdata.api/domain/tick_subjects.hpp=.
