* Purpose

Models a plain reference-data code: a small, tenant-scoped list of
records identified by a short human-readable code (an ISO code, an
enum-like designation) rather than a manufactured id, with no
relationship to any other entity and no nesting. The commonest shape
in the codebase by entity count — most reference data is exactly this
and nothing more — which is why it deserves a name rather than being
re-derived by hand each time.

* Reference entity

[[id:88E8E1FB-6F2F-495F-BEC4-8C7ABEF68563][country]] — a plain reference-data lookup with a human-meaningful text
natural key (ISO code), no self-reference, no parent, and just enough
Qt to be usable (change-reason cache, pagination) without the richer
optional surfaces =currency= exercises.
