#+title: Restarting the synthetic producer stops the market data ingest
#+description: Stopping and restarting the synthetic service leaves the market data ingest receiving nothing while the producer reports its feeds running, and restarting the ingest service does not recover it. The ingest is a core NATS subscribe, not a JetStream durable, so the stale-consumer repair does not apply.
#+type: capture
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :nats:marketdata:synthetic:ingest:reliability:
#+created: 2026-10-13
#+updated: 2026-10-13

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Make the ingest survive a restart of the producer. Restarting the synthetic
service must not leave the market data ingest receiving nothing, and an ingest
that has gone quiet must recover, either by itself or on its own restart.

* Why

Seen on brave_hopper on 2026-10-13, while measuring the cost of storing ticks.
The synthetic service was stopped and started again to take an idle baseline on
the database. Afterwards the producer reported its feeds running:

#+begin_example
synthetic.v1.feed_configs.list — 24 feed(s) running
#+end_example

and the ingest reported the opposite, per binding, every minute:

#+begin_example
INGEST STATUS: source='synthetic.bloomberg.zarzaronia' tenant='2850f4af-…'
party='25fe1820-…' ticks=1274 last_tick=5236s ago
#+end_example

The stored observations did not move at all over a twelve-second window
(=ores_marketdata_market_observations_tbl= held at 893,777), and the database
counters fell to their idle floor: about 57 transactions and 1.5 inserted rows
a second, against roughly 3,258 transactions and 95 rows a second while the
ticks were arriving. So the ingest was not slow, it was silent.

Restarting =ores.marketdata.service= did not recover it: the observation count
was still exactly 893,777 across the restart. That is the part that rules out
the hand fix below, and it is the reason this is worth a capture rather than a
note.

The ingest does not use a JetStream durable. =feed_ingest_loop::start()=
subscribes with core NATS:

#+begin_src cpp
const auto wildcard = std::string(domain::synthetic_tick_subject) + ".>";
tick_sub_ = nats_.subscribe(wildcard, [this](ores::nats::message msg) { on_tick(msg); });
#+end_src

(=projects/ores.marketdata/service/src/app/feed_ingest_loop.cpp:64-67=), while
the producer publishes with =js_publish=. The comment directly above those
lines already names a subject trap in this area:

#+begin_quote
=subscribe()= prefixes its subject for this environment, so the wildcard stays
relative here. =subjects_below()= returns the prefixed form a stream filter
needs, and passed to =subscribe()= it is prefixed twice and matches no tick.
#+end_quote

So the ordinary repair does not apply: there is no durable consumer for the
market data ingest to delete and recreate. What the subject the producer
actually publishes on is, and what the subscription resolves to after
prefixing, is the first thing to establish. The producer logged its publish as
=ores.dev.brave_hopper.synthetic.v1.ops.sandbox_tick.synthetic.refinitiv.eurjpy=,
which is the prefixed form of the wildcard above, so the mismatch is not
obvious from the names alone.

Two captures are adjacent but neither covers this. The producer side is
[[id:83FF505F-F58B-4AA6-9FA6-98F4EE29A9B7][Boot auto-start never starts a tenant's feeds]], where a synthetic restart
drops every tenant feed until a folder is started again — that explains why the
feeds had to be restarted, not why the ingest then heard nothing. The other is
[[id:FA7D1F14-B357-4BEA-8AF0-B9D7C16E3E4D][A subject rename leaves durable JetStream consumers unable to attach]], whose
mechanism is a stored consumer filter drifting from the code; it shows up as a
service that will not start, and it needs a rename.

To be explicit about what is not yet known: whether this is a subject mismatch,
a subscription that was torn down and never rebuilt, or something in
=refresh()= rebuilding the bindings without re-subscribing. The measurements
above are what has been established.
