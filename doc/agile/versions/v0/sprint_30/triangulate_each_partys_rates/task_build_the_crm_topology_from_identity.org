* Goal

A pair is composed from two legs of one publisher that share a base, so the base cancels in the level difference. Today the driver pairs are hand-listed in refdata and the reader hardcodes one publisher. Build the topology from market_series_identity (ccy, unit_ccy) and providers.precedence, make the publisher's pivot a root vertex whose leg is the identity, and refuse an FX level series whose identity is incomplete.

* Acceptance

- The topology is derived from series identity and provider precedence; crm_driver_pairs is no longer the source of the drivers.
- A pair whose leg is the publisher's pivot composes with no series, the leg being the identity.
- A series without ccy and unit_ccy is refused by name, and no currency or publisher name appears in the topology code.
