* Acceptance

- A sandbox is an entity with purpose, anchor, owner, visibility, status and review date, and its own portfolio tree that no official portfolio contains.
- Opening a sandbox needs the right at its anchor; visibility follows the anchor's permissions or named members.
- A virtual book belongs to exactly one sandbox, has no ledger book, and a booking names it only for a pre-agreement draft, a test or a hypothetical.
- Official reports cannot select a sandbox.
