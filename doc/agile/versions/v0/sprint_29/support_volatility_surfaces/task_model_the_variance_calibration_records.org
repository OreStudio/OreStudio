* Goal

Three versioned ores.synthetic records hold the whole audit trail: the input snapshot (pair, item, provider, policy and its version, window, inversion, counts, who and when), one point per fixing date (fixing, return, whether it was used and why not), and the calibration (the input it read, method and version, alpha, gamma, beta, persistence, long-run variance, starting variance, likelihood, convergence, admissibility, and a review state of proposed, approved, rejected or superseded with its reason). The calibration policy gains the variance window.

* Acceptance

- The three entities are generated across SQL, domain, repository, protocol, shell and TypeScript, with history for each.
- Checks on write: a calibration's shape is admissible (alpha, beta and alpha plus gamma not negative, persistence below one); its state is one of the four; an approved or rejected state carries a reason; a point that is not used carries a reason.
- At most one approved calibration per pair, provider and method is current, by a unique index; an authored calibration is a record of method authored with no input.
