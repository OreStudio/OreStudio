* Goal

A product with children is constructed whole. The writer appends what the constructor returned instead of rebuilding the children from the bag, so a basket swap cannot be constructed without a basket and the children's ordinals come from one place.

* Acceptance

- The basket swap's constructor refuses an empty basket and numbers its constituents and periods.
- The accumulator's constructor numbers the range bounds it is given, taking their start date from the product rather than from the bag.
- The writer appends the constructed children and stamps their audit columns; it assembles no child itself.
- A provisioning run books the equity family with the same rows and the same children, and the suites are green.

* Plan

A product with children returns them: =equity_swap_product= holds the swap's
row, its constituents and its periods, and =equity_accumulator_product= holds
the accumulator's row and its range bounds. The constructor numbers the
children from the order it was given them, so no caller can leave a gap or a
duplicate.

The terms gain the children as their own fact structs, so a caller states
what a member or a bound is rather than a row's fields. A bound's start date
is the product's, so it is stated once.

The writer keeps only the audit stamp: it appends what the constructor
returned and fills the audit columns, which is persistence and not product.

* Result

A product with children is constructed whole. The swap's and the accumulator's
constructors return their children alongside their row, numbering each child
from the order the terms stated it. A worst-of basket swap with an empty
basket is refused, which was previously an unstated assumption: the writer
looped over whatever the bag held, so a basket swap could be constructed with
no members and the database would accept it.

Proved by a provisioning run on a recreated database: =generate_trades=
completed, the family holds the same 17 rows, the same 2 constituents and 4
periods numbered from one, and the same 4 range bounds over 3 accumulator
rows. Both suites are green: =ores.trading.api.tests= at 447 assertions in 116
test cases, =ores.synthetic.core.tests= at 1733 assertions in 78.

The branch is held locally until PR #4025 merges, so that this lands as its
own change against main rather than as a stack.
