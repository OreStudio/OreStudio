* Notes

- The review's shape is the opposite of the FX one. FX had a clean structure and
  an unmigrated boundary; rates has the best structure of the three families
  reviewed and a boundary that disagrees with it about what a product contains.
- Thirteen claims changed under the two re-checks and one finding was
  withdrawn, all recorded in the investigation's *Corrections made while
  checking*. Five were found in the main thread — the conversion seam passes at
  the helper and fails at six sites beside it, the empty cap/floor fact table is
  a dropped field rather than a product with no fields, the variant is not a
  Regime 3 violation, the recorded decision that the variant was removed is
  false, and a floor is corrupted in the reverse direction. The other eight an
  independent verifier found by opening every citation: the soft-reference
  count, the leg-direction finding's two citations, that finding's claim that
  the import always supplies a direction, three =nats_call= citations, the
  task's copy of the money count, the batch's array count, a sequence-line
  citation, and a scored regime that contradicted its own checklist row. That is
  the step which should not have needed a second agent.
- A floor becoming a cap was found on a second pass, after the strike finding
  was written. Following a lost column in both directions is what raised it.
