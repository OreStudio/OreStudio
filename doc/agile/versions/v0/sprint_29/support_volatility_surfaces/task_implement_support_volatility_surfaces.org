* Plan

The user's analysis (two parts, quant and architecture) is the baseline, not
text to copy. Each claim is checked against the knowledge base and gets one
outcome: covered (link or extend the owning note), new (a new atomic note), in
conflict (a decision note), or discarded (listed in Notes with the reason).

Decisions agreed with the user on 2026-11-03:

- The MDN route is abandoned. A surface moves through its own parameter
  processes (ATM level, skew, curvature per tenor), with shocks correlated with
  spot but not driven by it.
- GARCH drives FX spot returns, not the surface. Proposed: the GMM stays the
  shape of each shock and GJR-GARCH scales it by the day's volatility.
- A surface follows the IR curve approach: state processes, a template ladder
  of pillars, one quote tick per pillar on an oresmd series, a blueprint that
  builds the surface (SABR or SVI) with static no-arbitrage checks, then a
  candidate and a published set. Quotes are published, not model parameters.

Steps:

1. Check the analysis's doubtful claims: FX implied vols on DBnomics (BIS,
   ECB), the MOVE index on FRED, the RR and BF formulas against the smile and
   market strangle conventions, and "SABR and SVI are free of static arbitrage".
2. Write the decision notes: surface parameter dynamics, and the role of GARCH
   with its coupling to the intraday volatility channel.
3. Write the new notes: SVI, a GJR-GARCH process as a generator kind, the
   surface pipeline that follows the IR curve, and FRED and DBnomics as public
   sources once their terms are read.
4. Extend the existing notes: the approach note (drop the MDN, update the open
   decisions and the out of scope list), Stochastic Processes, FX Volatility
   Surface, Market volatility channel, Synthetic prices are calibrated from free
   public sources, and Public sources publish daily fixings.
5. Write a Volatility surfaces structure note and link it from Market Data
   Architecture.
6. Name the later tasks of the arc in the story, each traced to its note.

Status of the approach note's open decisions, to record in step 4: OD-1 open
