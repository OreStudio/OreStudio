9. What does not hold yet
-------------------------
PUT /api/refdata/ir-curve-bootstrap-configs (a new blueprint with a fresh
  output_series_id)  204, and the row is visible to the party.

  But no operation mints the output series the write names:
  POST /api/curves/series-by-id with that series id answers {"series":[]},
  so the created blueprint names a series that does not exist and the
  build refuses it ("output market_series not found"). A blueprint's own
  fields cannot yield that series' identity: the oresmd URI of a curve is
  DISCOUNT/RATE/USD/USD-SOFR-FOMC, and the config carries no index name,
  only its currency and index tenor (curve_republish_service says the same:
  "this service has none of series_type/metric/qualifier to invent one
  from"). The screen therefore draws no Add, and says why above the list.

  The page's route serves the bundle: GET /market-data/curve-blueprints and
  GET /market-data/curve-blueprints/<id>?tab=check both answer 200.
