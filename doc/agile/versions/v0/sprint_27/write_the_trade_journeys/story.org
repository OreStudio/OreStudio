* Decisions

- A journey is a use case and names no screen. The screens come from the UX
  standards, and a trade is an entity, so one trade page serves most journeys.
- The journeys draw no wireframes. The prototype story draws the screens.
- The Qt lifecycle event window maintained a lookup. A per-trade event is a trade
  activity, so the lifecycle journey records an activity.
- An amendment is a booked activity, not a bare write to the trade.
- The reading found gaps that block every screen. They are listed on the hub page
  and for the owner to plan: no web trading route, a trade list that filters only by
  id, child tables with no by-trade read, no write of an activity with its versions,
  and the two digest gaps.

* Out of scope

- The prototypes. They are the next story and follow the UX standards, including
  the older =trade-entry= prototype under the books group.
- Fixing any gap the journeys list.
