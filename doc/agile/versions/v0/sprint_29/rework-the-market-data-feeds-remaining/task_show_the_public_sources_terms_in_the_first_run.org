* Goal

A system administrator who sets up a new installation reads the terms of each public source the system loads quotes from, and accepts or declines each one, before any of its quotes are fetched.

* Acceptance

- The first-run journey has a step between the administrator and ready that lists each publisher of the catalogue: its name, what it gives, its terms page as a link, the credit it asks for and whether it allows storage.
- The administrator accepts or declines each publisher. An accepted publisher records terms_read_at, the principal who accepted and the publisher version; a declined one stays disabled.
- The loader refuses to fetch from a publisher with no acceptance, and a test shows it.
- A publisher whose terms change gets a new version, and the system administrator is asked again before its next load.
- The terms can be read and changed later from the publisher catalogue, not only in the first run.
