* Goal

A surface should reach as far as its party's market data reaches and no further, and say where it stopped. Today a thirteen-expiry ladder is compiled into the derived source and the seeder, an expiry's length is a constant, and one wholly-unquoted expiry voids the whole surface. This story takes the ladder and the horizon out of the code and into data, and makes the calibration fit what it has.

* Acceptance

- The ladder a pair is quoted at is published data; no C++ array or duplicated list decides it.
- An expiry's length comes from the expiry-date rule the ladder and the feed already share; no years table survives.
- A surface's reach is computed from the party's sample, so a shorter sample shortens the surface instead of voiding it.
- A surface records the expiries it could not fit and is refused only below a configured minimum.
- Each party's surface is built by that party's own configuration, copied with its blueprints.
