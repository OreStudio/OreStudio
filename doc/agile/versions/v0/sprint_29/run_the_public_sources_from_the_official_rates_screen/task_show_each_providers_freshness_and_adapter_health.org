* Goal

A person reads, for each public provider, whether the data is arriving. The marketdata adapter read already answers for every adapter with its state, heartbeat, served and refused counts and the age of its last tick; this task joins it to the provider on the Official rates screen. Tick age alone says nothing about a source that publishes once a day, so the freshness is the last fixing's date, when it was received, and whether the next expected publication is late. An adapter that is down, a provider whose fixing is late and a provider that has never had a fixing must read as three different states.

* Notes

Late is a fact about a schedule, and the provider model carries none today. The
row can say the last fixing's date and when it was received, and it can say the
adapter is not serving. It cannot say "today's fixing is late" until the
catalogue states each publisher's expected rhythm: daily, weekly, monthly, and
the time of day it publishes. That fact belongs on the provider record, beside
its terms, and the run's schedule follows it. Until it exists the screen says
"no fixing today" and names the adapter's heartbeat, rather than claiming a
lateness it cannot compute.
