* Goal

Implement account as the richest IAM entity and the one entity_specification.org treats as its reference for what an entity screen contains. Secrets never leave the server. account_type and default_party_id are searchable selects, reports_to_account_id is a self-referencing select, the login-status panel is read-only, and the parties, roles and contact-information relationships are Related tabs. Lock, unlock and reset password are actions on the selection, after History.
