* Goal

Signed in as the super administrator, the system administration area reads the session's own tenant accounts. The services the platform runs on, its service and infrastructure accounts, are visible where the deployment is administered, and not only through the system tenant's screen on the roster.

* Acceptance

- The system administration area offers the deployment's own accounts a page at a time, each with its type and its picture or initials, and it is reachable without opening a tenant from the roster.
- The service accounts are shown as services, with the mark the tenant screen already gives them.
- The read is the ordinary accounts read of the session's own tenant; no cross-tenant read is added.
- The navigation prototype records the new entry and the Tenants-only decision it revisits.

* Plan

One list, framed by the mode. The people list already reads the session's own
tenant, and for a system administrator that tenant is the system tenant, so no
new read was added. =PeoplePage= takes the session mode and, in system
administration, names the list Accounts and leads with the accounts strings the
catalogue already carries in the three locales. A kind column mirrors the mark
=TenantPage= gives a service account. The system menu gains the Accounts entry
between Home and Tenants, and the prototype records the revisited Tenants-only
decision. Rejected: a new AccountsPage beside PeoplePage (the same list twice)
and a menu entry alone (it would leave the service mark out).
