* Acceptance

- The feeds screen shows the market state in force: calm, active or forced.
- A user sets the regime there: auto, pinned calm or pinned active, the
  transition rate, and the shared intensity's jump and decay.
- The same screen edits the per-feed sensitivity.
- A manual switch forces the state at runtime, from the screen and from a shell
  command, and takes effect on the next tick without a restart.
- The switch reports what it forced, so a test can assert it.
- Forcing is reversible: returning to auto hands control back to the state
  machine.
