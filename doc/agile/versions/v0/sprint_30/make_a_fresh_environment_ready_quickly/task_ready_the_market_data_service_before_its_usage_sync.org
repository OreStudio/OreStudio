* Notes

Seen on 2026-11-20 in =bright_faraday=, after =db recreate= and =services start=
(=journalctl --user -u ores.marketdata.service-bright_faraday=):

- 00:16:52 systemd starts =ores.marketdata.service=; by 00:16:58 it has
  connected, authenticated, checked the schema and opened its step streams.
- 00:16:58 to 00:18:22 it publishes 3869 =demand_interval_event=s, one per row
  written, and uses 3 s of CPU in 117 s.
- 00:18:22 "start operation timed out. Terminating." (the default 90 s).
- 00:18:49 "Dependency failed for ores.marketdata.official": that unit
  =Requires= and starts =After= the market data service, so its start fails
  with result =dependency= and nothing starts it again. =compass services start=
  waited 483 s and reported it inactive; a manual restart started it at once.
- 00:18:52 systemd restarts the market data service, which is ready in about a
  second, because the intervals are already written.

The cause is the order in =application.cpp=: =notify_systemd_ready()= runs
after =on_started=, which calls =supervisor->start()=; that runs =refresh()=,
whose catalogue listener calls =usage->sync()=, which writes every demand
interval of the catalogue one insert at a time
(=usage_recorder::sync=, =demand_interval_repository::write=).
