* Notes

The journeys name the record a *curve blueprint*, the word the market data group
already uses for the IR curve bootstrap config, and keep the model's words for
the permission codes.

The Qt workbench is prior art for the job and not for the layout. The journeys
record each place they part from it: a record page replaces the three-tab
workbench, the pillars become parts written one at a time, the source series is
not asked for at all, the output series is minted by the server, the derived
pillar order is stated rather than silently applied, and the copy becomes the
kind's own action with the three fields that name the curve.

Two defects in the prior art are named in the journey so they are not inherited:
the delete confirmation names a UUID twice instead of the curve, and the
discount-curve picker's first column is headed "Output Series Id" while it draws
the series' qualifier.

The prototype was rendered headless for every state the design note lists. The
verification needs a =--user-data-dir= and a writable profile: bare headless
Chrome fails with "Failed to create a unique user data directory".

* Result

Signed off on 2026-10-28, with two small points applied.

- Two journeys: maintain the curve blueprints, and check that a blueprint
  builds. They name the record a curve blueprint, its five tabs, its dialogs,
  and the Qt workbench's departures with a reason for each.
- The prototype draws the list, the record, five tabs, four dialogs, the in-dialog
  record picker, and the Market data area card, with 34 states verified headless.
- Every coded value is drawn through one renderer, after the app's CodedValue:
  a pill where the label catalogue paints it, the code where it does not. The
  note lists the six domains the seed would need and that the curve codes are
  plain until it has them.
- The card: Curve blueprints, first in the Market data area, at
  /market-data/curve-blueprints, reading refdata::ir_curve_bootstrap_configs:read,
  in the words the other cards use.

Open, recorded in the note and the journeys: the record's key, the minted output
series, retire, delete while published, whether Check is a tab, the Curves menu
entry, and whether the seed gains the six badge domains.

The journeys and the prototype are on branch =feature/design-the-bootstrapping-screens=
in two commits:

The prototype answers both journeys and does not mirror them. The design note
carries a coverage table for each, naming every step, the part of the screen
that carries it, and what is deliberately not drawn.

Four things a journey asks for have no home on the page, and each is recorded in
the note: a *retire* action, because the record has no status; a message under a
wrong field, because the only wrong value the page can draw is a pillar the Check
tab judges; a test result held against a version; and a save that writes the
recipe and its pillars together.

No code changed. No route, no BFF entry and no React screen was added.
