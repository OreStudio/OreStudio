* Goal

Establish, with evidence, whether the FX instrument family's persisted data
model meets the data-oriented design guidelines, and record the findings so the
remediations can be planned.

Bonds were reviewed this way first and were the method's pilot. FX was never
reworked to the method, so the question here is different: not whether the tree
still holds up, but whether it was ever built to the rules.

* Acceptance

- Every Regime 2 decision check is walked over the family's seven entity models, with the evidence for each result.
- The invariant checks are spot-checked once, with what was read stated.
- Each finding is mapped to a criterion and carries file-and-line evidence.
- The review separates a defect from a recorded decision.
- The review states what it did not check.

* Decisions

- The review is read-only. Filing the remediations is a separate decision, taken
  through the backlog once the findings are agreed.
- The remediations are staged as seven units under
  [[id:E60E95CC-810E-4DC1-A4E3-6C07DB2A28D9][Rework the FX instruments to the
  instrument table rule]], which the review narrowed to the deviations it
  found.

* Out of scope

- The FX reference data: =currency_pair=, =currency_pair_convention=,
  =fx_option_convention= and =fx_volatility_config=. The family's links to them
  are reviewed, the entities themselves are not.
- The ORE binding generator. The review records that the FX bindings lack the
  lexical form; changing the generator is a different work item.
- The ten FX trade-type codes that route to no instrument table.
