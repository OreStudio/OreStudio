* Goal

Move the fifteen lookup-template artefact tables onto domain models and retire the lookup artefact template, settle the owner of the DQ-only models including the GLEIF tables, and model the geo ip2country store so its staging table is generated from a model.

* Acceptance

- No DQ artefact table comes from the older lookup template.
- Each remaining ores.dq artefact model has a recorded owner decision.
- The geo ip2country store is modelled and its staging table is generated from that model.
- The hand-written lei_bic table moves with the other GLEIF tables.
