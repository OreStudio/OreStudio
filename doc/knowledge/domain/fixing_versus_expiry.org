#+title: A fixing automates the decision an expiry leaves to the holder
#+description: An agreed fixing source lets the exercise of a cash-settled option follow automatically; an expiry waits for the holder.
#+type: knowledge
#+audience: user
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:fixing:option:

* Summary

A fixing and an expiry both decide the fate of an option, but they decide it
differently. An expiry waits for the holder to exercise or to let the option
lapse, and the decision may be disputed when the option is near the money. A
fixing decides from an observation of a source that both parties agreed at
inception, so everything that depends on it can be automated. Fixings are,
in this sense, the automated counterpart of expiries.

* Detail

** Exercise determination

The agreement at inception sets how an option's exercise is determined:

- *Holder determined.* The holder exercises or lets the option expire. This
  is the classical expiry.
- *Fixing determined.* Exercise follows from a fixing agreed at inception.
  FpML calls this /automatic exercise/. A non-deliverable option needs it,
  because it can only settle in cash against a fixing; a vanilla option can
  use it to settle without manual intervention.

The market documentation presumes automatic exercise. The 1998 FX and
Currency Option Definitions apply automatic exercise to a currency option
unless the parties state otherwise, and the 2005 Barrier Option Supplement asks
parties who want manual exercise to say so in the confirmation. FpML records the
choice in =exerciseProcedure=, as =manualExercise= or =automaticExercise=. A
system should therefore record, for each trade, whether automatic exercise
applies, rather than assume it either way.

A cash-settled option is always fixing determined: the fixing both decides
whether the option is exercised and sets the amount it pays, and the second
implies the first. A deliverable vanilla can also be fixing determined, which
is common when it shares an expiry time with a digital in one structure.

** Consequences for a trading system

- A product that settles in cash should carry a fixing source, so that it
  needs no manual expiry processing. Non-deliverable forwards, non-deliverable
  options and digitals are the common cases; see
  [[id:CD3E82C6-AE56-4D06-B949-0B3A5CE7A5FC][Deliverability and non-deliverable instruments]].
- A trade that is fixing determined but has no fixing source cannot be
  processed automatically. It belongs on an exceptions report.
- The options of one structure should share a determination style, so that
  the structure is netted in one place, either at expiry or at fixing.
- Because a fixing's source is not in dispute, an error lies in how the
  observation was entered. The system must therefore undo every fix made from
  one observation and redo them; the unit of work is the set of trades
  attached to an observation, not a single trade. See
  [[id:CA61B445-B6DC-41D3-ACF9-D3FB32D0D342][The fixing process observes, authorises, applies and can undo an observation]].

** Which event handles which product

| Handled at expiry | Handled at fixing |
|-------------------+-------------------|
| European vanilla, knock-in, knock-out not triggered | European digital, digital with a knock-out barrier, average rate options, volatility swaps, non-deliverable forwards and options |

An American one-touch is handled by barrier monitoring when it is touched,
and at maturity otherwise.
