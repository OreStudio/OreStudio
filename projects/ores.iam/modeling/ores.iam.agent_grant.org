#+created: 2026-11-22
#+updated: 2026-11-22

An agent grant records that a person allows one named LLM agent to act for
them in one tenant and one party, bounded by a role made for the work, until an
expiry of at most ninety days or until the grant is revoked. It holds no secret: an agent key redeems it,
and IAM re-checks the grant and its person at every exchange. It is IAM's
second form of the Grant Management pattern, after the run grant, and
[[id:A7E2942C-66AD-449C-B80B-CA789EBC34FA][Agent grants: delegated consent for LLM agents]] states its checks and its lifecycle.

The table is readable over the wire and written only by IAM's own create and
revoke operations, which take the person's own session:
=:client_read_only:= removes the generic write verbs from the wire and keeps
the repository writable for those operations.

** agent_name
:PROPERTIES:
:type:        text
:cpp_type:    std::string
:natural_key: true
:END:

The name the person gives the agent, such as =claude-code= or =desk-assistant=.
A token made from the grant names it as its actor, so every call the agent
makes is recorded as this agent's. A person grants each agent once per party.

#+begin_src cpp :name generator
std::string("claude-code")
#+end_src

** role_id
:PROPERTIES:
:type:     uuid
:cpp_type: boost::uuids::uuid
:END:

The role the agent acts under. At creation the person must hold every
permission of the role, and the role may name no permission an agent is never
given; at every exchange the token carries the role's permissions the person
still holds, less those withheld from agents.

#+end_src

** not_after
:PROPERTIES:
:type:     timestamp with time zone
:cpp_type: std::chrono::system_clock::time_point
:nullable: false
:END:

The instant after which the grant serves no exchange: at most ninety days after
the grant was made. A grant is never extended; continuing is a new grant.

#+begin_src cpp :name generator
std::chrono::system_clock::now() + std::chrono::hours(24 * 30)
#+end_src

** revoked_at
:PROPERTIES:
:type:     timestamp with time zone
:cpp_type: std::chrono::system_clock::time_point
:nullable: true
:default_value: {}
:END:

When the grant was revoked; empty while it is active. Who revoked it and why
are the revoking version's =modified_by= and change commentary, as for any
change, so the grant holds no column of its own for them.
