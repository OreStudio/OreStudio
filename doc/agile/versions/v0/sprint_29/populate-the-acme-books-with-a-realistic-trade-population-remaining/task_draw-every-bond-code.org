* Result

The bond planner draws the ten codes the catalogue routes to the bond instrument
table, constructs each through its own constructor, and the writer persists what
the plan states. A provisioned tenant now holds every one of them, four trades
each across its four bond books, with the facts their tables expect: four
options, four forwards, four futures, four repos, four total return swaps and
four asset swapped convertibles, two call dates on the callable security and one
conversion target on the convertible. The tenant holds 178 trades where it held
162.

Three things came out of the work and are recorded rather than hidden.

- BondFutureOption is not routed. The catalogue states it as a bond product and
  leaves its instrument column empty, so no table can hold it and the planner
  must not draw it. Its constructor is written and never reached until the
  catalogue routes it, which is captured.
- The writer carried two `bond_instruments` branches in one dispatch chain, the
  second unreachable. The duplicate went with this change.
- The store states a repo's type as Fixed or Floating, not the word the ORE
  document uses, and the first run was refused by that check. The planner draws
  from the store's own set.

The population's mix moves again: bonds are 40 of 178 trades, 22% against the 4%
Trade Population Weights states. Sizing every class to the weights remains its
own captured task.
