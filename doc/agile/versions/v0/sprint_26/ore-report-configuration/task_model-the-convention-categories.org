* Notes

- =SwapIndex= is modelled: three fields, and it appears in forty-seven files.
  Its case asserts 1451 times over that corpus.
- =AverageOIS= is modelled: eleven fields across thirty-six files. It went in
  before the cheaper categories because it is the one that clears files: seven of
  the thirteen files with exactly one unmodelled category name it, so modelling
  it took the count of files that round trip outright from twelve to nineteen.
  The corpus makes three of its fields required and never sets its frequency, so
  the mapper reads the three unconditionally and the frequency only when present.
- =FxOption= is modelled: nine text fields across thirty-six files.
- =Future= is modelled: six fields, and it appears in thirty-nine files. Three of
  those carried no other unmodelled category, so the count of files that round
  trip outright went from nine to twelve. Its two enumerations are stored in
  ORE's own spelling, because they are closed enumerations with no aliases and
  the token is what an export has to write back.
- Each category costs one entity, its create, drop, notify trigger and row-level
  security, its seeded permissions, its component file-list entries, and a
  mapper in both directions. Sixteen were unmodelled and three have landed, so
  thirteen remain. The expensive ones are
  =CrossCurrencyBasis= at twenty-five fields across forty-nine files and
  =CommodityFuture= at thirty-four fields.
