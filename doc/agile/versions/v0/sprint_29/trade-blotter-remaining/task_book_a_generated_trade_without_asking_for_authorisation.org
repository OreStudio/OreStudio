* Acceptance

- The generated population books its trades with a nature that raises no authorisation request, and the blotter still shows them.
- A freshly provisioned Acme tenant shows an empty authorisation queue.
- The choice of booking nature over a sandbox book is recorded with its reason, and a test pins the population's nature so seeding cannot regress.

* Plan

The trading service already decides this in one place:
=operational_authorisation_service::raise_if_awaiting= returns nothing unless
the booking nature is actual, and the summary view reads =not_required= for
every other nature. Both behaviours are covered by the trading core suite, and
the ORE import already books a sandbox import as a test.

So the work was to stop lying about the nature and to make the lie impossible
to reintroduce:

- The population writer states =booking_nature::test=.
- Its request is built by =book_request_for=, a function separate from the send,
  so a test reads the terms the population books under without a session.
- Three cases pin them: the nature, the origin and the person, and the rows the
  slot carries.

A sandbox book was the alternative and was rejected: the nature is the one place
that decides, the readers already agree with it, and a book flag would be a
second, overlapping notion that every reader would have to honour.

* Result

A generated trade states the test booking nature, so the trading service raises
no authorisation request for it, and the trade summary reads =not_required=. The
one place that decides is the nature, and the population no longer claims an
actual deal.

The request the population sends is now built by =book_request_for=, separate
from the send and from the session, and three cases pin it: the nature, the
origin with the config it names and the person it is entered by, and the rows
the slot carries.

* What could not be proved

The acceptance asks for a freshly provisioned tenant with an empty queue, and
that proof stopped at provisioning: the =subscribe_market_data= step asks for a
market data source nobody serves, so the run aborts at step 7 and the trade
population never runs. That is recorded as
[[proj:doc/agile/product_backlog/inbox/provisioning_asks_for_a_market_data_source_nobody_serves.org][Provisioning asks for a market data source nobody serves]];
the local workaround was refused because the profile belongs to the system
tenant.

What is proved instead: the writer states the nature, and the service raises a
request for actual and none for test, both on this tree. The remaining step is
to provision once the environment can, and count the queue at zero.
