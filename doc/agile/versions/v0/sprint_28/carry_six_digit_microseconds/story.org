* Goal

Every timestamp the platform writes carries six digits of microseconds, and the parser reads them back, so a time_point survives a round trip through the database or the wire to the microsecond.

* Acceptance

- to_iso8601_utc writes six fractional digits and parse_utc_timestamp reads one to six back.
- A time_point survives to_iso8601_utc then from_iso8601_utc to the microsecond.
- The market observation test asserts the round trip and passes.
