This scenario verifies [[id:21C9CA52-3515-4D99-9B95-1F5BA8A05DCD][Attach a logo/image to a counterparty]]: a counterparty
can now carry a logo via a nullable =image_id= soft-FK, edited through a
new logo picker on =CounterpartyDetailDialog= (Menu: Reference Data >
Counterparties). Runs against the *Acme Bank* tenant (replacing
Barclays Plc as the system's reference/demo entity -- see
[[id:CB7351E9-8E7E-4BA4-9399-4EB22F6192D4][Commission Acme Bank: a realistic holding-group test entity]]), whose
provisioning now seeds a fixture counterparty, =Acme Corp=, with its
logo already attached (sourced from Wikipedia's [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acme_Corporation][Acme Corporation]]
article) -- so the first thing a tester sees is a *working example*
with no manual upload needed. The scenario then covers the full CRUD +
history + eventing sweep for the field on a second, freshly-created
counterparty -- create, read-back, update, delete, history, and a NATS
notification refreshing a second client. Pass means every step below
completes with the stated outcome and no unexpected error dialog
appears.

* Steps

Each step is its own heading — the title should be five to seven
words so it fits on one line in the QA Validation Runner's step list
without wrapping or truncating (e.g. "Edit and save the record", not
a full sentence describing the whole operation). The body below the
title is a bullet-point checklist, not a prose paragraph: give the
tester every piece of context needed to execute that one step without
looking anything up elsewhere — what UI state must already exist,
exactly what to click or type, and exactly what confirms the step
passed. The panel writes each step's PASS/FAIL/PENDING outcome and
notes back as a =*** Result= child heading directly under it.

** blue

*** Connect to tenant Acme Bank

- Log in as =tenant_admin@acme_bank= / =Secure-Password-123=.
- Select party *Acme Bank UK plc*.
- Open Reference Data > Counterparties.

*** Confirm the pre-populated Acme Corp logo

- In the counterparty list, find the row with short code *ACME* / full
  name *Acme Corp* -- this row is seeded automatically by Acme tenant
  provisioning, with its logo already attached.
- Open its detail dialog and confirm the *Logo* group box already
  shows an image (the Acme Corp logo, sourced from Wikipedia) without
  you having picked one -- this is the "already uploaded to database"
  half of the scenario: no manual upload needed to see a working
  example.
- Close the dialog without making changes (do not edit or delete this
  fixture counterparty -- it is reused by other scenarios and by the
  eventing step below).

*** Create a counterparty and set its logo

- Click Add to open a new =CounterpartyDetailDialog= in create mode.
- Fill in a short code (e.g. =ACMETEST=), full name (e.g. =Acme Test
  Counterparty=), party type, status, and business center; select any
  parent counterparty.
- In the *Logo* group box, click the flag/logo button and pick any
  image from the selector (the pre-populated Acme Corp logo, if
  offered, or any other available image).
- Confirm the button now shows the chosen icon and a "Logo changed
  (unsaved)" style before saving.
- Click Save, supply a change reason, and confirm the success
  notification appears and the row shows in the list.

*** Read back the saved logo

- Reopen the detail dialog for the counterparty just created (double
  click its list row).
- Confirm the Logo button shows the same image chosen in the previous
  step -- this is the =image_id= round-trip through save/read.

*** Update to a different logo

- With the dialog still open, click the Logo button again and pick a
  *different* image.
- Save with a change reason and confirm success.
- Close and reopen the dialog; confirm the button now shows the new
  image, not the original one.

*** Delete the counterparty

- With the counterparty still open (or selected in the list), click
  Delete.
- Confirm the deletion prompt and supply a change reason.
- Confirm the row disappears from the counterparty list.

*** Verify history shows every logo change

- Right-click the (now deleted) counterparty's row -- or reselect it
  via the list's deleted-record filter, if the list hides deletes by
  default -- and choose History.
- In the History dialog, confirm three entries are present -- create,
  update, delete -- each with its own change reason.

** red

*** Connect to tenant Acme Bank

- Log in as =tenant_admin@acme_bank= / =Secure-Password-123= (this
  is a second, independent client instance -- log in again here, do
  not assume it inherits the blue client's session).
- Select party *Acme Bank UK plc*.
- Open Reference Data > Counterparties and leave the list visible.

*** Confirm the logo change arrives via NATS

- Wait for the blue client to complete its "Create a counterparty and
  set its logo" step.
- Without manually refreshing, confirm the new counterparty appears in
  this (red) client's list -- this is the LISTEN/NOTIFY-to-NATS relay
  firing, not just the repository layer ctest already covers.
- Open the counterparty's detail dialog here and confirm the Logo
  button shows the same image the blue client set.
