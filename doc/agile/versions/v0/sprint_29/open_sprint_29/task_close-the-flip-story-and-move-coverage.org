* Result

The flip story closed with six tasks that belong to its arc — the scaffold, the
two construction shapes, the commodity facts, the credit reference type, the six
family flips and the bond security — and four that belong to coverage left it:
the bond product gap, the commodity charter, the credit family and the legal
entity. They live in the population story now.

The capture-index task went to this story, where the agile index is wired.

Two documents were deleted: the population story held a second, unstarted copy
of the credit task under the same branch as the delivered one, and the flip
story held the unused scaffold task every story is born with. Neither had an
inbound link from anywhere but the story table that named it.

The flip story's acceptance was rewritten to claim what the arc delivers: every
product a planner *draws* is constructed by name. Whether a planner draws all
the codes its family routes is a coverage question, and it is now stated there
rather than implied here.
