#+brief: Accepting a provider's terms.
#+filetags: :model:operation:marketdata:
#+created: 2026-10-28
#+updated: 2026-10-28

A provider with a terms page is fetched only when its terms, as they stand, were
accepted. This operation is the one writer of an acceptance: the service computes
the digest of the terms it holds, and takes the account from the session and the
time from its own clock, so a caller states only which provider it accepts.

/**
 * @brief Accepts the terms of a provider as they stand.
 *
 * The caller names the provider and nothing else: what is accepted is the terms
 * the service holds, by whom is the session's account, and when is the service's
 * clock. Terms that change afterwards no longer match, so they must be accepted
 * again.
 */
#+end_src
