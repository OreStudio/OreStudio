#+created: 2026-10-05
#+updated: 2026-10-05

The operations a component and a person take on notifications. Raising one
writes the notification, its arguments, a recipient row for each person told
and their in-application delivery, in one transaction; a permission audience
is resolved into the accounts that hold the permission at that moment. A
person reads, marks and clears only their own.

The handler is hand-written beside this model, because raising resolves the
audience and reading joins a person's copies to the notifications they name.

* Physical space

The shell commands are switched off. Raising takes a list of named values,
which has no shell token form, and a component raises a notification rather
than a person; the shell reads notifications through the notification entity
units.
