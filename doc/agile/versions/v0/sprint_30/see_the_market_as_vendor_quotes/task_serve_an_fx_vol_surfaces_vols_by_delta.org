* Goal

The quotes view compares two vendors' FX vol surfaces as vols by delta (10P, 25P, ATM, 25C, 10C), because a difference in a risk reversal is a difference in skew, not in a vol. The conversion depends on the pair's delta convention (spot or forward delta, premium-adjusted or not), its ATM convention and a smile fitted to the strangles, which only the server knows from the FX option conventions. The browser must not guess it.

* Acceptance

- A read returns a vendor's surface of a pair, or the consensus, as vols by delta for each tenor, converted by the pair's FX option conventions.
- The conversion is tested against known values for at least one spot-delta and one premium-adjusted forward-delta pair.
- A surface that lacks a quote the conversion needs is returned with that tenor marked incomplete, not converted from a guess.
- The quotes view's compare mode reads the server's vols by delta instead of the prototype's strangle approximation.

* Result

- The market data service answers =fit_fx_vol_quotes= (its own operation model,
  =ores.marketdata.fx_vol_quote_fit_operations=, with no shell command, since a
  list of quotes is not typed at a prompt): for each named source of a pair's
  quotes it builds a recipe from the quotes (=make_quote_fit_recipe=: the
  source's expiries in tenor order, its ATM, risk reversals and butterflies,
  and an output grid of 10P, 25P, ATM, 25C and 10C in the convention's delta
  type), reads the pair's convention =<BASE>-<QUOTE>-FXOPTION= and the party's
  published forward set at now, and fits SSVI with the calibration's own
  =check_recipe=, =prepare_calibration= and =calibrate_ssvi=. It writes
  nothing, and needs =marketdata::fx_vol_surface_calibrations:read=. A source
  that cannot be fitted is answered with why, and the others still are.
- =ores.http= and the BFF answer =POST /api/marketdata/quotes/vols-by-delta=
  with the pair and the sources; the instant is the route's own.
- The quotes view's surface compare reads as quoted or by delta. By delta it
  sends A's and B's quotes, B the consensus of the others as before, refits
  every ten seconds, and draws A − B at each delta, the picked tenor's smile
  and a delta by tenor, naming the convention and delta type the server used.
- Checked on the running fleet as UK plc: a fit of two sources over 13 expiries
  answered in 2 to 6 seconds; EUR/USD's derived surface read as a smile by
  delta on EUR-USD-FXOPTION (spot deltas), and a synthetic source with a −0.4
  risk reversal and a 0.2 butterfly fitted to wings 0.41 apart about a point
  0.19 above ATM, as quoted.
- Tests: the plan's quote fit (3 cases: the recipe, a flat smile, a skewed
  one), the fit codec round trips (4), the vols-by-delta route (2), the
  Playwright check of the by-delta compare (fitted in 5.4 s), and every web
  workspace.
