* Result

The market data documents now state the service's world, or name the target
where the app has not moved yet.

- The menu names the four service areas. The Bindings card is gone, replaced by
  subscriptions and routes. Synthetic sits in the Simulator area.
- The feed-tree note and row say demand starts a generator, and Start and Stop
  are the operator test controls.
- The feed standard's trail adds the Simulator level.
- The FX and IR journeys no longer say a saved feed is produced only when a
  person starts it. Demand starts it; the operator test is a separate act.
- The synthetic screens page says demand starts a generator before it describes
  the start and stop cascade.
- The IR authoring note no longer calls the source a binding.

Open, with the reason:

- The app routes still put Synthetic directly under Market data. The menu now
  names the Simulator area, so a route change is part of the screens task.
- The manage-synthetic-collections prototype and the shell operation surface
  still describe auto-start as the run trigger. The shell still offers the
  test controls, so those pages need the demand note, not a rewrite.
- The synthetic market simulator prototype note still summarises the window as
  starting and stopping. Its cascade is the test control, as the screens page
  now says.
