* Result

All four acceptance bullets are met.

[[id:AB71AFF8-0B08-4610-9F12-C5A84D8AB892][Trade modeling]] derives the entities a trade is built from — product
type, security issue, trade and instrument — from domain observations,
and sets out how each is identified. The document went beyond the
original three levels: the economic terms turned out to be a fourth
entity, and the prior art survey covers the ISDA taxonomy, FpML, CDM,
Calypso, Murex and ORE.

The trade type catalogue carries all 119 v17 product types. =Failed= is
excluded, with the reason recorded in the populate script itself: it is
the trade type ORE gives to a placeholder substituted for a trade it
could not load or build, so it carries no trade data and a trader
cannot pick it.

[[id:C5C95B29-2EF9-4DFC-9E04-428C41C1925D][ORE Products by Asset Class]] links through org-roam ids, replacing 89
direct links to a hardcoded host.

All 88 product pages carry an Identifiers table. An audit of those
tables corrected 19 asset classes that had been taken from
=product_type= rather than from the asset class taxonomy, and every
page gained its product family.
