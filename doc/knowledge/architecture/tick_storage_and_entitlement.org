#+title: A source tick is stored once and entitled when it is read
#+description: A tick belongs to a stream. The store holds each stream's points once, owned by the installation and with no row-level security, and a party's subscription, or its ownership of a derived stream, is checked when it reads.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :marketdata:ticks:storage:entitlement:
#+created: 2026-10-25

* Summary

A tick belongs to a stream. A vendor's or a simulator's source stream is shared
by every party that subscribes to it; a derived or blended stream belongs to the
party that makes it. The store holds the points of each stream once: rows of
stream, time, coordinate and value, owned by the installation, with no tenant
column and no row-level security. A party's quote series stays the party's own
row, under row-level security, and points at its stream. Entitlement is checked
where a party reads: the history read and the republish check the party's
subscription, or its ownership of a derived stream, before they return or send a
point.

* Detail

** What the store holds

| Table | Owner | Holds |
|-------+-------+-------|
| Streams | The installation | One row for each source series and each derived series: its source, its datum, and the party that owns it when it is derived |
| Points | The installation | One row for each tick of a stream: stream, time, coordinate, value |
| Quote series | The party | The party's view of a stream: its classification, its asset classes, its stream |

A point holds no tenant's data. It is an observation of a source, so it carries
no tenant column, and one tick of a source is one row however many parties
subscribe to it.

** Who can read a point

Only the marketdata service's roles hold grants on the streams and the points; a
tenant's session holds none. The service reads a point for a party after it has
checked, in the party's tenant and under row-level security, that the party's
quote series names the stream and that the party holds a subscription with the
right to read it, or owns the derived stream. The check is one indexed lookup
for each read, not one for each point.

** Who receives a tick

The live path republishes a tick to each party subscribed to its stream, on the
party's subject. The delivery meter counts each delivery for the party
([[id:3E51B0BD-D22B-4790-821F-E2FED44D152C][Usage is recorded as events and priced later]]), so billing counts deliveries, not stored rows.

** What it saves

At one hundred thousand feeds the store takes about 29,000 rows a second, one for
each source tick, whatever the number of parties. The points carry no
row-level security, so closed chunks compress ([[id:A79C25FA-9F70-453B-A52E-780B4402ABAA][The tick store is bounded by compression, narrow chunks and retention policies]]).
