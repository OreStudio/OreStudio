* Result

The UI review widened the task. The Market tab had two per-feed tables, Laws
and Arrivals, with two rasters of the same ticks. They are now split by scope:

- Arrivals reads the market whole: the folded heat map, a card a law with its
  feed count, and a card a price process with its feed count. Each card opens
  Feeds narrowed to its feeds.
- Feeds is the one per-feed table: feed, process, law, the lanes and seen /
  asked, with a law filter and a process filter carried in the address.
- The Laws tab is gone.

The process and the law share one summary card. Each shows its name only, and
the card opens on hover or focus. A click keeps the process card open. The law
stays a link to the feed's Arrivals tab, and its card holds its numbers. No
value shows in a row by default. The Fit table uses the same law tag.

The loop's health moved to the top of Fit. A feed's own Arrivals tab shows that
feed's trace, without the feed filter. On Arrivals the heat map sits below the
cards. Its hot end stays gold: red and green were tried and dropped.

Evidence: web tests pass (2253), typecheck clean, and the harness passes 16 of
16 live. It checks the tabs, the overview cards' links and filters, the law and
process cards on hover and on click, the feed's trace, and that health sits
above the fit table. Screenshots of every changed view were checked. Held for
the user's review of the UI before merge.
