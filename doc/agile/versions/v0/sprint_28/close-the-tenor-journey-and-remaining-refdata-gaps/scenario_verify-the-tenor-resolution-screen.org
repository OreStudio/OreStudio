This page documents a test scenario verifying [[id:C73F5C2E-1FE8-48BC-B2A2-1092EF828F3B][Build the tenor resolution screen]] in [[id:65F988DE-2077-4713-9D40-C2937B756BAE][Close the tenor journey and the remaining refdata gaps]]. It is filled in with the target screen and checklist of steps before testing starts; record the outcome by hand in =* Results=.

* Before you start

- The database must be freshly provisioned with Acme Corporation, so the
  tenant holds the seeded tenors, their conventions and the tenant
  administrator: =compass db recreate -y -k=, then the ACME bring-up
  (=projects/ores.shell/scripts/library/provisioning/how_do_i_provision_the_system_with_acme_corporation_holding_group.ores=).
  Confirm the run completes with no failed steps.
- The services must be up: =compass services start=. All of them, not just
  NATS: the screen reads the catalogue from =ores.refdata=, the pick lists
  from several services, and the history from =ores.history=.
- Sign in at =http://127.0.0.1:21602/= as =tenant_admin@acme_corporation=
  with =Secure-Password-123=.

*Known and expected.* The *Resolved dates* is **not** part of this screen.
The server serves no operation that resolves a tenor to a date, so the step
is absent rather than broken. Do not record it as a failure. See the task's
=* Result= for the sibling task that adds it.

* Steps

** Log in as the tenant administrator

- Sign in at =http://127.0.0.1:21602/= as =tenant_admin@acme_corporation=
  with the password =Secure-Password-123=.
- Open *Reference data* and find the card *Define how tenors resolve*.
- Confirms the step: the card is offered to this person. It is a workflow
  card, so it sits with *Define instrument conventions* among the
  reference-data cards, not on a screen list of its own.

*** Result

| Field  | Value |
|--------+-------|
| Status | PASS |

** Open the screen and read the rail

- Choose *Define how tenors resolve*.
- Confirms the step: the browser shows the tenor journey, and its rail
  carries all seven steps in this order: *Choose*, *Tenor*, *Convention*,
  *The schedule*, *Review*, *Outcome*, *History*.
- A rail of six, or one whose *The schedule* is missing, is a failure: the
  schedule step was added late and is the easiest one to lose.

** List the tenors and open one

- On *Choose*, the screen lists the tenant's tenors with their code, kind,
  unit and version, and lists the conventions beneath them.
- Type part of a tenor code into the search box (try =O/N= or =3M=).
- Confirms the step: the tenor list narrows to the matches. Open a tenor
  whose kind and unit are both set — a *period* tenor with a unit — because
  the next step saves it and a half-formed row is refused.

** Edit the tenor

- On *Tenor*, change *Display name* and *Description*.
- Confirms the step: the banner above the rail shows the tenor code and its
  version, and the change survives moving to the next step.
- Leave *Code* alone: it is the key, and the screen does not offer to
  change it.

** Edit the convention and the tenors it resolves

- On *Convention*, change *Description*. Do not change *Code*.
- In *Tenors in the set*, tick a tenor that is not in the set, and untick
  one that is.
- Confirms the step: the unticked tenor's anchor override control greys out,
  because a tenor not in the set has nothing to measure from.

* Notes

The journey's resolved dates and its rolled schedule are the two parts the
prototype draws that this screen does not. Both wait on the operation that
resolves a tenor to a date, which the server does not serve yet; both are
carried by `Resolve a tenor over the wire, with the refusal it carries`.
