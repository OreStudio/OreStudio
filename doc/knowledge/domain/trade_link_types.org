#+title: Trade link types
#+description: The catalogue of trade link types: what each one connects, which process creates it, and whether it carries an economic effect.

* Summary

A link type names the reason two trades are joined. The trade model
carries the types below; each row says what the link connects, what
causes it, and which page owns the detail. One property sorts them:
the economic effect. A type with an effect is a trade event, and a type
without is an annotation. Adding a reason means adding a row here, not
a special case in code.

* Detail

** How to read the catalogue

[[id:2AAC92EB-2320-4385-999E-947E9E8FE5C5][Trade links]] states the
principle: a link with an economic effect is a trade event, and a link
with no economic effect is an annotation. The two tables follow that
split. The first table's links cut a version of the trades they join,
and some of them need a confirmation. The second table's links are
recorded, displayed and reported, and they change no term.

** Notes

/On novation./ Novation changes the parties rather than the terms.
Under the ISDA Master Agreement a party may not transfer its interest
without the prior written consent of the other party, and the 2005 ISDA
Novation Protocol sets a same-day consent process between the
transferor, the transferee and the remaining party. The substance is
that a novation is a new legal relationship, not an edit to an existing
one, and a system that models it as an edit loses the audit trail. Our
model treats it as a link between two trades, directed from the
transferred trade to the trade with the new counterparty.

/Netting has two senses./ Merging trades into one is a trade event and
appears in the first table. Netting settlement amounts is a payment
mechanic: it nets cash flows, not trades, and it carries no trade link.

/Split has two senses./ Allocating a block splits it into child trades
and creates no trade event. Separately, the blotter's /Link Split/
breaks an existing link. This catalogue uses allocation for the first
meaning throughout.

/Expiry and downsizing are not links./ Expiry moves a trade to
=expired= and generates nothing; a partial close-out reduces the
notional of the same trade by amendment. Where exercise or expiry
produces a settlement, the resulting cash is
[[id:AAF5F9CB-D854-40A8-AEB5-50D998A9F0C0][linked cash]].
