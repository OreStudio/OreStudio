#+title: A tenant cannot list its own jobs

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The operations group builds the installation's screens: the services, the
grid, the bus, the logs and the versions. Its grid screen is the system
administrator's, offered in system administration mode alone, and it shows
the fleet and the installation's work counts.

Nothing serves a tenant administrator the other half — the workunits,
batches and results its own users are running. The compute screens that
would carry it belong to the ores.compute journeys, which have not been
extracted: fourteen journeys over forty-five legacy =ores.qt/compute=
screens, still a backlog story.

* Why

The tenant administrator's question — "what is my tenant running?" — has no
screen, and the operations screens do not answer it by design. The jobs
tables are already per-tenant, so the data is there; the journey and the
screen are the missing pieces.

The order matters. The operations group wrote its journeys, walked a
prototype, and then built. A screen ported without its journey is a screen
nobody agreed to, which is why this capture names the extraction as the
prerequisite rather than the screen.
