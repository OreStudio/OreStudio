trade set a run gathers: every ACCOUK trade visible at 2026-11-21T23:59:59Z.

2. What the market publishes at all
-----------------------------------
The acme tenant (code =acme_corporation=, five parties) holds 1,147 series, and
every one of them is a price or a curve. It holds no fixing series of any class.

    compass sql -- -c "select split_part(oresmd_uri,'/',3) as class, case when
      oresmd_uri like '%type=fixing%' then 'fixing' else 'series' end as form,
      count(*) from ores_marketdata_market_series_tbl where valid_to > now()
      and tenant_id = '<acme tenant>' group by 1, 2"

   class | form   | series
---------+--------+--------
 fx      | series |    612
 ir      | series |    535

The system tenant answers the fixing half of the requirement rule, and it holds

   class | form   | series
---------+--------+--------
 fx      | series |    138
 fx      | fixing |    132
 ir      | series |    109
 ir      | fixing |     51

So the tenant publishes market data for two asset classes and no more. The
shared database does hold 364 credit, 97 commodity, 77 equity, 62 security and
34 inflation series, but every one of them belongs to another tenant — the
=marketdata_corpus_*= and =marketdata_export_*= tenants the tests make. A
tenant-blind count of the table answers a question about the machine, not about
this tenant, and is the one mistake this measurement is easy to make.

In this tenant the equity, commodity, credit, inflation and security classes have
no series at all. Those 84 addresses cannot be priced for want of any market data
of their class, not for want of a name match.


3. FX: a source the market does not publish, and pairs it does not publish
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A trade's FX index names a pair and a source: =FX-TR20H-EUR-USD= is the EUR-USD
fixing of source TR20H. The generator states that source in one constant,
=trade_population_planner.cpp:1939= (=fx_fixing_source = "TR20H"=), and the
system tenant holds a TR20H fixing for two pairs only. Of the 24 pairs the
population names, nine already have a fixing series in the system tenant under
a publisher's own source:

    compass sql -- -c "with need(b, q) as (values ...) select n.b, n.q,
      (select string_agg(distinct substring(s.oresmd_uri from 'source=([A-Za-z0-9]+)'), ', ')
       from ores_marketdata_market_series_tbl s where s.valid_to > now()
       and s.tenant_id = 'ffffffff-ffff-ffff-ffff-ffffffffffff'
       and s.oresmd_uri like 'oresmd://fx/' || n.b || '?type=fixing&index=fx&source=%&ccy=' || n.q)"

   pair     | source published in the system tenant
------------+--------------------------------------
 CAD/JPY  | BOC
 EUR/AUD  | ECB
 EUR/CAD  | ECB
 EUR/GBP  | ECB
 EUR/USD  | ECB
 USD/BRL  | IMF
 USD/CAD  | IMF
 USD/INR  | IMF
 USD/JPY  | IMF
 USD/MXN  | IMF
 USD/SGD  | IMF

 The other thirteen — every AUD cross, GBP/AUD, GBP/CAD, GBP/CHF, GBP/JPY,
 GBP/NZD, GBP/USD, NZD/USD, USD/HKD — have no fixing under any source: the
 provision publishes fixings for EUR, USD and CAD bases, and for no other.

The eleven pairs the party prices are a different set again:

    compass sql -- -c "with p as (...ACCOUK...), need(b, q) as (values
      ('AUD','CAD'), ... ('USD','SGD')) select n.b, n.q, exists (select 1 from
      ores_marketdata_market_series_tbl s, p where s.tenant_id = p.tenant_id
      and s.party_id = p.id and s.valid_to > now() and s.oresmd_uri =
      'oresmd://fx/' || n.b || '?type=series&instrument=fx_spot&quote=rate&ccy='
      || n.q) as spot_held from need n"

    priced: AUD/USD  EUR/USD  GBP/USD  NZD/USD  USD/BRL  USD/CAD  USD/HKD
            USD/INR  USD/JPY  USD/MXN  USD/SGD
    none:   AUD/CAD  AUD/CHF  AUD/JPY  AUD/NZD  CAD/JPY  EUR/AUD  EUR/CAD
            EUR/GBP  GBP/AUD  GBP/CAD  GBP/CHF  GBP/JPY  GBP/NZD

The two sets overlap by eight pairs, so no single rule — "name a source the
market publishes" or "publish what the party prices" — closes the gap alone.

6. IR: indices no generated curve covers
----------------------------------------
The system tenant publishes 51 IBOR fixing series. Of the 15 IBOR addresses that
fail, every one names an index and tenor that list does not carry:

    compass sql -- -c "select substring(oresmd_uri from 'oresmd://ir/([A-Z]+)') as ccy,
      substring(oresmd_uri from 'name=([A-Za-z0-9]+)') as name,
      substring(oresmd_uri from 'tenor=([0-9A-Z]+)') as tenor
      from ores_marketdata_market_series_tbl where valid_to > now()
      and tenant_id = 'ffffffff-ffff-ffff-ffff-ffffffffffff'
      and oresmd_uri like 'oresmd://ir/%type=fixing%index=ibor%'"

    failed address                          | what the system tenant publishes
    ----------------------------------------+-------------------------------------
    ir/AOA fixing ibor name=GENERIC tenor=3M | nothing for AOA
    ir/BWP ... GENERIC 3M                   | nothing for BWP
    ir/IDR ... GENERIC 3M                   | nothing for IDR
    ir/KRW ... GENERIC 3M                   | KRW KOFR only, an overnight
    ir/MYR ... GENERIC 3M                   | nothing for MYR
    ir/OMR ... GENERIC 3M                   | nothing for OMR
    ir/COP fixing ibor name=IBR tenor=3M    | COP IBR, with no tenor stated
    ir/PLN fixing ibor name=WIBOR tenor=6M  | PLN POLONIA only, an overnight
    ir/SAR fixing ibor name=SAIBOR tenor=3M | nothing for SAR
    ir/THB fixing ibor name=BIBOR tenor=3M  | nothing for THB
    ir/JPY fixing ibor name=EYTIBOR tenor=3M| JPY LIBOR, TONA and TONAR
    ir/HKD fixing ibor name=HIBOR tenor=1M  | HKD HONIA only
    ir/HKD fixing ibor name=HIBOR tenor=2W  | HKD HONIA only
    ir/HKD fixing ibor name=HIBOR tenor=3M  | HKD HONIA only
    ir/USD fixing ibor name=SOFR tenor=1M   | USD SOFR, with no tenor stated

=GENERIC= is not the generator's invention: the reference data holds 92 =GENERIC=
IBOR conventions, one for each currency with no named Ibor, and their own
addresses state no tenor:

    oresmd://ir/AOA?type=fixing&index=ibor&name=GENERIC

The trade's requirement states a tenor because its leg has one, so even where the
catalogue holds the convention the fixing address the trade names is not the
address the convention states. The other two addresses of this shape are =COP
IBR= and =USD SOFR=, whose curves state no tenor where the trades book 3M and 1M.

So the IR half of the gap is a curve-coverage gap and not a fixing gap, which is
what [[id:06886D23-1A52-4BBC-B305-4609D606EEA7][Generate the historical fixings the generated trades need]] recorded
when it built the feed: an index with no generated curve has no fixing to derive
from, and inventing one would price a leg on a rate no curve produces.
