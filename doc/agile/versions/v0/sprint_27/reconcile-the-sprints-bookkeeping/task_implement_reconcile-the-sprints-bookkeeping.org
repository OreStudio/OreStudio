* Plan

Four groups, worked in the order that keeps them from cascading wrongly:

1. The story ready to close and the six tasks whose pull requests have all
   merged: closed with =compass task done= and =compass story done=. Two of the
   eight were left open -- see the story's decisions.
2. The seven table disagreements: two closed with =compass story done=, which
   rewrites the row, and five rows edited directly because compass has no verb
   that starts a story.
3. The twenty-five closed documents whose =Now= was not =Nothing.=: for each, the
   =Now= text was moved into its =Result= first, and only then blanked. Six had no
   =Result= section at all, so writing one was the whole point.
4. A second audit, which promoted five more stories to "ready to close" because
   their last task had just closed. Those were closed and given Results too.

The two left open are not oversights: one waits on five open items and a
decision, the other on a blocked dependency.
