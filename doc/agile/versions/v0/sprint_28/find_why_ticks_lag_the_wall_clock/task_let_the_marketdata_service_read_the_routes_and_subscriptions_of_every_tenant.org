* Result

The marketdata service reads the routes and the subscriptions of every tenant. The two models state
=rls_system_tenant_visible=, the generator writes the clause into both tenant policies, and the
widening gate lists the policies as temporary exceptions, with the reason and a pointer to the audit
story. A pgTAP test of eight assertions fails four on the old policies and passes all eight on the
new ones.

On a fresh database, with the services started first and Acme provisioned after, the ingest loop
accepted 26 of 26 ids for =bloomberg-a=, and 3,608 ticks in 104 series of one tenant reached the tick
table in about a minute, none of them dated in the future and the newest 20 ms old. Marketdata was
not restarted. The route and subscription change events reached it during provisioning and
refreshed the demand.

The policy lets a system-tenant session write the rows of another tenant too, as it does for
=feed_bindings=. The cause of the missing events that =eager_maxwell= saw earlier is not isolated:
they appear now, and the policy change is the only one made.
