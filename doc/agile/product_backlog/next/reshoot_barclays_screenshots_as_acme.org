This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Five manual chapters carry real UI screenshots captured against the
=barclays_plc= tenant, back when Barclays Plc was the system's
reference/demo entity: =chapter_tenants.org= (Tenants window, showing
the =barclays_plc= tenant row), =chapter_accounts_and_roles.org=
(New Account — Parties tab, assigning to =BARCLAYS PLC=),
=chapter_connecting.org= (logging in as
=tenant_admin@barclays_plc=), =chapter_parties.org= (Party History
dialog for =BARCLAYS BANK PLC=), and =chapter_counterparties.org=
(prose mentioning "Barclays' counterparties"). Now that
[[id:E3387565-DC9B-45BA-B98D-437ED80CEAED][Acme Corporation]] is the system's reference entity (see the story
below), re-shoot each of these against a freshly provisioned Acme
Corporation tenant, following [[id:43A1EE0A-374D-4EA1-BC72-BADDDC525D20][How do I capture screenshots for a manual
chapter?]], and update each caption/prose reference to match the new
image (e.g. =acme_corporation= tenant code, =tenant_admin@acme_corporation=,
an Acme legal entity in place of =BARCLAYS PLC=/=BARCLAYS BANK PLC=).

* Why

The [[id:3630BEB2-A5CE-42FD-85ED-22496B3DBCA7][Write user manual chapter: Acme Corporation as the system's
test/demo entity]] task deliberately left these five chapters
untouched — their screenshots are real UI captures, so editing the
prose without re-shooting the image would leave a caption describing
something the picture doesn't show. This capture is that deferred
re-shoot, so the whole manual is consistent about which entity is the
system's reference/demo tenant.
