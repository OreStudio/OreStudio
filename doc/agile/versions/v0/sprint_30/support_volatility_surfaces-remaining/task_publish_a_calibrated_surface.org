* Goal

A candidate surface becomes authoritative in the same three acts as a curve. A maker raises the publish, naming the blueprint and the as-of. A second person decides under the four-eyes rule. The approved publish applies once, stamps the publisher and the instant, ends the set it replaces and sends the notice. See [[id:53D14F31-EE32-493E-BBEB-140AA4906BE4][Constructing a volatility surface: from quotes to a published set]].

* Acceptance

- A maker raises the publish of a candidate, and the maker cannot approve it.
- An approved publish applies once, and a repeat changes nothing.
- The new set ends the set it replaces for the same blueprint, and history shows both.
- A candidate whose fit did not converge, whose pillars are beyond the tolerance, or that breaks an arbitrage condition shows so to the person who decides, before the decision.
- A published surface sends the same kind of notice as a published curve.
- A surface belongs to the blueprint's party, and only a person acting for that party can raise its publish.

* Plan

The publish follows the curve publish and the forward set publish. A new =fx_vol_surface_publish= record is the subject of a =marketdata.fx_vol_surface_publish= request: the blueprint, the as-of, the calibration and its version, and the calibration's checks copied at the raise, so the decider reads what they approve. The apply refuses a calibration rerun after the raise, publishes once, ends every live surface of the output series in one unit of work, and sends a =marketdata.surface_published= notice through the curve notices' helpers. A read, =get_fx_vol_surface_publish_subject=, gives the decider the subject. The services name the session's party through a shared =session_party=.

* Result

Done on 2026-11-11. On a fresh Acme tenant with the auto-approval off, a trader of ACCOUS calibrated EURUSD_SSVI at 01:58 and 01:59 and raised the publish of the first; the request waited. The trader could not decide it (=not_a_decider=). The Market Risk decider read its subject, converged with a largest error of 0.000012, no pillar beyond the tolerance and no violation, and approved. The trader applied it once, and a repeat was refused, naming when and by whom it was published. The second surface, published the same way, superseded the first: the first row's history reads raised, published, superseded, with the successor's as-of, and the second is the series' one live surface. Each apply sent one =marketdata.surface_published= notice. The scripts are =evidence/fx_vol_surface_publish_*.ores= and =evidence/fx_vol_surface_publishes.sql=.

The party rule is in the service and was not exercised with a second party. The record page the notice links to and the inbox's view of the subject are in the screens task.
