#+title: Kinds of cut
#+description: The classification of cuts by the occasion they are taken for: the official end-of-day valuation, the independent verification of prices, the intraday flash view and the start of the day, with what each is for, what it fixes, the attributes its occasion gives it, and its relation to the components.

This page classifies cuts by the occasion they are taken for. The
[[id:B5214F3A-A72E-421E-82BC-79338B2E95CB][Cuts]] structure note gives the
definition, the single purpose and the body; this page states why the occasion is
the property that classifies a cut, and places each kind under the occasion that
produces it.

* Summary

The kinds of cut classify the occasion a cut is taken for, and not the size of
the body. The purpose is single, a valuation that can be reproduced, so the body
is the entire fixed world of one valuation in every kind. Four occasions are in
use, the official valuation, the verification of prices, the intraday view and
the start of the day, and a process may name an instant of its own. Each kind is
stated by what it is for, what it fixes, the attributes its occasion gives it,
and its relation to the two reviewed halves of the body.

* The principle

A cut fixes a body of data at one timepoint, and its purpose is single: to make a
valuation reproducible. The
[[id:B5214F3A-A72E-421E-82BC-79338B2E95CB][Cuts]] structure note argues that
purpose and the size of the body that follows from it. Since the purpose is the
same for every cut, the body is the same for every cut. A verification and an
official report that read the same valuation must each hold every input to it, or
neither of them can be reproduced.

What varies is the occasion. A cut is taken at an instant, by a process, for a
use, and from a base. Those facts are what a kind records: the recurrence of the
instant, the centre that owns it, the use the result serves, and the base the cut
builds on. The classification is therefore a classification of occasions, and the
body is held constant across it.

Two consequences follow. An attribute that follows from the occasion is entailed
by the kind and needs no separate justification. And a cut missing a part of the
body is incomplete rather than smaller, whatever its kind: it holds less than the
definition requires, and the defect is the same defect in every occasion.
Completeness belongs to every kind, and the kinds differ in the occasion they
serve rather than in the standard they meet.

* The official valuation: end of day cut

*What it is for.* The day's official revaluation of a book, the movement from
the previous cut to this one, and the reports built on both.

*What it fixes.* The complete body, at the centre's close, with the approved
rates applied. The
[[id:B5214F3A-A72E-421E-82BC-79338B2E95CB][Cuts]] structure note argues the
extent of that body, and the
[[id:DD59B04C-DAD2-458C-B35E-3F6B0B3D6DC6][Market data cut]] and
[[id:19630B5D-A163-42B5-B9C4-61A87BAE8F5F][Trade cut]] pages state the two data
halves of it.

*What the occasion gives it.* Recurrence is daily, because a business day is the
period the reports cover. The instant is the centre's close, because a business
day ends at a close and the close belongs to a centre. Ownership is the centre's,
because the approved rates are the rates that centre signed off. The close is a
stated instant published by the settlement system for the currency, as one
settlement system's published hours show
([[https://www.federalreserve.gov/paymentsystems/fedfunds_about.htm][Fedwire Funds Service]]).

*How it relates to the components.* Both components, complete. The deals supply
the population, the market data the values, and neither is taken at a different
instant from the other.

The label *independent cut* is also applied to this kind, on the ground that
approved rates replace the rates the front office used during the day. The ground
is sound as far as it goes, and the label is better kept for the verification
kind below. The difference is one of provenance. The end of day cut's values are
approved by finance, which is to say that the firm signs off data the firm
produced. The verification cut's values originate with parties other than the
firm. Independence admits degrees, and the stronger degree belongs to the second
kind.

* The verification of prices: independent price verification cut

*What it is for.* Testing the firm's own marks against data the firm did not
produce. A firm that marks its book with its own prices cannot test those prices
from the same source. Replacing selected values with externally sourced ones
produces a second valuation, and the difference between the two measures how much
of the day's value rests on the firm's own marks.

*What it fixes.* The complete body of the end of day cut, with selected market
data items replaced.

*What the occasion gives it.* Recurrence, centre and instant are inherited from
the cut it is built on, since a second valuation of the same day must read the
same body at the same instant or the difference it measures includes the day.
What the kind adds is a condition on provenance, which is the whole of its
contribution to the definition.

A prudential standard fixes what may count as an independent source. It accepts
exchange prices in a liquid market, trades in the same or a very similar
instrument, tradable quotes from brokers and other market participants,
consensus service data, indicative broker quotes, and counterparty collateral
valuations
([[https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/101/chapter/I][EBA RTS (EU) 2016/101 on prudent valuation]], Article 3). The same article
names the independent price verification process as the process whose market data
range those sources serve. The standard fixes the admissible sources and leaves
the choice of items to the firm's own policy.

*How it relates to the components.* The market data component is altered, since
the substitution applies to values. The trade component is carried over
unchanged, since the test is of prices and the population is the object the
prices are tested on.

* The intraday view: flash cut

*What it is for.* An indicative view of value while the day is still moving,
delivered before the day's data settles so that a decision can act on it.

*What it fixes.* The complete body the run reads, at an instant before the official authorisation, with the values current at that instant. The cut records the taking identity and the authorisation as pending; the body is the four inputs at the flash instant. The official cut later records the authorisation. The deadline shortens the
interval and not the body, since a run that left out an input could not be
reproduced from what it kept.

*What the occasion gives it.* The instant precedes the official authorisation, which is entailed
by the occasion, since a view that arrived after the official statement would
serve no decision. Recurrence is intraday, since the view answers a question that
recurs while the market moves.

*How it relates to the components.* Both reviewed halves, at the flash instant.
The run values deals and reads prices, so it holds the deal population and the
market data of that instant, and the rest of the body is recorded with it.

The output is indicative by construction. Authorisation converts a candidate into
official data, and a cut taken to inform a decision precedes that conversion.

* The start of the day: start of day cut

*What it is for.* Opening a day from a known reference, so that the day's
movement has a recorded starting point and can be reproduced later.

*What it fixes.* The complete body of the previous end of day cut, carried forward.

*What the occasion gives it.* Recurrence is daily, since the day it opens is the
unit the reports cover. The instant is the centre's opening, which is a stated
instant for the same reason the close is. Ownership is the centre's, for the same
reason as at the close.

*How it relates to the components.* Both components, inherited rather than
approved at the opening. What the kind adds is a direction of inheritance.

* Where a kind sits against a component

A kind qualifies the cut as a whole, and the process that takes the cut chooses
it. A component names one half of the body, and the component pages take each
half. The kind does not select the components, since the body is the whole fixed
world in every kind. A complete description of a cut states its kind, which fixes
the occasion, and its body, which the purpose fixes.

* An instant a process names

A process may fix a body of data at an instant that answers to no schedule and no
centre: a rerun at a stated instant, a stress valuation, a verification run
before a report. Purpose is singular in such a case and therefore supplies no
recurring kind. The cut satisfies the definition on the same ground as the four
kinds above, since the body is fixed and one instant fixes it.

* What the sources establish

Two claims on this page have a public source. The admissible sources of an
independent price verification rest on the prudential standard cited above. The
stated open and close of one centre's business day rests on the settlement
system's published hours, cited above.

The rest is our working convention. The existence of the flash runs and of the
start of day cut, the order in which the components are fixed, and the arbitrary
instant a process may name have no public source we could verify. The definitions
of the four occasions are ours. Practice at a particular firm is a matter for that
firm's own record.
