* Goal

Decide how the synthetic feeds share one correlated market (open decision OD-1), so that spot, curves, surfaces and the asset classes after them move together under the regime and the Hawkes arrivals they already share, and a test can measure the correlation it states. The investigation states the options, their costs and a recommendation, and the approach is chosen from it.
