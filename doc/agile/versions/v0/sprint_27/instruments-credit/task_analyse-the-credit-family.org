* Result

The credit family is surveyed and the rework is designed. The family is eight
trade type codes in one 25-column table, and six of the eight have an
importer. The design finds fourteen divergences from the instrument table
rule, among them six columns no mapper writes, two importer-less codes, an
exported risk participation agreement that is not valid ORE, and money values
stored as float artifacts.

The proposal is a six-code header with the reference entity and the two
dates, six product fact tables keyed by the trade, the swap leg tables reused
with a =leg_group= column, the shared option and strike children reused, and
one new basket child. The rework is staged into seven units, from the digits
to the proof over the eight corpus documents.

Acceptance met: every credit type is inventoried, the divergences are
findings with evidence, the model is a table set and a diagram, the bare
float members the credit corpus uses are listed, and the rework is staged
with no code change.
