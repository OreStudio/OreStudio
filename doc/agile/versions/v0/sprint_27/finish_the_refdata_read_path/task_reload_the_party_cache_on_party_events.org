* Result

Party is off the hand-wired application.cpp path and onto the generated
event-mapping registrar. The trigger's created, updated and deleted
notifications now publish a party_event on refdata.v1.parties_events.<action>,
which is the subject IAM's party cache subscribes to; the hand-wired
party_changed_event registration and subscriber are gone.

Verified: the service builds clean; its startup log registers the canonical
mapping on channel ores_refdata_parties with prefix refdata.v1.parties_events,
subscribes the party_event handler, and listens on the channel; a notification
posted on the channel is received and dispatched. The full insert path was not
driven live: the hand-written party insert trigger refuses a copied fixture
row, and the party table announces changes on insert, not on the version-closing
update.
