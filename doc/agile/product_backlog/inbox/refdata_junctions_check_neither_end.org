* What

The insert functions of =currency_calendar=, =currency_country=, =currency_currency_group=, =currency_pair_convention_calendar=, =party_counterparty=, =party_country= and =party_currency= validate the tenant and manage versions, but do not validate either code they link. Every other refdata write validates its soft foreign keys. Add the same existence checks to the junction inserts.

* Why

A screen can only offer codes it read, but any other writer (the shell, an import, a script) can create a link to nothing, and the screens then show rows they cannot resolve. Found while writing the currency and desk group journeys.
