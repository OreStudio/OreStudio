dimensions the skill names, challenges its own readings, repairs the agile

* Acceptance

- The review measures the six dimensions the skill names: goal alignment,
  sprint load, PR velocity, story and task balance, focus signal and the
  overall verdict.
- Every number in the review is a measurement with the command that produced
  it and the commit it was taken at, or is marked as an estimate with its
  basis.
- The findings, the reconciliations and the close decisions are written into
  the review task's =* Result=, and the sprint's =* Health Review= table
  carries the review's summary row.
