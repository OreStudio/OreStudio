* Goal

A person sees the work they can do, in their own words. The home screen shows areas and actions, never journeys; system administration is tenant management, a list with add and remove; and a tenant's details open in place, with the session that reads them held for the screen and never offered as a step.

* Result

The [[id:DD68AA9D-6EA5-477A-8432-566962D938FB][Home screen prototype]] was accepted on 2026-10-04 after four rounds.

- The home page shows the state of the work: a system health card with a plain
  verdict and recent activity, what needs attention, and a short tenant list.
- No screen says *journey*; *Tenants* is one list with *Add tenant* and
  *Remove tenant* on it; a tenant opens in place, and its session is held for
  the screen and never offered as entering or leaving.
- Status colours, the health card and recent activity came from a design
  Gemini suggested; its *Act As* and *Edit* row actions were left out, and the
  record says why.
- The prototype is listed in the prototype index and deployed with the site;
  the application shell prototype's record says what this revision replaces.
