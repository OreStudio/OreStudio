* Acceptance

- A whole number in a double field decodes on the server, so a weight of 1
  saves and a mean of 0 previews.
- *Market data* is an area of the menu, drawn for a person who reads the
  feeds, with an *FX price feeds* card.
- The feed list reads one page at a time, searched and ordered on the server.
- A feed page shows its details, its mixture and its history. A writer adds,
  edits, reverts and deletes a feed; a reader sees no change offered.
- The mixture tab edits the Gaussians as one set, shows the weight sum, the
  return distribution and a batch of sample paths drawn from the values on
  the screen, and saves the set in one call.
- The mixture of one feed is read by its parent, never by reading every
  component.

* Plan

The user chose to do the whole of it in this task, the codec and the gaps the
screen needs included. Each step ends in a check.

1. *Codec.* A reflect-cpp overlay port whose msgpack reader reads either
   integer kind into a floating-point field. Checked by a wire codec test that
   decodes =1= and =-2= into doubles, and live by a save with a weight of 1.
2. *Model.* =fx_spot_config_id= on the GMM component is =:filterable:=, which
   gives the list an equals and a one-of filter on the feed. The feed's pair,
   source name and ORE key are =:searchable:= and =:sortable:=, and its tick
   rate =:sortable:=. Regenerated at every address.
3. *BFF.* The synthetic table declares =search=, =sortable= and =parentField=.
   =GET /api/synthetic/:resource= reads one page with the total when it is
   given a limit, and =GET /api/synthetic/:resource/by/:parent= reads one
   parent's rows. The page query schema moves to the shared helpers.
4. *Shared parts.* A record store (=refdata/store.tsx=) gives the shared parts
   their routes through a context, refdata by default. =RecordDialog= gains
   =complete= and =check=; a record field shows its label.
5. *Screens.* The Market data area, the feed list, the feed page and the
   mixture tab, with the mixture rules in a pure module and an SVG line chart.
6. *Words.* English, French and Portuguese.
7. *Documents.* The journey, the record screen standard and the menu
   structure; the codec capture resolved; the gaps task told what landed.
