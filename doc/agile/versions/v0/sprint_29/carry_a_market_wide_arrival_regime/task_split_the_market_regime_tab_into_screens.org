* Goal

A person who opens the market finds one question per tab: what is the market doing now, what moves it, how every feed's ticks arrive, whether each feed follows its law, and what the loop did. The page takes the review's look: a telemetry strip, cards with a titled header and an action on the right, each setting with the number it implies under it, and figures printed without float noise. It keeps the product's tokens, and nothing on it claims a control or a model the service does not have.

* Plan

The prototype [[id:51E4851F-5F8C-4143-A034-E3E1A2F9C7CE][Run the market]] was
signed off on 2026-10-28, and the build follows it: a strip of seven figures
over six tabs (Now, Settings, Arrivals, Laws, Fit, Trace), in the review's look on
the product's tokens. Two pull requests, because two figures need history the
service does not keep yet.

1. *The screens, on the reads that exist.* The Market regime tab of the
   Synthetic screen becomes the Market tab with its own six tabs, addressed by
   =?tab=regime&view=<tab>=. The strip; Now with the pace, the switch, who holds
   the state and the last changes; Settings as four cards, spells entered in
   seconds, each field with the number it implies; Arrivals with the
   feed-by-feed lanes, the 30, 60 and 120 second windows, and density once marks
   would saturate; Laws, read only, each row linking to the feed's Arrivals tab;
   Fit; and Trace with its filters. Every figure prints without float noise.
2. *The history.* The scheduler's sampler keeps the summed intensity λ beside
   the heat, and per-second tick counts by asset class for an hour, with tenths
   for the last ten minutes. A series carries its asset class so the loop can
   count by it. Now gains λ(t) against the ticks seen; Arrivals gains the folded
   raster in its two folds.

The prototype's load control is the prototype's own and is not built.
