* Goal

A person types a few words into a search bar in ores.web, "volatility" say,
and sees the knowledge pages that match, best first, each with its title, its
description and the section that matched. Opening one shows the page in
ores.web, with its links to other pages working. No model is involved: this is
a feature in its own right, and the infrastructure an agent will later search.

The knowledge base is ORE's org pages. They are loaded into PostgreSQL as they
are, parsed and indexed by a knowledge service, and searched with PostgreSQL's
full-text search. The analysis is in [[id:3237D7CF-8EBA-4B9B-9B54-7135A4A7AA4A][How the assistant answers from the knowledge base]].

* Acceptance

- Which pages are user-facing is declared on the page, a tag or a keyword.
- A script loads the user-facing pages into PostgreSQL as org, unchanged, one
  row per page keyed by its org id. Loading again replaces the previous load.
- A knowledge service indexes the loaded pages with =ores.orgmode= into pages,
  sections and links, whenever the load changes, replacing the index whole.
- A search over the sections uses PostgreSQL full-text search, weighted by
  title, heading and text, and answers pages best first with the section that
  matched.
- ores.http serves the search and a page by its org id, through the knowledge
  service. Nothing goes through the BFF.
- ores.web has a search bar, a list of results and a page view that renders the
  org with =@ores/org=, with links to other pages and anchors on headings.
- A set of at least twenty real questions, each with the pages that should
  answer it, is run against the search, and how many find their page in the
  first five is recorded.
- The gates cover the new code.

* Decisions

- *Store the pages as org, unchanged, and parse them in one indexing step*
  (2026-11-21). The script does not parse; the indexing step writes the shape
  readers need and can be rerun without reloading. ORE already parses org in
  C++ (=ores.orgmode=) and renders it in ores.web (=@ores/org=).
- *One service owns the knowledge base* (2026-11-21): the loaded pages, the
  indexing step, the search and the page reads.
- *It lives in =ores.orgmode=, made a compound component* (2026-11-21, agreed
  with the product owner): =ores.orgmode.parser= (the
  existing parser, moved from the component's root), =ores.orgmode.api= (the
  load, index, search and read-page messages), =ores.orgmode.core= (the raw and
  indexed tables, the indexing step, the search query) and
  =ores.orgmode.service= (the NATS service). The domain inside them is
  "knowledge", since search is one of its four jobs.

* Out of scope

- Anything an LLM does with the pages; see [[id:70A4D585-8A56-475D-BE32-803D08A5D0CF][Answer from the knowledge base over MCP]].
- Embeddings and meaning-based search, unless the measured search misses too
  often.
- Typesense or any other search server.
- Editing pages in ORE, and pages a tenant writes.
