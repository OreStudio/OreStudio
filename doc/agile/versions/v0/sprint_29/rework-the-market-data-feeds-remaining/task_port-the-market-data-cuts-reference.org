* Result

A durable knowledge page defines a market data cut: doc/knowledge/domain/market_data_cuts.org.

- The page carries both senses of the word, what a cut is, the deal cut and the
  market data cut, the four kinds, the end of day process and its rate overrides,
  multiple centres, what a cut holds, profiles, one cut per book, option-expiry
  cut codes, audit and rerun, our position, a glossary and references. No
  notebook is referenced and no vendor or bank system appears.
- Every non-repository claim rests on a public source fetched and read while
  writing: EBA RTS (EU) 2016/101 Art. 3 for independent price verification and
  its accepted sources; the ECB euro reference rates for an official daily rate;
  Fedwire operating hours for a centre's stated business day.
- Claims with no verifiable public source are marked as our working convention
  and listed under What the sources establish: the deal-cut then market-data-cut
  order, the flash and start-of-day runs, the six-step end of day sequence, one
  cut per book, the after-hours rule and the specific option cut times. The
  advisory sentence about inferring a cut from the volatility surface was
  dropped.
- Our own position is verified against the notes and the schema and linked: the
  as-of read and its lack of an as-of parameter; the resolution-side snapshot as
  a definition with no implementation; the cut time as a designed lookup with no
  table; the population as unmodelled; the report binding as unable to name the
  population.
- Registered in the knowledge index and linked from the snapshot notes, the
  pricing configuration, the backlog story and the report-binding design, where
  the cut is now the first open item.

Checks: compass lint valid; the org link check 0 unresolved; compass site page
published the seven touched pages with no failures. Open on the page: the option
cut times, whether a book may hold more than one cut, and whether the story's cut
time and a population cut are one entity.

The consequence for the report binding: binding a population needs a cut that
does not exist yet, so the cut model is the binding's foundation.
