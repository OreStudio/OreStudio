* Goal

A series says both whose item it is and what made the value. A real source and a
simulated source can then hold the same item for one party, the ranking replaces
one with the other, and a derivation needs no publisher of its own.

* Acceptance

- =market_series= names the producer that made the value and its kind, beside the
  publisher whose item the value is.
- The producer kind is one of real, simulated or derived, validated against
  refdata as =derivation_kind= already is.
- The identity key holds a real and a simulated view of one item as two series for
  one party, and the ranking replaces one with the other. A test proves the
  replacement.
- A read of a series and the screen that shows one state the producer and its kind.
- A derived value needs no publisher of its own: the producer is =ore= and
  =derivation_kind= names the computation.

* Plan

1. *Settle the shape.* The recommendation is a nullable publisher, so the column
   can say that no party owns an item, beside a producer code and its kind. The
   producer kind joins refdata's =producer_kinds=, which holds VENDOR and
   SYNTHETIC today and no reader.
2. *Schema.* Add the columns to =market_series=, move the unique key to include
   the producer kind, and keep =providers.precedence= as the rank. The identity
   table follows the same key.
3. *Stamp at land time.* The feed states the publisher, and its session states
   whether the source generates or observes: =sessions.supplier_code=,
   =adapter_code= and =environment= already hold it for every source.
4. *Reads and screen.* One read of a series carries the four facts: item,
   publisher, producer, kind.
5. *Do not re-file anything here.* Moving the items the =derived= provider holds
   is [[id:0B9995FC-96D1-4785-AEBD-512397ECA7D5][its own task]].
6. *Prove.* A test adds a real source of an item a simulated source already holds,
   for one party, and the calibration policy picks the real one.

* Notes

- The open point is a publisher that may be absent. The key's unique index must
  use =nulls not distinct=, as the identity table already does, or two values with
  no publisher collide on nothing.
- =composite= and =demo= stay publishers. Neither makes a value of its own: one
  names a rule that blends providers' series, the other a fictional publisher for
  exercises.
- The refdata table's own doc comment says the axis is "a property of the
  producer, not of the series". This task makes that true by stamping it, and
  removes the contradiction with the task that would delete it
  ([[id:8D5C8A02-A6E3-4195-9FD1-140A0293C78B][Remove the marks of the simulator]],
  backlog).
