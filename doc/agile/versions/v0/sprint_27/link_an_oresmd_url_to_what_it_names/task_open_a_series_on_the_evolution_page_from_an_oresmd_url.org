* Goal

A link can name a series by its URL. The Evolution page opens the series in the address, so the link is a real address that opens in a new tab and copies as a link.

* Acceptance

- The Evolution page reads uri from its address, parses it through the server and fills the identity of its form from the asset, the path, the instrument, the quote type and the identity fields of the row.
- A quote URL opens the series of its datum, and a coordinate field of the quote is not part of the identity.
- A URL the codec refuses, or a fixing, shows the reason on the page and does not open a series.
