* Result

Every approval function has an Acme persona that can decide, and the demo's
market data, permissions and authorisation model are documented.

- The seed's five decide permissions are now held by roles: Operations gains
  decide_operations, and Controller and Finance roles are added for their parts.
  The tenant administrator keeps iam::roles:assign.
- Eight personas join Acme, a controller and a finance person in the group and
  in each office, so every party has a decider for its book parts.
- Verified on a recreated database: a Head of Middle Office approves an
  operational authorisation; a requester deciding his own request is refused
  with four_eyes; the controller, finance and market risk personas each approve
  their part; a trader holds no decide code.
- The documentation is a Zettelkasten cluster under doc/knowledge/demo: the
  providers and sources, the catalogue and feeds, the subscriptions and routes,
  the seed profile, the permission matrix and the approval model, with three
  UML flows and the site index updated.

Open, recorded in the approval note: a part decider reads an empty queue because
the queue filters by kind; no policy emits the controller part; no market-data
approval kind exists; the journeys do not say which party's Market Risk decides
a tenant-wide change; and a hold has no reason lookup.
