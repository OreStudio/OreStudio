Evidence: the shell prototype's matrix, driven without a browser.

Command: node doc/agile/versions/v0/sprint_26/implement-profile-journeys/evidence/navigation_check.js
Run from: the repository root, on branch feature/prototype-the-account-menu.
Environment: eager_maxwell.

The harness runs doc/prototypes/navigation/prototype.js against a minimal DOM
shim and asserts what the prototype promises a reviewer: every sign-in and state
renders; the sign-in decides the mode and the note names it; the shell is drawn
in every state but the one where a journey owns the screen; the super
administrator lands in the system mode with its tenants and its bootstrap; the
tenant administrator lands in the tenant mode with its parties and its role
catalogue; a privileged party user gets the people of the party; a regular party
user gets neither the people of the party nor the sign-in audit; the avatar
carries the journeys about the person in every sign-in, and only the party modes
offer where they work; an area opens to its cards; and a journey opens under its
mode.

Four of the promises are about absence, which is where the mode does its real
work: the super administrator is not offered a party area or the people of a
party, the tenant administrator is not offered the bootstrap or a party area,
the system mode offers no party to choose, and there is no sidebar anywhere.
