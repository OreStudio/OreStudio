* Acceptance

- The standard is a knowledge page in doc/knowledge/ui and is wired into the UX hub reading order.
- The screen review checklist carries one line per rule, naming the standard and its section.
- The standard fixes the screen words for every configuration kind and states which model names they replace.
- The standard states which rules are universal and which a kind declares, including its sections and its lifecycle.
- The standard carries a conformance table for the two configuration screens that exist, with each gap named.
- The standard states the prototype changes it requires, and the story carries those tasks.
- The standard follows ASD-STE100 and the UX Language.

* Plan

** Why

The engine run settings work of sprint 28 produced a screen shape that every
configuration coming off an ORE document can use: a named configuration, a tab
per section, documentation read from data, editing in place, a read of the reach
and the shared timeline. The pricing engine configuration predates it and does
some of those things differently. The two kinds also carry names that a person
confuses. The owner decided the screen words and the per-section lifecycle on
2026-10-24, and asked that the journeys and prototypes carry them before this
page was written.

** What this page states

- The words, and the name of each of the 19 configuration kinds.
- The family: a configuration is a named, party-owned, versioned set that a
  report binds in a slot.
- The list, the record page and the fixed tab order.
- The two section shapes: a group edited in place, a collection edited in
  dialogs.
- Reading a group and changing it, including the value in force and the default.
- The one documentation contract.
- The reach, the history and the per-section lifecycle.
- The import and the export.
- The sentence that says what a configuration decides, and the related
  configurations card.
- The states, the owning-party rule, and how to add a kind.
- The conformance of the two screens that exist today, and the prototype changes
  the page still requires.

** Where it is wired

- The UX hub reading order, after the feed standard.
- The screen review checklist, as 14 lines that name this page.
- The menu structure, where the Reporting area now names its configuration
  screens.
