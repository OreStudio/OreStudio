This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

A system verb that raises the shared heat at once by a stated amount, or by a
stated number of jumps, applied at the loop's now and recorded in the trace as
its own kind. Every feed's pace rises by the heat's share and decays at the
heat's own rate, so the shock spreads and fades by the same law a real burst
does. The Market screen offers it beside the force switch.

* Why

Gemini's review drew *+25 ticks* and *+100 ticks shock* buttons. Today a tester
who wants to see a panic either pins the market active, which holds a level
rather than a spike, or waits for a burst. A shock is the impulse response of
the market: the one input that shows what jump and decay mean.

It is cheap: the heat is one number the loop already owns, and a command that
adds to it is the same shape as the pin and the settings. It must stay inside
the stability rule, because a shock adds heat but does not change the loop
gain.
