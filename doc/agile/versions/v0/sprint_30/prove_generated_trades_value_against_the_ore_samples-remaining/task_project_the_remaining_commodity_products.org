* Acceptance

- The projection writes and reads the accumulator, TaRF, worst-of basket swap, rainbow, best entry, generic and window barrier, strike resettable, basket variance swap, Asian, digital and digital average price options, the forward volatility agreement, the position, the spread option and the intraday power forward.

- The commodity entries leave the known lists, or stay with a market data reason.
