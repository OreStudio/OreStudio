* Goal

A package states which trades it groups. Today a composite leg states that in free text: =constituent_trade_id= is a =text= column whose generator writes a noun, so every leg the demonstration holds names =CD= and no leg names a trade. The package also cannot say what shape it is: =kind= and =template_code= live on =structures= and have no home on the composite.

This task gives the composite the columns a package needs: each leg is a uuid reference to a constituent trade, with the role it plays; the composite states the deal kind and template; and a trade sits in at most one composite at a time, which is the rule =structure_member= already states. No service writes these columns yet. The completion does, in the next task.

* Notes

- The row today points at a word. =ores.trading.composite_leg.org= declares
  =constituent_trade_id= as =text= and generates it with
  =faker::word::noun()=, which is where =CD= comes from. The demonstration holds
  ten leg rows and not one of them names a trade.
- The export already reads composite legs: =trade_export_service.cpp= collects
  them for every composite instrument id it exports. A uuid reference is what
  lets that read resolve a constituent to the trade it names.
- The rule this task copies is written on
  =ores.trading.structure_member.org=: the row is keyed by the trade, so a trade
  sits in at most one structure at a time. The composite leg takes the same
  rule, with a current-only unique index on =(tenant_id, constituent_trade_id)=.
- =kind= and =template_code= are the structure's words for the deal shape and
  have no home on the composite. They move to =composite_instruments=, and the
  leg's =role= moves to the leg row, so a package states the shape it was given
  without reading its legs.
- Regenerate the component file lists after the models change:
  =projects/ores.compass/venv/bin/python projects/ores.codegen/scripts/regenerate_cmake_component_files.py=.
