* Acceptance

- A wrapper claims, runs and submits one job at a time.
- A lost notice delays a job by seconds and loses nothing.
- The wrapper no longer subscribes to the assignments stream.

* Plan

- The wrapper runs a claim loop. It asks the service for one job, runs it, and asks again at once.
- An idle wrapper waits for the =compute.v1.ops.work_available= notice. It also asks every 5 seconds with jitter (=--claim-poll-seconds=).
- A failed ask backs off from 1 second up to 30 seconds, with jitter.
- The service claim marks the result in progress, so the wrapper no longer announces the start of a job.
- The dispatcher creates the result, queues it in the work ledger and rings the notice. It no longer mints a token or publishes to the stream.
- The wrapper no longer creates or reads the assignments stream.
