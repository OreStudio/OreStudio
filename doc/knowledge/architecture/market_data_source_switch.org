#+title: A source switch changes one route and nothing else
#+description: What happens when a party moves from one source of a provider to another, and why the series, the ids and the consumers are unaffected.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :marketdata:routing:source_switch:

* Summary

A party changes its source by changing one route. The service writes an audited,
effective-dated change, sends new demand sets to the two adapters, and closes and
opens the metering intervals. The party's series, its mapping and its consumers
do not change.

** The steps

1. The party administrator writes a new route with an effective date and a
   reason.
2. The service checks the subscription, the rights and the state of the new
   source.
3. The service closes the old metering interval and opens the new one.
4. The service sends the old adapter a demand set without the party's ids and the
   new adapter a demand set with them.
5. Ticks of the new source reach the same series. Each observation records its
   source.

** What does not change

- The series key (item, provider, party).
- The mapping.
- Every consumer.

** What a reader sees

The series page shows the source of each value. The history of the route shows
who changed it, when and why. Prices from the two sources can differ at the
switch. That is a market fact.
