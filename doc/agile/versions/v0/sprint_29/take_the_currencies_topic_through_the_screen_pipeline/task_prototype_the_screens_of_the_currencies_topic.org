* Notes

From the journeys' judgement, for this task:

- The pair's create dialog is the shared record dialog with two groups, the
  pair and its convention, and a derived pair code; the two writes behind it
  are the server's gap, [[id:56C6A5CA-1A0D-435A-9AC5-1263CBB29485][Write a currency pair and its convention together, and check the pair code]], and the dialog must say what a failed second write leaves.
- The currency's Day basis and ORE currency type are free text today; the
  prototype decides their kinds. The numbers are text inputs.
- The currencies list shows codes where the page shows names; the prototype
  draws the names.
- Export for ORE is an own action of the currencies list, drawn once the
  server returns the document; there is no import on this screen.
