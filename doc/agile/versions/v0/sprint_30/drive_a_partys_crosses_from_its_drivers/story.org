* Goal

A party chooses which of its pairs are driven, by which drivers and through which relationship, and its driven surfaces are calibrated from its driver surfaces rather than from the cross's own quotes, so a firm can manage fewer surfaces and keep its crosses consistent with them.

* Acceptance

- A party's driven pairs, their drivers and their relationship (correlation or basis) are the party's own configuration, copied with its blueprints.
- A correlation-driven surface is implied from its two driver surfaces and a stated correlation source; a basis-driven one from its driver plus a spread.
- A driven pair's quotes, if a vendor sends any, are not what its surface is calibrated from, and the publish says which drivers it came from.
