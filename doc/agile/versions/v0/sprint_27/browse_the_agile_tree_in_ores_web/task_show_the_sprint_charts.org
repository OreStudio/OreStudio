* Result

The Development area has a Charts tab. It draws four charts for a sprint, chosen by the same sprint picker as the Board: stories done in each sprint (the last eight), the burn-up of tasks against the scope, tasks done on each day of the sprint, and tasks by state. The BFF serves the series at =GET /api/agile/charts=. The burn-up and the day bars read the End column of each story's =* Tasks= table, as =ores.org-js= did. A day bar covers every day of the sprint from its start and end dates, where =ores.org-js= drew seven days. The charts are SVG with no library.

Two differences from =ores.org-js= are accepted. The stacked timeline bars are not ported: they read HTML snapshots that exist only for sprints 20 and 21, and the writer for them is a pending capture in the backlog (standardize the timeline bucket writer). The burn-up of the current sprint also shows a few early dates, because some story tasks tables carry old end dates.

I ran the series against the real doc tree: 27 sprints of velocity, a burn-up that reaches 423 done tasks against a scope of 573. The French and Portuguese text is my translation and needs a speaker. I did not view the tab in a browser.
