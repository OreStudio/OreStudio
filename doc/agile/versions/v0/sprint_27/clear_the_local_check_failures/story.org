* Decisions

- The shell drift is fixed by committing what the recipes tangle to, not by
  editing the scripts or the recipes. The scripts are generated, and the
  recipes are the source: the drifted ones were last regenerated before the
  recipes gained their arguments.
- The missing =account_credentials= scripts are the same defect on the other
  side. =git diff --exit-code= cannot see a file that was never added, so only
  the untracked-files step of =shell-script-drift.yml= catches them; it was
  added after the recipe landed, which is why main stayed red without a signal.
- The plugin versions are bumped to the product version rather than the product
  version being questioned. The plugin ships with the product and is upgraded
  with it, and =check-manifest.mjs= reads the version from =CMakeLists.txt=.

* Result

The shell and HTTP tangles are clean, both recipe inventories are current,
=component-drift= passes, and both DSH plugins carry 0.0.27 and pass their
manifest checks. Two failures remain and neither is this change's: =lint= and
=site-page= both name a dangling id link in
=projects/ores.iam/modeling/ores.iam.account_credential.org=, which PR #2730
introduced. The model's prose is emitted into generated SQL and C++, so
correcting it is its own change.

The story closed with one check left red. pgtap failed on ten of its
thirty-two suites, six of which ran no test at all, and the cause was the
trading model rather than the suites: no trading entity has been immutable
since the model became versioned, and the trade's children stopped copying the
party, so twenty-one fixtures named no audit column and thirteen named a column
that no longer existed. Every suite passes now, and the cases that proved the
removed guarantees are rewritten to the versioned behaviour.

One of them could not be. Nothing refuses a report into another party's
portfolio any more: the check and its message are gone from the generated SQL,
so the guarantee is missing rather than the test stale. That and the unguarded
version rewrite are filed on their own story. The same sweep found the ER
diagram stale after the rates-family header, and it is regenerated here.
