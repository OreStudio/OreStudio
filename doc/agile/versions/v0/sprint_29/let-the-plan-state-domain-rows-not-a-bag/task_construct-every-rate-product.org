* Acceptance

- One constructor per rate product the planner draws, with the trade type fixed by it.
- The terms hold only the facts each product states, and the closed vocabularies are refused at construction.
- The writer appends the constructed rows and assembles no rate product.
- A provisioning run books the same eight rate products with the same row counts, and the suites are green.

* Plan

A rates product is a header, two legs and one fact row, and the header states
no trade type: every code routes to one table, so the fact row is the
product's own statement. =rate_product= therefore carries a =std::variant= of
the eight fact rows, and each constructor produces exactly one alternative, so
the legs and the fact cannot disagree about what the trade is.

The writer keeps the choice of constructor, because something has to read the
recorded trade type, and loses the leg building, the per-product branches and
the field-by-field copy.

* Result

Every rate product the planner draws is constructed by a function that names
it. The writer no longer builds legs or branches per product: it fills the
terms, calls the constructor, stamps the audit columns and appends what it is
given.

Proved by a provisioning run on a recreated database, against a baseline taken
from the previous build: the population is identical on every count -- 24
headers, 48 legs, 48 amounts, 48 rates, 3 strikes, 6 vanilla swap facts, 3
FRAs, 3 caps and 3 swaptions, over the same eight products at three trades
each.

The suites are green: =ores.trading.api.tests= at 518 assertions in 138 test
cases with eight new rate cases, and =ores.synthetic.core.tests= at 1741
assertions in 79.

* What the constructors now refuse that nothing did before

- a leg without a currency, a day count, a business day convention or a
  payment frequency;
- a floating leg without an index;
- a rate product whose legs state no positive notional;
- a cross currency swap whose legs state the same currency;
- a cap or floor with no strike, which previously booked and paid nothing;
- a knock out swap whose barrier type is outside ORE's closed set;
- a swaption without an expiry, or with an exercise or settlement type the
  table refuses.
