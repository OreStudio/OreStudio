* Acceptance

- ores_trading_swap_leg_amounts_tbl and ores_trading_swap_leg_rates_tbl exist, keyed by the trade, the leg ordinal and an entry ordinal.
- swap_leg no longer carries notional, fixed_rate or spread; the children carry them.
- map_leg reads every notional and every rate or spread, not the first of each.
- The import writes the children and the export reads them back, so a stepped leg round trips.
- The component regenerates drift-clean and builds, and the codegen suite passes.
