* Acceptance

- The projection writes and reads the worst-of basket swap, rainbow option, best entry option and basket variance swap.
- Each of them is built by its builder, and the planner calls it.
- The carrier holds the periods a worst-of basket swap observes.
- Their entries leave the known lists, or stay with a market data reason.

* Plan

ORE's schema declares a =Commodity<Type>Data= element for all four types,
and the engine reads each from =<TradeType>Data=.

1. Rename the =equity_swap_period= entity to =basket_swap_period=, through
   codegen, and move the equity carrier, projection and builder onto it.
   Nothing in a period is equity's own, and ORE states the same worst-of
   swap for FX and commodities, so the three families share one table.
   Decided with the user.
2. Give the commodity carrier the periods, in the shared rows.
3. Add the four types to the projection's table of products. The rainbow
   option and the basket variance swap state their basket through the
   commodity basket constituents. The best entry option states one
   underlying. The worst-of basket swap states its basket, its initial
   prices and its periods, as the equity one does.
4. Add a builder for each type, register it, and call it from the planner.
5. Record the generated trades, run the ORE engine test, and update the
   known lists.
