* Goal

Hold the collateral terms of a netting set as their own entity, so a CSA can be added, changed or switched off without touching the set's trades.

* Acceptance

- csa and csa_eligible_currency are generated in ores.refdata across every layer the codegen covers.
- A CSA carries every field of ORE's CSA details.
- A netting set has at most one active CSA, enforced by the database.

* Plan

- Survey ORE's examples first: 99 files hold 191 netting sets; 189 carry
  CSA details and 83 of those are switched off. A CSA therefore exists on
  its own, and the active flag is the CSA's, not the set's.
- =csa=: the netting set (required), the active flag, and every field of
  ORE's =CSADetails= as a nullable column, so a round trip keeps what ORE
  leaves out. Checks: the two Bilateral-type columns against ORE's three
  values, the independent amount type against FIXED, thresholds and
  minimum transfer amounts not negative.
- =csa_eligible_currency=: one row per eligible currency, with its
  position in ORE's list so an export keeps the order.
- One active CSA per set: a unique index on the set over current, active
  rows.
- Wire by hand as for the netting entities, including tenant row-level
  security; neither table has a legal entity column, so no party policy.

* Result

- =csa= and =csa_eligible_currency= are generated in =ores.refdata= across
  every layer, and wired by hand into the SQL lists, permission seeds,
  core registrar, shell menu, CMake lists and tenant row-level security.
- =csa= carries the netting set, the active flag and every field of ORE's
  =CSADetails= as a nullable column. Checks hold the Bilateral-type
  columns to ORE's three values, the independent amount type to FIXED, and
  thresholds and minimum transfer amounts to zero and above.
- A unique index on the set over current, active rows allows at most one
  active CSA per set.
- A second unique index keeps each position of a CSA's currency list
  once; =test_csa_rules.py= guards both indexes against the models.
- In Postgres, in a transaction that rolled back: a second active CSA on a
  set was refused (23505, =csas_active_set_idx=); an inactive CSA beside it
  was accepted; an unknown Bilateral value and a negative threshold were
  refused (23514); a currency listed twice for one CSA was refused (23505).
  After review: a second currency at a taken position was refused (23505,
  =csa_eligible_currencies_csa_position_idx=); at the next position it was
  accepted.
- Build: linux-clang-debug-make clean after making the eligible currency
  generator use its counter for =position=, which the warning gate caught.
  ctest: 89 of 89 binaries passed on a recreated database, including the
  two generated CSA eventing integration tests.
