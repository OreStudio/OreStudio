* Summary

The market watch prototype argues that the market's exceptions are cleared where
they are seen, so the quotes and the fallbacks belong on one screen. It draws the
trader's watch screen three ways — beside the quotes, below them, and queue-first
— against three mock markets.

It is throwaway. It is not production code, it reads no service, and every value
on it is mock data. It exists because the
[[id:6ACE3F41-4ECD-4AE8-AC96-F557845193E0][journey]] settles what watching and
clearing do — the scope, the three kinds of failure, the fallback — and records
that no exception entity, no exception subject and no resolution subject exist
yet. It opens on variant *a* and market =clean=.

* Detail

** The question

The journey makes the watch and the clearing one act: a separate list and a
separate decision form would split one act across two pages. The raw points are
the evidence — time, oresmd URI, value, source and age — and the exceptions are
the points that fail, in three kinds at once: missing, stale and crossed. The
journey records the defects the screen is written against: an observation carries
no quality or rejection, no subject lists or resolves an exception, the scope is
not typed, and a fallback has no subject. The prototype asks how quotes, failure
and fallback sit together when none is stored.

** The variants

Switch with the *Exception list* buttons in the bottom bar, or =?variant=. The
variant is where the exception list sits, which is the screen's central design
question.

| Variant | Name              | The structural argument |
|---------+-------------------+-------------------------|
| =a=     | beside the quotes | The raw points and the exceptions share the width in two columns, with the session's resolutions across the foot. The exceptions stand where the offending values are. |
| =b=     | below the quotes  | One column: the scope, the raw points, the exceptions, the resolutions. The reading order the journey's wireframe draws, and the one that pushes the fallbacks below the fold. |
| =c=     | queue-first       | The exceptions and their fallbacks come first, the resolutions next, and the raw points sit behind a caption at the foot. The clearing leads and the evidence supports it. |

The variants disagree about structure, not colour: *A* gives the quotes and the
exceptions equal standing, *B* keeps the journey's order, and *C* leads with the
failures.

** The states

Switch with the *Market* buttons in the bottom bar, or =?state=. The state is the
mock market the page reads.

| State     | What it shows |
|-----------+---------------|
| =clean=   | Eight points, all fresh and present. The exceptions panel says *clean* and that every point in the scope is fresh, present and consistent with its neighbours. |
| =crossed= | A missing =2Y= point, a crossed =10Y= value at 4.612 above the =20Y= point at 4.022, and a stale =30Y= point six hours old from =vendor.x.feed=. All three kinds at once. |
| =stale=   | Two stale points, =1Y= at 1h 12m and =10Y= at 48m, against the threshold of 15m. |

The =crossed= state is the argument in one screen: all three kinds the journey
names, each with its detail in words. The =clean= state is the counter-case: with
nothing to clear, the screen says so rather than leaving a blank.

** Driving it

The scope, the raw points and the exceptions are live to read. A *Refresh* button
reports that it refreshed at 11:30:04 with the same scope and nothing written.
Every exception offers the three fallbacks — *carry forward*, *proxy*,
*escalate* — and choosing one records it in *Resolutions this session*, with the
actor and the reason the page holds for each. Any combination of variant and
market is a URL, because every button rewrites the query string. Each oresmd URI
opens the oresmd URL editor in an overlay. No other click writes.

** What is fake

Everything. The feed binding =synthetic.v1.ir_curve=, the eight points and their
times, values, sources and ages; the three kinds and their details; the three
fallbacks, their actors and their reasons; the threshold of 15m; and the 84-row
count are constants in the page. There is no server, no session and no build
step, and nothing is stored: a resolution lives on the page for the session only.

** Where the code lives

=doc/prototypes/market_data/watch-the-market-and-clear-its-exceptions/=. One
file, =index.html=, holding its markup, styles and script, with no framework and
no build step. The site copies the directory as it stands and adds nothing to it.
The URI button opens =../oresmd-url-editor/index.html= in an overlay, so the page
borrows the editor prototype beside it and embeds no copy of its own.

** The verdict

The group accepted the argument rather than a layout: the market's exceptions are
cleared where they are seen, so the quotes and the fallbacks belong on one screen,
and watching and clearing read as one act. The screen carries the scope, the raw
points with their source and age, and the exceptions with the fallback a person
chooses, offered on the exception itself rather than on a form of its own.

What stayed on the page as the record: the three variants and the three markets,
kept because the argument holds whether the list is beside, below or before the
quotes, and in a clean market as well as a broken one; the session-only
resolutions panel, kept as the record that a choice has no subject and no fields;
and the oresmd URL editor overlay.

** What it does not settle

Which variant wins. Beside, below and queue-first are all drawn and none is
chosen; the choice belongs to the operations the server will carry.

The exception. No subject raises, lists or resolves an exception, and no entity
holds one, so the three kinds are computed in the page and shown read-only.

Where a resolution is recorded. A fallback has no subject and no fields, so it
lives on the page for the session. A carried-forward value can be written through
=market_observations.put=, but that write carries no actor, no reason and no link
to the exception, so a cleared point reads as a market point.

The thresholds and the scope. Nothing stores the age at which a point is stale or
what counts as crossed, so the page holds one threshold of 15m for every scope,
and nothing names a set of quotes the server resolves.

The quality mark. An observation carries no quality and no rejection, so the
screen marks what it can compute from the values and the ages.
