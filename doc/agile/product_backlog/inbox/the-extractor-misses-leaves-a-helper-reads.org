This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Teach =extract_curve_terms.py= to follow a helper call. Every segment kind reads
=PillarChoice=, =Priority= and =MinDistance= through =common_settings(s, g)= and
not through an assignment the extractor sees, so the ORE parameter contract holds
no row for any of the three, for any of the twelve kinds.

* Why

The curve screen reads a segment's leaves from the projection's dispatch, so it
draws all five leaves a simple segment reads. Three of them have no contract row,
which the walk on 2026-10-30 shows as a dash and as *3 without documentation* on
the entry, and the page reads *238 of 289 parameters carry documentation* where it
read *238 of 238*.

The root cause is the one [[id:3D1208F2-1D51-4733-81FB-01B19D36E8EE][Record the
leaves a segment inherits]] fixed for a class's base: the extractor reads the
assignments of an importer and nothing that an importer calls. That task added
=Conventions= for every kind and nothing else.
