* Acceptance

- Each catalogued pair is quoted by the vendor contributors, with their noise profiles, and by one clean contributor no tenant subscribes to.
- A party subscribed to a vendor receives its volatility quotes over the routes it receives its rates on.

* Plan

*What is there.* The surface feed publishes raw market ticks under =source_name.contributor=,
addressed by oresmd URI: an older path. The spot and curve feeds instead run through the
simulator adapter: an adapter of each provider is offered the generators whose vendor ids its
notation writes, and starts one when the market data service demands one of those ids. Three
pieces are missing for a surface to take that path:

1. *A vendor id for an FX option quote.* =quote_description= is =fx_spot_quote= or
   =ir_curve_pillar=; no provider's notation writes or parses an id for a volatility point.
2. *Offers for a surface.* =system_generator_offers= offers spot and curve generators only.
3. *One latent path for every contributor.* Each adapter has its own generator runner, so a
   surface offered to the bloomberg, ice and tradeweb adapters would be three generators on
   three latent paths, and the vendors would quote three different markets. The contributors
   must observe one surface.

*Plan, in slices.*

1. Add =fx_option_quote= (pair, expiry, strike label) to =quote_description=, and teach each
   provider's notation to write and read it; the catalogue gains each contributor's vendor
   instruments, mapped to the fx_option items the blueprints read.
2. Hold one surface generator per feed in the fleet, shared by the adapters: the first adapter
   whose ids are demanded starts it, each adapter publishes only its own contributor's points
   through its own targets, and the surface stops when no adapter demands it.
3. Offer the surface's points to each contributor's adapter, and add the vendors the curves use
   (ice, tradeweb) and a clean contributor no tenant subscribes to, in the dataset.
4. Prove a subscribed party receives its vendors' volatility quotes on the routes it receives
   its rates on.
