The installation's expected services, one row per enabled registry entry:
the replica count, what the service does, and the IAM service account it
signs in as. Output
=projects/ores.sql/populate/telemetry/telemetry_expected_services_populate.sql=.

The services roster reads these rows, so a service the registry expects keeps
its row on the services screen whether or not it reports. A disabled entry is
not expected and has no row.

See the [[id:C4E8A91F-3B7D-4F2E-85A6-1D9C7E3B2F4A][Template variable reference]] for the complete list of available
variables and their semantics.
