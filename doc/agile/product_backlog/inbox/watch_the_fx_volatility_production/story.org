* Goal

A person can watch one pair's volatility being produced and see why it looks the
way it does. The page reads the chain in dependency order: the spot and the curves
the generator steps, the latent surface it holds, each vendor's view of that
surface, and the surface a party calibrated, with the error between them. Each
node states its own state, so a surface with no quotes says which input it lacks.

The chain is the debugger. It is not a quotes board and not a feed page: the
internal values have no party to read them, and no existing screen joins the
producer side to the consumption side.

* Acceptance

- A pair and an as-of answer the whole chain in one read, from the inputs to the
  calibrated surface.
- The page renders the latent surface, each vendor's view and the calibrated
  surface side by side, with the difference between them, and the quote ladders
  beside them.
- A broken chain names the node that failed and the input it lacked, and the
  node's own message log is reachable from it.
- The internal series of the latent surface and of the derived quotes are
  readable under the system tenant, and no non-system party can read them.
- A person can step the latent surface through a day and watch the views follow.

* Decisions

- *The internal values are stored and unentitled, not absent.* A latent surface and
  the derived quotes keep a series so their evolution can be watched; no
  non-system party may read them, and they are never metered.
- *The derived quotes serve one role.* They drive the generation of the simulated
  surface. They are not a quote source for a party.
- *The platform does not manufacture a surface for a party.* A party with no
  system-level quotes for its pairs authors its own surface, calibrates it and
  publishes it.
- *A separate card is not warranted yet.* The page sits under the simulator area
  until it covers more than FX volatility.
- *The graph is read as heatmaps first.* Expiry by delta for each surface, with the
  difference panel beside them; a 3D surface is a secondary view.
- *The producer of the platform's own arithmetic is =orestudio=.* It is a producer,
  not a publisher, and it replaces the mechanism-named =derived=.
