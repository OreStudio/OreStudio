#+title: The fixing sources ORE Studio supports, and the cut each one serves
#+description: The standard FX, NDF and interest rate fixing sources, the option cut each serves, and whether its values are observed or simulated.
#+type: knowledge
#+audience: user
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:fixing:cut:reference_data:
#+created: 2026-11-22
#+updated: 2026-11-22

* Summary

An option can expire at a cut only if a fixing source fixes the market at that
cut: a cash-settled or fixing-determined option needs an observation at its
expiry instant, and an option whose holder decides needs the spot at the cut on
record. ORE Studio therefore supports one fixing source for each cut it offers,
plus the sources its other products name: the FX reference rates of central
banks, the settlement rate options of non-deliverable currencies, and the
interest rate indices its curves project. Each source is either /observed/,
fetched from a public publisher, or /simulated/, fixed by the simulator from its
own market because no free publication exists.

* Detail

** The rule: a cut needs a source

A cut code names the instant an option expires; see
[[id:3B39B2E2-6E05-4DF8-B68B-6ACA12F2FCE9][The option-expiry cut code]]. The instant is useful only if a
source records the market at it. A cash-settled option settles on that
observation, a fixing-determined option exercises from it, and a holder who
decides late needs the spot at the cut that was set aside; see
[[id:970D334D-2576-41BF-8664-1AA4CB76D31B][An option near the money at its cut makes expiry a matter of judgement]].
So each supported cut is paired with one fixing source whose time and zone are
the cut's. A cut with no source cannot be offered.

** FX: the cuts and their sources

The two most common FX option cuts are 10:00 New York and 15:00 Tokyo; regional
cuts are also used, among them 11:00 Warsaw, 12:00 Budapest, 12:00 London and
12:30 New York. LSEG's WM/R service publishes closing spot rates at 16:00
London, the dominant FX benchmark, and intraday spot rates on the hour, with
half-hourly rates for some currencies, so a WM/R rate exists at the instant of
every cut on the hour. ORE Studio uses WM/R as the family of its cut sources.

| Cut | Instant | Source | Administrator | Label | Producer |
|-----+---------+--------+---------------+-------+----------|
| New York 10:00 | 10:00 America/New_York | WM/R intraday spot | LSEG | =WMR1000NYC= | simulated |
| Tokyo 15:00 | 15:00 Asia/Tokyo | WM/R intraday spot | LSEG | =WMR1500TKY= | simulated |
| London 16:00 | 16:00 Europe/London | WM/R closing spot | LSEG | =WMR1600LON= | simulated |
| London 12:00 | 12:00 Europe/London | WM/R intraday spot | LSEG | =WMR1200LON= | simulated |
| Warsaw 11:00 | 11:00 Europe/Warsaw | WM/R intraday spot | LSEG | =WMR1100WAR= | simulated |
| Budapest 12:00 | 12:00 Europe/Budapest | WM/R intraday spot | LSEG | =WMR1200BUD= | simulated |
| New York 12:30 | 12:30 America/New_York | WM/R half-hourly spot | LSEG | =WMR1230NYC= | simulated |

The label is the =SOURCE= of the ORE index =FX-SOURCE-CCY1-CCY2=, so the FX
fixing of EUR/USD at the New York cut is =FX-WMR1000NYC-EUR-USD=. WM/R is
licensed, so its rates are simulated. A pair the cut source does not fix
directly is derived through USD; see
[[id:6A625509-CD9A-4D13-8E16-37B75A4AED83][A derived fixing source composes the fixings of its parent sources]].

Four central-bank reference rates are observed, and serve FX trades that name
them rather than an option cut: the ECB euro reference rates at about 14:15
CET, and the reference rates of the Bank of Canada, the IMF and the Central Bank
of the Republic of Türkiye. Their labels are =ECB=, =BOC=, =IMF= and =TCMB=. See
[[id:F4BBEF34-D84C-4AE6-A3AA-DC47C3EE06EE][Public publishers publish daily fixings through a supplier session]].

=TR20H= is the label ORE's example data uses for FX fixings. ORE does not
document which source it stands for, so ORE Studio keeps it only for imported
ORE examples; generated trades name a source from the table above.

** Non-deliverable currencies

A non-deliverable forward or option settles on its currency's settlement rate
option, as defined in Annex A to the 1998 FX and Currency Option Definitions.
The source's own publication time is the cut of a non-deliverable option on the
currency.

| Currency | Settlement rate option | Producer |
|----------+------------------------+----------|
| KRW | =KFTC18= | simulated |
| TWD | =TAIFX1= | simulated |
| CNY | =SAEC= | simulated |
| INR | the FBIL reference rate, formerly =RBIB= | simulated |
| PHP | =PDSPESO= | simulated |
| IDR | =ABSIRFIX01=, now the JISDOR rate | simulated |
| BRL | =PTAX= (=BRL09=) | simulated |

The publication times are those Annex A states; they are recorded on each
source, not restated here.

** Interest rate indices

| Family | Examples | Administrator | Producer |
|--------+----------+---------------+----------|
| Overnight rates with a public adapter | SOFR, SONIA, €STR, TONA, CORRA, AONIA, SORA, SWESTR, NOWA, CZEONIA, CDI, CAMARA, TIIE | the central bank | observed |
| Overnight rates with no public adapter | SARON, HONIA, NZONIA, POLONIA, ZARONIA and the others a synthetic curve projects | the central bank or its agent | simulated |
| Term rates | EURIBOR, HIBOR, TIBOR, SHIBOR and the other term indices a synthetic curve projects | EMMI, the Treasury Markets Association and the others | simulated |
| Legacy term rates | USD, GBP, CHF and JPY LIBOR | ICE Benchmark Administration; ceased | simulated, for teaching |

The two families, term and overnight, are described in
[[id:5170B17B-188B-45CA-A1B6-1F27A97E1CF8][Interest Rate Benchmark Types: IBOR vs. RFR]]; legacy sources in
[[id:6E566C97-F2A4-4AE8-B509-BDE23568A0B8][A fixing source is identified by its rate source definition]].

** One name per index

Each index has one name. Three indices are currently written under two: the
simulated writer names the EUR, JPY and MXN overnight rates =ESTR=, =TONA= and
=TIIE=, while the public publishers map them as =ESTER=, =TONAR= and =FTIIE=.
The two halves land in different series, so a public value cannot stand over a
simulated one. The catalogue of sources must fix one name per index.

** How the values are produced

An observed source is fetched by the public sources adapters and is never
simulated. A simulated source is fixed by the simulator, through the same
adapter contract; see
[[id:F24FA489-790A-4065-B8E1-DCC141595356][A simulated fixing source is a publisher the simulator serves]].
