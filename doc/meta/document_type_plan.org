This page defines the =plan= document type — one entry in the
[[id:8B29A8D8-E004-4E87-B81D-ABEAF68F2B98][document types]] taxonomy. The general contract every document follows
(frontmatter, state, linking, tags) lives on the taxonomy page; this
page carries only what is specific to =plan=.

* Contract

- Implements :: loading. See
  [[id:F5CB5A45-6557-410B-9E4F-542957561A16][Regulatory Functions]].
- Lives /inside/ a task or story document as the =* Plan= section.
  Not a separate file. (Previously =plan.org= as a sibling file;
  that was retired when tasks became flat files.)
- Level :: =s1= (task plan) or =s2= (story plan).
- Transient :: cleared or summarised in =* Notes= when the task or
  story closes; distilled content moves to =* Decisions= on the
  parent story. Plans do not outlive their task.

* Not to be confused with =design=

A plan is /how we will do this work/. A [[id:7ADECB4C-3C03-4B8D-BB2F-A9753EFDA866][design]] is /what the target
state is, and why/ — the argument that produced the tasks, rather than
the strategy for executing one of them.

When a story-scoped argument spans many tasks, precedes them, and will
be distilled into a durable document, it outgrows a =* Plan= section and
becomes a =design.org= in the story folder. Everything else stays a
section, as above. A design that produces no durable outcome was a plan
all along.
