One row per address an incomplete trade named and the market did not answer,
written with the run.

A trade's readiness row states that it is incomplete and how many addresses it
lost. This table states which ones, so a reader of one trade sees its gaps
without asking another component what the trade names.

The cause is not repeated here. It belongs to the address, and the address
grain states it once for every trade that needed it: the two rows name one run
and one address, so a reader that wants the cause joins them. Writing the cause
twice would let the copies disagree, and a party acting on one of them would
buy the wrong thing.
