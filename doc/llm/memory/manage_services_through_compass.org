#+title: Manage services through compass, never systemctl
#+description: Start, stop, restart and inspect the fleet or one service with compass services, not systemctl or journalctl directly.
#+type: memory
#+memory_subtype: feedback
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :compass:services:feedback:memory:
#+created: 2026-10-05
#+updated: 2026-10-05

Every service lifecycle action goes through compass: ./compass.sh services start, stop, restart, status and logs. Restarting one service is ./compass.sh services restart followed by its short name, such as web. Never call systemctl --user or journalctl directly for this environment's units.

*Why:* I restarted ores.web with systemctl --user restart after rebuilding the web bundle; the user said services must be managed through compass. Compass regenerates and deploys the units before it acts, applies the .env log level, and waits for readiness, which a bare systemctl call skips.

*How to apply:* After rebuilding ores.web or any service, run ./compass.sh services restart with the service's short name; check the fleet with ./compass.sh services status; read a unit's output with ./compass.sh services logs and the service name.
