* Acceptance

- A basket or rainbow slot draws only from pairs with a second pair in their quote currency, still preferring the book's own currency.
- A fresh Acme setup's generate_trades step completes without warnings.

* Plan

- *The cause.* The FX planner draws a slot's pair from those that name the book's currency.
  The UK book's currency is GBP, and EUR/GBP is the only pair quoted in it, so a basket or
  rainbow slot that drew EUR/GBP found no second pair to pay in and refused the population.
  The basket and rainbow came to main in the window and generic barrier PRs, after the last
  provisioning run on this branch's base.
- *The fix.* A basket or rainbow slot draws from the pairs that have a partner in their quote
  currency, still preferring the book's own; a tenant with none at all still gets the refusal.
- *What the yellow run exposed.* The step threw on the first entry it could not write, so the
  US and HK books, which come after the UK's FX book, got no trades at all: 487 trades went
  unwritten for one bad slot. Each entry now fails on its own: the step names it, goes on to
  the next, and ends with warnings, keeping the trades it wrote.

* Result

A basket or rainbow slot draws from the pairs that have a partner in their quote currency, so
the UK book books its baskets; a regression test reproduces the provisioning refusal without the
fix and passes with it. The trade step writes each population on its own and ends with warnings
naming the ones it could not write, keeping the rest: with three bonds books renamed on purpose,
a re-run named those three and wrote the other thirteen populations, and with the books restored
a re-run completed clean. The first fresh run had lost 487 trades to one bad slot; a re-run wrote
them.
