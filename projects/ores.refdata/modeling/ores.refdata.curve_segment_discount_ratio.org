#+created: 2026-11-02
#+updated: 2026-11-02

The three curves a discount ratio segment is built from and the currency of
each, as ORE's =BaseCurve=, =NumeratorCurve= and =DenominatorCurve=. They hold
one invariant: the segment is the base curve times the ratio of the other two.
ORE requires all three curves and their currencies, so every discount ratio
segment has this part.

A segment holds at most one row of this part, so the generated table adds the
unique index on the live rows of =(tenant_id, curve_segment_id)=.
