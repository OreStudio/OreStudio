This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

=projects/ores.codegen/scripts/check_shell_recipes.py= finds =ores.shell=
under =build/output/linux-clang-debug-make/publish/bin=. It should take the
preset from =ORES_PRESET= in =.env=, as =compass= does, or accept a
=--preset= option.

* Why

A worktree that builds =linux-clang-release-ninja= has no debug binary, so the
check stops with "shell binary not found" and replays no recipe. The V04
evidence of the cross currency basis split was taken by loading the script and
replacing its =SHELL= path for one run, which a reader cannot repeat from the
script alone.
