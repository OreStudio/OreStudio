* Acceptance

- An approval request of kind =iam.role_grant= carries the ask, and the asker
  cannot decide it.
- An approval grants the role through the ordinary assignment path, exactly
  once.
- The member asks from *My access* and reads the answer there; the
  administrator answers from the Requests queue.

* Plan

The journey planned the request as a =workflow_instance= and named five IAM
request subjects. Both were replaced before anything was built: the request is
an approval request of its own with a kind, and the queue and the decision are
generic across kinds, so they belong to =ores.inbox=. The work moved to the
[[id:7CCED02B-8B95-4FF1-94F8-B67BE86C4256][Complete the ores.inbox approvals
and notifications]] story, whose tasks
[[id:F4531E16-EBDB-446A-B11A-549E2FEAB8A3][Land the role request kind]] and
[[id:6D9E6879-959D-4A78-9450-196D99593E1D][Build the request and notification
screens]] delivered it. This task is closed as covered, and the two gaps the
journey names that nothing has answered are new tasks on this story.

* Result

Covered, not built here. The request, the four-eyes guard, the grant, the two
notifications and both screens landed under the inbox story, and the evidence
for them sits beside its tasks. Its
[[id:6D9E6879-959D-4A78-9450-196D99593E1D][screens task]] records the live
walk: a member asked for three roles in turn, the queue named the role on every
row, each decision granted the role, and the member read the decision in the
bell.

Two of the journey's seven gaps were left unanswered, and they are now tasks of
their own on this story: nothing says which roles may be asked for, and a
request that nobody answers waits forever.
