* Acceptance

- A definition can select a step chain shorter than the full report execution workflow, and the chain it selects is the one that runs.
- The ACME definition triggers from the shell, the instance travels pending, running, completed through the FSM guard, and the compute results are retrieved.
- The run is reproducible from a freshly recreated database.

* Decisions

** The chain selection landed under the sibling story

PR #2289 delivered it as task 395C3931 on
[[id:AB57C960-7E15-4D02-88AE-CEA931B6F9A3][Run a full report: scheduling,
reporting and compute]], so this story's task 06147DF3 is closed as delivered
rather than re-scoped. Its premise is obsolete rather than wrong: it assumed
shortening the chain was a positional remap, and step results are now addressed
by the name of the step that produced them.

** "No preprocessing" is a code change, not a seeding choice

The chain is registered once, in code, and the trigger hard-codes its type, so
no definition can select a shorter one today. The story therefore starts with
the carrier and the second chain rather than with the fixture. Recorded in
[[id:A38F01BA-506A-49FD-B520-116F366CE97D][the investigation]].

** The reporting to compute handoff is in scope, though the code is not ours

The submit path publishes on a subject no wrapper subscribes to, leaves the
fields a wrapper needs empty, and hands over a URI in the wrong shape. Without
fixing all three the story cannot succeed, so the task is here rather than
waiting on ores.compute. It may be reassigned if that workstream prefers to own
it.

** Retrieval is in scope; analysis of the results is not

=collect_compute_results= currently completes without fetching anything. This
story makes it fetch the output and record where it landed. Parsing ORE result
data stays out.

** The story must still decide what the run executes

The reporting chain packages its own trades and market data blobs, not the
=Input/ore.xml= shape the ORE engine needs, so "run the example bundle through
the grid" and "run the reporting chain as it stands" are two different jobs.
The investigation states both; the choice is owed before the proof task starts.

* Out of scope

- Parsing or post-processing the compute results.
- Gathering trades or market data, and assembling a bundle.
