* Goal

Handed to clever dijkstra, who owns the IBOR work. The nine IBOR fixings (ESTER, SOFR, SONIA, TONAR, SORA, CORRA, NOWA, AONIA, CDI) are already complete: identity_kind index, asset_class ir, series_subclass index_fixing, ccy filled, unit_ccy correctly empty. What is missing is the statement: declare that an IBOR fixing requires ccy and its index and forbids unit_ccy, that an ir_swap or discount series requires ccy, instrument_type and quote_type and forbids unit_ccy, and collapse the two identical index_fixing classification rules to one.
