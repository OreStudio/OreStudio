* Goal

The codegen layer has no defect left that the component clean-up passes found.
Every defect is recorded with the evidence that shows it, and every fix lands
at its source with a regression test.

* Acceptance

- Every codegen defect the clean-up passes surface is recorded with the command or recipe that shows it, and with the file and line of its cause.
- Every fix is at the source, in a template, a loader or a script, and never in the generated output.
- Every fix carries a regression test that fails before the fix and passes after it.
- The tree is regenerated, and the component drift gate passes with each affected component listed.

* Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---+----------+-----|
| 1 | Every fix lands on one long-lived branch, =feature/codegen-improvements=. | The defects are found by every component's clean-up pass, so they accumulate across the whole programme. Merging them one at a time would regenerate the same components repeatedly, and the regeneration is exactly what makes the next pass's diff noisy. One branch merges once, when the components are clean. |
| 2 | The task is the register. | A defect that is fixed without being recorded cannot be told from one that was never found, and the record is what the next pass reads. |

* Out of scope

- Merging the branch. It merges when every component is clean, not when a defect is fixed.
- The defects already captured in the product backlog. They stay where they are until this story pulls one in as work.
