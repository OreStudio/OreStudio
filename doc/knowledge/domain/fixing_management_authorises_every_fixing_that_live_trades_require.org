* Summary

Fixing management is the operational view of the fixing process. One view
lists every historic and future fixing that live trades require, with the
observed value, its origin and its status, and lets operations authorise,
override or reverse it. A set of control reports finds what the process
missed: trades that should have fixed and did not, trades with no source, and
generated trades whose dates or amounts do not agree with their fixing.

* Detail

** Roles

Front office may key a value until it is authorised, and may not change it
afterwards. Only operations authorise, and only after the source's publication
time. Operations can authorise a published value or a keyed one, key over a
published value and authorise their own, and withdraw an authorisation and
authorise again. Each of these is a recorded act.

** What the view shows

| Field | Meaning |
|-------+---------|
| Source | The fixing source, and whether it is published, keyed or derived; a derived source shows its parents |
| Fixing date and time | In the source's time zone, and in the user's |
| Asset | A currency for a rate, a pair for FX |
| Value | The value to authorise |
| Published value | The source's own value, kept even when a different value is keyed |
| Previous value | The last fixing of the same source |
| Reference rate | The projection for a rate, the current spot for FX, the forward for an FX forward source |
| Origin | Published, keyed by operations, keyed by front office, keyed in a trade, or defaulted from spot or from the valuation |
| Status | Official or unofficial |
| Audit | Last change, by whom, and version |
| Trades | The trades this observation fixes |

The view filters by source, time, date range, origin and status, and by
whether the fixings have been applied.

** Control reports

| Report | Purpose |
|--------+---------|
| Missing fixings | Fixings that were due and were not done |
| Unfixed trades | Trades that are about to fix, or should have fixed and did not |
| Fixing audit | What was keyed, authorised and changed, by whom and when |
| Non-deliverable forward warnings | Holiday conflicts, fixing dates in the past with no fixing, and trades with no source |
| Non-deliverable forward fixings by pair | The forwards fixed today and their cash balance, which should be flat |
| Exceptions | Fixing-determined trades without a fixing |
| Trigger checks | Discrete barrier observations checked against the stored fixings |
| Post-cut checks | After each cut, the expected settlement and fixing trades exist with the right dates |
| Control explains | Pairs that should net to zero, such as a forward and its fixing trade, or a projected and an observed coupon |

The cut referred to in the post-cut check is the cut of
[[id:B5214F3A-A72E-421E-82BC-79338B2E95CB][Cuts]]. The fixing activities, fix and unfix, are activity codes
of their own, so a report can tell an automated fix from a manual one.

** A trade's own fixing view

A swap's trade view lists, per leg, the fixing source, time and zone, and an
FX fixing source and time for a non-deliverable leg. A fixing grid per leg
shows each period's fixing date, accrual dates, rate, FX date and rate,
notional, coupon and present value, with totals. When a non-deliverable coupon
is converted, the original stays visible and is marked as replaced.
