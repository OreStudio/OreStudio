* Result

** Outcome

The Connectivity screen draws the adapter catalogue read-only: code, process,
module version, classes, services and the configuration schema's field names,
with the count of sessions configured from each. A publisher record shows its
code, kind, classes, terms state and the feeds that carry it.

The screen is the delivery tree on the left and the record on the right, and
the adapters catalogue is one click from the rail. No adapter is created from
the screen, as the story decided.
