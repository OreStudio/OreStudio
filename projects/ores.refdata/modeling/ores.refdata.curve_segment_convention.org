The convention a segment's instruments are priced with, as ORE's
=Conventions=. Eight of the twelve segment elements state it, and two of those
may leave it out, so it is a part rather than a column of the segment: a
weighted average, a yield plus default, a fitted bond and an ibor fallback
segment have no convention to state.

A segment holds at most one row of this part, so the generated table adds the
unique index on the live rows of =(tenant_id, curve_segment_id)=.
