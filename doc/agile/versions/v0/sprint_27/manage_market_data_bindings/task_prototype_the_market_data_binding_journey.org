* Goal

The prototype argues what the binding screen shows and does: the list of bindings, the source and the series behind each, the last tick, the enabled state, bind and rebind, and start and stop. It has a synthetic scenario, which a person can follow to the end, and a real data scenario, which shows how a vendor binding would look and says that real feeds are not supported, so the design holds both and no screen offers to connect a real feed.
