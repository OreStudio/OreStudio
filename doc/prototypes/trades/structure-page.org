* Summary

A structure is an entity: an immutable identity and legs that are trades. The prototype draws its page and the workflow [[id:3899F17C-232B-408C-B667-FA5C630BB71B][Group trades into a structure]].

** Structure and package

The two are different rungs of the composition ladder, and the page treats them differently. The test is how the customer holds the trades. See [[id:FC31FC9C-7A6E-4EFD-8A62-6DE4137F7160][Trade Structures and Deal Composition]].

#+ATTR_HTML: :class hug-leading
| | Strategy, typed, dynamic | Package |
|-+--------------------------+---------|
| The customer holds | One deal | Each trade under its own terms |
| Confirmation | One document for the deal | Each leg confirms on its own |
| Pricing | As a whole | Leg by leg: the price is the sum of the parts |
| External version | Of the deal | Of each trade |
| Template and roles | A template fixes them (strategy, typed). Dynamic reads them from data | None. Any role is allowed |
| Product family | A strategy has one. Typed and dynamic may mix | Any mix |
| Unlinking a leg | Changes the deal. A partial close-out is a lifecycle event | The other legs are unaffected |

STR-0004 is a package of a swap and a cap. STR-0007 is a risk reversal, a strategy. The page shows the chips, the card "How it confirms and prices", and the members table differently for each. The Members tab of a package shows each leg's external version, because the customer quotes the one for the trade they hold. The trade page says which kind a trade's structure is, and the amend dialog says whose confirmation a change reaches.

A composite trade is neither. It is one trade with its own legs, and it is not in a structure in these prototypes.

** What the prototype argues

- The version is read from the legs and is never stored beside them. The rule is the one the [[id:7B737119-099C-451D-9319-1D6BEE73A063][Trade Entry Prototype]] shows: a change to any component moves the structure, and authorisation does not.
- The kind is chosen at grouping, with its consequences stated beside each choice, because it decides what the customer receives.
- The client states the rules the database enforces: every leg shares the party and the counterparty, a structure nests one level at most, and a strategy has one product family. The person sees the check before the server refuses it.
- A trade moves from one structure to another without being rewritten.
- Pricing and composition stay in the Trade Entry Prototype. The Workflows menu links to both.

** What is fake

The structures. Grouping stores the new structure in the browser.
