* Acceptance

- Each of the four routes names its same-address C++ route in the inventory.
- A test proves each route against the answer the BFF gives.
- The local gate is green.

* Plan

Serve =GET /api/accounts/:username/sign-ins= at the BFF's address in
=sign_in_routes=: the account by username, its sign-in record and a page of its
sessions, newest first, within the BFF's page of 1 to 200, 20 when left out.
An account that is not there answers 404.

The other three routes are the browser's session's work, not IAM reads:
sign-up and the password policy are read before a session exists and sign-up
is rate limited per address, and a password change updates the session the
browser holds. They move to [[id:D4275B6D-BF22-4A77-9F00-018D776966CB][Serve the browser's session routes, sign-up and password]].
