* Acceptance

- No convention kind still carries a column that belongs to another kind's part.
- Commodity future's expiry anchor, option expiry, power and averaging blocks are parts, not columns of one row.
- The cross currency basis and fix float conventions model their two legs as parts, as the tenor basis swap kind does.

* Plan

** The rules a split obeys

The data-oriented model decides the shape, not the column count. Its rules, and
the reasons behind them, are in
[[id:BBD43BF2-9FEE-42B0-A667-9468AF759DC2][Data-Oriented Design in ORE Studio]] and
[[id:56AD6F46-99E0-482E-B7B5-C9FDEC8A7641][The data classification checklist]]; this task applies them and
does not restate them. In short:

- a type holds one component: fields that share one invariant, hold at one
  instant, and are keyed by the same key;
- a part is joined by key and never nested, so the wire carries one typed array
  per component and no part's fields appear inside another's object;
- a field read on a different cadence, or by a different screen, job or service,
  goes with its readers and not with the row it happens to sit in;
- the classification comes first. A compile error never reclassifies data: if a
  shape has to be denormalised for a compiler, the classification stays and the
  denormalisation is recorded;
- the centre keeps the identity and whatever the parts must agree on. A part one
  reader needs does not stay on the centre.

** This grouping

Three convention kinds never moved onto the family's shape. The family's other
kinds are a central [[id:9477A085-0822-485F-BDF7-3C8F4A306E93][convention]] row
plus parts; these three are still one flat row each:

| Type | Members | Columns | The blocks that should be parts |
|------+---------+---------+---------------------------------|
| =commodity_future_convention= | 53 | 53 | expiry anchor, option expiry, power peak and off-peak, averaging, the ORESmd link |
| =cross_currency_basis_convention= | 27 | 27 | =flat_*= and =spread_*=, two legs |
| =cross_currency_fix_float_convention= | 20 | 20 | the fixed and the floating leg |

Each block has its own invariant: the option expiry fields only mean anything
when the convention states an option expiry, the averaging fields only when the
future is averaged, and each leg of a cross-currency swap carries its own index,
tenor and conventions. That is what makes them parts rather than columns.

The target shape is the family's, not a new one: see
[[id:B607E5B7-190F-4D38-B515-8A7B27FB6B47][The atomised entity: a central row and its parts]] and the
tenor basis swap kind, which already carries a pay/receive and a long/short
part.

** What proves it

- Only the central convention row carries the kind.
- A kind-specific block exists in exactly one part, and no other kind's row or
  part carries it.
- The ORE projection round trips: the document states the convention once and
  each part's fields once.
