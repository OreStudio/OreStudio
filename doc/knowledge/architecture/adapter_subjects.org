* Summary

Adapter traffic uses one subject family under =marketdata.v1=. Ticks arrive on
=marketdata.v1.ingress.<adapter>.<id token>=. Control messages use
=marketdata.v1.adapters.*=. After the service stores a tick it delivers it on
the per-party stream =marketdata.v1.ops.market_tick.<tenant>.<party>.<token>=.
No subject contains the word synthetic or any other mark of the source kind.

* Detail

** The ingress subject

The service subscribes once to =marketdata.v1.ingress.>=. The =<adapter>= token
is the opaque adapter id, for example =bloomberg-sim=. Code never parses the
token to learn what the adapter is. The registered source says that.

** The token rule

A vendor id can hold characters that NATS does not allow in a token: a RIC such
as =EUR== holds an equals sign, and a Bloomberg ticker such as
=EURUSD Curncy= holds a space. The builder percent-encodes every byte outside
letters, digits, hyphen and underscore, so the token holds no dot, space or
wildcard. The tick payload carries the exact id, so nothing depends on
decoding the token.

** The control subjects

Announce, demand and heartbeat are in [[id:5DFA094B-4F9F-4190-8251-8D4D0B77A5C7][An adapter and the service meet at four messages]]. They are the only
subjects an adapter publishes or answers besides ingress.

** The party stream

=market_tick_subject(tenant, party, oresmd_uri)= builds the subject that a party
reads. The token is the canonical oresmd URI as one token. A party reads all its
data with a wildcard that ends in =.>=. The payload is described in [[id:1F609D0D-5C72-4A21-B988-6957F18D426F][The market tick]].
