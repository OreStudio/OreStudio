* Goal

The architecture makes domain grounding an obligation: a method's framing phase names the concepts the work touches, resolves each to a knowledge document, and treats an unresolved concept as a gap whose closure is the first task. That obligation needs a mechanism or it stays aspirational. The anchors already exist. The glossary holds our terms, the modeling documents hold entity names, and the graph holds the links. Build a check that takes a story or task, extracts the domain concepts it names, tests each for a resolving knowledge document or structure note, and emits the misses as captures or tasks.

* Acceptance

- A compass command takes a work item and reports the domain concepts it names that have no resolving knowledge document.
- The check draws its vocabulary from the glossary and the modeling documents rather than a hand-written list.
- Misses can be filed as captures or tasks in one step.
- The check is exercised against at least three existing stories and its findings are reviewed for false positives.
- The method plane's grounding phase names this check as how the gap list is produced.
