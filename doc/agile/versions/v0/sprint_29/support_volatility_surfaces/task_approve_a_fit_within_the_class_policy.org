* Goal

A fresh installation runs the fits that pass objective checks without a click. The calibration policy holds an approval mode, manual or auto, and the rules a fit must pass: a minimum of returns, starts that agree, a persistence range, and a variance that reacts to moves. In auto mode each new fit is checked; one that passes is approved through the review verb with the policy, its id and its version as the decider, and the pair's feeds are re-pointed; one that fails stays proposed with the rule it failed as its reason. The review verb records its decider, a person or a policy, so history reads the same either way.

* Acceptance

- The FX policy is seeded in auto mode with the rules: at least 250 returns, starts agree, persistence from 0.80 to 0.995, and an average reaction, alpha plus half of gamma, of at least 0.01. Each class's policy holds its own values.
- An applied run approves each fit that passes and leaves each that fails proposed, naming the failed rule; on the data of 2026-11-03, 13 pairs pass and 11 wait, as =evidence/design_fx_preview_2026-11-03.txt= records.
- An approval's history entry names its decider: the person, or the policy with its id and version.
- A fit approved by the policy can still be rejected, and an exception approved, by a person.
