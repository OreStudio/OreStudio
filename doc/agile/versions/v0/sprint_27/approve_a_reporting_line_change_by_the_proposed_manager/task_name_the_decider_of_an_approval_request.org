* Goal

A kind can say that its requests are decided by one named person. The request records that person, the queue offers it to them alone, and a decision by anybody else is refused with a clear message, whatever permissions they hold.

* Acceptance

- approval_request carries an optional assigned decider, and approval_kind declares whether a request of the kind needs one.
- The decide operation refuses anyone but the named decider for such a kind, and still refuses the asker.
- The queue read offers a request to its named decider, and to nobody else.
