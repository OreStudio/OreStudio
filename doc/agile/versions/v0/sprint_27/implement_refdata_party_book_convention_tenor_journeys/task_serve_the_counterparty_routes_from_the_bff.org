** Decisions and limits

- The server's counterparty list filters by id only. The BFF reads the tenant's
  whole list and applies the search and the status before it cuts the page.
  This is fine for a tenant's counterparties and wrong for a very large one.
- The history route served only the record table's types, so the counterparty
  History step would have returned 404. It now serves the counterparty and its
  identifier and contact types.
- A closed counterparty is any status other than =Active=.
- The business centre write closes the dropped centres, then links the new
  ones, one call each. A refusal part way leaves the earlier changes written.
- The visibility write links one party and removes none. The client sends one
  party id, so "replace" is not what it asks for.
