and a builder; a trade of it is booked, exported as the element ORE reads, and

* Acceptance

- the type names an instrument table, and each batch is a row with its option
  terms, name, currency, strike, strike currency and quantity
- the hedging volatility, drift rate, observation start and payment date are
  columns
- the projection reads and writes the element, value for value
- the type has a builder, and the catalogue routes it
- the generator books one trade of it through its builder, and the recorded
  trades hold it
- the ORE engine test reads and builds it, with no error of the trade kind
