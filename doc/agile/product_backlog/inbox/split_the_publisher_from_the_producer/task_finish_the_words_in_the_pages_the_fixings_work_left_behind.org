* Acceptance

- No living market data page uses publish for the producer's act, except where the
  sentence names the broker's own mechanism.
- The fixings page's title and its eighteen references agree on one spelling, and
  the title itself no longer repeats publish.
- The pages render, every id resolves, and the doc ratchet reports no new
  diagnostic.

* Plan

1. *Inventory.* The page titles and body lines are in the Notes below, with the
   count of references for each title.
2. *Decide the fixings title.* A public body fixes a benchmark; an adapter quotes
   what it fetched. The candidate is =Public sources fix daily benchmarks through
   a supplier session=. The phrase "public sources" collides with the free-data
   sense the page itself owns, so the decision is part of the step.
3. *Sweep the bodies.* The fixings generation page, the provider pages, and the
   naming scheme. Most naming scheme uses are the tick subject and the market
   board: those are the broker's and the board's words, so reword only where the
   sentence is about the producer's act.
4. *Render and gate.*
