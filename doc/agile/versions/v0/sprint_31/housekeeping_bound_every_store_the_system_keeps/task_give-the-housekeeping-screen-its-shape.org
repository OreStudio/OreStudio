* Goal

A person reads one store as three questions in three places: what it is, how it moves, and what bounds it. The plot states its own window and its axes, so the effect of a policy is a shape and not a hope.

* Acceptance

- A store's page holds two tabs, Details and Bound, and the bound is not part of the form the record lives in
- The plot draws a time axis and a size axis over a horizon the person picks: the last day, the last week or the last month
- The samples read answers the window it covered, so the axis states the deployment's window and not the browser's
- Every verdict the list states and every mechanism the page names has a sentence that says what it means
- The system administrator's home carries a housekeeping panel stating the stores nothing bounds, and opens the housekeeping screen
