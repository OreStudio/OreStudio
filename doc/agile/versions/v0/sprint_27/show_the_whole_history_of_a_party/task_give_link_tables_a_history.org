* Result

A junction that states =:history: true= now generates its history, and the party and person timelines draw it.

What changed:

- Codegen: a junction model states =:history: true= (and, when the reads of its rows are guarded by another code, =:history_read_permission:=). It then gets a repository read =read_all_by_<parent>= of every version of every link of one parent, closed links included, a field mapper, and a history provider registrar. The parent is the side a list is read by. The row is a =<junction>_history_row= that holds the link, the actor and the time, and =closed_at=.
- =ores.database= has =validity_end(valid_to)=, which answers the instant a row stopped being valid and nothing for a current row. =ores.platform= has =datetime::from_utc_format=. The ORE log parser and the test database manager now use the platform helpers, and no code outside the platform parses or formats a =std::tm= for these needs.
- The party country, currency and counterparty links, and the account party link, state the flag. The providers are wired into the refdata and IAM registrars.
- The timelines: the party timeline reads each kind of link by the party and the person timeline reads the party links by the account. Each link is a record of its own, its opening is "Created", a change is "Changed", and its closing is "Closed" with the time and no author. A counterparty or a party is said by name. The five gap notes that said the links keep no versions are gone, and a gap is stated only when a kind could not be read.

Evidence:

- Codegen: 1,382 tests pass, 8 of them new for the junction history.
- C++: =ores.platform=, =ores.database=, =ores.ore.core=, =ores.testing=, =ores.refdata.core= and =ores.iam.core= pass. The refdata tests include a closed link, a link of another party, and the provider called through the dispatcher with each link numbered on its own.
- Web: every workspace suite passes (1,199 tests in the web package).
- Against a recreated database with Acme provisioned, the timeline of Acme Corporation Plc holds 27 currency, 9 country and 8 counterparty links, named, and the person timeline of =tenant_admin= holds its 5 party links. Confirmed in the browser by the product owner.

Not done:

- A closing has no author, because the delete rule records when a link ended and not who ended it. An end-version row would record it, and it changes every table.
- No junction has a change event or a notify trigger, so a screen that watches links does not hear a change.
- A party's history opened from a session that cannot see its parts shows no link entries and no gap note. That is the party-scoped rule, and it was already so for identifiers.
- The 17 other junctions state no history. Each needs the flag and the registrar lines.
