* Goal

A person who runs the synthetic market sees and changes how each FX
volatility surface is generated, on the same screens as the FX spot and IR
curve feeds. The Provider tab of =/market-data/synthetic= lists a
provider's vol surface feeds beside its spot and curve feeds, and a vol
surface feed has its own page: its pair, ticks and arrival, the SSVI
start and dynamics, the correlations with spot, the expiry ladder and the
contributors that quote it.

Gamma, the SSVI power-law exponent, shows on the page as fixed at one half
and cannot be edited. The generator's arbitrage bounds hold only at that
value: with gamma at one half, condition 2 of Gatheral and Jacquier holds
for every rho and condition 4 reduces to eta at most the square root of
two, which is the bound the curvature driver keeps. Any other gamma makes
both conditions depend on theta and rho expiry by expiry, so an edited
gamma could make the generator publish an arbitrageable surface. See
[[id:5910304A-FA45-461E-B2A2-8F24FA798769][SVI Volatility Parameterisation]] and
[[id:BB2B7C4C-FD4E-4DCA-BF1D-00F929622B87][Synthetic market data generation: approach]].

* Acceptance

- The Provider tab lists a provider's vol surface feeds, grouped and counted
  as its FX spot and IR curve feeds are.
- A vol surface feed reads as running while a provider's simulated source
  holds its surface. Its page shows that state and offers no Start or Stop,
  and the service refuses a hand start of it with the reason.
- A vol surface feed's page follows the FX and IR feed pages: details,
  calibration, its own tabs and history, with add, edit and revert.
- A person edits the SSVI start (skew and curvature), the skew and curvature
  dynamics, the three correlations, and each expiry's ATM vol and
  forward-variance process, and the screen refuses a value outside its
  bound before the save.
- Gamma shows as fixed at one half, with the reason, and no form edits it.
- The screens are in English, French and Portuguese, and tested.

* Plan

The vol surface feed is already a registered feed kind in the synthetic
service (=feed_kind_registry.cpp=), so it starts and stops like the others,
and the BFF and =ores.http= serve its records. Three things keep it off the
screen:

- The Provider tab's list is one SQL union of the FX spot and IR curve
  tables (=feeds_query= in =feed_filter.cpp=), which the selection start
  and stop also read. The folder counts in =feed_overview_handler.hpp=
  name the same two tables.
- The BFF gives the four vol resources no write schema, so it refuses
  every write to them.
- The web app has no feed kind for them: no path, no page, no words.

The 14 live vol feeds each sit in a folder of the Bloomberg collection,
with asset class =fx= and a =synthetic.= source name, so they fit the
union as they stand.

1. *List the vol feeds with the others.* Add the vol table to
   =feeds_query= as a third branch, kind =fx_vol_surface=, labelled and
   keyed by its pair, engine =ssvi=, and to the folder counts. A test of
   =feeds_query= over a vol feed fails first.
2. *Let the BFF write them.* Write schemas for =fx-vol-surface-feeds=,
   =fx-vol-surface-feed-expiries=, =fx-vol-surface-feed-contributors= and
   =fx-vol-contributors=, bounded as the tables' checks are. They stay
   system owned, so only a system session writes them.
3. *The vol feed kind in the web app*, in the FX and IR language: a
   =FeedKind= in =volFeeds.tsx= at =/market-data/synthetic/vol=, its
   page through =FeedPage=, =feedPageOf= and the engine tag taught the
   kind, and the Add dialog offered it. Its tabs:
   - *Surface*: the SSVI start (skew rho, curvature eta), gamma shown
     fixed at one half with the reason, the skew and curvature dynamics
     and the three correlations; the edit dialog edits all but gamma,
     with the tables' bounds.
   - *Expiries*: the ladder, as =PartsPanel= edits the IR template, with
     each expiry's ATM vol and forward-variance reversion and
     volatility. The feed already refuses a ladder whose total variance
     does not rise.
   - *Contributors*: which vendors quote the surface, and each one's
     noise profile.
   - *Arrivals*: the shared =ArrivalsTab=.
4. *Words and tests*: English, French and Portuguese, and tests beside
   the FX and IR ones.
5. *Run state.* A vol surface runs while an adapter holds it, under its
   feed's source name, but each hold runs under
   =<source_name>.<provider_code>=, so the screens read every vol feed as
   stopped. A hand start built a second, independent surface. So:
   - =basic_shared_surfaces::held()= answers the held surfaces.
     =generator_fleet::running_source_names()= adds their source names, which
     the folder counts read, and =generator_fleet::running_feeds()= adds them
     as feeds, which the BFF reads for each feed's =running=.
   - =feed_kind::held_by_adapters= marks the vol kind, and
     =feed_kind_registry::make_feed= refuses a feed of such a kind. The
     single start and the bulk start both go through =make_feed=; the
     adapters build their holds without it.
   - =FeedKind.heldByProvider= hides Start and Stop on the vol feed page and
     says why.
   A control to start and stop a provider's simulated source is a separate
   task: [[id:BC75AE1F-BD6B-46E0-BA50-794DBD35576E][Start and stop a provider's simulated source from the Provider tab]].

Gamma stays a constant in the generator, so the page states it rather than
reading a stored value.

* Result

The Provider tab lists each provider's vol surface feeds beside its spot
and curve feeds, in the folder lists and the folder counts. A vol surface
feed has its own page at =/market-data/synthetic/vol/<id>=, in the FX and
IR language: Details, Calibration, Surface, Expiries, Contributors,
Arrivals and History, with add, edit and revert. The Surface tab edits the
SSVI start, the skew and curvature dynamics and the three correlations,
each card with its own Edit, and the forms refuse values outside the
tables' bounds. Gamma shows as fixed at one half with the reason, and no
form edits it. The BFF writes the four vol resources for a system session
only, and reads rows whose ids the DQ publish derived.

A vol surface feed reads as running while a provider's simulated source
holds it. Its page shows that state with no Start or Stop, and the service
refuses a hand start with the reason. A control for the simulated source
itself is the follow-on task
[[id:BC75AE1F-BD6B-46E0-BA50-794DBD35576E][Start and stop a provider's simulated source from the Provider tab]].

All acceptance items are met. The screens are in English, French and
Portuguese.
