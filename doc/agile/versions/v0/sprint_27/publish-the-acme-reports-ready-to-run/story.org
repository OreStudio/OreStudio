* Goal

Today a clean ACME provision leaves report definitions that cannot run. The four configuration documents a risk report needs (pricing engines, today's market, curve configuration and conventions) are Acme's own crafted data, inspired by the ORE samples, and not ORE import data. Three of the four have no DQ staging or publish. Nothing publishes the bindings from a definition to its documents. The risk configuration publish skips any definition without them. Design and publish all of it through DQ datasets, with declared dependencies in the order configurations, then definitions with bindings, then risk configurations, so that provisioning alone leaves ACME ready to run. Exporting to ORE happens when a job runs and is not part of publishing.

* Acceptance

- After a clean provision of the acme_demo profile, every ACME report definition has its required configuration documents bound, a risk report configuration with its party's base currency, and a scope that resolves to books, with no import step.
- Every static input the reports need is published by a DQ dataset. The order is configurations, then definitions with bindings, then risk configurations, and each dataset declares its dependency on the one before.
- The configuration documents are crafted for each party's reports, inspired by the ORE samples, and independent of ORE import data.
- A readiness check states, for each definition, what it lacks to run. It passes for every ACME definition after a clean provision.
- Market data comes from the feeds. Trades are out of scope.

* Decisions

Taken with the product owner on 2026-10-11:

- The configuration documents are Acme's own crafted data, inspired by the ORE
  samples. They are not ORE import data, and no ORE import step exists.
- Publishing leaves every report ready to run. Exporting the configuration to
  ORE happens when a job runs and is not part of publishing.
- The publish order is the configurations, then the report definitions with
  their bindings, then the risk report configurations. Each dataset declares
  its dependency on the one before.
- All the static data a report needs is published through DQ datasets with
  dataset dependencies. Market data comes from the feeds.
- Trades are out of scope.

The rules and the reasoning are in the sprint 27 investigation
[[id:6A1EE441-E132-4230-BCD9-E8BB786D721F][Which reports each ACME party runs]], rules D15 to D17.
