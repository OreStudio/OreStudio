The documentation is signed off and delivered as two target-state documents.

- [[id:FDD02CA9-1DAD-4162-95AC-C7C5C611DDDC][Tracking market data identity from fixing to report]] states the target: identities that distinguish observed, generated, derived and published data, lineage that connects them, and reports that reference published curves through the publish record and the as-of. It names the three changes that reach the state: the report's market binding, the removal of the ORE spelling from the runtime, and the creation of the output series.
- [[id:446F28DF-155A-47EC-8D66-FDB90A8FBFA7][Bootstrapping a curve: from quotes to a published set]] states the construction: the recipe, the named quotes, the dates, the solve, the six checks, the candidate and the publication. It names four changes: the named series, the validation operation, the cubic spline, and the output series.
- The settled decision [[id:7AE57098-08D4-4D4A-83D6-918FE520B68E][A pillar names the market series it reads]] and its migration task [[id:EAD9F02F-3B4B-495E-B37D-71BF766F7970][Name a curve pillar's market series]] are carried on the branch.
- Both documents are structure notes: registered in the knowledge index, linked from the market data hub and from each other, 24 structure notes with their back-links, and the service patterns they use name them in turn.
- The tenor architecture's stale paragraphs are corrected: the config no longer claims a source series, and the pillar names its series rather than composing it.
- The public-source and calibration notes document the Frankfurter route at the path level; the in-flight loader design remains prime origin's work and is linked, not duplicated.
