* Result

A run's results are read at the level the reader chooses, and the server does
the rolling up.

One control in the results header says *Aggregate by* and offers Trade, Book and
Counterparty. The choice sits in the address as =?by=, so a refresh keeps it and
a colleague can be sent the book view.

| Level | Row | Columns |
|-------+-----+---------|
| Trade | a trade | TradeId, TradeType, NPV, NPV(Base), Notional, Book, CounterParty |
| Book | a book | Book, Trades, NPV, Notional |
| Counterparty | a counterparty | CounterParty, Trades, NPV, Notional |

Each level is sorted largest first. The totals line always states the run's
trade count and its NPV in each currency, so a level's rows and the run's whole
are read together.

A row opens its record: a book to its page, a counterparty to its page. *Show
the trades* then reads that book's or counterparty's trades at trade level, with
the filter stated above the table and a way to clear it.

The run that shows it is =9ff0e92e-f21d-4137-bb24-ad4956fa99f0=. At book level
it shows one book, *Liquidity Buffer Bonds*, with eight trades,
=31,607,092.99= HKD of NPV and =31,719,872.60= HKD of notional. At counterparty
level it shows eight names, largest first, from *The Bank of East Asia, Limited*
at =9,690,510.31= HKD down to one at =1,142,973.71= HKD.

Two facts the levels state rather than hide. A trade with no booking is grouped
under an unbooked row rather than dropped, so the books cannot add up to less
than the run. And the exposure the counterparty prototype shows is absent here,
because an NPV report runs no SA-CCR analytic to produce it.
