* Summary

=ores.inbox= holds what waits on a person and what a person is told. Its
approval requests are one record and one queue for anything in the system that
needs a decision: a member asking for a role, a trade awaiting operational
authorisation, a change awaiting a second authoriser. Its notifications are
raised by any component, about any activity, to named people or to everyone
who holds a permission. The two are designed in [[id:0FE6BDD9-EB24-4B07-8B0C-D816AAF1BEBF][Approval requests]] and [[id:7FAF3C0D-1131-4C32-9C29-3D1EF6D1005D][Notification requirements]].

* Inputs

- Approval requests raised by any component, and the decisions people make on
  them.
- Notifications raised by any component.

* Outputs

- The approval queue and each person's own requests, over NATS.
- Each person's notifications, their unread count and their read state.
- Change events on every entity, which the owning component of an approval
  kind listens to in order to apply an approval.

* Entry points

- =api/= — domain types, JSON I/O and the NATS protocol.
- =core/= — repositories, services and NATS handlers.
- =service/= — the NATS service entry point.

* Dependencies

- =ores.iam= — accounts and permissions, which requests and notifications name.
- =ores.service= — the NATS service host.
- =ores.database= — persistence.
