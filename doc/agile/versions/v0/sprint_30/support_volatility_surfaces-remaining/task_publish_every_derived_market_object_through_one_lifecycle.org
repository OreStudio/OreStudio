* Goal

Curves, FX forward sets and FX volatility surfaces are published through the one lifecycle [[id:B74CEF18-B533-45A1-B65E-F7DF836988D6][Publishing derived market data]] states: a stored candidate, a raise for one candidate version in one transaction, a decision or a stated rule, the trader's apply that supersedes and tells, a withdrawal of its own, and one read rule. Auto-approval works and shows as [[id:71146F3F-B884-4E61-BA8B-A8A0731703C6][A request closes without a person only under a stated rule]] states, inside the raise's transaction, and a context bound to a transaction refuses a change of tenant or party. The investigation [[id:44E630B8-3CA4-42C5-BA94-75618F121327][The publish of derived market data: curves, forward sets and surfaces]] records why.

* Acceptance

- One implementation of the lifecycle in =ores.marketdata.service=, parameterised by kind ([[id:186B44DD-51EA-45AA-971E-0C2D4D21A6DD][A derived market object is published through one lifecycle]]). One publish record shape with the shared columns and a subject part per kind. The curve, forward set and surface each declare only their subject, compute, checks and approval kinds.
- Each kind's compute stores a versioned candidate with its lineage, its input sets and its checks, each blocking or advisory, before any raise; an unchanged compute converges on the existing version; series reads by as-of never return a candidate ([[id:24950B8E-7FC4-4F0A-8AA4-9603B344F970][A candidate is computed and stored before a publish is raised]]). The curve publish no longer bootstraps at apply; the forward publish no longer computes inside the raise.
- One raise for every kind ([[id:BF14C932-D699-4224-98DA-0481E49FA394][A publish raise names one candidate version, in one transaction]]): the session party owns the subject; a failed blocking check is refused; one open request per subject and as-of; a candidate older than the live set is refused, an equal as-of is a correction; no reason is refused; the request and the subject record naming the candidate version are one transaction; a request a stated rule closes is applied inside that transaction, and a refused apply rolls the raise back.
- One apply for every kind ([[id:86D8986F-5247-4138-85C7-F8899C029F24][The trader applies an approved publish, which supersedes the live set and tells its readers]]): the checks of the note, including the candidate's inputs not withdrawn; then publish, supersede and write the notice as an outbox row in one transaction, delivered after the commit; an approved request lapses after the kind's apply window. Forward sets are superseded and noticed like the others.
- One withdrawal for every kind ([[id:E474FB93-2ACE-4981-9761-33E214B3D268][A published set is withdrawn through a request of its own]]), with a withdrawal approval kind per object kind, applied by the trader, for a live or a superseded set. Forward sets and surfaces gain it.
- One read rule for every kind ([[id:86158AAD-423A-421A-8BE2-CC5123109297][A reader takes the newest published set at or before its as-of]]): the newest published, unwithdrawn set at or before the as-of, the last published at an equal as-of; a read by publish record returns the set whatever its state.
- =context::with_tenant= and =with_party= throw on a context bound to a transaction ([[id:31F9FEF5-4693-4C63-AE93-E74BA88DE175][A context bound to a transaction cannot change its tenant or party]]); every caller that relied on them inside a unit of work reads before or beside the transaction.
- The inbox reads a raise's policy (the approvals mode, the rule and the system actor) with =policy_for= before the unit of work opens, under the tenant with no party restriction, and raises inside it with that policy through =raise_under=, so a raise inside a unit of work by a party's trader closes under the =tenant= rule ([[id:71146F3F-B884-4E61-BA8B-A8A0731703C6][A request closes without a person only under a stated rule]]). A test raises in a unit of work as a party with the tenant's mode =auto_approved= and sees the request approved under =tenant=.
- Provisioning holds a grant per run, naming its kinds and an expiry, in place of changing the tenant's mode; the run releases it however it ends, and the =provisioning= rule closes only the granted kinds ([[id:F8559BFA-9FF8-4F6D-8D70-780ECE9C5737][Provisioning approves without a person only while it runs]]).
- Every screen of a tenant shows an =auto_approved= mode or a held provisioning grant, with who set it, when and why, and each request shows the rule that closed it ([[id:71146F3F-B884-4E61-BA8B-A8A0731703C6][A request closes without a person only under a stated rule]]).
- Tests: for each kind, the raise's refusals, the rule-closed raise and its rollback, the apply's refusals and the lapse, supersession and an equal-as-of correction, withdrawal of a live and of a superseded set and the reads after each, and the read rule; the provisioning grant's release and expiry; and the bound-context refusal.
- The Acme provisioning publishes every party's curves, forward sets and surfaces through the lifecycle, and the evidence shows each request's rule.

* Notes

The architecture is the [[id:B74CEF18-B533-45A1-B65E-F7DF836988D6][Publishing derived market data]] cluster, written and reviewed before this task starts. The decisions it rests on are recorded in the investigation's Recommendations and the story's Decisions.

PR 1 (bound context) found three causes behind a surface publish by a party's trader that waited for a person. First, the approvals mode was read through a re-scoped copy that still ran under the trader's party, so the tenant's =auto_approved= row was invisible. Second, the raw query helpers opened their own connection and could not see the request the transaction had just written. Third, a read made beside the transaction took a second pool lease and deadlocked a pool of two. Fourth, a unit of work kept its connection after its commit until it went out of scope, so the apply and the notice that followed a commit waited on a second connection. A unit of work now returns its connection when it commits. A scan found the same shape in eight places: the surface and curve publishes, the trade booking, execution and batch announcements, and four convention services that read the stored row back. The fix makes a bound context refuse a change of tenant or party. The refusal then found every other raise inside a unit of work: the book and counterparty proposals, the engine settings proposal, the cut take and the trade's operational authorisation. All of them now read the kind and the policy before the unit of work and raise with =raise_under=. Kind lookups read on the context they are given, because every tenant may read the system tenant's kinds. The same PR makes two order-dependent tests stable: the manual-points read gains an order, and the scrypt downgrade case sets its own fast flag.
