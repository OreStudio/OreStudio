* Acceptance

- The screen opens in automatic mode: the defaults calibrate every feed, and
  nothing is required of a person.
- A person may customise the class policy: the window, whether an inverse is
  allowed, the provider order and the fallback. The page returns to automatic.
- Every feed derives its candidates from its pair and that policy. No feed
  carries a handwritten list; a feed pins an item or a window only as an
  exception.
- The coverage is visible: how many feeds resolve from a fixing, keep a level
  or are blocked, and a recalculation records every feed's start.
- A feed may pin an item or a window, and the page says it overrides; the
  pin is optional and reversible.
- Three variants sit on one static page under
  =doc/prototypes/market_data/calibrate-a-generator/=, switchable by =?variant=
  and a bottom bar, with no build and no session.
- It carries no vintage control, and it states that vintage is retired.
- The group index lists it with review none, and the design note sits beside
  the page.

* Notes

The page is at
[[file:../../../../../prototypes/market_data/calibrate-a-generator/index.html][calibrate-a-generator]]. It carries three variants of the calibration screen,
switchable by =?variant= or the bottom bar:

- *List* — the class switch, the policy in one line, the counts, the filters
  and every feed with its start and state. No policy form.
- *Feed detail* — two tabs. *Feed record* carries the resolved start, the
  derived candidates with their reasons, the measures and the three
  exceptions. *Flow* explains the path from policy to process and lists the
  recent recalculations.
- *Policy* — the class-level policy screen, with its coverage and the
  recalculation record.

Every variant shows the level a run takes, the item and provider behind it,
the time received, the inversion, the daily and hourly spread, the factor
applied to the mixture and the fallback or refusal reason. The page has no
vintage control, and it states that vintage is retired.

The mock data is six FX feeds and twelve items across four providers at a
clock of 2026-10-30. The defaults order ECB, IMF and BoC and allow inverses:
EUR/USD resolves from the ECB's 30 October fixing, GBP/USD only as an inverse
of the IMF's USD/GBP, and AUD/USD keeps its level because no source covers it.
Moving the clock to 31 October takes the newer ECB fixing; adding a feed from
its pair, recalculating and naming a feed's items explicitly all work on the
page. Turning the inverse off sends GBP/USD to its fixed level; the refuse
fallback blocks AUD/USD. The state panel prints the policy, the recalculation
count and every start.

The measures card reports the realised volatility over the window, the
annualised drift, the daily range, the samples and freshness, the jump count
and the dispersion against another provider. Only the volatility is marked as
used today; the rest are marked as future inputs.
