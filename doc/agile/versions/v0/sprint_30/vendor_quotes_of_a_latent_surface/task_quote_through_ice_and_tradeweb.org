* Goal

Each surface is quoted by the vendors the curves use, through their own adapters, on one latent path; a clean contributor no tenant subscribes to records the latent surface itself; and a subscribed party receives its vendors' volatility quotes on the routes it receives its rates on.

* Acceptance

- The ice and tradeweb notations write and read an FX volatility point, with round-trip and refusal tests.
- The surface dataset lists ice, tradeweb and a clean contributor, and the catalogue tool writes their vendor ids.
- Two adapters attached to one surface publish from one latent path, shown by one surface started and two contributors' ticks.
- A provisioned party's volatility series receive ticks from the vendor routes.

* Plan

1. *Notation.* ICE and Tradeweb write a volatility point in the dot form they
   already use for pillars: the pair, =VOL=, the strike label and the expiry,
   =EURUSD.VOL.25RR.1Y==, on the bid field. The first word has no index
   tenor, so the two forms never read as each other. The grammar is shared
   in =notation_support.hpp=.
2. *Dataset.* Each surface gets four contributors: Bloomberg, ICE and
   Tradeweb with noise of their own, and a clean one. The clean contributor
   is =demo=: the publish accepts only known provider codes, and the demo
   notation writes no volatility id, so no adapter is offered its points.
   The vendor seed is regenerated by =tools/vendor_catalogue=.
3. *The clean contributor publishes.* A surface sent market ticks only when
   no adapter at all was attached, so a contributor no adapter serves went
   silent once a vendor held the surface. Each contributor now publishes
   market ticks unless an attached adapter serves it.
4. *Proof on the fleet.* Provision Acme and read the deliveries and ticks by
   provider. The first run showed the three vendors quoting the same number:
   the registry never passed the contributors' noise profiles, so every
   contributor was the clean one. The registry now passes each row's profile
   with its name.

* Notes

- The clean contributor's market ticks are proven by the broker test, not on
  the fleet: no party subscribes to them, so the service stores none, and
  reading them off the fleet's broker needs its mTLS certificates and the
  wire codec.
- The surface registry's =build_input= has no unit test: it reads feeds,
  expiries, curves and calendars from a database. The noise fix is shown on
  the fleet instead.

* Result

Each latent surface is quoted by Bloomberg, ICE and Tradeweb from one latent
path, each through its own noise, and a provisioned party receives all
three.

- ICE and Tradeweb write and read a volatility point, =EURUSD.VOL.25RR.1Y=,
  with round-trip and refusal tests.
- The surface dataset lists ice, tradeweb and the clean contributor, demo,
  and the catalogue tool writes 910 ids for each vendor.
- On a provisioned fleet the three vendors tick at the same instants, and
  five Acme parties receive 910 ICE and 910 Tradeweb volatility items.
- A contributor no adapter serves still publishes market ticks, so the clean
  contributor records the latent surface while the vendors quote it.
- The registry now passes each contributor's noise profile. Before this,
  every contributor quoted the clean surface.
