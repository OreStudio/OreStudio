One row per currency a [[id:29A8E20A-0F02-4D54-830E-8DDD668E86C0][CSA]] accepts as collateral. ORE lists them
under the CSA's =EligibleCollaterals=, in order; the position keeps that
order so a CSA written back to ORE lists them as it read them.
