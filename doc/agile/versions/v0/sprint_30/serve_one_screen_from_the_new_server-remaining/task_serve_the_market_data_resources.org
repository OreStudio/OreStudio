* Acceptance

- The gateway part builds and its routes are reachable.
- Each route in scope names its C++ equivalent in the inventory.
- A test proves each new route against the answer the BFF gives.
- The local gate is green.
- The ORE URI codec routes, =GET /api/oresmd/schema= and =POST /api/oresmd/{parse,parse-many,compose}=, forward to the market data service from this part. They moved here from [[id:5A7F2BF9-5AA7-4C19-B146-E73AC2999D27][Serve the transport routes]], because the codec is the market data service's.

* Plan

The part is stood up by the adapter recipe, copied from =ores.http.trading=:
its model, its CMake files, the composite's parts list, the umbrella header,
the aggregate, the file lists, the diagram, and the server's link and
registration. It links =ores.marketdata.api= and =ores.refdata.api=.

The area's resource routes are ported in groups, each one pull request:

| Group | Routes | BFF source |
|-------+--------+------------|
| The part and the adapters | 1 | =adapters.ts= |
| Subscriptions and routes | 6 | =subscriptions.ts= |
| The public rates | 5 | =public-rates.ts= |
| The curves | 12 | =curves.ts= |
| The series observations | 1 | =series.ts= |
| The ORE URI codec | 4 | =oresmd.ts= |

The composed views belong to their own tasks: the catalogue, the connectivity
list, the fixing series, the public rates view and its coverage and file, and
the market data catalogue's neighbours.
