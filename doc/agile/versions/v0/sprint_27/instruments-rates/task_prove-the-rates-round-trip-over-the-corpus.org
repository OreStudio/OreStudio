* Result

The rates family round-trips over the whole ORE corpus. All 47 rates
documents (70 trades) come back as the same family rows after an import, an
export of the trade on its own and a second import. The comparison is of the
rows the family stores, so a field the mapper drops shows as a difference. It
found two losses, the leg day count and payment convention, and the mapper now
carries both. The set of known differences is empty.

The ten rates tables have a generated eventing integration test. Each also
writes, reads and updates a row through its service, which closes the
repository and service gap. The generated tests exposed two defects, now
fixed in the models: the fact tables lacked the session party, and the header
generator could draw a maturity before the start.

No migration is written, by the project rule that a schema change recreates
the database.

Acceptance met: the corpus round trip is equivalent, there is no migration,
the eventing tests are generated, and the measurements carry their commands.

After the rebase onto main, which holds money as an exact decimal, the proof
failed on 20 documents: the exporter wrote a rate as a nine-digit float, so
0.0065 came back as 0.00650000013. The exporter now writes the digits a leaf
carries. The reapply script patches the generated writer, the rates and
stress test reverse mappers set the digits on the leaves, and 73 golden files
now hold the document's own digits. The FRA's notional and strike are bare
floats in the ORE binding, so they carry no digits and are not covered. That
is a separate defect, noted for a follow-up task.
