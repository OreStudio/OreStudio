* Summary

What a person does with a trade: book it, amend it, read it, bring it in from ORE and send it out, record what happens to it, and group trades into one deal. The [[id:1B23A456-EC3C-4C5F-AE3A-34F21E7C7B33][Trade journeys]] are use cases. These prototypes are the screens that serve them.

4 prototypes answer to this group. Each one is a static page under =doc/prototypes/=, opens with no build and no session, and reads mock data only. They share one stylesheet and one data file, copied into each folder, so the four open on the same trades. They link to each other, and to the older [[id:7B737119-099C-451D-9319-1D6BEE73A063][Trade Entry Prototype]] for pricing and for composing a structure.

* Decisions the prototypes need from the owner

- *A Trades area.* The [[id:339F0405-43AE-4049-B6B3-EB12E5A38CA4][Menu structure]] page names five areas and none holds trades. The prototypes put the trade list in a new area, Trades, which is the name of the journey group. The page must change if this holds.
- *The kind of change comes first.* The prototypes follow [[id:8BC3A226-6DD8-49C8-959A-F1AAC97A3F97][Trade Versioning]]: the person chooses the activity and the activity bounds the fields. A form that offers every field and asks the reason afterwards is the opposite. Say if the owner wants the second.
- *Booking: page or sequence.* The prototype shows both. A decision should follow a trial by a trader.
- *The page header.* The entity specification puts only the identifier under the title and the version on each card. The trade page also draws the state, the authorisation and both versions under the title, because the two-version rule is what a person reads a trade for. The specification should say so for a trade, or the prototype should move them to the cards.
- *Authorisation.* The state machine has operational authorisation, and no journey covers it. The trade page draws an Authorise button so that the machine reads whole. It is filed as a capture.

* What is not drawn

- Pricing and the composition of a structure are the [[id:7B737119-099C-451D-9319-1D6BEE73A063][Trade Entry Prototype]]. The prototypes link to them and do not repeat them.
- A person does not confirm anything with a counterparty here. Confirmations have no journey.
- Currencies, books and the ORE directory wizard are other groups.
