* Plan

The change has three layers, and each one is checked before the next is built on
it. The cadence is one fact stated in three places, and a reader must be able to
get from any of them to the same expression.

1. *The catalogue and its seeds.* The four cadences move in
   =report_catalogue.json=, which is the one statement the party sets are derived
   from, and the two descriptions that claimed a report ran on demand lose that
   claim. The generated files follow: =report_definitions.json= by
   =generate_report_data.py=, and the five populate scripts by the two
   =generate_sql.py= generators. Northwind reads the ACME catalogue, so it gains
   the same statements without a second edit.
2. *The pages.* Each page's Frequency section states the expression its seed
   states, in words, and the spot ladder and trades since last in lose the
   on-demand claim in the places it was made rather than in one place only. The
   index gains a Frequency column, because the index is what a reader opens
   first, and the families note reads the triple as hourly.
3. *The live environment.* A development environment stays up while the seeds
   move, so the change is applied in the order the layers stand in: stage and
   publish the catalogue, then move the schedules. A job carries its own copy of
   the expression, so the publish does not move a schedule: the definitions are
   unscheduled and scheduled again as the tenant's administrator for each party,
   which is the operation that writes the job and asks IAM for the run grant.
   Then the scheduler is watched, and the fires are the evidence.

* Notes

**The environment holds two naming families.** The catalogue renamed three
reports after the 2026-11-03 publish, so the live database holds both the old
name and the new one for the triple, the spot greeks and the spot ladder. The
triple's older row held the fifteen-minute job, and that job is the one this task
ended. The spot greeks and the close triple keep jobs at the cadences the agreed
table states, so they do not contradict it, but the report runs twice for each
firing until the work that retires the older catalogue lands. That duplication
was there before this task and is not created by it.

**The job is not a view over the definition.** A definition states when a report
runs and the scheduler holds the expression the job was made with. A publish that
moves a definition therefore leaves a schedule where it was, and the two
operations in the shell are the only path that moves both. The scheduling
operation also asks IAM for the run grant the runs act under, so writing job rows
directly would leave runs that cannot act in their party; no job row was written
by hand here.

**The environment's own binaries are used from this worktree.** This worktree
holds no compiled binaries and no vcpkg submodule, so it cannot build a shell.
=compass shell= resolves the binary under this worktree's =build/output/<preset>/
publish=, and that directory is a symlink to the publish directory the running
services use. Nothing in the main worktree was modified; the symlink is inside
=build/=, which the tree does not track.

**What the runs came to.** The spot ladder and trades since last in completed
every phase. The triple vol spot ladder met the engine and the engine refused the
book it was handed, with 235 errors, which is the failure the ladder family's
pages state: the dataset holds no volatility market data. A cadence is not a
figure.

* Result

The product owner's table is stated in the catalogue, in the seeds, on the pages
and in the live scheduler, and the scheduler fires the reports at it.

- *The catalogue and the seeds.* Four rows moved and every other row stands: the
  triple and the surface ladder run hourly at every hour, the spot ladder every
  half hour through the London day and trades since last in every fifteen
  minutes, and the spike, strike and option maps keep the never-firing
  placeholder. The two descriptions that claimed a report runs on demand lose
  that claim, because the seed now schedules it. The fifteen seeded rows across
  the five datasets state the same thing for every party, and a second run of
  the generators writes nothing.
- *The pages.* The four affected pages state the expression their seed states,
  with the reason for the hourly cadence where the product owner set it; the spot
  ladder loses the paragraph that argued a definition must not fire by itself and
  trades since last in loses the on-demand claim in four places. The index gains
  a Frequency column for all fourteen reports, and the families note reads the
  triple as hourly and the desk reports as scheduled or on demand rather than
  all on demand. The business catalogue's Spot Ladder and Trades Since Last In
  rows read "on-demand · scheduled".
- *The live environment.* The catalogue was staged and published, which moved
  twelve rows; eleven definitions were then unscheduled and scheduled again as
  the tenant's administrator for each party, which wrote eleven jobs and eleven
  run grants with nothing written by hand. Every job states its definition's
  expression, and all eleven fired within four seconds of the hour with no person
  acting. Trades since last in fired again at 15:15 and 15:30, the spot ladders
  at 15:30, and neither triple, which is the table working.
- *The legacy triple.* The environment still carried the older name of the same
  report with a job at the old fifteen-minute expression, and that job fired the
  report at 15:15. The job was ended, and no triple fired at 15:30. The
  definition itself stays, and the notes state the seven other jobs the older
  publish left on three other names, at cadences the table does not contradict.
- *The gates.* The reporting pgTAP suites pass, 188 tests over 19 files, and the
  five publish suites pass alone; SQL hygiene is clean over 3342 files; the
  org lint, the link check and the site page pass. The eight checks that failed
  did so because this worktree has no codegen venv, and each passes when run with
  the main worktree's venv, which the evidence names.
- *What is not verified.* No figure is produced: the triple still meets the
  engine on inputs the environment does not hold, and that is the accepted
  failure. The duplicated spot greeks and close triple runs are the older
  catalogue's and are left for the work that retires it. Nothing was pushed,
  merged or built in this worktree.
