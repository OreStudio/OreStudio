Describes how ORE builds a cross-currency swap whose fixed leg pays one
currency and whose floating leg pays an index in another. Corresponds to the
<CrossCurrencyFixFloat> element in ORE conventions.xml.

The two legs share nothing but the swap they belong to: the fixed leg states a
currency, a frequency, a convention and a day count fraction, and the floating
leg states an index and the rules its fixing follows. ORE writes both, so the
family carries one part for each leg and a convention fills both.

The family's central row is the shared [[id:9477A085-0822-485F-BDF7-3C8F4A306E93][convention]], which names this kind
with =kind_code= =CROSS_CURRENCY_FIX_FLOAT= and holds the ORE document id, the
settlement terms the two legs agree on, and whether the schedule rolls end of
month. Everything one leg alone states is in that leg's part.
