PR 3: the verification cycle after the rebase

Measured 2026-09-27 on feature/model-missing-trading-families, rebased
onto dbfb31d45d (the merge of PR #2240, the hotfix that migrates the FSM
and dataset consumers). The record lands as the branch tip.

The rebase
----------
Fifteen commits replay onto main. One conflict: the port commit's
regenerated commodity_instrument.hpp against main's hand-written
decomposition. The port's shape wins, which is what its message already
states: the four nested blocks become the flat instrument the nine
modelled siblings use. The second conflict is one word of prose in
trade.org, where the port's reading of the header list is kept.

The tree carries trading, its ores.ore and ores.shell consumers, and
their records, and nothing else.

The database
------------
=./compass.sh db recreate -y -k= succeeds, exit 0, with no local
workaround. Every defect pr3_environment_blocker.txt records is fixed on
main: the dq script headers, the storage service in the service registry
with its role, its account row and its description, the unique artefact
index, the seed upserts, and the publish functions that now state the
replace they make. The populate runs to the end and applies the grants.

The build
---------
=cmake --build build/output/linux-clang-debug-make -j 3= exits 0 with no
errors, the first green default build of this work. PR #2240 migrated
the two consumers that dq's remodellings left behind: workflow reads its
FSM states through the repository, and iam reads datasets through the
list the migration left.

The services
------------
=./compass.sh services start= reports 24 of 24 units ready in two
seconds, against 482 seconds and 22 of 24 before the hotfix. busctl is
the transport: =ORES_USE_BUSCTL=1= is in .env and =busctl --user list=
answers.

The suite
---------
=ctest --test-dir build/output/linux-clang-debug-make --output-on-failure=
runs 86 tests in 310 seconds: 81 pass, 5 fail. Every failure is in
another component:

    ores.dq.api.tests         the coding-scheme table drops its description
                              and its subject area
    ores.dq.core.tests        a written row stays at version 0;
                              ores_dq_lei_entities_tbl and
                              ores_dq_lei_relationships_tbl do not exist;
                              a change reason names a missing category
    ores.shell.api.tests      to_iso8601_utc states a space where the test
                              wants 'T'
    ores.refdata.core.tests   the suites insert a business unit, a book and
                              a portfolio whose natural keys the seed holds
    ores.marketdata.core.tests
                              a series classification rule event never arrives

Every suite this branch owns is green, eight in all: ores.trading.api,
ores.trading.core, ores.trading.service, ores.ore.api, ores.ore.core,
ores.ore.service, ores.shell.trading and ores.shell.application.
