* Plan

A new area in =projects/ores.web/packages/web/src/development/=, drawn from the prototype in =doc/prototypes/testing/test-scenarios/=.

- =shell/areas.ts= gets a Development entry in =MENU=, with no permission and no scope, so the menu draws it for everybody. =AppRoutes.tsx= gets =/development= and =/development/tests/:id=.
- =DevelopmentArea= draws the breadcrumb and the tab bar through =useTabs=. The only tab is Tests. Board, Stories and Tasks join it in story 2.
- =TestsQueue= reads =GET /api/qa/scenarios= through =api/qa.ts= and draws two cards, Waiting and Done. A row shows the scenario, its story and task, its target and clients, a progress bar with "n of m steps", a state tag and the completion time. A row opens =/development/tests/:id=. The tab has no heading of its own.
- =queue.ts= holds the logic and is tested alone: the state of a row, the percent done, and the order. Waiting scenarios in progress come before those not started. Done scenarios come newest completion first, and undated ones last.
- =ScenarioPage= is the page a row opens. It names the scenario and lists its steps, and says the runner is not built yet. Task 5 replaces its body with the runner.
- The catalogue gets a =development= block and a menu key in English, French and Portuguese.
- =@ores/contracts= now serves the browser as well as the BFF for the QA shapes, and its comment says so.
