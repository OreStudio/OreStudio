* Plan

1. *Catalogue (J08).* One journey, /Watch the vendors' quotes/, as the product
   owner set it out on 2026-11-18: the market as the vendors quote it, the same

   with a salt wireframe rendered beside it, and add it to the group page.
3. Check every subject in the coverage table against the generated protocol
   headers (X01).
