* Goal

One loop schedules every feed. Feed i fires when tau reaches its next mark over its sensitivity times its base rate, so a market change moves no heap entry and takes effect at once. tick_clock, refresh and re_rate are gone, and every feed takes a scheduler.

* Acceptance

- A tick costs O(log n) in the number of feeds, with a benchmark at 100,000 feeds.
- On the manual clock with a fixed seed, a test asserts the exact sequence of feeds and event times across a pinned regime change.
- The per-series checks still hold: mean rate, Fano factor and time rescaling.
- The service-test fixtures and the adapter test run on a scheduler with the manual clock, and tick_clock is deleted.
- Control operations reach the loop as commands applied at the clock's now.
