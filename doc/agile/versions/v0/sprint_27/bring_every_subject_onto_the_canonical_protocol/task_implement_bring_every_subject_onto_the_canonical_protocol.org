* Acceptance

- Every subject has four segments, is lower snake_case, and its verb is one of get, get_many, list, list_by_<relation>, put, put_many, delete, delete_many.
- No resource is spelled twice: no subject-part exists in both snake_case and kebab-case.
- An operation that is not an entity verb lives in {component}.v1.ops.{operation}, and its model states why it is not one.
- A gate runs the census and fails a non-conforming subject, with the accepted exceptions recorded in one file beside their reason.
- The specification's Conformance section names a list of adopted components; that list exists as an artefact.
