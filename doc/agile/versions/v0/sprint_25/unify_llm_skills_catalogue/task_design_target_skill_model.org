* Goal

The [[id:C7473361-9825-43DF-8610-EBB028A250BE][design]] document states the model; this task makes it
binding. Amend the [[id:7D779BB3-BBEF-40D2-B6AC-E02BCA72AB85][skill naming conventions]] to carry the compass-
prefix, the six planes, the =principle-= and =method-= segments, the
verbs the ported methods need, and the mandatory =#+level:= keyword.
Land the terminology table in the [[id:47341FC6-B8FB-499E-AAE0-F4EE0ABFDA05][glossary]] so it governs every document,
not only skills. Amend the rule forbidding reference-only skills to
exempt the stance plane. Record every register extension in the
decision log, as the policy already demands.

- The naming conventions document carries the compass- prefix rule, the six planes, and the =principle-= and =method-= segments.

- The verb register gains every verb the ported methods need, each with its decision-log entry.
- The terminology table lands in the glossary, naming the retired synonyms and the two traps: spec means an Allium spec, persona means a Council member.
- The reference-only-skills rule is amended to exempt the stance plane, with the reasoning recorded.
- The placement procedure from the architecture is reproduced where an author will meet it.
