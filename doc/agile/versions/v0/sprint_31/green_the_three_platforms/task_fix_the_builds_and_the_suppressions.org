* Result

- =index_fixing_feed.cpp= includes =<chrono>=. Only =<format>= and
  =<stdexcept>= were named, and libstdc++ happens to declare =locate_zone=
  through them, which is why Linux built and libc++ did not.
- =site-cdash.yml= installs the pinned compass release before it configures
  the environment, on the same lines =nightly-linux.yml= uses: the pin names a
  release, the checksum is verified, and =compass-ng= links to the binary. The
  job previously reached =env configure= with no =compass-ng= on PATH.
- =custom.supp= gains two entries, both anchored on the distinctive frame
  rather than on the allocator or on a compiler's initializer name: the boost
  exception factory for the two static exception objects, and the Catch2
  reporter stream for the file and its buffer. Proved against a sixty-line
  reproduction that leaks the same four shapes: three records fall to one, and
  the survivor is a libc-global allocation that matches neither entry.
