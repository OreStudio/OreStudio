#+title: Story: Cut the continuous build time
#+description: The continuous clang jobs no longer fit the six-hour limit. The 2026-10-14 linux-clang-release job reached only 5422 of 7187 build steps in 5h39m, against about 1.4 s per step for the whole build on the last green run. Linking is 53 of those 7187 steps, so a faster linker is out. This story trials the levers that cut total compile work, and raises the sccache ceiling for the two remaining variants if the measurement supports it.
