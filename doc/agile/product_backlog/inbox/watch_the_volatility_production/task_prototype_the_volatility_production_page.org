* Acceptance

- The prototype holds the latent surface, each vendor's view, the calibrated surface and the difference between them as heatmaps on the axes a surface declares.
- The axes are those of a sample surface the prototype reads, so a surface with another pair of axes is a change of the sample and not a change of the page.
- The quote ladders, the calibration checks and the chain's states sit beside the graph.
- The prototype is reachable from the journeys and states what it does not show.
