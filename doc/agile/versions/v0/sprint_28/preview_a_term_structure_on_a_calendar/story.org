* Goal

A person picks a tenor convention, a calendar and a horizon date in ores.web. The page lists every tenor of the convention with the date it resolves to, so the term structure can be read at a glance. This replaces the approximate date column of the retired Qt Tenor Management screen.

* Acceptance

- One wire call resolves all the tenors of a convention on a calendar and a horizon date, and returns a date or a refusal for each.
- A preview page in ores.web lets a person pick a convention, a calendar and a horizon date. It lists each tenor of the convention with its resolved date, in tenor order.
- A tenor that cannot resolve shows the server's refusal in its row and does not hide the other rows.
- The page has a loading state and a retry, as the entity screen specification requires.
