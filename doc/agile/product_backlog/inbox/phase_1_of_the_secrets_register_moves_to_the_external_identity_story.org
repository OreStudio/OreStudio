#+title: The register's phase 1 — E4, a provider's client secret — is…
#+description: A provider's client secret waits on the token exchange the external-identity story builds, and the closed task hands it over rather than carrying it silently. The register's phase 1 — E4, a provider's client secret — is the only piece of the secrets work that has not landed, and it waits on something another story owns: the token exchange. A provider's registry row carries client_secret_ref, which names a lookup rather than a value, and nothing reads it until the exchange asks for it. When the external-identity story builds that exchange, the gateway resolves the secret through the vault by secure introduction, exactly as the service passwords now resolve at start, and the provider's row names the vault entry.

What is already in place: the vault serves the write, the resolve and the sealed delivery; the gateway holds its own key pair and registers its public half; provisioning writes any consumer and any value the deployment states; and the boot order that lets a workload reach the vault with no token is written down and exercised by every service. What is missing is the exchange itself, which belongs to the story that mints tokens for external identities.

This capture exists so the closed task does not carry it silently. Promote it under that story when the exchange exists, and delete this note.
