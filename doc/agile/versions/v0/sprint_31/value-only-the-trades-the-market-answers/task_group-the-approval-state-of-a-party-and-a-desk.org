* Goal

The approval chain gave a party and a desk one more field each, and the rule that
keeps C++ types small allows a listed type to shrink but never to grow: =party=
stands at 20 against a recorded 19, =party_entity= at 21 against 20,
=business_unit_entity= at 17 against 16, and =business_unit= crossed fifteen and
may not join the list at all. The flag belongs with the other facts of an
approval, so the fix is to give it a type of its own in the models and let
generation carry it, which returns all four to what their rows state.

* Acceptance

- The approval flag lives in a type of its own in =ores.refdata.party.org= and
  =ores.refdata.business_unit.org=, generated through codegen, so the entity, the
  wire protocol and the web types each state it once rather than four times.
- =party=, =party_entity=, =business_unit= and =business_unit_entity= hold no
  more fields than their =* Ceilings= rows state, and =business_unit= is at or
  under fifteen so it joins no row.
- No ceiling in =compass/checks/wide_types.org= rises: every row is the count its
  type has.
- The wire roundtrip tests, the web typecheck and the approval pgTAP files pass
  unchanged, because the chain's behaviour does not move.
