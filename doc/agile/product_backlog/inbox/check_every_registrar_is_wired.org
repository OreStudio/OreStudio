* What

=calendar_name_registrar.cpp= and =day_counter_registrar.cpp=, and their history providers, were generated but never called from =registrar.cpp=; a request to their subjects answered with no responder until this was fixed by hand. Add a check, in the codegen drift gate or a unit test, that every generated handler and history provider registrar is called by its service's registrar.

* Why

A new entity can ship with no server answering it, and nothing fails until a screen asks.
