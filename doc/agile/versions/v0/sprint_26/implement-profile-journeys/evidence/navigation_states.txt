The harness runs doc/prototypes/navigation/prototype.js against a minimal DOM
shim and asserts what the prototype promises a reviewer: every combination of
model, actor and state renders; the shell keeps its brand; the render leaks no
undefined; the catalogue's own scale is what the models carry, being 22
journeys in 8 groups, of which a tenant administrator navigates 20 in 7 groups
and a member 6 in 4; the navbar carries the tenant's groups; the sidebar draws
a sidebar and opens the current group; the index draws the index and states
what has not been built; the data model lands on the things a journey hangs
off; the account menu still opens its menu; every model reaches the same
journey screen; the today state carries only what has been built, and a member
with nothing today is told so; and the narrow state is the narrow shell.

The two-axis model has its own promises: the areas sit in the header and the
journeys down the side; an area whose journeys have not been extracted is named
rather than filled; an empty area says so in as many words; and a full-screen
journey is offered as a run from the area that owns it, not as a sidebar entry.

The full-screen journey is asserted against every model at once: it always
offers the way out, and it never draws a header, a navbar or a sidebar, because
a journey that owns the screen is outside all five.

Three of the promises are about absence: a member must not reach the tenant's
groups or an administrator's journey, and the door must not appear as a place a
signed-in person navigates to.
