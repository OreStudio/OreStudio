* Goal

A binding names a source, not a series, and the ingest loop keeps its tick count and last tick in memory with no read. The bindings list and the record page show the series a source feeds and when it last ticked. Add a read over the binding rows that returns, for each binding, the series its source has written and the time of its newest observation. Decide whether the answer is a field of the list reply or its own subject, and write the decision in the task result.
