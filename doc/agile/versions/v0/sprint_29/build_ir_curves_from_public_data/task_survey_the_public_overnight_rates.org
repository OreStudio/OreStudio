* Goal

Each supported currency has a named public source for its overnight rate, or a stated reason it has none. The public curve sources documented so far are the US Treasury par curve, SOFR, SONIA and the ECB's euro short-term rate; the other currencies' publishers, their access routes and their terms are not yet known.
