Describes how ORE builds a cross-currency swap whose fixed leg pays one
currency and whose floating leg pays an index in another. Corresponds to the
<CrossCurrencyFixFloat> element in ORE conventions.xml.

The fixed leg states a currency, a frequency, a convention and a day count
fraction, and the floating leg states an index and the rules its fixing
follows. The swap terms state what applies to both legs at once: the
settlement days, calendar and convention, whether the coupons roll to the end
of the month, and whether the notional resets. ORE writes all three blocks, so
the family carries one part for each and a convention fills all three.

The family's central row is the shared [[id:9477A085-0822-485F-BDF7-3C8F4A306E93][convention]], which names this kind
with =kind_code= =CROSS_CURRENCY_FIX_FLOAT= and holds the ORE document id. The
central row is shared by every kind, so it holds nothing this kind alone
states: those fields are in this family's parts.
