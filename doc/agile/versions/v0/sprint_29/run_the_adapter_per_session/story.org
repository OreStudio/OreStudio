* Goal

The session is what the service knows. One adapter id, one announce, one heartbeat, one demand set, and the feeds it serves. The official adapter reads its transport and its scopes from the catalogue instead of flags, and the simulator appears as one simulated session.

* Acceptance

- One announce, heartbeat and demand set per session; feeds are scopes inside it.
- The adapter reads endpoint, credential, settings and feed scopes from rows, not command-line flags.
- The simulator runs as one simulated session on the same contract.
- The status read answers session health and the feeds the session serves.

* Decisions

- The adapter identity is the session name, so the service registry and the status read key on the session.
- Feed health is freshness, not session state; the two are read from different facts.
- The policy and replay feeders remain sessions of built-in adapters.
