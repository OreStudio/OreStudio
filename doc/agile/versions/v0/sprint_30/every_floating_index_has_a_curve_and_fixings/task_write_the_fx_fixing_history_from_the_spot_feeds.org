* Goal

Today no generator writes an FX fixing. The planner names oresmd://fx/BASE?type=fixing&index=fx&source=TR20H&ccy=QUOTE on 124 acme_demo trades, and no series holds that address. An IR curve feed already writes its index's history through fixing_history_writer, under the system party, on the days no publisher fixed. Give the FX spot feed the same behaviour: when it starts, it plans a daily history for its pair from its process, and the writer keeps it in the pair's fixing series. A value a publisher fixed stays.

* Acceptance

- A started FX spot feed writes a daily fixing history of its pair under the system party, only on the days the series does not hold.
- The IR and FX histories go through one writer, not two.
- On a fresh acme_demo provision, every FX fixing address a trade names has a series with values.

* Plan

- One plan type. =fixing_plan= moves from =ir_curve_feed= to
  =index_fixings.hpp=, beside =derive_fixing_history=, and carries its walk.
  A =states_fixings= mixin holds it on both feeds; the service's start
  callback casts to the mixin and hands the plan to =fixing_history_writer=.
- Two walk scales. A rate moves by an amount, as before; a price moves by a
  proportion, =level * exp(deviation)=, so a price stays positive and EUR/USD
  and USD/JPY move alike. The FX walk is 0.6% a day, near a 10% annual
  volatility, a constant as the IR walk's 2 basis points is.
- One address. =fx_fixing_uri(base, quote)= writes the pair's fixing address
  with source TR20H; the feed writes to it and the planner names it, so the
  two cannot drift. The planner's own constant is removed.
- One derivation kind. The writer filed IR histories under
  =IR_CURVE_BOOTSTRAP=, which they are not. A new kind,
  =SIMULATED_FIXING_HISTORY=, covers both. Only the writer read the old one.
- Out of scope: the five cross pairs the trades name with no spot config of
  their own (AUD/CAD, AUD/CHF, GBP/AUD, GBP/CAD, GBP/NZD), and the seven pairs
  two collections configure. The planning task decides the first; the
  fixing source task decides which config states a pair's fixings.

* Result

Abandoned. A spot feed is a quote generator, not an index administrator, so it should not write fixings; and the simulator must not write to the store. Superseded by [[id:A18D3933-9817-4885-BC5A-B343427651CE][Generate simulated fixings through the simulator adapter]], after the fixings knowledge cluster set out the model. The work on a proportional walk and a shared fixing plan is reusable there.
