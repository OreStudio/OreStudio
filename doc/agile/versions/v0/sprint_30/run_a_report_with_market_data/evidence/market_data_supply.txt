The market data supply, the parties' rights, and where the data sits
====================================================================

Measured on the provisioned acme_demo tenant at commit 7bb52278f6. Each table
names the query file beside this one that produces it.

Every FX fixing mapping belongs to a public publisher and none to a data vendor:
ECB 44, IMF 48, BOC 27, TCMB 21, and two =ore= rows that name test ids. The
vendors supply FX spot only. So a trade that names the 4pm London convention,
which ORE spells =TR20H=, names a convention with no supplier in this catalogue.

Every route names =fx= and every one points at a vendor or the derived feed. No
acme party routes or subscribes to a public publisher, and none routes an
interest rate publisher at all. The system tenant's party does: 21 (publisher,
asset class) pairs, including ecb/fx, imf/fx, boc/fx, tcmb/fx and sixteen
interest rate publishers.

The rights granted are the same for every subscription: display, non-display and
derived. So the columns do not distinguish the parties; the providers do.


3. Where the data sits
----------------------
Query: market_data_storage.sql

      tenant      | asset_class |  form  | series | parties
------------------+-------------+--------+--------+---------
 acme_corporation | fx          | series |    612 |       5
 acme_corporation | ir          | series |    535 |       5
 system           | fx          | fixing |    132 |       1
 system           | fx          | series |    138 |       1
 system           | ir          | fixing |     51 |       1
 system           | ir          | series |    109 |       2

                   tenant                   | fixings | parties
--------------------------------------------+---------+---------
 system                                     |  325571 |       1
 marketdata_corpus_20261121_184404_129_9767 |   13639 |       1
 marketdata_export_20261121_184351_129_3223 |       2 |       1

The acme tenant holds prices and curves and no fixing of any class; the system
tenant keeps the durable fixings, as the design intends. The other fixing rows
belong to the market data tests' own tenants, not to a provision.
