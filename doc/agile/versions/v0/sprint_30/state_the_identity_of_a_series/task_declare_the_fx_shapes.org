* Goal

The CRM composes a pair from two legs that share a base, which it reads from ccy and unit_ccy. Declare the FX shapes (spot, option and surface series, and the FX fixing index) and have the topology factory refuse a level series that lacks either field, naming the series. Measured today: all 246 spot, 148 option and 148 surface series carry both, and all 132 FX fixings carry both.

* Plan

Find the code that builds a CRM topology from series identity, and check what
it does with a sparse row. It is =read_fixing_sources= in
=ores.marketdata.service/app/fixing_sources.cpp=: the CRM ingest bridge reads a
party's FX fixing series from the identity table, takes each series' =unit_ccy=
as the base its publisher quotes against and its =ccy= as a currency it quotes,
and builds the star from that. It already refuses a party whose FX fixing series
lacks either field, naming the series, and the bridge then builds no engine for
the party and logs the refusal. Nothing tested it. Add the tests, show that they
fail without the refusal, and measure the seeded tenant.

* Result

The FX shapes are declared: =fx.spot=, =fx.forward=, =fx.volatility= and
=fx.index_fixing.fx= require =ccy= and =unit_ccy=, and the identity writer
refuses a row that lacks either (from
[[id:B003AE67-4F98-49AB-908E-13A1F7674E98][State the shape of each series class as data]]).

The CRM topology factory, =read_fixing_sources=, refuses a party whose FX fixing
series lacks =ccy= or =unit_ccy=, naming the series' oresmd URI, and the CRM
ingest bridge builds no engine for that party. That behaviour was on main
already; this task adds the tests that hold it:
=a fixing series without its unit currency is refused by name= and
=a party with a fixing series without its unit currency gets no CRM engine=.
Both fail when the refusal is switched off and pass with it.

No FX series in the seeded tenant is refused: all 402 Acme FX level series carry
both fields, and the service logged no CRM refusal. This provisioning holds no
FX fixing series in Acme, so its CRMs keep their configured pairs; the 162 FX
fixing series of the earlier database had no shape breaks.
