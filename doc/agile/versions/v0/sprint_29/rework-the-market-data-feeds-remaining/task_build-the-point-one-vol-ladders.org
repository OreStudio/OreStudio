* Acceptance

- Both ladders bind sensitivity documents that differ only in ShiftStrikes, and the difference is visible in the stored configuration.
- The shift size of each states its type and its unit, so the .1 is not left to the reader.
- The ATM ladder produces one row per currency pair and expiry; the surface ladder one per pair, expiry and strike.
- The ladder runs only where the run holds the vol surface for that pair, and a pair with no surface produces no row rather than a zero.
- A test compares the two ladders on a book with a smile and proves they differ.
