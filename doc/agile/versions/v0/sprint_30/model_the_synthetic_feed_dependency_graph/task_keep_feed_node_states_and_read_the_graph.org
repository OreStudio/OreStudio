It captures the goal, current status, acceptance, and any notes or results.

* Goal

An operator stops an upstream spot or curve, or a latent surface, as they stop a source or a view, and the nodes below it stop producing and say why: each starved node names the node that starved it. One read answers every node with its kind, state, reason and cause, and every edge with its role, so a screen can show the graph from any node.

* Acceptance

- The stopped feed node table admits the kinds source, upstream, latent and view; the node verbs stop and start each of them by name.
- A stopped upstream is withheld from its source's offers, and a stopped latent surface withholds every view of it; both resume at once on a start.
- The service derives each node's state, running, stopped or starved, with its reason and, for a starve, the input that caused it, by one rule walked down the edges.
- synthetic.v1.feed_graph.get answers the nodes and edges, the whole graph or one node with its upstreams and downstreams, and the BFF and ores.http serve it.
- Live: stopping a Bloomberg spot shows the AUD/USD latent surface and its three views starved, each naming its cause; starting it shows them running.

* Plan

One PR, in slices that each end in a check. It applies choices 3 and 4 of the
[[id:2376A086-4C3C-4D61-92E5-29B122DB6A41][synthetic feed graph]] design: state derived in the service, and one read.

1. *Every kind stops.* The stopped feed node's kind check admits =upstream=
   and =latent= beside =source= and =view=. The fleet keeps one set of stopped
   node names, read at start, in place of the stopped views. The offers leave
   out an upstream whose name is stopped, a latent surface whose name is
   stopped, and a view whose name is stopped; every running adapter re-applies
   its demand on a stop or start, so the change lands at once. The handler
   resolves a name to a source, an upstream, a latent surface or a view.
2. *The graph.* A pure function builds the graph from the system tenant's
   candidates and the served sources: an upstream's edge to its source, a
   latent surface's edges to its spot and its two curves, a view's edges to
   its surface and its source. It derives each node's state by the design's
   rule, walked down the edges: =stopped= when an operator stopped it,
   =starved= when an input does not run, naming that input, and =running=
   otherwise. =failing= needs each node's last diagnostic, which the
   diagnostics task brings; until then no node is failing. Check: unit tests
   of the rule on a small graph, the precedence and the cause included.
3. *The read.* =synthetic.v1.feed_graph.get=, with an optional node name that
   narrows the answer to that node, its upstreams and its downstreams. The BFF
   and =ores.http= serve =GET /api/synthetic/feed-graph=, with =?node=. Check:
   handler and route tests.
4. *Proof.* Live: stop the Bloomberg AUD/USD spot and read the graph: the
   latent surface and its three views are starved, naming their causes; start
   it, and they run.
