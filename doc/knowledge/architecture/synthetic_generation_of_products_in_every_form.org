* Summary

The synthetic trade generator books every product in every form it admits
through the booking API ([[id:3085E65E-413B-4E7A-B60A-5844A124C999][The Product Booking API]]). It draws each product's terms against
the product's schema ([[id:290A1069-A1EE-4DD1-8D23-1C60F76F8949][Product Schemas for Native and Scripted Products]]) according to rules held as data for each
field: an underlying taken from the generation scope, a level at a stated
distance from the underlying's price, or a monthly schedule to expiry. The path
by which a user books is therefore the path that generated trades exercise, and
the ORE engine test demonstrates that ORE reads, constructs and values every
product in every form.

* Detail

** What generation demonstrates

- Every product is booked in every form: the generated population contains one
  trade of each product in each of its forms.
- ORE reads and constructs each trade without an error attributable to the
  trade; the test's list of known errors contains only entries concerning market
  data and engine configuration.
- The two forms of a product agree: the test values both and compares them, and
  any pair that differs is listed with its reason and a bound.

** The drawing of levels

A barrier or strike stated as a fixed literal is reached for some underlyings
and never for others. A level drawn at a stated distance from the underlying's
price is reached as the product intends, so that the barrier logic of a script is
exercised.

** Divergence from the current implementation

The generator books native trades through their builders and then books their
scripted forms by evaluating recipes over the native rows within
=ores.synthetic.core=. Scripted-only products are booked from the rows of a
carrier type, with barriers set at levels that no underlying reaches.
