What the template needs
-----------------------
cpp_service.cpp.mustache states the conversions inline, as
{{#is_uuid}}...{{/is_uuid}} arms, at every site, and states none for a
number. It also reads the domain object's members flat.

- key_from loops: v.{{column}} must carry the column's group_prefix.
- The version references must carry domain_entity.scaffold_prefix.
- The audit members must carry domain_entity.audit_prefix, which is what
  change_commentary needs.
- Every key-record conversion needs the numeric arm _key_as_text has:
  lines 51, 54, 342, 844, 1084 and 1131 of the template.

The durable fix is to prepare each of those expressions in core.py, as
string fields on the primary_key dict, and have the template splice
them, the way it already splices v_args and removal_key_args. That
removes the inline conversion arms instead of adding a fourth type to
each of six of them.

The same class of site may exist in the other templates that reach the
domain object: the service header, the table I/O, the JSON I/O, the
generator and the history-difference payloads. The build will name them
one at a time; each is the same prefix or the same text conversion.
