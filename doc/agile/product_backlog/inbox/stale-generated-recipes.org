#+title: Generated recipes are stale, and nothing watched them
#+description: Sixty generated recipes disagree with the models that produce them, in two different ways, and no gate could see either because the drift check rendered per technical space and never produced a recipe at all. A further 76 files sit at the generated recipe path shape with no model behind them.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Two bodies of generated recipe debt, with different causes and different owners,
plus a third question about files no model produces.

* Why

The drift check renders a component's technical spaces one at a time, and a facet
whose =#+requires_facet:= names a facet in another space never fires that way. The
recipe facets are exactly that shape, so no run of any component has ever
produced a recipe, and the 247 files at the generated recipe path shape have gone
unchecked since the facet landed. Both bodies below were found the day the sweep
started rendering the whole =ores= address.

** 1. Fifty-eight recipes are a placeholder behind the template

Every one differs in the same region and nowhere else: inside its
=#+begin_src ores-shell :tangle ...= blocks, an argument placeholder that the
committed file writes as =__none__= is a hyphen in the current render, and in
some files the render also carries trailing entries the committed file does not.
Not one byte of prose differs, so these are stale rather than hand-edited. The
direction is the shell owner's call: either the recipes are behind the template
and want regenerating, or the template is ahead of the shell and wants
correcting.

** 2. Two recipes are a staging change behind main

=doc/recipes/shell/lei_entities/lei_entity.org= and
=doc/recipes/shell/lei_relationships/lei_relationship.org= are staging shims, and
main's staging-only change means the generator now renders them with no command
sections at all: 77 lines, where the tree held 224 before this branch added the
marker and holds 223 once it is removed. Regenerating would delete 146 lines of
generated recipe content, and whether a staging shim should keep a
recipe at all is the =dq= owner's call. These two are why the marker task
carries 111 marked recipes rather than 113.

This is also a live instance of the sweep's recorded limit: a change that retires
a path leaves nothing behind for the comparison to walk, so the deletion is
silent until some later branch happens to touch the file. Here the marker task
touched it.

** 3. Seventy-six files at the shape no model produces

The shape holds 247 files; the render accounts for 171 of them once the 14 it
produces but the tree lacks are set aside, leaving 76 with no model behind them,
which is what a retired or renamed template leaves. Nothing deletes them, and
nothing should: a clean-up that removed files no render produces would delete
whatever a hand-written nested recipe looks like.
