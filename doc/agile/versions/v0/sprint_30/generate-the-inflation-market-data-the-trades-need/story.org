* Acceptance

- The demands the inflation trades resolve are satisfied by series the synthetic service generates and publishes, in the oresmd the conventions name.
- The data are deterministic for a seed, and a rerun of a generate step writes the same values.
- The export writes the inflation series the population requires, read at the run's as-of.
- A report prices every generated inflation type on a freshly provisioned tenant, with no market data error the trade causes.
