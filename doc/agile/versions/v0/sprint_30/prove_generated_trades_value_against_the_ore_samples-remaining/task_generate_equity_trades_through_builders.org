* Acceptance

- convention_lookup answers what each equity builder asks.
- The equity planner draws values and calls the builders; its construct_* functions and *_terms structs are deleted.
- The equity entries leave the recorded trades' known lists, and the recorded files change in the same commit.
- The ORE engine test reads and builds every booked equity type.
- The roundtrip tests read every ORE sample trade of each booked equity type and write back every element ORE needs.

* Plan

- One builder per booked equity type, in =equity_builders.hpp=, following the
  FX builders: a builder fills =equity_instrument_data= with the product's row
  and the shared rows ORE needs, the option block with its style and payoff
  type, schedules, range bounds, barriers, underlyings and legs. Products of
  one shape share a header template, defined in the header so nothing is
  exported or instantiated by hand.
- The ORE errors that are the trade's: the accumulator's payoff type, the Asian
  option's averaging schedule, the cliquet's missing node, the digital's
  option style, the swap's floating leg, the TaRF's range bounds, the touch
  option's equity index, and the worst-of basket's underlying type, which
  states =share= where ORE wants =Equity=. The rest are equity curves and
  spots the Products example lacks, which belong to the market data task.
- An underlying is an oresmd equity address the projection's underlying mapper
  writes; no builder or planner spells an ORE name.
- The =construct_equity_*= functions, their terms and the plan's =equity_terms=
  are deleted. The round-trip known list loses the equity lines the builders
  settle.
