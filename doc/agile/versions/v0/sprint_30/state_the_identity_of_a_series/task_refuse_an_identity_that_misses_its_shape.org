* Goal

Identity is written from an oresmd URI when a series is created. Today a row that misses a field its class needs is stored anyway, and the absence surfaces much later as a consumer that cannot resolve the series. Validate at the writer and refuse, naming the class and the missing or forbidden field.

* Acceptance

- An identity row missing a required field is refused at the writer, naming the class and the field.
- An identity row carrying a field its class forbids is refused the same way.
- No existing fixing series is rejected: all 141 carry the fields their family requires.

* Plan

The refusal is already on main, so the work left is the third acceptance
bullet: show that no stored series is refused. Provision Acme on a fresh
database, then hold every current series against the shape its family states,
in SQL, with the same rule =identity_shape_book::check= applies. Project any
series that is still missing an identity row with the shell's backfill, and read
the backfill's refused count.

  publish, which writes series in SQL and projects nothing. Filed as

* Result

The writer refuses an identity row that misses a required field or carries a
forbidden one, naming the series, the family and the field. That shipped with
[[id:B003AE67-4F98-49AB-908E-13A1F7674E98][State the shape of each series class as data]]. This task measured the third
acceptance bullet on a freshly provisioned Acme database: no stored series
breaks its shape, including all 143 IR fixing series and all 162 FX fixing
series. The 35 curve series the provisioning left unprojected were projected by
the backfill, and the shape refused none.

The measurement found a defect in the backfill, fixed here. A context that sees
only some parties tried to project series of parties it cannot see, and the
identity table's party policy refused the write, which stopped the whole
backfill. =project_missing= now drops those series first. The new test
=a_backfill_by_a_party_leaves_the_series_of_parties_it_cannot_see= failed before
the fix with the same policy error the live backfill gave, and passes after it.
The marketdata publish handler's log line now also reports the refused and
unstated counts.
