* Acceptance

- The par-rate ladder reads parsensitivity.csv, names the par instrument and tenor of each figure, and its configuration sets parSensitivity with a convention on every par instrument.
- The theta ladder reads the Theta risk factor rows of sensitivity.csv and states its ThetaPeriod.
- Neither ladder reports a figure in the zero-rate domain while claiming to be a par figure, and a test proves the two differ.
- The stored figures carry the shift family, so the reader can tell which size produced them.
