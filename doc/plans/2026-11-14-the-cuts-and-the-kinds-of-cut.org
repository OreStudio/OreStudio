* Why this note exists

Ten routes remain of the reporting area's fifty. Nine draw the cuts and the
kinds of cut they are taken under, and one draws a report configuration. Four of
the nine are the kinds' write, read and removal, which are ordinary reference
data. The other five each state a row whose three columns are codes and whose
screen reads three words: the kind's family, its resolution rule, and the
approval kind that signs a cut of it, with the permission its decider needs.

That is the question this note settles. A code is the store's, a word is
another component's, and a join placed in the wrong layer either states a rule
twice or makes a service read another's tables.

* What the BFF does today

** The kinds, and the three words

=kindRows= reads four collections on every request: the kinds, the cut
families, the cut resolution rules, and the inbox's approval kinds. The first
three are the reporting component's own; the fourth is the inbox's, reached over
the bus. Each kind's row is then stated as its family's name, its rule's name,
and the approval kind's name with =approvals_required= and
=decide_permission_code= beside it.

The kind's own =signed= flag is not stored: it is the approval kind's presence,
and the design says a firm that gives the close to another function changes one
approval kind and no kind.

A refusal of the inbox read is not a failure. An account that may not read the
inbox gets an empty map, because the cut's own facts already state whether a
signature was required, so the absence costs the decider's words and nothing
else. Any other failure is raised.

** The choices, and a lookup nobody keeps

=families=, =resolutionRules= and =approvalKinds= are three collection reads.
=retentionDestinations= is not: no lookup states the tiers, so the answer is the
distinct =retention_destination_code= values the kinds themselves hold, sorted.
=businessCentres= is the reference data resource the take fixes a body against.

** The cuts, whose words are already the service's

=list_cut_rows= answers a cut with its state and its label already derived, its
kind's name, the acts that made it and the count of runs that priced from it.
=get_cut_record= answers the body, the derived words, the kind, the gaps and the
approval detail. The BFF derives neither, and says why: a second derivation would
be a second rule.

=runsOfCut= reads the runs by the cut they resolved to, one read, and their
states beside them, which a failed read leaves empty rather than failing the tab.

** The take

=take_cut_request= takes =kind_code=, =scope_code=, =business_centre_code=, the
timepoint with its zone, the cut time, a reason and =preview=, and answers the
cut it wrote, the parts, the gaps, the counts, the three versions and the
approval request it raised. One request serves the floor's assembly and its
press, because an assembly computed by a second path is a preview of that path.

* Where the cost lands

- **The gateway.** Four small collection reads per kind request. That is the
  whole cost, because every derived word arrives derived.
- **The services.** No service reads another's tables. The inbox's approval kind
  is reached over the bus, by the code the kind holds.
- **The store.** The list counts each kind's cuts from one read of the newest
  page of the party's cuts, so a type list costs one cut read and not one per
  row.

* The decisions

** The gateway joins, because two of the three words are beside it

The kind's row is assembled in the gateway from the four reads. The alternative
is a composed read in the reporting service, and it does not close the question:
the family and the rule are reporting's own, but the approval kind is the
inbox's, so a service-side join would either cross a component boundary or split
one row's words across two answers.

One answer that states every word of a row is worth four small reads. The reads
are a closed set a firm seeds, not a table that grows, so the cost does not
follow the estate's size.

** A refused inbox read costs the decider's words, and nothing else

A 403 from the inbox is answered as an empty set of approval kinds. The cut and
the kind still draw, because both state whether an authorisation was required
without naming who decides it. An outage is a failure and is raised.

This is an asymmetry on purpose: a refusal is a fact about the account, and an
outage is a fact about the estate.

** The retention destinations are the kinds' own codes

No lookup states the tiers a cut may be kept for, so the choices answer states
the distinct codes the kinds hold and invents none. A form shows the destination
its kind keeps rather than asking a person to type one.

If the estate gains a retention tier lookup, this decision is the one to
revisit: the answer would then read that lookup and state it whole.

** The page's search and sort stay in the gateway

The kinds are a closed set of a handful, read whole, so the search and the sort
are a slice of what was read. Asking the service for a page and then filtering it
would page a set the filter changes.

** The take is a forward, and the assembly stays where it is

The gateway does not assemble a body. It validates the request and forwards it,
preview or press, and states the answer as the service wrote it. The family's
heaviest route is already one operation, and porting it must not move the logic
into the process that answers HTTP.

** A cut whose kind was retired still draws

=get_cut_record= answers the kind only while the store holds it, and the cut
records what it copied from its kind when it was taken. The gateway therefore
states those facts as the row when the kind cannot be read, rather than refusing
to show a body that exists.

* What a screen costs now

| Step | Today | Agreed |
|------+-------+--------|
| The kinds | 4 collection reads, joined in the BFF | the same 4 reads, joined in the gateway |
| One kind | 4 collection reads and one cut read | the same |
| The cuts | 1 service read, the words already derived | the same |
| One cut | 1 service read, one kind join, one read of the superseding body | the same |
| The take | 1 service call | the same |

* The work, and who does it

| Work | Owner |
|-------+-------|
| The kinds' four reads and the join of the three words | this task, in the gateway |
| The choices, the derived retention destinations and the business centres | this task, in the gateway |
| The cuts list, one cut's record, its runs and the take | this task, in the gateway, each a forward |
| =GET /api/reporting/configurations/:id= | this task |

No service change is needed. Every read this family makes already exists, which
is the difference between it and the family before it.

* What this does not change

A cut stores no state and no label, and the service keeps deriving both. A kind
stays reference data the reporting component owns, and the inbox keeps the words
about who signs a cut. Nothing is copied and no second rule is stated.
