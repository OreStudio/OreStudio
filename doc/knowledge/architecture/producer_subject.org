#+title: The producer subject
#+description: The one subject builder for every asset class, the token sanitising rule, and the sandbox prefix the ingest loop never subscribes to.

* Summary

=producer_subject=, in =ores.synthetic.api/feeds/producer_subject.hpp=, is
the one subject builder for every asset class. A producer supplies only its
source name and its binding mode, and receives the subject it publishes on.
The same function serves FX spot and interest rate curves, so a new asset
class adds no subject grammar.

* Detail

** The token rule

The source name is copied character by character. A character is kept when
it is alphanumeric or one of the three safe punctuation marks, which are
the dot, the underscore and the hyphen. Every other character becomes an
underscore. The dot is kept deliberately, because it is the NATS hierarchy
separator and source names are dotted. The rule exists so that a stray
source name cannot produce surprise routing through a wildcard, or a
publish error.

** The two prefixes

A bound feed yields ="synthetic.v1.ops.tick.<token>"=. A sandboxed feed yields
="synthetic.v1.ops.sandbox_tick.<token>"=. Both prefixes are constants in
=ores.marketdata.api/domain/tick_subjects.hpp=, which is also where the
ingest loop's own wildcard is built, by appending the NATS multi token
wildcard to the bound prefix.

** The fact the code shape does not show

The sandbox prefix is the mechanism and not a naming convention. The ingest
loop subscribes to the bound wildcard only, so a sandboxed tick cannot
reach storage even when a binding names its source. This is why
[[id:04720FEB-1224-4398-B112-0B787B768D46][Binding mode]] can say a sandboxed feed is unreachable by ingest, and why
the feed controller gives a sandboxed feed no binding at all.
