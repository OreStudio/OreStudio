This structure note gathers the report pages. It states the four families the
pages fall in, what each family prices from, and the order in which to read
them. The classification axes and the measure definitions stay on
[[id:04C68B55-E0B5-41CD-B90E-D670C17530B6][Risk reporting]], and the inventory
of pages stays on [[id:6E218159-C7FB-47AE-9779-B2645CE19B05][Report pages]].
Return to [[id:7474935C-95D1-3034-B833-83DCD9FB3A09][Knowledge]].

* Summary

A report page describes one report. The pages divide by what the report prices
from and by who reads it, and the division is the one the business catalogue
draws with its cycle axis. An intraday report prices from the live cut and its
figures are indicative. A close twin prices from the authorised end of day cut
and its figures stand. The desk maps read the book's shape rather than a
valuation. The ladder family reads one bumped risk factor at a time, and it is
the family the store can describe and cannot yet run.

* The four families

** The intraday monitors

These run through the trading day on the live cut. They answer what the book
looks like now, and every figure on them is replaced by the close.

- [[id:0744CB78-6EBF-4340-8885-837D0EEE6A95][Intraday Risk Monitor]], every thirty minutes, on the queue policy, because a
  monitor that skips a half hour answers a question nobody asked.
- [[id:500AC7E3-E87C-4EC8-88A2-B46FB310F865][Headline Position]], every ten minutes, the shallow overview a reader opens
  first.
- [[id:826BE0EC-E26D-478A-84C9-73A42021037F][Bump and Reprice Spot Greeks]], every fifteen minutes, for directional risk.
- [[id:F5288E4A-3F36-4FFB-992C-F1812E772232][Bump and Reprice Triple Vol Spot Ladder]], every fifteen minutes, for vol and skew risk, and
  only in the entity that holds the option books.

** The close twins

A report on both cycles is two definitions and not one, because a definition
states one cut selection. The close twin carries the same analytics, the same
run settings shape and a different market data type, so it values the portfolio
at the weekday before the run date.

- [[id:07F92413-794A-4855-B761-73CC3CAF03C6][Headline Position (EOD)]], the figures market risk signs off.
- [[id:B845A078-591B-4F4B-8FF6-5FA606B2DBA8][Bump and Reprice Spot Greeks (EOD)]], the directional statement of record.
- [[id:84864DB2-3499-4B59-BDE3-14800CC2EA7D][Bump and Reprice Triple Vol Spot Ladder (EOD)]], the vol risk statement of record.

** The desk maps and the activity reports

These read the shape of the book rather than a single figure. They are on demand
or event driven, and three of the five need a grid the store does not hold.

- [[id:DE8B8E26-DD38-4686-AE79-831083B0351C][Bump and Reprice Spot Ladder]], the forward curve at every tenor.
- [[id:2A5A5552-F1A4-46CD-AF3A-ADB2BD0ACA5D][Spike Map]], option notional against expiry and spot.
- [[id:454304DE-E361-4E35-9ABE-41800C751118][Strike Map]], the same view against strike.
- [[id:A952CB92-98B8-4F5A-B4AB-ECBBB9248521][Option Map]], delta beside strike.
- [[id:289D0246-660B-46E9-A403-9D4BAF591E89][Intraday Change]], what moved since a stated time.
- [[id:3A74B881-4D97-485F-9E9B-0179FA90ACBA][Trades Since Last In]], what was booked since a stated instant.

** The ladder family

A ladder reads one bumped risk factor at a time, and the number a reader sees is
a value change rather than a derivative. The bump is a setting of the sensitivity
configuration, not the identity of the report, so a 0.1 vol point ladder and a
one basis point ladder differ only in the document they bind.

- Volatility, across the smile: [[id:D19FBAFA-E9D7-40B7-8EA2-A59AB15609E6][Bump and Reprice ATM vega ladder]],
  [[id:8A3CF082-FDA8-4D54-9272-8388A9ED3A50][Bump and Reprice Risk-reversal vega ladder]],
  [[id:AEAE5CC8-9F55-4F2A-91EC-8C1BBCAAAE5F][Bump and Reprice Butterfly vega ladder]],
  [[id:A6316CB6-8ADA-4A79-9AC2-B55F3A18DF47][Bump and Reprice Three-pillar vega ladder]] and
  [[id:4F1792D8-A563-4860-B230-AE337C4F6BB0][Bump and Reprice Surface vega ladder]].
- Rates: [[id:51B46B4E-1D7E-4ED6-8A49-8CD30F4D485B][Bump and Reprice DV01 ladder]] and
  [[id:C004FD5F-33F0-49D1-893D-9F66FC62342E][Bump and Reprice Rho ladder]].
- The second order, and the cross terms: [[id:7E02BB05-E808-48BC-A916-6C41B761F82D][Bump and Reprice Volga ladder]],
  [[id:225423E8-D86B-45E1-BA1B-CAC159647AA0][Bump and Reprice Vanna ladder]],
  [[id:2696A263-DDBF-4467-9CE3-9F951A653734][Bump and Reprice Cross-gamma ladder]],
  [[id:FD5EACF2-9CA1-498B-A298-6FC13549B711][Bump and Reprice Gamma ladder]].
- The first order: [[id:5F6C35FB-90B0-44B7-9991-38F5F3F67F0F][Bump and Reprice Delta ladder]].
- The clock: [[id:0C87306E-9668-428B-93FD-D65FD9946DA9][Bump and Reprice Theta ladder]].

* The order to read them

A reader new to the folder starts with the shallow report and narrows.

1. [[id:500AC7E3-E87C-4EC8-88A2-B46FB310F865][Headline Position]], for the overview and the pointer to the deeper report.
2. The report the overview points at: a
   [[id:5F6C35FB-90B0-44B7-9991-38F5F3F67F0F][delta ladder]] for a directional
   question, a [[id:D19FBAFA-E9D7-40B7-8EA2-A59AB15609E6][vega ladder]] for a
   volatility question, or [[id:826BE0EC-E26D-478A-84C9-73A42021037F][Bump and Reprice Spot Greeks]]
   for a spot scenario.
3. [[id:0744CB78-6EBF-4340-8885-837D0EEE6A95][Intraday Risk Monitor]] when the
   question spans several books at once.
4. The close twin of whichever report answered the intraday question, once the
   market shuts, for the figures that stand.
5. [[id:289D0246-660B-46E9-A403-9D4BAF591E89][Intraday Change]] or
   [[id:3A74B881-4D97-485F-9E9B-0179FA90ACBA][Trades Since Last In]] when a
   figure moved and a reader needs to know why.

* What every page states, and what no page states

Every page states the six configuration types a definition binds, that the run
document is generated from those bindings and is not stored, the market data
type that fixes the valuation date, and the cut the run selects or mints. Every
page separates what the report produces from what the store holds, because the
result store is designed and not built.

No page quotes a priced figure. No run has produced a priced row, because the
store holds no volatility market data, so a volatility factor has nothing to
shift. Where a run stopped in one environment is a fact for the task that ran
it, and not for the page.

* How a report's schedule reaches the scheduler

A definition states a cron expression, and nothing fires until that expression
is on a scheduler job and the definition holds a run grant. Only one operation
makes that pair: the reporting service's schedule operation, which writes the
job with its own client and asks IAM, on behalf of the person scheduling, for
the grant the runs act under. So a definition states *when* a report runs and
the operation states *that* it runs.

Two consequences follow, and both are deliberate.

- A report that is only ever asked for by hand states the placeholder
  =0 0 31 2 *=, the thirty-first of February, which no calendar reaches. The
  catalogue reads it as on demand. A step that schedules a catalogue leaves
  such a definition alone rather than turning a report a person asks for into
  one that fires by itself.
- A tenant that is provisioned has its catalogue scheduled by the run that
  builds it: =schedule_reports= reads each party's published definitions,
  selects the ones whose schedule is a real cron, and calls the schedule
  operation as the run's administrator. A re-provision asks for nothing that
  already holds a job, so no second job and no second grant is created. See
  [[id:6014CFE1-D978-4ABA-BBA9-D87040FE44BA][the acme_demo profile]].

The Schedule action on a report's screen is still a separate feature and not
part of this: a person scheduling one definition from the UI is a later piece
of work, and until it lands the provisioner is the only thing that schedules a
catalogue.
