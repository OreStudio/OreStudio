* Goal

A party routed to more than one vendor's spots holds the fixing of every pair it
trades. The index decides which fixing exists, not the shape of the party's spot
book.

* Acceptance

- A party with spot series from several vendors holds the fixing of every pair the
  run's indices name.
- A fixing that cannot be derived says so as a fact about the index, not about the
  party's book: the log names the index and the series it lacked.
- A test reproduces the several-base case and passes after the fix.
- The measured difference is on this page: fixings before and after, for one
  provisioned tenant.

* Plan

1. *Reproduce.* Route one party to two vendors' spots and to the fixing
   administrator, run the derivation, and read the count of fixing series,
   observations and fixings. The smokehouse tenant shows the symptom: three
   subscribing vendors, one fixing administrator, and no fixing at all.
2. *Read the cause.* =fixing_sources.cpp= infers the base of a fixing series from
   the party's whole spot book: it groups the spot series by base and cancels a
   pair only when one base remains. A party with EUR and NZD legs cancels nothing,
   and every fixing is left out.
3. *Key by the index.* The index names the pair. Derive the fixing from that
   pair's own spot series, and leave the rest of the book out of the question.
   Where the run holds no series for the pair, say so against the index.
4. *Test.* A party with three vendors' spots for one pair holds its fixing; a party
   with two pairs holds both.
5. *Measure.* One provisioned tenant before and after.

* Notes

- The capture =the-report-market-names-what-ore-cannot-resolve= on the smokehouse
  branch records the symptom and the reasoning, with the log line.
- The bug is older than the fixing publishers. What changed is the source that
  states the fixing: it is now one source per administrator, so the inference runs
  once per administrator and a party routed to several administrators asks the
  same question several times.
- A trade with a single EUR or a single NZD leg would cancel and derive. The
  smokehouse swap's SONIA coupon has no FX leg at all, which is why the two tasks
  are separate: one asks who states the index, the other asks how a fixing is
  derived once someone does.
