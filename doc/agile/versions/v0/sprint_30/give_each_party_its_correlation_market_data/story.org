* Goal

Correlations are market data like any other: the system quotes them as a vendor would, each party receives them by subscription, holds and maintains its own correlation tables through screens, and uses them to drive its crosses and to price in ORE, so a real vendor's correlations can replace the simulated ones with no change.

* Acceptance

- The flow from source to use is designed and recorded before it is built, naming what already exists (the correlation datum, its classification, ORE's CORRELATION type, curve_correlation_config).
- Each party holds its own correlation tables, maintained through screens, with the lineage of every value.
- A party's correlations reach its driven surfaces and its ORE runs.
