* Goal

A synthetic IR curve feed with a public source starts from that source's latest stored rate and moves from there, under a calibration policy of the IR class reviewed through the same capture, fit and approval chain as FX. Today the seed of synthetic_calibration_policies_populate.sql covers FX alone and every IR feed starts from a fixed level.

* Acceptance

- The IR class has a calibration policy naming the provider order and window for each currency with a source.
- Each such feed's start record names the calibration it runs and the public rate it started from.
- A feed with no source keeps a stated fixed level, and the calibration screen says why.
- The CHF feed is not calibrated from SARON without a SIX Data License, because SIX counts a curve built from SARON as derived data; it keeps a fixed level until that is decided.
