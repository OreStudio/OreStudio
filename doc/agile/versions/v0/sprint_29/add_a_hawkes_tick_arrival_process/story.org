* Decisions

- /One spelling first:/ =i_arrival_process= is snake_case, so the three
  interfaces beside it are renamed before the new interface lands, in their own
  task.
- /The record keeps the old spelling:/ a sprint page says what was true when it
  was written. The rename leaves =doc/agile/= alone, and the check names the
  records it allows instead of hiding them. The rename script is a record too,
  because it must name both spellings.
- /The diagrams are renamed in place:/ a full regeneration of the four component
  class diagrams would add about 140 unrelated lines from the parser gap in
  =doc/agile/product_backlog/inbox/diagram_parser_drops_wrapped_declarations.org=.
  The diagrams keep their shape and a later regeneration converges.

- /The arrival regime is per config row:/ =fx_spot_generation_config= is keyed by
  config, base and quote currency, and =ir_curve_generation_config= by currency and
  index, so a major pair, an emerging pair and a curve each carry their own mode,
  branching ratio and decay. One feed, one process, one regime.
- /One stream, no cross-excitation:/ a pair does not excite another pair. The
  multivariate form is described in the Hawkes note and is out of scope.

- /A tick is the unit of time, not a constant:/ a value process steps by the
  elapsed number of nominal ticks, so an irregular arrival and a long gap both
  advance it by the time that passed. One tick remains exactly the old step.
- /A process that cannot scale says so:/ the interface refuses a step other
  than one rather than pretending, and a feed refuses to pair a non-periodic
  arrival with such a process.

* Out of scope

- A market-wide arrival regime, so every series can speed up together in a fast
  market or a panic. Captured in
  =doc/agile/product_backlog/inbox/market_wide_arrival_regime_for_fast_markets.org=;
  per-stream regimes come first.

* Result

analytics.quant can draw ticks from a periodic timer, a Poisson process or a
Hawkes process, and a feed picks its regime from its own config row. The clock
handed a feed the gap it waited, so an irregular arrival steps the value process
by the time that passed rather than by a nominal tick.

Three things made the work honest. The initial arrival processes refused a
branching ratio of one because a stream that does not settle has no long-run
rate to check against. The config gained four columns and the codegen facets,
the staging defaults and the web editor followed, after a review found that the
columns had stopped at the domain type. And the value process gained a step
measured in ticks, with the processes that cannot scale refusing it rather than
guessing.

The market-wide factor is the next step, and it is designed in
=doc/knowledge/domain/market_arrival_regime.org=.
