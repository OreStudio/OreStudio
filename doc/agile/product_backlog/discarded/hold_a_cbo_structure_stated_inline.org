* Discarded

Absorbed by [[id:1A039407-DDBF-4EB6-B9A2-92746C0CB6CD][Hold the native rates, bond and credit types on their tables]], which
holds the CBO natively with its structure.

* What

Hold a CBO's =CBOStructure= when a trade states it inline: the day counters,
payment convention, currency, fees and equity kicker as columns, the
=BondBasketData= as the bond sub-trades a basket holds, the =CBOTranches= as
tranche rows with their legs, and the =ScheduleData= as a schedule. Let the
CBO builder state one, so the generator can book a CBO the engine prices
without reference data.

* Why

The CBO is held as its =CBOInvestment= and names a structure the reference
data holds, as ORE's own example does. A trade that states the structure
inline is read without it, so an import followed by an export loses the bond
basket, the tranches, the fees and the schedule. The engine test stages no CBO
reference data, so the generated CBO is built but not priced.
