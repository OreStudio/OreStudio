* Result

The cut is designed end to end, and signed off.

- The concept lives in the Cuts cluster: the hub, the kinds, the market data and
  trade components, the option-expiry cut code and the naming decision.
- The design note: doc/knowledge/architecture/the_cut_is_the_body_a_run_prices_from.org.
  A cut records the body (the four inputs), the occasion, the gaps and the acts;
  the state is derived and stored nowhere; the kind is reference data carrying
  the approval kind (a nullable FK to the inbox approval kinds), the indicative
  declaration, the resolution rule and the retention.
- The kind declares whether a cut is signed: end of day, month end and year end
  point at the Finance approval kind, independent verification at Market Risk;
  start of day, flash and LIVE point at none.
- The report side: a report definition carries a cut selection (a kind or one
  cut); the run resolves it (EOD, EOM, EOY, SOD, IPV, LIVE, or a pinned cut),
  records the resolved cut and its timepoint, and prices from its body. LIVE
  mints a cut at the run instant, needs no authorisation and is indicative.
  The report shows a badge of its selection kind. The market bindings retire in
  favour of the cut; the earlier decision is marked superseded in part.
- Retention: the kind states the period and destination; a cut copies it when
  taken; retention is a floor and a legal hold outranks it. EOM and EOY are
  kinds in the end-of-day family with longer retention.
- The journeys: give a report its cut, take a cut, and rerun and audit a cut.
- The prototype: eleven surfaces and every state, rendered and read.
- Checks: lint valid, org links 0 unresolved, structure notes green (25),
  prototype inventory 99 in 23 groups, site pages published with no failures.

The implementation is the next work: the cut entity, the kind lookup with its
approval FK, the report's selection field, the resolution operation, the
retention copy, and the removal of configuration_market_binding.
