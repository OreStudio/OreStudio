The paying and receiving leg form of a tenor basis swap. ORE writes a tenor
basis swap as =PayIndex= and =ReceiveIndex= with an optional frequency each,
plus the averaging and sub-periods fields. A row of this part holds that form.
The long and short form lives in =tenor_basis_swap_long_short=, and a
convention fills exactly one of the two.

* Artefact columns
:PROPERTIES:
:key: convention_name
:END:

The part stages the convention it belongs to by name and kind, never by
=convention_id=: the central row's uuid is made by the database when the
publish inserts it. The name and the kind together are the central identity the
publish already holds, so the part needs no uuid of its own either. The stored
party is likewise the party the publish names.

The part's own fields follow the link, because the live table and the staging
table must carry the same payload.
