The payloads the bundle-publish workflow puts on a target service's
publish-from-dq subject, and the result that service hands back.

Neither struct is a bus message. The workflow's step builds the command and
serialises it onto the dataset's target subject, and the handler serialises the
result into the workflow step's result_json — so neither declares a subject, and
the command carries no reply. The subjects the dq service subscribes are
registration detail and stay in registrar.cpp.
