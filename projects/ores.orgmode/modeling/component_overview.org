* Summary

=ores.orgmode= is org-mode in ORE. =ores.orgmode.parser= parses org pages
into documents and resolves =id:= links; =ores.orgmode.service= is the
knowledge service, which will load ORE's org pages, index them into sections
and answer searches and page reads. Its =api= and =core= parts are added by the
tasks that give them their first messages and tables. The service parts are
built by [[id:05FE6068-3CBD-4B93-B6AE-47A5689F1FCB][Search the knowledge base from ores.web]].
