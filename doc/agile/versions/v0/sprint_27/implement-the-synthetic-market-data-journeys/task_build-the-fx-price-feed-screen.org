* Acceptance

- A whole number in a double field decodes on the server, so a weight of 1
  saves and a mean of 0 previews.
- *Market data* is an area of the menu, drawn for a person who reads the
  feeds, with an *FX price feeds* card.
- The feed list reads one page at a time, searched and ordered on the server.
- A feed page shows its details, its mixture and its history. A writer adds,
  edits, reverts and deletes a feed; a reader sees no change offered.
- The Price behaviour tab, named as in Qt, edits the Gaussians as one set, shows the weight sum, the
  return distribution and a batch of sample paths drawn from the values on
  the screen, and saves the set in one call.
- The mixture of one feed is read by its parent, never by reading every
  component.
- Each feed shows a Running or Stopped label, on the list and on its page. A
  writer starts or stops one feed from its page, and the chosen feeds of the
  list in one action; each refusal is named with the server's reason.
- A Ticks tab plots the last 50, 200 or 500 ticks of a feed, with the last
  price and the move over them, read again every two seconds while it runs.
- The Price behaviour tab explains the engine, each column and each row in the
  engine's own terms, warns that the arithmetic engine can go below zero,
  offers volatility profiles, and gives a mean reverting feed its half-life
  and band. The engine, tick rate, price source, enabled and auto start
  fields carry help in the form.

* Plan

The user chose to do the whole of it in this task, the codec and the gaps the
screen needs included. Each step ends in a check.

1. *Codec.* A reflect-cpp overlay port whose msgpack reader reads either
   integer kind into a floating-point field. Checked by a wire codec test that
   decodes =1= and =-2= into doubles, and live by a save with a weight of 1.
2. *Model.* =fx_spot_config_id= on the GMM component is =:filterable:=, which
   gives the list an equals and a one-of filter on the feed. The feed's pair,
   source name and ORE key are =:searchable:= and =:sortable:=, and its tick
   rate =:sortable:=. Regenerated at every address.
3. *BFF.* The synthetic table declares =search=, =sortable= and =parentField=.
   =GET /api/synthetic/:resource= reads one page with the total when it is
   given a limit, and =GET /api/synthetic/:resource/by/:parent= reads one
   parent's rows. The page query schema moves to the shared helpers.
4. *Shared parts.* A record store (=refdata/store.tsx=) gives the shared parts
   their routes through a context, refdata by default. =RecordDialog= gains
   =complete= and =check=; a record field shows its label.
5. *Screens.* The Market data area, the feed list, the feed page and the
   mixture tab, with the mixture rules in a pure module and an SVG line chart.
6. *Words.* English, French and Portuguese.
7. *Documents.* The journey, the record screen standard and the menu
   structure; the codec capture resolved; the gaps task told what landed.

After the first test the user asked for what the Qt market simulator had and
the screen lacked:

8. *Ticks.* The ticks are published on =synthetic.v1.ops.tick.<source>=; the
   stored observations need the feed's series identity, which [[id:3CD588CB-1B74-40BA-896F-75CFF8157EAF][Name a feed
   by its oresmd URI]] brings. So the BFF keeps the newest 500 ticks of each
   running feed it hears of, as Qt does, and serves them at
   =GET /api/synthetic/feeds/:id/ticks= after reading the feed as the session.
9. *Runs.* A status label from =GET /api/synthetic/running=, read every five
   seconds; Start and Stop on the feed page; row choice and Start chosen and
   Stop chosen on the list. Start all and Stop all for a collection, through
   the folder routes, belong to the collection screen task.
10. *Quant help.* The Qt editor's words, ported: the engine, the columns and
    the rows per engine, the arithmetic warning, the profiles and the OU
    half-life and band. The Qt simple mode, sliders for drift, volatility and
    jumps, is a second editor for the same mixture and is left for its own
    task.
12. *Engine.* The engine moves from the feed form to the Price behaviour
    tab, beside the mixture it decides the meaning of. Changing it asks the
    synthetic service to convert the draft (=convert_fx_mixture=, with its
    logic in =ores.synthetic.api/feeds/fx_mixture_conversion.hpp=), and Save
    writes the feed's engine and the mixture under one reason.
11. *Login.* A login waiting on a party made =GET /api/session= answer 500,
    which kept the party picker from showing. It now answers 401.

* Result

The FX price feed screens shipped under *Market data*, and their shape became
the [[id:303D85B2-0039-4B1C-83BD-BB866BC0AEE1][Feed screen standard]] for every
asset class.

- *List as dashboard.* Paged, searched and sorted on the server, with a
  Running or Stopped label and start and stop of the chosen feeds.
- *Feed page.* Details, Price behaviour, Ticks and History tabs; Start or Stop
  in the header; the engine in the subtitle; delete stops the feed and
  removes its mixture first.
- *Price behaviour.* The mixture as a read-only list with a dialog per
  component, Share as a computed column, unsaved changes marked per row and
  cell, and one Save that asks the reason and offers to restart a running
  feed. The engine lives here, and changing it converts the mixture on the
  server through the new =convert_fx_mixture= operation. The distribution and
  the sample paths preview the draft.
- *Ticks.* A price chart of the last 50, 200 or 500 ticks from a BFF buffer,
  with the stats strip, high and low, an EMA, CSV export and the process
  beside it.
- *History.* One timeline of the feed and its components, with Revert.
- *Along the way.* The msgpack reader takes a whole number into a double; the
  synthetic models gained the parent filter and the feed search; the shared
  record parts take a store; a pending login no longer breaks the session
  read; the Gemini review of the ticks is filed and read.
