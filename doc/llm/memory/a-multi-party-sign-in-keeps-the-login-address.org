* What the API says

=POST /api/session= answers =outcome: active= when the account works in one
party, and =outcome: party-required= with =availableParties= when it works in
several. The second case completes with =POST /api/session/party= and a
=partyId=.

* Why it matters to a test

The chooser renders in place, at =/login=, so a UI test that signs in and then
navigates to a route sends the person back to sign in and looks like a client
defect. Wait for the heading =Choose a party=, or sign in as a persona in one
party, which needs no choice.
