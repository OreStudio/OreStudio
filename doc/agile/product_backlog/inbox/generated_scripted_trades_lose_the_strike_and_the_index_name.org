* What

The synthetic generated scripted trades export their index by its ORE name and keep every Number variable, and =the generated trades are the ones recorded= passes again on main.

* Why

=ores.synthetic.core.tests= fails on main at =generated_trade_export_tests.cpp:505= for ScriptedTrade:

- trade 39b359af: Index '.SPX' became 'EQ-.SPX', and Number[3] Strike (1.0853) is lost.
- trade 1af56dfc: Index 'GB0005405286' became 'EQ-GB0005405286', and Number[3] Strike (0.813) is lost.

The test runs in memory, with no database. It passed on 735c13adfe and fails after cf010d9c18 merged in #4734, whose message says the export writes the ORE name an index address resolves to. Re-recording would accept the loss of the strike, so the cause comes first: the planner's rows, or the export's resolution without a market data context. Found while testing the batch-read story.
