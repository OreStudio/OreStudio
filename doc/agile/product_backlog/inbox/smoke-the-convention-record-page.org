This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Open a tenor basis swap convention on the record page of a running fleet and
confirm the terms of both leg forms read as terms: the paying and receiving
form's nine, the long and short form's seven, and the central row's Id.

* Why

The parts were grouped onto field groups and their payloads now nest one level.
The BFF zip flattens them, and its suite feeds the nested shape, so the logic
is covered. What is not covered is the page: the leaf renderer, the two leg
forms drawn from one central row, and whether the long and short terms read
with their new documentation.

* Acceptance

- A tenor basis swap convention draws its terms on the record page, each with
  its label and its documentation.
- The long and short form's five newly documented terms show text rather than a
  bare label.
- A convention with one form shows no terms of the other.
