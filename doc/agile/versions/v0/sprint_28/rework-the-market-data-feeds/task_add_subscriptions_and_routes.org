* Acceptance

- Subscription and route entities exist with history, row-level security by party, and permissions marketdata::subscriptions read, write and delete.
- A route is refused when the source belongs to another provider or is not ready.
- A demand outside the subscription is refused with not_entitled.

* Plan

(Implementation strategy. Written when work starts; key decisions
are distilled into the parent story's =* Decisions= at close, but the
plan itself stays — it is the historical record of what we did.)

1. Model the subscription (party, provider, display, non_display, derived) and the route (party, provider, source) as codegen entities with history and row-level security by party.
2. Check in the route trigger that the party holds a subscription to the provider and that the source belongs to the provider.
3. Seed the permissions marketdata::subscriptions and marketdata::routes (read, write, delete).
4. Test the rights, the keys, the provider check and the route change in the repository.
5. Fix the generated series generator, which emitted a provider code with a numeric suffix that the catalogue refuses.

Two acceptance points depend on later tasks and are not built here: a source that is not ready needs the adapter state of the task that tracks adapters, and the not_entitled refusal of a demand needs the demand of that same task.

* Result

A party holds a subscription to a provider with the rights display, non_display and derived, and a route names the one source that serves it. Both are codegen entities with history, row-level security by party and the permissions marketdata::subscriptions and marketdata::routes (read, write, delete). A subscription must hold at least one right and is unique for a party and a provider. A route is refused when the party holds no subscription to the provider or when the source belongs to another provider, and a party has one route for each provider. Eight repository tests cover these rules and the change of a route with its history. The build of the marketdata and shell test targets passes.

The series generator of the previous task emitted a provider code with a numeric suffix, which the catalogue refuses. This task fixes it with =no_generator_suffix= and applies the same to the two new models.

Left for later tasks:
- The refusal of a source that is not ready needs the adapter state of the task Track adapter state and demand.
- The not_entitled refusal of a demand needs the demand of the same task.
- A new subscription does not create its route at the default source yet. The ingest task resolves a missing route.
- The generated eventing test of the route is off, because a generated route cannot satisfy the checks of the trigger.
- No database test has run, because the worktree =.env= is at version 32.
