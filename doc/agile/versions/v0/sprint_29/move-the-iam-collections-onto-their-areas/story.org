* Decisions

- The session door keeps =/api/v1/auth/sessions= and the generated session collection moves to
  =/api/v1/access/sessions=. The door mints the credential and the collection is the ledger an
  administrator reads, so they are different things that wanted one path.
- A model that renders no route unit does not declare an area. The declaration would be inert
  and would read as a decision nobody can observe.

* Result

The IAM collections answer on their published areas, the collision between the session door
and the session collection is settled with a reason a reviewer can check, and the generated
tests pass against the built unit. The operation routes are the remaining IAM work, and they
need the declaration to reach operations rather than entities.
