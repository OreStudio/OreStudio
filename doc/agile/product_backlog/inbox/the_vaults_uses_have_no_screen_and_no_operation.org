This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The vault keeps a record of every secret it resolves, and a person cannot read
it. The journey that keeps an installation's secrets names a *Uses* screen as its
last step, and that step is the only one with no rule behind it and no operation
under it: the record screen standard has no shape for a list of events across
records, and the vault serves no read of the uses.

* Why

A reveal is recorded twice, which is the price the design sets for putting a value
on a screen. A record nobody can read pays that price for nothing: the audit
exists, and the person who has to explain a use cannot see it.

It is a capture rather than an assumption because both halves are missing at once.
A screen alone would have nothing to call, and an operation alone would have no
shape to draw; settling one decides the other, so they belong to one piece of work.

The record itself exists and holds the value's name, the consumer, the actor, the
time and the key version, which is what a screen would need.
