* Plan

A fixing reaches the store as an ordinary adapter tick: the market data service
stores it as durable because its vendor id maps to a =type=fixing= address, and
it stores it for each party routed to the source that sent it. So a simulated
fixing needs a vendor id, a mapping, a route of the system party, and a
generator that sends the tick.

1. *Name a fixing in a notation.* Add =fixing_quote= to the vendor quote
   descriptions: the fixing's oresmd address. A simulated fixing notation writes
   its id as the ORE index name the ORE index codec writes, such as =USD-SOFR=
   or =FX-WMR1000NYC-EUR-USD=, with the field =value=, and reads it back. One
   provider per administrator of a simulated source uses it. Check: notation
   round trip tests.
2. *Install the simulated publishers.* For each administrator of a fixing source
   whose fixings the run simulates, a provider, a publisher and a
   =synthetic-local= source; a vendor instrument and a mapping for each fixing
   item, written by =tools/vendor_catalogue=; a route of each tenant's system
   party to the source. Check: a recreated database holds the rows, and the
   party demand of the system party names the fixing items.
3. *Generate the fixings.* A fixing generator kind that the simulator offers one
   generator per fixing id. On start it sends the history the direct writer
   wrote, from the same =derive_fixing_history=; then at each fixing time it
   fixes the item from the run's market board: an IR index from the curve's
   rate, an FX fixing from the pair's spot at the cut instant. Check: unit tests
   of the history and of the FX value at the cut.
4. *Remove the direct writer.* =fixing_history_writer= and the curve feed's
   fixing plan go. Check: the synthetic service writes nothing to the store.
5. *Prove it end to end.* A fresh acme_demo provision; the fixing gap query for
   IR and FX under the system party.

* Notes

The task asks for a publisher for each administrator of a simulated source. The
data names an administrator for six interest rate indices only, and the curves
fix many more, so one provider, =simulated-fixings=, publishes every simulated
fixing. A publisher per administrator needs a fixing source row for each index
first.

The simulated fixing notation names a fixing by its oresmd address rather than
by ORE's index name, so ORE's spellings, such as =EUR-ESTER=, stay at ORE's
boundary.

The run's market board held curves only for the currencies they discount, so a
term index such as LIBOR 3M could not be read from it. Each curve now states its
index's rate on the board, under the index's fixing address, and the fixing
generator reads it there.

Every generated FX trade observed =TR20H=, which ORE's examples name and the
design page keeps only for imported examples. An FX trade now observes the
fixing source of its pair's default cut. A cross no vendor collection quotes has
no spot to fix, so the FX draw keeps the pairs a spot config quotes; deriving a
cross through USD is captured in the inbox.

* Result

The simulator writes nothing to the store. A simulated fixing is an adapter tick
of the simulated fixing publisher, =simulated-fixings-a=, which the market data
ingest stores as a durable fixing for the system party. =index_fixing_feed=
states an item's history on its first level, leaving the days the market holds,
and then fixes it once a day from the run's board: an FX pair at its cut on the
cut zone's clock, an index once a run day from the rate its curve states.
=fixing_history_writer= and the curve's fixing plan are gone.

Acceptance:
- The simulator writes nothing to the store; every simulated fixing arrives
  through the adapter: 42,640 fixings over 82 items, all from
  =simulated-fixings-a=, none from the removed writer. Met.
- A fixing of EUR/USD at the New York cut equals the run's EUR/USD spot at
  that instant: =an_fx_fixing_is_the_pairs_spot_at_the_cut= ticks before and
  after 10:00 New York and finds the board's spot, stamped on the cut's date.
  The value is the spot at the first tick past the cut, which the generator
  takes every ten seconds of the run. Met.
- On a fresh acme_demo provision every FX and IR fixing address a trade names
  has a series with values under the system party: 18 IR and 18 FX addresses,
  none empty. Met, once the FX draw kept the pairs a spot config quotes.
