* Result

* Abandoned

A projection was built — the table, its indexes, a rebuild function, and a
measurement of 6 to 11 ms against 260 to 320 ms from the view — and then closed
without merging.

It treated the symptom. The blotter row is an expression because the model has
two spellings of "many trades as one" and neither is the row the screen shows.
[[id:FC31FC9C-7A6E-4EFD-8A62-6DE4137F7160][Trade Structures and Deal Composition]]
settles it: a structure is one deal whose legs are themselves trades, and this
tree's =CompositeTrade= is that structure. So the row is a trade, its columns are
its own facts, and nothing needs copying.

The projection branch is deleted. [[id:7C1F0E52-9A44-4B31-8E6D-2F5C7A1B9D03][The
blotter lists trades]] carries what replaces it.
