* Purpose

Models a high-volume, append-only measurement stream — a price tick, a
sensor reading, anything time-indexed where rows are written far more
often than edited and there is no meaningful concept of a user-facing
detail-edit form. Backed by a TimescaleDB hypertable rather than the
standard bi-temporal table shape; no Qt surface at all, since nobody
hand-edits a tick.
