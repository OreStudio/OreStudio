#+title: The feed ingest tests fail while the environment's services run
#+description: Two feed ingest loop tests count the rows and republishes their own feed produced, in the environment's database and on its NATS subjects; when the environment's marketdata, official and synthetic services run they add ticks of their own, and the tests fail.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

=a_failed_copy_loses_no_other_tick_and_stores_none_twice_while_the_next_batch_waits=
and =a_small_batch_does_not_pass_the_batches_that_are_in_the_stages= in
=ores.marketdata.service.tests= count the ticks their own feed stored and
republished. They read the environment's database and NATS subjects, where the
environment's marketdata, official and synthetic services write ticks of their
own; while those run, the counts are off (6203 stored where 6200 were sent)
and the tests fail every time. With the three services stopped they pass.

* Why

A suite that fails because the environment is up reads as a regression and
gets rerun or ignored. The tests should count only their own ticks: scope
them by a feed, party or subject of their own, or run on a database and
subject prefix no service shares. Found on 2026-11-06 while moving the oresmd
codecs.
