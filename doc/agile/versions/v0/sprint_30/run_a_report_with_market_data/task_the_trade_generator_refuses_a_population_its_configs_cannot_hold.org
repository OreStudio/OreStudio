* Goal

At main =da9ee3cece=, provisioning =acme_demo= ends its =generate_trades=
step with a refusal: "The population of 1000 trades gives 'bond' 40 trades,
and its 4 configs state at least one trade per code it cycles, which is 44.
A total of 1100 is the smallest that holds them." No trade is booked for any
party, so every report runs on an empty book.

The refusal is right: a config shorter than its code list books a prefix and
silently drops the product types past it, which is the opposite of what the
generator is for. The seeded total is what did not keep up: the option on a
bond future joined the bond code cycle, taking it from ten codes to eleven,
and four entities each book a bond config. So the fix is the seeded total,
and the planner's refusal is the guard that finds the next such drift.

** What

step with one warning: "The population of 1000 trades gives 'bond' 40
trades, and its 4 configs state at least one trade per code it cycles,

booked for any party. Either the seeded population grows to the size its
configs need, or the planner books what it can and names the codes it left
out.

** Why

Every report of a freshly provisioned tenant runs on an empty book, so no
run can show what the market data work does to the trades.
