* Goal

A report over the trades the generator books runs to completion, and ORE
values every generated trade type from market data the system holds.

The generator books trades that ORE builds, and a report over them fails.
The first task measured why, on ACCOUK's NPV run over 460 trades. The
market file was not empty: it held 3,313 FX and IR data points. But 416
trades failed, for three reasons:

- The party's market configuration names only OIS curves, overnight
  indices and FX. ORE cannot find any IBOR index, equity, commodity,
  inflation index or credit curve (352 failures).
- No equity, commodity, inflation or credit quotes exist.
- The fixings file is empty, because every fixing sits under the system
  party and the export writes only the reporting party's series.

The pieces this story needs were spread over eight stories, and no story
owned the whole path. This story is that path. It is one arc:

1. *Know why the run fails.* Measure the cause on a freshly provisioned
   ACME, and record the ORE errors by trade type. That record is the
   baseline every later task reduces. Done.
2. *State what the trades need.* Resolve the generated population to the
   market data it requires, as oresmd addresses. Name every object it uses
   in the market configuration, and give each generated type its market
   data and its pricing engine configuration.
3. *Supply what is missing.* Give each party the fixings of its indices,
   seed the public fixings history, generate the fixings no publisher
   gives, and make the curve quote names match the series the feeds
   produce.
4. *Export it at the run's as-of.* Narrow the export to the population's
   requirements, read at the as-of of the run.
5. *Prove it.* Value every generated type in a report against ORE's own
   NPVs, then run the whole chain from a freshly recreated database.

The trade population is the one the generator books. The relative-time DQ
trade corpus that [[id:86B6BF9C-CEEA-4FCB-8048-7CA94E927584][Seed a relative-time trade population with the market data it needs]]
proposed is not built: it predates the generator, and the generated
population answers the same question.

* Acceptance

- The cause of the failing run is measured and recorded, with the command
  and the commit, before any fix lands.
- The market data a generated population requires is resolved from its
  trades, not listed by hand.
- Every generated trade type has its market data and its pricing engine
  configuration, or leaves the generator with its reason stated.
- Every index that a generated floating leg uses has fixings back to the
  leg's earliest reset, and the fixings are deterministic for a seed.
- The export writes only the population's requirements, read at the as-of
  of the run.
- A report over every generated type completes, and each type matches ORE's
  own NPV within 0.01% of the reference NPV, or 0.0001% of notional if that
  is larger.
- The same run completes from a freshly recreated database with no hand
  step and no file imported from =external/ore=.

* Decisions

Settled on 2026-11-17, when this story was formed:

1. *One story owns the path.* The open BACKLOG tasks moved here from the
   stories that held them, with their UUIDs intact:
   - From [[id:E4801A4B-938E-48FD-A7F2-E57E91587FEF][Gather each trade set and its market data — the remaining work]]:
     resolve the requirements, and export the market data.
   - From [[id:47DEDD3C-082E-46A9-BD01-67DFAB9CFFA5][Make the generated trades value correctly in ORE]]:
     the market data and engines, and the valuation.
   - From [[id:1226D830-9285-4C45-9F5C-72F72AFC7352][Seed the public fixings history from a DQ snapshot]]:
     its only task.
   - From [[id:441E3343-F754-49B1-9763-960BBC2D4532][Complete the ACME report inputs and prove they run]]:
     the curve quote names.
   - From [[id:C9FC5224-69D3-444A-8D9E-4669ACC88F36][Prove the report grid run end to end — the remaining work]]:
     the run on generated data.
   STARTED tasks stay where they are and are dependencies. The owners of
   the source stories review the moves later.
2. *The generated trades drive the requirements.* The relative-time DQ
   corpus is not built.
3. *The public fixings history is not seeded from a DQ dataset.* A provision
   already fetches about ten years of FX fixings and 2,522 days of SONIA from
   the public sources, so the DQ snapshot would only spare their API. Its task
   stays BACKLOG in this story and is not on the arc; it returns to the product
   backlog when the story closes.
3. *One run is one trade set.* The moved tasks say "trade set". Until the
   fan-out exists, a run's whole population is its one set.

Evidence already on record:

- PR #4708 (merged 2026-11-14, under the valuation task) put every product
  type ORE documents into every pricing engine configuration. A provisioned
  ACME book of 1,000 trades then ran to "Report execution completed." with
  1,440 priced rows and no pricing engine errors. The PR states that the
  remaining errors are market data. So the engine half of
  "Give every generated trade type its market data and pricing engines" is
  mostly done, and the market data half is open.
