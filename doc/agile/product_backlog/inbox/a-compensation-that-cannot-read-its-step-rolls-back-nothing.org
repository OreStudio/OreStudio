#+description: A workflow compensation that cannot decode its step's command or result rolls back nothing and reports no error. In ores.ore.api's ore_import_workflow.hpp and run_configuration_import_workflow.hpp, build_compensation reads cmd_json and result_json with json::try_read and, when either read fails, still returns a rollback request with empty saved-id lists, so the rollback step deletes nothing and succeeds. The saved currencies, portfolios, books, trades or documents stay behind with no trace of why. The behaviour predates the codec move (reflect-cpp's failed reads did the same). Make a failed read fail the compensation, or at least log it, and check the other workflows' compensations for the same pattern. Raised by the review of PR #4360.
