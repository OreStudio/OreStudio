* Goal

Serve =GET /api/timeline= from the gateway. The route answers one subject's story
for three subjects, and each is its own composition: a request is the story the
part already serves, wrapped with its subject and no gaps; a person is the
account, its contact record, the roles it was given, the parties it works in and
its sign-ins; a party is its own versions, the versions of each part it holds and
the links it keeps. Every source a caller may not read is a gap in the answer
rather than a failure, which is why the answer carries a list of gaps at all.

* Plan

The work was sized while the workflow composed views were served, and it was left
to this task on purpose: a person's timeline that silently left out the sign-ins,
or a party's that left out the parts it holds, would look complete while being
short, which is the failure worth refusing.

Each subject is served as its own family, smallest first, and each states its
composition once:

| Subject | What it composes | Standing gaps |
|+--------+------------------+---------------|
| request | the story the part already answers, wrapped with its subject | none |
| person | the account's own versions, the roles it was given and the parties it works in read as link histories, its contact record, and its sign-ins | the sign-in log is a rolling window, so an older sign-in is not shown |
| party | its own versions, the versions of each part it holds, and the links it keeps | no read lists a part that was removed from the party, so its closing is not shown |

The entity keys are =ores.iam.account=, =ores.iam.account_role=,
=ores.iam.account_party=, =ores.iam.account_contact_information= and
=ores.iam.auth_event= for a person, and =ores.refdata.party= with its parts and
links for a party. Every one of them is read through the gateway's own history
read, reached by =history_subject_for(entity_type)=, so no route invents a subject.

Two rules the families share with the rest of the part. The events are ordered by
the one rule the request story already uses: newest first, and within one second
the later act first, because the timestamps on the wire carry whole seconds. And a
read that fails is a fault while a read the caller may not make is a gap: only a
refusal becomes a gap, so a service that is down is not reported as a permission
the person lacks.

The answer carries an ETag and stays fresh for 30 seconds, as the inventory
records for this view.

Each family is finished when its subject answers at the BFF's address with the
BFF's member names and status codes, its events carry the kinds and fields the
screen draws, its gaps are stated with the reason the screen shows, and its route
is paired in the inventory.
