* Goal

A person who can act learns that a store has no bound, or no effective one,
without opening the housekeeping screen. Housekeeping raises a notification into
=ores.inbox= of the administrator who owns the store — the system's for a
platform store, the tenant's for one a tenant writes — when a store has no
policy, when a sweep records a policy skipped or failed, and when a store passes
a size budget. The notification names the store, says what happened and links to
its page; the numbers a person needs to act stay on the page, where the plot and
the policy are.

* Acceptance

- Housekeeping adds a notification kind for each case it raises, with its message key and its arguments, as a seed row and no change to the subsystem
- The audience is the holder of the administrator permission in the store's own tenant: the system tenant for a platform store, a tenant for a store that tenant writes
- A notification names the store and links to its page, and carries no size, no growth rate and no policy period
- One sweep that skips four policies raises one notification, not four: the inbox groups by kind and subject
- Raising never fails the sweep: a delivery that fails is recorded and retried, and the sweep completes
- A store that comes back under a bound raises nothing, so the count of open housekeeping notifications follows the count of unbounded stores
