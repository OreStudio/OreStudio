* Result

The analysis of the equity family is written and the story is promoted from
the product backlog into sprint 28.

=design.org= states the family, inventories its surfaces, records 16 findings
with the command behind each number, proposes the target model and stages the
rework into ten units. =design_er.puml= draws the proposal. The PNG is not
rendered yet; design.org names the command.

What the analysis found, in brief: the catalogue holds 32 codes of product
type equity, 19 are routed, 17 of those to an equity table, and 13 have no
instrument table at all. Nine routed models and one child table hold 222
columns, 17 to 26 per table, of which 17 of the 45 distinct business columns
are assigned by no ORE import mapper. The family has no header. Regime 2
scores 1 of 5 and Regime 3 scores 1 of 5, both debt High.

The design was reviewed with the data-model review skill; its comments are in
the Review table. It was re-measured after the rebase onto PR #3384, which
added five equity columns, and the review corrected the finding count from 33
to 16 and declined three claims.

Merged as PR #3389.

Three follow-ups this task does not take.

- Ten staged units are named in the design and none is scaffolded as a task
  yet, so the story stays STARTED.
- =design_er.png= is not rendered; design.org names the command.
- Running the build and the suites for the analysis turned up a defect
  outside this family: =to_iso8601_utc= formats through =to_time_t= and
  ="%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S"=, so it drops the whole fractional second, and
  =parse_utc_timestamp= parses with the same seconds-only format and silently
  discards a fraction. A market observation therefore loses up to a second of
  its timestamp. The test that caught it is
  =ores.marketdata.service.tests=. Taken as its own unit.
