#+title: A product is booked in one of the forms it offers
#+description: A product's forms are its own element, a ScriptedTrade over a library script, or both; the form is chosen at booking and recorded on the trade.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :scripting:trading:architecture:
#+created: 2026-11-21
#+updated: 2026-11-21

* Summary

A product is booked in one of the forms it offers. Its /native/ form is the
element ORE reads it as, such as =EquityBasketOptionData=. Its /scripted/ form
is a generic =ScriptedTrade= over a library script with the same payoff, such as
=VanillaBasketOption=. Some products offer both, some only the native form, and
some only the scripted form, because ORE has no element for them. The form is
chosen when the trade is booked and recorded on the trade, so it reopens, amends
and reports in the form it was booked in.

* Detail

** The three cases

| Product offers | Example | Booked as |
|----------------+---------+-----------|
| native only | =Swap=, =FxForward= | its element |
| both | =EquityBasketOption=, =FxGenericBarrierOption= | its element, or a ScriptedTrade over the script the =Scripted forms= table of [[id:A8E9807F-1BC8-4DA5-9A66-720D2444DA1B][the trade type catalogue]] names |
| scripted only | a gamma swap, a corridor variance swap | a ScriptedTrade over its library script |

A product with both forms states the same economic terms in either; booking it
as scripted maps its terms onto the script's inputs, as
[[id:206312C0-6398-4B9D-86F8-D8AEC9A5CEAD][A twin recipe maps a native product's terms onto its script]] states. A scripted-only product's fields are its script's inputs, as
[[id:92449D70-09E2-4391-B716-A1001F22B271][A script's free variables are its inputs]] states.

** Why a person chooses

The two forms of one product price through different engines, so they cost and
behave differently: a native element may have an analytic engine, while a
script runs through ORE's scripted engines, by Monte Carlo or finite
differences, as the pricing configuration sets for the script's product tag. The
choice is shown with that cost, from the product's schema
([[id:290A1069-A1EE-4DD1-8D23-1C60F76F8949][Every product has a schema of its fields and its forms]]). The ORE engine test values both forms of every product that
has both and compares them, so a difference between them is seen.

** What is recorded

A trade records its product and the form it was booked in. A scripted trade
also records the script and version it ran on ([[id:358CC548-3892-45A1-A916-DF4A48ADED03][A scripted trade names a library script or states its own]]). Reporting and
risk group a scripted trade under its product, not under =ScriptedTrade=, which
is only its form.

** Where today's code differs

- The =Library scripts= table of [[id:A8E9807F-1BC8-4DA5-9A66-720D2444DA1B][the trade type catalogue]] lists scripts whose product a
  type of ours holds, the accumulators, the TaRF, the outperformance option,
  and the basket and rainbow variants, with no scripted form booked.
- A trade records neither its form as a choice nor its product when booked as
  scripted: a ScriptedTrade is a trade type of its own.
- A scripted-only product is booked from another product's rows, a carrier, not
  as a product of its own.
