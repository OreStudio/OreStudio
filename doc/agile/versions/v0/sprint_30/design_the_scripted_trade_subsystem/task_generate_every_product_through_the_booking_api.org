* Goal

The generator draws each product's terms against its schema, by per-field rules held as data, and books every product in every form through the booking API, so the ORE engine test proves the path a person books on.

* Acceptance

- every product is generated in every form it offers, script-only products included
- the carriers and the Script-only recipes table are removed
- barrier and strike levels are drawn a stated distance from the underlying's price, so barrier logic is exercised
- the scripts whose product has an element of its own, the accumulators, the TaRF, the outperformance option and the basket and rainbow variants, are booked in their scripted form and compared
- the ORE engine test raises no error the trade causes and compares every product's forms
