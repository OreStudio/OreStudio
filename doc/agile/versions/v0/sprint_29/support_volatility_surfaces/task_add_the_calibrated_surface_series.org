* Goal

oresmd can name a calibrated FX volatility surface as a series of its own, separate
from the =fx_option= quotes it is calibrated to, as a bootstrapped curve's
=discount= series is separate from its swap quotes. A blueprint creates the series
and its calibration alone writes it.

* Result

oresmd names a calibrated FX volatility surface:
=oresmd://fx/EUR?type=series&instrument=fx_vol_surface&quote=rate_lnvol&ccy=USD&surface_id=EURUSD_BROKER=,
whose points add =expiry= and =strike=, a delta, an ATM or an absolute level.

- =fx_vol_surface= is the first of oresmd's own instrument types, after ORE's 45;
  =is_ore_type= tells them apart, the ORE key codec refuses to write it and never
  reads it, and a compile-time check holds the schema to one row per type in order.
- The field =surface_id= is an identity field; the series identity keeps it in a
  column of its own, and the FX identity view shows it.
- A series of the type is classified as FX volatility.
- The oresmd note names the type, its field and its URI.
- Tests: 64 datum, codec, classification and identity test cases pass, three of
  them new.
