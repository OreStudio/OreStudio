* Goal

No trade holds an ORE name: every remaining column that names market data holds an oresmd address, and ORE names exist only in the ORE documents we read and write.

* Acceptance

- Credit legs, commodity legs, FX indices, bond indices, curves, underlyings and entities hold an oresmd address; the inventory of about 60 columns is in the design.
- The importer converts ORE names to addresses and the exporter converts back, both through =ore_projection=; the corpus roundtrip still loses nothing.
- A curve imports as an address built from its ORE name, and the market configuration is used only on export.
- Each family is one PR.
