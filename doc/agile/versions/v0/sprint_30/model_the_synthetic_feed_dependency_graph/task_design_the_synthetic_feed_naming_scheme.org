It captures the goal, current status, acceptance, and any notes or results.

* Goal

Every synthetic feed gets a source name that says who produces it, what asset class and instrument it is, and which subject it quotes, spelled the way oresmd spells them, so the names sort usefully and map back to the series they publish. The scheme is designed here, signed off by the owner, and written into the documentation; no feed is renamed by this task.

* Acceptance

- A knowledge page states the scheme: its parts, their spelling from oresmd, the sort order of subjects, and one name for every kind of synthetic feed the generator runs.
- The open points are settled: the instrument of a whole curve, the place of an index tenor, and how a name maps back to the oresmd series it publishes.
- The page lists today's names beside their new names, for every feed the seeded datasets publish.
- The owner signs the scheme off, recorded on the task.

* Plan

1. List every synthetic feed the seeded datasets publish, by kind and
   provider, from the system tenant: 51 FX spots, 67 curves, 14 latent vol
   surfaces and 56 vendor views.
2. Read how =oresmd= spells asset classes and instruments
   (=ores.marketdata.api/datum/schema.cpp=), and take its precedent of
   =fx_vol_surface= for a whole surface beside =fx_option= for its quotes.
3. Write the scheme as a knowledge page,
   [[id:90D85E90-E7EA-48D8-9245-313ECA3F9392][Synthetic feed naming scheme]]: what a name does, its parts and
   rules, one name for each kind, the open points with a recommendation each,
   and today's name beside the proposed name for all 188 nodes, generated from
   the database.
4. Get the owner's sign-off on the scheme and each open point, and record it
   here; then mark the page as adopted.
