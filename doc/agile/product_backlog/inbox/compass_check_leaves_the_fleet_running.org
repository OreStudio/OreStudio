This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

On brave_hopper, the first =compass check= passed its =db= step. Its
=ticks-smoke= step provisions a tenant on the running fleet. After it, all 25
services were running. The second =compass check= then failed =db= with
"there are 2 active connection(s) to ores_dev_brave_hopper", and its ctest ran
against a database it had not rebuilt. The cause is inferred from that order;
the check source was not read. Stop the fleet at the end of =ticks-smoke=, or
stop it before =db=.

* Why

A second check run in one environment reports false failures, and a reviewer
cannot trust its ctest result.
