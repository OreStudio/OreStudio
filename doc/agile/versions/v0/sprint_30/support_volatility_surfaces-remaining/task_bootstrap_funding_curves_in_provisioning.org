* Goal

Every provisioned party has its own published funding curves. A curve is derived data, so it belongs to a party and not to the system. A provisioning step creates each operational party's blueprints, bootstraps them from the raw quotes the party subscribes to, and publishes them through marketdata.curve_publish under the tenant's auto-approval. The 20 system-tenant curves are not what a party prices from. Check what else reads them before removing them.

* Acceptance

- Provisioning Acme on a fresh database ends with published funding curves for ACMCOR, ACCOUK, ACCOUS and ACCOHK, with no seed.
- Each publish names the party, the raw quotes used and the rule that closed its approval.
- With the auto-approval switch off, the curves wait for a person.
- A curve that does not bootstrap is reported by party and name, stops the step, and a retry publishes nothing twice.
- After provisioning, a regular user raises and publishes a new curve through maker-checker.
- The task records whether anything still needs the system-tenant curves.
- Each operational party has a published funding curve in all 20 configured currencies.
