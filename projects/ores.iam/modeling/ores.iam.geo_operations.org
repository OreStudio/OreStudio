Resolve an address to the country it came from.

The ranges live in =ores_geo_ip2country_tbl=, published per tenant from the
=dq.v1.ops.publish_ip2country_from_dq= dataset. The authentication records
are the caller: a session says where it came from, and an administrator who
cannot place a session cannot account for it. The lookup is served from here
rather than from =ores.geo= because =ores.geo= has no service of its own and
the address it resolves is an authentication one.
