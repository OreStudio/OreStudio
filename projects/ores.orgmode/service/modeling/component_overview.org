* Summary

=ores.orgmode.service= will be the knowledge service: it runs the indexing
step when the loaded pages change, and answers searches and page reads over
NATS for ores.http. It is a scaffold until the knowledge search story's tasks
fill it; see [[id:05FE6068-3CBD-4B93-B6AE-47A5689F1FCB][Search the knowledge base from ores.web]].
