#+title: A reader takes the newest published set at or before its as-of
#+description: Every read of a derived market object answers with the newest set published for the subject at or before the as-of that is not withdrawn, with its state and its approval.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :architecture:marketdata:publish:
#+created: 2026-11-11
#+updated: 2026-11-11

* Summary

Every read of a derived market object answers one question: which set did the party hold for this subject at this as-of? The answer is the newest set published for the subject whose as-of is at or before the asked as-of and that is not withdrawn. The read returns the set with its state, its approval and the candidate it published, and it never returns a candidate.

* Detail

** The rule

| Set | Taken for an as-of |
|-----+--------------------|
| Published (live) | at or after its own as-of |
| Superseded | at or after its own as-of and before its successor's |
| Withdrawn | never |
| Candidate | never |

A read at an as-of before any published set refuses, naming the subject and the as-of.

** What it returns

The set's points, its as-of, who published it and when, its state, the request and how it was decided, and whether the decision was a person's or a stated rule's ([[id:71146F3F-B884-4E61-BA8B-A8A0731703C6][A request closes without a person only under a stated rule]]). A forward set also returns the state of each curve it was built from.

** Who reads it

Screens, the ORE export of a run, a report's market binding ([[id:FB8F701C-E56A-49A9-AA90-1B6BD0B02092][A report binds to a published curve, not to a name]]) and the cut a run prices from ([[id:DD59B04C-DAD2-458C-B35E-3F6B0B3D6DC6][Market data cut]]). All of them read through this rule, so two readers at one as-of see one set.
