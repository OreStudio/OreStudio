* What

Decide what a row of a read-only classification list opens. RS-LIST-10 says a row opens the record page on a click. The harness baseline of 2026-10-25 finds the five read-only lists, business day convention type, currency pair classification, curve role, derivation kind and leg type, open nothing on a click, so their record checks cannot run. Either the row opens a read-only record page with its history, as RS-WRITE-33 describes, or the standard names the exception.

* Why

The harness measured every record screen before the screen pipeline changed
any, so each topic starts from a known gap and the sign off has a before and an
after. This is one of the four rules the baseline found broken.
