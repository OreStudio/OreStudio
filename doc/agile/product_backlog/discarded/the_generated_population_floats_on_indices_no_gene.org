#+description: The generated population floats on indices no generated curve covers. The 808 trades of the acme_demo provision name 21 interest rate fixing addresses, and 15 of them answer with no fixing because no curve states their index at their tenor: the seeded curve configs hold no AOA, BWP, HKD, IDR, MYR, OMR, SAR or THB curve, no JPY EYTIBOR, no PLN WIBOR, no COP IBR at a tenor and no USD SOFR at a tenor. The other 6 answer because a curve states their index. A fixing is derived from the curve of its index and tenor, so the curve is the gap. Measured at 0c5420fcb6 with doc/agile/versions/v0/sprint_30/run_a_report_with_market_data/evidence/fixing_coverage_query.sql. The same provision wrote 808 of the 1100 trades the mix states: the ACCOUK rates config is refused for a PKR KIBOR cross-currency index and the ACCOUS rates config for a forward rate agreement whose index the tenant holds no convention for.
