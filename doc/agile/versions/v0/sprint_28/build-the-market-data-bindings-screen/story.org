* Decisions

- A binding is named for what it writes, not for what it consumes. The output target is the title of the record page and the first column of the list; the source it consumes follows, and stays in the key line. Marco decided this on 2026-10-15, reversing the prototype's order, which named the page for the source and put the series in the subtitle.
- The series on the list is plain text, not a link. Marco decided this on 2026-10-15; the link misled.
- The access mark is drawn before the Edit action exists; the actions arrive with the task Add, edit and delete a binding. Marco accepted the mark standing alone on 2026-10-15.
- The Bindings card lives in the Market data area, after Synthetic, gated on =marketdata::feed_bindings:read=. Built under this story rather than left to its own task.
- The sources a party may bind are joined to its bindings in the BFF, not in the database. No service user reads the other service's tables, and this screen is not the reason to start. Which running feed holds a series stays a server read, because only the synthetic service's feed controller knows it.
- A binding's source is chosen from a record picker over a read-only store, not a choice. A choice renders every option through a translation key, and the sources are the party's own rather than a fixed enumeration the words can name.
- The title cases in the marketdata core tests get no CI job of their own. The Continuous Linux run already executes the binary, and its log names both cases, so a second way to run them would be a job to keep green for no new evidence.
- The pair store keeps one direction per pair, and the series page does not look up the reverse pair. The stored places describe the stored direction's magnitude, so the page says that the pair has no convention in the direction the series quotes, and keeps the plain format. Decided on 2026-10-18 from 179 pairs and 179 conventions with no reverse twin.
