* Goal

A person who opens the market finds one question per tab: what is the market doing now, what moves it, how every feed's ticks arrive, whether each feed follows its law, and what the loop did. The page takes the review's look: a telemetry strip, cards with a titled header and an action on the right, each setting with the number it implies under it, and figures printed without float noise. It keeps the product's tokens, and nothing on it claims a control or a model the service does not have.
