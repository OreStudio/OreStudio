The day the expiry of a commodity future is anchored to, as ORE's
=AnchorDay=: a day of the month, a number of calendar days before the month
end, a number of business days after the month start, the nth weekday, the last
weekday, or a weekly day. It is a part because its fields hold one invariant:
together they state one anchor, and ORE allows one form of it at a time. The
element is optional, so a convention fills this part at most once.
