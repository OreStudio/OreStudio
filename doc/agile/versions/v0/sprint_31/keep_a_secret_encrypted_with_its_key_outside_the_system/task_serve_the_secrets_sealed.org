* Goal

Build =ores.vault= as a Domain composite of =api=, =core=, =service= and
=client=, and put the five operations on the bus: write, describe, resolve,
delete and rotate. A write arrives sealed to the vault's transport key. A
resolve leaves sealed to the caller's registered key, and the vault chooses that
key by the subject in the verified token rather than from the request. The key
that seals every stored row is a system credential of the unit, and it reaches
no table, no file and no variable in the tree. Every resolve records the
subject, the name, the version and the time, and no operation records a value.

* Acceptance

- The component is scaffolded from four overviews and registered in the service registry, with its database role, NATS account and start order.
- write, describe, resolve, delete and rotate work over NATS, and only resolve lets a value out, sealed to the caller's registered key.
- The key that seals every stored row is a system credential of the unit, and appears in no table, file or variable of the tree.
- Every resolve appends the subject, the name, the version and the time, and no operation records a value.
