#+title: Storage of fixing observations apart from trades
#+description: Observations live with their source; a trade expands its own schedule into observed and projected fixings on load.

* Summary

An observation belongs to its source and its fixing date, and it is stored
once there. It is not written onto each trade it fixes: a popular source fixes
thousands of trades, and stamping the value on each would multiply the writes
and the corrections. Each trade keeps only its own schedule of fixing dates and
the source of each. When the trade is loaded, its schedule is expanded into
observed fixings, read from the store, and projected fixings, computed from the
market.

* Detail

** Why not on the trade

A fixing changes the state of every trade that depends on it, but the change is
derived: given the trade's schedule and the source's observations, the state
follows. Storing the value on each trade duplicates a fact that has one owner,
so a correction to the observation would have to find and rewrite every copy.
Keeping the observation with its source means a correction is one write, and
the trades pick it up when they are next read.

** Three invariants

1. *Observations are never stamped onto trades.* A trade holds its schedule,
   not its fixings.
2. *An observation's history is append-only.* An override records a new value
   and keeps the published one, so the record shows both what the source said
   and what was used.
3. *Official observations of a source form an unbroken prefix in time.* This
   is what lets a future fixing keyed in advance stay dormant until the
   fixings before it are official. See
   [[id:CA61B445-B6DC-41D3-ACF9-D3FB32D0D342][The fixing process: observation, authorisation, application and reversal]].

** Projected fixings are not observations

A projected fixing is a value computed for valuation, such as a forward rate
for a future coupon. It is not saved as an observation, or, if saved, it is
marked as a projection, so that it can never be mistaken for a published value.
See [[id:D4CA27E5-8194-470B-A422-B77C53545560][Effects of fixings on valuation, risk and P&L]].

** In ORE Studio

The market data store already follows the first two invariants. A fixing is
stored once in the fixing series of its index, under the system party, and a
correction is a new version of the row; trades name the index's address and
read the values at valuation. See
[[id:F4BBEF34-D84C-4AE6-A3AA-DC47C3EE06EE][Public publishers publish daily fixings through a supplier session]]
and [[id:78B65BF0-C003-48F6-84E3-615A36D39266][Lineage and provenance]]. The third, the official prefix,
needs an authorisation status the store does not hold yet; see
[[id:2400E0FE-AEF0-4F9A-8392-F7D2E0E2A1E2][A data model for fixings]].
