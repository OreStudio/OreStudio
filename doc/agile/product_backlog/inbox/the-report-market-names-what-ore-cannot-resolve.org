* What

A report run prices a trade from the market data it names. When an index the trade
floats on has no fixing in that market, ORE refuses the trade and says which index
and which date it wanted, so the refusal is a precise statement about the market
the run carried.

Round four of the smokehouse tenant priced two of its three trades and refused the
swap:

#+begin_example
2nd leg: OvernightIndexedCouponPricer: missing GBP-SONIAON Actual/365 (Fixed)
fixing for fixing date November 24th, 2026.
#+end_example

Round five, on the tree that states every fixing under its administrator, prices
all three trades and records no market-data gap. The swap floats on
=GBP-LIBOR-12M=, not on an overnight index, and its ORE package carries 520
fixings for it. Its first period starts on the valuation date, so it needs no
fixing before them.

* Why the trade that does need one would still fail

Two defects the run exposed outlive it. Neither is exercised by this tenant while
its swap floats on an index whose fixings the store holds.

The first is the source. No source states an overnight index in a synthetic run:
every fixing sits under its administrator now, and the administrators that also
have a public adapter have no simulated source, because that adapter is not
installed. It is
[[id:7D79E648-0905-4FA7-81E5-42FC781A7DCB][State the overnight indices a
synthetic run needs]].

The second is the derivation. A fixing series is derived from the party's spot
series by cancelling one base against the other, so a party whose book spans
several bases cancels nothing and every fixing is dropped:

#+begin_example
The simulated-fixings fixing series of party <id> quote against EUR and NZD at
once, so no one base cancels; it is left out.
#+end_example

It is [[id:DD7F623E-7CB3-4C6A-AB99-6C722D14A241][Fix the fixing a party's spot
book hides]].

* A third defect the rebase exposed

The subscription step routes a party by the asset classes of the provider's
catalogue, but a route names a feed and a feed serves only its own scopes. Because
the =derived= provider now maps interest rate term indices as well as FX
volatility quotes, the step asks for an =ir= route on an FX feed and the store
refuses it, and the provision records a warning. It is
[[id:537E5CFC-4B41-468B-9B38-C931E489A683][Route a party only to the classes its
feed serves]].
