#+title: Rebuild business days when their inputs change, and read them by date range
#+description: Business days are regenerated only on request with no record of when, and they cannot be read for one year.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Writing a calendar rule, exception or base does not regenerate =calendar_date=; only =refdata.v1.calendar_dates.regenerate= does, and nothing records when a calendar was last rebuilt. The =calendar_dates_filter= takes calendar codes only, so a screen showing one year pages through every materialised date, and a side-by-side view of two calendars doubles that. Rebuild on input change (or record a rebuilt-at time), and add a date range to the filter.

* Why

Every consumer of business days reads stale dates after an edit until someone rebuilds, and the calendar screen cannot show a year cheaply.
