* Goal

The system tenant simulates FX volatility quotes as if they were public market data: a latent surface, struck against the simulator's own spot and forward curves, quoted by vendor contributors with their own noise. Each party receives those quotes by subscription and calibrates and publishes its own surfaces through the blueprint process, as it bootstraps its curves, so replacing the simulated quotes with a real vendor's changes data, not code.

* Acceptance

- Every catalogued USD pair's latent surface is quoted along the blueprint ladder by the vendor contributors, seeded by the derived source. The crosses' surfaces are implied from these by correlation, in the correlation story.
- The system blueprints and every party's copy read vendor contributor series; no tenant subscribes to the derived source.
- A provisioned party's surfaces agree with the latent surface within the contributors' noise, and with the forwards they are struck against.
- Nothing a party reads names the generator: its feeds, providers and series are the vendors'.

* Decisions

- *The clean contributor is demo.* The publish accepts only known provider
  codes, and the demo notation writes no volatility id, so no adapter is
  offered a demo point and no tenant subscribes to it. It records the latent
  surface as market ticks.
- *A latent surface quotes in its pair's stored convention.* Each expiry takes
  the convention the pair's =BASE-QUOTE-FXOPTION= row states for its tenor,
  through reference data's mapper, which the calibration also reads. A pair with
  no stored convention is not quoted.
- *ICE and Tradeweb name a volatility point in their dot form*,
  =EURUSD.VOL.25RR.1Y=, on the bid field, beside the pillar form they share.

- *A cross's surface comes from correlation, not from its own spot.* The task
  that quotes the crosses moved to the story [[id:91E40364-DB1A-4EA4-AE99-4904849AED22][Give each party its correlation market data, end to end]]: a cross's
  latent surface is implied from its legs' surfaces and the correlation the
  simulator quotes, so it waits on those quotes. This story quotes the USD pairs.
