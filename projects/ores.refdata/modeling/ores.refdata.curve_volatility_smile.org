ORE's =volatilityStrikeSurfaceConfig=, =volatilityDeltaSurfaceConfig= and
=volatilityMoneynessSurfaceConfig= each state an optional
=ParametricSmileConfiguration=: the calibration the surface's smile is fitted
with. It belongs to the kind, not to one surface type, because the kind row is
what the parts share -- a kind is one of the seven, and at most one of them
states a smile. A kind that states none has no row, which is the conditional the
schema cannot state.

The parameters are a list the document writes in order, so they are a part of
their own keyed by this row.
