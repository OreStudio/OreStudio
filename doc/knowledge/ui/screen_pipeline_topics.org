* Summary

The 63 Qt reference data entities of the
[[id:C82ED7AD-7E13-46FA-B7E9-C4E66E823478][Qt to web reference data matrix]] fall
into eleven topics. A topic is the unit the screen pipeline takes: its journeys
are written, its screens are prototyped and signed off, built, tested and signed
off, and then the next topic starts. Calendars go first, by the owner's choice,
because the family has parts, a derive action, real field gaps and a missing
dates view, so it exercises the whole shape of the pipeline. Each later topic is
a capture in the next bucket, tagged =pipeline=, and becomes a story when its
turn comes. The curve bootstrap configuration leaves the reference data pipeline
for the market data area.

A topic is judged before its journeys are written. Most are standard: the
record screen standard covers them with its list, record page and dialogs. A
few need a workflow beside the standard screens, where the goal has steps and
several writes, such as onboarding a party. The judgement here is a first
reading, and the journeys of each topic confirm or change it.

** How a topic was judged

- /Standard./ The goal is to find, read, add, amend or delete records of one
  kind, or of one kind with its parts. The record screen standard covers it, and
  the entity screen specification where the subject is several kinds of record.
- /Workflow./ The goal has steps, several writes and a decision a person reads
  first, such as onboarding a party or deciding a convention change. The screen
  offers it under Workflows, as the standard says, beside the standard screens
  of the same records.
- /Decision first./ The entity's place in the product is open, as for the
  currency risk configurations, so a decision precedes any screen.
- /Elsewhere./ The web already serves the goal in another area under another
  standard, as for the curve bootstrap configuration.

The judgement names the shape the pipeline starts from. The journeys of a topic
are written first, and they confirm or change it. A journey that turns out to be
a CRUD variation stays a standard screen; a journey that turns out to have steps
becomes a workflow.

** What each topic produces

The pipeline runbook of the fourth story states the steps. Each topic produces
its user journey documents, a prototype per screen with its design note, the
screens built from the shared parts with any server side extension, a test
scenario, and a sign off recorded on the task that owns the screen. The matrix
page is re-read at the start of each topic for the gaps it carries, and the
audit page for the rules its screens must follow.
