#+title: An FX-implied curve is backed out from a discount curve and forward points
#+description: Covered interest parity in reverse: a discount curve and outrights give an FX-implied curve in the other currency.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

Parity runs in both directions. A discount curve in one currency, spot and the outrights give an /FX-implied/ curve in the other currency.

* Detail

With $s$ the spot date:

$$\frac{D_q(T)}{D_q(s)} = \frac{S}{F(T)} \times \frac{D_b(T)}{D_b(s)}$$

This curve holds the cross-currency basis, so it is not the same as the other currency's own OIS curve. QuantLib bootstraps such a curve with =FxSwapRateHelper=, and ORE builds =SEK-IN-USD= from FX forward and basis quotes.
