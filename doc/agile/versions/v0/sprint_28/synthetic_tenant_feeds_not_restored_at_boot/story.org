* Goal

After a services restart every tenant's auto-start feeds run again, so ingest
stores ticks and the plots fill. Before, only the 24 system-tenant feeds ran and
the 54 ACME auto-start feeds did not.

* Acceptance

- After services stop/start, the auto-start feeds of the ACME tenant run.

- System feeds start once.

* Decisions

- The boot walk visits each active tenant through a context on the tenant's
  root party. A tenant context alone is not enough: the party policy hides a
  party's collections from a context with no visible party set.
- The 13 vintage-priced ACME FX feeds are out of scope. They need tenant
  authority at boot that the service token does not have.

* Out of scope

- The 13 vintage-priced ACME FX feeds. They need a tenant-scoped way to read
  series at boot.
