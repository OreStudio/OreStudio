A fresh environment provisions the system and Acme to completion, every step
of it, and each party holds every convention its trades and curves name.

The conventions reach every tenant one way: the refdata.conventions DQ
dataset, a system dataset curated in
=projects/ores.seeder/datasets/conventions/= with a methodology, a source for
each row, a place in the bundles of the system party and of every party, and
declared dependencies from each dataset that names a convention.

  dataset that names a convention depends on it.
