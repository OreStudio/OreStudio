* Decisions

- Currency groups and CRM topologies stay separate. A group is a tenant-level set of currencies. A topology is a per-party graph of pairs with a pivot, and its currencies do not match any group. The CRM gets no foreign key to a group.
- The groups and their members publish as two DQ datasets. The members dataset needs the groups dataset first.

* Out of scope

- Seeding a CRM topology from a group's members.
- Changes to the web screens beyond checking that an Acme user sees the groups.
