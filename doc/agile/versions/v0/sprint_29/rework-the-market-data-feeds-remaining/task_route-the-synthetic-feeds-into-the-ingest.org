* Notes

** The diagnosis

The IR feeds did publish, and the ingest did store their ticks, but only for a
party a route named. Two things were missing, and each one hid the other.

- The seeded USD SOFR ladder series belongs to the system tenant's system party.
  No route named a source for that party, so the ingest found no consumer to
  store the tick for it and the series held no point. The check therefore
  answered =no observed rate for pillar '3M' in the raw grid=.
- A feed a person starts by hand was built with no adapter target, so it
  published a =market_tick= on =synthetic.v1.ops.tick.<source_name>=. No source
  and no route names that subject, so the ingest dropped every tick as an
  unbound source, while the start reported success and the feed's log named that
  subject on every batch. The feed's log came from the producer subject the feed
  does not use for a served pillar, which is what made a stored tick and a
  dropped one look the same in the journal.

** What the fix rests on

The adapter contract, in both places. A feed publishes as a source adapter, on
the ingress subject the ingest maps, stores and delivers for every routed party;
and a source and a route are what make demand start an adapter and the ingest
accept its tick. Neither path has a branch for interest rates or for the
simulator.

** What the new test pins

=feeds_publish_subject_tests.cpp= asserts that every subject a synthetic feed
publishes on is one the ingest consumes: the producer subject of a feed with no
adapter, and the adapter ingress of a feed that serves one. The ingest's two
families are =ingest_subject_wildcards()= in the market data API, which the
loop subscribes to and the test reads, so a change to either side fails there.

** The smoke check

=compass smoke ticks --fresh= passed: the tenant provisioned, =generate_trades=
completed for every entity, and 38,096 ticks arrived. The abort the previous
session saw was a stale binary: the synthetic service registers
=synthetic.v1.trade_generation_configs.list= (its journal shows the subscription
on every start), and the current build runs the step. No registration is
missing.

* Result

Synthetic-provider ticks reach the store with oresmd identities, and the seeded
blueprint reads them. The system party's subscription and route make the ladder
series tick; a feed started by hand publishes as its provider's adapter; and one
test holds the publish subjects to the families the ingest subscribes to.

Live, on a recreated and provisioned environment:

- The ladder series stored 60 ticks over 12 datum URIs, each
  =oresmd://ir/USD?type=quote&instrument=ir_swap&quote=rate&fwd_start=0D&tenor=1D&term=<tenor>=.
- The check returned 7 points for the seeded blueprint, and 7 for an Acme-owned
  blueprint on the same stored quotes.
- A raise, an approval and a publish completed between two people of one party,
  and the published set read back with =point_count= 7.
- =compass smoke ticks --fresh= passed.

Evidence: =evidence/synthetic_feeds_ingest_diagnosis.txt=,
=evidence/synthetic_feeds_ingest_live_pass.txt= and
=evidence/live_pass_curve.sql=.

** What this leaves open

A tick is still stored once for each party that routes to its source, so the
same quote reaches the store as one series for each party. The story decides one
tick for each source, owned by the system tenant, in
[[id:D71F9446-C62E-48A6-A87B-136EF598A59E][A source tick is stored once and entitled when it is read]]; the build task for
that decision is still open, and this task did not take it.
