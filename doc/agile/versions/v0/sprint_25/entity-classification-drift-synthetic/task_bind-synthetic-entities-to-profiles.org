- Every entity discovered via the synthetic catalogue entry is surveyed against all Variability Profiles.
- Every matching entity is rebound and regenerated with zero additional diff versus its pre-rebind checked-in output.
- No-match entities are recorded with their actual feature values, not silently dropped.

- Full local build and ctest green after regeneration.

* Plan

Unit plan, written at start on 2026-09-07. The canonical method is the
codegen-fix-drift skill (steps 1-8); the byproduct-sweep and two-mode
drift rules of the regen-byproduct-hygiene memory are in force
throughout.

- *Phase 1 — baseline and survey (skill steps 1-2).* Probe done (see
  Notes); survey every model file, classify by metatype, compute
  resolved features per entity.
- *Phase 2 — resolved features and binding (skill steps 3-4).* Bind
  matches with =:profile:=, record every no-match with its actual
  feature values.
- *Phase 3 — per-entity regeneration (skill step 5).* One entity at a
  time through the scripted pipeline: baseline regen, bind, regen,
  require the new diff to be an exact subset of the baseline.
  Regenerate a second time and require byte-identical output.
- *Phase 4 — generated-code review, build, tests (skill step 6).*
- *Phase 5 — registrar wiring check, gate extension, PR (skill steps
  7-8).* The history-provider families materialized by the baseline
  probe must be composed into the registrar fan-out (or recorded as
  deliberately unwired); then the registry entry and the =--all= run.
