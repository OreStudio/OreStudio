* Goal

A party's history shows every change to the party and to everything attached to it: its identifiers, contacts, business units, countries, currencies and counterparties, each with who made it and why, and says nothing is missing because nothing is.

* Acceptance

- A history provider finds a row by its whole key, so the history of one party's contact is never another party's.
- A link table has a history read, a history provider and a field mapper from its model, and a closed link is still in its history.
- The party timeline shows the opening and closing of a country, a currency and a counterparty link, and states no gap for them.
- The same holds for any entity with a composite key or a link, not only parties.

* Decisions

- A history provider reads a row by its own id when the key the model declares is shared by rows of other scopes, such as a contact type every party holds. A composite string is never sent as an address.
- A link table is a junction model with =:history: true=. Its history is read by the parent, closed links included, and each version names its link as =parent/child= in its =ID= field.
- A link is closed by ending its validity, and it records who closed it, so a Closed entry names its author like any other.
- A junction that states =:events: true= announces its changes. A close is announced as a deletion only when no newer version of the link replaces it, and a link closed twice in one transaction is told once.
- A junction states a history and events only where its component can serve them. Each model that does not says why.

* Out of scope

- A history and events for the junctions of the assets, compute and reporting components, which serve no generic history.
- Change events for the calendar date junction, whose key is a date, and for the inbox and marketdata junctions, whose components compose no events yet.

* Result

A party's whole story is read: its parts, its links and their closings. A person's story shows the roles taken away and the parties left. Both name the account behind a closing.
