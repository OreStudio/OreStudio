The workflow steps that publish the configuration documents analytics owns from a
DQ dataset: the pricing engines document and the today's market document. Each is
a trigger, as the reporting component's publish steps are. The DQ publisher sends
it and reads no reply, so a step states a subject and no response, and its body is
the DQ artefact the server-side function knows how to expand.

The SQL function a step calls is derived from its subject, so the spelling of a
subject matters twice: once as the subject the registrar subscribes and once as the
function the handler calls. That is why each subject is declared here and
referenced, never spelled out at a call site.
