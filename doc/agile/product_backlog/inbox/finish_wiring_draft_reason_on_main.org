* What

Main carries the =draft_reason= entity (commit =5ff1c72221=) without its
wiring. Six local checks fail on it:

- =handler-permissions=: =trading::draft_reasons:read=, =:write= and =:delete=
  are checked but never seeded.
- =sql-reachability= and =er-diagram=: =trading_draft_reasons_drop.sql= and
  =trading_draft_reasons_notify_trigger_drop.sql= are in no aggregator.
- =tangle-shell= and =recipe-inventories=: its shell scripts and the shell
  recipe inventory are not committed.
- =codegen-tests=: the trading shell aggregator renders =draft_reason_commands=,
  which =test_the_aggregator_lists_every_opted_in_trading_unit= does not expect.
  It fails with main's model, so the reflect-cpp branch did not cause it.

Its C++ files were also missing from the CMake component lists. PR #4278 fixes
that part.

* Why

Every branch's local gate fails on these checks until main finishes the entity.
