* Result

Implemented =libor_market_model_process=: a forward-LIBOR engine under the
spot measure with displacements. The drift is the public
=lmm_spot_measure_drift= free function, mirroring QuantLib's
=LMMDriftCalculator= semantics (numeraire 0); correlated shocks come from
the Eigen Cholesky factor of =diag(sigma) * rho * diag(sigma)=.
=discount_factor()= is the spot-measure rollover discount. 15 Catch2 tests
covering the drift formula against hand-computed vectors, the spot-measure
martingale property and its measured Euler bias (statistical), caplet
repricing against Black's formula (statistical), drift sign and correlation
effects, positivity with displacement, zero-vol frozen rates, seed
determinism and validation. Registered in =component_files.cmake=.

All 368 =ores.analytics.quant= tests pass on Linux. Not yet wired into
=process_factory= (deferred to PR 2: system infra wiring).
