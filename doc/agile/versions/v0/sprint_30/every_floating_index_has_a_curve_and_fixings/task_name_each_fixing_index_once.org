* Goal

ORE Studio names market data by market convention, and ORE's peculiarities are
mapped where data enters or leaves ORE. Three overnight indices break this. The
conventions and the public mappings name two of them by ORE's spellings,
=ESTER= and =TONAR=, while the synthetic curves name them =ESTR= and =TONA=; the
synthetic curves name Banco de México's funding rate =TIIE=, the name of its
term rates, while the mapping names it =FTIIE=. Each index has two fixing
addresses, so an OIS
trade on ORE's spelling finds no simulated history, and a public value cannot
stand over a simulated one. Name each index by its market name, map ORE's
spelling in the ORE index codec, and add a check that refuses a second address.

* Acceptance

- The ORE index codec reads =EUR-ESTER= and =JPY-TONAR= as =ESTR= and =TONA=,
  and writes them back as ORE names them; the codec tests prove the round trip.
- The conventions, the public mappings and the synthetic curves name each index
  by its market name; the overnight indices of HUF and ILS have conventions.
- A check fails when a synthetic curve states a fixing address that no
  convention or mapping names.
- On a fresh provision, no overnight index has two fixing addresses.

* Plan

1. *The mapping in the codec.* =ore_index_codec= holds a table of the indices
   whose ORE name differs from the market's: per currency, ORE's name and the
   market's. =read= returns the market name, =write= the ORE name; an index
   with no row keeps one name. Rows: EUR =ESTER= / =ESTR=; JPY =TONAR= /
   =TONA=.
2. *The data follows.* Regenerate =oresmd_index_map.tsv= from the codec and the
   conventions artefact from it; the OreStudio alias conventions =EUR-ESTR= and
   =JPY-TONA= state the market addresses; the public mappings of the ECB, the
   BoJ and Banxico name =ESTR=, =TONA= and =FTIIE=.
3. *The synthetic families.* Rename =tiie=, whose overnight curve is F-TIIE, to
   =ftiie=. =estr= and =tona= are already the market names.
4. *Conventions for every curve index.* Add overnight index conventions for
   =HUF-HUFONIA= and =ILS-SHIR=, so ORE reads every index a curve projects.
5. *The check.* A pgTAP test fails when a synthetic curve's fixing address is
   named by no convention or mapping.
6. *Proof.* Codec, seeder and pgTAP tests; a fresh provision with no overnight
   index on two series.

Out of scope here: ORE's overnight alias =MXN-TIIE=, from its Python notebook
conventions. It is the only convention the TIIE term indices (=MXN-TIIE-28D=
and the rest) resolve through, so mapping it to =FTIIE= leaves them undefined;
the fix is IborIndex conventions of their own, which change how ORE builds
those indices; see [[id:E018113F-F2C6-4723-8376-08F09E0C4BF0][TIIE term indices without conventions of their own]]. Also out of scope: other ORE peculiarities, such as the FX fixing source label
=TR20H=, follow the same rule in their own tasks.

* Result

Each overnight index has one name in ORE Studio, its market name. On a fresh
provision, each of the 28 currencies with an overnight curve has one overnight
fixing address. No series is held at an ORE spelling.

- The ORE index codec maps =EUR-ESTER= to =ESTR= and =JPY-TONAR= to =TONA= on
  read, and back on write. The codec tests prove the round trip.
- The index map changes three of its 604 rows. The conventions seed, the ECB and
  BoJ public mappings, and the alias conventions =EUR-ESTR= and =JPY-TONA= now
  state the market addresses.
- The synthetic family =tiie= is now =ftiie=, so the MXN funding curve states
  its fixings at =FTIIE=, as the Banxico mapping does. The vendor identifiers
  follow.
- New overnight index conventions =HUF-HUFONIA= (ACT/360, per the MNB HUFONIA
  swap index regulation) and =ILS-SHIR= (ACT/365.FIXED, per market convention
  tables; the Bank of Israel states no day count).
- The pgTAP test =synthetic_curve_fixing_addresses_test.sql= fails when a
  synthetic curve states a fixing address that no convention or mapping names.

Not done: ORE's overnight alias =MXN-TIIE= still reads as =TIIE=. The TIIE term
indices resolve only through it, so mapping it waits on the capture
[[id:E018113F-F2C6-4723-8376-08F09E0C4BF0][TIIE term indices without conventions of their own]].

Two series per address remain for ESTR, TONA, FTIIE and others: a public
publisher and the simulated history. One producer per address is the subject of
the task Generate simulated fixings through the simulator adapter.
