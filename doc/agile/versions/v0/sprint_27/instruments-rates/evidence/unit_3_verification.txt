case; it is 954 in 141 with it.

7. First build attempt: the three errors it found
-------------------------------------------------

The first build failed with three -Werror,-Wunused-parameter errors, all in
projects/ores.ore/core/src/domain/swap_instrument_mapper.cpp: the reverse
mappers for the plain swap, the cap/floor and the callable swap took the
product's fact row and no longer read it, because the fields they used to
read moved to the header. The parameters are marked [[maybe_unused]], the
codebase's established form, and the second build is the exit-0 one above.
