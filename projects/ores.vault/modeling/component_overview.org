* Summary

=ores.vault= keeps the secrets ORE must present to another party. A secret is
sealed under a key that lives outside the database, the key is a system
credential of the service's unit, and a consumer receives the secret sealed to
its own key rather than in the clear. The component decides no policy: IAM
issues the permission code a caller must hold, and the vault records each use.

* Inputs

- A sealed payload to write, which only the vault's transport key opens.
- The name of a secret to resolve, and the subject of the caller asking for it.
- The key encryption key and the vault's transport key, from system credentials.

* Outputs

- A sealed row: the nonce, the tag, the ciphertext and the key version.
- A sealed answer, bound to the caller's registered key.
- One audit record per resolve, holding the subject, the name and the version.

* Entry points

- =ores.vault.service= — the runnable host, on =vault.v1.ops.*=.
- =ores.vault.client= — the facade a consumer links.
