#+description: The grid screen shows counts of batches, workunits and results and no row of any of them. The journeys Browse the grid's jobs and Follow a job are written and prototyped. * What

The operations group builds the installation's screens: the services, the
grid, the bus, the logs and the versions. Its grid screen is the system
administrator's, offered in system administration mode alone, and it shows
the fleet and the installation's work counts.

Nothing serves anyone a list of the workunits, batches and results themselves.
The grid screen counts them. The journeys that define the screens exist:
[[id:952AE445-1470-40D1-8824-9618DF6A73EF][Extract the ores.compute user journeys]]
holds the extraction, and =doc/knowledge/journeys/compute/= holds Browse the
grid's jobs and Follow a job, each with a wireframe and a clickable prototype
under =doc/prototypes/compute/=. The wire protocol types for the compute reads
are generated, and no BFF route calls them.

* Why

The tenant administrator's question — "what is my tenant running?" — has no
screen, and neither has the operator's "where is this job?". The jobs tables
are per-tenant and the journeys are written, so the BFF routes and the screens
are the missing pieces. The run statistics of Draw the node heatmap and carry
the engine run statistics feed the timing and size columns of those screens.
