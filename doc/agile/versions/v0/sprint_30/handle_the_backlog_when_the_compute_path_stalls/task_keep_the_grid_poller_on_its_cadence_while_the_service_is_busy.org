#+title: Task: Keep the grid poller on its cadence while the service is busy
#+description: compute_grid_poller runs poll_once synchronously on the io_context and only arms its steady timer afterwards, so any blocking work elsewhere in the service pushes the sample interval out. In the incident the chart lost every bucket between samples. Sample on a timer that measures from the previous expiry rather than from the end of the work, and run the sampling where a busy executor cannot delay it.

* Result

=compute_grid_poller= expires its timer at the next point on a fixed
steady-clock schedule instead of after each read, so a slow read no longer
displaces the samples that follow it, and an interval a read overran is
skipped rather than caught up on.

PR #4881. =compass check --exclude-heavy=: 28/28 passed and the compute
service unit builds clean. The cadence is verified against the incident's
timings by reading the loop; no unit test covers the scheduling arithmetic.
The bridge and the lease reaper still measure from the end of their work,
which the chart does not show.
