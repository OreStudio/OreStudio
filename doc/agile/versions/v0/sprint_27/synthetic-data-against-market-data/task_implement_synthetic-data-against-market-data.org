This page documents a [[id:31C868B9-306E-4282-BA8C-07E647021B34][task]] in the [[id:18458C9D-FF7C-4CFE-AB13-7E5EEBAB8408][Implement synthetic data generation against the new market data model]] story. It captures the goal, current status, acceptance, and any notes or results.

* Goal

Prove and complete the path from a synthetic feed's tick to a row the new
market data model owns, and fix what does not hold. The feed already speaks
oresmd and can auto-create a =feed_binding=; the work is to show, end to end,
that its series is registered under the oresmd series identity, that each
observation lands under the right tenant, party, source and point identity,
that a reader can tell the data was generated rather than observed, and that a
consumer such as the curve bootstrap reads it and says where it came from.

* Acceptance

- A synthetic feed ticks and a market_series row exists whose oresmd_uri is the series identity the feed publishes, registered before any observation lands.
- Every observation the feed publishes reaches market_observation with its point identity in the oresmd URI, its source, and provenance that says generated rather than observed.
- The path is exercised end to end against a running service: start a feed, see the series and observations appear, and read them back by oresmd URI.
- The curve bootstrap consumes a synthetic feed's observations the same way it consumes market observations, and the resulting points record the synthetic provenance.
- The gaps found are fixed or captured as their own tasks, and the story states what a workspace-scoped or scenario feed would still need.
- IR and FX run one pipeline: the same feed abstraction, tick, binding, producer kind, ingest and persistence. Any remaining difference is the datum, the config fields or the process, and the fork inventory justifies every one of them.

* Plan

** What exists today, traced

- A feed publishes a =market_tick= (=oresmd_uri= quote URI, =value=,
  =observation_time=, =source=) on =synthetic.v1.tick.<source>= (or the
  sandbox prefix).
- =ores::marketdata::service::app::feed_ingest_loop= subscribes to every
  producer's ticks. =refresh()= caches the enabled =feed_binding=s by source;
  each binding is one consumer. =persist()= runs per binding:
  - derives the series URI with =oresmd_uri_codec::write(series_of(datum))=;
  - creates the =market_series= row if the tenant+party+series URI has none,
    with =series_subclass= from =series_classifier= and
    =market_series_asset_class= rows;
  - writes one =market_observation= with =series_id=, =observation_datetime=,
    =value=, =source= and the point =oresmd_uri=;
  - drops a tick from a source with no enabled binding, one warning per source.
- The synthetic =feed_controller= auto-creates the =feed_binding= for a bound
  feed, and a sandboxed feed never gets one.

So acceptance 1 and most of 2 already hold on paper. What is missing is proof
and the provenance axis.

** Gaps

1. *Nothing says generated.* =market_series.derivation_kind= defaults to
   =OBSERVED=, and the =OBSERVED= seed's own description says it is the default
   "for every existing producer (synthetic, vendor feeds)". The model therefore
   treats a synthetic series as observed, because *observed* currently means
   "directly published, not derived", not "real". There is no axis that says
   real versus generated. =market_observation= carries only =source=.
2. *No test proves the persist path.* =app_tick_plan_tests= covers routing, and
   =feed_controller_tests= covers start/stop, but nothing drives a tick through
   =persist()= and reads back the rows.

** Decisions

**D1. Provenance is a second axis, not =derivation_kind=. Decided.** =derivation_kind=
answers "was this published or computed?" (=OBSERVED= vs =IR_CURVE_BOOTSTRAP=,
=CRM_DERIVATION=), and a synthetic feed is genuinely published, so calling it a
derivation would break that meaning. Real-versus-generated is a separate axis
about the *producer*, carried by the =feed_binding= and readable from the
series an observation belongs to. Decided: a =producer_kind= *reference-data
code table* (=VENDOR=, =SYNTHETIC=) alongside =derivation_kind=, referenced by
=feed_binding= and =market_series=, listable in the UI and validated by the
seed rather than free text.

**D2. One pipeline, asset class only where it is real. Decided.** The
generator pipeline is common to every asset class, and IR and FX must not have
separate paths with spurious differences. An asset class may vary only where
the difference is genuine: the shape of its datum, its config fields, and the
process that produces its numbers. Everything around that — the feed
abstraction, the tick, the oresmd identity, the subject, start and stop, the
producer kind, the ingest, the provenance, the series and observation write —
is one path parameterised by the config, not a branch per asset class. Any
existing FX/IR fork in the pipeline is either justified against this rule or
removed.

**D3. Work in slices.** Slice 0 is done: the fork inventory is at
=evidence/asset-class-fork-inventory.org= — 24 forks, 13 spurious, 11 genuine.
It changed the order, because it found that binding auto-creation is
unreachable from the live control plane (=feed_controller::start(ore_key, GMM)=
is FX-only with no production caller, and it is the only caller of
=ensure_feed_binding=), and that =binding_mode= is not expressible for IR, so a
sandboxed IR curve publishes on the ingested subject.

- 1. One producer subject, mode-aware for every kind: collapse the FX
  =synthetic_producer_subject(source, mode)= and the IR hardcoded
  =synthetic_tick_subject(source)=, thread =binding_mode= into the IR build
  input and through the one =add()= / =start()= path. Fixes the IR sandbox
  bug; test both subjects.
- 2. Binding creation on the one live start path, gated by =binding_mode=,
  deleting the FX-only ad-hoc start.
- 3. The =producer_kind= from D1, on the shared binding and the shared persist
  path.
- 4. The =persist()= integration test: series and observation written, an
  unbound source dropped.
- 5. Collapse the remaining spurious forks the inventory lists: the
  =feed_config_handler= kind resolution and duplicated start/stop, the
  duplicated vintage scan, the duplicated tick clock, the duplicated
  simulate/preview envelope, and the common config plumbing fields.
- 6. The curve bootstrap consumer check.

* Notes

** Slice 1 done, 2026-10-06 (=f09d43f5bf=). One =producer_subject(source,
mode)= in =ores.synthetic/api/include/ores.synthetic.api/feeds/producer_subject.hpp=
serves FX and IR; the IR build input and =make_ir_curve_feed= carry the mode,
and the auto-start path, =feed_config_handler= and =folder_feed_control_handler=
pass the configured mode instead of a default. =IFeed= gains =nats_subject()=
so the subject is assertable at the public seam. IR sandboxing is fixed.

The =ores.synthetic.service.tests= and =ores.synthetic.core.tests= binaries
refused to start while the local database schema fingerprint (=0e5e94985734f233=)
was behind the checkout (=416d79146b6d63e3=). The database was recreated on
2026-10-06 (fingerprint now =416d79146b6d63e3=) and services restarted.
=ores.synthetic.service.tests= now passes, so the renamed =feed_controller=
subject cases run and pass. =ores.synthetic.core.tests= has one unrelated
failure from the fresh database, in =feeds_fomc_curve_resolution_tests.cpp:92=:
the country import inside its fixture cannot find the =ISO_3166_1_ALPHA_2=
coding scheme, so a populate step the fixture depends on is missing after
=db recreate -k=. That is an environment gap, not this change, and it needs
capturing as its own task before the service-level tests are dependable.

** Slice 2 done, 2026-10-06 (=2e3ca06d17=). Auto-creating a =feed_binding= was
unreachable; the only caller of =ensure_feed_binding= was an FX-only
=start(ore_key, GMM...)= with no production caller. Both live paths, the
config-driven =add()= and the on-demand =start(IFeed)=, now call one
=bind_feed(source, mode, token)= after releasing the mutex. It binds when the
mode is =bound= and a caller token exists, and does nothing for a sandboxed
feed or an internal start with no session. The marketdata round-trip sits
behind a one-method =feed_binding_store= seam, so the rule is tested without a
live service. The dead path and the =should_ensure_feed_binding= two-mode
contract it needed are deleted.

Open decision from slice 2: boot auto-start passes an empty token because no
end-user session exists, so it still creates no binding. A binding for an
auto-started feed must come from provisioning, or from a service-account
identity decision about whose tenant and party the row lands in. Neither is
settled and both are product calls, so they are recorded here rather than
guessed at.

** Slice 3 done, 2026-10-06 (=08b308b968=). A =producer_kind= reference-data
table holds =VENDOR= and =SYNTHETIC=. =feed_binding= carries it, defaulting to
=VENDOR=, and the synthetic binding store sets =SYNTHETIC=. The ingest stamps
the series it creates from the binding and leaves an existing series alone.
Everything is generated from the org models, including the SQL and the shell
surface for the new entity. The codegen sweep reports that no file this branch
touched would change. All nine affected suites pass, including the generated
producer-kind eventing test.
