* Goal

Rework swaps, FRAs, caps and floors, swaptions and the other rates trade types to the instrument table rule of the trade shape investigation (section 14), as the bonds were reworked, with the data-oriented review deciding the tables.

* Acceptance

- The family header's trade_type check lists exactly the family's codes, and each product fact table checks its own.
- Every instrument row is keyed by its trade and pinned to the anchor's trade type and party by a composite foreign key.
- The family's trade types route to their tables through the trade-type catalogue, not hand-written code.
- The ORE round trip stays lossless across the family's corpus.
- The blotter's common-date view is generated, mapping each family's dates to common names; this first family creates it and each later family adds to it.

* Decisions

Distilled from =design.org=, which carries the argument and the
evidence. The design stays in this folder as the record of why these
decisions are what they are.

- *The family boundary is the asset class, not the catalogue's
  =product_type=.* The rates family is the =interest_rates= codes:
  Swap, CrossCurrencySwap, FlexiSwap, ForwardRateAgreement, CapFloor,
  Swaption, BalanceGuaranteedSwap, CallableSwap and KnockOutSwap.
  =RiskParticipationAgreement= is a credit product and
  =InflationSwap= an inflation product; the refdata asset class
  catalogue and the two domain notes say so, and =product_type= is the
  misnamed column defect 10 records.
- *One family header, named =rate_instruments=.* It carries the
  coverage check over the family's codes, the trade-activity key, the
  fields every product shares, and the =* Delete cascade= that owns the
  family's rows. It is a coverage and ownership device first: nothing
  owns the family today, so a delete leaves the legs and the children
  behind.
- *No security level.* A rates trade has no issuer and no ISIN, so
  there is no master entity to normalise the economics onto.
- *In the target model the legs are re-keyed to the trade and given their
  schedules.* =(trade_id, leg_number)= replaces the surrogate =id= as
  =bond_legs= is keyed, and amount and rate children carry the amortisation
  and step schedules the single columns cannot. =payer= has since joined the
  row; the re-key itself is deferred behind the compound-key template defect
  the capture below records.
- *=netting_set_id= leaves the instrument.* =trade_booking= already
  holds it, as a uuid foreign key where the instrument's copy is bare
  text.
- *The ORE mapping repair is the family's largest unit, not a
  follow-up.* The import never writes the legs it builds, overwrites a
  cross-currency swap's type to =Swap=, and leaves seven columns that
  exist permanently empty.
- *RPA is a credit product, and its catalogue row now says so.*
  RiskParticipationAgreement is classified =credit= and routed to
  =credit_instrument=, which is the table the importer always wrote; the
  credit check gains the code and the trade-types seed carries the class.
  The old =rpa_instruments= table is unproduced and is recorded for
  retirement rather than removed here. Reason: the =rpa.org= domain note,
  the refdata instrument codes and the importer all called it =credit=
  already, and the routing was the only source that disagreed.
- *A leg records its direction, and the import writes the legs it builds.*
  =payer= joins =swap_leg= and is mapped both ways, and the handler writes
  each leg after its instrument and fails the item when a leg fails. A
  stored swap is an instrument rather than a row.
- *The legs keep their surrogate key for now.* Re-keying =swap_legs= to
  =(trade_id, leg_number)= fails to compile a generated test: the
  compound-key path inserts each extra key column's raw domain value where
  the generated service takes every key part as text. The fix is a template
  change that regenerates every component under test, so it belongs with the
  codegen work and the re-key is recorded as a capture. Reason: a family
  rework should not carry a tree-wide regeneration behind it.
- *A leg's notionals and rates are child rows, and they cross the wire.*
  =swap_leg_amount= and =swap_leg_rate= hold the schedules; =swap_leg= no
  longer carries =notional=, =fixed_rate= or =spread=, because one column
  cannot hold a schedule. The mapper reads every entry of each ORE list
  instead of the first, and the carrier and its hand-written codec were
  extended in the same unit, because the export rebuilds a leg from the
  payload and nothing else: without that, removing the flat columns would
  have lost the leg's economics on the way out.
