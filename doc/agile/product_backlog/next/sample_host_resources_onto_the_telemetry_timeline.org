* What

Sample each fleet unit's memory and CPU from outside the services, from systemd's
=MemoryCurrent= and =CPUUsageNSec= per unit, and store them on the same time
axis as the tick counters. The services do not measure themselves: memory and
CPU are the host's concern, and systemd already accounts for every unit.

* Why

On 2026-10-26 the machine went down overnight. Two defects together caused it: the shared Hawkes heat ran the synthetic feeds at about 300 times their configured rate, and the NATS client kept every message it received. Finding both took ad hoc sampling of systemd and /proc, parsing marketdata's once-a-minute INGEST STATUS log line, NATS monitoring endpoints, Postgres row counts and a core dump. The leak was a straight line of about 195 MiB a minute in
marketdata's memory, matched to the tick count. Seeing that took a hand-written
sampler and a script to line it up with the log. With both series on one
timeline it is one chart.
