* Goal

A person opens the curve evolution screen and chooses the curve or surface from a list of the series their party can see, instead of typing its asset class, scope, instrument type, quote type and fields.

* Acceptance

- A server operation returns the typed identity of each series the caller's party can see: asset class, scope, instrument type, quote type and the other identity fields, with the owning party.
- The BFF routes that list for a session, and a session sees no series of a party it cannot read.
- The screen offers the list as a picker, filled in from the chosen row, and still lets the person type an identity.
- An empty list says so in words and does not read as an error.

* Decisions

- *The list comes from the party-isolated projection.* The series table is isolated by tenant only, and the
  identity projection is isolated by party. The lister takes visibility from the projection and the rows from
  the table. Reading the table first showed one party another party's series, which a test caught.
- *A listed identity names its series.* The identity carries the owning party, so passing it to the snapshot,
  slice or evolution read names that series and no other party's.
- *A series with no declared shape is listed and cannot be chosen.* It has no grid, so the evolution would refuse
  it. The option says so, and the picker does not hide it.
- *The list is cached under the party.* A switch of party does not clear the cache, so the key carries the party
  and the list of the party left behind is never shown.
- *The importer looks a series up by party.* The lookup was tenant-wide, which was harmless until the axes became
  party-isolated. A second party's import reused the first party's series and was refused. The earlier import
  tests ran with no party, so none could see it. The fix passes the context's party, and a test with two parties
  under row-level security failed first and now passes.
- *Tests that write a surface use a pair of their own.* The test tenant is shared across tests, so a surface one
  test imports is still there for the next.

*Left for later.*

- Provisioning seeds market series without their identity rows, so a fresh party's picker can be empty until
  =marketdata backfill-identity= runs. Capture =provisioning_projects_seeded_series_identities=.
- The synthetic IR curve feed screen, and a shape declared when a feed starts. Capture
  =synthetic_feed_declares_its_shape=. The screens belong to the synthetic implementation story.

* Risks

- *An empty picker with no error.* Market series that provisioning seeds have no identity rows until
  =marketdata backfill-identity= runs for the party, so a fresh Acme environment lists nothing for a
  party. The picker says the list is empty and why a list can be empty. The fix is the capture
  =provisioning_projects_seeded_series_identities=.
- *A cut list read as a complete one.* The list holds at most 5000 series. The picker must show the
  =truncated= flag and say to narrow by party.

* Out of scope

- Searching or filtering the list beyond what a short list needs.
- A series with no declared shape stays a refused read.
- Changing which party a person works for.
