* Result

Login with one party, party selection and party switching now read the
account's effective permissions with =authorization_service= in the tenant's
context and put them in the token. Refresh reads them again through
=refreshed_permissions= instead of copying the old list; a token whose subject
or tenant cannot be read fails the refresh. The party-choice token is unchanged
and carries none.

Proof, against the running deployment, with the probes in =evidence/=, which
sign in over NATS the way the BFF does and decode the token:

| Token | Before | After |
|-------+--------+-------|
| System administrator, login and refresh | =[]= | =*= and the nine tenant codes |
| Acme administrator, party choice pending | =[]= | =[]= |
| Acme administrator, after selection, switch and refresh | =[]= | =*= |

=refresh_rereads.mjs= grants Viewer to Acme's administrator and refreshes, and
the token gains Viewer's three read codes; it revokes Viewer and refreshes, and
they go (=evidence/refresh_rereads.out=). The build is clean with no warnings,
=ores.iam.core.tests= passes 187 of 187, and the tenant live check passes 15 of
15.

Until the next task, an empty list still passes the token check, so a token
issued before this change keeps working until its next refresh fills it.
