* What

The images search fails with Maximum Payload Exceeded. GET /api/images?offset=0&limit=50&search=eu, the read the image chooser and the flag map's placeholder lookup use, answered 400 invalid-request with 'NATS publish failed: Maximum Payload Exceeded' on brave_hopper on 2026-10-25 with Acme provisioned. The search for 'eu' matches many images and the reply, or the request, exceeds the bus's payload limit, so a person cannot pick a flag by name in the image chooser. Found while giving the TARGET calendar the EU flag through the API; the image map read, which lists every image by code, answered.

* Why

The image chooser is how a person gives a record its flag or photo; a search that
fails on a common letter pair makes the chooser unusable for the flags.
