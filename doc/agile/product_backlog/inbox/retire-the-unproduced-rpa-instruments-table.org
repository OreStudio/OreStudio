This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Retire =ores_trading_rpa_instruments_tbl= and the =ores.trading.rpa_instrument=
model behind it, once the credit family has decided where the risk
participation agreement's own facts live.

* Why

RiskParticipationAgreement routes to =credit_instrument=, so nothing writes
the =rpa_instruments= table and nothing reads it. Before the routing repair
the generated route pointed at =rpa_instruments= while the ORE importer
wrote =credit_instruments=, whose check refused the code: the import was
refused and the export found no row to rebuild from.

The table's own columns — =reference_counterparty=, =participation_rate=
and =protection_fee= — were never written by any mapper, and the credit
mapper does not fill them either. They are the credit family's RPA facts,
so the credit family decides whether they get a home or are not persisted.

Retiring a generated entity is its own unit: it touches the model, the
generated C++ and SQL, the shell command unit and its recipe, and the
=rpa_instrument= leaf of the rates wire variant, and no tool prunes the
files it leaves behind.
