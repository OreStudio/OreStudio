* Goal

Every provisioned party has published FX forward curves. A step of its own, after the funding curve step, publishes a forward set for every supported USD pair the party holds and every cross with a convention, under the tenant's auto-approval, and records which pairs it published and why it skipped any.

* Acceptance

- Provisioning Acme on a fresh database ends with published forward sets, with provenance, for each party's USD pairs that have a spot, a convention and two funding curves.
- The step's result names each party and pair published and each one skipped, with the reason.
- The forward step cannot start before the funding curve step has completed.
- A retry publishes no set twice.
- Crosses are published for every convention without a USD leg whose two currencies have curves, which is up to 26 pairs in Acme, and no other crosses.

* Plan

A step kind =publish_party_forwards=, after =publish_party_curves=, with the same arguments: the parties it names.

- The pairs are the tenant's currency pair conventions. The step does not choose which pairs a party can build: the forward service decides that from the party's spot and published funding curves, and the step records its answer. A copy of that rule in the step would drift from the service's.
- For each party and pair, the step reads the party's published set. A pair with one is already published, so a rerun publishes nothing twice.
- Otherwise it asks =marketdata.v1.ops.publish_fx_forward= at the present instant. Under the run's auto-approval the raise closes and the set publishes in the same call.
- A pair the service refuses before raising a request is skipped, with the service's reason: a missing spot, convention or funding curve.
- A pair whose request is raised and not published, waiting for a person or refused at apply, fails the step, named with its party. A rerun is safe.
- The result names each party and pair published, already published, skipped and not published.
