* Goal

The loop records every tick, regime transition, heat jump and applied command in a bounded ring, each stamped with clock time and tau, and with the operational and wall gap for a tick. The BFF serves the trace and the scheduler's own numbers.

* Acceptance

- Recording an event takes no lock on the tick path and allocates nothing.
- A test replays a fixed-seed run on the manual clock and asserts its trace.
- The scheduler publishes ticks a second, heap size and lateness, the actual wake time less the planned one.
- The BFF route returns the trace filtered by feed and by window.

* Plan

- =service/scheduler_trace.hpp= holds =trace_event= and =trace_ring=. The
  event is trivially copyable. The ring allocates its slots once and
  overwrites the oldest, so recording is one copy and allocates nothing.
- The loop records while it already holds its own lock: a tick with the gap
  its law drew and the wall gap it took, each regime turn, each pin and
  unpin, and each add and remove. No record takes a lock of its own. A
  reader copies a filtered slice under the scheduler's lock.
- =advance_to= steps to each transition in turn, so a turn is stamped with
  the instant it happened, not the instant the loop noticed it.
- =series_spec= carries the feed's source name, so the trace names its
  series. The service filters by source name through =series_key=.
- The loop's numbers: ticks a second over each heat sample, smoothed; the
  heap size; and lateness, the time the loop fires a tick less the time it
  was due, smoothed, with the maximum since the last read.
- =synthetic.v1.ops.get_market_trace= serves a window of the trace, newest
  kept under a cap, with the numbers. The regime read also carries the
  pace, tau, the enrolled rate, the loop gain and the run seed, for the
  screen in the next task.
- The BFF serves =GET /api/synthetic/market-trace?source=&seconds=&limit==,
  and the shell has =synthetic regime trace [source|all] [seconds]=.

* Result

The scheduler now says why ticks arrived, not only that they did. Every tick,
regime turn, pin and change of the feeds is in a preallocated ring, stamped
with clock time and tau, and a tick carries the gap its law drew and the wall
gap it took. A test replays a fixed-seed run on the manual clock and asserts
its trace exactly, across a pin. The loop reports its ticks a second, its heap
size and its lateness. The trace and the numbers reach a reader through
=synthetic.v1.ops.get_market_trace=, the BFF's =/api/synthetic/market-trace=,
and the shell's =synthetic regime trace=. On the live run a periodic feed's
ticks are exactly one unit of tau apart, and their wall gaps are one over the
pace in force.
