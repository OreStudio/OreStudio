#+title: ores.refdata.publish_operations
#+description: Codegen operation model for the workflow steps that publish a refdata dataset from DQ.
#+type: ores.codegen.operation
#+component: refdata
#+subcomponent: api
#+entity_singular: publish_operation
#+namespace: ores::refdata::messaging
#+brief: The refdata publish-from-DQ workflow steps.
#+filetags: :model:operation:refdata:
#+created: 2026-10-18
#+updated: 2026-10-18

The workflow steps that publish the tables refdata owns from a DQ dataset. They
are triggers, as the reporting and analytics components' publish steps are: the
DQ publisher sends one and reads no reply, so a step states a subject and no
response.

The SQL function a step calls is derived from its subject, so the spelling of a
subject matters twice: once as the subject the registrar subscribes and once as
the function the handler calls. That is why each subject is declared here and
referenced at the call site.

The subject is also the =target_subject= of the DQ artefact type that names it,
in =projects/ores.sql/populate/dq/dq_artefact_types_populate.sql=, so the two
are one value written twice and both move together.
