* Result

=doc/knowledge/architecture/jetstream_streams_and_consumers.org=, wired into
the reading order of [[id:4682A7D5-04FC-494B-BD7C-6816500EB3C9][Message Queue]]
after the library page.

It covers what JetStream adds against plain NATS and the three consequences that
decide where we use it; why the ticks, the workflow engine and compute
assignments need it and nothing else does; the two calls that are the whole
surface, =ensure_stream= and =js_queue_subscribe=, with the naming rules that
make a subject address the right bus; the five streams and their subject
filters with the service that creates each; the three durable consumers with
the subject each reads; and what a subject rename strands, with the command to
read a stored filter and a pointer to the memory.

Every stream, filter, consumer and default was read out of the tree rather than
recalled: the four =ensure_stream= call sites, the three =js_queue_subscribe=
call sites, =make_subject= and =make_stream_name=, and the =max_age_days=7=
default.

The gap it closes is that the cluster explained a message and a subject and
never the state that holds them, which is why the stranded-consumer failure had
to be rediscovered rather than looked up.
