* Acceptance

- sandbox and sandbox_member are generated in ores.refdata: purpose, anchor, owner, visibility, status and review date.
- A portfolio belongs to the same sandbox as its parent, or both to none, so no official portfolio contains a sandbox portfolio.
- Opening a sandbox needs the open_sandbox right at an official anchor; a function answers whether an account may see a sandbox.

* Plan

- =sandbox=: name, purpose (experiment, sample, import), anchor portfolio
  (fixed), owner account, visibility (private, shared, members), status
  (open, archived), review date. Its insert trigger refuses an anchor that
  is itself a sandbox portfolio, and asks for =open_sandbox= at the anchor
  when the sandbox is opened or changes owner; a version that keeps the
  owner, such as archiving, does not ask again.
- =sandbox_member=: a sandbox and an account, for visibility =members=.
- =portfolio.sandbox_id=: optional and fixed. The portfolio trigger keeps a
  portfolio in its parent's sandbox, or both in none, so a sandbox's
  portfolios form their own tree and no official portfolio contains one.
- =ores_refdata_account_sees_sandbox_fn=: the owner always sees a sandbox;
  anyone with =read= at the anchor sees a shared one; members see a
  members one. PL/pgSQL, with the caller's rights.
- The generated tests open a sandbox the way a user would, through seed
  snippets that create a service account and grant it =open_sandbox= at
  the seeded anchor.

* Result

- =sandbox= and =sandbox_member= are generated in =ores.refdata= and wired
  by hand like the earlier refdata entities, with tenant row-level
  security. =portfolio= gains =sandbox_id= and a list-by-sandbox read.
- In Postgres, in a transaction that rolled back, with the portfolio
  trigger live:
  - an account holding =open_sandbox= on desk A opened a sandbox anchored
    at A's sub-desk; without the right it was refused (42501);
  - an official portfolio under a sandbox one, and a sandbox portfolio
    under an official one, were refused (23514);
  - a sandbox anchored at a sandbox portfolio was refused (23514);
  - moving a portfolio out of its sandbox was refused (23514, fixed column);
  - visibility held as specified: the owner sees a private sandbox and
    another account does not; a shared one is seen with =read= at the
    anchor and not without; a members one is seen by a member and not by a
    non-member.
- The generated sandbox and member tests open a sandbox through seed
  snippets that grant =open_sandbox= first, and pass.
- After review, as the refdata service role in a transaction that rolled
  back: the owner saw the sandbox portfolio, another account and a session
  with no actor saw none, another account's write was refused (42501, row
  security), and a write into an archived sandbox was refused (23514).
- Build clean; ctest 89 of 89 on a recreated database.
