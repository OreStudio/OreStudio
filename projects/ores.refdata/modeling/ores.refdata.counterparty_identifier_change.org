** is_authoritative
:PROPERTIES:
:type:     boolean

:nullable: false
:default:  false
:END:

Whether this is the identifier the tenant resolves the counterparty by. At most
one live identifier per counterparty may hold it.

A counterparty carries identifiers under several schemes, and nothing in them
says which one decides who the counterparty is. A screen that needs one applies
a precedence of its own, LEI first and then BIC. Once the tenant can state the
choice, that precedence is what it starts from.
