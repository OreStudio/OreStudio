* Notes

- *The manager candidates come from the tree, not from a read.* The picker
  offers everybody in the shape except the person and their branch, computed
  from the same answer the tree is drawn from. The server refuses a ring too;
  the screen refusing to ask for one is what keeps the two honest rather than
  relying on the refusal.
- *The version the write states comes from the account read, not the tree.*
  The tree read answers the shape and no version, so selecting a person reads
  their account for it. That read is the administrator's ordinary account read,
  and a failure there states that the write will carry no version rather than
  pretending to have one.
- *The journey's remaining gaps are drawn, not hidden.* The story's acceptance
  is that every gap a journey records is closed or stated on the screen, so the
  panel at the foot names the three that remain rather than leaving a reader to
  find them.
- *The entry point is the people list.* The administrator arrives from the
  roster they were already reading, so the button sits on that screen's header
  rather than in the member's account menu.
