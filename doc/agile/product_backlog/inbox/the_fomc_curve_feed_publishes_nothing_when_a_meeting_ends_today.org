#+title: The FOMC-dated SOFR curve feed publishes nothing when a pillar ends today
#+description: make_pillar_quote_key refuses a spot pillar whose end date is the spot date, so the whole curve batch fails and the feed publishes no tick.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

On 2026-10-04 the synthetic feed =synthetic.2026realistic.usdsofrfomc=, the
SOFR curve with FOMC-meeting-dated pillars, published no tick. Every minute
=ir_curve_feed= logged:

#+begin_example
SYNTHETIC CURVE PUBLISH FAILED: ... pillar key: a spot pillar must end after
spot, but 2026-10-04 is not after 2026-10-04
#+end_example

=make_pillar_quote_key= refuses a spot pillar whose end date is the spot date.
The tick loop builds every pillar's key inside one =try=, so one refused pillar
drops the whole curve batch. The other 20 curve feeds ticked normally.

* Why

The feed and its five bound parties get no observations while any pillar ends
on the spot date, which here is a meeting date that falls today. Either the
template should not yield a pillar that ends on spot, or the feed should skip
that pillar and publish the rest. The defect is older than the market tick
change: the same call sat in the same loop before it.
