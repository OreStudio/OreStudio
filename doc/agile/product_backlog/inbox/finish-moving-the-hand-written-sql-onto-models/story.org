* Acceptance

- The four populate pipelines — FPML, crypto, flags and ip2country — are generated from the ores.codegen.dataset archetypes, or each is recorded with the reason it cannot be.
- Every table a codegen shape covers has a model, so its schema, drop, notify trigger and C++ come from one source.
- The tables that stay hand-written are listed with the reason, so the boundary is a statement rather than an omission.
- No hand-written table-creating SQL is left that a codegen shape could carry.
