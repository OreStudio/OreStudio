One =curveconfig.xml= document, held as the thing its curve entries hang off.
The document itself is only a list of sections, and the entries are rows of
=curve_definition=. This row is the document's identity, so that a tenant may
hold more than one and a report may choose between them through the reporting
configuration it is registered as.
