#+title: Column-list chains exhaust MSVC's template budget in a unity unit
#+description: windows-msvc-release-ninja fails C1202 at trade_entity.hpp:95 in ores.trading.core unity unit 55. MSVC probes fit the cause: a unit spends a budget of about 1024 column-list operator| calls across every entity's columns() chain, and the first chain of 18 to 20 bindings after that fails. The rule predicts 9 to 12 failing units, so the fix is in the generated columns(), not in the trade entity.

* What

The nightly of 2026-11-19 (run 44485188155, head =e866b76f3e=) failed in
=windows-msvc-release-ninja= with =fatal error C1202: recursive type or
function dependency context too complex= at =trade_entity.hpp:95=, the
trade entity's =columns()= chain, in =ores.trading.core= unity unit 55.
That unit reaches =trade_entity.hpp= after 56 other entity headers and
1,179 bindings. Unit 54 reaches it after 91 bindings and compiles.

The MSVC job built units 66 down to 55 and stopped at 55; it never
reached the lower units. Only the clang job compiled them.

compass's MSVC probes (MSVC 14.51, ORE's release flags) fit a rule that
predicts all 298 probe outcomes, including ORE's own 62 entity headers
compiled in units 53 and 55's include order:

- A unit has a budget of about 1024 column-list =operator|= calls,
  counted over every entity's =columns()= chain in include order.
- Once the budget is spent, MSVC fails at the first chain of 18 to 20
  bindings. compass is pinning the exact budget and length.
- Shorter chains never fail, and field lists (=wire_fields=) never fail
  at any count.

At =e866b76f3e= the rule predicts 9 to 12 failing units in
=ores.trading.core=: 55, and 53, 50, 48, 44, 43, 38, 36, 34 and more
below it. Units 56 to 66 compile, which matches the CI log. Fixing the
trade entity alone would only move the failure to the next unit down.

The likely fix is in the generator: emit =columns()= as one variadic
call, as =field_list= is built (for example
=make_columns<e>(key(...), bind(...)...)=), instead of a chain of
=operator|=. compass's probes are testing that form now. If it never
fails, one generator change removes the whole class. Smaller unity
batches would only move the failures.

* Why

The failure is on MSVC release alone; the Linux and Windows clang legs
compile the same tree. No MSVC Windows artefact can be built while it
stands, and every entity a model adds spends more of each unit's budget.
