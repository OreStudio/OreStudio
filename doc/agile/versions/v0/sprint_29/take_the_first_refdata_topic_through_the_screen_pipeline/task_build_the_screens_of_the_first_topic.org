* Plan

The calendars screen exists, so the build moves it to the signed off prototype
and lands each move in the shared part the standard names, so that no screen
builds a dialog of its own and every record screen gains the fix: a
=RecordTimeline= over the generic history, drawn by the shared =Timeline=,
replaces =HistoryPanel= in the History tab and in every parts panel's history
dialog; =RecordHeader= takes Refresh with its load time; =RecordDialog= and
=PartsPanel= take the load time under the title and the display field in the
title; a record field with a flag draws the flag and the name. On the calendars
screen itself: Rebuild business days becomes an operation dialog opened from
the header beside Derive, the Business days view names what produced each day
and gives the calendar's counts and the union's, and its empty state names the
action. The harness gains the check that the History tab is the timeline, so
the legacy shape cannot pass a sign off. The strings exist in English, French
and Portuguese before the screen ships.

* Result

each move landed in the shared part the standard names. =RecordTimeline=
draws a record's history as the shared =Timeline= and replaces the legacy
=HistoryPanel= in the History tab and in every parts panel's history dialog;
=RecordHeader= takes Refresh with its load time; =RecordDialog= and

display field; a record field with a flag draws the flag and the name. On
calendars, Rebuild business days is an operation dialog opened from the header
beside Derive, the Business days view names what produced each day and gives
the calendar's counts and the union's, and its empty state names the action.

calendars screen it now passes that check, the page's load time and the
dialog's title and load time, which the baseline found broken. The strings
exist in English, French and Portuguese. A test calendar, Acme.Test, derived
from TARGET, stays on the environment for the sign off task. The country is
a flag and a name; its link waits for the countries page.
