The legal entities a trade may reference, keyed by LEI. A credit trade states
the legal entity its risk is on, and until now that reference was a string a
caller typed: a credit curve named =ACME= in an engine document and an LEI were
the same column, so nothing could be joined to either.

The LEI is the key because it is the identifier the market uses and the one the
DQ dataset carries. A published dataset of 38,000 entities becomes readable by
every party through this table, which is what world data means: a legal entity
is not one tenant's private row.

The name, the jurisdiction and the status travel with the entity because they
are facts about it, and a screen that shows only an LEI shows nothing a person
can read.

This model holds the vocabulary only. Which entity a given trade references,
and whether that reference is an LEI or an internal identifier, is the trade's
statement and lives on the trade.

* Columns

** lei
:PROPERTIES:
:type:        text
:cpp_type:    std::string
:primary_key: true
:END:

The entity's Legal Entity Identifier: twenty alphanumeric characters, as the
Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation issues them.

Examples: '5493001KJTIIGC8Y1R12', '529900T8BM49AURSDO55'.

** legal_name
:PROPERTIES:
:type:        text
:cpp_type:    std::string
:END:

The entity's legal name, as its own registry states it. It is not a natural
key: two legal entities in different jurisdictions may carry the same name, and
the LEI dataset holds many that do.

#+begin_src cpp :name generator
std::string(faker::company::companyName())
#+end_src

** jurisdiction
:PROPERTIES:
:type:     text
:cpp_type: std::string
:nullable: true
:END:

The jurisdiction the entity is registered in, as the LEI dataset spells it.

#+begin_src cpp :name generator
std::string("GB")
#+end_src

** entity_status
:PROPERTIES:
:type:     text
:cpp_type: std::string
:nullable: true
:END:

Whether the entity is active, as the LEI dataset states it. A merged or retired
entity still exists and a trade may still reference it, so this is stated and
not enforced.

#+begin_src cpp :name generator
std::string("ACTIVE")
#+end_src

** entity_category
:PROPERTIES:
:type:     text
:cpp_type: std::string
:nullable: true
:END:

The category the LEI dataset states, such as a bank, a fund or a corporate.

#+end_src
