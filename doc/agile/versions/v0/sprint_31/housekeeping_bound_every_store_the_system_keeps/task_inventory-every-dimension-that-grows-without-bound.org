* Result

The inventory is written as
[[id:FEAF481D-C631-4452-9896-22E5486CF2F1][Investigation: Every dimension
that grows without bound]]. It measured the cluster and surveyed the code, and
it separates the problem into two failures: twelve hypertables and every
artefact store declare no bound at all, and the thirteen that do declare one
get nothing from it because the only mechanism is licence-gated and this host
runs the community edition. =timescaledb_information.jobs= holds zero rows.

The growth is concentrated in four stores, and the two largest are measurable
per unit of work: about 8,828 cashflow rows for each of 1234 report runs, and
1,260,554 versioned rows behind 23 delivery counters.

The recommendations order the work by what it saves rather than by what is
tidiest: carry the delivery count forward, bound cashflows by run age, decide
whether calendar dates are stored at all, then give the artefacts a lifecycle,
and put a data-configured policy and a sweep behind all of it.
