* Goal

A person sees the market as the vendors quote it: every quote of every provider they subscribe to, live and in any asset class, in one view that reads the same from any party, and every provider from the system tenant. The ingest publishes each vendor's ticks once, on a live subject per vendor, and a party reaches them through its subscriptions rather than through a copy made for it.

* Acceptance

- The market data documentation states the target state: a live subject per vendor that the ingest publishes, and the subscription as the only door to it.
- The ingest publishes each vendor's accepted ticks once, on that vendor's live subject, and no live tick is copied per party.
- Every live consumer reaches the vendor subjects through its party's subscriptions, and the system tenant reaches every vendor.
- The quotes view is designed as a journey and a prototype the product owner signs off, and is then built.
- A party sees only the providers it subscribes to, and two parties with the same subscriptions see the same quotes.

* Decisions

- *A party sees only the providers it subscribes to.* Vendor streams are paid
  for, so the quotes view keeps the subscription as the door, as the history
  read does today. Two parties with the same subscriptions see the same quotes.
  The system tenant sees every provider. (2026-11-18, product owner.)
- *The ingest publishes each vendor's ticks once, on a live subject per
  vendor.* Today the ingest publishes live ticks on party subjects,
  =market_tick.<tenant>.<party>.…=, after routing, so every live consumer sees
  the market through a copy made for one party. A party reaches a vendor's
  subject through its subscription instead. (2026-11-18, product owner.)
- *The routing comes before the screens.* The system must keep working after
  the routing tasks, so the vendor subject is added first, the consumers move
  to it, and the party subjects are removed last.
- *The view is not specific to an asset class.* Every tick is a series URI, a
  coordinate, a value and a time, so FX spot, IR pillars and vol quotes are rows
  of the same view. A layout for one class, such as the FX montage, is a later
  lens over the same rows.
- *A screen needs the display right.* A vendor licenses the non-display right
  for machines, and it does not allow a screen. So the quotes view, live and
  history, needs the display right, and a service with no screen needs the
  display or the non-display right. (2026-11-18, product owner.)
- *The ingest publishes the ticks of a feed that no party routes to, and does
  not store them.* The system tenant watches every feed, so its quotes view
  needs them. The store and the bill are unchanged, because no party receives
  them. (2026-11-19, product owner.)
- *The synthetic display tap is not the base.* =synthetic.v1.ops.feed_tick= is
  published by the simulator's FX and IR feeds only, and a real vendor never
  publishes it.
