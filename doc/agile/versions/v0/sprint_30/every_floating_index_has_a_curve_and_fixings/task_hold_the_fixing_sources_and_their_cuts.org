* Goal

Hold the fixing sources of [[id:5AF9CEEF-A878-443A-8C32-C6E9F6C26FC9][Fixing sources for option cuts and non-deliverable currencies]] as reference data: label, administrator, kind (published, keyed, derived), asset, time and zone, holiday calendar, rounding and status (active or ceased). Give the cut code its entity, as the cuts story designed it, and have each cut name its fixing source. Seed the WM/R cut sources, the central-bank FX references, the settlement rate options of the non-deliverable currencies and the interest rate indices.

* Notes

The cut and publication times are time-of-day columns: PostgreSQL =time=
without time zone, the zone in its own IANA column, and
=ores::platform::time::time_of_day= in C++. compass-ng v0.0.12 cannot render a
=:type: time= column: it derives the type flags from =:type:= only, and an
unknown type leaves the generated TypeScript type empty. The task waits on
compass v0.0.13, which carries the type.

Shapes chosen, all in =ores.refdata= with the =simple-lookup= profile of
=ores.reporting.cut_kind=: =fixing_source_kind= (published, keyed, derived),
=fixing_source_status= (active, ceased), =fixing_source= (code, name,
description, kind, asset class, administrator, publication time and zone,
calendar, rounding, status) and =cut_code= (code, name, description, cut time
and zone, fixing source); =currency_pair.default_cut_code= references the cut.
