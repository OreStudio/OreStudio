* Goal

Raw market data has one owner. The system tenant holds raw IR quotes and FX spots, as a vendor such as Bloomberg would. A party reads them through its subscriptions, and everything derived from them belongs to the party. Today the FX spots sit under each Acme party and the system tenant holds none, and no party has IR quotes.

* Acceptance

- After provisioning, the system tenant holds the raw IR quotes and FX spots, and no party holds its own copy.
- A party with a subscription reads a raw quote, and a party without one is refused, naming the subscription.
- A curve bootstrapped by a party records the raw series and observations it used.
- Every provisioned party has the subscriptions its funding curves and FX pairs need.
- A raw quote has one owner, so a change to it is visible to every subscribed party.
- Every operational party subscribes to the raw quotes of all 20 funding curve currencies and the USD spot of each.
