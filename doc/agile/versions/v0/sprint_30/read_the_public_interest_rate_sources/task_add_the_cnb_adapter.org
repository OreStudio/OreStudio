* Acceptance

- The adapter reads the Czech National Bank alone, and it does not copy the announce, the demand, the backfill or the publish code.
- The Czech National Bank is a supplier in the catalogue, its series maps to the CZK-CZEONIA item, and a session and a feed scoped to that item are seeded in the system tenant.
- A fresh environment stores the history as fixings within minutes of provisioning, and the log names the source and the count it stored.
- Each fixing is the decimal of the published percent, with the credit Source: CNB.
- The depth of the history is measured and written into the calibration sources note.
- Unit tests parse a recorded response, and a malformed response gives an error, not a fixing.

* Result

The adapter reads CZEONIA from the yearly text files the Czech National Bank
publishes, one request a calendar year, with no key. CZEONIA is not in the
ARAD API, so the key the owner registered is not needed for it. The fetcher
supplies only its parse and its requests to the shared official adapter. The
bank is a supplier in the catalogue, its series maps to the CZK-CZEONIA item,
and a session and a feed scoped to that item are seeded in the system tenant.

On a fresh environment the service sent 273 fixings about 1.7 minutes after
it started. Each is the decimal of the published percent, with the credit
Source: CNB. The files reach back to 2002-01-02; the calibration sources note
records the route and its depth. Unit tests parse recorded files and refuse a
malformed one. The system and Acme provisioned to completion.
