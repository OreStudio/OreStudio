* Acceptance

- Each daily Frankfurter provider with a terms page has its terms read and recorded on the sources page, and those that allow storage are providers with a source.
- Each provider's currencies are vendor instruments mapped to FX fixing items of the form FX-SOURCE-CCY1-CCY2.
- The system party subscribes and routes to each public provider.
- EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY are each covered by at least three providers.
- Each provider records the time of day it publishes, in its own zone, so the adapter asks for a day's fixings after they are out. The store keeps the date of a fixing alone.
