* Summary

=ores.http= is the platform's REST gateway. It terminates HTTP,
authenticates the caller, and forwards each request to the owning service
over NATS, so it depends on the services' wire protocols and not on their
libraries. It is a composite of six parts: three hand-written
infrastructure parts, and one adapter per component whose routes it
serves.
