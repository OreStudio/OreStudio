* Goal

A pinned panic produces gaps and heavy tails, not only a wider walk: as activity
rises, an FX feed's mixture moves weight toward its wide components, and the shape
of its returns changes with the market.

* Acceptance

- The weights move with =A= by a stated rule, renormalised to sum to one.
- The reweighting is variance-preserving: the stdevs are rescaled after the
  weights move, so the loading remains the only channel to sigma and the
  calibration test of the previous task still holds.
- The mean of the series is unchanged.
- The kurtosis of returns rises with activity; a test states the window and the
  sampling interval it measures.
- A feed with the tail channel off behaves exactly as it does today.

* Plan

1. The reweighting function on the mixture the FX feeds carry, with its
   renormalisation and the variance-preserving rescale.
2. The test: the same seed under calm and under a pinned active market, kurtosis
   and mean compared.
3. A switch per feed, so the channel can be turned off while it is being proved.
