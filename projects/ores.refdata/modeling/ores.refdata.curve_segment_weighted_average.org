The two curves a weighted average segment averages and the weight of each,
as ORE's =ReferenceCurve1=, =ReferenceCurve2=, =Weight1= and =Weight2=. They
hold one invariant: the segment is their weighted sum. ORE requires all four,
so every weighted average segment has this part.

A segment holds at most one row of this part, so the generated table adds the
unique index on the live rows of =(tenant_id, curve_segment_id)=.
