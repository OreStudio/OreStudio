This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

=trading.v1.ops.complete_structure= writes a structure, its members and takes
every leg live in one transaction, with two cases proving a refusal leaves every
leg a draft and writes no member. Nothing calls it.

The grouping screen's route (=POST /api/structures=) still writes the structure
through the entity write and then each member through the member write, one call
at a time, and never executes a leg. The book-a-trade flow adds a leg the same
way. So grouped legs stay drafts and a grouping that fails halfway leaves a
half-grouped deal standing, which is the state the operation exists to make
impossible.

* Why

An operation with no caller is not a capability, it is a comment. The arc's own
acceptance says a completed structure has every leg live and unauthorised, and
nothing in the product completes one.

Wiring it also forces a decision the arc has not taken: grouping and completing
are two different acts, and the grouping screen currently does the first while
pretending it did the second. Either the grouping screen completes (and a leg can
no longer wait as a draft), or the structure page carries a Complete action and
the grouping screen keeps making drafts. The prototype drew the structure page's
own actions, which is the second.

* Shape of the work

Not designed here. The pieces that appear necessary:

- Decide where completing happens: the structure page's action, or the grouping
  screen's confirm step.
- Point the route at the operation: one call carrying the anchor and the legs,
  in place of the loop of entity writes.
- State what the screen shows when the store refuses one leg: the completion is
  all-or-nothing, so the message is about the deal rather than about a row.
- A render test, since the screen's submit path is what changes.

* What done looks like

Grouping a set of trades, or pressing Complete on a structure, produces one
structure whose every leg is live and waiting on Operations, and a refusal leaves
the trades as they were.
