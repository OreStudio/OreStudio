* Goal

The ACME curve configurations name their quotes with ORE keys that are based on tenors. The synthetic feeds name their series by date based pillar keys, and another environment is refactoring the feed names to a URI form. A curve whose quotes do not match a series cannot be built. When the feed refactor lands, compare the quote keys of every ACME curve configuration with the feed series, list the mismatches, and fix the side that is wrong.

* Acceptance

- A check lists, for every curve configuration of every ACME party, the quotes that have no matching feed series.
- The check passes for every curve the reports need, or each gap is listed with its cause and an owner.
- The feed refactor and this check agree on one naming rule, recorded in a knowledge page.

* Plan

1. Read the naming rule of the refactored feeds.
2. List the quote keys of every ACME curve configuration.
3. Match each key to a feed series and list the keys that have none.
4. Fix the side that is wrong, and record the rule in a knowledge page.
