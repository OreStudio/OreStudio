* Goal

ores.http serves ORE's documents by forwarding to compass's document service:
a search, a few words in and the best documents out with the section that
matched; a list filtered on metadata, such as a sprint's tasks; and a document
read by org id, with its metadata and its body as HTML. ORE Studio holds no
index and no document. The route requires a session, maps the caller's role to
one of the views ORE declares in its compass configuration, and sets that view
on the forwarded request, so no caller reaches documents its role may not see.
The routes are in the route registry and the OpenAPI document. Nothing goes
through the BFF.

The endpoints, their filters and the views are compass's to define, in a
proposal handed to compass and not yet published there.

* Acceptance

- A search for volatility through the route answers the Volatility pages
  first, each with the section that matched.
- A search can be restricted by type, tag or keyword, and a search in the
  knowledge view answers no sprint or task document.
- A document read by org id answers its metadata and its HTML, and an unknown
  id is refused by name.
- A caller without the role for a view never receives a document outside it,
  shown by a test; a view sent by the client is ignored.
- The routes are in ores.http's route registry and OpenAPI document, with
  their schemas.
