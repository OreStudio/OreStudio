* What

Three volatility parts have no DQ artefact table: =fx_volatility_smile=,
=cap_floor_proxy_config= and =swaption_proxy_config=. They are also not in the
table list of =ores_refdata_publish_curve_configuration_from_dq_fn=. Their
entries are in both. When they were split, the columns left the entries'
artefact tables, and nothing took their place.

* Why

A staged curve configuration with an FX smile or a proxy publishes the entry
without it, and nothing refuses the publish. No seed states these blocks today,
so no data is lost yet. A dataset that does state one will lose it. The fix gives
each part an artefact table, as the swaption smile and the inflation seasonality
have, and adds them to the publish list and its test.
