* Acceptance

- A document per risk class exists for the classes the store holds market data for, and each states its shift type, shift size, scheme and bucket list.
- The .1 family and the basis-point family share one document per risk class, selected through the keyed ShiftSize, ShiftType and ShiftScheme entries.
- Every seeded document validates against external/ore/xsd/sensitivity.xsd.
- A document round trips through the store without losing a block or a keyed override.
