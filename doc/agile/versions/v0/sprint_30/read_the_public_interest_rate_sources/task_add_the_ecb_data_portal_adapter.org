* Goal

€STR is stored as fixings in the system tenant, with its history, read from the ECB Data Portal through series =EST/B.EU000A2X2A25.WT= of =data-api.ecb.europa.eu=, in CSV. The adapter is specialised: it reads the ECB Data Portal alone, and it supplies only its fetcher and its settings to the shared half of [[id:0DB85F8F-4C7D-4428-B514-AF69CE8E5D86][Separate the public source adapter's plumbing from its fetching]].

* Acceptance

- The adapter reads the ECB Data Portal alone, and it does not copy the announce, the demand, the backfill or the publish code.
- The ECB Data Portal is a supplier in the catalogue, its €STR series maps to the €STR item of [[id:0D8B2280-F6E1-4198-8372-6712D1E8DFD8][Catalogue the overnight rate items]], and a session and a feed of it are seeded in the system tenant.
- The session's terms are accepted at setup, as the terms of the FX sources are. A session whose terms are not accepted is skipped and reported, and provisioning does not fail.
- A fresh environment stores the €STR history as fixings within minutes of provisioning, and the log names the source and the count it stored.
- Each fixing is stored as published, with the credit that the terms ask for.
- The depth of the history is measured and written into [[id:3F9D6EAE-9FB8-4A0D-9BAF-1EA3EA93AB4F][the calibration sources note]].
- Unit tests parse a recorded response of the ECB Data Portal, and a malformed response gives an error, not a fixing.

* Plan

- =ecb_fetcher= implements =fetcher=: adapter =ecb_portal=, scheme =ecb_sdmx=, field =OBS_VALUE=. A source's key is the full series key, =EST.B.EU000A2X2A25.WT=, split at its first dot into the dataflow and the key within it. A read is one GET of =format=csvdata&detail=dataonly= over the window.
- The CSV is read by its header: =KEY=, =TIME_PERIOD= and =OBS_VALUE=. A row of another series is skipped, and an empty answer or a header alone holds no fixing. The value is the decimal of the published percent, as for SOFR.
- The portal answers a query it has not cached with a 504 after thirty seconds, and the same query again from its cache in under a second (probed 2026-11-10). A read tries three times, each with a sixty-second limit, and fails with the last error.
- The seeds add the =ecb= supplier, the =ecb_portal= adapter, the =ecb-data-portal= session, and the =ecb-estr= feed scoped to the €STR item. The ECB's default feed stays =ecb-frankfurter=, because a publisher has one default. The system party already subscribes to the ECB, so it gains only an =interest_rates= route to =ecb-estr=.
- The pgTAP test of the feeds asserted that every feed of the ECB and the Bank of Canada reads through Frankfurter. It now asserts that of the four Frankfurter feeds.

* Result

€STR is stored as fixings in the system tenant, read from the ECB Data Portal by a specialised fetcher that the official adapter hosts beside Frankfurter and the New York Fed. A fresh environment stored 279 fixings, the last 400 days, with the log line "Sent 279 fixing(s) of 'ecb-estr'". The values are the decimals of the published percents, such as =0.02443= for 2.443, and the provider =ecb= carries the credit "Source: ECB statistics". The calibration sources note records the change of unit, the 1,820 rates the portal serves since 2019-10-01, and the portal's 504 on an uncached query.

Unit tests parse a recorded answer and a negative rate, read an empty answer as no fixing, refuse four malformed answers, check the URL of a read, ask again after a 504, give up after three, and refuse a key with no dataflow.
