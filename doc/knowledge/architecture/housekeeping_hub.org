* Summary

Housekeeping is the practice by which a store the platform retains is made
subject to a retention policy. The cluster treats the subject in two pages, and
the order between them is fixed by vocabulary: the first establishes the policy,
the executor that applies it and the telemetry that records what it did, and the
second assigns to each class of retained data the retention period or the other
treatment that its contents require.

* Detail

** Terms

The cluster uses the following terms in one sense each. The register that retires
their synonyms is the [[proj:doc/meta/glossary.org][glossary]].

| Term | Meaning |
|------+---------|
| Housekeeping | the practice of making every retained store subject to a retention policy |
| Retention policy | the declared rule for one store: what it targets, what action is taken, the period or threshold, the cadence, and the fallback where the engine will not act |
| Retention period | the length of time a store keeps a record before the policy acts on it |
| Lifecycle rule | the object store's form of a retention policy, naming a key prefix and the age at which its objects expire |
| TTL | the expiry carried by a single object or directory whose value ends at a known time |
| Eviction | the removal of a cache entry, which a later use can rebuild |
| Deduplication | the reduction of several identical copies to one, addressed by its content digest |
| Executor | the scheduled process that reads policies and applies them; a sweep where it runs on a cadence |
| Telemetry | the per-policy record of what the executor did, with the size before and after and the outcome |

** The sequence

1. [[id:E92D8CA4-C33F-4AE2-808B-9092B100E9C5][Housekeeping of retained
   data]] states the model. It gives the shape of a retention policy, the actions
   an executor takes, the path an executor follows where the storage engine will
   not apply a policy itself, and the telemetry a policy reports. It precedes the
   classification because an action is meaningful only as the application of a
   declared policy, and the terms for declaring one are established there.

2. [[id:21D539CD-AF3B-4479-81D5-F195756C064F][Classification of retained
   data]] applies the model. It names the classes of data a platform retains and
   the retention period or treatment each class takes, and it establishes why
   age-based retention serves only those classes whose worth decays with time.

** Scope

The identity, ownership and interfaces of a store are described by
[[id:F1BC2C10-B23B-4979-9692-8FA7C29319CB][Data storage]] and its spokes. The
treatment of what a store holds is described here. The mechanics of the schedule
that fires an executor are described by
[[id:DFC57D4F-9AB2-4428-BDEC-BF44D106A75D][Scheduler]]; the cluster states that
an executor runs on a configured cadence and takes the mechanics as given.
