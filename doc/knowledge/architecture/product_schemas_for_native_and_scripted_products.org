* Summary

Every product is described by a single schema, comprising its fields and the
forms it admits ([[id:D18BD092-AF0C-449F-ACDA-F621655BA831][Scripted Trade Product Booking]]). Each field carries a type, its multiplicity
(scalar or list), the values it accepts and a description. The booking interface
derives its layout from the schema, the shell presents it, import validates a
document against it, and synthetic generation draws terms from it. A product's
fields are therefore stated once, and a product added to the catalogue becomes
available to every interface simultaneously.

* Detail

** Sources of the schema

- For a product with a native form, the fields are those of its element: the
  terms accepted by its builder, which correspond to the facts its rows hold.
- For a product with only a scripted form, the fields are the inputs of its
  script ([[id:92449D70-09E2-4391-B716-A1001F22B271][Derivation of Script Inputs from Free Variables]]): each with a name, a type (=Number=, =Event=, =Index=,
  =Currency= or =DayCounter=), a multiplicity and the rule its values satisfy.
- Annotations, held as data, supply what neither source states: labels, the
  codes admitted by a coded input (such as the values one to four of
  =BarrierType=), defaults, and descriptions drawn from ORE's User Guide.

** Forms within the schema

The schema enumerates the forms a product admits, and for each scripted form the
script it executes, the engine assigned to its product tag by the pricing
configuration, and the associated cost. For a product admitting both forms, the
schema is the native one, and the scripted form is reached through the product's
twin recipe ([[id:206312C0-6398-4B9D-86F8-D8AEC9A5CEAD][Twin Recipes: Mapping Native Terms to Script Inputs]]).

** Consumers of the schema

| Consumer | Use of the schema |
|----------+-------------------|
| booking interface | derivation of fields and of the available forms |
| booking API | validation of terms prior to booking ([[id:3085E65E-413B-4E7A-B60A-5844A124C999][The Product Booking API]]) |
| ores.shell | presentation of a product's description ([[id:B01292A4-0394-40E8-A0B4-2DBA3F46C052][Scripted Trade Commands in the Shell]]) |
| import | validation of a document's terms |
| synthetic generation | field-by-field drawing of terms ([[id:8BA8D977-46CC-4DC6-9EFE-3FBE56B76A37][Synthetic Generation of Products in Every Form]]) |
