This page documents a [[id:31C868B9-306E-4282-BA8C-07E647021B34][task]] in the [[id:E44D54EF-14D9-4010-A626-12AF1EDC8F26][Add proper curve support to ores.marketdata]] story. It captures the goal, current status, acceptance, and the result.

* Goal

Every market data journey is reviewable without a session. Each journey gets
one self-contained plain-HTML prototype under =doc/prototypes/=, in the
agreed design tokens, with variants and states driven by query parameters. A
screen that shows an oresmd URL calls one shared editor rather than asking a
person to type a URI. A term structure or a surface is plotted, because a
curve cannot be argued without seeing its shape. Each prototype is listed in
the one inventory and published by the site build.

* Acceptance

- One prototype per market data journey, self-contained and openable as a
  file, with no framework, no build step and no session.
- The oresmd URL editor composes, parses and validates a URL from the schema,
  offers the codes the existing reference data keeps as pickers, and is called
  from every dialog that shows a URL.
- Term structures and surfaces are plotted with the shared chart helper.
- Every prototype is listed in =doc/prototypes/index.org=, deployed by
  =ores-build-site.el=, and the inventory check is green.
- The story's proposed acceptance is exercised where a prototype can: the
  composite object, its evolution, its declared shape and a hand-keyed point.

* Result

Closed by the work on =chore/analyse-curve-support=, in PR
[[https://github.com/OreStudio/OreStudio/pull/2694][#2694]]:

- 15 market data journey prototypes, plus the synthetic data generation
  screens, the oresmd URL editor and the oresmd URL editor source view.
- =doc/prototypes/oresmd-url-editor/index.html=: the composer, the managed-URL
  list, the blueprint modal and the field-source view, with 23 fields backed
  by an existing table and 10 by ORE's own vocabulary.
- A shared, dependency-free SVG chart helper embedded in the curve and
  surface prototypes: line, bars, heat map and isometric mesh.
- =check_prototype_inventory.py= reports 40 prototypes, all listed and
  published; =compass lint= is green.
