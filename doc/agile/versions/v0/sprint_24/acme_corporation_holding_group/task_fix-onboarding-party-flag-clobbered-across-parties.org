* Goal

Scope remove()/save() for party-level settings (onboarding.party) by party_id as well as name, either by adding a party_id predicate to the delete query or a dedicated RLS policy/column check, so completing onboarding for one party never clears another party's flag. Confirmed via Acme Corporation provisioning: after --source acme activates all four parties in sequence, only the last-processed party (HK) retains onboarding.party=true; the holding company and UK/US lose theirs, causing the Party Provisioning Wizard to incorrectly reappear on login.
