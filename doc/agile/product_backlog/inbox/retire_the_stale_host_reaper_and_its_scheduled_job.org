#+title: Retire the stale-host reaper and its scheduled job

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Remove the =reap_work= handler in =work_handler=, the =reap_work_message= in the work model, and the scheduled job =compute.v1.reap.stale_results= that publishes it every minute. Remove the job from the compute populate script and from the live job table.

* Why

The lease reaper now returns the jobs of lost nodes to the queue, with an attempt limit. The older reaper picks results that are in progress on a host whose node samples stopped. It lists results as the system tenant, so it sees no tenant work, and it does nothing for the grid. It stays for now because a scheduled job publishes its message, and removing the job needs a change to the populate script and to the live job table. That change belongs in its own task.
