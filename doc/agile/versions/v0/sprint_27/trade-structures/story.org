* Acceptance

- structure is an immutable anchor with party, counterparty, kind and template; its legs share its party and counterparty.
- structure_member is keyed by the trade and temporal, so a trade sits in at most one structure at a time and can be linked and unlinked.
- A template's roles and leg counts are checked when a leg is linked; structures nest one level at most.
- The structure's internal version is read from its legs; its external version lives on the agreement.
