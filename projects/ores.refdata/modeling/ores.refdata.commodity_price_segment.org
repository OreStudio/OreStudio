#+created: 2026-10-03
#+updated: 2026-10-03

One =PriceSegment= of a commodity curve: the kind of instrument the segment's
prices come from, its priority, its convention, and its quotes. An off peak
power segment lists its off peak and peak quotes separately, as rows of
=curve_quote= with the list names =OffPeakQuotes= and =PeakQuotes=.
