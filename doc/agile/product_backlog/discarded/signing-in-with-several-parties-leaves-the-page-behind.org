* Why this was discarded

It reported that a multi-party account stays on the sign-in page. That is wrong.
A Playwright run against the running fleet shows the app does what it should:

  URL after sign in      : http://127.0.0.1:20802/login
  choose-a-party visible : true
  headings               : ['Choose a party']

So the client asks which party to work in, as designed, and the address stays on
=/login= until the choice is made. The earlier snapshot was the sign-in form
because the test navigated to =/trades= before making the choice, and the app
sent it back to sign in.

* What is worth keeping from it

=POST /api/session= answers =outcome: active= for an account in one party and
=outcome: party-required= with =availableParties= for an account in several, and
the chooser keeps the =/login= address. A UI test must therefore wait for the
chooser rather than assert on the URL.
