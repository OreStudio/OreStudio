#+title: A party takes a provider's feeds through a subscription
#+description: What a subscription records, the rights it carries, how it limits what a party can demand, and why the party names a provider and never a source.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :marketdata:subscription:entitlement:
#+created: 2026-10-06
#+updated: 2026-10-22

* Summary

A subscription says that one party takes the feeds of one provider, with a set
of rights, for a period. It is an entitlement. A party names the provider and
never the source, because the route chooses the source. See [[id:37B27CA8-9F44-4A61-9B02-3EAABD8C3818][A party routes to exactly one source for each provider]]. The
party does not need to know whether the source is simulated, and it can look it
up.

* Detail

** What a row records

| Column | Meaning |
|--------+---------|
| =tenant_id=, =party_id= | The consumer |
| =provider= | The provider whose feeds the party takes |
| =rights= | A set of =display=, =non_display= and =derived=. See [[id:B746D4F2-F1B5-4563-A6E4-D36BDF8E6BBE][Display, non-display and derived rights are modelled on the subscription]] |
| =valid_from=, =valid_to= | The period of the subscription |
| =enabled= | A suspend flag. Nothing is deleted. |
| Audit columns and version | The standard members |

The natural key is the party, the provider and the period. A tenant admin or a
party admin can read the subscriptions. A person with the write permission
changes them.

** What a subscription allows

- The party can demand the items that map to instruments of the provider. A
  demand outside the subscription is refused with =not_entitled=.
- The party gets a route to one source. See [[id:37B27CA8-9F44-4A61-9B02-3EAABD8C3818][A party routes to exactly one source for each provider]].
- The party's usage is metered against the subscription. See [[id:3E51B0BD-D22B-4790-821F-E2FED44D152C][Usage is recorded as events and priced later]].

** The enabled flag is a suspend

Setting =enabled= to false closes the party's demand and stops delivery. The
service keeps the row and the party's stored series. Setting the flag to true
restores service without a new row.

** Who sees what

A party sees only its own subscriptions. The system tenant sees all. The catalogue
rows are readable by every party. See [[id:68CE1E1D-7568-4911-BB71-CCF5A9E07BB7][Every source belongs to a provider in the catalogue]].
