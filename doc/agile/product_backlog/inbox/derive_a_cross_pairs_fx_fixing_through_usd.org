This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Fix a cross pair's FX fixing from its two USD legs at the cut: GBP/AUD at
WMR1000NYC is GBP/USD over AUD/USD at the New York cut. Seed the cross's fixing
id, offer a fixing generator that reads both legs' spots on the run's board, and
let the FX draw take the cross back.

* Why

The simulator fixes a pair from the spot a spot config quotes, and no vendor
collection quotes a cross such as GBP/AUD or AUD/CAD. On a fresh acme_demo
provision on 2026-11-23, FX trades named five such crosses (AUD/CAD, AUD/CHF,
GBP/AUD, GBP/CAD and GBP/NZD) whose fixing nothing published. The task Generate
simulated fixings through the simulator adapter took those pairs out of the FX
draw. The design page says a pair the cut source does not fix directly is
derived through USD, which would let the draw keep them.
