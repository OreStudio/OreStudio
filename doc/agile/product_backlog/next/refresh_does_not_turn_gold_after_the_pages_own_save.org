* What

Refresh does not turn gold after the page's own save. ES-REFRESH-15 says that after a save the dialog closes, Refresh turns gold and the person presses it. In the sign off scenario of 2026-10-25 on the calendars record page, the Edit dialog saved a new name, the dialog closed, and the page kept the old name and version with Refresh plain for fifteen seconds; a second tab that reloaded showed the new name. After a derive, which navigates to the new page, Refresh was gold at once. Either the entity change the server announces for the page's own write does not reach the stale logic, or the page's load time is taken after the write; either way the person has no sign that the page is behind their own save. Found on calendars with the shared RecordHeader and RecordDialog, so every record page has it.

* Why

ES-REFRESH-15 is the contract between a write and the page: nothing reloads by itself, and
the gold Refresh is how the person knows the page is behind. Without it a person who just
saved reads stale values with no sign.
