* Goal

A kind is a declaration of its subject, its compute, its checks and its approval kinds, and the lifecycle reaches a kind only through one interface. The interface is small enough that a fourth kind adds a declaration and no branch.

* Acceptance

- The declaration names the subject, the compute, the checks and the two approval kinds, and the three kinds declare theirs in one place each.
- The interface carries the four facts the lifecycle needs from a kind, and no step of raise, apply, supersede or notice reads a kind code.
- A test proves that a kind which declares no withdrawal kind is refused a withdrawal at the raise, and not at the apply.
