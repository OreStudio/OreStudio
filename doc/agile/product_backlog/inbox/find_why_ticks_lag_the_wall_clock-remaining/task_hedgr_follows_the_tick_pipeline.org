* Goal

Hedgr filters the whole party tick subtree on the client, its live tick carries no coordinate, and its bucket ring drops the newest bucket when full. Make Hedgr subscribe for each series that it watches, keep the coordinate of a live tick, and overwrite the oldest bucket.

* Acceptance

- Hedgr subscribes for each watched series, after the live subject has tokens, and it no longer decodes the ticks of series that it does not watch.
- A live tick of a series with coordinates, such as the pillars of a curve, reaches the bucket of its own coordinate, checked against a live curve.
- The bucket ring overwrites its oldest bucket when it is full, and it counts the drops.
- Hedgr seeds a chart with one history call for a span of time, subscribes to the live subject before it calls, and merges the reply with its live ticks by coordinate and time.
