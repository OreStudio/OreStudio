* Plan

=account_role_repository::remove_all_for_account(caller.id)= is called after the
two accounts are written and before the service is constructed. The junction row
is closed rather than deleted, and =read_effective_permissions= no longer sees
it, so the caller reaches the gate holding nothing.

The account trigger gives Member to a person account at version 1 and never
again, so an administrator can leave an account in exactly this state. The case
is not asserting something unreachable.
