#+entity_title: Curve Volatility Delta Surface
#+brief: The delta and expiry grid a DeltaSurface kind states.
#+created: 2026-10-30
#+updated: 2026-10-30

ORE's =volatilityDeltaSurfaceConfig= states a grid of deltas against expiries: the
delta type, the ATM type and the ATM delta type that name what the grid's points
mean, the put and call deltas, and how the grid interpolates and extrapolates.
Those are the kind's own fields; the four terms every kind may state and the
=priority= are on =curve_volatility_kind=, and a row of this part exists only when
the entry holds a kind of this type.

Seven of this part's columns name the same things =curve_volatility_strike_surface=
names: the expiries and the six ways the grid interpolates and extrapolates. They
are stated in both because a part mirrors one ORE complex type, and each type
states its own grid. A column shared with a sibling type is not a fact held twice:
neither row can stand for the other, and a surface that could read its grid from
either would make the pair interchangeable, which the schema does not say.
