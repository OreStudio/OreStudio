* Acceptance

- A read that returns a term structure's points also returns each point's
  instant and when it was recorded, so age is derivable without a second call
- A drawn term structure shows per-point age, the oldest point's age, and the
  spread between the oldest and the newest
- A curve stitched across market horizons is visibly different from one built
  from a single instant, without the reader asking
- A prototype settles the encoding, and the threshold at which the view warns
  rather than drawing silently is stated by a journey

* Plan

The read a term-structure view already uses is the one that gains the
capability, so no second read returns the same points. Three steps.

1. *The repository.* =market_observation_repository::read_as_of_records= is the
   read: one =DISTINCT ON (oresmd_uri)= query plus the existing manual overlay,
   returning each point beside the =valid_from= of the row it came from. The
   private =read_manual_points= returns the same pair, so the manual row carries
   its own record time into the overlay. =read_as_of= keeps its signature and
   behaviour and becomes a projection of it, so the paging rule and the overlay
   exist once. The pair is =repository::observation_record=, and the record time
   is read by =as_of_recorded_at=, beside =as_of_observation= in =as_of_rows=.
2. *The wire.* =get_curve_snapshot_response= gains =as_of=, a parallel
   =recorded_at= aligned index-for-index with =observations=, and the derived
   =oldest_age_seconds=, =spread_seconds= and =warning=. The change is additive:
   no existing field moves. =operations.org= is edited and the protocol is
   regenerated, never hand-edited.
3. *The summary and the view.* =summarise_staleness= in
   =curve_snapshot_staleness.hpp= computes the ages from the read's own instant,
   so a client needs no clock of its own and the rule is unit-testable; the
   handler fills the parallel array and calls it. The prototype draws a per-point
   age band, the oldest age, the spread and the warning, and the evolution
   journey states the threshold at half an hour of spread.

* Notes

- The threshold is half an hour of spread, stated in
  [[id:28D1545F-1BE8-4C14-8B69-292DC12BC45F][Watch a curve or surface evolve]]
  under =* The staleness threshold=. The number is in the journey, not in the
  code: the read reports the crossing and the view decides what to draw.
- The two ages answer different questions and both are derivable from the same
  row. Market staleness, the read's instant minus the point's instant, is the age
  of the market state and is what the spread measures. Record staleness, now
  minus the point's =valid_from=, is how long ago the value arrived and is what
  =recorded_at= carries. The capability added here reports the first and carries
  the second, so a consumer never needs a second call for either.
- =valid_from= is projected rather than added as a column: it is already stored
  and selected by every as-of read, so the gap was one projection. The reasoning
  is recorded in
  [[id:E4736D88-03B2-4A40-B7F4-01B06B9B9FD7][Series and observation]], where a
  reader who wonders why a transaction-time column reaches the wire will look.
- The prototype's =stable= market is a curve read from one instant and its
  =churn= market is one stitched across market horizons, so switching between
  them shows the warning appearing at the threshold without editing the page.

* Result

The read a term-structure view already uses now carries each point's record time
and a derived staleness summary, from one query and one manual overlay, with no
schema change.

=read_as_of_records= is the read and =read_as_of= is its projection, so the
paging rule and the manual overlay exist once and a value and its record time
cannot come from two queries that disagree. The manual overlay carries the manual
row's own =valid_from=, so a hand-keyed point is not reported as having arrived
when the feed wrote the value it replaced.

=curve_snapshot.get= states the instant it is relative to and returns the record
times as a parallel array aligned index-for-index with =observations=, plus the
oldest point's age, the spread between the oldest and the newest, and the warning
that the spread crosses the threshold. The change is additive: no existing
response field was renamed or removed. A snapshot whose points share one instant
has a spread of zero however old that instant is, so age alone never warns; only
mixing does.

The prototype settles the encoding -- an age band per point, the oldest point's
age, the spread, and a visible warning -- and the journey states the threshold at
half an hour of spread.
