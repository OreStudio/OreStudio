* Plan

Two artefacts: the skill for the judgement, the recipe for the commands.

The skill is =compass-code-investigate-trunk-break=, named against the verb
register: =investigate-= is diagnosing to root cause, and the object is the
break on trunk. =blame= is not a verb of the register and is also the name of
the checker's command, which the conventions forbid naming a skill after. The
recipe is [[id:477E9949-CFA4-44D8-A021-D7463BEC4C0D][How do I blame a break on main?]], under =doc/recipes/git/=.

The report shape is the task's real deliverable, so the skill holds it as a
template with four headings: *Owner*, *Related commits*, *Technical description*
and *Disposition*. The owner is an environment rather than a person, because
the environment holds the branch, the task and the session that made the
change, and =compass fleet= is where that is read.

Building it surfaced a defect in the skill scaffold: the template emitted
=#+title:=, =#+author:= and the org options, and none of =#+description:=,
=#+type: skill= or =#+level:=, so every new skill was born outside the contract
that [[id:44D7A328-F69C-4EE9-83A5-74BD3634AD69][compass-skill-review]] checks it against. The template is fixed in
=ores.doc.knowledge.skill.org= and re-tangled, and the new skill carries the
three lines.

* Result

The skill and its recipe are in the tree, the catalogue lists the skill, and
the levels table gives it a row.

=compass-code-investigate-trunk-break= states when to reach for the procedure,
the five steps, the report shape and the disposition rule. The recipe holds the
commands: classify the failures by re-running them alone, find the commit with
=git log -S=, read the owner from the trailers and the fleet, trace a check
finding with =compass-ng blame=, and prove the mechanism with a rolled-back
insert.

The disposition rule is three conditions for fixing inline -- inside your diff's
blast radius, blocking your gate, and no live branch of another environment on
the file -- and handing back when the change touches schema, behaviour or a
contract, or when another environment owns the file. When both read true the
report goes back to the owner.

The skill scaffold was fixed on the way: =ores.doc.knowledge.skill.org= now
emits =#+description:=, =#+type: skill= and =#+level: cross=, and the tangle
regenerated =doc_skill.org.mustache=.

The first gate run after the skill was written failed with one finding,
=skill_levels/skill_without_level=, which is the check doing its job: a skill
with no row in the levels table is an error rather than a default. The row was
added, the level codec stamped the skill, and the non-heavy gate passes 13 of
13.
