* Result

** Outcome

The route key is (party, publisher, class). The route stores =publisher_code=
and =asset_class=, and the unique index over the three is the rule: a party has
one route for a publisher and class.

A route's feed must belong to the publisher, and, when the feed declares a
scope, the scope must serve the class. A feed without scope rows, such as a
simulator feed, is not constrained by the class check yet.

The existing routes migrate: the four public publishers route at FX and the
curve route at IR. The runtime follows the key: the demand calculator, usage
planner, ingest loop and IAM provisioning read and write publisher and class.
