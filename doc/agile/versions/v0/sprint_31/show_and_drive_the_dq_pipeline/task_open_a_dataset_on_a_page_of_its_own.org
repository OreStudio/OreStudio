* Goal

A row of the coverage list opens the dataset's own page at
=/data-quality/datasets/:code=: the record page the
[[id:2BF7F03F-6BF4-45FF-8E26-4D3B4E2D943D][Record screen standard]] shapes. It
states where the dataset's rows are staged, what a publish dispatches to, both
counts, when it was last published and by whom, and the reason it cannot publish
with the owner of that reason. Its Publications tab lists every publish of the
dataset with its outcome and what the run said.

* Acceptance

- A row of the list opens =/data-quality/datasets/:code=, and the breadcrumb runs
  Home, Data quality, Reference data coverage, then the dataset code.
- The Details tab states name, code, bundle and level, artefact type and table,
  target subject, staged, published, optional, last published with who, and the
  readiness with its owner.
- The Publications tab lists the publishes of that dataset with the outcome and
  the messages, and says which half is not served while the run log has no writer.
- A dataset that no bundle carries answers a page that says so, and not an error.
- The two web suites cover the list's states and the page's tabs.

* Notes

Signed off on 2026-11-26 with the prototype, which draws both screens.

Two figures the page draws are not served yet, and their tasks are:

- The published count, from the provenance a publish writes:
  [[id:16FD5B35-83E7-4F85-9578-8AD20D33667E][Count the rows a dataset published]].
- The Publications tab, from a run log that has no writer and no outcome:
  [[id:7DCAF97F-EF6C-447F-BC1B-561DE85B3F49][Record each publication with its
  outcome and what it said]].

The tab is drawn and states the absence, because a tab that says "none yet" would
claim nothing was published.

The History tab is not in this task: the dataset's versions are served already
(=dq.v1.datasets_versions.list=), and drawing them is a screen of its own.

* Result

The two screens shipped in PR #5188, with the review's findings answered in the
same PR: the state filter offers only what the roster answers, the second read
states its wait and its failure, each level says where its rows live, the page
states the last publication as unserved beside the published count, and the
read-only access mark is drawn.

The page is at =/data-quality/datasets/:code=, reached from a row of the coverage
list and reachable from a saved address: the page names the dataset and the
server resolves the bundle that carries it, so it does not need the list to have
been read first.

The Details tab draws from the roster the coverage list already reads, and states
every field the acceptance names. The Publications tab states why it holds
nothing yet, and names both halves: the log has no writer, and no column holds an
outcome or a message.

Not drawn, on purpose: the History tab. The dataset's versions are served already,
and drawing them is a screen of its own rather than a tab this task adds.
