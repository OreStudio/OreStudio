This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Let the basket builder take the Asian payoff with its observation dates, and
let the generator book an Asian basket beside the Vanilla one.

* Why

The basket option states Vanilla or Asian. The scripted rows hold an Asian
basket's ObservationDates as a product schedule, and the projection reads and
writes it, but the builder refuses any payoff other than Vanilla. So no
generated trade proves that the ORE engine builds an Asian basket from these
rows.
