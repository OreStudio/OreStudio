* Acceptance

- One definition holds the steps between the checkout and the artifact uploads, for every operating system and tier.
- Each build job states its runner, its container and its matrix, and calls that definition.
- Every leg keeps the steps it had, in the order it had them.
- actionlint and the CI YAML check report nothing new.

* Plan

One composite action holds what a leg does after its platform is prepared:
bring up the services, build and test, publish what the leg produces, and keep
the compiler cache. The four callers keep their runner, their container, their
matrix entry, their permissions, their platform preparation and a single call.

A called workflow was the proposal, and it cannot take the caller's container,
which the two Linux legs run in. The steps that are genuinely per-platform —
freeing disk, selecting Xcode, the developer prompt, the Visual Studio Clang —
are not duplicated anywhere either, so they stay in the caller where they read
as platform preparation rather than as branches of one file.

Verification without a runner: expand the calls into the steps they hold under
each leg's platform, compiler, build type and tier, and diff that list against
the previous revision. Then lint with actionlint and the repository's own CI YAML
check.

* Result

Four build jobs became four calls. What a leg does is defined once, in
=.github/actions/build-leg=, and each job now states its runner, its container,
its matrix, its platform preparation and the call. Of the 820 lines left in the
four workflows, most is the matrix, the preparation and the jobs around the
build.

The proposal was a called workflow. The container decided against it: a called
workflow cannot take the caller's =container=, and the two Linux legs run in the
CI image. The second reason came from the tree: the steps that differ per
platform are not copies of each other, so a single file would have moved them
into branches rather than shared them.

Eight legs were diffed against their previous revision, one per matrix entry and
build type. Two classes of difference remain, both intended: a run body that
names the preset now reads it from the action's inputs, because a composite has
no matrix, and =sccache --show-stats= no longer reddens the two hosted legs,
which is what its own comment always claimed. On Windows three preparation steps
swap places, because the setup that used to sit between them now lives in the
leg.

What this does not prove: no leg ran before the merge. The dispatch that follows
on the branch is the first statement about a real one.
