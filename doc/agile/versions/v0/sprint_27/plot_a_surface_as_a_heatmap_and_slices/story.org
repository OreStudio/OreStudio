* Goal

A person who reads a surface on the evolution screen sees its shape at once: a heatmap of the first axis against the second, and one line for each value of the first axis across the second. A hole is drawn as empty. The plot needs no server change, because the snapshot already returns the whole grid.

* Acceptance

- A surface with two axes shows a heatmap with the first axis down the side and the second across the top, in the order the shape declares them.
- A hole in the grid is drawn as empty and never as the lowest colour.
- The person can choose the colour scale: the whole surface, or each column.
- One line is drawn for each value of the first axis across the second axis, in the declared order, and a hole breaks the line.
- A shape with one axis keeps the existing chart, and a shape with more than two axes says it is not drawn.
- The words say slice and not smile, and the screen does not convert risk-reversal or butterfly quotes to strikes.
- The plot works in English, French and Portuguese.

* Decisions

- The plot reads the snapshot the screen already holds, so it adds no server call and no wire change.
- The lines are called slices. The demo surfaces hold ATM, 25RR and 25BF on the strike axis. These are quotes in market convention and not points on a smile, so a line across them is not a smile.

* Out of scope

- The calendar and butterfly arbitrage checks of the surface slices journey. They need new server checks, and they are captured as =check_a_surface_for_calendar_and_butterfly_arbitrage=.
- A 3D view, which needs a charting library.
- A conversion of risk-reversal and butterfly quotes to strikes.

The web typecheck and the market, access and language tests pass. See the task for what was not checked.
