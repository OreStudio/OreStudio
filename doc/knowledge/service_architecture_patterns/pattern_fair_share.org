This page defines the [[id:68FAC606-569C-4B9B-932B-3B3A0181AC8B][service architecture pattern]]
Fair Share in the [[id:EA249899-6611-407F-A43A-B37019EBD1A7][pattern language]].
Shared capacity is divided among tenants by weight, so one busy tenant cannot
starve the others. In ORE Studio the reporting design applies it at three
queues: admission, step concurrency and grid dispatch.

* Also known as

- /Fair-share scheduling/.
- /Weighted fair queuing/, its network form.

* Context

Many tenants share one pool of capacity: workers, slots, grid nodes. Demand is
uneven: one tenant fires five hundred reports, another three.

* Problem

How is shared capacity divided so that every tenant progresses, whatever the
others ask for?

* Forces

- Arrival order lets the largest tenant delay everyone.
- Fixed slices waste capacity a quiet tenant does not use.
- Some tenants pay for more.
- Order within a tenant should still hold.

* Solution

When capacity frees up, give it to the tenant furthest below its share: the lowest
work in flight per unit of weight, skipping a tenant at its cap. Within a tenant,
take the oldest waiting item. A quiet tenant's share flows to the busy ones until
it has work again.

* Failure handling

#+ATTR_HTML: :class hug-leading
| Concern | What the pattern does |
|---------+-----------------------|
| Errors | A failed item frees its slot like a finished one. |
| Timeouts | Does not apply: the share decides order, not duration. |
| Retries | A retried item waits in its tenant's queue again. |
| Idempotency | Does not apply. |
| Observability | Each tenant's work in flight and wait time are measured. |

* Pitfalls

- *Fairness at one queue only.* A fair admission queue in front of a first-come grid still lets one tenant fill the grid.
- *Weights nobody revisits.* Paid capacity changes; the weights must follow.
- *Shares computed per replica.* Each replica is fair locally and unfair in total.

* Security

Availability across tenants: no tenant's load can starve another's.

* Resulting context

- Benefits :: Every tenant progresses during any peak.
- Liabilities :: The largest tenant waits longer than under arrival order, and weights need care.
