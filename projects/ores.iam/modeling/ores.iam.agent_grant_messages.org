#+updated: 2026-11-22

Creating and revoking agent grants and their keys, and exchanging a key for a
token. The agent grant and agent key tables are readable through their
generated entity protocols and writable only here, because each write needs a
check no row write can express: a grant is made by its person in their own
session, never by an agent, and only its person or an administrator revokes
it. [[id:A7E2942C-66AD-449C-B80B-CA789EBC34FA][Agent grants: delegated consent for LLM agents]] states the rules.

#+begin_src cpp :name comment
/**
 * @brief Records a person's consent that one named agent acts for them.
 *
 * Sent by the person, with their own session, who must hold
 * iam::agent_grants:create and every permission of the role. The grant's
 * tenant and party are the session's, and its grantor is the session's
 * account. Refused when the session names an agent, so an agent never makes a
 * grant, and when the role names a permission withheld from agents. The
 * response carries the grant's first key, the only time it is shown.
 */
#+end_src
