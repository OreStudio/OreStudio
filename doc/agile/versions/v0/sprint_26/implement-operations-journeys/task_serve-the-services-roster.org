* Goal

The services screen shows the installation as a roster: one row per expected
instance, kept whether it reports or not, with a state word for each. The
screen needs two reads and neither exists
([[id:B7B53CD1-4A11-403E-8844-13177864F0E7][The services roster has no read]]).
The expected services and their replica counts live in the service registry,
a code-generation model for the deployment tooling with no operation. The
states beyond running have no source at all: running means a sample arrived
within five minutes, and stopped or missing are answerable by nothing. This
task serves the roster: the expected rows with their replica counts, and the
last report of every instance at any age, so a quiet instance keeps its row
with the age of its last report.
