* Acceptance

- each of the three types has a builder for its own terms, and the generator
  books one trade of each with its embedded trades, joined by a structure of the
  type's template
- the writer books the container and its embedded trades as one group, and the
  recorded export writes each container with its embedded trades inside it
- the ORE engine test reads and builds each, with no error of the trade kind
- the three types leave the list awaiting builders, and the coverage test holds
  them as generated
