* Decisions

- *A merged pull request does not close a task by itself.* Two of the eight the
  audit flagged are not finished. =Bring ores.synthetic to the clean standard=
  waits on five open items (B06, V08, M06, P03, V04) and on a decision to accept
  any of them as an exception, and =Split every codegen template org into
  commented literate blocks= is BLOCKED behind the refdata entity model drift.
  Both are left open, and the audit's merged-PR rule is what produced the false
  positives.
- *The story file is the record and the table is a view.* Where the two
  disagreed, the table was moved to match the story file. Five rows were edited
  directly because compass has no verb that starts a story, only =story done=.
- *A closed document's outcome moves to its Result before its Now is blanked.*
  Six of the twenty-five closed documents had no Result at all, so their Now
  held the only record. Blanking those would have destroyed it.

* Out of scope

- The two findings left open. They need their owners, not a bookkeeping pass.

* Result

Done. The audit reports two findings, both deliberate: the synthetic clean-up
task that still has five open items, and the blocked literate-blocks task. One
story and six tasks were closed, five sprint rows were reconciled, and
twenty-five closed documents had their outcome moved into a Result before their
Now was blanked.
