#+title: A deleted calendar leaves its business days behind
#+description: A calendar derived again under a deleted code shows the old calendar's built days before any rebuild.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *next* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

A deleted calendar leaves its materialised business days behind. In the sign off scenario of 2026-10-25, Acme.Scenario was derived from TARGET, rebuilt for 2026, deleted, and derived again under the same code: the new calendar's Business days tab showed 260 business days before any rebuild, because the calendar_date rows of the deleted calendar were still there and are keyed by code. Deleting a calendar should remove, or close, its materialised days with it, or a rebuild should start from nothing for a calendar whose first version is newer than the days. Found while running the scenario Verify the holiday calendar screens; the screen shows what the server holds.

* Why

The Business days view tells the person what the calendar produces; days that belong to a deleted calendar
make it lie, and a rebuild never rewrites them.
