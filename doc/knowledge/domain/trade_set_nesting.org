#+title: Nested trade sets
#+description: Where trade sets contain each other, membership is part of the trade's identity: a trade is a path in a tree of sets. Where they do not, membership is an annotation. The test, and what it decides for the model.

* Summary

Some trade sets contain others, and a trade sits at a path in that tree.
The portfolio tree is the large case, and a structure is the same shape
in miniature because a structure's identity is its membership. Where sets
nest, membership is part of what the trade is and belongs on the trade.
Where they do not, membership is an annotation that the trade outlives.

* Detail

** The portfolio tree

[[id:5FC58A79-DBC9-46AF-9AAA-2C80B3A47850][Books and portfolios]] gives the large case. A
portfolio holds books and other portfolios, a [[id:130A3261-E788-4222-9BD7-371AD92982FB][book]] holds deals,
and the tree is written as a path, =AP.CHINA.IRS.OPTION=.

The sets along that path contain one another. The set for =AP= contains
the set for =AP.CHINA=, which contains the set for =AP.CHINA.IRS=. A
trade in the innermost set is in all of them, because it is in the
innermost set at all.

** Membership is identity

The path is the trade's address. Two systems that agree on the path agree
on where the trade is, and a trade that moves to another book has changed
a versioned attribute of the trade rather than a row in a membership
table.

So the entity and the set are two readings of one thing. The book is the
set's extent, and the set is the book's address. Neither is prior to the
other, and a model that stores them separately has two facts to keep in
step.

** The structure

A structure is the same shape in miniature. Its identity is its
membership: a structure whose legs differ is a different structure, and
that is why [[id:FC31FC9C-7A6E-4EFD-8A62-6DE4137F7160][Trade Structures and Deal
Composition]] can say that a change to any leg is a change to
the structure. The customer confirms the structure, not the legs, and the
confirmation names one deal.

** The test

Ask whether a trade can be in two sets of one kind at the same time.

A trade is in one book, faces one counterparty, sits in one netting set,
and belongs to at most one structure. It is in many batches, many
selections and many report subjects over its life.

Where the answer is one, the sets nest and membership is part of the
trade's identity. Where the answer is many, they do not nest and
membership is an annotation.

** The counter-case

A batch. A trade is in many batches over its life, and none of them says
what the trade is. Removing the trade from a batch, or the batch from the
record, leaves the trade identical in every respect that matters to
anyone else.

The same holds for a report subject and for a user's selection. These
sets are real, and they can be recorded and referred to, but they are not
part of the trade's address.

** What it decides

- Nested membership is a key on the trade and is versioned with the
  booking.
- Flat membership is a row in a [[id:D6CF158A-69D1-4C1A-8FF3-0F1F4836B16B][trade group]] and is not versioned
  with the trade.
- A question about the trade's book is well formed. A question about the
  trade's batch is not, because there is no single answer.
- Putting a trade into a batch is not a trade amendment. Moving it
  between books is, and [[id:6CB4879F-3F4B-48CE-A01E-E1B9AEE866B0][Book moves]] describes the
  versioned form of the change.
