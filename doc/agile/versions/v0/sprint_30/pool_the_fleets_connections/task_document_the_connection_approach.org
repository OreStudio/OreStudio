* Goal

A reader of the knowledge graph can say, without reading code or this sprint,
which connections a service holds, how many it may burst to, what stands between
the fleet and a shared PostgreSQL, why a developer's own database has neither, and
what to change to hold fewer. The pages state the target state, as if it had
always been so.

* Acceptance

- The knowledge graph carries a note per concept - the service's connections,
  the pooler, the switch between them, the paths that stay direct, and the
  sizing rule - each one idea, linked to its neighbours, not one long page.
- The existing pages that state connection behaviour today are corrected rather
  than duplicated: the =ores.database= component overview, the multi-tenancy page
  that mentions pooling, and the knowledge index.
- Every page describes where the system lands, not the route taken to it: no
  history, no "previously", no measurement of what was. Measurements that justify
  a number live in the agile record, which the pages link to.
- A reader can tell from the pages alone whether they need to install anything to
  work on ORE Studio, and the answer is no.
- =compass lint= and the org link check pass, and every new page is reachable
  from the knowledge index.

* Result

The pages were corrected rather than added to, because the cluster already
described most of this approach as though it were deployed.

- *New:* [[id:929A4722-8F06-4DEF-B759-D0800D4AC6D5][A pooler stands where the database is shared]] — the address a service opens its connections to, which deployments put something at it, what must hold in both, and what a pooler costs and buys.
- *Rewritten:* [[id:729F746E-36DD-40A2-A592-6972239E31F0][A service holds a few long-lived connections and never connects per query]] — the pool now states a floor it keeps and a ceiling a burst may reach, sized by the service's workers and lanes, with what happens at the ceiling.
- *Corrected:* [[id:42930B6A-544A-4F7E-ACF4-9ABC60F2B408][The environments share the database through a transaction pooler]] — it stated the pooler as a fact of every environment; it now says where one stands, adds the idle timeout and the administrative paths to what goes around it, and states the prepared-statement condition instead of assuming it.
- *Corrected:* the =ores.database= component overview and the multi-tenancy page, which both asserted a pooler in front of PostgreSQL for every service.
- *Wired:* the new page into the knowledge index beside the data path.

Every page is present tense and describes where the deployment lands, not the
route taken; the numbers that motivate the shape stay in the story and the tasks.
