* Result

* Result

A case tagged =slow= now runs with ten times the limit, and every other case
keeps sixty seconds. The case the clang debug leg timed out on carries the tag.

The cause was cost, not a hang: the case sends a whole page of quotes plus
five through the board and reads it twice, which takes 0.57 s natively and
thirty seconds under valgrind. The watchdog's sixty second limit was written
for a native run.

Verified locally: with a six second base limit the case still passes under
valgrind, in thirty seconds, which it cannot do unless the tag raised its
limit; and the limit still kills a case that is not tagged.
