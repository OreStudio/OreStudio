* Acceptance

- The export writes the volatility type, the shift and the grid into the swaption volatility block of the market data file, and the curve configuration names the surface.
- The package a report uses carries the block, and ORE reads it with no warning about a missing volatility.
- A book of swaptions or caps prices: the report counts the trades it priced and names no swaption volatility among its market gaps.
- A currency with no published set leaves its trades unpriced and names the volatility the report lacked, instead of pricing at a default.

* Notes

- ORE's today's market already names =SwaptionVolatilities= and =CapFloorVolatilities= as components the pricing reads (=analytics_todays_market_collection_kinds_populate.sql=). Nothing has ever written them.
- The ACME books declare =Swaption= in 10 books, =CapFloor= in 10 and =SwaptionStraddle= in 2 (=books.json=), so the gap blocks a real population, not a hypothetical one.
