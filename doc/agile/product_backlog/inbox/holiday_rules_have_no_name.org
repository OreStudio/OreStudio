* What

=calendar_rule= describes a holiday by its shape (fixed date, nth weekday, Easter offset) and has no name. =calendar_date= carries whether a day is a business day and how it was produced, nothing more. Only exceptions (description) and diary events (name) carry words. Add a name to the rule and carry it onto the materialised date.

* Why

A person reading a calendar's holidays sees dates and shapes, not the holidays they know.
