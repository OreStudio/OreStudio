The projection curves of a tenor basis segment's legs, as ORE's
=ProjectionCurvePay=, =ProjectionCurveReceive=, =ProjectionCurveLong= and
=ProjectionCurveShort=. They hold one invariant: each names the curve one leg
of the basis swap projects on. Only a tenor basis segment states them, and each
is optional, so a segment has this part only when its document names one.

A segment holds at most one row of this part, so the generated table adds the
unique index on the live rows of =(tenant_id, curve_segment_id)=.
