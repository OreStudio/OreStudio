M05 classification, PR 3: the twenty-four hand-written domain types

Measured 2026-09-27 on feature/model-missing-trading-families. The task
said twenty-four hand-written domain types have no model and must each be
classified. Read against the models that exist, they fall into four
states, not three: five already have a model, in the shape the
meta-model gives them.

Already modelled, as a field group (5)
--------------------------------------
=trade_audit=, =trade_classification=, =trade_identity=, =trade_lifecycle=
and =trade_parties= are the component's field groups. Each has an org
under =projects/ores.trading/modeling/= (=trade_audit_field_group.org= and
its siblings) and the entity models compose them. Nothing is missing; the
B02 census counts them, which is why they appeared in both lists.

Model to author (9)
-------------------
=activity_type=, =fpml_event_type=, =commodity_instrument=,
=composite_instrument=, =composite_leg=, =credit_instrument=,
=scripted_instrument=, =swap_leg= and =product_type=. Each carries a table
and a hand-written stack and no org. Eight are tables and this PR authors
them; =product_type= is an enumeration on the trades table and waits for
PR 4, which owns that shape (defect 10).

A message, not an entity (10)
-----------------------------
=rates_instrument_variant=, =equity_instrument_variant=,
=fx_instrument_variant=, =instrument=, =instrument_identity=,
=instrument_payload=, =trade_instrument=, =bond_instrument_data=,
=bond_schedule_data= and =trade_envelope_data=. M05 says a joined or
denormalised shape is a message rather than an entity, and each of these
is one: the three variants are discriminated unions over the instrument
sub-types, =instrument= with its identity and payload is the polymorphic
container the wire and the ORE mapper carry, =trade_instrument= is the
trade's joined instrument view, and the three =*_data= types are the
denormalised shapes the mappers produce. None owns a table of its own,
which is the test that separates them from the nine.

Infrastructure (0)
------------------
None. Every type in the list is either modelled, to be modelled, or a
message; there was no residue to record as infrastructure, and nothing is
deleted from this PR.
