* Acceptance

- Each of the fourteen synthetic surfaces publishes bloomberg, ice and tradeweb quotes in a provisioned ACME environment.
- The quotes board of an ACME party carries 910 fx_option quotes for each of bloomberg, ice and tradeweb.
- A surface that publishes nothing logs why, once, and logs again when it publishes.

* Plan

The quotes screen showed no vendor volatility quotes. The vendor
surfaces are generated, offered and demanded, but eleven of the fourteen
surfaces refused every tick: each tick reads the pair's two currency
curves from the run's board, and the board held one curve per currency
that every curve feed wrote. A USD LIBOR curve, the FOMC short end and
the Uniform Volatility Demo collection's three-pillar curves all end
before a surface's longest expiry, so whichever ticked last decided the
board, and the surface published nothing.

1. Show the failure with a test: a projection curve's tick publishes its
   currency's board curve.
2. Let only the curve that discounts a currency write the board: a
   discount or self-discounting curve on the overnight index.
3. Name a board curve by its collection as well as its currency, so a
   surface reads its own vendor's curves.
4. Log why a surface publishes nothing, once when the reason changes, so
   the next failure of this kind is visible.

* Notes

- The refusal reasons came from a temporary log of the surface tick's
  status, which the scheduler drops: =the quote curve ends at 2028-11-22,
  before 2029-11-23= and =the base curve ends at 2036-11-22, before
  2036-11-24=. The permanent log in this task replaces it.
- Spots stay keyed by pair. The surface's quotes do not depend on the
  spot's level once the discounts are held (the design note measures it
  to 1e-12), so another collection's spot changes nothing a vendor quotes.
- Provisioning step 66, =give_party_surface_blueprints=, still ends with
  warnings: the parties' blueprints wait for derived quotes. That is the
  derived end-of-day source, not the vendor quotes, and is out of scope.

* Result

Every vendor volatility surface publishes. In a fresh ACME environment
all fourteen synthetic surfaces publish their bloomberg, ice and
tradeweb quotes, and the quotes board of Acme Corporation Plc carries
910 =fx_option= quotes for each of the three vendors: fourteen pairs,
thirteen expiries, five points each. A curve now writes the run's board
only when it discounts its currency, and a board curve is named by its
collection and its currency. A surface that publishes nothing logs why.
All three acceptance criteria are met.
