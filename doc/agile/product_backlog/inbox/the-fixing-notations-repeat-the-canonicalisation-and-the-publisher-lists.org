* What

Three vendor notations carry the same two blocks: the read-write-compare
canonicalisation of a fixing address, and the scheme and field prelude that every
read opens with. Beside them, the publisher codes are typed twice in
=provider_notation.cpp= — once as a static instance and once in the candidate
list — and six times in the fixing publishers' SQL seed.

* Why

Adding a publisher is eight edits, and the compiler refuses the tests when a
unity build merges two notations that share a helper name. Both costs come from
the same shape, and both disappear when the canonicalisation and the prelude live
in =notation_support.hpp= and the instances are one table of ={code, notation}=.
