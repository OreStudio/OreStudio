* Goal

Today every ACME party holds the same 31 report definitions. No party has a risk report config. The portfolio trees and currencies are not reviewed for realism. Design, per party, the reports that party would run, justify each choice in a document that a reviewer can read later, then seed the matching configuration, portfolio tree, root portfolio and currencies.

* Acceptance

- A design document states, for each of the four parties, which reports it runs and why, from its role, its jurisdiction and its place in the group. Each decision names its justification and the alternative rejected.
- Provisioning gives each party only the reports in the design. Each party publishes its own report definitions dataset, and the generic tiered set no longer reaches the Acme parties or the system party.
- Each party's report definitions carry the configuration they need, with a base currency that matches the party.
- Each party has a realistic portfolio tree, root portfolio and currencies, and each report scope names portfolios that exist for that party.
- The seeds carry no comment that states a count. Existing count comments in the files the task touches are removed.
- Trades are out of scope. The task proves the definitions, configs and trees on a clean provision, and records what it did not prove.

* Plan

Decided with the product owner on 2026-10-11:

1. Each party runs reports designed for it. The same reports on every party
   are not realistic. The design is documented and justified so a reviewer can
   check each decision later.
2. After the reports are chosen, add the configuration those reports need.
3. Trades are ignored for now.
4. The portfolio tree, root portfolio and currencies must be realistic.
5. Seeds carry no comment that states a count, because the count goes stale.

Steps:

1. Write the design document: for each party, its role, jurisdiction and place
   in the group, then the reports it runs, each with a justification and the
   alternative rejected.
2. Give each party its own report definitions dataset, and stop the generic
   set reaching the Acme parties. Done: the tier and jurisdiction filter cannot
   express the design, so the generic set moved to its own bundle and the Acme
   office bundles carry the per-party datasets.
3. Craft and publish each party's configuration documents (pricing engines,
   today's market, curve configuration, conventions), then publish the bindings
   from each report definition to them, then the risk report configurations
   with a base currency that matches the party (design rules D15 to D17). The
   publishing side of three of the four documents does not exist yet, so this
   step needs its own tasks.
4. Rework the portfolio tree, the root portfolio and the currencies per party.
   Done: one Global Portfolio per trading entity with a trading branch and a
   banking branch, the books by market convention, an XVA desk in London, and
   the party currencies from the books.
5. The market data feeds for the new currencies are not part of this task. Other
   work is refactoring the feeds, so this task leaves them alone and only keeps
   the report data correct.
6. Remove the count comments from the seeds the task touches. Done for the report
   definitions seed.
7. Prove on a clean provision, and record what was not proved.

* Notes

Tree and currency rework, 2026-10-11.

- The three trading entities share one builder. London's tree no longer
  replicates the test fixture: its Americas and APAC portfolios, its risk
  management units and its Regulatory Capital root are gone. The staff keep their
  usernames and photos, and only the unit code of the three London desks moved.
- After a clean provision each trading entity has one root named Global
  Portfolio with two branches, in its own currency: GBP, USD and HKD. The holding
  company keeps its treasury portfolio as its root. Every sample sandbox
  anchors under the root.
- The sweep books moved to the banking branch, because a sweep is cash
  management and not trading intent. The IG bond books became the liquidity
  buffer, also in the banking branch.
- The party currencies follow the books and the FX pairs.

Market data feeds, 2026-10-11.

- The product owner ruled that this task does not change the feeds, because other
  work is refactoring them. A first version of the feed change was written and
  removed from the branch before it was pushed.
- The party currencies now follow the books and the FX pairs, so some currencies
  have no feed yet. That is for the feed work to close.
