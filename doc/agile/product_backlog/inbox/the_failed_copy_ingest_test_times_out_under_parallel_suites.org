* What

The test in =projects/ores.marketdata/service/tests/app_feed_ingest_loop_tests.cpp= failed at line 1120: the warm-up tick of a series was not readable within the 60 second wait. The run was the gate's ctest at =-j 4= on 2026-11-03. The same commit passed in a second ctest run at =-j 4= and in two runs of the suite alone. The test uses a source unique to the run, and no other suite uses the tick writer role, so a NATS clash is not the cause. The cause is not known: lock contention in the shared database, or load from the parallel suites.

If the cause cannot be found, give the suite =RUN_SERIAL=, as =ores.marketdata.core.tests= has.

* Why

The gate runs the suites at =-j 4= since the story "Run only the checks a change needs". A flaky suite makes the gate red for changes that do not touch it.
