The file name keeps the first scope of the scenario, the list and the record page. The steps now cover the whole screen.

* Before you start

- The services run. Confirm with =./compass.sh services status=: 24 services running and =nats: running=.
- The web app serves this work tree. It listens on =ORES_WEB_PORT=, which is 21402, so the site is at =http://127.0.0.1:21402=.
- The web bundle that the server serves holds the fix of PR #3601, which reads a binding's history by its source name. Without that fix, step 13 fails with *There is no such binding for this party.* The task branch does not hold the fix until PR #3601 merges, so do not rebuild the web bundle from the task branch before that.
- The database holds the Acme Corporation tenant with a party that owns feed bindings. The bindings are created by the =start_market_feeds= seed step (dataset =synthetic.feeds.bloomberg=), which runs during provisioning:
  #+begin_src sh
  ./compass.sh shell -f projects/ores.shell/scripts/library/provisioning/how_do_i_provision_the_system_with_acme_corporation_holding_group.ores
  #+end_src
- The party holds 55 feed bindings when provisioning finishes, and every one is a synthetic source, enabled and set to auto start. The default party is *Acme Corporation Plc*.
- The source names read =synthetic.bloomberg.<pair>= for an FX spot, such as =synthetic.bloomberg.usdcad=, and =synthetic.bloomberg.<index>= for an interest rate curve, such as =synthetic.bloomberg.dkkdestr=. The feeds tick, so the series column and the last tick carry values.
- A binding names a source. An IR source writes one series for each maturity of its curve, 12 series for =dkkdestr=. The Series column shows the title of the series with the newest observation. All pillars tick at the same instant, so the pillar that shows is not stable. The title leaves out =term=, so it reads *DKK swap rate · fwd_start=0D, tenor=1D*. This is known and is not a failure of this scenario. The task *Label a curve binding by its curve* covers it later.
- The party can bind 24 more sources. They are 14 Refinitiv FX spots, such as =synthetic.refinitiv.audjpy=, and 5 ICE and 5 Tradeweb interest rate curves. The Add dialog offers these sources. No running feed holds any of their series, so the refusal for a held series cannot be reached from the seed data. The steps say what to record.
- Provisioning stopped before on a missing JetStream stream for the =refdata.v1.ops.*= step subjects. If provisioning stops at =publish_bundle: refdata.currency_groups= with =JetStream publish failed: Timeout=, this build does not hold the fix.

** Open the Bindings list from the Market data menu

- In a terminal in this work tree, run =./compass.sh open-as tenant_admin=. Chrome opens on the sign-in page with the form filled in as =tenant_admin@acme_corporation=.
- Press *Sign in*. The home page loads and shows the Acme Corporation tenant.
- Open the *Market data* area. The area shows a card named *Bindings* after the card *Synthetic*. The card reads *The feeds that write market data for this party: the series each one writes and whether it runs.*
- Press the *Bindings* card. The address is =http://127.0.0.1:21402/market-data/bindings=.
- The page shows the title *Bindings* and the lead *The feeds that write market data for this party: which source each one names, the series it writes and whether it runs.*
- The trail above the title reads *Home* then *Market data* then *Bindings*.

* Notes

- The scenario verifies the whole Bindings screen of the story: the menu card, the list, the record page, add, edit and delete, the history timeline with Revert, and the oresmd snags (the series window, the linked URI column, the hint tooltip and the copy hook).
- The environment was verified on 2026-10-15: the Acme tenant is active, the party holds 55 enabled synthetic bindings, and the feeds tick. It was provisioned again on 2026-10-21 on a fresh database. The list states in steps 3 to 6 therefore show rows, not the empty state.
- Only synthetic sources exist in the seed, so the vendor row in step 11 is absent and that step records the absence.
- The scenario was rewritten on 2026-10-21. It had stopped at the list and the record page, and its step that required Edit and Delete to be absent would now fail by design.
- Steps 12 to 15 change data. Step 12 sets the binding back to enabled. Step 13 adds a Revert version. Step 15 deletes and adds a binding. Recreate the database to reset the environment afterwards.
- A curve binding shows one arbitrary pillar in the Series column. This is known and is not a failure. The task *Label a curve binding by its curve* covers it.
- The steps that need a state the seed cannot give, such as a vendor binding, a refusal for a held series, or an empty window, say what to record when the state is out of reach.
