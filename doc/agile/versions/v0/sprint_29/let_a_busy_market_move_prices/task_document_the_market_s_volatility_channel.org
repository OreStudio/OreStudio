* Acceptance

- The note says what the existing stochastic processes already carry, and names
  the three pieces of plumbing that are missing rather than proposing
  infrastructure.
- The note separates sampling from time: a rate change changes how often a series
  is observed, and a process that steps once per tick currently gains variance
  per wall-clock second. It says which processes behave which way and what the
  fix is.
- The note states the volatility loading: the same factor, its own per-feed
  loading, and the normalisation that keeps a run's long-run sigma at the sigma
  its configuration states.
- The note states the tail channel through the FX mixture, and why a diffusion
  scale alone cannot fatten a panic's tails.
- The note states the control plane: the existing switch, the existing tab and
  one new number per feed. No new verb and no new service.
- The note states, for each part, the test that proves it, and which of the two
  meanings of volatility that test measures.
- The note says what is out of scope, and that the bivariate Hawkes is a capture
  rather than a task.
- The three tasks the note scopes are raised, in the order it recommends.

* Result

* Notes

The note's sections, which are also the outline of the work:

1. *What the factor is today.* One shared state and one shared heat, read by the
   arrival processes as a rate multiplier. The arrival work, and what it left
   open.
2. *Sampling against time.* Business time, integrated activity, and why a
   diffusion's variance follows elapsed time and not the number of observations.
   The processes that take the gap against the ones that step once per tick, and
   the inconsistency between them.
3. *The volatility loading.* The factor in the link, the loading per feed, the
   setters, and the normalisation that keeps calibration. Two meanings of
   volatility, and which one a test measures.
4. *The tails.* The Gaussian mixture, reweighting toward the wide components as
   activity rises, and why a diffusion scale cannot produce a panic's kurtosis.
5. *The control plane.* What a person sees and sets, and what it costs: the same
   switch, the same tab, one number per feed.
6. *Out of scope.* The bivariate Hawkes, its 2x2 kernel and the spectral-radius
   stability condition, captured rather than scoped.
