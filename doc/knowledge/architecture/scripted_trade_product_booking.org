#+title: Scripted Trade Product Booking
#+description: The native and scripted forms in which a product may be booked, the basis on which one is chosen, and what a booking records of the choice.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :scripting:trading:architecture:
#+created: 2026-11-21
#+updated: 2026-11-21

* Summary

A product admits one or more booking forms. Its native form is the element
through which ORE reads it, such as =EquityBasketOptionData=; its scripted form
is a generic =ScriptedTrade= over a library script that encodes the same payoff,
such as =VanillaBasketOption=. Products differ in the forms they admit: some
have only a native form, some have both, and some have only a scripted form,
because ORE provides no element for them. The form is selected at booking and
recorded on the trade, so that the trade is subsequently presented, amended and
reported in the form in which it was booked.

* Detail

** Classification of products by form

| Forms admitted | Examples | Booking |
|----------------+----------+---------|
| native only | =Swap=, =FxForward= | the product's element |
| native and scripted | =EquityBasketOption=, =FxGenericBarrierOption= | the element, or a ScriptedTrade over the script named in the =Scripted forms= table of [[id:A8E9807F-1BC8-4DA5-9A66-720D2444DA1B][the trade type catalogue]] |
| scripted only | gamma swap, corridor variance swap | a ScriptedTrade over the corresponding library script |

Where a product admits both forms, the two express identical economic terms,
and booking in the scripted form maps those terms onto the script's inputs, as
described in [[id:206312C0-6398-4B9D-86F8-D8AEC9A5CEAD][Twin Recipes: Mapping Native Terms to Script Inputs]]. Where a product admits only the scripted form, its
fields are the inputs of its script, as described in [[id:92449D70-09E2-4391-B716-A1001F22B271][Derivation of Script Inputs from Free Variables]].

** The basis of the choice

The two forms of a product are priced by different engines, and therefore differ
in cost and behaviour. A native element may be priced analytically, whereas a
script is priced by ORE's scripted engines, by Monte Carlo simulation or finite
differences according to the pricing configuration of the script's product tag.
The choice is accordingly presented together with its cost, which the product's
schema states ([[id:290A1069-A1EE-4DD1-8D23-1C60F76F8949][Product Schemas for Native and Scripted Products]]). For every product admitting both forms, the ORE
engine test values the two and compares the results, so that a divergence
between implementations is detected.

** What a booking records

A trade records its product and the form in which it was booked; a scripted
trade further records the script and version on which it was booked
([[id:358CC548-3892-45A1-A916-DF4A48ADED03][Library and Inline Scripts in Scripted Trades]]). Reporting and risk aggregation classify a scripted trade under
its product, the form =ScriptedTrade= being a property of representation rather
than of economics.
