* Acceptance

- convention_lookup answers what each bond builder asks.
- The bond planner draws values and calls the builders; its construct_* functions and *_terms structs are deleted.
- The bond entries leave the recorded trades' known lists, and the recorded files change in the same commit.
- The ORE engine test reads and builds every booked bond type.
- The roundtrip tests read every ORE sample trade of each booked bond type and write back every element ORE needs.

* Plan

- The export test joined a bond's issue but none of its other rows, while the
  export reader joins them all. So the recorded bonds were not what a real
  export writes: Bond, CallableBond and ConvertibleBond lacked their coupon
  leg, BondOption its strike, and BondFuture, BondTRS and ForwardBond were
  refused. The reader's assembly becomes a pure function over the rows,
  =bond_legs_of= for the issue's legs and one for the trade's rows, which the
  reader and the test both call.
- The test scope states a reference curve, as a tenant's generation config
  does; the Products example names =EUR-EURIBOR-6M=.
- A booking carries a bond trade's own legs: the trade-type catalogue lists the
  trade-keyed bond leg tables, so the generated batch and its writer carry a
  repo's cash leg, a TRS's funding leg and an Ascot's swap leg through the
  same path as every other row. The user chose this on 2026-11-11.
- One builder per booked bond type. A bond issue builder states the security
  and its coupon leg; each trade builder states the rows its product needs:
  the option block and strike of an option, the cash leg of a repo, the
  funding leg of a TRS, the swap leg of an Ascot, the identifier of a position.
- The =construct_*= functions, their terms and the plan's =bond_terms= are
  deleted.

* Result

A bond issue builder states the security and its coupon leg, and one builder
per booked bond type states the rows its product needs, in =bond_builders.hpp=.
The planner calls them; the bond constructors, =bond_terms= and the old
=bond_instruments.hpp= are deleted, and =bond_product= has a header of its own.

The export test now assembles each bond through =assemble_bond=, the reader's
own assembly, so a recorded bond is what a real export writes. Three apparent
ORE failures were the test's: the coupon leg and the option strike were never
joined, and BondFuture, BondTRS and ForwardBond were refused.

ORE now reads every booked bond type. Bond, BondRepo, BondTRS and ForwardBond
build and price. The rest fail only on what the Products example lacks: equity
curves for the convertible's shares (ConvertibleBond, Ascot), a bond volatility
curve (BondOption), reference data for the generated security (BondPosition),
the issuer's credit curve (CallableBond, whose engine dereferences an empty
default curve without one) and a BondFuture engine.

The callable bond writer's call stand-ins are now the Products example's:
Bermudan, at par, clean, with accrual. They were an empty style and a price of
zero, which ORE refused. Storing each call's own terms is the capture
[[id:A75186C1-ECE1-4CD2-8511-64F4EA2ED729][Store a callable bond's call terms]].
