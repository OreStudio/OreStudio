#+title: An NDF pays a cash difference on its settlement date
#+description: The fixing date sets the rate and the settlement date sets the cash flow, so discounting runs from the settlement date.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:

#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

An NDF settles the difference between its contracted rate and the fixing rate, on an agreed notional, in a deliverable currency. The fixing date sets the rate. The settlement date, usually one or two business days later, sets the cash flow.

* Detail

ORE values a cash-settled forward by discounting from its payment date. Before the fixing date it uses the forward rate. After the fixing date it uses the fixing, so spot no longer changes the settlement amount.
