#+title: Add a panic state to the regime
#+description: A third state above active: rare, short and much faster, entered only from active and left back to active, so the chain can show a market that is not just busy but disorderly. The chain becomes a three-state Markov chain, the calm multiplier is derived so the mean pace stays one, and a person can pin panic as they pin active.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The chain gains a third state, panic, above active. Panic is entered only from
active and left only back to active, so a panic is always a spike on top of a
busy spell and never a jump out of a calm one. It has its own multiplier, for
example four, and its own mean spell, short (a minute rather than five), and a
rate at which an active market tips into it, low (once an hour on average). The
calm multiplier is still derived, from the three states' long-run shares, so
the mean pace stays one; a panic that is too frequent or too fast leaves no
positive calm multiplier and is refused, naming the share, as the two-state
chain refuses today.

Everything downstream follows the pace, so it needs no change of its own: the
heat's share rides on the panic multiplier, every feed's rate scales by it, and
the tail loading tilts by =ln 4= rather than =ln 2=, so prices gap hardest in a
panic. A person can pin panic as they pin active, and the Market screen gives it
its own colour (the product's =down=) on the state badge, the bands and the
folded hour.

* Why

Two states say a market is quiet or busy. A stress run and a demonstration want
the third thing a desk means by a bad day: not busier, but disorderly, for a
short while. Today the nearest is to pin active and raise the tail loading,
which makes a busy market rougher everywhere rather than a panic that comes and
goes. Gemini's review drew a third state as well (*volatile burst*).

The chain is small and owned by one loop, so a third state is a contained
change: the dwell draws, the transition rule, the calm multiplier's derivation
and the inverse =time_to_reach= all generalise from two states to a little
three-state matrix. The settings grow by three numbers, the read and the trace
by one state name, and the screens by one colour.

It is independent of making the transition rates depend on the heat: a panic
can be exogenous, entered by the chain's own draws, and the heat coupling can
come later and apply to every transition, panic's included.
