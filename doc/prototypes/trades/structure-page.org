* Summary

A structure is an entity: an immutable identity and legs that are trades. The prototype draws its page and the workflow [[id:3899F17C-232B-408C-B667-FA5C630BB71B][Group trades into a structure]].

** What the prototype argues

- The version is read from the legs and is never stored beside them. The page says so and lists the legs' external versions. The rule is the one the [[id:7B737119-099C-451D-9319-1D6BEE73A063][Trade Entry Prototype]] shows: a change to any component moves the structure, and authorisation does not.
- The client states the rules the database enforces: every leg shares the party and the counterparty, and a structure nests one level at most. The person sees the check before the server refuses.
- A trade moves from one structure to another without being rewritten.
- Pricing and composition stay in the [[id:7B737119-099C-451D-9319-1D6BEE73A063][Trade Entry Prototype]]. The Workflows menu links to both.

** What is fake

The structures. Grouping stores the new structure in the browser.
