#+title: Adding an asset class
#+description: The two places a new asset class plugs in and the list of edits a new kind still requires outside the factory seam.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :marketdata:feeds:producers:extension:
#+created: 2026-10-06
#+updated: 2026-10-07

* Summary

A new asset class plugs into two places. Its configuration rows become a
typed build input, and its producer implements =IFeed= behind a new kind
string. Everything outside those two places stays as it is, which covers the
feed controller, the producer subject builder, the market tick type and the
ingest loop.

* Detail

** The two plug in points

1. The build input. Add a struct to =feed_build_input= in
   =ores.synthetic/api/feeds/feed_factory.hpp= that carries the new config
   entity plus the child rows its builder needs, and add the struct as a
   variant member.
2. The producer. Implement =IFeed= and register it in
   =make_default_feed_factory= through =feed_factory::register_kind=, using
   a kind constant declared beside the producer. The two constants in the
   tree today are =fx_spot_feed_kind= and =ir_curve_feed_kind=.

** The edits a new kind requires

A new kind is four edits, and none of them is in a handler.

1. Its own codegen entity model, regenerated, because the config table is
   the asset class's own.
2. A struct in =feed_build_input= and a builder registered in
   =make_default_feed_factory()=, in =ores.synthetic/api=. The inputs
   genuinely differ, so the variant is real.
3. One =register_kind= call in =make_default_feed_kind_registry()=, in
   =ores.synthetic/service/src/feed_kind_registry.cpp=. That entry carries
   the kind's config read permission, its config-row loader, its lazy
   build-input resolver, its vintage anchor and its simulate decode/reply
   pair.
4. Its permission row and role grant in the IAM populate SQL. The gate over
   a span of kinds is the conjunction of every registered kind's config read
   permission, so a kind with no grant refuses every caller of =list=, the
   folder cascade and vintage validity. This step is easy to forget because
   it is data, not code.

=feed_config_handler=, =folder_feed_control_handler=,
=vintage_validity_handler=, =simulate_handler=, =ir_curve_preview_handler=
and =application.cpp= need no edit, and =feed_controller=,
=producer_subject=, =market_tick= and =feed_ingest_loop= stay untouched.

** Why the list is the point

The list is the evidence about where the seam ends today. It shrank from
three service call sites to a registration and a grant when the per-kind
registry landed; before that, a note claiming one registration would have
been wrong. The one entry point the registry cannot absorb is the SQL grant,
so it is named rather than implied.
