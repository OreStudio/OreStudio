* Goal

The commodity types ORE builds with their own classes, rather than as
scripted trades, are written and read by the projection and built by their
builders, and the ORE engine test builds each one.

* Acceptance

- The projection writes and reads the digital option, the digital average
  price option, the position, the spread option and the intraday power
  forward.
- Each of them is built by its builder, and the planner calls it.
- The commodity row gains the columns these products state, through the
  codegen model.
- Their entries leave the known lists, or stay with a market data reason.
- The rest of the commodity work is in three tasks:
  - [[id:00D52EE5-E337-493E-8442-280910E072B5][Project and build the single commodity scripted products]]
  - [[id:4A8E426E-9DC6-4864-80C1-4F73799DCEAF][Project and build the commodity basket scripted products]]
  - [[id:1F6D904E-2BCA-4336-94F7-3EFD4805CABF][Close out the commodity constructors]]

* Plan

1. Add the columns the products state to the commodity model, through
   codegen. Only the intraday power forward needs new ones: the delivery
   date, and the delivery start and end as seconds of that day.
2. Keep a digital option's cash payoff in the row's quantity, as the equity
   digital option keeps its payoff in its notional. A payoff column of its
   own would state the same amount twice.
3. Keep the spread option's strike in the strike column and its two legs in
   the shared leg rows. The row names the first leg's commodity and keeps
   the second leg's as the spread commodity.
4. Replace the projection's three lists of trade types, option data
   elements and readers and writers with one table of products, and add the
   five types to it. The delivery terms land through the scalar terms
   table, and the position's commodities through the shared underlying
   rows.
5. Add a builder for each type, register it, and call it from the planner.
   A spread option draws a second commodity quoted in the first one's
   currency, because ORE requires one currency.
6. Record the generated trades, run the ORE engine test, and update the
   known lists.
