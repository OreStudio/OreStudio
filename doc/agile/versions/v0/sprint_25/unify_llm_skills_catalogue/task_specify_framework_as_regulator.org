* Goal

The framework states what it regulates, why those seven classes of document and
no others, and where it stops. Its terminology agrees with the cybernetics it
borrows from. Its relationship to upstream skill collections is stated per skill
and is checkable.

* Acceptance

- Scope is derived from the good regulator theorem, requisite variety and the
  black box, rather than stipulated, and the derivation settles which targets
  the framework models.
- The seven classes are derived from the primitives of a control loop, so a
  candidate eighth has a test rather than a preference.
- The session role is named System, after Beer, and the seven classes are named
  regulatory functions; neither collides with the other senses the corpus
  carries.
- Every upstream pstack skill carries a regulatory function and a disposition,
  and nothing upstream is forked.
- The corpus has a writing standard for argumentative prose, grounded in
  published work and applied across the cluster.
- Rules the framework states are enforced by lint wherever they can be.
