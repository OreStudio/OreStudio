#+title: A client's forward rate folds the sales margin into the all-in rate
#+description: The market forward points are separate from the provider's margin, and the client sees one rate.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

Forward points are market-driven. A provider's margin is separate, but it is folded into the all-in forward rate, so a client sees one number.

* Detail

To measure a margin, compare the all-in rate with the market outright at the same time.
