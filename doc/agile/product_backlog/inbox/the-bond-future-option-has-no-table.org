#+description: BondFutureOption is a bond product with no routed table. The trade type catalogue lists it with product_type bond, has_options true and an empty instrument column, so its row cannot be booked anywhere and the planner cannot draw it: a tenant holds no option on a bond future, and the constructor written for it is never reached. Either the catalogue routes it to bond_instrument, which needs its own fact shape because it states a contract and an option at once, or it is declared an unbookable product with the reason recorded.
