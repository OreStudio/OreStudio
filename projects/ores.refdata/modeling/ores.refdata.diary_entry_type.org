Reference data classifying what a [[id:B20050A5-1245-4944-A328-2A0893C92AEC][calendar_event]] entry
represents. Values include: =holiday=, =central_bank_meeting=,
=data_release=, =other=. Lets a single =calendar_events= table hold
heterogeneous diary entries -- a central bank's meeting schedule, a
statistical office's release calendar, an open-ended memo entry --
without collapsing their distinct semantics.

The vocabulary is deliberately open-ended: consumers (e.g. the tenor
resolution engine's event-lookup schedules) key off specific codes,
but new codes can be added without schema change.
