* Goal

The republish subject ends in one token, the whole URI of the datum, so a wildcard can select a whole party and nothing smaller. Split the tail into the instrument type, the series token and the coordinate token.

* Acceptance

- A client that subscribes to one series receives only the ticks of that series.
- A subscription to a class and a subscription to a datum work the same way.
- The notes on the producer subject and on the market tick state the new subject.
- Hedgr subscribes for each watched series and no longer filters the whole party on the client.
