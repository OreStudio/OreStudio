* Decisions

- *Qualify the two calls; do not move the spelling helpers.* =spelling_of= is a
  free function in the file's anonymous namespace and =instrument_spelling= /
  =quote_spelling= are static members of =oresmd_uri_codec=, so the call needs the
  class name. The alternative — making =spelling_of= a static member too — fixes
  the same lookup but moves four helpers and both overloads to buy nothing.
- *Route the reader through the same two members.* The header says the helpers
  exist so that the spelling a reader compares against is the spelling the writer
  produces. =read()= still re-derived =lower(ore_name(...))= itself, so the claim
  was not true. Both directions now call one function.

* Out of scope

- Why the break reached =main=. It landed through the merge-without-checks
  workflow and the hourly validation build had not yet reached it.
