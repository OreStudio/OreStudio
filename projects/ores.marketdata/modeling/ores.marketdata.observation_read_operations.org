#+title: ores.marketdata.observation_read_operations

#+brief: Reading the durable observations of a series.
#+filetags: :model:operation:marketdata:
#+created: 2026-11-03
#+updated: 2026-11-03

A consumer, a backtest first, asks for the observations of a series over a window of
days and gets each value with its coordinate, the time it is for, and its source. The
item is the series' oresmd URI, so one request reads every point of a surface or a
curve on every day in the window. The values stay decimal text, as they are stored.
