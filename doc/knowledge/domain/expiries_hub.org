* Summary

An option /expires/ at its expiry date and cut, and at that moment it is
exercised, expires worthless, or settles from a fixing. Expiry is where an
option stops being a risk and becomes a trade, cash or nothing, so errors are
costly and visible to clients. This structure note gathers the notes on
expiries. Fixings are their automated counterpart and have a structure note of
their own: [[id:784D9D97-721A-42EC-BA91-A85764979CD0][Fixings]].
