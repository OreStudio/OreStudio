* Goal

Loading the public rates is an ordered, recorded and retryable act, not a side effect of an adapter being accepted. The official service serves a step command that backfills every accepted public source over its window and completes when the fixings are stored, reporting per source what it stored. The step is idempotent: a repeat stores nothing it already holds. The hourly poll stays as it is.

* Acceptance

- A run of the step on a fresh installation completes only after each accepted source's fixings are stored, and its result names each source and its count.
- A second run of the step stores nothing new and completes.
- A source whose terms are not accepted is reported as skipped, not as a failure.
