* Summary

=ores.http.marketdata= is the adapter part that projects the market data domain onto
the HTTP server's REST surface. It holds the units the market data screens call
at the addresses the BFF answered: the adapters' status, the subscriptions and
routes a desk follows, the public rates, the curves, and the ORE URI codec.

The units speak the canonical protocol over NATS and hold no service,
repository or database context: the market data service stays the only place that
decides, and the caller's own token travels with every forwarded request.

The part declares =#+component_kind: adapter=. Its =CMakeLists.txt= files are
hand-authored, because the link libraries belong to the HTTP surface rather
than to the domain, and codegen writes only the two =component_files.cmake=
lists. A regeneration therefore never writes over the build.

The units answer under the =market-data= area of the route inventory.

* Inputs

- HTTP requests on the market data screens' paths the units register.
- The caller's bearer token, forwarded to the service on every NATS request.
- The connected NATS client the hand-written aggregate passes to
  =register_routes=.

* Outputs

- The routes and their OpenAPI schemas, registered on the router and the
  endpoint registry.
- NATS request messages to the market data service, one or more per route.

* Entry points

- =include/ores.http/routes/marketdata/= -- the unit headers.
- =src/routes/marketdata/= -- the unit implementations.
- =marketdata_routes= -- the aggregate the host calls to install every unit.

* Dependencies

- =ores.http.api= -- the router, the route builder, the OpenAPI registry and
  the request/response types.
- =ores.http.nats= -- the gateway's one forwarding step to the services.
- =ores.nats= -- NATS transport for service calls.
- =ores.marketdata.api= -- the canonical protocol messages the units send.
- =ores.utility= -- the codecs and the wire support types.
- =ores.logging= -- the logger factory the part's tests log through.
