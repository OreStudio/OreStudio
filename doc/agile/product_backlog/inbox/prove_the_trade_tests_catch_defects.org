This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Two checks that prove the trade tests themselves: a property-based roundtrip
that writes and reads back values neither the samples nor the generator state,
and mutation testing that lists every reader or writer line no test watches.

* Why

The roundtrip and ORE engine tests prove only what the samples and the
generator happen to state, so a reader or writer line no test reaches can be
wrong unseen. The work was a task of [[id:0CAC2F0E-69EC-4497-8587-4E0A6593BC7D][Hold every product type the engine
supports]], which was abandoned for the scripted trade redesign; it is kept here
so it is not lost.
