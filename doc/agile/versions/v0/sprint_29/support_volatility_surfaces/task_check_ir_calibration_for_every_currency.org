* Goal

Every supported currency's synthetic IR curve starts from public data, or the gap is stated. Today only FX has a calibration policy: the seed in synthetic_calibration_policies_populate.sql covers FX alone, while the IR curve configs cover about twenty currencies. The public curve sources documented so far are the US Treasury par curve, SOFR, SONIA and the ECB's euro short-term rate. For each supported currency, find its overnight rate or par curve publisher, read its terms, and set up the source, the policy and the history, or record why not.

* Acceptance

- A table lists every currency an IR curve config supports, with its public source, the state of that source's terms, its calibration policy and its stored history.
- Every currency with a usable public source has a calibration policy, and its IR curve feed starts from the latest published value.
- Every currency without one keeps a stated fixed level, and the reason is recorded in the calibration sources note.
- The calibration screen shows the IR coverage, as it shows the FX coverage.
