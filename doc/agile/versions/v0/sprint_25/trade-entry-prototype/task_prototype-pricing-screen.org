* Goal

Ticket type: prototype (human in the loop).

Build the throwaway prototype of the pricing screen and its Book handoff.
The prototype fakes the valuation. The point is the two pricing modes and the
handoff.

The prototype covers:

- single-leg mode and structure mode;
- the condensed and the expanded view of a structure, with hedges at leg
  level;
- market data shown as ticking;
- inputs the user can move, such as vols and rates, with the NPV responding;
- the NPV readout;
- the Book button, which carries the economics to the booking screen.
