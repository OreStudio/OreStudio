#+brief: Market data provider that owns a namespace of vendor ids: a vendor, an exchange, a broker, or a reference set.
#+created: 2026-10-22
#+updated: 2026-10-22

The catalogue of market data providers: data vendors such as Bloomberg and LSEG,
exchanges such as CME, brokers, and reference sets such as the ORE examples. A
provider owns a namespace of vendor ids. Every source belongs to one provider, and
every vendor instrument and mapping belongs to a provider, so a live and a
simulated source of one provider share their ids and their mapping. The name
carries no mark of any source kind.

The system tenant and the system party own the rows. Every tenant reads them and
only the system tenant writes them.

** terms_accepted_digest
:PROPERTIES:
:type:          text
:cpp_type:      std::string
:nullable:      false
:default:       ''
:default_value: ""
:END:

The digest of the terms when they were accepted: the SHA-256 of the terms page, the
credit and the storage permission. The terms hold while it matches the digest of the
terms as they stand, so a change of any of them clears the acceptance with no further
write. Empty when the terms were never accepted.
