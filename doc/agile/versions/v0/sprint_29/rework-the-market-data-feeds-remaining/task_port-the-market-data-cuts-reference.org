* Result

A durable reference for cuts, as a structure note and four child pages.
[[id:B5214F3A-A72E-421E-82BC-79338B2E95CB][Cuts]] states the definition and the
body, and beneath it sit [[id:33E879B7-496A-406D-B3F2-72637379AD02][Kinds of cut]],
[[id:DD59B04C-DAD2-458C-B35E-3F6B0B3D6DC6][Market data cut]],
[[id:19630B5D-A163-42B5-B9C4-61A87BAE8F5F][Trade cut]] and
[[id:3B39B2E2-6E05-4DF8-B68B-6ACA12F2FCE9][The option-expiry cut code]]. The
single page this task first produced was split into those pages at the product
owner's direction, and its verified content moved with it. The kinds later moved
out of the hub into a page of their own, since a classification is a subject in
its own right.

- The cluster carries both senses of the word, what a cut is, the deal cut and
  the market data cut, the four kinds, the end of day process and its rate
  overrides, multiple centres, what a cut holds, profiles, one cut per book,
  option-expiry cut codes, audit and rerun, our position, a glossary and
  references. No notebook is referenced, and no vendor system appears in the
  conceptual pages. The prior-art survey in [[id:6810B313-1BE8-4889-A3C0-30174ADCBEDD][Naming the cut]] names
  systems, because the naming question requires it.
- Every non-repository claim rests on a public source fetched and read while
  writing: EBA RTS (EU) 2016/101 Art. 3 for independent price verification and
  its accepted sources; the ECB euro reference rates for an official daily rate;
  Fedwire operating hours for a centre's stated business day.
- Claims with no verifiable public source are marked as our working convention
  and listed under What the sources establish: the deal-cut then market-data-cut
  order, the flash and start-of-day runs, the six-step end of day sequence, one
  cut per book, the after-hours rule and the specific option cut times. The
  advisory sentence about inferring a cut from the volatility surface was
  dropped.
- Our own position is verified against the notes and the schema and linked: the
  as-of read and its lack of an as-of parameter; the resolution-side snapshot as
  a definition with no implementation; the cut time as a designed lookup with no
  table; the population as unmodelled; the report binding as unable to name the
  population.
- The hub is registered in the knowledge index as a hub, and linked from the two
  snapshot notes, the pricing configuration, the market data architecture hub,
  risk reporting, report processing, the reports index, the report-binding
  design and its journey, the market bindings prototype and the backlog story.
  Each child links the hub back, and the structure note check passes.
- The report binding's open question now points at the hub: a binding names a
  published set and cannot name the population, so the cut model is the
  binding's missing foundation.

Checks: compass lint valid; the org link check 0 unresolved; the structure note
back-link check green across 25 notes; compass site page published the sixteen
touched pages with no failures. Open on the pages: the option cut times, whether
a book may hold more than one cut, and whether the story's cut time and a
population cut are one entity.

The consequence for the report binding: binding a population needs a cut that
does not exist yet, so the cut model is the binding's foundation.
