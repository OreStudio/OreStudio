* Result

The generator books the three containers, each with the trades it holds, and
ORE builds each.

- New builders in ores.trading.api/builders/composite_builders: the composite
  states its currency and notional calculation; the contract for difference its
  return leg; the total return swap its return leg and a funding leg, the
  floating leg of a vanilla swap on the index's conventions, paid by the side
  that receives the return.
- The equity planner books the containers. A composite is a synthetic forward,
  a long call and a short put at one strike and expiry. A total return swap and
  a contract for difference pay the return on a position in an equity quoted in
  the scope's currency. The held trades are trades of their own, joined to the
  container by a structure of its template, CompositeTrade or TotalReturnSwap,
  in the roles the import books: composite or total_return_swap, and sub_trade.
  The package grouping leaves them alone.
- The recorded export writes each container with its trades inside it, and the
  read-back test joins the imported trades to their container as the round trip
  does. A container states its counterparty's envelope: ORE gives a container's
  trades the container's envelope and refuses a trade without one.
- The composite no longer writes its description as a basket name.
- The three types leave the list awaiting builders, and the coverage test holds
  them as generated. The engine raises no error on any of them.

Verified: trading api 4650 cases, synthetic 146 with the ORE engine.
