This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

=cut= (26 members) carries =requires_authorisation= with
=approval_request_id=, =approved_by=, =approved_at=, =refused_at= and
=refusal_comment= beside it: a flag followed by its block, while
=cut_approval_detail= is already a part of the cut. =report_instance= (21)
carries =cut_label=, =cut_kind_code= and =cut_timepoint= beside =cut_id=: a copy
of the cut that the instance names by key.

* Why

The re-inventory of the "Split the types that carry too many columns" story
lists both types over the twenty-member threshold. A verdict outside refdata is
a reading, so the reporting owner confirms it or argues it down. The copy can be
deliberate, for example a record of the cut as it was when the run started. If
so, the model says so and the reading is closed.
