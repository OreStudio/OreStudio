This page documents a [[id:F44766FA-1E9C-416F-849A-E131101B2ECA][user journey]]: A trader or an operations user reads a trade, its instrument, its parties and how it came to be in its present form.

A user journey describes what one person is trying to do, before anything is
built. It is a design document that outlives any sprint. These trade journeys are
use cases, so they name the operations and messages each step needs and leave the
screens to the UX standards.

* Goal

"I want to see this trade as it stands, who it is with, what it is, and how it came to be
this way, so that I can answer a question about it without asking anyone." The journey is
done when the person has read the trade and, if they asked, any earlier version of it.

* Actor and trigger

The actor is a trader or an operations user who may read the trade's party. The trigger
is a question: a counterparty query, a break in a report, an audit request. The journey
starts at a list of trades, or at a link from another record, and ends when the person
leaves the trade.

* Steps

1. *Find the trade.* The person searches the trades by identifier, counterparty, book,
   trade type, state or date. The system lists them a page at a time.
2. *Read the trade.* The system shows the parties and the book, how the deal was
   struck, the state, the instrument terms with their legs and schedules, the
   identifiers, the party roles, the portfolios and the additional fields. Every other
   record is a link to its own page.
3. *Read the history.* The system lists every version of the trade, and every activity
   that caused one, with who did it, when and why. The person sees the difference between
   two versions.
4. *Read the trade as it stood.* The person chooses a date. The system shows the trade as
   of that date.
5. *Follow a link.* The person goes to the counterparty, the book, a structure the trade
   belongs to, or a linked trade.

* Screens

This journey is a use case. The standard screens serve it: the trade list, the trade page
with a tab for each aspect, and the History timeline, all as the [[id:BD492939-118E-4B55-AE5B-300F7A1C3B41][Entity screen specification]] and the
[[id:2BF7F03F-6BF4-45FF-8E26-4D3B4E2D943D][Record screen standard]] describe. The Qt client had a detail dialog and a history dialog. They become
a page and a tab, and the person reads one subject on one page.

No wireframe is drawn here. The prototype story draws the screens, from the
[[id:00BA1689-B060-4BD8-BC46-67034D663B7F][UX standards]] and from this use case.

* Operations and messages

#+ATTR_HTML: :class hug-leading
| Step | Operation | Subject | Status |
|------+-----------+---------+--------|
| Find | Search the trades by identifier, counterparty, book, state or date | =trading.v1.trades.list= filters only by trade id | missing |
| Read | Read the trade and its instrument | =trading.v1.trades.get=, =trading.v1.<instrument>s.get= | exists |
| Read | Read the booking and the state of a trade | =trading.v1.trade_bookings.list=, =trading.v1.trade_states.list= with =trade_id_one_of= | exists |
| Read | Read the identifiers, party roles, portfolios, additional fields and the instrument's legs of a trade | =trade_identifiers.list=, =trade_party_roles.list=, =trade_portfolios.list=, =trade_additional_fields.list=, =bond_legs.list= and the other child tables: no by-trade filter | missing |
| History | List the versions | =trading.v1.trades_versions.list=, =trading.v1.trades_versions.get= | exists |
| History | List the activities of a trade | =trading.v1.trade_activities.list= filters only by activity id | missing |
| As of | Read the trade and its parts as of a date | the =as_of= field of the list requests (checked on trades, trade states and the bond instrument) | exists |

Status is one of =exists=, =partial=, or =missing=. A subject that contains
=<instrument>= stands for the one instrument table the trade type routes to. The plural
is written with a plain =s=. A table whose plural is irregular, such as
=trade_activities=, has its own subject: check the protocol header, not the pattern.

* What is missing

- The trade list filters only by trade id (=trades_filter= has one field). A person
  cannot search by identifier, counterparty, book, state or date. Each filter is a
  server change. Until it exists, a screen cannot list trades usefully.
- The identifier, party role, portfolio, additional field and instrument child lists
  (checked on =bond_leg=) have no by-trade filter. They list the whole table. A trade
  page cannot read its own parts without reading every trade's.
- The activity list filters only by activity id. It cannot return the activities of one
  trade, so the history cannot show what caused each version.
- =as_of= exists on the reads checked above. It is not checked on every child table.
- The list needs the paging rule of the [[id:BD492939-118E-4B55-AE5B-300F7A1C3B41][Entity screen specification]]: the pager is the request.
