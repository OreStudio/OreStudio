* Goal

A synthetic run states the fixing of every overnight index a trade floats on, in
the administrator's own notation, so a swap's overnight coupon prices without a
live adapter.

* Acceptance

- A provisioned synthetic tenant holds the fixings of ESTR, SONIA and SOFR, each
  under its administrator and in that administrator's notation.
- No item has two producers in the run, and the source a party reaches is the one
  its route picks.
- The overnight coupon of a swap prices from its index's fixing.
- The decision is written on the story, so a reader knows why the simulator states
  an index that a real administrator also publishes.

* Plan

1. *Inventory.* Seventeen fixing administrators have a simulated source on
   =synthetic-local=. The indices whose administrator also has a public adapter
   are left to it: =ecb= for ESTR, =boe= for SONIA, =nyfed= for SOFR. The release
   installs three adapters, frankfurter, synthetic and derived, so none of those
   three is served.
2. *Decide.* The recommendation is that the simulator states them, on a source of
   the same publisher, because the adapters are separate work and the simulator
   already states the other administrators. The sample's public feed stays in the
   catalogue and is the one a live installation switches to.
3. *Add the rows.* One source per publisher on =synthetic-local=, the vendor
   instruments in the administrator's notation, and the mappings to the items that
   are already mapped.
4. *One producer.* Prove the one-producer rule holds for each item: either the
   public feed or the simulated source is the producer a route reaches, and never
   both.
5. *Prove.* A provisioned tenant holds the three fixings, and a swap prices.

* Notes

- The public feeds already exist and are the truth of who administers the item:
  =boe-sonia= on =boe-database=, =nyfed-sofr= on =nyfed-markets=. A synthetic
  installation needs the same items with a simulated producer, not a second
  identity.
- ESTR is the euro short-term rate, which the ECB publishes. The ECB's adapter in
  the release is Frankfurter, and Frankfurter serves the FX reference rates alone.
- SONIA is the one this platform's own smokehouse tenant needs: its overnight swap
  floats on it.
