* Result

Decided: store one tick for each source, owned by the system tenant, and
check the subscription at read time. The page is
[[id:D71F9446-C62E-48A6-A87B-136EF598A59E][One shared tick or one copy for each party]].

On the provisioned Acme tenant the per-party copy is five identical copies of
26 series: 3,427,320 rows and 369.7 MB, where one copy for each source is
685,464 rows. The copies add 80% of the store, and every new routed party adds
another identical copy.

Billing does not need the copies. The delivery meter counts deliveries by
(tenant, party, provider, source, datum, period) in memory and writes
=ores_marketdata_delivery_counts_tbl= (215 rows); the usage recorder writes
=subscription_intervals= (12 rows) and =demand_intervals= (2 rows). Neither
names a series or a tick row.

The shared shape strengthens the tenant boundary: the store holds no tenant's
data, the ingest stops reading every tenant's routes, and the read checks the
subscription and its rights. The costs are the series key losing the party in
the point store, an indexed subscription check on every history read, and the
ingest losing its routes read. There is no migration: the database is
recreated.

What follows: store one tick for each source and point the per-party series at
it; check the subscription in the history read; remove the routes read from
the ingest; update the ingest loop and quote series pages.
