The market and trade building part of a named engine settings configuration. How the engine builds the market and the trades, and what it does when that fails. A change here changes the numbers. A column that is null means the engine default,
and a configuration with no row in this part uses the default for every setting in it. A write to this part bumps the version of the
central =engine_settings= row in the same transaction.
