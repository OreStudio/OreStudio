* Acceptance

- Each Critical and Important finding of the rates review, other than the one recorded as the component's, is closed by a unit that proves it, with the finding's evidence as the test's expected value.
- No column in the family is left without a producer or a consumer at the boundary, or the exception is recorded with its reason.
- A check fails when a model column has no producer or no consumer at the ORE boundary.

The story exists because of
[[id:4A8B1C6E-52D4-4E7A-9F31-6C2D8E4B7A10][Investigation: Rates family data model review]].
Its findings are the units below, and each unit proves its own finding. The
order is the three Criticals first, because they corrupt or block data, then the
Important ones by the cheapest ratio to the most expensive — which is not the
review's recommendation order, where the fact tables and the strike come before
the carrier and the digits — and last the check that would have caught the
family's whole class of defect.
