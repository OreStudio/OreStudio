This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Give the checks one parse of the model and org corpus to share, and run the
codegen suite in parallel.

* Why

About fifteen checks re-read the same corpus in their own process —
=model-drift=, =model-sql-types=, =cmake-sources=, =physical-inventory=,
=recipe-inventories=, =protocol-twins=, =subject-conformance=,
=handler-permissions=, =generated-column-refs= and the rest — and several
sit at 10-23s each (codegen-tests 44.1s, sql-hygiene 23.1s, exported-types
19.8s, paste-blocks 14.8s, enum-attributes 10.9s). =codegen-tests= runs
1425 pytest cases single-threaded, and =pytest-xdist= is not installed in
=projects/ores.codegen/venv=. A parse cache keyed on file content, or one
importable shared module, removes the repeated cost; scoping =sql-hygiene=
and =tangle-shell= to the changed files removes most of theirs.
