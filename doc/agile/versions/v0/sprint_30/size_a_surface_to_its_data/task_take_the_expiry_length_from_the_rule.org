* Goal

derived_fx_expiries holds thirteen labels with a years figure each, a second source of truth beside the blueprint pillars and the expiry-date rule. Read the years from the rule for the as-of day and delete the table, so a label means the same thing to the ladder, the calibration and the derivation.

* Acceptance

- An expiry's years are the ones the surface is calibrated on, =fx_vol_tenor_years=, beside the
  expiry-date rule. (Revised: the first draft read "computed from the pair's convention and the
  expiry-date rule, for the day being derived"; see the Plan.)
- derived_fx_expiries is deleted, and nothing duplicates the ladder it used to state.
- A test pins the years of every label to the calibration's.

* Plan

- *The premise did not hold.* The goal is that a label means the same thing to the ladder, the
  calibration and the derivation. The calibration (=fx_vol_surface_calibration_plan.cpp=) and
  the synthetic surface feed read a pillar's length with =fx_vol_tenor_years=, beside the
  expiry-date rule in =fx_expiry_dates.hpp=, whose documentation keeps the two apart on
  purpose: the rule dates when the cash moves, and the tenor's year fraction is the axis a
  surface is quoted and fitted on, so producer and consumer cannot disagree. Taking the
  derivation's years from the rule's dated expiry would have made it disagree with the
  calibration (1M as 30 or 31 days over 365 against 1/12). The derivation takes the same
  function instead.
- *What is deleted.* The array went in the ladder task; this one deletes the tenor parse that
  task added, which was a copy of =fx_vol_tenor_years=. Nothing in the source states an
  expiry's length now.

* Result

The derived source reads an expiry's length with =fx_vol_tenor_years=, the function the
calibration and the synthetic surface feed read it with, so a label names the same time to the
source that quotes it and to the calibration that fits it. Nothing in the source states a
length, and a test pins every label's years to the calibration's. The first acceptance bullet
was revised: the expiry-date rule dates settlement, which the surface's axis deliberately does
not follow. The formulas are the ones the parse used, so the quotes the source publishes are
unchanged.
