#+title: An end-of-day FX rate set must be complete and independently verified
#+description: End-of-day rates form one consistent set, and rates sourced from the desk need independent price verification.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

An end-of-day market data process aims for one complete and consistent price set that represents the market on that day. It usually runs before P&L computation. A rate that comes from the trading desk needs independent price verification.

* Detail

Valuation control performs this check independently of the end users, in line with prudent valuation guidance.
