#+title: Forward points are the difference between the outright and spot
#+description: The definition of forward points, premium and discount, and what the quoted points contain beyond parity.

* Summary

Forward points are the difference between the forward rate and spot, stated in pips. A forward above spot is at a /premium/ and has positive points. A forward below spot is at a /discount/ and has negative points.

* Detail

With $S$ and $F$ in quote currency per unit of base currency:

$$F = S + \text{points} \times \text{pip factor}$$

By [[id:49687AD7-396A-4CD8-B96B-B3F6675C9779][Covered Interest Parity and FX Forward Pricing]], $F > S$ when the quote currency has the higher interest rate, and $F < S$ when it has the lower one.

Parity gives a theoretical value. Dealer quotes also hold the cross-currency basis, funding and liquidity conditions, the bid-ask spread and credit adjustments.

Parity with positive inputs always gives a positive forward, even when a rate is negative and a discount factor exceeds one.
