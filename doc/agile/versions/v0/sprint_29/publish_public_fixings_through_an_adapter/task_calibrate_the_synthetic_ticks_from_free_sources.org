* Acceptance

- The task reads fixings through [[id:BB5DC80B-EC57-4029-8A8B-6A98E0EE7D6D][Read the latest fixing and a window]]. It fetches nothing itself.
- A generator config carries =price_source= of =fixed= or =fixing=. For =fixing= it names the FX fixing items it may start from, each with a rank, a policy (=latest_on_or_before_clock= or =exact:<date>=) and a window in days for the volatility. The task drops the vintage columns.
- A feed starts from the latest fixing of its best-ranked item that has one, on or before the date of the market clock, so a feed that stops and starts takes the newest value. Its volatility is the spread of the log returns over the window.
- A pair with no fixing keeps its fixed level, and the feed screen says so.
- The feed screen shows the provider, the item, the vendor time and the time received of the value a feed started from.
- The 2016 ORE vintage and the vintage datasets calibrate no generator, and the task removes the dependency rows that required them.
