This page documents a test scenario verifying [[id:1994DF76-4830-4510-96BD-3B524E782ED2][Prove the connectivity screens]] in [[id:4D2D0EF9-986A-49F3-91CD-29DF44548590][Build the Connectivity screens]]. It is filled in with the target screen and checklist of steps before testing starts; record the outcome by hand in =* Results=.

* Before you start

The tester opens the client on a ready environment. Do this yourself before handing over.

- The build is green and the services run: =./compass.sh build= then
  =./compass.sh services start=.
- The database is provisioned and the catalogue is published: call
  =ores_marketdata_publish_adapters_from_dq_fn= and
  =ores_marketdata_publish_suppliers_from_dq_fn= for the marketdata dataset.
- Terms are accepted for =ecb=, =imf=, =boc= and =tcmb=, so the public rates
  adapter has demand. Without them the feeds announce and fetch nothing.
- At least one day of fixings is stored for a public publisher; Public rates
  shows no data for a publisher that has never delivered.
- The environment serves the screens at the address =compass services status=
  prints, on the paths =/market-data/connectivity= and =/market-data/public-rates=.
- Have a system administrator account, =super_admin= or a tenant administrator
  who may accept terms, and its password.

* Steps

Each step is its own heading. Its body is a checklist: what to click, what to
type, and what confirms the step passed. Record the outcome in a =*** Result=
child as PASS, FAIL or PENDING with one line of evidence.

** Log in as super_admin

- Open the client at the address the environment prints.
- Log in as =super_admin= with the password from the provisioning run.
- Confirm the shell opens on the home screen and the Market data area appears.

*** Result

** Open Connectivity

- Choose Market data, then the Connectivity card.
- Confirm the title reads =Connectivity= and the pane opens on =Delivery overview=.
- Confirm the rail lists the suppliers and the publishers, and the overview
  shows five counts.
- Confirm the counts match the catalogue: 11 publishers, 2 suppliers,
  2 adapters, 2 sessions and 10 feeds when this was written.

*** Result

** Read the adapter catalogue

- Click =Adapters catalogue= in the rail.
- Confirm =frankfurter= and =synthetic= are listed with their module versions.
- Confirm the frankfurter row names the process =ores.marketdata.official=,
  the class =fx=, the services =end_of_day= and =reference=, the schema fields
  =url=, =backfill_days= and =poll_minutes=, and one session.
- Confirm the synthetic row states no schema field.

*** Result

** Read the frankfurter supplier

- Pick =Frankfurter= in the delivery tree.
- Confirm the dashboard shows its sessions, feeds and publishers, and the
  services it offers.
- Confirm the Sessions table lists =frankfurter-eu= as enabled and in
  =production=.
- Confirm the Coverage table lists ECB, IMF, BoC and TCMB with class =fx=.
- Confirm the Contract card states the contract reference and the support
  address the catalogue holds.

*** Result

** Open the frankfurter-eu session

- Click =frankfurter-eu= in the tree.
- Confirm supplier =frankfurter=, adapter =frankfurter=, environment
  =production= and endpoint =https://api.frankfurter.dev=.
- Confirm the credential reference is stated and the enabled state reads
  =Enabled=.
- Confirm the Settings table shows =url=, =backfill_days= and =poll_minutes=
  with their values, not raw JSON.

*** Result

** Open the ecb-frankfurter feed

- In the Frankfurter session, click the feed =ecb-frankfurter=.
- Confirm publisher =ecb=, session =frankfurter-eu=, service =end_of_day=,
  provider key =ECB= and default =Yes=.
- Confirm the Underlyings table lists the items in scope with class =fx=.
- Confirm the Routes table names the system party, publisher =ecb= and class
  =fx=.

*** Result

** Read the public publisher list

- Choose Market data, then Public rates.
- Confirm the title reads =Public rates= and the four tabs are Publishers,
  Availability, Compare and Fetch.
- Confirm the list holds ECB, IMF, BoC and TCMB, each with its routed feed
  =*-frankfurter=, its supplier =Frankfurter= and its service =end_of_day=.
- Confirm the Last fixing column states a date, and the freshness is =Fresh=
  or =Late=, never =No data= for a publisher that has delivered.

*** Result

** Read the ECB publisher

- Click ECB in the list.
- Confirm the record shows its terms, the series picker and a chart for the
  chosen pair, and the latest fixings newest first.
- Confirm a fixing value matches the one the availability tab states for the
  same pair and date.

*** Result

** Read the availability of a pair

- Choose the Availability tab and the pair =EUR/USD=.
- Confirm every publisher that fixes the pair is listed with its feed, its
  supplier, the first and last date and the value count.
- Confirm a publisher that does not fix the pair is absent rather than shown
  with zeros.

*** Result

** Compare the publishers on a pair

- Choose the Compare tab and the pair =EUR/USD=.
- Confirm one line is drawn for each publisher that gives the pair, and each
  row states how it gives it (Published, Inverted or Crossed).
- Confirm the values agree with the availability tab for the same date.

*** Result

** Download historical fixings

- Choose the Fetch tab.
- Pick publisher =ECB=, leave the underlying at =Every item of the class=, and
  set a range of the last 30 days.
- Press =Fetch and download=.
- Confirm the browser saves a file named =ecb-fixings-<from>_<to>.csv=, and
  the screen states the file name and the number of fixings.
- Open the file and confirm the header is
  =publisher,date,pair,item,value=.
- Spot check three rows against the availability tab; the values must match.
- Press the button again for the same range and confirm the file holds the
  same rows, so a second fetch writes no duplicates.

*** Result

** Close the walkthrough

- Confirm no screen shows a console error, a raw JSON payload or a stack trace.
- Record the counts you saw (publishers, suppliers, adapters, sessions, feeds,
  public publishers and fixings downloaded) in the Results section.
