* Acceptance

- A sandbox is an entity with purpose, anchor, owner, visibility, status and review date, and its own portfolio tree that no official portfolio contains.
- Opening a sandbox needs the right at its anchor; visibility follows the anchor's permissions or named members.
- A virtual book belongs to exactly one sandbox and has no ledger book.
- Official reports cannot select a sandbox.

* Decisions

- Rights at a node of the portfolio tree did not exist; the story adds the
  smallest model that meets its acceptance: an account holds a named right
  at a node, inherited down the tree. General book access adopts it later.
- The rule that a booking names a virtual book only for a draft, a test or
  a hypothetical moves to the story that splits the trade, which creates
  the booking.
