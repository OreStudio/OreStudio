* Goal

A person reading a report sees, per book, which trades were not valued and why, and can tell the run's preprocessing errors from the engine's and the postprocessing's.

* Acceptance

- The report states, per book, the count of trades booked, valued and not valued, and names each trade not valued with its cause.
- The run's errors are read by phase, and a person can open the errors of one phase.
- A trade excluded in preprocessing is never also shown as an engine error.
