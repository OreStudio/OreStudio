* Result

The design states that the model phase is complete. Fifteen equity entity
models, twelve fact tables, the header and the basket child, and every
additively-modelled product has its own table.

It also names what blocks the rest, with the evidence that made the difference
between a claim and a finding. The three remaining splits and the routing
re-point are mapper-coupled, and this was tested rather than assumed: the
mapper writes the digital path's barrier columns at lines 578 to 580 and 668 to
669, writes basket_json at line 840 and reads it at 1229, and the routing enum
it would have to follow is consumed by the trade component queries and the
export service.
