#+title: The bond's legs are not on the instrument tab
#+description: The prototype draws a Legs card for a bond and the trade page shows none, so a coupon or amortising bond cannot be read from its page.
#+type: capture
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :web:trading:instrument:
#+created: 2026-10-25
#+updated: 2026-10-25

The prototype's bond card carries a second card beside the issue:

#+begin_example
{id: 'legs', title: 'Legs', table: 'bond_leg'}
#+end_example

The trade page's Instrument tab shows the trade type, the notional and the issue. It shows no legs, and a bond's legs are where its coupons, amortisations and rates live in the model.

Reading them is the same shape as the issue read: =bond_legs= by issue id, with =bond_leg_amounts=, =bond_leg_rates= and =bond_leg_amortizations= under them. It is left out here because the tab was already the last piece of its task and the legs are a table of their own rather than a fact.
