* Goal

A change to any refdata entity publishes its event on that entity's own
subjects, so the caches, the audit trail and any other consumer learn of it.
Every entity that generates an event-mapping registrar has it composed in
=event_registrar.cpp=, and the hand-wired mapping and subscriber blocks in
=application.cpp= are gone. One composition point carries every entity's
events, and a gate keeps it that way.

* Acceptance

- Every generated =*_event_registrar.hpp= under =ores.refdata.service/messaging= is composed in =event_registrar.cpp=.
- =application.cpp= registers no entity event mapping of its own and subscribes to no =*_changed_event= itself.
- The service starts, registers each composed mapping on its channel, and subscribes each handler.
- A gate fails when a generated registrar is left uncomposed and passes when every one is composed.
