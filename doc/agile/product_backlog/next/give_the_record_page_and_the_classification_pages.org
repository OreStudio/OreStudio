* What

Give the record page and the classification pages the shared Refresh button with its load time. ES-REFRESH-06 and ES-REFRESH-16 say every list and record page has Refresh in the header with the load time under it. The harness baseline of 2026-10-25 finds the record pages of currencies, desk groups, currency pairs and calendars and every classification list and row page draw neither: the list screens have it through RecordList, the record pages and the classification screens use PageHeader directly. 27 screens.

* Why

The harness measured every record screen before the screen pipeline changed
any, so each topic starts from a known gap and the sign off has a before and an
after. This is one of the four rules the baseline found broken.
