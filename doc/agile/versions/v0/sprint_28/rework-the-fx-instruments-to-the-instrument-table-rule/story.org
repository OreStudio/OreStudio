* Decisions

- The family was reviewed on 2026-10-16, and the review is recorded as [[id:38931409-479B-40C3-A118-16D234C687C9][Investigation: FX family data model review]]. It found that the per-type tables already meet the explicit-variation check, so a family header and per-product fact tables are not required by the checklist.
- The units below carry the deviations the review found. If the header and the fact tables are still wanted for another reason, record that reason here and add the acceptance it serves.
- The units run digits first, because that unit moves the goldens.
- The nullable-string convention is not a family finding. The trading component holds 91 columns whose domain member cannot carry a NULL; the FX family carries 19 of them. A component task owns the fix.
- *The family carries typed arrays, not a variant.* =fx_instrument_variant= is an aggregate that carries an entity, and
  [[id:B607E5B7-190F-4D38-B515-8A7B27FB6B47][The atomised entity]] forbids it: the wire carries one typed array per part, and the array an element sits in states its type, so no element and no carrier needs a discriminator. The legacy carrier is deleted, and the range bound is one more typed array beside the instrument arrays, not a field inside an aggregate.
- *The other three variants carry the same debt.* =equity_instrument_variant=, =credit_fact_variant= and =rates_fact_variant= are the same shape. They are out of scope here and want their own story.

* Out of scope

- The ten FX trade-type codes that route to no instrument table. The catalogue records them as not yet covered.
- The ORE binding generator. The digits unit patches the generated binding through the reapply script; changing the generator is a different work item.
- The FX reference-data entities themselves.
- The equity, credit and rates variants, which are the same defect in other families.
- The pipeline-wide move onto =instrument_batch=. This story removes the FX variant only; the importer, the exporter and the planner keep their per-family carriers for the other families.
