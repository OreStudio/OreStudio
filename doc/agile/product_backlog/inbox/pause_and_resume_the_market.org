This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

A system verb, =pause= and =resume=, on the market's one loop. While paused the
loop takes no tick and the market's clock stands still: tau, the heat, the
chain's dwell in progress and every series' next mark keep their values. On
resume the run time the pause took is skipped, not replayed, so no feed catches
up with a burst of the ticks it missed. The trace records both, and the Market
screen's strip says the market is paused.

* Why

Gemini's review drew a *Pause engine* button in the header, and it is a real
need: a demonstration or an investigation wants to stop the whole market at a
moment and look at it. Today the only way is to stop every feed, which loses
their place, empties the screens and restarts each law from scratch.

The scheduler already owns the market and applies commands at its now, so a
pause is one more command. The open questions are what the wall clock means to
a paused run (the tick times a consumer sees) and whether a pause is the system
tenant's alone, as the pin is.
