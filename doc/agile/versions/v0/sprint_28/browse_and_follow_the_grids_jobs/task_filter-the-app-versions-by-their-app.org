* Acceptance

- The app version model declares a list by app_id, and the generated list and its protocol carry it.
- The app version page reads the versions of its app with that list.

* Plan

The workunit and the result already declare a list by their parent with =:list_by: true= on the
foreign key. Do the same for =app_id= on the app version, regenerate the files it drives, and read
the versions of an app through the generated list, in place of the whole catalogue.
