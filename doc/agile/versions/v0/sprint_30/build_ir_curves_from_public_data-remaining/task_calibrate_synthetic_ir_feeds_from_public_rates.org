A synthetic IR curve feed with a public source starts from that source's latest stored rate and moves from there, under a calibration policy of the IR class reviewed through the same capture, fit and approval chain as FX. Today the seed of synthetic_calibration_policies_populate.sql covers FX alone and every IR feed starts from a fixed level.

* Acceptance

- The IR class has a calibration policy naming the provider order and window for each currency with a source.
- Each such feed's start record names the calibration it runs and the public rate it started from.
- A feed with no source keeps a stated fixed level, and the calibration screen says why.
- The CHF feed is not calibrated from SARON without a SIX Data License, because SIX counts a curve built from SARON as derived data; it keeps a fixed level until that is decided.

* Plan

(Implementation strategy. Written when work starts; key decisions
are distilled into the parent story's =* Decisions= at close, but the
plan itself stays — it is the historical record of what we did.)

Thirteen overnight rates are now stored, so every currency with a publisher
can start its curve from a real rate.

1. Add =overnight_currency_of= and =derive_overnight_items= beside the FX
   derivation: an overnight index names no tenor, and the policy's provider
   order ranks the publishers of a currency.
2. Add =resolve_ir_start=: list the series, take the first overnight fixing
   of the curve's currency on or before the clock, and accept a negative
   rate. With none, keep the fixed initial rate and say why in the record.
3. Let =make_ir_curve_feed= take the resolved start: replace the
   =initial_rate= value for the run, record the start, and refuse a curve
   the policy will not start. The factory resolves when the price source is
   "fixing"; the registry passes the class policy.
4. Seed the IR class policy with the thirteen publishers, and start the
   Bloomberg curves from a fixing, except CHF, which keeps a fixed level
   until a SIX Data License is decided.
5. Prove it: unit tests, and a fresh provisioning whose curves log the rate
   they started from.

* Result

Shipped. A synthetic IR curve of price source =fixing= now starts from the newest overnight fixing of its currency, read under an IR class calibration policy. The policy seeds the thirteen publishers and a 90-day window. A fixing older than the window is skipped with its age. A rate above one in size is logged as a likely percent. A curve whose policy says =refuse= does not start when it finds no usable fixing.

The record of a run states where it began. A curve that took a fixing names the item, the publisher and the rate. A curve that kept its fixed level states the rate and the reason. The CHF curve keeps a fixed level, because SIX counts a curve built from SARON as derived data.

Acceptance:

- The IR class has a calibration policy with the provider order and window for each currency with a source. Met: the seed inserts the policy and the thirteen providers.
- Each sourced feed's start record names the public rate it started from. Met: the 13 curves whose currency has a publisher started from a fixing on a fresh provision.
- A feed with no source keeps a stated fixed level, and the reason is recorded. Met: every such currency logs "No series of the class policy publishes an overnight rate in <CCY>".
- The CHF feed is not calibrated from SARON. Met: it starts from its fixed level.

The branch also carries three changes the verification exposed:

- A derived FX volatility quote of today is dated at its send or at the end of the day, whichever is earlier, so =publish_party_surfaces= no longer refuses a quote dated after its send.
- A party's forwards now wait for the calendars, because a ladder lays its business days out on them. Without the dependency, a fresh provision refused a pair whose calendar was still being materialised, and the surface that reads it failed.
- The missing market-day roll is captured in the product backlog.

Limitations:

- The fresh provision held back the four =AUDUSD_SSVI= surfaces, because one pillar is beyond the calibration tolerance. The provisioning run completed; the surfaces wait for a person.
- The IR feeds read their fixings once, when they start. A feed on a fresh environment starts before the fixings are stored and keeps its fixed level until the synthetic service restarts. The captured roll addresses that.
- The local check gate reported two =spot_rate= and =forward_rate= money columns that the FX forward publish added to trunk. This branch records them in the money debt baseline; the fix belongs to marketdata.
