#+title: Calendars hold no holiday rules, so value dates ignore holidays
#+description: No calendar_rule rows exist in any tenant and provisioning materialises no calendar dates, so a rebuilt calendar is weekends only and FX value dates land on holidays.
#+type: capture
#+level: cross
#+filetags:

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

No tenant holds any =calendar_rule= or =calendar_exception= rows. A rebuild therefore marks weekends only: =UnitedStates.Settlement= has 104 closed days in 2026, and 2026-12-25 reads as a business day in =TARGET=, =UnitedStates.Settlement= and =Japan=. Provisioning also builds no =calendar_date= rows, so a new tenant has none until someone runs *Rebuild*. Load the holiday rules for every published calendar and build the dates during provisioning.

* Why

Business-day logic depends on these rows, and it is silently wrong without them. The FX forward ladder gave EUR/USD a spot date of 2026-11-11, which is Veterans Day. A date on a holiday moves the value date, so it moves the forward. Without a rebuild the ladder refuses the pair because the calendar has no dates.
