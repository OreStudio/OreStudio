* Goal

A tenant owns a bucket per data class, the storage service derives the tenant from the bucket, a step service reaches its tenant's buckets with its run token, a run's inputs and outputs are removed at run end with a 24-hour backstop, and the compute reaper acts inside each tenant.

* Acceptance

- A tenant owns an inputs bucket and an outputs bucket, and the platform owns the package and artefact buckets.
- Storage derives the tenant from the bucket, and a step service reaches its tenant's buckets with its run token.
- A run's inputs and outputs are removed when the run ends and its data is in the database, with a 24-hour backstop, and a tenant's data leaves with its buckets.
- The compute reaper acts inside each tenant, so a stale host's work is requeued.
