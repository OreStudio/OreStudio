* Goal

A definition cannot choose what it runs: the chain is fixed in report_execution_workflow.hpp and the trigger hard-codes its type, so every definition gathers and assembles before it submits. Give the definition a carrier and register a shorter chain that starts from a bundle that already exists.

* Acceptance

- The chain a definition selects is the one that runs.
- The shorter chain's positional step results resolve, and a missing position fails loudly rather than silently yielding an empty field.
- A definition on the shorter chain completes without gathering trades or market data and without assembling a bundle.
