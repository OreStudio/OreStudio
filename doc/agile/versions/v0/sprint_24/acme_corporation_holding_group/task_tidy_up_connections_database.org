* Goal

The Qt client's saved connections database (=connections.db=, managed
via the Connections toolbar button) has accumulated stale entries from
before Acme Corporation replaced Barclays as the reference test
entity (Barclays, NatWest, etc.), and has no way to label a saved
connection with who/what it's for — every entry is just a bare
host/credential pair, so picking the right one for a given persona
(e.g. "the Head of Desk for FX Rates UK") means remembering usernames
by hand rather than reading a label.

This task:

1. Adds a =description= field to each saved connection (schema +
   UI), so a connection can be annotated with something like "Head of
   Desk, FX Rates UK" instead of just a bare username.
2. Adds around 10 curated Acme connections: every department head
   (Head of Desk / Head of Trading / Head of Middle Office / Head of
   Market Risk / Head of Risk / Country Head / COO, per office) plus a
   representative sample of individual contributors (a Trader, an
   Analyst, ...), each with a description identifying the role.
3. Removes the stale non-Acme connections (Barclays, NatWest, etc.),
   leaving only =tenant_admin=/=super_admin= plus the new curated
   Acme set.
