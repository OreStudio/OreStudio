* Result

The rate family states its rows. The planner builds the product with its two
legs, their amounts, their rates and its strike, and the writer persists what
the plan carries. The writer's rates arm is gone: the translation that named
each product's constructor moved to the planner beside the drawing.

The population is unchanged. A fresh tenant books 24 rate headers, 48 legs, 48
amounts, 48 rates and 3 strikes, which is what it booked before the flip, and
every other family's count is unchanged too: 127 trades in total.

Two facts about the family came out of the measurement and neither is a defect
the flip introduced:

- Only the cap floor states a strike. The planner draws a strike for the
  swaption as well, but the constructor it calls takes the expiry and no
  strike, so the drawn value is dropped. That is how the family booked before
  the flip and the rows are the same.
- The knock-out swap is never drawn. Each seeded rate config states eight
  slots and the planner cycles nine routed types, so the ninth is never
  reached. Captured rather than fixed here, because a flip moves rows and does
  not add products.

The facts and the strikes now carry their change reason and commentary. Before
the flip the writer stamped the headers and the legs, and left the strikes and
the fact rows with empty audit columns while writing them; the walk over the
batch stamps every row it sends.

The plan's bag narrowed as part of the same move. A family that states its rows
no longer has its terms in the slot's variant, so commodity and rates left it
and four remain: bond, whose security still travels in the bag, and the three
families that have not flipped. The two removed structs stay as what their
planners draw, and their comments say so.
