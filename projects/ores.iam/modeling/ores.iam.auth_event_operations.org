The authentication event log read.

=ores_iam_auth_events_tbl= records one row per authentication event: a
successful login, a failed attempt, a logout, a token refresh, a rejected
refresh and a signup. It is the record an administrator reads to account
for who is signed in and who is failing to get in, and no subject read it.

The table carries no row-level security: it is a system-level audit log.
The read therefore scopes on =tenant_id= itself rather than relying on a
policy, and it answers only the caller's tenant.

The event is not an entity. It has no versions, no diff and no revert,
because nothing writes one after the fact, so it has no entity model and
no generated CRUD. The message shape here is the whole of its protocol.
