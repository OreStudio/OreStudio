** Switch to a different party

- Click *Switch Party...*.
- The same party-picker dialog shown at login opens, listing all
  four Acme parties (the holding company plus UK/US/HK).
- Select a party other than the one currently active (check the
  current party name, e.g. in a window title or status area, before
  switching) and confirm.
- A status-bar message confirms the switch, naming the newly
  selected party.

*** Result

** Confirm newly opened windows reflect the new party

- Open a party-scoped window you have not already opened this
  session (e.g. *Market Data > Cross-Rates Matrix*, or *Reference
  Data > Business Units*).
- Its content matches the party just switched to, not the original
  login-time party -- confirms the new JWT/session context from
  Switch Party is actually being used for new requests, not just
  cosmetic.
