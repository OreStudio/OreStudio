* Acceptance

- InflationSwap, BalanceGuaranteedSwap and FlexiSwap round-trip through the mapper: an import writes the fact row and the export writes the element back.
- The three products have round-trip cases with literal expected values.
- The rates carrier and its codec are either generated from the models or carry a recorded reason why the carrier stays hand-written and the codec is the only hand-written surface left.
- The hand-written rates variant and the payload container are gone, or their retention is recorded.

* Plan

1. *The three products.* Read the import paths of =InflationSwap=,
   =BalanceGuaranteedSwap= and =FlexiSwap= and the exporter's dispatch, and
   write the reverse mapper each one is missing. They take the header and the
   fact row, as the six existing reverse mappers do.
2. *Cases.* One round-trip case per product with literal expected values, at
   the shape unit 3 left the family in.
3. *The carrier's disposition.* Compare the hand-written rates carrier and
   codec against the generated per-entity protocols. Replace them if the
   generated path carries the legs and the shared children; otherwise record
   why the carrier stays hand-written and make the codec the only hand-written
   surface left.
4. *Removal.* Either way, the hand-written rates variant and the payload
   container are gone, or their retention is recorded with its reason.
