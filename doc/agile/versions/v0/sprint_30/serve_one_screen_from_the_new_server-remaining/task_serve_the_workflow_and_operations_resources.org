* Plan

The area holds twenty-three routes the BFF answered and the gateway does not,
and they are not one kind:

| Kind | Routes |
|------+--------|
| Resource | /api/approvals-mode, /api/hold-reasons, the four notices, the three
  paths a person's own requests take, one request, its decision, the retry of a
  run, the services roster and the logs |
| Composed view | /api/requests, /api/queue/authorisations, /api/requests/:id/story,
  :id/changes, :id/checks, /api/timeline, /api/workflow/:instanceId and
  /api/operations/bus |

The composed views are the story's own task, as they are for the other areas:
each weaves two or more collections, and the design of what it weaves is
settled before it is served. This task takes the fourteen resources.

The part comes first, by the adapter recipe that stood up =ores.http.compute=:
the model, the four hand-authored CMake files, the composite root, the generated
umbrella header, the hand-written aggregate, the file lists, the diagram, and
the server's registration. It is named =ores.http.workflow= after the area it
serves, and it holds the operations roster and logs beside the workflow routes
because both are answered from the telemetry service and neither is big enough
to be a part of its own.

The routes then follow in families, one commit each:

- The requests a person raises and withdraws: the two reads and the write of
  their own, against the version they read.
- One request and its decision: the read of a request this person may open, and
  the decision they give it.
- The notices they are given: the page, its unread count, the marks that read
  them, and the clear.
- The run: the retry of a stopped run, which the engine decides.
- The operations roster and the logs.
- The two lookups the request screens ask for: the approvals mode and the hold
  reasons.

Each route keeps the BFF's path, its JSON member names and its status codes, and
is paired in the inventory under the =workflow= or =operations= area.
