* Plan

The term curves are not built from their overnight curve. Each is a provider
feed of its own: a Cox-Ingersoll-Ross process with a stated level, whose
quotes a projection bootstrap config reads and discounts on the currency's
overnight curve. Deriving a term index from its overnight curve is the task
Derive a term index from its overnight curve. This task follows the existing
pattern, and states the level as HONIA plus a spread.

1. Feeds (=synthetic_feeds_providers_populate.sql=): HKD HIBOR 2W, 1M and 3M
   in the Tradeweb dataset, at HONIA's 4.50% plus 10bp, 20bp and 35bp, with
   HONIA's kappa and sigma. The spreads are illustrative, not fitted to a
   source, and the seed says so.
2. Template: a curve states the fixings of its index from the deposit or FRA
   that ends at the index tenor. The shared seven-pillar template starts at
   1M, so a 2W curve would state no fixings, and GBP LIBOR 1W already does not
   (=evidence/fixing_gap_by_trade.txt=). A curve whose tenor is shorter than
   one month now starts with a spot deposit at its tenor. This covers HKD 2W,
   GBP LIBOR 1W and EURIBOR 1W.
3. Series and configs (=marketdata_market_series_ibor_curves_populate.sql=,
   =refdata_ir_curve_bootstrap_ibor_configs_populate.sql=): three projection
   configs on HKD-HONIA, A365F, with the seven pillars and their output and
   quote series. A script wrote the rows; each id is a version 5 UUID of the
   URL namespace over =ores:ir_curve:<curve>:<part>=.
4. Vendor instruments: regenerated with =ores.marketdata.vendor-catalogue=
   from a seeded database, as the seed's header says. It added 24 rows: 22
   for HKD HIBOR and the 1W deposits of GBP LIBOR and EURIBOR.

* Result

HKD has HIBOR term curves at 2W, 1M and 3M. Each is a Tradeweb feed at
HONIA's level plus a stated, illustrative spread, with a projection bootstrap
config on HKD-HONIA and its series and pillars. On a fresh acme_demo
provision with no restart, each of the three HKD HIBOR fixing addresses holds
520 fixings, and no ACCOHK trade floats on an empty HKD HIBOR address. The
empty interest rate fixing addresses went from 15 to 12; the 12 left belong
to the tasks Draw only the indices the market can serve and Derive a term
index from its overnight curve (=evidence/hkd_hibor_fixings.txt=).

A curve now starts with a spot deposit at its tenor when no deposit or FRA of
the template ends there. This gave GBP LIBOR 1W and EURIBOR 1W their fixings
too, 520 each; before, they stated none.
