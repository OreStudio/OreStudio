* Acceptance

- InflationSwap, BalanceGuaranteedSwap and FlexiSwap round-trip through the mapper: an import writes the fact row and the export writes the element back.
- The three products have round-trip cases with literal expected values.
- The rates carrier and its codec are either generated from the models or carry a recorded reason why the carrier stays hand-written and the codec is the only hand-written surface left.
- The hand-written rates variant and the payload container are gone, or their retention is recorded.

* Plan

1. *The three products.* Read the import paths of =InflationSwap=,
   =BalanceGuaranteedSwap= and =FlexiSwap= and the exporter's dispatch, and
   write the reverse mapper each one is missing. They take the header and the
   fact row, as the six existing reverse mappers do.
2. *Cases.* One round-trip case per product with literal expected values, at
   the shape unit 3 left the family in.
3. *The carrier's disposition.* Compare the hand-written rates carrier and
   codec against the generated per-entity protocols. Replace them if the
   generated path carries the legs and the shared children; otherwise record
   why the carrier stays hand-written and make the codec the only hand-written
   surface left.
4. *Removal.* Either way, the hand-written rates variant and the payload
   container are gone, or their retention is recorded with its reason.

* Result

One of the three products is closed. The other two are blocked on model
fields, and the carrier question was already answered by earlier units.

- *InflationSwap* has a reverse mapper, an exporter dispatch entry and a
  round-trip case with literal values: the two legs' types, the currency, and
  the schedule's start and end dates. The product states nothing beyond its
  legs, so the export is complete.
- *FlexiSwap* and *BalanceGuaranteedSwap* have no reverse mapper. I wrote
  both and removed them. The schema requires fields the model does not hold.
  A flexi swap needs its option long or short and its lower notional bounds;
  the code would have written the enum's default in place of the document's
  =Short=. A balance-guaranteed swap needs its reference security and at
  least one tranche; with none, the corpus walk could not re-read
  =IR_BalanceGuaranteedSwap.xml=, a document that exported as a bare trade
  before. The two products stay unexported, as they were. The task
  =Carry the FlexiSwap and BalanceGuaranteedSwap fields= adds the missing
  model fields and exports both.
- *The carrier and the codec* needed no change here. The hand-written rates
  variant and the hand-written payload codec are gone: unit 3 removed the
  variant, and the export carries typed batches generated from the trade-type
  catalogue. The remaining hand-written parts are =swap_instrument_data=, the
  struct that groups a header with its facts and children, and the ORE mapper.
  The grep in Measurements shows no reference left.
- The stale =swap_instrument_data= box in the API class diagram now shows the
  struct as it is.

The acceptance holds for InflationSwap, for the carrier and for the removal.
It did not hold for the other two products when this task closed; the follow-up task closes them.
