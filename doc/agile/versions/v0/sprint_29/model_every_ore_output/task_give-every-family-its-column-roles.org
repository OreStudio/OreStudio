* Acceptance

- Each family states =key_columns=, =qualifier_columns=, =measure_columns= and whether its measures are open.
- A column every variant of a family states is required; a column only some variants state is a qualifier another analytic added or an open measure, and the family says which.
- A configuration-driven family states its measures as open, so a curve, factor or quantile the samples do not show is still a measure.
- The catalogue's test fails when a family's required columns disagree with a sample it covers.

* Plan

A column's role is a fact about how the writer uses it, and the corpus shows it: a column every variant states is required, and a column that only some variants state is either a qualifier a caller prepended or a measure the configuration added. The writers settle which: =curves.csv= adds one column per curve from the market, and the sensitivity analytic adds =Type=, =IsPar=, =Factor_1= and the shift before it writes an exposure file. The roles go in the generated data, and a reviewer checks the inference against the writers.
