* Result

Done 2026-09-23. Fourteen journeys in six topic groups, all under
=doc/knowledge/journeys/=.

| Group | Journeys |
|-------+----------|
| Profile | Present myself; Keep my details current; Change someone's details |
| Credentials | Protect my account; Rescue access; Audit sign-ins |
| Access | Know what I may do; Change someone's access; Shape the role catalogue |
| Membership | Choose where I work; Draw the reporting line |
| Directory | See who has access; Bring someone in |
| Tenancy | Tune the tenant |

Each document carries the nine required sections, a rendered PlantUML =salt=
wireframe embedded with a caption, and a coverage table marking every operation
=exists=, =partial= or =missing=.

Verified mechanically on 2026-09-23:

- 14 documents, 14 wireframe sources, 14 rendered PNGs. Every =salt= diagram
  renders, carries no =title= directive (the combination fails), and is ASCII.
- 105 subjects claimed in coverage tables were checked against the generated
  TypeScript under =packages/wire-protocol/src/generated/=. All 105 exist.
- Every =[[id:...]]= link in the corpus resolves repo-wide, against 5,593 org
  files.
- The filetags are =user_journey= and =security= throughout.

*What the exercise found.* The journeys were expected to surface modeling errors
and missing server-side operations, and they did. The server-side defects are
captured separately, because they are defects rather than design gaps:

- [[id:2662D094-99B0-4C18-88DF-C36A304CA806][Account secrets reach the browser in the accounts wire shape]] --
  =password_hash=, =password_salt= and =totp_secret= are on the wire.
- [[id:E87A316A-A387-4D0B-8221-9716F8A6AF07][Change-password ignores the current password]] -- a stolen token is
  enough to set a new one.
- [[id:653E5D7E-13BC-4588-B5BC-40752F49D823][IAM handlers skip their permission checks]] -- role assignment is
  unguarded and the reads are too, which compounds the first two.

The design gaps, recurring across journeys: no self-service write of a member's
own profile or contact record; no image upload anywhere, so a photo cannot be
set; no operation to end another account's session; no subject for session
statistics or the auth event log; no forced password change; no joined read for
the account roster; no reporting tree; no permission-to-role write, so a role
bundle is read-only; and the three lookup validators read system-tenant rows
only, which makes a tenant administrator's lookup writes inert.

Next: take one journey at a time to prototype, starting with accounts.
