#+title: The FX spot generation config
#+description: One FX pair feed, its datum identity, its GMM process choice and its tick clock.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :synthetic:config:fx:
#+created: 2026-10-06
#+updated: 2026-10-22

* Summary

An =fx_spot_generation_config= describes how to synthesise the tick stream
for one FX spot rate. It is declared in
=ores.synthetic.api/domain/fx_spot_generation_config.hpp=, it hangs under a
=market_data_generation_config= through =config_id=, and its mixture
components are separate =gmm_component= rows.

* Detail

** What the row names

- The pair, as =base_currency_code= and =quote_currency_code=. Both are soft
  foreign keys to currencies.
- The datum, as =oresmd_uri=, the quote URI of the spot rate that the vendor
  instrument maps to, for example
  ="oresmd://fx/EUR?type=quote&instrument=fx_spot&quote=rate&ccy=USD"=. It is
  derived from the pair rather than typed. The feed reads it with the strict
  codec at construction, and refuses a URI that is not an =fx_spot= quote or
  whose pair differs from the two currencies. See
  [[id:C3E053CA-0D4B-480B-9119-E11530160EC1][oresmd: ORE Studio Market Data URI]] and
  [[id:CE99CD2A-B31F-4306-865F-CBC519FBC1B8][The oresmd datum identity]].
- The vendor instrument, as =provider_code=, =id_scheme=, =external_id= and
  =field=, for example =bloomberg=, =bbg_ticker=, =EURUSD Curncy= and =PX_LAST=.
  It is the id the simulator publishes under, and it is unique among generators.
  The generator carries no source name and no mark of the simulator.
- The cadence, as =ticks_per_hour=.

** Where the initial price comes from

=price_source= holds ="fixing"= or ="fixed"=, and a SQL check restricts the
column to those two. A new config starts on ="fixing"=.

- Under ="fixing"= the feed starts from the newest public fixing of its pair,
  read at feed start, and its mixture is sized to the spread of the fixings
  over a window. The candidates come from the class's calibration policy and
  the series that cover the pair, unless the config names its own items in
  =fx_spot_fixing_item= or overrides the window in
  =calibration_window_days=. See
  [[id:9FEC9AF7-BF8A-494B-AEF8-B0C41CFD5758][Publish public fixings through an adapter]].
- Under ="fixed"= the process starts from =gmm_initial_price= as entered.
  This is the stated fallback for a pair no public source fixes. A
  ="fixing"= config that finds no fixing also keeps =gmm_initial_price=,
  unless the policy refuses to start it.

=gmm_initial_price= is greater than zero under either value, since it is the
fallback level. No feed reads a dated reference observation as its start.

=ticks_per_hour= has its own check and must be greater than zero. It sets the
publish cadence only. The mixture moments are already per tick, so the
process step does not change with the cadence.

** The process choice

=process_type= holds ="geometric"=, ="arithmetic"= or
="ornstein_uhlenbeck"=, and a SQL check restricts the column to those three
strings. The default is ="geometric"=.

The column is a plain string and not a foreign key. An unrecognised value is
therefore not rejected at write time. It is forwarded to
=process_factory::make_process=, which falls back to the geometric engine for
any string it does not recognise. See
[[id:E1C64302-FA31-4C25-8E92-38265013F644][The generator library]].

The mixture itself is not on this row. The means, standard deviations and
weights live as =gmm_component= children. See
[[id:7493D798-DD0D-43A5-8DAB-6E63377253DC][The GMM component]].

** enabled and auto_start

The two flags are orthogonal and they answer different questions.

- =enabled= says the config is startable at all. A disabled config may not be
  started by an auto-start pass or by a manual Start action.
- =auto_start= says the config starts on its own when the service comes up,
  in every active tenant. A generator reads its fixings as the system service
  and needs no grant.
  A config can be enabled and not auto-start, which is the shape a legacy or
  alternate-index variant takes when it lives beside a currency's primary
  config.

Starting a generator is refused outright when another generator already serves
the same vendor instrument. Nothing auto-disables the running one, so a switch is
a Stop followed by a Start.

** The fact the code shape does not show

=folder_id= is optional, and only the bundle path fills it. A rate made by hand
has no position in the Market Simulator tree.

The pair is not the process identity. Two configs for the same pair differ by
their component rows, and the collision rule binds them by the vendor
instrument alone, so they cannot run at the same time whatever else differs.
