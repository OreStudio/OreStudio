* Goal

The market data pages say one thing for each act, and a value has a word for
whether a party can ever read it. A producer quotes a price or fixes a benchmark.
The ingest records the observation and delivers the tick. We publish a set, and
that act alone carries an approval, a candidate version, a supersession and a
withdrawal. A value is internal or external.
