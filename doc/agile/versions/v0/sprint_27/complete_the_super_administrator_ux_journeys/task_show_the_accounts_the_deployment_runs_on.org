* Goal

Signed in as the super administrator, the system administration area reads the session's own tenant accounts. The services the platform runs on, its service and infrastructure accounts, are visible where the deployment is administered, and not only through the system tenant's screen on the roster.

* Acceptance

- The system administration area offers the deployment's own accounts a page at a time, each with its type and its picture or initials, and it is reachable without opening a tenant from the roster.
- The service accounts are shown as services, with the mark the tenant screen already gives them.
- The read is the ordinary accounts read of the session's own tenant; no cross-tenant read is added.
- The navigation prototype records the new entry and the Tenants-only decision it revisits.

* Notes

The pieces the entry reaches for:

- The shell menus are data, in
  =projects/ores.web/packages/web/src/shell/areas.ts= (=SHELL_MENUS=),
  where system administration holds Home and Tenants today.
- The read already exists: =GET /api/accounts= in
  =projects/ores.web/packages/bff/src/server.ts= answers the accounts of
  the session's own tenant, a page at a time, and the session's tenant is
  the system tenant for a super administrator. Service accounts are
  among the rows, and nothing filters on =account_type=.
- The lists to reuse or mirror: =TenantPeople= in
  =projects/ores.web/packages/web/src/pages/TenantPage.tsx= marks a
  non-user account as a service, and the tenant administrator's screen is
  =projects/ores.web/packages/web/src/access/PeoplePage.tsx=.
- The system tenant is already on the roster and its screen opens
  (=d4525844bc=, "Show the system tenant"), so this task adds the super
  administrator's own entry, not the underlying reads.
- =TenantsPage.tsx= carries a comment (lines 49-51) that says the system
  tenant is not on the roster; =d4525844bc= made it reachable, so the
  comment is stale and goes with this work.
- The prototype page that fixed the Tenants-only menu is
  [[id:E6E32AB5-2F25-4230-AB93-E38E75DE23B6][Application shell prototype]]; the
  new entry and the decision it revisits are recorded there.
