* Goal

Screens and reports can read a published surface. The reads return the published grid for a blueprint and an as-of, and a slice of it at one expiry or one strike. They read the published set and never the quotes or a candidate. See [[id:53D14F31-EE32-493E-BBEB-140AA4906BE4][Constructing a volatility surface: from quotes to a published set]] and Inspect a surface's slices.

* Acceptance

- A read returns the grid of the published set for a blueprint and an as-of, with the two axes in order.
- A slice read returns one expiry across strikes, or one strike across expiries.
- A read for an as-of with no published set says so, and returns no candidate.
- A read names the publish it answers from.
- A read returns only the session party's surfaces.

* Plan

One operation, =get_published_fx_vol_surface=, reads a blueprint's published surface at an as-of: the newest published set at or before it, superseded ones included, named by its publish request. An optional expiry or strike cuts the slice. A pure =surface_grid_of= orders the expiries by length and the strikes in the blueprint's order, and refuses a slice the surface does not hold. The grid is the output series' observations at the publish's as-of, so a calibration at the as-of of a published surface is now refused: a published surface never changes. A new repository query, =read_published_for_blueprint=, reads the publishes of one blueprint.

* Result

Done on 2026-11-11. On the Acme tenant with ACCOUS's EURUSD_SSVI published as of 01:58 and superseded as of 01:59, a trader of ACCOUS read the surface at 02:30 and got the 01:59 set, named by its request: seven expiries, 1W to 1Y, by five strikes in the blueprint's order. A read at 01:58:30 answered from the superseded 01:58 set and said so. The 3M expiry read across the strikes and the ATM strike across the expiries. A 5Y slice was refused by name, a read at 01:00 said no surface was published, and a calibration at the published 01:59 was refused. A trader of ACCOUK, reading the same blueprint, was told it is not visible to their party. The scripts are =evidence/fx_vol_surface_read.ores= and =evidence/fx_vol_surface_read_other_party.ores=.
