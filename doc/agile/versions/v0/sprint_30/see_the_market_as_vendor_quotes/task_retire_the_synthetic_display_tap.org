* Plan

The product owner decided (2026-11-19) that a feed's Ticks tab and the arrivals
raster show what the vendor publishes, not the simulator's clean price: after
injected faults, conversion by the adapter, and the ingest's conflation and
rejections. The tap carries the clean price under the feed's own source name,
=synthetic.bloomberg.usdjpy=; the vendor subjects carry the adapter's ticks
under the adapter's source, =bloomberg-a=, so a server cannot find a feed's
ticks by its name. The feed itself knows where they land: its adapter's
provider and the series it quotes.

1. =adapter_target= carries the provider whose notation wrote its id, and
   =i_feed= gains =vendor_watch_subjects()=: for each series the feed quotes,
   =feed_tick_watch_subject(provider, "*", series)=, the series on any feed of
   the provider. The FX spot and IR curve feeds implement it; a feed with no
   adapter watches nothing.
2. The synthetic service's running feeds reply states each feed's watch
   subjects (=running_feed.watch_subjects=, in the operation model, rendered to
   C++ and TypeScript).
3. The FX spot and IR curve feeds stop publishing
   =synthetic.v1.ops.feed_tick=, and =feed_display_subject= is deleted.
4. =ores.http='s =feed_tick_buffers= and the BFF's =FeedTickBuffers= watch a
   source on the subjects the running feeds reply names, so the Ticks tab and
   the raster read the vendor subjects.
5. The feed screen standard states the Ticks tab's new source.

A feed started by hand already publishes through its collection's adapter
(=feed_kind_registry::make_feed= looks the adapter up), so a manual run's ticks
reach the vendor subjects like a demanded run's; the runner comment that said
otherwise is corrected.

* Result

- The FX spot and IR curve feeds no longer publish =synthetic.v1.ops.feed_tick=,
  and =feed_display_subject= is gone. Each feed names the vendor subjects its
  ticks land on (=i_feed::vendor_watch_subjects=, from its adapter target's new
  =provider_code= and its series, through =provider_series_watch_subjects=), and
  the synthetic service's running feeds reply carries them
  (=running_feed.watch_subjects=, in the operation model, rendered to C++ and
  TypeScript).
- =ores.http='s =feed_tick_buffers= and the BFF's =FeedTickBuffers= listen to a
  source on those subjects, keep a stopped feed's ticks, and follow a feed whose
  subjects change. The Ticks tab and the arrivals raster read through them.
- Checked on the running fleet as UK plc after a fresh provisioning: the raster
  showed ticks for all 51 running FX feeds within 20 seconds, and for 39 of the
  63 IR curve feeds within about three minutes, rising as their one-a-minute
  rate predicts.
- Tests: =ores.marketdata.api.tests= 1170 cases (a new watch test),
  =ores.synthetic.api.tests= 1021, =ores.http.synthetic.tests= 146 (a new test of
  a source that stops and one whose subjects change), the synthetic service's
  simulator adapter cases bar the known announce race (captured twice in the
  backlog), and every web workspace (the BFF's buffer and route tests moved to
  the vendor subjects).
- The feed screen standard states the Ticks tab's source, and the capture about
  a curve feed's missing display tick is discarded as moot.
