** Outcome

The rail groups each supplier with its sessions and their feeds, and a second
tree groups each publisher with its feeds. A search box narrows both trees.

A supplier's pane is a dashboard: counts of sessions, feeds and publishers,
the sessions table with environment and state, coverage by publisher and
class, the contract, and what needs attention. Attention names a disabled
session, a feed with no scope and a feed no party routes.
