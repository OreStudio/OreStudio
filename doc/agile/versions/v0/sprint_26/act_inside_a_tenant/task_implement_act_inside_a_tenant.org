* Result


The journey [[id:0CD07C06-080B-4BBE-83DE-F6C3841BD209][Act inside a tenant]] is
written, with its wireframe, and the tenancy hub lists it. Its decisions:

- Entering is the domain operation =iam.v1.ops.enter_tenant=, and leaving
  =iam.v1.ops.leave_tenant=. Both check =iam::tenants:impersonate=, refuse the
  system tenant, and record the event in the audit trail of the tenant and of
  the system tenant.
- The tenant session acts as the tenant's system party, which sees every party,
  in the tenant administration mode. Its token carries a claim naming the
  tenant the administrator acts from.
- The session reads only: it carries the read permissions of the
  administrator's roles. Writing inside a tenant is left as a decision.
- The session is not refreshed. On expiry, and on leaving, the browser returns
  to the administrator's own system session without a new sign-in.
- The BFF routes are =POST= and =DELETE= on =/api/session/tenant=.
