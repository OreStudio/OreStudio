* Acceptance

- convention_lookup answers what each commodity builder asks.
- The commodity planner draws values and calls the builders; its construct_* functions and *_terms structs are deleted.
- The commodity entries leave the recorded trades' known lists, and the recorded files change in the same commit.
- The ORE engine test reads and builds every booked commodity type.
- The roundtrip tests read every ORE sample trade of each booked commodity type and write back every element ORE needs.

* Plan

The commodity family differs from equity in one way that sets the order of
work. The projection writes only eight of the twenty-five booked types:
forward, option, swap, swaption, variance swap, average price option, option
strip and basket option. The other types never get their product element, so
ORE stops at "data node not found". Each step below ends in a test run.

1. Builders in =commodity_builders.hpp=, following the equity builders. A
   builder fills =commodity_instrument_data= with the product's row and the
   shared option, schedule, barrier, underlying and strike rows. A commodity
   is an oresmd commodity address; no builder or planner spells an ORE name.
2. The eight projected types first. The planner calls their builders, and
   the trade errors ORE reports go: the forward's settlement currency, the
   option and swaption's settlement type, the strip's schedule end date, the
   swap's legs and the basket option's payoff type.
3. The scripted products ORE builds from the equity and FX classes with a
   commodity underlying: accumulator, TaRF, worst-of basket swap, rainbow,
   best entry, generic and window barrier, strike resettable, basket variance
   swap, Asian, digital and digital average price options, the forward
   volatility agreement, the position and the spread option. Each gets a
   writer and a reader in the projection, built on the shared blocks the
   equity projection uses. The carrier gains range bounds and basket periods
   where a product needs them.
4. =CommodityPairwiseVarianceSwap= leaves the generator: ORE has no builder
   for it. The reason is recorded beside the type list, as for
   =EquityOutperformanceOption=.
5. The =construct_commodity_*= functions, their terms and the plan's
   =commodity_terms= are deleted. The trades are recorded again, and the
   known lists lose the commodity lines the builders settle. Errors left are
   market data, which belongs to the market data task.
