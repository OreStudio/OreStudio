* Goal

An arrival law is a value: given its state and its stream, it returns the next gap in operational time, and its state changes only when an event happens. The periodic phase is an explicit start offset, and a Hawkes law starts at its stationary intensity.

* Acceptance

- Periodic, Poisson and Hawkes laws each have exact tests on a fixed stream and statistical tests on fixed seeds: gap mean and variance, phase uniformity, and the time-rescaling check.
- Drawing a gap without an event leaves a Hawkes law's state unchanged.
- The Hawkes law runs in operational time: its branching ratio is invariant under the market factor, and the Arrivals panel help says a burst fades faster in a busy market.
- set_baseline_multiplier is gone: the market reaches a law only through the clock.
