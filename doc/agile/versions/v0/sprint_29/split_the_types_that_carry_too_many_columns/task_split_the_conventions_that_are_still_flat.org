* Goal

The three remaining convention kinds are on the family's shape: a central convention row carrying the kind, and one part per block or leg. Commodity future's five blocks and the two cross-currency pairs each become parts, as the tenor basis swap kind already does.

* Acceptance

- No convention kind still carries a column that belongs to another kind's part.
- Commodity future's expiry anchor, option expiry, power and averaging blocks are parts, not columns of one row.
- The cross currency basis and fix float conventions model their two legs as parts, as the tenor basis swap kind does.
- The wire, the ORE projection and the fixtures state one typed array per part.
