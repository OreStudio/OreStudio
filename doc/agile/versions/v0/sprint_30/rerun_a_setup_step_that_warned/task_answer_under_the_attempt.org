* Acceptance

- A step's answer is published under the attempt its command was sent as, and under the step id when the command names none.
- Two re-runs of one step in a row on a provisioned tenant both complete.

* Plan

- *The cause.* The re-run sends its command under a new idempotency key, but the service
  answered under =publish_identity_headers(step_id)=, so both attempts' answers shared one
  =Nats-Msg-Id=. An answer inside the stream's duplicate window of the first was stored once,
  as the first: the step stayed in progress, the shell waited, and the deadline settled the
  step 20 minutes later. The first round of re-runs escaped it only because each step's last
  answer was more than two minutes old.
- *The fix.* The step context reads the attempt from the command's =Nats-Msg-Id= and answers
  under it; a command that names none is answered under the step id, as before.
- *Not changed.* The compute bridge answers report-run steps under the step id; those are never
  sent again as a new attempt.
