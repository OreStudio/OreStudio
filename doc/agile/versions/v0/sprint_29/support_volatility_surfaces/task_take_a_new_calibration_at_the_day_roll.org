* Goal

A daily recalibration reaches the running feeds without a restart. At each market day roll the GJR-GARCH process already updates its variance; there, a running feed checks whether its config names a newer approved calibration or a new spread, and if so takes the new shape and deviations for the day that starts. The price path continues unbroken, and the feed records which calibration each market day ran under.

* Acceptance

- After an approval, a running feed of the pair runs the new calibration from its next market day roll, with no restart and no gap in its ticks.
- The feed's Calibration tab names the calibration the run uses now and the market day it took it.
- A calibration that changes nothing leaves the running feed as it is.
