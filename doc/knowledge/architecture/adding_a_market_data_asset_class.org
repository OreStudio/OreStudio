#+title: Adding an asset class
#+description: The two places a new asset class plugs into the simulator and the catalogue entries it needs outside the generator seam.

* Summary

A new asset class plugs into two places in the simulator: its configuration rows
become a typed build input, and its generator implements =i_generator= behind a new
kind string. It also needs catalogue rows for its vendor instruments and
mappings. Everything else stays as it is: the runner, the adapter contract, the
ingest loop and the party tick.

* Detail

** The edits

1. A codegen entity model for the config, regenerated.
2. A struct in =generator_build_input= and a builder registered in
   =make_default_generator_factory()=.
3. One =register_kind= call in =make_default_generator_kind_registry()=. It carries
   the kind's config read permission, loader, build-input resolver, vintage anchor
   and simulate decode and reply pair.
4. A permission row and a role grant in the IAM populate SQL.
5. Catalogue rows: vendor instruments and mappings for each provider that quotes
   the class, in the system bundle. See [[id:922C155B-6733-4D2B-A675-A7361A5BDC63][A system bundle installs the catalogue and the simulator installs its own universe]].

Steps 1 to 4 are the simulator's. Step 5 is the service's, and a live adapter
needs the same rows.

** What needs no edit

The runner, the subject builders, the adapter contract, the party tick and the
ingest loop. The only exception is the CRM hook in [[id:00D63182-C7FF-4AC9-8A83-9A5D984B2876][The ingest loop and the one pipeline]].
