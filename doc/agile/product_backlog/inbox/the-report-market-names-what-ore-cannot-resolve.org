* What

A smokehouse run now prices two of its three trades. The forward prices at
80,815.55 GBP, and the option prices at zero, which is correct: its premium is
its value on the valuation date, and ORE's NPV nets the two.

The swap records no pricing at all:

#+begin_example
2nd leg: OvernightIndexedCouponPricer: missing GBP-SONIAON Actual/365 (Fixed)
fixing for fixing date November 24th, 2026.
#+end_example

* Why

The swap's floating leg reads an overnight index fixing for the day before the
valuation date, and the run's =fixings.txt= carries none. The tenant has never
been subscribed to the provider that publishes them: its profile names
=bloomberg=, =ice=, =lseg= and the derived volatility source, and the fixings
come from =simulated-fixings=.

Two things are needed, and the second is why this was recorded rather than just
fixed:

1. The profile gives the party the =simulated-fixings= provider.
2. The subscription step writes a route per provider with the asset class =fx=,
   stated once in code. A fixing is an =ir= datum as well, and the system's own
   routes for this provider name both =fx= and =ir=. So a party subscribed to the
   fixings provider through the profile reaches the FX fixings and not the
   interest rate ones, which are the ones an overnight swap coupon reads.

The route's asset class therefore has to stop being a constant. A provider
publishes what it publishes, and the party's routes should name the classes it
actually serves, rather than every provider being routed as FX.
