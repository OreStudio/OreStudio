* Goal

Every random draw a run makes comes from a stream keyed by the run seed, a stable series key derived from the source name, and a purpose, with the draw index as the counter. The same seed gives the same draws for a series whatever else runs.

* Acceptance

- The generator reproduces the published Random123 Philox4x32-10 test vectors.
- Two streams with different keys are independent, and one key gives the same sequence whatever order the streams are made in.
- A stable series key is derived from the source name, the same on every platform.
- The stream drives the standard distributions, so a law can draw a gap from it.
- Philox is written in-house, with no new vcpkg dependency.

* Plan

The task adds the stream primitive and does not convert its callers. About 400
construction sites take a defaulted seed. Most of them build arrival processes
or a market_regime, which the law and market-clock tasks replace, so changing
them now is work those tasks throw away. The arrival laws and the market clock
take a stream when they are written. The price processes get their own task,
[[id:076811A4-F95D-4ABF-B202-55AE0CFAAFFB][Move the price processes onto keyed streams]].

- =math/rng_stream.hpp= is header-only and constexpr: the Philox4x32-10 block
  function, the FNV-1a 64 series key, the purpose enum, =stream_key= and
  =rng_stream=.
- The run seed is the Philox key. The series and the purpose mix through
  SplitMix64 into the high half of the counter, and the draw index fills the
  low half. So each stream is a disjoint range of one generator.
- =rng_stream= returns 64 bits a call, two per block, and counts its draws so a
  trace can say where a stream is.
- The known answers are checked at compile time, and a scratch program checked
  the block function against libstdc++ 16's =std::philox4x32= as well.
