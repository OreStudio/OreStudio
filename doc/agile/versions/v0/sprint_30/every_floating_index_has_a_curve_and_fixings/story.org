* Goal

A report over the generated trades finds the fixings and the curve of every
index a trade floats on. Each index a population can draw is one the market
can serve, and a fresh environment holds the same fixings as a restarted one.

** What is missing

The acme_demo provision on main at c0f6ed8c38 booked 899 trades. They name 21
interest rate fixing addresses, and 15 have no fixings
(=evidence/fixing_gap_by_trade.txt=):

- *HKD HIBOR at 2W, 1M and 3M.* HKD is the home currency of ACCOHK. These
  three addresses are the floating index of 127 of its 252 trades: swaps,
  swaptions, caps, FRAs, callable, flexi, knock-out and balance-guaranteed
  swaps, and one Ascot. HKD has its overnight HONIA curve and no HIBOR term
  curve.
- *11 cross-currency foreign legs*, one trade each, all ACCOHK. The planner
  draws a cross-currency swap's foreign leg evenly over every foreign swap
  convention of the tenant (=trade_population_planner.cpp=, the
  =CrossCurrencySwap= branch), so it reaches indices no curve covers. Seven
  are currencies with no curve at all: AOA, BWP, IDR, MYR, OMR, SAR and THB.
  Four are currencies with an overnight curve but not this index: COP IBR 3M,
  JPY EYTIBOR 3M, KRW GENERIC 3M and PLN WIBOR 6M.
- *USD SOFR 1M*, one ACCOUS Ascot whose bond leg floats on term SOFR.

A fixing is derived from the curve of its index and tenor, so the curve is
the gap in each case, and a trade without the curve cannot be valued either.

The count understates the problem. The same provision refused the ACCOUK
and ACCOUS rates configs, for a PKR KIBOR cross-currency leg and an FRA on an
index that is not an Ibor. Once those configs book, the UK and US parties draw
the same uncovered foreign legs.

** A fresh environment loses more

The first reading after the provision found 18, not 15. At 03:17 the fixing
writer asked for the series of 7 term indices before the market data service
answered, logged =no service is handling subject ...market_series.list=, and
did not ask again: GBP LIBOR 3M, 6M and 12M, USD LIBOR 1M and 6M, CHF LIBOR
3M and JPY LIBOR 3M. Their curves were published. A restart of the synthetic
service wrote all 7. The 15 the task Generate the historical fixings the
generated trades need recorded were also read after a restart.

This is one case of a general gap. A service's startup work that calls
another service runs once, and the service registry declares only
=ores.iam.service= as the dependency of almost every service: the synthetic
service calls market data and does not declare it. The story
[[id:E5314CD1-6AB9-4F65-8858-AF2B08FBAEAC][Make a fresh environment ready quickly]] meets the same order
problem from the market data side.

* Acceptance

- On a fresh environment, without a restart, the derived fixing series are
  the same as after a restart.
- The query in =evidence/fixing_gap_by_trade.sql= reports no empty interest
  rate fixing address on a fresh acme_demo provision. The currencies with no
  curve meet this by leaving the draw, under the task Draw only the indices
  the market can serve.
- ACCOHK's HKD HIBOR trades have a curve and fixings at each tenor they use.
- No population config can draw an index the market cannot serve, and a check
  says so before a provision does.
- The ACCOUK and ACCOUS rates configs book, or the reason they cannot is a
  named gap with its own task. The task Draw only the indices the market can
  serve owns this.

* Decisions

- The curve is the gap, not the fixing: a fixing comes from the curve of its
  index and tenor, so each task adds or derives a curve, or stops the draw.
- The writer is fixed first, because every later measurement depends on it.
- Trades are planned on the market data a party is configured to receive, not
  on what has arrived: a vol quote or a bootstrapped curve can take a while to
  appear. The expected set is the outputs of each generator that publishes an
  item the party demands, plus what the party derives.
- A fixing source is an index's administrator, not a quote generator. An
  observed source is fetched; a simulated one is a publisher the simulator
  serves, fixed from the run's market at the source's time and published through
  the adapter. Each supported option cut has one fixing source, of the WM/R
  family. See [[id:5AF9CEEF-A878-443A-8C32-C6E9F6C26FC9][Fixing sources for option cuts and non-deliverable currencies]].
- The capture [[id:D88ED203-3FCC-434E-86AA-A564EADC4D51][The generated population floats on indices no generated curve covers]]
  recorded the 15 on 2026-11-20; this story took it over.

* Out of scope

- A curve for AOA, BWP, IDR, MYR, OMR, SAR or THB. These leave the draw until
  someone needs them.
- The generic wait for a service's dependencies across every service. Task 1
  makes the fixing writer the first case; the general mechanism is the story
  [[id:1DB97F87-9AE4-4848-94CC-3CC9D0B46430][A service waits for its dependencies and recovers when they return]].
- Equity, commodity, FX and inflation fixings.
