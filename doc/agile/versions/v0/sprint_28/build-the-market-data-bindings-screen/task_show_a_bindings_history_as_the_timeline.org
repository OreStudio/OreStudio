* Goal

The History tab is the shared timeline of the entity screen specification, for the binding's own versions, with the chart, the one-line entries, the collapsed details, the current-version emblem and Hide system activity. Revert is offered on an older version to a person who may write, refuses a version whose source another binding holds or a running feed writes, and writes a new version. A vendor binding offers no Revert.

* Acceptance

- The History tab is the shared timeline and matches the prototype.
- Revert writes a new version against the current one, and refuses a version that would break the one-feed-per-series rule.
- A vendor binding offers no Revert.

* Plan

The page gains the two tabs the record screen standard gives a versioned
record, through =useRecordTabs=, the hook the feed screens already use.

=BindingHistory= reads the binding's own versions through the generic history
request, =api.history=, and draws them with the shared =Timeline=: the chart, the
one-line entries, the details on demand, the current-version emblem and the
system-activity toggle. Nothing about the rail is drawn here.

Revert is offered by =revertOffer= in =bindingView.ts=, a pure function rather
than a condition inside the component, so the three gates the prototype states
are covered by a test: the person may write, the binding is not a vendor's, and
the version is older than the newest. The fourth gate is the series: a version
whose series a running feed holds offers no Revert, because writing it back
would take a series another feed is publishing. That is the same read the add
dialog refuses on, =bindings.sources()=.

* Result

The History tab is the shared =Timeline= over the binding's own versions, beside
a Details tab, with the tab bar between them. A person who may write a synthetic
binding is offered Revert on every version but the newest, and on none of them
when a running feed holds the series the binding writes. A vendor binding offers
no Revert at all.

The rule lives in =revertOffer=, so its five cases are covered without rendering
the page: an older version, a vendor binding, the newest version, a reader who
may not write, and a series a running feed holds.
