* Goal

The trading service books a product in any form it offers, and a free-form scripted trade, through book, validate_script and book_scripted; every scripted booking passes the server's validation, and a trade records its product and form.

* Acceptance

- book validates the terms against the schema and builds the native element, or the scripted form through the twin recipe or, for a script-only product, the script's inputs
- validate_script parses with ORE's grammar and checks the variables against the inputs by name and type, booking nothing
- book_scripted books a library script by version or an inline script, for a person permitted to book one
- a trade records its product and the form it was booked in, and reopens in that form
- the twin recipe evaluator lives in ores.trading.api
- validate_script returns the diagnostics as structured data, located by line and column, or by variable for a finding about the trade's variables
- a booking rejected by the server's validation carries the same diagnostics, and a validation that reports only warnings books
