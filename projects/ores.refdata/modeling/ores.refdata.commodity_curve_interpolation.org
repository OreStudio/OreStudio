How a commodity curve built from its own quotes or price segments
interpolates, as ORE's =DayCounter= and =InterpolationMethod=. The direct and
piecewise constructions read them; a cross currency curve takes its shape from
its base curve, and a basis curve states its own inside its basis
configuration.

A commodity curve holds at most one row of this part, so the generated table
adds the unique index on the live rows of =(tenant_id, curve_definition_id)=.
