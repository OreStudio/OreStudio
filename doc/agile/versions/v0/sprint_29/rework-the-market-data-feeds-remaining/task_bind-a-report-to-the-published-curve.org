* Result

The binding is designed, and the cut it depends on is defined.

- The decision: doc/knowledge/architecture/a_report_binds_to_a_published_curve.org.
  The binding names the publish record and the as-of under its market role; the
  export refuses a set that is not published; the ORE XML is built from the set;
  the market configuration name is derived.
- The journey: doc/knowledge/journeys/reporting/journey_bind_a_report_to_a_published_curve.org,
  with the Market bindings tab, the five row verdicts, the picker, the refusals,
  the pinned as-of and the run that names the set.
- The prototype: doc/prototypes/reporting/market-bindings/, four surfaces and
  seven states, reviewed and signed off.
- The dependency: the cut. The side quest ported the concept and the
  reproducibility purpose into doc/knowledge/domain/cuts_hub.org and its
  children (kinds_of_cut, market_data_cut, trade_cut, option_expiry_cut_code),
  with the naming decision in naming_the_cut.org. A report cannot name a cut
  until the cut model exists.
- The two-axis review was run and its findings closed: the body stated once
  (five parts, the taking identity and the authorisation record, a pre-official
  kind records authorisation as pending), vendors confined to the naming note,
  the journey's verdicts and subject corrected, the scenario set to PENDING with
  a real commit, the unrelated edits reverted, the repetition reduced, and the
  summaries corrected.
- Checks: compass lint valid, org links 0 unresolved, structure notes green
  across 25 notes, prototype inventory 98 prototypes, site pages published with
  no failures.
- The test scenario remains PENDING: the screen and the read pass, the manual
  hand-start click-through is outstanding.

The implementation of the binding waits on the cut model. The report binding's
next work is the cut entity (the named cut time as reference data, the
population, the engine version).
