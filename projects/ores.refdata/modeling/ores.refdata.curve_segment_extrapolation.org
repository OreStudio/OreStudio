Whether a fitted bond or a bond yield shifted segment extrapolates flat past
its last bond, as ORE's =ExtrapolateFlat=. Only those two elements state it,
and it is optional, so a segment has this part only when its document states
the flag.

A segment holds at most one row of this part, so the generated table adds the
unique index on the live rows of =(tenant_id, curve_segment_id)=.
