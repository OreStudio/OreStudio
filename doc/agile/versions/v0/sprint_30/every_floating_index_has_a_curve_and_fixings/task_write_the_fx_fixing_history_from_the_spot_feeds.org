* Goal

Today no generator writes an FX fixing. The planner names oresmd://fx/BASE?type=fixing&index=fx&source=TR20H&ccy=QUOTE on 124 acme_demo trades, and no series holds that address. An IR curve feed already writes its index's history through fixing_history_writer, under the system party, on the days no publisher fixed. Give the FX spot feed the same behaviour: when it starts, it plans a daily history for its pair from its process, and the writer keeps it in the pair's fixing series. A value a publisher fixed stays.
