#+title: The fixing process: observation, authorisation, application and reversal
#+description: An observation is collected, authorised by operations, applied to every subscribed trade by an event, and can be undone.

* Summary

The fixing process takes an observation from its source to the trades that
need it, in four stages. The system collects the observation, or a person keys
it. Operations authorise it after the publication time, against the prevailing
front-office rate. Authorisation raises an event, and every trade that depends
on the observation is fixed: coupons, settlements and state changes are
generated and labelled with that event. Because the source is agreed, an error
lies in the observation, so the whole set of fixes it produced can be undone
and redone.

* Detail

** Subscribe and observe

Booking a trade that needs fixings subscribes it to its sources. The process
then concerns only the sources some live trade needs. Observations of sources
that are set up are collected automatically; the others are keyed. A trader may
also key an observation agreed with a client as part of a sale.

** Authorise

Operations authorise every observation, and only after the source's
publication time. The system compares each observation with the current
front-office rate and highlights large differences. An override keeps both the
published and the overridden values, and may apply to only some of the trades
subscribed to the observation. Front office may key a value but may not
authorise one, and may not change a value once it is authorised; this is a
four-eyes control. A trade that must carry a fixing source and has none is
reported, not silently skipped.

** Apply

Authorisation sends an event. Services find the affected trades, generate the
action each kind of trade needs and label every action with the event, so that
the event can later be reversed. The progress of a large application is
visible, some books or trades can be excluded, and ideally all affected trades
go live together after the P&L impact has been seen.

Generated trades cover the period from the last generation to now. Real
events run in settlement mode; simulated events, such as a time ladder that
rolls a portfolio forward, run in valuation mode and generate nothing durable.
A trade remembers the start of its settled generation, which is its first
unkeyed fixing.

Official observations of a source form an unbroken prefix in time: there
cannot be an official fixing, then an unofficial one, then an official one. A
future fixing keyed in advance therefore stays dormant until every fixing
between now and its date is official.

Observations are stored once and never stamped on the trades; see
[[id:C6A619F3-B2B6-42D5-9C41-5269A84BC96C][Storage of fixing observations apart from trades]].

** Undo

The process can be undone as a whole, or for one time, book, currency or
trade. Undo sends an undo event and runs the same chain in reverse. A trade may
be amended freely before its fixing date; after it, it is amended as a fixed
trade or unfixed first.

** Checks at booking

A trade whose fixings generate trades, such as a swap's coupons, is checked at
booking: every fixing already applied must have its settlement trade. A fixing
activity is either a fix or an unfix, and a fixed non-deliverable forward must
have its fixing trade in the right state.

** A bounded context of its own

Fixing management is a bounded context in the sense of domain-driven design:
it owns sources, observations and their authorisation, and the products link to
it rather than embedding it. An event-sourced design, in which every fix and
unfix is an event, gives the undo and the audit for free.
