* Goal

Finish the skills catalogue that [[id:2FCD10F0-E7B5-4CBA-A7C7-E28845DB8AAC][Lay the foundation of the LLM skills catalogue]]
started in sprint 25. The foundation shipped: the compass- namespace,
the levels, the principles and methods, compass-helm and the selection
instrument. What remains makes the catalogue work as one system: the
target-and-action dispatcher, the verification ladder, an eval harness
with mechanical verdicts, the writing standards, the reconciliation of
duplicate skills, and an end-to-end check of the deployed bundle.

The story also carries the codegen findings that the catalogue work
surfaced (SQL type spellings, decimal and date types, the nullable
tenant, the scheduler audit findings, generated-output markers and the
component audit in CI). These moved with the rest of the open tasks at
sprint 25 close. At sprint 26 planning, decide whether each one stays
here or joins the codegen story for its module.

The story's design and knowledge documents are listed in the Documents
table of the parent story.

* Acceptance

- Terminology is consistent across all skills: one word per concept,
  recorded in the naming conventions document.
- No imported skill duplicates an existing one. Overlaps fold into one
  skill, and the loser is retired through compass-skill-delete.
- The dispatcher resolves a target's type and offers only the verbs
  registered against it. Generated artefacts never offer a hand edit.
- The verification ladder and the eval harness exist and run on real
  tasks.
- The writing standards state which authority governs which genre.
- The deployed bundle builds, every skill loads, and no stale directory
  is left behind.
- Each codegen finding is either fixed here or moved to the codegen
  story for its module.
