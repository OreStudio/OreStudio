#+title: ores.marketdata.operations
#+description: Codegen operation model for the marketdata component's domain operations: the subjects that are not entity verbs and so do not belong in the common resource grammar.
#+type: ores.codegen.operation
#+component: marketdata

#+brief: The marketdata component's domain operations.
#+filetags: :model:operation:marketdata:
#+created: 2026-09-30
#+updated: 2026-09-30

These are the component's messages that are not entity verbs. Each one acts
on an aggregate, a live computation or a whole-tenant export rather than on
one row of one resource, so none of them belongs in the common resource
grammar that =ores.marketdata.market_series= and its siblings drive. They
were hand-written headers until this model; the header and its TypeScript
twin now generate from here, so the two cannot drift.

Three groups share the file because they share the namespace and the wire
conventions. The CRM group reads live rates from a rate engine. The curve
group bootstraps, republishes and reads a curve's snapshot, its slice and its
evolution. The import and export group moves whole ORE files in and out of the
tenant.

** resolve_series_identity_request

:response: resolve_series_identity_response

Requests the series a typed identity names, so a caller never builds an
=oresmd_uri= by hand and never matches on one. The fields the URI grammar puts
in a fixed position are stated on their own -- the asset class, the scope, the
instrument type and the quote type -- and every remaining identity field
travels in =fields=.

#+begin_src cpp :name comment
/**
 * @brief Requests the series a typed identity names.
 *
 * The caller states the identity as typed fields and the server resolves it to
 * the series rows that carry it, so no caller builds an oresmd_uri and no
 * caller matches on one. The asset class, the scope, the instrument type and
 * the quote type are stated on their own because the URI grammar puts them in a
 * fixed position; every other identity field travels in `fields`.
 *
 * `party_id` states whose series is wanted. An empty one asks across the
 * parties the caller can see, which is why the answer is a list.
 */
#+end_src
