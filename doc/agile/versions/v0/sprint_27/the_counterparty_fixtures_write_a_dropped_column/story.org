* Decisions

- *Only the counterparty's column went; the party's stays.* Both entities have
  a =business_center_code=, and only the counterparty's was replaced. Every
  other occurrence -- the party generator test, =auth_handler=,
  =tenant_provisioning_handler=, the BFF and the wire protocol -- was read
  before being left alone.
- *Fix the fixtures, not the schema.* The column is gone by design and a pgTAP
  test asserts it is gone. A fixture that still writes it is stale, not a
  reason to put it back.

* Out of scope

- The counterparty's service and shell surfaces, if any read the centre. The
  scan for the member covers the whole tree and reports none.
