#+title: Trade journeys
#+description: What a person does with a trade: book it, amend it, read it, bring it in from ORE and send it out, record what happens to it, and group trades into one deal.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:user_journey:trading:
#+created: 2026-10-11
#+updated: 2026-10-11

A trade is an anchor that never changes, a booking and a state that do, and an
instrument chosen by the trade type. Every change to the booking, the state or the
instrument is caused by an activity, which is the record of one business event. The
firm holds a trade exactly as agreed. When the agreed terms change, the trade's external
version moves.

These journeys are use cases. Each one names a goal, an actor, the operations it
needs and what is missing. A journey does not define a screen. The screens come from the
[[id:00BA1689-B060-4BD8-BC46-67034D663B7F][UX standards]]. A trade is an entity, so one trade page with a tab for each aspect serves
most of these journeys, and one journey can reach several screens. The Qt client was
built around the trade as the entry. The prototypes keep that entry and use these
journeys to decide the cards and the workflows on the page.

* Prototypes

The [[id:741FE353-C420-41C1-913D-EA99A73754CA][Trades prototypes]] serve these journeys. Review, amendment and lifecycle events are one trade page. Booking, ORE import and export, and grouping are workflows reached from the trade list.

* What blocks all of them

- The BFF and the web app have no trading route.
- The trade list filters only by trade id, and most child tables have no by-trade read.
- No operation writes an activity together with the versions it causes, except booking.
- The economic digest leaves out the option exercise prices and a composite trade's legs.

* How the operations were checked

Every full subject (for example =trading.v1.trade_types.list=) was checked by script
against the protocol headers: 33 of 33 exist. A row that names a pattern, such as
=trading.v1.<instrument>s.put=, or a short form, such as =trade_states.list=, was read
by hand in the headers and cannot be checked by script. The filters, the =as_of= field
and the by-trade reads were read in the protocol headers of =trade=, =trade_state=,
=trade_activity=, =trade_identifier= and =bond_leg=. They were not read for every
child table.
