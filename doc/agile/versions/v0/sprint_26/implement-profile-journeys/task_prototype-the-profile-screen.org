* Goal

A clickable prototype of the profile screen that the design can be judged against, on a throwaway page with mock data. It renders the screen for both actors — the member on their own record and the tenant administrator on a colleague's — and argues three structural answers to the questions the journey documents leave open: whether the panels of one screen carry their own save, how a photo is replaced now that an upload path exists, and how the screen shows a reporting-line change that a superior or an administrator must approve before it takes effect.

* Acceptance

- The prototype is published beside its description and the other prototypes, and it reads no service.
- Each open layout question has at least two structural answers a reviewer can switch between.
- The photo picker, the read-only state and the reporting-line approval state are all renderable without reading the code.
- The verdict is recorded on the prototype page and the losing variants stay as the record.

* Plan

1. Read the three journey documents and the group page. Write down every
   requirement each states, marked settled or open.
2. Take the open ones to the product owner. Four were decided on 2026-09-30 and
   are distilled into the parent story's =* Decisions=; two more were settled
   from the documents themselves.
3. Build the prototype as a static page under =doc/prototypes/profile/=, which
   is how the tree keeps throwaway prototypes: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript,
   no framework and no build step. The tokens and the rail classes are copied
   from the application, not invented, and from the registration door prototype
   where it already copied them.
4. Publish it with the site build, beside the trade entry and registration door
   prototypes, so a reviewer opens it beside the description that argues it.
5. Drive the whole state matrix without a browser, so every combination of
   variant, actor and state is proved to render, then look at the screens in a
   browser.
6. Record the verdict on the prototype page and distil the decisions into the
   story.

* Result

The prototype is [[id:0AB29C42-742A-4E63-AE31-E8B6787D314E][Profile screen prototype]], at
=doc/prototypes/profile/= and published on the site beside the trade entry and
registration door prototypes. It renders three structural variants, for the
member and for a tenant administrator, in the six states the requirement work
named, and it uses the repository's own stock faces.

The verdict is recorded on that page: variant *B*, one page with a save per
panel, is accepted; *A* and *C* stay as the record. The reporting line is
proposed by the person whose line it is and approved by their hierarchical
superior or a tenant administrator.

The requirement work this task opened is on the parent story: four decisions
the product owner took, and two journey documents corrected where the
reporting-line decision changes them.

What the review did not settle is recorded rather than hidden: how an approver
is told, where the queue lives and what happens when a superior and an
administrator disagree are the Membership group's, and the prototype states the
gap where a claim would otherwise be implied.
