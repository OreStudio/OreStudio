#+title: Task: Keep the grid poller on its cadence while the service is busy
#+description: compute_grid_poller runs poll_once synchronously on the io_context and only arms its steady timer afterwards, so any blocking work elsewhere in the service pushes the sample interval out. In the incident the chart lost every bucket between samples. Sample on a timer that measures from the previous expiry rather than from the end of the work, and run the sampling where a busy executor cannot delay it.
