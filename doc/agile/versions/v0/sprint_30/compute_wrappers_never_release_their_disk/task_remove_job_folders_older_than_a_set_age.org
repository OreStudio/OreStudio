* Goal

A wrapper removes each job folder under =jobs/= that is older than a configured
age. The engine's results and logs are uploaded and published before the job
ends, so the folder is scratch data after that. Find the age from the existing
folders and from what a person needs to inspect a failed job.

* Acceptance

- A wrapper removes job folders older than the configured age, at start and
  then at a regular interval.
- The age is a wrapper setting with a stated default.
- A job that runs now is never removed.
- Decide whether a failed job's folder is kept longer than a successful one.
