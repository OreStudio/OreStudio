* Acceptance

- A chart draws B(t) = M(t) + C(t)/R stacked into its two parts, and the state bands come from the trace.
- The tab shows the run seed, the command log, and the heat's loop gain against one.
- The raster switches between clock time and tau, and a periodic feed's marks are evenly spaced in tau.
- Each feed shows observed against expected count over the window and a QQ plot of its rescaled gaps.
- Each raster row and the Arrivals panel show the law, base rate, sensitivity, effective rate now, a periodic phase and a Hawkes excitation, which closes the capture 'Show each feed's process in the regime raster'.
- The tab shows the scheduler's ticks a second, heap size and lateness.

* Plan

Everything the tab draws comes from the scheduler's own record, so the
trace first carries what the screen needs.

- Each trace event records the regime's multiplier and the pace after it,
  so the pace is drawn as a step stacked into the regime's part and the
  heat's, and the state bands come from the record rather than from the
  screen's polling.
- Each tick records its compensated gap, the law's intensity integrated
  over the gap from the law's state before the event
  (=domain::compensator=). By the time-rescaling theorem these are Exp(1)
  for a feed that follows its law, so a QQ plot against Exp(1) checks every
  law, a Hawkes one included; a periodic feed's are all one.
- The trace reply lists every running feed's law: its mode, rate,
  sensitivity, effective rate now (=domain::intensity_after= times the
  sensitivity and the pace), periodic phase and Hawkes excess.
- =marketTrace.ts= holds the pure functions: the pace steps, the bands, the
  command log, the tau span and positions, each feed's fit, and the Exp(1)
  quantiles. The tab and the Arrivals panel only draw them.
- The raster switches between clock time and tau; in tau a periodic feed's
  marks are evenly spaced. The switch is a radio group, so the regime switch
  stays the one pressed button.
- The fit expects the rate times the sensitivity times the tau the window
  spans, and leaves out each feed's first gap, which began before it.

* Result

A person watching the Market regime tab can now see how fast market time runs
and why, and check each feed against its law while the run goes on. The tab
draws the pace stacked into the regime and the heat, takes its state bands
from the scheduler's own record, shows the run seed, the loop gain against one
and the loop's numbers, and lists what the loop applied. The raster switches
between clock time and tau, and in tau a periodic feed's marks are evenly
spaced. Each row and the Arrivals panel show the feed's law, base rate,
sensitivity, rate now, and its initial phase or its excitation. The fit sets
each feed's count against its rate times its sensitivity times the market
time that passed, and plots its rescaled gaps against Exp(1). On Acme a feed
at a tick a second showed 123 ticks against 123.1 expected. The live check
also found a fault that was already on main: a pin from the screen took effect
and then reported a failure, because its reply lacked the heat the BFF reads.
