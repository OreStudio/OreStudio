W01 composition read, PR 2

Measured 2026-09-26 on feature/sync-trading-drift, by counting the
generated registrar families and their composition points rather than by
trusting the drift gate.

| Family kind                 | Declared | Composed | Composition point                              |
|-----------------------------+----------+----------+------------------------------------------------|
| Messaging handler registrar |       57 |       57 | core/src/messaging/registrar*.cpp              |
| History provider            |       31 |       31 | the same registrar sources                     |
| Event mapping               |       61 |       61 | service/src/app/application.cpp                |

The handler registrars are reached from the component's single
composition entry point, ores::trading::messaging::registrar::register_handlers,
which service/src/app/application.cpp and the core registrar detail both
call.

Twenty-five event mappings were declared and unwired:

  ascot, bond_forward, bond_future, bond_future_delivery_basket,
  bond_issue, bond_issue_call_date, bond_issue_conversion_target,
  bond_leg, bond_leg_amortization, bond_leg_amount, bond_leg_rate,
  bond_option, bond_repo, bond_trs, instrument_option,
  instrument_option_exercise_fee, instrument_option_payment_date,
  instrument_option_premium, instrument_schedule,
  instrument_schedule_date, instrument_strike, trade_envelope,
  trade_envelope_additional_field, trade_envelope_portfolio_id, trade.

The trade family is the one that matters. application.cpp carried a
hand-written legacy block for it -- register_mapping<trade_changed_event>
plus a subscribe that published the legacy {entity, timestamp, entity_ids,
tenant_id} payload -- while the generated register_trade_event_mapping was
never called. The block left the canonical family unwired and kept the
superseded trade_changed_event header alive. The other twenty-four were
simply never added when the regeneration materialised them.

All twenty-five are now composed. The legacy block is deleted, its
publish_entity_event wrapper with it, and the fifty-seven superseded
hand-written eventing headers and the api test that exercised one of them
are deleted:

    projects/ores.trading/api/include/ores.trading.api/eventing/*_changed_event.hpp
    projects/ores.trading/api/tests/eventing_trade_events_tests.cpp

The four families with no model -- commodity, composite, credit and
scripted -- keep their hand-written header, because their registrar still
subscribes to the legacy type and their event protocol is PR 3's. Who
still reads the legacy shape is recorded as part of the eventing payload
capture.

What the read does not cover: the permissions each handler checks (W02)
and the shell surface (S01, S02) are PR 6.
