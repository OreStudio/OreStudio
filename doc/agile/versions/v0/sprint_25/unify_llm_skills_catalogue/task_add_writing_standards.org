* Goal

We are about to hold three writing standards at once and they give
different advice, so the first job is to say which governs what rather
than leaving an author to guess. The division is by what the reading is
for. ASD-STE100 governs the agent's replies to a person, where the
reading is to be informed now and the demand is fast unambiguous
parsing. Thesis prose governs the org-roam documentation, where the
reading is to understand durably and the demand is an argued paragraph,
a definition at first use, constant terminology, and hedging matched to
the strength of the evidence rather than the strength of the claim.
Writing for agents governs anything loaded to change agent behaviour —
skills, memories, CLAUDE.md, specifications, plans and acceptance
criteria — where the reading is to act reliably and the demand is
context economy, progressive disclosure, clear completion criteria and
positive targets.

The standards compose within a document rather than one per file: a
recipe is exposition above and executable steps below, and a task
divides the same way between its goal and its acceptance. Some artefacts
take none of the three, and that exclusion list needs stating.

* Acceptance

- A single page states which standard governs what, divided by what the reading is for, and an author meets it before choosing how to write.
- The page says that standards compose within a document, and gives the recipe and task cases as worked examples.
- The exclusion list is explicit: commit messages, code comments, frontmatter, tables and generated blocks follow their own conventions and take none of the three.
- Thesis prose is adopted for the org-roam documentation, with the empirical-thesis chapter structure, hypothesis ordering and citation machinery excluded as out of scope for us.
- Writing for agents is adopted for skills, memories and CLAUDE.md as well as specifications and plans, and supersedes the prose guidance currently in skill-add.
- doc-review-ste100 is narrowed to the agent's conversational output and states what it does not cover.
- ASD-STE100 governs every conversational reply, including design discussion. It is not relaxed for a native speaker or for a technical exchange. Decided 2026-08-26 after the agent broke the rule repeatedly in this story's own discussion.
- The scope note says plainly that metaphor and borrowed jargon are out of scope for replies, and gives examples: write "essential" rather than "load-bearing", and "is worth its cost" rather than "earns its keep".
- Leading words are named as a technique for documents an agent reads. The scope note forbids carrying them into replies.
- Both imports carry their upstream repository, path, commit and licence per the attribution contract. Thesis writing is https://github.com/santifs/thesis-writing-skill, MIT.
- The rule against steering by prohibition is applied to our own documents: the architecture page and this design are swept for negations that should state a positive target.
- Hedging in our own documents is matched to the evidence: claims we have not tested are stated as proposals rather than as findings.
