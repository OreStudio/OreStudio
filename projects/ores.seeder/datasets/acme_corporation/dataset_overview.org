* Summary

Source data for Acme Corporation: four synthetic legal entities (Acme
Corporation Plc, ACME Corporation UK plc, ACME Corporation US Inc, ACME
Corporation HK Ltd), each with its own LEI, business units (trading desks
+ support functions), portfolios, books, and staff accounts. This is task 1 of
the Acme provisioning architecture (see [[id:CB7351E9-8E7E-4BA4-9399-4EB22F6192D4][Commission Acme Corporation]]'s =*
Decisions=): JSON only — no SQL, no server-side wiring yet.

* Naming

"Acme" is the archetypal fictional-company name -- from the Acme
Corporation, the mail-order supplier of absurdly over-engineered
products in Looney Tunes' /Road Runner/ cartoons, now a byword for
any generic placeholder business (see [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acme_Corporation][Acme Corporation on Wikipedia]]).
Reusing it here signals plainly, to anyone browsing the data, that
Acme Corporation is a synthetic reference/demo entity, not a real
institution. The =ores_assets_images_tbl= logo attached to the
=Acme Corp= counterparty fixture (see [[id:21C9CA52-3515-4D99-9B95-1F5BA8A05DCD][Attach a logo/image to a
counterparty]]) is sourced from that same Wikipedia article
([[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acme_Corporation#/media/File:Acme-corp.png][File:Acme-corp.png]]).

* Why this dataset

Acme Corporation is
designed to integrate with the system's real reference data — its
LEIs are checksum-valid on GLEIF's reserved =9695= test prefix so it
fits the same import machinery real tenants use, and it's meant to
trade against real GLEIF-imported counterparties. Each of the four
legal entities needs its own independent organisational structure
(existing publish-from-dq functions are single-party-scoped per
call), which is why every business-unit/portfolio/book/account
record carries a =company_code= tag.
