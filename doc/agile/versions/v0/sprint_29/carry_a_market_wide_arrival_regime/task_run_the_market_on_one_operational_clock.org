* Goal

One object owns the market's time: the regime chain, the heat and tau. It advances tau exactly across a constant regime and an exponentially decaying heat, and inverts that advance to tell the scheduler the clock time at which tau reaches a mark.

* Acceptance

- advance and time_to_reach are exact inverses across a regime transition and a heat decay, to a stated tolerance.
- A pinned state, an enrolment and a heat jump each change only how fast tau moves from that instant.
- The heat's loop gain is checked when the market is built, and a set whose gain is one or more is refused with the gain named.
- The chain takes its draws from the keyed stream. The heat draws nothing.

* Plan

=domain/operational_clock.hpp= sits beside market_regime and
shared_intensity. The scheduler and the service still use those two, and
the loop that will own the new clock comes in
[[id:6207909E-FC52-4EC4-86DE-DA76CEDB8D98][Let one loop own the market]],
which deletes them. The class is not named market_clock, because
marketdata.api already has a market_clock for another purpose.

- One loop owns the clock, so it holds plain values and no atomics.
- Between events the pace is M plus a heat share that decays, so tau has a
  closed form. time_to_reach inverts it with Newton's method from below.
  The function is increasing and concave, so the method does not overshoot.
- Each dwell is a unit exponential drawn in order and kept in a queue. The
  k-th transition uses the k-th draw, whether time_to_reach or advance asks
  for it first, so the two agree across transitions.
- The chain starts in its stationary distribution. The loop gain,
  jump over decay, is checked when the clock is built.

* Result

=domain/operational_clock.hpp= gives the market one clock. Over 400 targets
that cross more than fifty transitions while the heat decays, time_to_reach
and advance agree to one part in 10^12. A pin, a heat jump and an
enrolment leave tau where it was and change only its slope from that
instant. A heat whose loop gain is 1.25 is refused, and the message names
the gain. The chain spends two fifths of its time active as configured, and
the mean pace over 20,000 seconds is one. The loop takes this clock over,
and the old market types go, in
[[id:6207909E-FC52-4EC4-86DE-DA76CEDB8D98][Let one loop own the market]].
