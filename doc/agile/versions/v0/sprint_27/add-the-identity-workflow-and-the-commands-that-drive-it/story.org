* Goal

The identity workflow is a registered definition whose steps are
declared by the request, so one fixture covers many workflow
configurations that emulate real ones, and =ores.shell= drives it end to
end. The fixture and the cross-tenant engine shipped with the source
story; this story is the identity workflow and its commands.
