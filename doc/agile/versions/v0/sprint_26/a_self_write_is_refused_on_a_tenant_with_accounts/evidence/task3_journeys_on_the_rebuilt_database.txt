--- the three failures ---

Two trace to the upload over the message fabric's payload cap alone
(capture 820AC660-C3FC-4A45-998D-A65818FA4BB8): the oversized upload is
refused by the transport with 503 before the validator's own answer is
reached, so the refusal cannot name the size rule.

The third does not trace to that defect. The stale claim answers
outcome=conflict with code=version_conflict, which the checker asserts, and
with an empty message, which it also asserts. No service in
ores.iam/core/src/service sets a message on this branch: the panel composes
the sentence itself (ProfilePage.tsx draws the record-changed sentence from
the conflict outcome), and the self contact write carries no version claim,
so no member path reaches the empty message. The assertion is stricter than
the journeys state.
