This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

A draft now says why it is not live, and the reason names the desk that owns it.
A report of drafts would list each one with its age — how long it has sat since
it was captured — its reason, and the desk responsible, oldest first.

Nothing reports that today. A draft is a row in the blotter that has not moved,
and the only way to find the ones that have sat too long is to read them.

* Why

The reason and the responsible desk exist so that an aged draft is somebody's
work. Without a report they are two columns nobody reads: the desk that should be
chasing a confirmation cannot see that a draft has waited a week, and the
operations manager cannot see which desk is carrying them.

The trading side already stores what the report needs. What is missing is the
report itself, and the reporting side is where it belongs.

* Shape of the work

Not designed here. The pieces that appear necessary:

- A report definition over the drafts of a party or a tenant: the reference, the
  kind, the trade date, the age, the draft reason and its responsible desk.
- An age that means something: days since the trade was captured, counting
  business days against a calendar the tenant already holds.
- A group by desk, since the question the report answers is "who is late", not
  "what is open".
- Whether a draft that has sat past a threshold raises a notice, which the
  approval side already knows how to do.

* What done looks like

A person can read how many drafts a tenant holds, how long the oldest has waited,
and which desk owns each one, without opening a single deal.
