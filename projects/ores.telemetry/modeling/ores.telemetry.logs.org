#+created: 2026-09-26
#+updated: 2026-09-26

The log surface: a filtered read of the stored log entries, and a
fire-and-forget publish that ingests entries from a wrapper node.

These are operations rather than entity verbs. The list is a filtered read
over the log store, and the publish arrives from outside the service and is
stored as it arrives, so neither is a row a caller reaches by primary key.
