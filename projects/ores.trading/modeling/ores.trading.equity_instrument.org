#+created: 2026-10-17
#+updated: 2026-10-17

One row per equity trade, the family's own header. It holds only what every
equity product states the same way: the trade it belongs to, the trade type
code, the party, the activity that wrote the version, the instrument's start
and maturity dates and its free text. It holds no product economics.

No economic field is common to all the routed codes. The underlying is one
name for most products and a basket for the worst-of swap, so it stays on the
facts and in =equity_basket_constituents= rather than on the header. A header
column has to hold for every code, and the underlying does not.

The products' own fields stay in their fact tables, keyed by the same trade
and joined to this row by the trade id. A fact table carries no =party_id= and
no =trade_type_code=: the header is the family's party boundary and its
routing boundary.

The dates are the family's common dates, the one set of names for the start
and the maturity of every equity product, so the blotter reads one column for
each across the whole family.

The trade this instrument belongs to, and the instrument's own key.

The trade id identifies both the trade and its instrument, so the header
carries no identity of its own: this column is the key, and the trade it
names gives the instrument its scope. Every fact and child row of the family
names the same trade.

* Delete cascade

The rows an equity instrument owns. Each is keyed by the instrument's own
trade id, so the delete rule closes them all with one statement per table and
the family does not outlive its header.

The legs, the strikes and the schedules are the shared shapes the bond pilot
and the rates family built; the equity family writes to them rather than
widening them.

* C++

The C++ domain class nests the scaffolding columns and the columns marked
=:group: identity= below into an =instrument_identity identity;= member (see
the ores.trading.instrument_identity field-group model), the implicit audit
columns fold into an =ores::dq::domain::audit_record audit;= member (see the
ores.dq.audit_record field-group model), and the dates and the description
stay flat in between. The SQL schema, DB entity and column lists are
unaffected.
