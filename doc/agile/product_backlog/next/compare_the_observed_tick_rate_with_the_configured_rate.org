* What

Show the tick rate each source observes beside the rate its running feeds
configure: the sum of =ticks_per_hour= over the running feeds, times the
multiplier of the market regime in force. Flag a source whose ratio is far from
one, in the funnel view and as a warning in the log.

* Why

On 2026-10-26 the machine went down overnight. Two defects together caused it: the shared Hawkes heat ran the synthetic feeds at about 300 times their configured rate, and the NATS client kept every message it received. Finding both took ad hoc sampling of systemd and /proc, parsing marketdata's once-a-minute INGEST STATUS log line, NATS monitoring endpoints, Postgres row counts and a core dump. The synthetic service published about 9,500 ticks a second
against a configured 32. A ratio check would have flagged the runaway in its
first minute, before the machine ran short of memory.
