* Acceptance

- The journeys of the topic are written as user journey documents, and each is judged as a CRUD variation the standard screen covers or as a workflow the screen offers under Workflows.
- A prototype of each screen follows the settled standard and is signed off before any code.
- The screens are built from the shared record parts, with the server side extensions they need, and no screen builds a dialog of its own.
- A test scenario covers list, record page, create, amend with a stale version, delete, history and revert.
- The harness passes on each screen, the manual checklist lines are walked, and the sign off is recorded on the task.
- The pipeline is written as a runbook and the next topic story is scaffolded from it.

* Decisions

- The calendars topic has one journey of its own, judged on 2026-10-25 as a
  CRUD variation of the standard record screen with parts, two own actions and
  one derived view; no workflow. Signed off by the owner the same day.
- /Rebuild business days/ moves from the Business days tab to the header, as
  RS-PAGE-03 places a kind's own actions (owner, 2026-10-25).
- The prototype was signed off on 2026-10-25 after one correction: History is
  the shared timeline of the Entity screen specification, never the two-pane
  compare of the legacy =HistoryPanel=; a reference to a country is a flag and
  a link to the country (owner, 2026-10-25). The build moves the live screen
  to the prototype.
- A record's History is the record's story: its own versions and those of
  every part it holds now, merged and named by their part, with a deleted part
  stated as a gap. RS-HIST-01 changed on 2026-10-25 when the owner's run of
  the calendars scenario looked for an exception's change in the calendar's
  history and did not find it.
- The pipeline is a runbook of five steps, three of which stop for the owner;
  each topic adds what it taught, and the next topic's story is scaffolded
  from it at the close.
- A supranational calendar carries its own flag from the seed; a national one
  takes its country's on the screen (owner, 2026-10-25, for TARGET).
