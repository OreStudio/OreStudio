* What

Two pages name command subjects for the party-provisioning saga that do not
exist:

- The header comment of
  =projects/ores.refdata/api/include/ores.refdata.api/workflow/provision_parties_workflow.hpp:41-47=
  says step 0 publishes =refdata.v1.parties.save= and step 2 publishes
  =iam.v1.account_parties.save=.
- =projects/ores.refdata/core/modeling/ores.refdata.protocol.org:64=
  lists =refdata.v1.parties.save= in its Parties table, and
  =refdata.v1.parties.history= on the line below, which is absent too.

The code uses different names. The definition's real step subjects are
=refdata.v1.parties.put= (=provision_parties_workflow.hpp:81=),
=iam.v1.accounts.save= (=:134=) and =iam.v1.account_parties.put_many=
(=:173=). No =nats_subject= anywhere uses =parties.save=,
=account_parties.save= or =parties.history=.

* Why

The refdata protocol page is where a reader learns which subjects the
component serves, and the comment is where a reader learns what the saga
does. Both send the reader to a subject no broker will route, and the
mismatch already misled the journey architecture audit into recording the
=save= names as the saga's steps.
