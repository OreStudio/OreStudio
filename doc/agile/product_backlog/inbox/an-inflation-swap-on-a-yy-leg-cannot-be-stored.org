This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Give the inflation swap's year-on-year leg the treatment the CPI leg just got:
read =Index=, =BaseCPI= and =ObservationLag= from =YYLegData= on import, and
write that group back on export. The alternative is a named refusal, so a YY
document reads as a product the family does not model rather than as a raw check
violation.

* Why

The rates review's finding 2 was that an inflation swap cannot be stored, and
that is fixed for a CPI leg. A year-on-year swap states the same three values on
a YY leg, and neither direction of the mapper has a YY branch, so the fact row's
=inflation_index_code= stays empty and the table's non-empty check refuses the
insert. The trade is then left half-written exactly as before: a trade row and a
=rate_instruments= header, no fact, no legs.

No document in the corpus uses a YY leg, which is why nothing noticed, and the
same absence of a witness is the reason the CPI half survived so long. ORE's
schema makes YY a first-class alternative to CPI, so this is a product the
family routes and cannot store.
