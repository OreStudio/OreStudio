* Result

The trade shape is settled and recorded in
[[id:FDC35F65-CBF0-40A0-96D0-EA180F2237EE][Investigation: the trade shape]], sixteen sections, each with the
decision and the alternatives it set aside. The trade becomes an
immutable anchor with components per access pattern; sets, groups and
links are told apart; activities, authorisation decisions, the agreement
and structures are entities; every instrument family follows the rule
the bonds arrived at; workspaces leave the trade model and sandboxes
replace them.

The knowledge base needed to tell one story first, so the design produced
seven notes — Trade sets, groups and links; Netting sets; Trade
compression; Trade merge; Trade allocation; Book moves; Sandbox — and
brought the link catalogue, Trade Links, Trade Versioning, Trade
Activity, Trade Structures, the book cluster and the funding and sweep
notes into line. The book move and sandbox notes were checked against
Basel RBC25 and MAR12, IFRS 9, EMIR Article 9, MiFIR Article 25 and
MiFID II Article 16(6).

Seven implementation stories are in sprint 26 and seven instrument family
stories in the backlog. The deal id to trade id rename is a task of its
own on the story.
