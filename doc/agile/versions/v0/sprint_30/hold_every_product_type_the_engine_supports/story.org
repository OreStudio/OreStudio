* Goal

Every product type the ORE engine supports can be held, imported, exported,
booked by a builder and generated. The catalogue states each type once and names
the table that holds it; a trade of the type can be read from ORE's document,
written back as the element ORE reads, and built from typed terms; and the
generator books at least one of each. A type the engine cannot carry leaves the
system, with the reason kept where a reader can find it.

* Acceptance

- every product type in the catalogue names an instrument table, and a type the engine cannot carry is absent from the catalogue with its reason recorded
- every routed type has a data model of its own: the tables its facts need, the checks those tables state, and the relationship rows the shape requires
- every routed type has a projection reader and writer, so a document imports into rows and exports as the element ORE reads
- every routed type has a builder, and a term a builder is not given does not compile
- the generator books at least one trade of every routed type, and a test fails when a type is added to the catalogue without a route, a builder and a generator
- where ORE reads a product through a scripted trade, the domain still models the product's facts, and the projection writes the scripted element ORE reads rather than a bare script name
- the coverage is visible on the catalogue's own page: every type with its form, its table, its builder and its generator, and the types that left with their reasons

* Decisions

- A product's facts are modelled once, in the shape its terms demand, whether or
  not ORE reads the product through a script. Where ORE carries it as a
  =ScriptedTrade= subclass, its element is a fixed schema the projection writes
  member by member, as it already does for =DoubleDigitalOption=; where ORE
  carries it natively, the element is written natively. The catalogue records
  which of the two forms each type takes, and both are supported.
- A type the engine cannot carry is removed from the catalogue rather than left
  routing nowhere, so nothing can book or import it. Its reason and the evidence
  from ORE's source stay in the coverage note, and a curation exclusion list
  names it, so a later ORE release that starts supporting it fails a check
  instead of passing unnoticed.
- Market data and pricing are another environment's work. This story ends when a
  type can be held and built, not when it prices.

* Out of scope

- Market data, pricing engine configuration and valuation. Another environment
  owns them; this story stops at the trade's data model, its builder and its
  generator.
