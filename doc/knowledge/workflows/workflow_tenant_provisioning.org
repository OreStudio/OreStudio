#+title: Tenant provisioning
#+description: A tenant is created from a starting point and brought to a working state, with its administrator, its reference data and its parties.
#+type: workflow

Tenant provisioning creates a tenant from a starting point and brings it to a
working state. The caller chooses the starting point, describes the tenant and
its administrator, and confirms; the request creates the tenant and its
administrator at once and opens a workflow instance for the work that follows.
When the workflow instance finishes, the tenant holds its administrator, its
reference data and its parties, and the workflow instance rests in a terminal
state. A step that fails stops the workflow instance and keeps every completed
step, so a person retries from the failed step or discards the tenant.

* Purpose

Tenant provisioning turns a chosen starting point into a live tenant. The
request creates the tenant and its administrator before it answers, and the
workflow instance performs the operations the starting point orders: it
publishes a bundle, imports the legal-entity hierarchy, provisions every party
the tenant holds, and completes the tenant. When the workflow instance reaches
its terminal state, the tenant is ready with its administrator, its reference
data and its parties, and a client can hand the tenant over to that
administrator. The workflow instance is the platform's record of the work, so
any client observes the outcome without the caller keeping state of its own.

* Participants

The caller chooses the starting point, describes the tenant and its
administrator, and confirms. The process manager owns the workflow instance: it
builds the step list from the definition, dispatches one step at a time, records
each outcome, and moves the workflow instance to its terminal state. The
definition declares the workflow type, the ordered steps, and the failure policy
the workflow follows. The component owning the operations performs the work each
step names and reports the step's outcome. The store holds the workflow
instance, its steps, and their results and errors, so any reader follows it. The
client follows the workflow instance through the progress read and renders one
line per operation.

* Steps

The workflow runs the step kinds a starting point orders, in the order that
starting point states, and it appends the completing operation last. Every step
kind shares one failure rule: a failure stops the workflow instance on that step,
keeps every completed step and its result, and waits for a retry.

#+ATTR_HTML: :class hug-leading
| Operation                          | Produces                                                                 | On failure                                                                                  |
|------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Publishing a bundle                | The reference data the bundle gathers, published into the tenant          | Stops the workflow instance; every completed step is kept; a retry resumes from this step    |
| Importing a legal-entity hierarchy | The legal entities a root LEI anchors, with their relationships            | Stops the workflow instance; every completed step is kept; a retry resumes from this step    |
| Provisioning a party               | One party, with the reference data its bundle holds, once per party the tenant has | Stops the workflow instance; every completed step is kept; a retry resumes from this step |
| Loading staff                      | The tenant's staff accounts and their parties                              | Stops the workflow instance; every completed step is kept; a retry resumes from this step    |
| Attaching images                   | The images the tenant's records display                                    | Stops the workflow instance; every completed step is kept; a retry resumes from this step    |
| Starting market feeds              | The tenant's market data feeds                                             | Stops the workflow instance; every completed step is kept; a retry resumes from this step    |
| Completing the tenant              | The tenant in its ready state, and the workflow instance's final step      | Stops the workflow instance; the tenant stays unready until a retry completes the step       |

* Failure and recovery

Tenant provisioning declares the stop-and-retry failure policy. Its completed
steps are published reference data and parties that a person expects to keep, and
every step is idempotent, so a retry is cheaper and safer than a rollback of work
that already succeeded.

A retry resumes from the failed step. The retry re-dispatches that step under its
stable identity and clears the workflow instance's error, so the work that
succeeded is not repeated. The retry reads the workflow instance's persisted
record: every completed step keeps its result, and the failed step keeps its
position and its error. A caller may name the step to resume from, and a workflow
instance that has stopped resumes from the step that failed when the caller names
none.

Discarding a tenant is a separate action from a retry. Discard removes the tenant
and stops the workflow instance the tenant owns, so no workflow instance keeps
running behind a discarded tenant.

* Progress

A client follows the workflow instance through a read that returns the workflow
instance's status, the number of steps it declares, the step it is executing, and
one summary per step naming the operation, the step's state and its error. The
read exposes the workflow instance states pending, in progress, completed and
failed, and the step states pending, running, done and failed. The client renders
one line per operation, so the rail shows every step as the workflow instance
advances. A failed step carries its error until a retry resumes it, and a
completed workflow instance shows every step done.

* Patterns used

- Saga is not used: provisioning declares stop-and-retry rather than
  compensation, because its completed steps are published data.
- [[id:98177604-4092-4A95-B2C3-EE6F82A3185E][Process Manager]] — the engine runs
  the provisioning plan's steps.
- [[id:26AE458C-5E23-423B-BEBE-1B55D83A7AA5][Idempotent Receiver]] — every step
  is idempotent, so a retry is safe.
