A tester's results land in the org file through compass, the only writer of the org tree. compass scenario lists the scenarios and their state, shows one with its steps, its story and its task, and records each step's status, note and image names plus the overall state.

* Acceptance

- compass scenario list shows every scenario with its state, target and story, and tells waiting from done.
- compass scenario show returns the steps, each with its client, status and note, and the ids of the story and the task, as JSON.
- compass scenario record writes each step's Result table and the overall State and Results in place, and stamps the journal.
- Tests cover a single-client and a multi-client scenario and a re-record over an earlier result.
