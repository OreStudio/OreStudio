* Plan

whose task files are all DONE. Each was closed with =compass story done=, which
flips the story, its sprint row and the journal together. The Results were
appended afterwards: the seven hotfixes from their merged PR numbers, read out
of the task files, and the five larger stories with a sentence saying the close
records task state rather than a fresh verification.

and one half closed, where the table beneath it marks all seven closed and the
two sweeps that answered the seventh are described further down the same page.
