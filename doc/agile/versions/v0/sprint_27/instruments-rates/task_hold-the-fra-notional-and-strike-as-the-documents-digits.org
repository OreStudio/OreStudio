* Goal

A FRA's notional and strike are stored as the exact decimal the document wrote, and the exporter writes the same digits back. 0.0065 is stored as 0.0065, not as 0.006500000134110451.

* Acceptance

- The ORE binding keeps the digits of the FRA notional and strike, through the reapply script, not by hand.
- The forward mapper stores the document's digits for both, and the reverse mapper hands them back.
- A mapper test holds the digits of a FRA strike and notional through the round trip.
- The golden files that change are regenerated and every changed line is a numeric leaf.
- The corpus proof and the ORE suite pass.

* Plan

1. *The binding.* Make the FRA's =Strike= and =Notional= a lexical leaf,
   =moneyFloat=, instead of a bare float. The change is made by
   =scripts/reapply_ore_lexical_form.py= on =domain.hpp= and =domain.cpp=, so
   a regenerated binding keeps it. The script refuses to patch when an anchor
   is missing or not unique.
2. *The mapper.* The forward mapper already reads a lexical leaf through
   =exact_decimal=, so it now stores the document's digits. The reverse mapper
   hands the stored digits back with =set_exact=.
3. *Tests.* Correct the FRA forward test, which pinned the float artifact, and
   add a test that the digits survive the reverse mapping.
4. *Goldens.* Regenerate the one golden that changes and check that the change
   is numeric text only.

* Result

A FRA's strike and notional are stored as the digits the document wrote and
exported as the same digits. IR_FRA.xml states a strike of 0.005; the mapper
stored the float nearest to it and the exporter wrote 0.00499999989. The
binding now holds both members as a lexical leaf, through the reapply script,
the mapper reads and writes the digits, and the one golden that changed
holds the document's text.

Acceptance met: the binding keeps the digits through the script, both mapper
directions carry them, a test holds them through the reverse mapping, the
golden change is one numeric line, and the ORE suite passes.

Other bare float members of the binding are not covered, and no task is
open for them.
