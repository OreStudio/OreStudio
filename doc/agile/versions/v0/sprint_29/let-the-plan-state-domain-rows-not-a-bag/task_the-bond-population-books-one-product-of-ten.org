* Goal

Ten trade types route to the bond instrument table. The planner draws exactly one of them, a plain Bond, so every one of the twenty-four bond trades in a provisioned tenant is a Bond and the other nine codes never appear. The family's own fact rows -- the option, the repo, the future, the total return swap, the ASCOT, the callable's call dates, the convertible's conversion targets -- are all empty, because the writer fills only the instrument header. A tenant therefore cannot see a bond option, a repo or a convertible, and nothing says so.

* Scope, before any code

Making the other nine products book needs, in order: a decision about which of
them a population should draw, what each one's economics should say (a bond
option's strike, a repo's rate and haircut, a convertible's conversion
targets), a planner change, and fact-row construction. The tables for those
facts exist and are empty.

None of that is a constructor's job, and the decisions are not the
constructor's either: they say what ACME's bond trades are.
