#+title: Service registry
#+description: Codegen service-registry model — catalogue of ORE Studio backend services and fleet processes, with two independent aspects per entry: DB access (psql var, IAM role, DDL grant prefixes) and deployment (replicas, enabled, startup dependencies, extra CLI args). Not every entry has both: the DB-access aspect only applies to services with their own NATS-domain-service role, while the deployment aspect applies to every process compass systemd generate needs to know about. Platform-agnostic by design: nothing in the deployment aspect is systemd-specific, so a future Windows-service or macOS-launchd generator can consume the same model.
