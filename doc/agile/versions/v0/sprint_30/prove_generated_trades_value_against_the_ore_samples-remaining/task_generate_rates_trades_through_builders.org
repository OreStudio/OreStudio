* Acceptance

- convention_lookup answers what each interest rate builder asks.
- The interest rate planner draws values and calls the builders; its construct_* functions and *_terms structs are deleted.
- The interest rate entries leave the recorded trades' known lists, and the recorded files change in the same commit.
- The ORE engine test reads every booked interest rate type, and builds each one or fails only on market data or engine configuration the Products example lacks.
- The roundtrip tests read every ORE sample trade of each booked interest rate type and write back every element ORE needs.

* Plan

- Every booked rate type wraps a vanilla swap or its floating leg, so each
  builder starts from =vanilla_swap_builder= and adds its own rows: the cross
  currency swap's foreign leg and notional exchanges, the FRA's fact, the cap or
  floor's strike, the swaption's and the callable swap's option and exercise
  dates, the knock out swap's barrier, the flexi swap's option side, and the
  balance guaranteed swap's tranche.
- The inflation swap is booked from the same constructors, so it moves here
  too. =convention_lookup= answers an inflation swap convention by the index
  address, and the CPI leg takes its calendar, day count and lag from it.
- The planner draws the index from the tenant's swap conventions in the scope,
  as the RPA does, rather than from the FpML catalogue by name. A forward rate
  agreement and a cap need an Ibor index, which a swap convention names.
- The =construct_rate_*= functions and the =rate_*_terms= structs are deleted,
  with the plan's =rate_terms=.
