* Acceptance

- The review measures the dimensions the skill names: goal alignment, sprint
  load, PR velocity, story and task balance, focus signal, velocity and the
  overall verdict.
- Every number in the review is a measurement with the command that produced it
  and the commit it was taken at, or is marked as an estimate with its basis.
- The findings, the reconciliations and the close decisions are written into the
  review task's =* Result=, and the sprint's =* Health Review= table carries the
  review's summary row.
