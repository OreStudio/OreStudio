* Plan

A new area in =projects/ores.web/packages/web/src/development/=, drawn from the prototype in =doc/prototypes/testing/test-scenarios/=.

- =shell/areas.ts= gets a Development entry in =MENU=, with no permission and no scope, so the menu draws it for everybody. =AppRoutes.tsx= gets =/development= and =/development/tests/:id=.
- =DevelopmentArea= draws the breadcrumb and the tab bar through =useTabs=. The only tab is Tests. Board, Stories and Tasks join it in story 2.
- =TestsQueue= reads =GET /api/qa/scenarios= through =api/qa.ts= and draws two cards, Waiting and Done. A row shows the scenario, its story and task, its target and clients, a progress bar with "n of m steps", a state tag and the completion time. A row opens =/development/tests/:id=. The tab has no heading of its own.
- =queue.ts= holds the logic and is tested alone: the state of a row, the percent done, and the order. Waiting scenarios in progress come before those not started. Done scenarios come newest completion first, and undated ones last.
- =ScenarioPage= is the page a row opens. It names the scenario and lists its steps, and says the runner is not built yet. Task 5 replaces its body with the runner.
- The catalogue gets a =development= block and a menu key in English, French and Portuguese.
- =@ores/contracts= now serves the browser as well as the BFF for the QA shapes, and its comment says so.

* Result

The top menu has a Development entry, drawn for everybody with no permission. It opens an area whose only tab is Tests. The tab has no heading and draws no Board, Stories or Tasks tab. Two cards, Waiting and Done, list the scenarios the BFF serves. A row shows the scenario with its story and task, its target and clients, a progress bar with "n of m steps", a state tag and the completion time, and it opens /development/tests/:id. Scenarios in progress come before those not started, and done scenarios come newest completion first. The page a row opens names the scenario, lists its steps and says the runner is not built yet. Task 5 replaces its body.

The queue logic is in queue.ts and is tested alone. The screens are tested as rendered markup with a seeded query cache, for an empty queue, a mixed queue, a failed load and the loading state, and the routes are tested under the shell. The web suite passes with 745 tests. Breaking the ordering, the error branch, the row link or the click guard fails its own tests. The review found one real fault, a click that navigated twice, and it is fixed.

No test drives a browser against a live BFF, because a sign-in needs the NATS broker. The French and Portuguese text is my translation and needs a read by a speaker. The Completed column replaces the prototype's Updated column, because a summary carries a completion time and no edit time. Task 5 builds the runner.
