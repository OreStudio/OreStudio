This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Trade population books BalanceGuaranteedSwaps with no tranche rows. The
ORE writer requires at least one Tranche, so an export that reaches one of
them stops: =required element 'Tranche' is absent from 'tranches'=.

* Why

After Acme provisioning, an export of the whole tenant fails on these
trades, so no portfolio can be exported until they are filtered out. A
generated trade should be one ORE can read.
