* Goal

A caller builds a complete swap by stating its economic terms; the builder resolves its references and derives what conventions fix, and a missing term fails to compile or names itself.

* Acceptance

- Each builder requires the terms the [[id:45DFB9BE-495C-4BE5-954B-D06673BB178F][review]] lists as mandatory for its type.
- =required<T>=, =build_error{term, message}= and the registry: each builder states its trade type, and a test fails when the builders and the trade type catalogue differ.
- The fixed and floating leg sub-builders derive frequency, day count, calendar, adjustment and fixing days from the swap and index conventions found through =convention_lookup=, and emit each leg's schedule row.
- A vanilla swap built on EUR-EURIBOR-6M from ORE's example conventions writes the same XML as ORE's own sample, up to the economic terms.
