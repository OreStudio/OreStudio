* Goal

No component carries a workspace: no table has a workspace_id, no request or session carries a workspace context, and ores.workspace is gone. Sandboxes and virtual books, which replaced workspaces in the trade shape design, are the only way to keep work apart from official data.

* Acceptance

- No codegen profile or model sets has_workspace_id, and no generated or hand-written table has a workspace_id column.
- No request context, NATS header, session or database setting carries a workspace; ores_utility_live_workspace_id_fn and its kin are gone.
- The ores.workspace component, its tables, protocol, shell and web surfaces are removed, with no references left.
- Every component builds and its tests pass after each step; main is never half migrated.
- The trading part is done by the trade shape story, Split the trade into an anchor and its components; this story covers the rest.

* Decisions

- Workspaces are removed, not reworked (owner's choice). Section 13 of
  [[id:FDC35F65-CBF0-40A0-96D0-EA180F2237EE][Investigation: the trade shape]] replaced them with
  [[id:4AB0BC63-D73A-4FC3-B9AF-16C1BB90653F][Sandbox]]es and virtual books, which shipped in
  [[id:3724EAAA-A905-4D1E-AF24-42A047CD7276][Add sandboxes and virtual books]]. A layered workspace is an
  application-level database branch that pushes a workspace into every key,
  query and foreign key.
- Trading is removed by [[id:AA3E4655-8E80-4F69-845C-9D77B3985835][Split the trade into an anchor and its components]],
  whose acceptance already requires that no trading entity carries a
  workspace. This story removes the rest and then the component.

Most refdata and trading hits are generated repositories and generators, so
a profile and field group change carries them. The plumbing (database,
nats, service, utility) is the request context.

* Proposed sequence

1. Inventory and sequence: confirm the counts, find every reader of the
   workspace context, and decide each task below.
2. Drop =has_workspace_id= from the remaining profiles and models, one
   component at a time (refdata, analytics, reporting, marketdata), each a
   regeneration and a build.
3. Remove the workspace from the request context, NATS headers, sessions
   and database settings.
4. Remove =ores.workspace=: its tables, protocol, service, shell and web
   surfaces.

* Out of scope

- The trading tables and code, which [[id:AA3E4655-8E80-4F69-845C-9D77B3985835][Split the trade into an anchor and its components]] removes.
- Migrating rows of workspaces other than the live one: the databases are
  recreated, so no data is carried across.
