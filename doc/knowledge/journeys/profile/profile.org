#+title: Profile journeys
#+description: How a person is represented: name, photo, job title and contact details.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:user_journey:security:
#+created: 2026-09-23
#+updated: 2026-09-23

A person is represented by more than one record. The
[[id:81D4D50F-A8D4-47F8-9500-89F6AC460469][account]] carries the name, photo,
job title and reporting line; a separate contact record carries the address,
telephone and web page. These journeys treat both as panels of one screen,
because a person thinks of them as one thing.

Two of the journeys are self-service and one is the same screen in an
administrator's hands. The self-service pair are the journeys with the largest
known gap: the server exposes no operation for a member to change their own
name, photo, job title or contact details. Only an administrator can.
