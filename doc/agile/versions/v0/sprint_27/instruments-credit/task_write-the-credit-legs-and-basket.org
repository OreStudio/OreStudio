* Acceptance

- swap_legs has a nullable leg_group, and a credit leg carries its group.
- credit_basket_constituents holds the names of an index swap or a CDO basket.
- The four payment groups of a credit linked swap are all written, not the first contingent leg only.
- The corpus documents' legs come back as the same rows.

* Plan

1. *Order.* The legs land before the header. The header unit drops the wide
   columns, and a leg read from a column that is gone would lose data. The
   design and the story carry the swap.
2. *The leg group.* =swap_legs= gets a nullable =leg_group=, closed to
   premium, independent, contingent, default, recovery and underlying.
3. *The basket child.* A new =credit_basket_constituent= model, cloned from the
   commodity constituent: keyed by trade and sequence number, with the issuer,
   qualifier, curve, notional, weight and currency. The notional is an exact
   decimal and the weight is continuous.
4. *The carrier.* =credit_instrument_data= holds the instrument, the legs, the
   amounts, the rates and the constituents. It replaces the bare instrument in
   the wire variant, the batch, the batch mapper, the export service, the
   exporter and the import handler.
5. *The mapper.* The credit mapper reads and rebuilds legs through the rates
   mapper's leg functions, made public as the shared seam. A credit linked swap
   writes its four payment groups. An index swap, an option and a CDO write
   their basket.
6. *The binding.* =nameData.Notional= is a money leaf, so the basket keeps its
   digits.
7. *Prove it.* Update the tests that used the bare instrument, add the leg and
   basket tests, regenerate the one golden whose digits change, and run the
   suites.

* Notes

- The ctest runs went straight to ctest, not through =compass test run=, because
  the host-wide build lock was held by other worktrees for over an hour. The
  user allowed it for these runs.
- The telemetry roster test was broken on main: it reported three instances on
  one host, and the roster keeps only the newest instance per host. The fix
  gives the fresh instance another host. It rides in this task to save a PR.
- The drift run regenerated four create scripts this task does not model:
  the compute apps, app versions and platforms, and the IAM tenants. Their
  delete rules were missing the own-tenant limit that the templates now state.
  Main carried that drift, so the regenerated files ride in this commit.
- The digest components section of the catalogue covers neither the swap legs
  nor the constituents. The gap is older than this task and is left as it is.
- The basket keeps six columns: issuer, qualifier, curve, notional, weight and
  currency. Recovery rate, prior notional and weight, and the auction and
  default dates wait for unit 6.
- The commodity constituent model says the weight is exact, but its column is a
  double. The credit child uses a double on purpose, because a weight is
  continuous.
- Risk participation agreements have no legs yet. Their underlying legs belong
  to unit 6.
- The bespoke basket golden changed from =100000= to =100000.0=. The source
  document writes =100000.0=, so the old golden had lost the digits.

* Result

The credit swap family now writes its legs and its basket as rows. A premium
leg and the four payment groups of a credit linked swap are rows of the shared
leg tables, told apart by =leg_group=. The names of a basket are rows of the new
=credit_basket_constituents= table. The import, the export and the reverse
mapping all read them through the =credit_instrument_data= carrier.

Acceptance met: =swap_legs= has a nullable =leg_group= and a credit leg carries
its group; the basket has its table; the credit linked swap writes all four
groups; and the corpus documents come back with the same legs and basket names.
The header and the six fact tables follow in unit 5.
