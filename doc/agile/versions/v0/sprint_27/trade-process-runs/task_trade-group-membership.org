* Acceptance

- The trade group carries a foreign key to its run, and the membership is keyed by the group and the trade with enforced foreign keys.
- The role comes from the process the run is a run of, not from a global list.
- The roles tell what the run read from what it wrote, and a trade may be in both.
- No row of the membership references a trade activity: the activity is the stamp on an amendment, and the run is the parent here.
