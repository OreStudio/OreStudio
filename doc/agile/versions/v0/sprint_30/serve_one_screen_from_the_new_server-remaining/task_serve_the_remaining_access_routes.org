* Plan

The pairing gave ten of the access area's 30 routes a C++ route, but none of
them at the address ores.web calls: =GET /api/accounts/:accountId/access= pairs
with =POST /api/v1/iam/ops/get_account_roles=. The story's decision of
2026-11-11 records that each area task serves the BFF's own address and shape.

This task serves the seven reads of the access screens in a hand-written unit,
=access_read_routes=: the roles the caller and an account hold, a permission
page of each, a page of the tenant's roles, a page of what a role grants, and
a page of who holds a role. Each forwards the caller's token, reads the query
within the BFF's limits, refuses a page that is not one before it forwards, and
answers the camelCase shape the BFF gave. A refused outcome answers with the
status its outcome names, as the other C++ routes do, where the BFF answered
every refusal with 409.

The other 23 routes move to [[id:3E4FCAC5-4E49-4853-AC51-470E73F6D66A][Serve the access writes, profiles and the reporting tree]].
