#+title: The delivery meter keeps rejected counts forever
#+description: A delivery count the store rejects goes back into the meter and is retried every flush, with no limit, so a count that can never be written grows the meter for the life of the service.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

When the store rejects a delivery count, =usage_recorder::flush= puts the count
back into the delivery meter, and the next flush tries it again. A rejection
that will never succeed, such as a bad =modified_by= or a terminated tenant,
keeps the count forever, and the meter grows by one entry per party, series and
hour. Retry a count that failed because the store was down; give up on, and
report, a count the store refused.

* Why

Every delivery count failed this way until the recorder used the service
account. The growth is slow, about half a megabyte an hour at the synthetic
feed's size, but it has no end, and the code already warns that memory grows
until the store answers.
