* Result

The rule, in =projects/ores.seeder/datasets/acme_corporation/generate_party_data.py=:

- A party trades with three groups of counterparties. The banks it holds a
  master agreement with, read from =netting_agreements.json= as before, so the
  two datasets cannot disagree. Its sister entities. And the banks it approves,
  which is the new part.
- The approved banks are the GLEIF entities whose legal address is in one of
  the countries the party declares in =COUNTRIES=, and which the GLEIF BIC
  mapping names. The BIC mapping is the registry's own statement that the
  entity is one a payment can be addressed to, so the set is a bank set rather
  than one inferred from a name. Active =GENERAL= entities only: a retired
  entity cannot be traded with, and a fund or a branch is not the legal entity
  a master agreement names.
- The list is sorted by LEI. An LEI is derived from the entity itself and never
  reassigned, so the list is stable across an extract refresh.
- The consequence is that each party faces a different set, because each books
  in different countries: =ACCOUK= faces British, Irish, German and French
  banks, =ACCOUS= faces American and Canadian ones, and =ACCOHK= faces Hong
  Kong, Singapore and Japanese ones.
- The three hand-written =APPROVED= lists are deleted. Every bank they named
  that was not a bank of the party's own countries, =BARCLAYS PLC=,
  =LLOYDS BANKING GROUP PLC= and =NATWEST GROUP PLC= for the United Kingdom,
  is kept through its master agreement instead, so no link that existed before
  is lost.

Counts, after a =compass db recreate -y -k= and an ACME provision: 916 links
over 456 counterparties, =ACCOUK= 411, =ACMCOR= 453, =ACCOUS= 27, =ACCOHK= 25.
Before: 38 over 23.

Two findings that are data defects rather than under-population, and are not
fixed here:

- =2HI3YI5320L3RW6NJ957=, the Hong Kong and Shanghai Banking Corporation, is
  staged by =acme.acme_hk.party_counterparties= and has no counterparty row to
  join to. The publish drops it, which is why =ACCOHK= takes 25 links from 26
  staged rows. It was in the hand-written list before this change and is
  carried over, so the defect is older than the rule.
- The sixth sister entity, the group's LEI as seen from a subsidiary, is
  absent from the staged rows for the same reason. The seeder's own rule
  implies it and its own dataset cannot carry it.

One thing the environment hides, recorded because the next recreate will meet
it: =compass db recreate -y -k= leaves the fleet holding dead database
connections, and the provisioning run then stalls part-way with no error in
the workflow. =compass services restart= after the recreate clears it.
