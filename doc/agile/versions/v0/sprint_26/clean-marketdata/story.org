* Decisions
- **The identity is the oresmd URI.** =market_series.oresmd_uri= is not null,
  non-empty and unique with =party_id=, and the registry's three columns no
  longer key the row. A key the grammar cannot name is dropped and reported
  rather than stored with no identity.
- **A fixing's key is an index name, not the series key grammar.** The fixing
  key space has its own identity path, and the corpus's index names read back as
  they arrived.
- **The asset class lists are declared once.** The refdata product taxonomy and
  the oresmd namespace are two different lists that overlap; one codegen model
  declares both and generates the seed SQL, the DQ badges, the briefs and the
  docs, and validates the oresmd authorities against it.

* Result

Closed with the checklist at 28 items Pass, four Fail and one Partial. The
acceptance says every applicable item passes; the record does not, and names
each item that is not met with its disposition in
[[id:F9E8C6BE-9606-48E5-AEE3-431642C0EB28][the checklist task]]'s =* Result=.
Two are captured for follow-up (the six unmapped protocol headers, and the
vacuous permission check), one is deferred to backlog story =F15A8480=, and one
is a deliberate stop recorded rather than forced.

Three things this story delivered, and one thing it exposed:

- Every fixing index name has an oresmd identity.
- The asset class lists are declared once and generated.
- The oresmd URI is the series identity.
- Cutting the identity over to the URI exposed that the grammar cannot say what
  a datum is: a surface coordinate is one comma-joined string, and the model
  subtype is dropped from the series identity, so the corpus's lognormal and
  normal vol for one underlying collide. That is
  [[id:12DAAEC7-2E9D-4487-80E5-E690737071B3][Make the oresmd URI grammar explicit
  and typed]], opened at this close.
