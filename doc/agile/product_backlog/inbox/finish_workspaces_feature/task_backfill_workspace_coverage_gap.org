* Goal

Decide -- and record the decision -- on the ~20 hand-crafted trading
instrument sub-types (bond, every equity_* variant, every fx_*
variant, commodity, composite + composite_legs, credit, scripted,
swap_legs) that carry =workspace_id= in SQL but have no codegen model
at all: bring them under codegen (large, separate effort) or
explicitly accept them as permanently hand-crafted and move on.
Either way, verify their hand-written repository/service code
actually filters by =workspace_id= correctly today.

(=book=/=portfolio= were originally scoped into this task too, on the
mistaken belief they lacked =has_workspace_id=. They don't lack it --
both inherit it from the =fk-scoped-child= profile, and were
regenerated/verified with the inheritance-query fix in PR #1846. See
the audit task's correction note.)
