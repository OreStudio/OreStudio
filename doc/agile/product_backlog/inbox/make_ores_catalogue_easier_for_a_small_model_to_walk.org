This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Change what the catalogue shows a small model so it reaches the operation it
needs more often, each change measured with the request set:

- Name a screen route's entity as its REST route names it, so =session= and
  =sessions=, or the four =series_identity_shape= entities, are one entity.
- Hide, or mark, a =web_= read that duplicates a generated read, as
  =web_get_seed_profiles= duplicates =list_seed_profiles=.
- Reword the area purposes that misled: curve bootstrap configurations and
  index families were looked for in market-data.
- Make =list_areas= say that the next step is =list_entities=: in four runs the
  model answered after it instead of walking on.
- Have =find_operations= keep every word asked for: "tenant" ranked
  =list_tenants= above =list_tenant_types=.

* Why

LFM3-1.7B reached 10 of 20 operations by browsing, in 4.7 steps when it did,
and 13 of 20 by search, in 2.0 steps. Three of the misses chose a duplicate
read that also answers the request. The rest trace to the causes above.
