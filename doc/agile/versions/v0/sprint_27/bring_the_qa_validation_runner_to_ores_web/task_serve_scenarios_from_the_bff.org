* Acceptance

- A route lists the scenarios, a route reads one with its steps and the ids of its story and task, a route reads a doc by id, and a route saves a run.
- The routes require a signed-in session and refuse a request with no session.
- A save that names a step the scenario does not have is refused with a clear message.
- BFF tests cover the routes with the store replaced by an in-memory one.
