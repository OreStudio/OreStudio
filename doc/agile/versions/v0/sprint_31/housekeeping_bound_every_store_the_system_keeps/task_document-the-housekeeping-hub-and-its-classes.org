* Result

The target state is written as a cluster of three pages under the architecture
section of the knowledge graph, plus the refactor of the pages that already
owned parts of the subject.

- The hub is [[id:C45999F0-2B86-48D7-814F-F10727BC9565][Housekeeping]], a
  structure note. It argues the reading order, says what the cluster excludes
  and which neighbouring cluster owns it, and holds two pages in an =# aside=
  section because they bear on the cluster without belonging to its sequence.
- The model is
  [[id:E92D8CA4-C33F-4AE2-808B-9092B100E9C5][Housekeeping of retained data]]:
  a bound requires a declaration, an executor and a measurement together, with
  the shape of the policy record, four removing actions and two transforms, one
  executor on the scheduler's configured cadence, the fallback where the engine
  runs no job, and the telemetry each policy reports.
- The classification is
  [[id:21D539CD-AF3B-4479-81D5-F195756C064F][Classification of retained data]]:
  eleven classes of retained data with the mechanism each takes, and the reason
  one mechanism does not serve.

The refactor touches four pages that already held parts of the subject.
[[id:F1BC2C10-B23B-4979-9692-8FA7C29319CB][Data storage]] gains the
boundary between what a store is and how long it keeps what it holds.
[[id:A79C25FA-9F70-453B-A52E-780B4402ABAA][Bounded retention in the tick
store]] loses a false statement — it claimed the instance runs the licensed
edition, which the measurement contradicts — and now states the licence as a
requirement and defers the fallback to the model.
[[id:286B7167-9B8A-4937-BBA5-E9D285AFECE3][Object Storage]] and
[[id:F37D960D-9687-47DC-8737-605CA38D0CF2][Storage policy]] both point at the
classification for the lifecycle of their keys.

Every page links both ways, and the gate's link lint confirms it: the first run
failed with two =lint/missing_backlink= findings for the aside pages, which is
the check that the aside section is read as cluster membership.
