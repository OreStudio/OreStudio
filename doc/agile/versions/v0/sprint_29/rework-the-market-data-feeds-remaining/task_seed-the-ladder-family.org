* Plan

The product owner overruled the design's decision to hold the ladder family back,
so the design's =* Reports to build= list is the seed list. Rows 1 to 10 are the
family; rows 11 to 14 wait on swaption, cap and floor, credit and equity
volatility data and stay stated and unseeded.

- the catalogue gains the ten reports through =report_catalogue.json= and the two
  generators, so the artefact populates are regenerated rather than hand written.
  A row the design marks =eod and intraday= is two catalogue entries, because a
  definition states one cut selection. That is fifteen definitions per party.
- the report names are the ones the ladder pages already carry, because the index
  lists a report by its page. The design's rates delta ladder is the Rho ladder and
  its currency pair spot ladder is the Delta ladder, which is the family's general
  first-order reader.
- every ladder runs =npv= and =sensitivity=, except the par-rate DV01 ladder, which
  reads the par column and asks for no valuation. The two analytics already have
  profiles, so a ladder binds the run settings the sensitivity reports already
  bind: the same rows, which name =sensitivity.xml= and =simulation.xml=.
- the engines are =Risk Sensitivities=, the same set the delta, gamma and DV01
  reports use, because a bump wants the prices that carry analytic sensitivities.
  A close twin takes the default official engines, as the pricing table says.
- the definition binds the six configuration types through the existing artefact
  pattern, so nothing about the binding mechanism is new.
- the ladder family is decided before the party rules. A definition that produces
  no figure costs nothing to hold, and the catalogue should state what the platform
  runs. Every party therefore holds the fifteen, the holding company included, and
  Northwind holds them through the London rows its catalogue reads.
- the environment is shared and already provisioned, so the seeds reach it by being
  applied: the artefact populates are staged again and the publish carries them onto
  the rows the parties hold. =evidence/apply_the_ladder_family.sql= does that and
  states the two secrets of the order: run settings before definitions, and the
  Northwind rows staged and not published, because this environment holds no
  Northwind tenant.

* Notes

** The cycle each row runs on

The design's cycle column is the whole rule. Five rows are =eod and intraday=: the
rates delta, the ATM and surface vega ladders, and the two derived volatility
ladders. Each of those is a live definition that runs every half hour through the
day on the =LIVE= cut and an "(EOD)" twin that runs at the close on the =EOD= cut.
The other five are end of day alone. A sensitivity run reprices the whole scope, so
the live definitions run on the half hour like the intraday risk monitor rather than
every fifteen minutes like the triple vol spot ladder.

** The two ladders the design's list does not seed

The Volga and the Vanna are not in rows 1 to 10 and no definition states them. The
Three-pillar is the surface shift under its other name, so the surface vega ladder
is the report and the page says so. Nothing about those three is a defect: the
design's list does not reach them, and the design is what the seed follows.

** Why the holding company holds them

The ladder family is decided before the party rules that say the holding company
runs no trading analytics (D2) and that only London runs the option reports (D8).
That is the product owner's call on 2026-11-04 and the pages state it, in
[[file:../../../knowledge/reports/reports.org][Report pages]], under "Which entity
runs which report". A reviewer who disagrees can remove the holding company and the
two core entities from =LADDER_REPORTS= in =generate_report_data.py= and regenerate;
nothing else would change.

** What a run stops on

No definition was run for this task. Each ladder binds run settings that name
=sensitivity.xml=, and no table holds a =SensitivityAnalysis= document and no mapper
reads one, so the engine has nothing to shift. The dataset also holds no
=FX_OPTION/RATE_LNVOL= quote and no FX spot, so a volatility ladder has no factor to
bump even with the document in place. The acceptance put this out of scope: a
definition exists and fails for the same reasons the rest do.

** The new definitions hold no scheduler job

The provisioner's =schedule_reports= step creates a job and a run grant for each
published definition whose schedule is a real cron expression. It runs while a
tenant is provisioned, and these fifteen definitions reached a tenant that was
already up, so they are published and unscheduled. The schedule column still states
when each one would fire, and a re-provision asks for the jobs. A report a reader
opens from its page is unaffected: the schedule action is a separate feature.

** An environment that carries an older catalogue

The ACME parties in this environment also hold five, three and three definitions
named "Bump and Reprice Spot Greeks", "Bump and Reprice Spot Ladder" and "Bump and
Reprice Triple Vol Spot Ladder", published on 2026-11-03 from a catalogue that used
the Northwind names. They are current rows with scheduler jobs, they are not this
task's, and the publish left them alone. The catalogue on this branch names those
reports "Spot Greeks", "Spot Ladder" and "Triple Vol Spot Ladder", so the publish
inserted those names beside them. A re-provision returns the parties to one name
each.

because five of the rows run on both cycles and each cycle is a definition:

Each definition binds the six configuration types: =conventions=,
=todays_market=, =curve_configuration=, =pricing_engines= — =Risk Sensitivities=
for the plain name and the official engines for a close twin — =risk_report_config=
and =engine_settings=. The run settings are the party's existing sensitivity rows,
so the =sensitivity= analytic carries =recalibrateModels= =Y=,
=outputSensitivityThreshold= =0.000001=, =parSensitivity= =Y=,
=sensitivityConfigFile= = sensitivity.xml and =marketConfigFile= = simulation.xml.
The definitions run through the existing publish, so no table had to be invented
for the shift: the size is still a setting no table holds, and the pages say so.

Rows 11 to 14 are stated and not seeded, because they wait on swaption, cap and
floor, credit and equity volatility data.

*What the run stops on.* Nothing was run. No table holds a =SensitivityAnalysis=
document and no mapper reads one, no table states a shift size, the dataset holds
no volatility quote and no FX spot, and the report inputs story is open. That is
the same list that stops "Delta and Gamma" and every other sensitivity report, so
the ladder fails like the rest rather than in a way of its own.

*Docs.* The index's ladder table states the definitions and their cuts, "what is not
built" names the four gaps in one place, the pages of the five dual-cycle ladders
state both cycles, and the design page records the overrule and the name each row is
seeded as.

reporting pgTAP suites pass (62 tests) and the two sensitivity analytics suites pass

=org-links=, =heading-numbers=, =structure-notes= and =pattern-uses=. The SQL and
