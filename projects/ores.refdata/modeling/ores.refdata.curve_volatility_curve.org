#+brief: How a Curve volatility kind interpolates and extrapolates its quotes.
#+created: 2026-10-30
#+updated: 2026-10-30

ORE's =volatilityCurveConfig= states its quotes in a =Quotes= list, and states how
those quotes are interpolated and extrapolated. The quotes are rows of
=curve_quote= on the entry, because a quote is a market data row and not a
property of the kind. What is the kind's own is the shape it reads them in:
those three columns are this part, and a row of it exists only when the entry
holds a kind of this type.
