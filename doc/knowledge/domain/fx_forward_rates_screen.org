#+title: A forward ladder shows each pair's tenors as outrights and points

* Summary

A forward ladder shows the tenors of one pair. For each tenor it states the outright and the forward points.

* Detail

- Standard tenors run from the short dates through 1W to the long end.
- Broken dates fall between standard tenors. Straight interpolation is a poor estimate around turns, such as year end, where adjusted forwards differed by 1 to 15 pips in LSEG's study.
- A forward-forward calculator states a rate between two chosen dates without changing the curve.
