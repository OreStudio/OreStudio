* Goal

One page states the model the story builds: the phases a run passes through, what
each phase can refuse and how it says so, the three grains of a run's market
readiness, the phase every error carries, and the two approval matrices that
decide what a party and a book may trade.

The page is the reference the later tasks read, so a builder of the readiness
record and a builder of the error phase do not have to re-derive either.

* Acceptance

- The four phases are named, with what each refuses and the vocabulary it refuses in.
- Each grain of the readiness record states what it answers and who reads it.
- The phase axis is stated beside the engine's scope, with the reason the scope stays the engine's.
- The two matrices are stated with the subset rule, and the population rule that follows from them.
- The page states what the story does not build, so a later reader does not take the simulator's expected set for the platform's rule.
