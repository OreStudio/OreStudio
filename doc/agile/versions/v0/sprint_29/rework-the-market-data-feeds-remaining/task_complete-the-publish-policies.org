* Result

The three publish policies are built, verified and merged (PR 4020).

- A kind whose approvals_required is zero closes its request approved in the
  same act, decided by the service account and never the asker, with the comment
  naming the kind and the count; a one-approval kind is unchanged.
- approval_kind carries raise_permission_code beside decide_permission_code,
  nullable so every earlier kind keeps its rule; the two market data kinds name
  marketdata::curve_bootstrap:publish.
- marketdata.curve_published and marketdata.curve_withdrawn are seeded; the
  publish notice is raised once, after the record's transaction, and the
  withdrawal's by the withdrawal, with the subject and act and no values.
- The series read accepts the ids the store writes, the blueprint and tenor
  reads are granted per tenant, and the curve blueprint messages exist.

Evidence: inbox and marketdata zero-path and maker/checker cases, the notices'
cases, ctest 10/10, BFF 1128, web 2318, contracts 24, refdata suites, shell
refdata 2809, lint clean, 39 docs components; live raise as henry.jones, decide
as mark.reid, apply, a replacement cycle, the zero kind, one notice of each
kind with 24 recipients.

Open: no seed ships a zero kind and no screen offers the path; the consuming
services cannot be notice recipients and read the change event; mail is not
expressible per kind; the queue row carries no subject.
