* Decisions

- *The flags are wrong, not the tables.* =entry_channel_type= and its two
  siblings are tenant-less by design, and =structure_member= has no party
  column by design. The policy flags, not the entities, are the mistake.
- *Refuse the shape in the generator.* Both rules go into
  =validate_rls_isolation=, which already refuses three other combinations on
  the same principle. The database is the wrong place to find out.
- *Do not reopen the gap.* =structure_member= keeps the restrictive party
  policy the hand-written =trading_rls_policies_create.sql= gives it; only the
  generated duplicate that cannot load is gone.

* Out of scope

- Why the flags and the columns could drift apart. A gate over the generated
  policies rather than the model would catch any missing column, not just the
  three a policy names; the two rules here are the ones with a proven failure.
