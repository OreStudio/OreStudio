* Goal

A rate a firm does not observe, it triangulates: the driver pairs are the ones its vendors publish, the pivot is the pivot its publisher quotes against, and the choice of CRM is the firm's own. So triangulation runs at the party, on demand, and is never stored as if a publisher had observed it. This story gives the CRM the data it needs to do that over history rather than one instant, and takes the derived volatility source off its hardcoded publisher, legs and currencies.

* Acceptance

- A CRM topology is built from series identity and provider precedence, and no driver pair is hand-written.
- A publisher's pivot is a root vertex, so a pair whose leg is the pivot composes without a series.
- The rate engine serves a pair's daily levels over a date range, from a publisher's fixings, and the streaming path is unchanged.
- Each party derives its own volatility quotes; the derived source names no currency, publisher or transform.
- When no provider can compose a pair the refusal names the provider and the date its fixing stopped.
