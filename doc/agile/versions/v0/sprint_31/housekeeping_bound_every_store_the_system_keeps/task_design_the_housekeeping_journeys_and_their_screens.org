* Result
Housekeeping had no journey and no prototype. It now has both, and both say
what the server does not provide.

- Three journeys in the Operations group: *See what the system keeps*, *Change
  what is kept, and for how long*, and *Read the housekeeping log*. Each states
  its actor, its steps, the fields it shows, every operation with its subject
  and whether the server provides it, and what is missing.
- Three wireframes, one per journey, drawn in PlantUML salt and rendered beside
  them.
- One static prototype under =doc/prototypes/operations/housekeeping/= arguing
  three shapes — the table of every store, cards grouped by where a store
  lives, and the list beside the policy editor — and five states: fresh,
  skipped, failed, disabled, and no policy at all. The runs log is part of the
  same screen, because the person who asks what is kept asks what was done
  about it in the same sitting.

The findings that matter more than the drawings: no size-of-target read exists,
no wire type reads the runs ledger until PR #5126, and the sweep performs one of
the three actions the policy model states, so a policy can read as bounded while
nothing bounds it. The prototype draws that last state as a first-class warning
rather than an error, because it is a decision the platform made and not a
fault of the store.

The three journeys are the design; the prototype is the argument. Nothing is
accepted yet: the screen cannot be built until the size read and the runs read
exist, and both are named in the journeys' * What is missing.
