#+brief: The calibration of a party's FX volatility surfaces.
#+filetags: :model:operation:marketdata:
#+created: 2026-11-11
#+updated: 2026-11-11

The calibration behind the calibrate journey. A caller names a blueprint of its
party and an as-of. The service checks the recipe before it reads a quote, reads
the quotes the pillars name and the party's published forward set, fits the
smile model and writes the candidate: the grid as observations of the output
series with lineage, and the checks as a calibration record. The reply carries
what the record holds, so a caller reads the fit and its errors at once. A
refusal is a reply with =success= false and a message that names what failed.
