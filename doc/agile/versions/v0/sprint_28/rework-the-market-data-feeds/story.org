* Goal

ores.marketdata owns the catalogue, the mapping, the ingest path, routing and metering. Adapters, including the simulator, publish vendor-id ticks over NATS and answer a demand set. Every simulated feed belongs to the system tenant and the system party, a party takes a provider by subscription, and no part of the system behaves differently because a source is simulated.

* Acceptance

- The catalogue holds providers, sources with a kind, vendor instruments and mappings, installed by a system bundle.
- An adapter announces itself, answers a demand set, publishes vendor-id ticks and sends a heartbeat, and the service maps, checks, stores and delivers them.
- A party holds a subscription with rights and one route per provider, and a source switch is audited and effective-dated.
- The simulator is an adapter under ores.marketdata.synthetic, its generators belong to the system tenant and system party, and no subject, table or constraint carries a mark of the simulator.
- Usage events, rate cards, rating and allocation give each party a bill, and a simulated source gives a shadow bill.
- The screens and the shell follow the architecture pages, and a scenario shows a development party on a simulated source and a prelive party on a live source side by side.

* Decisions

- Market data has one owner, =ores.marketdata=. Adapters, including the simulator, are separate NATS services that meet it at four messages: announce, demand, tick and heartbeat. Marco decided this on 2026-10-22.
- The mapping from a vendor instrument to an internal item is on the provider, not the source, so a live and a simulated source of one provider share it. Decided on 2026-10-22.
- A series is keyed by item, provider and party. The source is a route and an observation records it for information. Decided on 2026-10-22.
- A party has one active source for each provider. A source switch is audited and effective-dated. Decided on 2026-10-22.
- Demand drives the adapters, ticks travel on core NATS, and a system bundle installs the catalogue and the simulator universe. Decided on 2026-10-22.
- Vendor ids follow the vendor's notation. An id the system must invent carries the minted flag and a deviation note. Decided on 2026-10-22.
- Composite licensing rights are modelled now: display, non_display and derived on the subscription. Decided on 2026-10-22.
- The cost model records usage as events and prices it later with a rate card on the source. The platform pays the vendors and allocates the cost to parties, splitting a fixed fee equally among active subscribers. A simulated source has a simulated rate card and gives a shadow bill. Decided on 2026-10-22.
- The simulator stops at the adapter seam. It imitates vendor data and failure behaviour, and it does not imitate vendor protocols. Decided on 2026-10-22.
- ores.synthetic splits into ores.marketdata.synthetic and ores.trading.synthetic. Decided on 2026-10-22.
- The story Subscribe tenants to provider feeds and prove the browser at scale describes feed bindings and overlaps this story. It needs a decision from Marco.
