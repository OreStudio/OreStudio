* Goal

The calibration refuses a surface the moment one expiry lacks the pillars SSVI needs, so a pair with eleven quoted expiries and two silent ones publishes nothing. Fit what is quoted, name what is not in missing_pillars, and let the published surface declare the horizon it actually has through its output expiries.

* Acceptance

- A surface with some expiries unquoted calibrates and publishes the rest, naming the gaps in missing_pillars.
- A surface below the configured minimum of expiries is refused, naming how many it had.
- The published surface's output series carries only the expiries it fitted.

* Plan

- *Where the surface was voided.* =prepare_calibration= refused the whole surface as soon as one
  expiry was short of the pillars SSVI needs, unquoted pillars included. A pair whose sample did
  not reach 10Y had no surface at all.
- *Fit what has quotes.* An expiry short of its pillars is now left out of the fit and named in
  =unfitted_expiries=, with why; its pillars stay in =missing=, which the calibration record
  writes as =missing_pillars=.
- *Refuse below a minimum.* A surface that fits fewer than =minimum_expiries= is refused, naming
  how many it fitted and why the others were left out. The minimum is a named setting of the
  calibration service, three, beside =pillar_tolerance=; "Give each party the configuration its
  surface is built with" moves both into the party's configuration.
- *The output carries only what was fitted.* The grid leaves out an unfitted expiry and any
  expiry past the longest fitted one, so a surface never quotes past its data.

* Result

A surface fits the expiries that have their pillars and names the rest: an expiry short of what
SSVI needs is left out and listed in =unfitted_expiries=, its pillars in =missing_pillars=, and
the output grid stops at the longest expiry the fit covers. A surface is refused only below the
minimum of three fitted expiries, naming how many it had and why the others went. With the
reach task, a pair whose sample stops short of 10Y can now have a surface out to where it stops.
