#+title: ores.refdata.convention
#+description: Codegen entity model for the refdata =convention= table. The central row every convention kind shares: its key, the party that owns it, the kind it is, and the ORE document id it came from.
#+type: ores.codegen.entity
#+component: refdata
#+filetags: :model:entity:refdata:
#+entity_singular: convention
#+entity_plural: conventions
#+entity_title: Convention
#+brief: The central row every convention kind shares.
#+created: 2026-10-14
#+updated: 2026-10-14

The central row of the atomised convention. Every convention kind -- a swap, a
deposit, a tenor basis swap -- is one row here plus the parts its family names.
The row carries the key, the party that owns the row, the kind the row is, and
the ORE document id it was read from.

The ORE id is the identifier ORE itself gives the convention, the =<Id>=
element of the document. It is a plain stored field: it is kept so a round trip
through the ORE XML is lossless, and it carries no uniqueness condition of its
own. Two kinds may hold the same ORE id; the tenant, the party and the kind
together are what make a convention the one a caller means.

A convention's kind-specific fields live in that kind's parts, reached from
this row by its key. The central row carries no kind-specific column.

** ores_id
:PROPERTIES:
:type:     text
:cpp_type: std::optional<std::string>
:nullable: true
:END:

The ORE document's own id for this convention, its =<Id>= element. Kept so a
round trip through the ORE XML is lossless.

A plain stored field: it carries no natural-key role, no uniqueness and no
extra condition.

#+begin_src cpp :name generator
std::nullopt
#+end_src

** oresmd_uri
:PROPERTIES:
:type:     text
:cpp_type: std::optional<std::string>
:nullable: true
:END:

The oresmd URI of the market data this convention needs, when the kind names
one index. The column holds the address as a value, so refdata depends on the
oresmd format as a contract only and never on the marketdata library or its
tables.

Nullable, because no convention is required to state its address yet.

std::nullopt
#+end_src
