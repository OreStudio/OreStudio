* Notes

Raised by the generator task's runtime PR: the surface must move with spot by a configured correlation, and reaching into the spot feed for its shock was rejected. The investigation is [[id:8D5466B2-74B5-4C0D-B6DC-528CD466A47F][How the synthetic market is generated jointly]]; it recommends shared Brownian factors in the market's simulated time, and leaves four decisions to the owner.

The owner answered with a technical report that formalises Path F. Its content
went into the knowledge base as atomic notes, not as a copy of the report:
[[id:25C467BE-07A3-432C-8CB8-6B194E748570][Shared Brownian factors give every synthetic series a signed correlation]],
[[id:14C79EA2-4E36-4EF2-A0DB-0EB932655029][A keyed Brownian path is a pure function of seed and time]],
[[id:9B577C48-A237-445B-A331-AD0CB42FAAE1][Counter-based random number generators]] and
[[id:92644AAC-BE5F-43B6-96E4-084226CDA094][Tick-to-tick returns understate a correlation that holds exactly]].
Reading the report against the code corrected nine statements before they
reached the notes: the bridge resolution at depth 16 (about 55 ms, not
microseconds), the driver as a stochastic integral when loadings change with
the regime, the correlation of increments over unequal intervals (the Epps
effect), the condition that tick times be independent of the noise, business
time in the standardisation, the heat's bound on R4, who holds static
arbitrage (the generator, not ORE), the non-cryptographic status of Philox and
Threefry, and the cost ($O(KD)$ a tick). The approach note's OD-1 is resolved.

* Result

[[id:8D5466B2-74B5-4C0D-B6DC-528CD466A47F][How the synthetic market is generated jointly]] recommends shared Brownian factors in the market's simulated time: low rank and hierarchical, read by each series as increments of its own driver, exact to the bridge's resolution under any sampling. The owner chose it, with the report's answers to the four decisions: Path F;
block factors first and global factors in Phase 5; static loadings first,
regime loadings later; the surface's forward from the published spot and
curves. The build is
[[id:9331995E-79ED-40B3-923D-524975949FB5][Build the latent market of shared Brownian factors]].
