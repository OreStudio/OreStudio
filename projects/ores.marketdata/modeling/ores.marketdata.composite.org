A composite is an internal item that the service derives from several quote series by a rule. A blended
EUR/USD spot from Bloomberg and LSEG is the first case. The composite is published by an internal provider with
one source of kind =derived=. It has no rate card: its use counts as demand on its inputs.

A party that uses a composite needs the =derived= right on the provider of every input. A composite with a
missing right gets no values and shows the reason. The rule is named by a code, so a later rule needs no schema
change. The system tenant owns the rows and every tenant reads them.
