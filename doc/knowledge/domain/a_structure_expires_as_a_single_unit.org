#+title: A structure expires as a single unit
#+description: Each product has a default expiry action; a structure is expired and netted as one, by FX or by cash.
#+type: knowledge
#+audience: user
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:expiry:option:structure:
#+created: 2026-11-22
#+updated: 2026-11-22

* Summary

Each product has a default expiry action: a deliverable vanilla or barrier is
exercised into an FX trade, a non-deliverable forward is cash settled in its
deliverable currency, a digital is cash settled in its rebate currency. A
structure is expired as one: if any of its trades needs expiry management, the
whole structure does, and its results are netted, as one FX trade or as one
cash flow, before they reach the ledger.

* Detail

** Defaults by product

| Product | Default action at expiry |
|---------+--------------------------|
| Non-deliverable forward | Cash settled in the deliverable currency |
| Deliverable vanilla or barrier, outside a structure | Exercised into an FX trade |
| Digital and other binary barriers | Cash settled in the rebate currency |
| Dual currency deposit | Exercised as a digital |
| Generic structure | Cash settled if it holds digitals that share a rebate currency; otherwise exercised into FX |

The default is a starting point that the holder's decision can override; see
[[id:970D334D-2576-41BF-8664-1AA4CB76D31B][An option near the money at its cut makes expiry a matter of judgement]].

** Which process handles which product

A product is handled by expiry management, by fixing management, or by barrier
monitoring, according to whether its outcome waits for the holder or follows
from a fixing. The table is in
[[id:0CBDBE85-17DF-4A77-A231-FFB2101FC0CC][A fixing automates the decision an expiry leaves to the holder]].
Barrier monitoring and expiry management can share their logic, but they serve
different decisions and show different fields, so traders use them as two
views.

** Structures expire as one

The trades of a structure should share one determination style, either
holder-determined or fixing-determined, so that the structure is netted in one
place. If some trades of a structure could be fixed and others need expiry
management, the whole structure goes through expiry management.

Netting has three styles: none; /as FX/, where the results become one FX trade,
for example a rebate netted into the exercise trade at a rate different from
the strike; and /as cash/, one payment per currency. The ledger receives the
netted structure. If each component reached the ledger on its own, assets and
liabilities would both be inflated. When netting leaves no FX at all, no trade
or confirmation is produced, but the outcome is still reported to the client.
The composition of structures is in
[[id:FC31FC9C-7A6E-4EFD-8A62-6DE4137F7160][Trade Structures and Deal Composition]].

** Write-offs

A very short-dated, deep out-of-the-money vanilla can be sold back to back into
a separate write-off book for a very small premium, to remove its gamma and
time decay before expiry. Expiry management shows the write-offs done as part
of an expiry.
