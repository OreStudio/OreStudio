* Result

Delivered as [[id:259EA767-EDF7-45EF-BE4D-B1A8FA96F7BD][Report execution
modes]]. It states the stage list with an owner for each, the storage-URI seam
that makes the two modes interchangeable, the rule that step results are
addressed by role rather than index, the full NATS contract hop by hop, and
two PlantUML interaction diagrams whose rendered images were read.

The design's most useful output is not the diagram but the distance it
measured, because that is what changes the plan:

- The common tail already exists and works when the shell drives it, and does
  not exist for the workflow, for three reasons recorded in the design and
  owned by the ACME smoke-test story.
- The ORE conversion is much less complete than "we have an XML layer"
  suggests. The bindings cover the whole ORE vocabulary, but the ORE Studio to
  binding mappers cover four document kinds — portfolio, currency config,
  calendar adjustments and conventions — and none of the four configuration
  documents the engine needs.
- =prepare_ore_package= ships =trades.msgpack= and =market_data.msgpack=,
  where the wrapper's contract is =Input/ore.xml= plus six named files, so the
  step's name and the workflow's own description run ahead of its code.
- Nothing reads the engine's output at all, and gathering is a blanket tenant
  dump rather than a resolution of what the selected trades need.

Three questions are left open on purpose rather than settled by assertion:
where the export's inputs live between stages, whether the demo input binding
is a stage or an absence, and what a run should do when no grid is present.
