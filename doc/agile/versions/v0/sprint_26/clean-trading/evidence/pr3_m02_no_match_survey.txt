Where a no-match is recorded: there is no in-model convention. No entity
model in the tree carries :profile: none or a no-match marker, and the
loader does not read the names the spec's RecordNoMatch rule uses
(classification, no_match_reason), so writing them would be silently
inert. The record is prose in the task or story, which is what the
completed components did: clean-compute/task_clean_compute.org records
"M02, the workflow batch link has no profile" and clean-iam's task
records its account and permission entities "as genuine no-matches with
their feature values". The machine-readable list in
check_model_drift.py is for a binding that contradicts its profile, which
is a different case.

The rest of the component: 31 models bind a profile today -- 26 trading-instrument,
3 simple-lookup, 1 workspace-scoped-lookup and 1 trading-record. The six field-group
models, the module and the component overview are not domain_entity models and are
excluded from the survey.
