* Goal

The work a request may do is bounded by what it is asked about, not by the size of the catalogue. A calibration reads only its own blueprint's pillars and sources, the largest calibration the service accepts is stated, and a request that cannot finish inside its deadline answers with a reason rather than running on.

* Acceptance

- A calibration reads only the pillars and pillar sources of its own blueprint.
- The service states the largest calibration it accepts, and refuses a larger one with a reason.
- A request that cannot finish inside its deadline answers with a reason, or abandons its work.
- The cost of one calibration does not grow with the number of blueprints in the tenant.
