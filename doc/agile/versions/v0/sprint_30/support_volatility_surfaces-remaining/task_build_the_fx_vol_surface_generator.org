* Goal

Synthetic FX volatility quotes move through the day with spot. A generator of kind =fx_vol_surface= holds an SSVI state. Its forward variances follow CIR processes, its skew a tanh of an OU process and its curvature a bounded logistic of an OU process, with shocks correlated with spot. Contributors publish noisy observations of that state into the series the pillars name, and the clean contributor is the truth. See [[id:D4C7C4A7-CD2D-4307-AB06-DA9BE3C3A334][A synthetic contributor publishes noisy observations of a latent surface]].

* Acceptance

- Every surface the generator emits passes the SSVI no-arbitrage conditions.
- A generator feed publishes quotes in the pair's own convention into series a blueprint's pillars can name.
- Each contributor's quotes differ from the clean contributor's by its noise profile.
- The correlation of surface moves with spot moves matches the configured value within a tolerance the test states.
- A blueprint that reads contributor quotes calibrates through the same operation as one that reads derived quotes.

* Plan

Three PRs, each ending in a check.

1. *The engine, in =ores.analytics.quant=.* =fx_vol_surface_generator= starts from a fitted SSVI surface and steps its state: a forward variance per expiry segment, each a Cox-Ingersoll-Ross process with a floor, and an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck driver each for the skew and the curvature, every process reverting to the start. The shocks are joined to spot's by the lower Cholesky factor of one correlation matrix with spot first, so the spot shock a step reads is the one the spot feed drew. =fx_vol_surface_quotes= reads ATM, risk reversals and butterflies from a surface in each expiry's convention, through =read_smile=, as the calibration does. =observe_fx_vol_point= turns a clean point into a contributor's observation under a noise profile; the profile with every field zero is the clean contributor.
2. *The feed.* Codegen entities for the =fx_vol_surface= feed configuration and its child rows (template points, process parameters, correlations, contributors), and the feed built and registered under the kind =fx_vol_surface=, with its permission and grant.
3. *The catalogue and the routing.* A provider for each contributor, vendor instruments and mappings in the system bundle, and a live check that a blueprint reading contributor quotes calibrates through the same operation as one reading derived quotes.
