* Steps

** Log in as the tenant administrator

- Open =http://localhost:21002=.
- Log in as =tenant_admin@acme_corporation= with password =Secure-Password-123=.
- Choose the party *ACME Corporation HK Ltd*.
- The reporting area opens. If it does not, the login or the party failed; stop here.

** Open a product pricing configuration

- Open Reporting, then Product pricing.
- The list shows the configurations the HK party holds.
- Open the one named *XVA Overnight*.
- The page title is the configuration name.

** Check the five tabs

- The page shows these tabs, in this order: Details, Global parameters, Products, Reports, History.
- The first tab is open.
- Reload the page with a tab chosen; the same tab is open after the reload.

** Read the details

- The Details tab shows the name, the party, the variant, the person who changed it and when.
- The description reads.
- The header offers Edit and the page offers no field to type in.
- Clicking Edit opens a dialog that asks for a change reason.

** Read the product tree

- Open the Products tab.
- The left side lists the configuration's products under their asset class.
- The products of one asset class sit together.
- Selecting another product changes the workbench on the right.

** Read the workbench

- The workbench states the product, its asset class and its instrument.
- It then states what a model is, names the model in force, and gives the model's description.
- It lists the model's parameters; each has its value and a Documentation disclosure.
- It then does the same for the engine: what an engine is, the engine in force, its description, then its parameters.
- Opening a Documentation disclosure shows the parameter's help, its format and whether it is required.

** Edit a product parameter

- Click Edit on the workbench.
- Change the value of one parameter, give a change reason, and save.
- Reopen the product: the new value is in force.
- The History tab gains a version.

** Read the global parameters

- Open the Global parameters tab.
- The tab states that these parameters apply to the whole document.
- It lists the global parameters with their values.
- Each row has a Documentation disclosure.

** Read the reach

- Open the Reports tab.
- The tab states that a report needs a price for every product type in its portfolio.
- It lists the reports that bind the configuration; open one and its page opens.

** Read the history

- Open the History tab.
- The timeline lists the versions, newest first, each with who changed it and why.
- The save made in the edit step is in the list.
