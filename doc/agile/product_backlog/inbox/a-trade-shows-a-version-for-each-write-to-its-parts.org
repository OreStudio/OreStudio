This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

A trade's timeline shows one entry for every write to one of its parts. The trade
row is immutable and written once, so those entries are version bumps the store
makes to partition the trade's windows: their comment says which child bumped
them, and their only changed field is the economic digest, a hash folded from the
parts.

Draw the trade's story from what a person can act on: the booking, the
identifiers, the party roles, the state, the membership, each named. Either leave
the digest out of the fields a history compares, or leave the trade's own bumped
versions out of the stream and state that the trade is built from its parts.

* Why

The History tab of a trade reads as a list of bumps, each saying *Economic
Digest* changed, beside the part entry that says what actually happened. The
digest is derived, so the bump is not a fact about the trade a reader needs. The
standard's rule for a composite record, ES-HIST-24, leaves out versions that
changed none of the fields the record carries; the digest makes those versions
look like changes.
