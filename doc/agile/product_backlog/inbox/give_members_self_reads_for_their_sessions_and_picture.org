#+title: Give members self reads for their sessions and picture
#+description: A member's own sessions and picture are read through privileged reads, iam.v1.sessions.active and iam.v1.accounts.get, so both answer 403 to a member; each needs a self read on the allow-list, and the shell should report a refusal rather than a decode failure.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Two of a member's own screens read through privileged reads, so a member is
refused:

| Route | Subject it reads | Needs |
|-------+------------------+-------|
| =GET /api/me/sessions= | =iam.v1.sessions.active= | =iam::sessions:read=, and answers every open session in the tenant |
| =GET /api/accounts/<own username>/picture= | =iam.v1.accounts.get= | =iam::accounts:read= |

Each needs a self read that takes no account id, as
=iam.v1.account_contact_informations.mine= does for the contact record: the
member's own open sessions, and the member's own account or picture. Each goes
on the allow-list of Authorised reads, and the BFF's =me= routes move onto
them.

The shell reports a refusal badly. A refused read answers with an empty body
and =X-Error: forbidden=, and the shell prints "Failed to parse response:
Failed to unpack msgpack data." and aborts the script. It should read the
header and say the read was refused, naming nothing it was not told.

* Why

Found on 2026-10-06 while proving the guard-every-read story through the BFF as
=ashley.moore= (Member and Viewer): 51 of the 53 member routes answered 200, and
these two answered 403. Both reads were guarded before that story, so this is
not a regression, but "Protect my account" and the header picture do not work
for a member.
