* What

The feed graph shows =synthetic.latent.fx.fx_vol_surface.usdzar= failing with
=no market: curve:synthetic.bloomberg.ir.yield_curve.zaronia.zar=, and its four
vendor views starved on it, while the ZAR curve's node reads as running. Seen
live on 2026-11-23 after a provision of Acme. A curve writes the run's board
only while an adapter runs it, so a curve no tenant demands leaves a surface
that names it without its discounts; the graph's running state for an
upstream is the rule's, not a run's. Settle whether a curve a surface names
should run whenever the surface does, and whether an upstream's state should
say that nothing runs it.

* Why

A vendor's vol quotes of USD/ZAR stop for a reason no operator chose, and the
graph says the curve runs.
