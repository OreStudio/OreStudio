* Acceptance

- A tick of a feed that no route names is published once on the feed subject, after the age gate.
- It is not stored, not metered and not counted in any party's stats.
- A test shows a tick of an unrouted feed on its feed subject and no row in the tick table.

* Plan

1. Model a source as =source_feed=: the catalogue feed's provider and id, and
   the consumers its routes make. =refresh()= makes one for every feed of the
   catalogue, so a feed with no route is a source with no consumers.
2. =plan_tick= drops a tick only when the catalogue names no feed for its source
   (=tick_drop::unknown_source=). A tick of an unrouted feed gets a delivery with
   the feed subject and no targets, so it makes no item and no row.
3. =announce()= publishes each planned tick that has no target once on its feed
   subject, after the age gate. It is not stored, metered or counted.
4. Test the plan and the loop: the tick arrives once on its feed, makes no
   series and leaves no row in the tick table.

* Result

- Every catalogue feed is a source of the ingest (=source_feed=). A tick of a
  feed that no route names is published once on its feed subject, after the age
  gate, and is not stored, metered or counted. Only a tick from a source the
  catalogue does not name is dropped (=tick_drop::unknown_source=).
- Tests: the plan of a tick of an unrouted feed has the feed subject and no
  target; the loop publishes such a tick once on its feed, makes no series and
  writes no row. The market data service suite passes (316 cases), and the ticks
  smoke test passes.
- On the demonstration fleet the only real unrouted feed, =composite-derived=,
  produced no tick in the smoke run, so no live tick of an unrouted feed was
  seen; the loop test covers the path.
