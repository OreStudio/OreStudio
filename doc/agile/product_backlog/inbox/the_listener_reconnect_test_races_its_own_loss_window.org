#+title: The listener reconnect test races its own loss window
#+description: ores.database.tests postgres_listener_service_reconnect_surfaces_loss_window kills the listener backend, then sends the NOTIFY it expects to be lost; the listener reconnects on its own, and when it wins that race the NOTIFY lands on the new session and the test reads it as the first payload.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Make =postgres_listener_service_reconnect_surfaces_loss_window= deterministic, or state the guarantee it can actually hold. The case kills the listener's backend, waits for it to leave =pg_stat_activity=, then sends the NOTIFY it expects to be lost, and asserts that the first payload the callback ever sees is the recovered one. The listener reconnects on its own schedule, so between the "gone" check and the send it can already be listening again: the NOTIFY then lands on the new session, and the callback sees the "lost" payload first.

* Why

The case failed once inside =./compass.sh check= on 2026-10-16, in the =ctest= step, on a branch that touches neither =ores.database= nor the listener:

#+begin_example
postgres_listener_service_tests.cpp:368: failed:
  received[0] == recovered_payload
  for: "{"entity":"ores.database.lost"}" == "{"entity":"ores.database.recovered"}"
#+end_example

It is not flaky hardware: the same binary passed the case three times alone, and the whole suite twice, and the gate passed on the re-run. It is a race in the test's own construction, and it widens under the parallel load =ctest= applies, which is exactly when a gate runs it. The window cannot be held open from the test as the service stands: the reconnect is the listener's own loop, and nothing suspends it. A fix therefore needs either a seam on =postgres_listener_service= that holds reconnection while the loss window is written to, or a test that states the weaker guarantee it can keep — that the recovered payload arrives, and that the loss counter rose — without asserting on which payload came first.
