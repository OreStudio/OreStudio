The dry-run preview an FX spot editor asks for before it saves or starts
anything.

The live feed streams ticks over time; this generates the whole batch in one
response, through the real GMM price process, so a screen can contrast the
configured price behaviour without persisting a config or starting a feed.
Nothing is stored and nothing is published.

The batch bounds are not part of this protocol. They are the service's clamp
and a screen's spinner maxima, so they are declared once, in
=simulate_paths_common.hpp=, beside the IR curve bounds they share.
