#+title: The population cannot book an FX variance swap
#+description: The acme_demo trade population aborts partway: the FX variance swap it plans states an end date on or before its start date, and ores_trading_fx_variance_swap_instruments_tbl_check1 refuses it. Every profile that generates trades stops there, so a provisioned tenant gets a partial population.

* What

The population stops partway with:

#+begin_example
Booking trade 9f756808-1bc9-56e1-9644-9f29e145a3d5 failed: Repository error:
ores_q_39: ERROR: new row for relation
"ores_trading_fx_variance_swap_instruments_tbl" violates check constraint
"ores_trading_fx_variance_swap_instruments_tbl_check1"
#+end_example

The constraint is =check (end_date > start_date)=. The generator plans a
variance swap whose end falls on or before its start, so the row is refused and
the run ends. Decide where the dates come from -- the planner's tenor arithmetic
or the instrument's own stated dates -- and make a planned swap state an end
after its start.

* Why

Found on 2026-10-31 proving that a seeded trade raises no authorisation request.
The population wrote 156 trades and then hit this, so an Acme tenant arrives
with a partial population: the trades of the configs that ran, and none of the
rest. Nothing reports the shortfall to the person who provisioned it.

* Fixed

Fixed on 2026-10-31 by =e65378f4d0= -- *[synthetic,utility] Let a generated date
be a real date*, merged as PR #4206, environment =festive_dijkstra=. The
population anchors on the day it runs and steps back by months, keeping the day
of the month, so an anchor on the 31st asked for 2026-09-31. The reflector wrote
a date it could not state as the empty string, the store encoded the absent date
as 0002-11-30 BC, and the variance swap's window collapsed to two equal values,
which its table refuses. The planner now holds a day to the month's last and the
reflector refuses an invented date at the first hop.

The three cases below were written before the fix and all passed, because they
anchored on 2026-01-02 -- a day every month has -- and handed the wire and the
store explicit valid dates. The lesson is recorded as
[[proj:doc/llm/memory/test_calendar_arithmetic_from_a_month_end_day.org][Test calendar arithmetic from a month-end day]].

* Where it came from

The row the population books is built by the variance swap arm of the FX
batch, which arrived with =e8318cbc32= -- *[synthetic] Let an fx trade state its
rows*, 2026-10-30, environment =festive_dijkstra=. The check constraint is older
and is not the change: it is in the table from =3734c23655=, 2026-04-09, and the
population reached no variance swap before the arm existed.

* What is ruled out

Three cases, added with this capture, say the obvious places are not the fault:

- =a_planned_fx_variance_swap_ends_after_it_starts= -- the planner states an end
  after its start for the band the profile uses (one month to two years).
- =a_wire_round_trip_keeps_a_variance_swaps_dates= -- the msgpack round trip of
  =book_trade_request= returns the same two dates.
- =a_booked_variance_swap_keeps_its_dates_in_order= -- the trading service books
  an ordered variance swap into its own table without complaint.

So the refused row comes from the live booking path and not from the plan, the
wire or the storage. The next step is to state the dates the refused row
carries: run the population with =log_parameter_max_length_on_error= set and
read the database's detail for the failing insert.
