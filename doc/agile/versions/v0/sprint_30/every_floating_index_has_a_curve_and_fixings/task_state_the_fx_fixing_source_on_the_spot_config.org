* Goal

The planner hard-codes the FX fixing source TR20H (trade_population_planner.cpp, fx_fixing_source), and its comment says the source belongs to the market data configuration. Add a fixing_source to the FX spot generation config, with TR20H as the default for the synthetic collections. The feed writes its history under that source, and a trade on a pair names the source of that pair's config. A public fixing, such as ECB, keeps its own source and does not collide.
