* Plan

Two views, one unit, two routes: =GET /api/blotter= and =GET /api/books/tree=,
both owned by =ores.trading= and answered from the collections the trading and
reference data services hold.

- The book tree is two collection reads, woven into one answer.
- The blotter reads its rail from every row a filter matches, because a count
  cannot come from a page, and then cuts the page from the rows it ordered.
- A structure is placed where its legs are: on the book they share, or on the
  lowest portfolio that holds them all. Its date is its earliest leg's, and its
  state is its legs' when they agree.
- Each view is kept under its cache policy: an ETag over the whole answer, and
  a freshness the view's own writes decide.

Decisions made:

- The party's label is empty. The BFF answered it from its session, and the
  caller's token names the party by id only; the rail draws the id it already
  falls back to.
- The list is ordered in the gateway, not by the service, because a structure
  has no date of its own: ordering on the facts the rows state keeps the order
  a person sees and the order they paged in the same one.
- The blotter is fresh for 30 seconds and the book tree for 60, as the route
  inventory records the obligation of each.
- The rail's labels compare folded, so a counterparty called "ACME" sorts with
  "Acme" rather than after every lowercase name.
