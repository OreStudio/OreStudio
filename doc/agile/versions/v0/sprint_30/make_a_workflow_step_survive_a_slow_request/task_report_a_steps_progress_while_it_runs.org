* Goal

A running step reports its progress as it works, and the run's progress read answers the latest line while the step is still in progress. A person can tell a slow step from a stopped one without reading a service log.

* Acceptance

- A step can publish a progress line naming the item it is working on and how much of the work is done.
- The progress read answers the latest line for an in-progress step, not only for a finished one.
- A client that polls late still sees the lines the step published, because they reach the run's record.
- The engine's deadline still judges the step, and a progress line does not extend it.
