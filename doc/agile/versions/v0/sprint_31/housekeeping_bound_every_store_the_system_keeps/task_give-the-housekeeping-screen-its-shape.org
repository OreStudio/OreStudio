* Acceptance

- A store's page holds two tabs, Details and Bound, and the bound is not part of the form the record lives in
- The plot draws a time axis and a size axis over a horizon the person picks: the last day, the last week or the last month
- The samples read answers the window it covered, so the axis states the deployment's window and not the browser's
- Every verdict the list states and every mechanism the page names has a sentence that says what it means
- The system administrator's home carries a housekeeping panel stating the stores nothing bounds, and opens the housekeeping screen

* Plan

The read had to state the horizon before the screen could draw one, so the work
went in three layers.

- The read. =scheduler.v1.store_samples.list= takes a =range= (=24h=, =7d=,
  =30d=) in place of a count of points, and answers =from_at= and =to_at= beside
  the points. The hours a range covers are one function, so an unknown range
  answers the week rather than the whole history, and the window the plot draws
  is the deployment's and not the browser's. The three presets are the sampler's
  cadence and not the grid's: the sampler writes once an hour, so an hour-long
  window would usually hold nothing.
- The screen. The store's page is the two questions a person asks of it, in the
  =useTabs= tabs every other page uses. Details carries the record and a chart
  with a time axis, a value axis and one mark per measurement; Bound carries the
  verdict, the mechanism's sentence, the last run and the form. The grid's range
  bar became =ui/Presets.tsx=, so both screens ask a horizon in one shape.
- What the words mean. The verdicts and the mechanisms are the read's own words,
  so the list carries a folded note stating each once and the store's Bound tab
  states the sentence for the store in hand.
- The home. The system administrator's dashboard gains a full-width Housekeeping
  panel that reads the screen's own query, so the panel and the screen cannot
  disagree about how many stores are unbounded.

* Result

The store's page is its two questions, the plot states its own window and its
axes over a horizon the person picks, every verdict and mechanism the screens
state has a sentence that says what it means, and the system administrator's
home carries housekeeping beside the services, the grid and the queue.

Two things the work found and did not fix here. The stores outside a relation —
two object prefixes and two compute folders — still have no measurement, because
only the component that owns a store can measure it;
[[id:AB5A72D3-3C4F-4898-81C9-B3FDA3974C16][the capture]] states what that would
take. And the three presets are the sampler's cadence rather than the grid's, so
a person asking for the last hour of a store would read an empty window; the
screen says the window holds no measurement rather than drawing nothing.
