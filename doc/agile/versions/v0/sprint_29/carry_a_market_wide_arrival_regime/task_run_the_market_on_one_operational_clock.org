* Goal

One object owns the market's time: the regime chain, the heat and tau. It advances tau exactly across a constant regime and an exponentially decaying heat, and inverts that advance to tell the scheduler the clock time at which tau reaches a mark.

* Acceptance

- advance and time_to_reach are exact inverses across a regime transition and a heat decay, to a stated tolerance.
- A pinned state, an enrolment and a heat jump each change only how fast tau moves from that instant.
- The heat's loop gain is checked when the market is built, and a set whose gain is one or more is refused with the gain named.
- The chain and the heat take their draws from the keyed stream.
