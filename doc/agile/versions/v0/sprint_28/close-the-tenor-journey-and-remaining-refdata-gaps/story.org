* Decisions

- Closed at sprint 28 close with its 3 done tasks. The 7 open tasks and the original acceptance moved to [[id:2BCBF0AB-EF49-4F28-9725-06F3D03E4BDF][Close the tenor journey and the remaining refdata gaps — the remaining work]] in the product backlog inbox.
- The tenor resolution screen is its own screen, reached from its own card at
  =/refdata/tenors=, and not a step inside the instrument-convention journey.
  Tenors are their own reference-data job.
- A tenor and the convention that resolves it are one rail, because they are
  read together and the review draws one difference list over both. The
  convention is not a second journey.
- The confirm is a sequence of writes rather than one write, and a refusal names
  the step that refused and the calls already written. A person has to be able to
  see what landed.
- The resolved dates, and a schedule axis expanded into dated rolls, wait on one
  operation the server does not serve yet: resolving a tenor to a date. Both are
  carried by [[id:CFE1A720-6BB6-4D94-AA26-28FBC1A2978D][Resolve a tenor over the wire, with the refusal it carries]].
- Every word the screen draws comes from the catalogue. The journey's state
  module names its changes and its problems by key, never in English.
