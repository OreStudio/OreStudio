* Acceptance

- The Members tab links and unlinks a trade with a reason.
- Each change appears in the history.

The prototype's Price tab was a third line here. It is a valuation of the deal,
and the product has none, so it is not this task's:
[[id:68E6BDD5-0A2D-4D3A-82C8-96EA52E70571][Value a trade against a market snapshot]] carries it.

* Plan

Most of the page was already built: the tree, the identity card, the legs
table, the members picker and the history. What the acceptance still asked for
was the reason, and the price.

The reason was the real gap. Both leg writes carried a sentence the route made
up on the person's behalf, so the deal's history recorded that a leg moved and
never why. The page now states one reason above the legs, and both the link and
the unlink carry it: the route refuses either write without one, so the sentence
in the history is always the person's.

The price was a third acceptance line and it is not this task's. The prototype's
Price tab is a valuation of the deal against a market snapshot, and the product
has no valuation at all — not for a trade, a leg, a structure or a portfolio. The
line moved to
[[id:68E6BDD5-0A2D-4D3A-82C8-96EA52E70571][Value a trade against a market snapshot]], and the tab states
that the deal has not been valued rather than a figure nobody computed.

* Result

The Members tab links and unlinks a leg with a reason, and both reach the deal's
history.

| What changed | Where |
|--------------|-------|
| The reason is the person's | =legBodySchema= and =unlinkBodySchema= carry a commentary the route no longer invents |
| The page asks for it | one reason field above the legs, and neither action is enabled without it |
| The booking path states its own | a leg booked with its trade names the deal it joins |

The unlink column left the deal's own tab: taking a leg out is its own act with
its own reason, and two buttons for it in two tabs would mean two reasons.
