* Goal

The sign-in screen offers each provider the deployment enables, beside the password, and the door leads where the person expects: the provider's own consent, then back to ORE Studio with a session, then the party choice a password sign-in already asks for. A first sign-in that follows the registration policy says what the person will get and, when the policy requires an approval, that the account waits for one. A refusal comes back to the screen with its reason — the provider is not enabled, the address's domain is not allowed, the registration policy refuses one, the link is missing — and every string is translated.

* Acceptance

- The providers the deployment enables appear on the sign-in screen without a build change, and one that is not enabled does not.
- A person returns from the provider to the party choice, and the session they land in is the one the gateway issued.
- Each refusal shows the reason it happened, translated, and never a raw error code.
- A first sign-in states what the policy will give the person, including the waiting state when an approval is required.
- The screens are pinned by tests, and the harness journey signs up and in through the test provider.
