#+updated: 2026-09-30

These are the component's messages that are not entity verbs. Each one acts
on an aggregate, a live computation or a whole-tenant export rather than on
one row of one resource, so none of them belongs in the common resource
grammar that =ores.marketdata.market_series= and its siblings drive. They
were hand-written headers until this model; the header and its TypeScript
twin now generate from here, so the two cannot drift.

Three groups share the file because they share the namespace and the wire
conventions. The CRM group reads live rates from a rate engine. The curve
group bootstraps, republishes and reads curve snapshots. The import and
export group moves whole ORE files in and out of the tenant.

** export_market_data_request

Request to write the tenant's market data back out as ORE text. It takes no
argument: the export is the whole tenant's, because that is the scope the
files have. An ORE =market.txt= is a snapshot of everything a run needs, not
a slice of one series, and an export that could name a subset would produce
a file that reproduces nothing.

** market_tick
:PROPERTIES:
:subject: marketdata.v1.ops.market_tick
:auth: none
:END:

One live observation of one market datum, whatever its asset class. A producer
publishes it on its own feed subject, and the ingest loop stores it and
republishes it to each consumer under this subject, extended with the
consumer's tenant and party and the datum's canonical ORE key. The
tick carries no owner: its =source= finds the consumers through their feed
bindings.

#+begin_src cpp :name comment
/**
 * @brief One live observation of one market datum.
 *
 * The datum is named by its oresmd quote URI, which carries the asset class,
 * the instrument and every field, so no asset class has a tick type of its
 * own. A curve is the set of ticks one producer publishes with one
 * observation time.
 */
#+end_src
