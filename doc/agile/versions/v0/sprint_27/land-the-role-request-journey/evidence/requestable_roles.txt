ACME is a provisioned tenant, so this also shows the tenant provisioner
carrying the flag across. Run at commit 36f1396b9d on a database recreated
from scratch.

Twenty-two rows are not askable: the twenty roles a platform service signs in
with, and the two that grant every permission. Eight are askable: the roles a
person takes on to do a job. That is the rule the screens used to hold as a
name suffix and a permission count, now stated by the tenant and carried to
every tenant it provisions.
