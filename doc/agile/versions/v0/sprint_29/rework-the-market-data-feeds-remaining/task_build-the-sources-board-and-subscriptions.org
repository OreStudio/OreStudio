* Result

Operations now has the market data sources board, and Market data has the
subscriptions and routes screen.

- The board reads the adapter status once and draws the tiles, the alert, the
  table and the detail panel. Four prototype fields are stated as gaps because
  the status does not carry them: the source kind, the quality drops, the routed
  parties and the state history.
- The subscriptions screen reads the party's rights and its routes through the
  BFF, and creates and removes them through the service's put and delete.
  Creating needs the session's party; deleting states the version and asks for
  the reason.
- Two BFF reads were added: subscriptions and routes, plus a catalogue read for
  the providers and sources a route names. No service operation was added.

Open. The party administrator role has no marketdata subscriptions or routes
read in the seed, so a human party account sees nothing until the role is
granted. The running web service serves the new bundle after a build and a
restart. The usage and cost screen has no price read.

Deferred: the usage and cost screen, and the versions history, which has no BFF
read.
