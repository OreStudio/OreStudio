* Result

The part =ores.http.marketdata= answers all twenty-nine resource routes of the
market data area, in seven units:

| Unit | Routes | What they answer |
|------+--------+------------------|
| =adapter_routes= | 1 | Every adapter the service tracks, with the kind of source its feed reads. |
| =subscription_routes= | 6 | The subscriptions and the routes the caller's party holds, their two creates and their two removals. |
| =public_rate_routes= | 4 | The fixings of up to a dozen series, and the three answers a person gives to a publisher's terms. |
| =curve_routes= | 13 | The series an identity names, the URIs of a series, the snapshot, the slice, the evolution, the computed points, the manual points, the published sets, the FX forward ladder and the withdrawal. |
| =series_routes= | 1 | One series' observations inside a window, oldest first. |
| =oresmd_routes= | 4 | The codec's table, one URI read, a batch read and a compose. |
| =marketdata_routes= | 0 | The aggregate the server calls, which registers the six above. |

Twenty-nine routes: twenty-five in the area and the four codec routes.

The six routes still unpaired in the area are the composed views their own
tasks serve: the catalogue, the connectivity list, the fixing series, the
public rates view and its coverage and its file.

**Deviations, each recorded where it was decided.**

- The groups travel on one branch and not one pull request each, because the
  part is new and each group's pairing builds on the one before.
- The public rates group states four routes and not the five the Plan named:
  the publisher list, its coverage and its file are composed views.
- =http_status= gained =unprocessable_content= (422), which is the status the
  BFF answered a service refusal with on the curve and codec routes.
- The shared part gained =record_routes::object_without_members=, because an
  operation's reply carries the protocol's success and message beside the
  payload and a screen reads the payload alone.
- A series read that names no window reads all of them, which is the default
  the contract states; a batch read of no URI answers no parts, as the BFF did.
- =require_done= answers each outcome its own status where it answered 409 for
  every failure. A conflict still answers 409, which is the house's own
  reading of it; a denial is 403, a missing row 404 and a service that cannot
  answer 502, which is what the BFF's own refusal did.
