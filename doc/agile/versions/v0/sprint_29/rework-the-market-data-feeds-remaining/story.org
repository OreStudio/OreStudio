* Goal

ores.marketdata owns the catalogue, the mapping, the ingest path, routing and metering. Adapters, including the simulator, publish vendor-id ticks over NATS and answer a demand set. Every simulated feed belongs to the system tenant and the system party, a party takes a provider by subscription, and no part of the system behaves differently because a source is simulated.

* Acceptance

- The catalogue holds providers, sources with a kind, vendor instruments and mappings, installed by a system bundle.
- An adapter announces itself, answers a demand set, publishes vendor-id ticks and sends a heartbeat, and the service maps, checks, stores and delivers them.
- A party holds a subscription with rights and one route per provider, and a source switch is audited and effective-dated.
- The simulator is an adapter under ores.marketdata.synthetic, its generators belong to the system tenant and system party, and no subject, table or constraint carries a mark of the simulator.
- Usage events, rate cards, rating and allocation give each party a bill, and a simulated source gives a shadow bill.
- The screens and the shell follow the architecture pages, and a scenario shows a development party on a simulated source and a prelive party on a live source side by side.

* Decisions

- The intraday reports run at the cadences a desk expects, decided by the product owner on 2026-11-04 and stated in the catalogue, the pages and the live scheduler. The triple vol spot ladder and the surface vega ladder run hourly at every hour rather than inside the London day, because each costs a full sensitivity run and a desk reads the shape between the closes. The spot ladder runs every half hour and trades since last in every fifteen minutes, so neither is a definition that can never fire. The spike, strike and option maps keep the on-demand placeholder, and every other report keeps its cadence. The close twins do not move. See [[id:F2FE9A6D-1367-46C5-9532-56019FE92340][Set the intraday frequencies a desk expects]].

- The ladder family is stated in the catalogue at every party and fails at the engine (2026-11-04). The product owner overruled the design's decision to hold it back until the report inputs and the volatility data arrive: a definition that produces no figure costs nothing to hold, and the list should show what the platform runs. Ten of the design's fourteen rows are seeded as fifteen definitions per party, because five of them run on both cycles, and they bind the sensitivity run settings a party already holds. Rows 11 to 14 stay unseeded on their market data. See [[id:B124F617-0859-4F78-9F81-85DEA485D97B][Seed the ladder family and let it fail like the rest]].
- A schedule is made by the one operation the shell calls, and a screen adds no
  schedule of its own (2026-11-04). The BFF relays =schedule_report_definitions=
  and =unschedule_report_definitions= on the person's own session and answers
  their count, their failed ids and their words, gated on the permission each
  operation checks — the definition's write to schedule it, its delete to take
  the schedule away. Activating an on-demand report writes the expression the
  job will fire on and schedules it in one call, because the scheduler reads the
  expression from the definition. A definition with no scheduler job is on
  demand, and so is one whose expression never fires, so the list and the page
  read one word. See [[id:38DDC0B4-A56D-42B4-B260-6C98BA356E0B][Schedule a
  report from its own page]].

- The tick store holds one tick for each source, owned by the system tenant, and the history read checks the subscription (2026-10-25). The per-party copy added 80% of the store on the measured tenant, and neither the usage recorder nor the delivery meter reads the store. See [[id:D71F9446-C62E-48A6-A87B-136EF598A59E][A source tick is stored once and entitled when it is read]].

- Split from [[id:BC8B619A-D766-4208-B4D1-4785F24AC67D][Rework the market data feeds into a market data service with adapters]] on 2026-10-24 at sprint 28 close. The acceptance above is copied from that story; its done tasks are recorded there.
- The route is the one consumer record (2026-10-24). The feed binding retires, with its two tables, its screen and its policies. A projection of the routes would keep two records for one fact and need a writer to hold them in step. This reverses the sprint 27 decision that consumption is a binding. Evidence and consequences are in [[id:E53B0439-85D8-44FF-9D3F-6C7C644E0D62][Decide the binding's place in the route architecture]].
