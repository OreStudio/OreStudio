* Decisions

- Manual chapter presents Acme Corporation directly, with no
  "replacing Barclays Plc" framing -- Barclays/historical references
  removed per user direction.
- ORE Studio's RBAC roles (Trading/Operations/Viewer) are seeded at
  the tenant level, not scoped per desk/business unit -- the chapter
  states Acme's departmental separation is organisational, not (yet)
  an access-control boundary, rather than overclaiming enforcement
  that doesn't exist.
- Re-shooting the 5 other manual chapters still showing real Barclays
  screenshots (Tenants, Accounts and Roles, Connecting, Parties,
  Counterparties) was scoped out of this task and filed as its own
  capture, since their prose is tied to the real screenshots too.
- Acme's synthetic feeds are started via the same folder-scoped
  mechanism PR #1741 introduced for the Qt Market Simulator, resolved
  deterministically from the per-(tenant, party, dataset) container
  =synthetic_publish_from_dq.sql= already keys -- not a second,
  Acme-specific start path.
