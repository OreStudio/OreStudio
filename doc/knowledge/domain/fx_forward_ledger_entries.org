#+title: A forward carries net fair value on balance sheet and its notional off it
#+description: Forwards are derivatives recorded at net fair value; the notional is a memorandum amount.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

FX forwards are derivatives, so only the net value is recorded, at fair value. The fair value is zero at inception and changes with exchange rates. The notional is the gross face amount and is not the fair value.

* Detail

The BIS observes that this net treatment hides the full notional exchanged at maturity, which a gross record would show.
