* Acceptance

- The cap/floor strikes are rows of instrument_strikes and its schedules are rows of instrument_schedules and instrument_schedule_dates, written by the import and read by the export.
- The swaption's option data is rows of instrument_options and its four children, with premium_amount, premium_currency, premium_pay_date, exercise_prices and settlement_fixing_date typed rather than text, and instrument_option_exercise_fees.start_date typed as a date.
- The rates header's Delete cascade names the shared children, so a header delete closes them.
- instrument_schedules.owner_role's check lists the rates family's owner roles beside trs_funding, repo, ascot_swap, option and trs.
- The children's registration sits in the root that owns them, not in registrar_bond.cpp.
- The ORE round trip keeps a cap/floor's strikes and schedules and a swaption's option block.

* Plan

Stage 1, merged as PR #2986, is the family's ownership of the children: the
header's cascade, the catalogue's child arrays, and the schedule owner role.

1. *The shapes.* The bond pilot's =instrument_strike=, =instrument_schedule=,
   =instrument_schedule_date=, =instrument_option= and its keyed children are
   the target. The family writes to them; it does not widen them.
2. *The retyping, finding R5, decided.* ORE states =PremiumAmount=,
   =PremiumPayDate=, =ExercisePrices= and the option premiums'
   =SettlementFixingDate= as =xs:string=, and the schema copied the string.
   The storage is typed and the boundary converts, as the family's =end_date=
   does: =premium_amount= becomes =numeric(28, 10)=, =premium_pay_date= and
   both =settlement_fixing_date= columns become =date=.
3. *=exercise_prices=, the fourth child.* The column holds a list in a string
   and the option block has three keyed children where the design's entity
   table names four. =instrument_option_exercise_price= is the fourth: the
   trade, the ordinal, the exercise date and the price, as
   =instrument_option_premium= is keyed. The text column is dropped.
   =premium_currency= stays =text=, because a currency code is text in every
   other table here and soft-FKs to =ores_refdata_currencies_tbl=.
4. *The registration root, finding R11.* Move the shared children's
   registration out of =registrar_bond.cpp= into the root that owns them.
5. *The mapper.* =swap_instrument_data= gains one vector per child;
   =forward_capfloor= fills the strikes and the schedules,
   =forward_swaption= fills the option block, and the reverse mappers read
   them back. The import handler calls the =save_strike=, =save_schedule= and
   =save_option_block= helpers the bond path already has, with =owner_role=
   =swap= for a leg's schedule and =option= for the exercise dates.
6. *The export.* The export service reads the child tables for the rates
   trade ids into the batch arrays and =as_swap= gathers them by trade id.
7. *The proof.* A cap/floor round trip that keeps its strikes and schedules,
   and a swaption round trip that keeps its option block.
8. *Order.* The cap/floor first, end to end, with a build and the round trip
   between it and the swaption.

* Result

The rates family writes the shared children the bond pilot built, and they
survive the round trip. The cap/floor states its strikes as
=instrument_strikes= rows and its underlying swap's schedule as
=instrument_schedules= and =instrument_schedule_dates= rows; the swaption
states its option block as =instrument_options= and four keyed children,
including =instrument_option_exercise_price=, the fourth child the design's
entity table names and the models never had. The option block's text dates
and its price list are typed: =premium_amount= is a decimal,
=premium_pay_date= and both =settlement_fixing_date= columns are dates, and
=exercise_prices= is the child rather than a string. The header's cascade
names all eight children, and their registration sits in
=registrar_rates.cpp= rather than the bond family's root (finding R11).

Three residuals, all deliberate:

- *The database-level end-to-end proof is unit 8's.* The acceptance's
  "written by the import and read by the export" is proved by the mapper
  round trips and by the wiring compiling, not by importing a rates trade
  into the database and exporting it. The corpus round trip at the new shape
  is already the task =Prove the rates round trip over the corpus=, which is
  where that proof belongs, and duplicating it here would prove it twice.
- *Bonds now carry their exercise prices through the fourth child.* The
  =instrument_option= shape is shared with the bond family, so retyping the
  option block and dropping =exercise_prices= would have silently dropped
  bond prices; the bond writer and reader convert at the boundary and write
  and read the child instead. This is a behaviour change for the bond family
  and is called out in the PR. The price list pairs token *i* with exercise
  date *i*, so a document stating more prices than dates loses the tail.
- *=split_numbers= is duplicated* in the ORE mapper and the import handler,
  in two components' anonymous namespaces. Not worth a shared API yet.

An earlier attempt at this unit bundled a cherry-pick of a trunk hotfix and
two merge commits; both are gone, and the branch is three commits on
=ceced2447a= with a clean history. The first database recreate failed because
the branch's base predated the trunk seed hotfix, which is what rebasing onto
current trunk fixed; no trunk fix is carried in this unit.
