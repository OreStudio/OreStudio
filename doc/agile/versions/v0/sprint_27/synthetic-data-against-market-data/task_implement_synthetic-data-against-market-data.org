* Goal

Make the synthetic feeds first-class producers in the new market data model. A feed already speaks oresmd and can auto-create a feed_binding, but nothing yet proves the whole path end to end: that its series is registered under the oresmd identity, that each observation it publishes carries per-point provenance and is marked generated rather than observed, that the ingest loop lands it in market_observation under the right tenant and source, and that a consumer such as the curve bootstrap reads it and says where it came from. This story proves that path, fixes the gaps it finds, and leaves the synthetic feeds able to drive the curve and surface work with data that can be plotted.

* Acceptance

- A synthetic feed ticks and a market_series row exists whose oresmd_uri is the series identity the feed publishes, registered before any observation lands.
- Every observation the feed publishes reaches market_observation with its point identity in the oresmd URI, its source, and provenance that says generated rather than observed.
- The path is exercised end to end against a running service: start a feed, see the series and observations appear, and read them back by oresmd URI.
- The curve bootstrap consumes a synthetic feed's observations the same way it consumes market observations, and the resulting points record the synthetic provenance.
- The gaps found are fixed or captured as their own tasks, and the story states what a workspace-scoped or scenario feed would still need.
