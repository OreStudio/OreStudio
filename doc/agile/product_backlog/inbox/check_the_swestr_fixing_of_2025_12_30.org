* What

Find out whether the Riksbank published SWESTR for 2025-12-30 as -6.03 per
cent, or whether the Riksbank adapter stored a wrong value. If the adapter is
wrong, fix its parse and add a recorded response for that day to its tests. If
the Riksbank published it, decide whether the fixing stays or the calibration
leaves it out.

* Why

On 2026-11-12 a fresh environment stored these SWESTR fixings:

#+begin_example
2025-12-29  0.01649
2025-12-30 -0.0603
2026-01-02  0.01675
#+end_example

The synthetic IR feeds calibrate from these fixings, so one outlier of eight
points distorts the volatility the calibration finds for SEK. Every other
overnight rate in the system tenant stays in a plausible range.
