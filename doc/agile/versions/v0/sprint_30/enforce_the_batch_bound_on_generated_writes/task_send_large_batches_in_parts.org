* Goal

Every hand-written caller that can send a put-many or delete-many of more than 1,000 elements sends it in parts of at most 1,000, sized from one named constant, so the refusal the next task adds breaks no caller.

* Acceptance

- The bound is one named constant, and the get-many refusal in the service template reads it.
- The variance calibration points, the synthetic counterparty and trade-type scope, the iam provisioning plans and the ORE import rollback send their batches in parts of at most 1,000.
- A test proves the split: each part holds at most 1,000 elements, the parts keep the order, and together they hold every element.
