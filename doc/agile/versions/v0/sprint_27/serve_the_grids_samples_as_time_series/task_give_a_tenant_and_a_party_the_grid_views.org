* Acceptance

- The poller writes a job-counter row for each tenant and each party that has work.
- A tenant session reads the series of its tenant and a party session the series of its parties, each with the fleet capacity from the system rows.
- The grid screen opens to both with Overview and Usage, and keeps Machines, Nodes and Heatmap for the operator.

* Plan

Decisions of the owner: a tenant sees the installation's capacity as a summary, the machines, nodes and heatmap stay with the operator, and a party user sees all the statistics of the party. No backfill and no backward compatibility.

- The grid sample table gets a =party_id=. The poller writes, at one instant, a row of job counters for each party of each tenant that has work, and one for the work with no party. The row of the system tenant's work with no party also carries the hosts.
- A tenant is listed by a small table that the installation owns, =ores_compute_work_tenants_tbl=. The dispatcher adds a tenant when it first dispatches for it. The poller needs it because the compute service cannot read the tenant roster, and the tenants' own work is not visible from the system tenant. The table holds ids and a time, and its policy admits only the system tenant, as the work ledger's does, so no policy is widened.
- A restrictive party policy on the sample table hides the rows of other parties from a party session, and passes the rows with no party.
- =ores_compute_grid_stats_fn= takes a tenant, a party and a flag for the fleet.
- The series read sums the rows of the session's tenant for each instant, which gives a tenant its totals and a party session the totals of its parties. It takes the host counts from the system tenant's row, which every session may read.
- The BFF serves the series to a tenant's or a party's session without the deployment read. It reads no node series and no host for them.
- The grid card needs =compute::grid_samples:read=. The Member role holds that and =compute::batches:read=, so every person gets the grid for the work they may see.
- The grid screen shows a tenant or a party member the Overview and the Usage tabs. The operator keeps the five tabs.

* Result

A tenant administrator and a party member now read the load, throughput and outcomes of their own work over time, with the installation's capacity as counts. The poller writes a row for each party of each tenant that has dispatched work, and the series read sums the rows a session may see. The Overview and the Usage tabs are offered to them, and the machines, nodes and heatmap stay with the operator. The development database was changed by hand to match: the party column, the policy, the roster table and the function. A new database gets them from the create scripts. The Member role gets the two reads from the populate script, and the live roles of Acme and Barclays were not changed, so a party member of those tenants needs the role refreshed before the screen opens for them.
