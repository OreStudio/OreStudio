* Goal

The calibration follows the public rates as they arrive. A scheduler job starts synthetic_calibration_workflow once each business day after the public sources publish; the fits the policy allows are approved, the exceptions reach the reviewer's inbox, and a running feed takes its new calibration at its next market day roll, with no restart.

- The job's runs show on the scheduler and the workflow screens.
