* What

Make main pass the local check gate again, so a branch that is green on its own
change can raise its PR on a green report.

* Why

On 2026-11-17 =compass check= on =feature/state-the-identity-shape-of-a-fixing=,
rebased on =545cafca45=, failed these checks. The branch changes none of their
inputs.

- =pgtap=: =refdata_fx_vol_surface_blueprints_publish_test.sql= expects ten
  derived pairs and =acme_fx_volatility_configs_test.sql= expects 22 surfaces.
  The seed now has 35 pairs (commits 765ec2ce2a, a4b7491c12 and 0121534b78, on
  2026-11-14 and 2026-11-16); the tests last changed on 2026-11-11.
- =pgtap=: =iam_tenant_purge_test.sql= hit a deadlock, because the services of
  the smoke test were still running when the suites ran.
- =codegen-tests=: =test_domain_includes= finds trading headers that include
  =<optional>= and use no =std::optional=, and the two
  =test_rls_own_or_system_tenant_rows= tests no longer find the policy body
  they expect.
- =component-drift=: =ores.marketdata.service/config/options.hpp= differs from
  its model in the default of =stale_seconds_ir= and =stale_seconds_fx=.
- =tangle-shell=: the =barrier_types=, =commodity_instruments= and =leg_types=
  recipes tangle to scripts that differ from the committed ones, and the
  =regulatory_corporate_sector= recipe tangles into =doc/recipes/shell/=.
- =ticks-smoke=: the late-subscriber bug in
  [[id:2659F47C-2C7D-48B4-9FA0-46BBE1214485][Send the derived FX vol quotes to a party that subscribes late]].
