* Goal

Every IBOR feed of every currency has a bootstrapped curve that reads its own IBOR quotes and discounts off the currency's overnight curve, publishes like any other curve, and carries the projection role so no consumer takes it for a discount curve.

* Acceptance

- Each IBOR feed has a bootstrap config, a quote series and an output series, and names the currency's overnight config as its discount curve.
- A fresh database seeds them, and a bootstrap run publishes every IBOR curve beside its overnight curve.
- The IBOR curves publish with role projection, and no consumer selects one as a discount curve; a test states the rule.
- The curve identities and names are readable from the market data and coverage reads.
