* Goal

A person can calibrate a surface, see the candidate and its errors, send it to publish, and read the published surface. The screens implement the journeys Calibrate or refresh a surface and Inspect a surface's slices. The web app already has =SurfacePanel= in =market/=, a heatmap and slices of any snapshot with two axes. These screens reuse it.

* Acceptance

- A person starts a calibrate from a blueprint and an as-of, and sees each pillar's error and the arbitrage results.
- A person raises the publish of a candidate from that screen.
- A published surface shows as a heatmap and as slices through =SurfacePanel=.
- The Evolution page shows a party's published surface, and how it moved over a range, through =SurfacePanel=. A surface is a party's derived data, so it shows there; the raw market vols show on the synthetic screen.
- A candidate is always labelled as a candidate.
- The published surface has a record page at =market-data/published-surfaces/<series>=, where the surface publish notice links.
- The approval inbox shows a surface publish request's subject, the candidate and its checks, as it shows a curve publish's.
