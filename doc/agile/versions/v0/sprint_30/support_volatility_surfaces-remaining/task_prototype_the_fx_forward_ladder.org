* Acceptance

- The prototype is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript under =doc/prototypes/market_data/=, with the web app's tokens.
- It shows one pair at one as-of as a ladder of forward rate, discount factors, forward points and swap points, and a chart of the selected measure.
- A control starts the ladder from today, tomorrow or spot, and marks the rows it moves.
- It shows spot and the pip factor, and labels the numbers as implied by the IR curves, with no cross-currency basis.
- Each state has an address, the design note says what is fake, and the group page lists it.
- The owner signs the prototype off before any code.
