* Acceptance

- Every family that holds a size is named, with the table it lives in and what the number means.
- The composition screens state either a family's own fact or nothing, and never a column of dashes.
- A recommendation is recorded: whether a composition screen is about what is grouped or about exposure.

* Plan

The question is answered from the store rather than from opinion: every column in
the trading schema whose name says it is a size, grouped by the shape it takes.
The screens are then read against that list, and the one place that draws a size
unconditionally is fixed.

The scope was deliberately narrow. The alternative — deciding what a composition
screen is *for* and rebuilding it around an exposure number — is a design of its
own, and this task exists to settle the fact that a size is not one fact.

* Result

There is no such thing as the size of a trade. The store states four shapes, and
only one of them is a number a column can hold.

| Shape | Families | Where it lives | What the number means |
|-------+----------+----------------+-----------------------|
| One money amount | bond, FRA, equity notional products, bond future (as =contract_notional=), FX variance swap | the instrument table's =notional= | the amount the deal is struck on, in the trade's currency |
| Two amounts in two currencies | FX forward, FX vanilla option, FX barrier option | =bought_amount= and =sold_amount= | what is bought and what is sold; neither is the deal's size alone |
| A quantity | commodity, equity forward, equity position | =quantity= | units of the underlying, not money |
| A schedule | rate swaps and every amortising family | =ores_trading_swap_leg_amounts_tbl= (=amount= per leg per =start_date=), =ores_trading_flexi_swap_lower_notionals_tbl=, =ores_trading_bg_swap_tranche_notionals_tbl= | the amount in force over a period; there is no single figure, and a bond's =face_value= is its convention rather than its size |

A structure makes this worse rather than better: its legs are trades of different
families, so a structure's size would be a sum across a notional, a quantity and
a schedule, in more than one currency.

* Recommendation

A composition screen is about what is grouped, not about exposure. It states the
legs, their roles, their families and their currencies, and the number of legs.
It does not state a total, because a total across those shapes is not a number;
exposure is valuation's question, which is
[[id:68E6BDD5-0A2D-4D3A-82C8-96EA52E70571][Value a trade against a market snapshot]].

Where a screen states one trade's own fact, it states that family's fact: the
notional where the family has one, bought and sold where it has two, the quantity
where it counts units, and nothing where the family holds a schedule.

* What changed

The trade page drew "Notional" for every family, which is a dash for a swap, an
FX trade and a commodity. It now states the family's own fact: the notional, the
bought and sold amounts, or the quantity, and nothing where the family states a
schedule.

* What is left

Nothing in the composition screens draws a total today, so there is nothing to
remove. A future screen that wants an exposure number should read this task's
recommendation first.
