The workflow step that publishes the script library trading owns from a DQ
dataset. It is a trigger, as the analytics component's publish steps are. The
DQ publisher sends it and reads no reply, so the step states a subject and no
response, and its body is the DQ artefact the server-side function knows how to
expand.

The SQL function the step calls is derived from its subject, so the spelling of
the subject matters twice: once as the subject the registrar subscribes and once
as the function the handler calls. That is why the subject is declared here and
referenced, never spelled out at a call site.
