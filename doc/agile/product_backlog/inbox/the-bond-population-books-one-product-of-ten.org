#+title: The bond population books one product of ten
#+description: Ten trade types route to the bond instrument table. The planner draws exactly one of them, a plain Bond, so every one of the twenty-four bond trades in a provisioned tenant is a Bond and the other nine codes never appear. The family's own fact rows -- the option, the repo, the future, the total return swap, the ASCOT, the callable's call dates, the convertible's conversion targets -- are all empty, because the writer fills only the instrument header. A tenant therefore cannot see a bond option, a repo or a convertible, and nothing says so.
