#+title: List images without exceeding the NATS payload
#+description: GET /api/v1/assets/images answers internal_error: the assets service's list reply exceeds NATS's maximum payload, so its publish fails and no caller can list images.

* What

=list_images= in the assets service answers a page of images small enough for
one NATS message, or answers the images without their data, so
=GET /api/v1/assets/images= lists them.

* Why

On 2026-11-23 the assets service logged
=assets.v1.images.list failed: NATS publish failed: Maximum Payload Exceeded=,
and the route answered =internal_error=. A list that carries every image's
bytes outgrows the payload limit as images are added, so the list fails for
every caller, an LLM agent's =call_operation= included. Found while checking
the operations the small-model measurement expects.
