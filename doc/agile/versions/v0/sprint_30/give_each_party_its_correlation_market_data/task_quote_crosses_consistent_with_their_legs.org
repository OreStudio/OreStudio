* Goal

Every blueprint cross has a latent surface implied from its two USD legs' latent
surfaces and the correlation between them that the simulator also quotes, so a
cross's volatility is consistent with its legs by construction.

* Acceptance

- A cross's latent ATM vol at each expiry is
  $\sigma_{cross}^2 = \sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2 + 2 s \rho \sigma_1 \sigma_2$, with
  $\sigma_1, \sigma_2$ its legs' latent ATM vols and $\rho$ the correlation the
  simulator quotes for that pair of legs at that tenor; no cross surface is
  simulated from the cross's own spot.
- The cross's spot and forwards, which set its strikes, are its legs' spot and
  forwards triangulated; they set no volatility.
- The rule for the cross's smile (risk reversals and butterflies) is decided and
  recorded before it is built, because the correlation fixes only the ATM vol.
- The 21 crosses are added to the dataset from the blueprints, with no pair
  listed by hand.
