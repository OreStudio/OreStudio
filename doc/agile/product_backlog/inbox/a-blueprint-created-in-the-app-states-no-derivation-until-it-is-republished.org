This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Stamp a blueprint's output series when the blueprint is created, so the series
states its derivation from the first moment and the republish service stops
being the first thing to say how a curve was made.

* Why

The population script stamps every seeded blueprint's output series
=IR_CURVE_BOOTSTRAP=, so an installed curve states its derivation before
anything republishes. A blueprint created in the application is different: the
creator writes an output series that reads =OBSERVED=, and the only thing that
changes it is the republish service, on its first republish, in
=curve_republish_service.cpp=.

Until that republish the store holds two statements about the same curve. The
publish record names the blueprint it was built from, and the series says it was
observed. The published curve screen reads the builder from the record, so it
reports the truth, and the disagreement stays invisible there. Any other reader
of the series — the fixing screens, a lineage query — sees the wrong claim.

Nothing writes the series on the creation path today: refdata holds no market
series repository use at all, so the series is minted by whoever creates the
config (the provisioning step, or the client that saves the pair). That is what
makes this more than a one-line change, and why it was captured rather than
added to the task that introduced the kinds.
