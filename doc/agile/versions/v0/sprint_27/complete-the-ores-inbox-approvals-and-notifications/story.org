* Decisions

- *A role request is one call.* IAM raises the inbox request as the person,
  passing on their token, then records the roles; it withdraws the request if
  the record fails.
- *Approvals are applied by reconciliation.* IAM grants every approved role not
  yet held, at start and on every request change, in every tenant not
  terminated, as the approver. The change event is only a nudge.
- *IAM reads the inbox through two functions it owns*, so its role holds no
  grant on the inbox's tables; the cross-tenant one runs for the IAM service
  only.
