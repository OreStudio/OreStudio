* Before you start

- *The code under test is on main.* This branch only carries this document; the grouping route, the completion and the draft booking are all merged.
- *The environment is ready.* Built, the services up (=compass services status=), and Acme provisioned, which leaves the queue empty and the picker full.
- *The database must be in sync.* =compass db status= states it; if it has drifted, =compass services stop= then =compass db recreate -y -k=, and provision again.
- The two drafts the scenario books face one counterparty, because a structure's legs share one and the screen refuses a mixed pair.

* Steps

** Log in as the tenant administrator

- Open http://127.0.0.1:20802.
- Sign in as =tenant_admin@acme_corporation= / =Secure-Password-123=.
- Choose party *ACME Corporation UK plc* when the tenant asks for one.
- Passes when the home page greets *Welcome* by name and the status bar names tenant *Acme Corporation* and party *ACME Corporation UK plc*.

** Book the first deal as a draft

- Open =/trading/trades/new= (Book a trade).
- Fill it as usual: an open book of the UK entity, a counterparty, today's trade date, an issue from the list, and a notional such as =1000000=.
- In *Draft reason*, choose *The deal is a leg of a structure that is not complete*.
- Press *Book*.
- Passes when the trade opens on its own page and the blotter states it as *draft*.

** Book the second deal as a draft

- Book another trade the same way, with the *same counterparty* as the first and the same draft reason.
- Passes when both drafts are in the blotter.
- Open =/operations/authorisation-queue=: it shows no rows. A draft waits, it does not ask.

** Open the grouping screen

- Go to =/trading/structures/group= (or follow *Group trades* from the Trading area).
- Passes when the screen offers a kind, a counterparty filter, a chosen count reading *No trade chosen yet*, and a picker of ungrouped trades.

** Pick the two drafts and group them

- Filter the counterparty to the one the two drafts face, and tick both drafts in the picker.
- Press *Group trades*.
- Passes when the new structure's page opens with a =PKG-…= reference and the Members tab lists both drafts as legs.

** Confirm both legs are live

- Open =/trading= and find the two trades.
- Passes when each states *live* rather than *draft*, and the row names the structure it belongs to.

- Open =/operations/authorisation-queue=.

- The queue was empty before the grouping, so both requests came from the completion taking the drafts live.
