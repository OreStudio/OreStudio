as the accepted access screens prototype draws them: a member's own access, the
administrator's grant and take-away, and the role catalogue with its permission

* Acceptance

- *My access* lists the roles held with who gave each one and why, answers
  "Can I…?" with the role behind a yes, and draws what the roles allow by area.
- *People* lists the tenant's accounts with pictures and roles; a person's page
  gives a role for a reason and takes one away, never from oneself.
- *Roles* hides service roles unless asked; a role's page ticks what it allows
  by area and saves the whole set after showing the difference.
- Every account the screens name shows its picture, and so does Rescue access.

* Plan

- The protocol package gains readers and writers for the role subjects, and
  browser-safe types for a held role, a role and a permission.
- The BFF adds one route per screen action and a picture route by username,
  because most places that name an account know a username, not an image.
- One catalogue module turns the 890 codes into areas, resources and actions,
  answers "covers" with wildcards, and searches in a person's words; one grid
  component draws it read-only or with ticks.
- The screens follow the prototype. Roles per person are read one account at a
  time, as the server has no joined read yet.

* Result

The access screens run in =ores.web=.

- *My access* (=/access=, from the account menu behind the person's picture):
  the roles held with who gave each one, when and why, a *Can I…?* search
  naming the role behind a yes, and the permissions by area; a role that
  grants everything says so.
- *People* and a person (=/people=, =/people/:username=), for the tenant
  administrator: pictures and role chips; *Give a role* with a reason from the
  access category and a note; *Take away*, disabled on one's own page.
- *Roles* and a role (=/roles=, =/roles/:roleId=): service roles hidden unless
  asked; a new role starts empty; the permission grid by area with "All of it",
  a filter, "Only what it allows", and a save that lists what it adds, removes
  and whose access changes. A role that grants everything, or a service's
  role, is not edited; a role somebody holds is not offered for deletion.
- *The header* holds the person's picture alone, as most applications do; the
  account menu behind it names the person, the tenant and the party, and holds
  My access, Security and Sign out. The tenant stays in view beside the brand.
- Pictures: =/api/accounts/:username/picture= serves any account's picture, and
  the header, "Given by", People, a person, "Held by" and Rescue access use it
  or the account's image.

Tests: BFF 149 (seven new route cases), web 246 (eleven new for the catalogue
and the screens), protocol 70; typecheck, menu and route checks clean. Not
checked live with a sign-in: the recreated deployment has no administrator yet.
Open: roles per person cost one read per account, and the delete guard and
the edit lock are the screen's alone.
