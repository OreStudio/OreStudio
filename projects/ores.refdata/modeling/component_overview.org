* Summary

=ores.refdata= provides the reference-data foundation for ORE Studio — currencies,
counterparties, books, business units, party relationships, and associated
conventions. It is structured as a composite component with four sub-components
and has no code of its own: every generated and hand-written artefact lives
under one of the sub-components below, never directly under this directory.
