This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The regime raster gives every feed a row of the ticks it published, and the row
cannot say why it looks as it does. Add a column group after the name: the
arrival mode, the branching ratio, the decay and the sensitivity — the four
numbers a generation config carries — plus the feed's own current intensity if
the model can state it cheaply.

* Why

A reader sees a quiet row and cannot tell a feed that ticks once a minute from
one that clusters, or one with a sensitivity of zero from one the market has
not moved. The raster currently shows an expected rate from =ticks_per_hour=,
which explains the cadence but not the clustering: a Hawkes feed at the same
mean rate looks different, and that difference is the point of the regime.

The fields are on each generation-config row, not on the folder-feeds view the
raster reads, so this is a view and protocol change before it is a layout one:
=ores.synthetic_fx_spot_generation_configs= and the IR curve equivalent carry
=arrival_mode=, =branching_ratio=, =decay= and =sensitivity=, and
=list_folder_feeds= returns none of them.

* References

- Measured on =prime_origin=, 2026-10-26: 20 IR feeds at a mean of 0.017 ticks a
