* Result

The fx family states its rows. The planner builds the row each type routes to,
beside the drawing that feeds it, and the writer persists what the plan carries.
The writer's fx arm is gone, and with it the per-row stamping: the walk over the
batch states the audit columns on every row and on the accumulator's range
bounds.

The population is unchanged: a fresh tenant books 8 forward, 4 vanilla option,
12 barrier option, 4 digital option and 4 asian forward rows with 4 range
bounds, and 127 trades in total, which is what it booked before the flip.

Two fx products are absent and the flip did not remove them. The fx planner
cycles sixteen routed types and each seeded config states eight slots, so the
last eight types are never drawn: no fx variance swap and no fx accumulator
exists in any provisioned tenant, though both construct and both route. That is
the same positional defect the rate family shows with its knock-out swap, and it
is captured once for both rather than fixed here.

The plan's bag narrowed again. Fx left the variant, so three remain: bond, whose
security still travels in it, and the two families that have not flipped.
