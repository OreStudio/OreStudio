#+title: One booking API books a product in either form
#+description: Describe, validate and book a product in any of its forms, and book a free-form scripted trade, through one API with one validation path.

* Summary

One API, on the trading service, books every product in any form it offers.
=describe_product= returns a product's schema ([[id:290A1069-A1EE-4DD1-8D23-1C60F76F8949][Every product has a schema of its fields and its forms]]).
=book= takes a product, its terms and a form, validates the terms against the
schema, and books the native element through its builder or the scripted form
through the scripted builder. =book_scripted= books a free-form scripted trade,
a library script and version or an inline script, with its variables. Every
scripted booking passes the server's validation ([[id:C2A45123-A9B3-49BA-BEFD-07E1703F7B84][A scripted trade is validated in three layers]]), so a booking
form, the editor, import and the generator share one path.

* Detail

** The operations

| Operation | Takes | Does |
|-----------+-------+------|
| =describe_product= | a product | returns its fields and forms |
| =validate_script= | a script, by version or text, and variables | parses with ORE's grammar and checks the variables against the inputs, booking nothing |
| =book= | a product, its terms, a form | validates, then builds the native element, or maps the terms onto the script by the product's twin recipe ([[id:206312C0-6398-4B9D-86F8-D8AEC9A5CEAD][A twin recipe maps a native product's terms onto its script]]), or for a scripted-only product takes the terms as the script's variables |
| =book_scripted= | a script and its variables | validates, then books the ScriptedTrade |

=book= of a scripted form and =book_scripted= both end in the scripted builder,
which states the trade's rows ([[id:B3FBDD1A-8FC6-4702-9600-B75581FC6494][ores.trading.scripted_instrument]],
[[id:8D12E0ED-B6D9-4FDC-A98C-35AE84B8045F][ores.trading.scripted_parameter]]).

** What a booking records

The product, the form, and for a scripted form the script's version or inline
text ([[id:358CC548-3892-45A1-A916-DF4A48ADED03][A scripted trade names a library script or states its own]]), so the trade reopens in the form it was booked in.

** Where today's code differs

A trade is booked from rows a builder already made; no operation takes a
product, terms and a form, and none describes a product or validates a script.
