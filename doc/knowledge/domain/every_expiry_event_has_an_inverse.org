#+title: Every expiry event has an inverse
#+description: An exercise or an expiry can be reversed; an option past its cut is in a waiting state until the holder decides.
#+type: knowledge
#+audience: user
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:expiry:option:lifecycle:
#+created: 2026-11-22
#+updated: 2026-11-22

* Summary

Every life-cycle event of an option has an inverse. An unexpiry makes an
expired option live again; an unexercise cancels the exercise trade and makes
the option live again; each records its reason and is attributed separately. An
option whose expiry has passed without a decision is in a state of its own,
waiting for the holder, and is reported until it leaves it. Reversal has limits
outside the system: once a termination has been reported to a trade repository,
it is corrected by a further report, not withdrawn.

* Detail

** Undo

Each event has an undo that does the right things: it cancels the trades the
event booked and makes the option live again, with a reason such as /wrong
rate/, after which the option is expired again. Undo events are attributed in a
bucket of their own; see
[[id:49E61902-364C-4729-A53E-D55873D9A03D][Expired and exercised options are valued for control, not for P&L]].

An unexpiry is constrained by trade reporting. Once a termination has been
reported, the status cannot simply return to live; a cancellation of the
termination is sent instead.

** States

The option's state machine needs states between /live/ and /exercised/ or
/expired/: waiting for a decision, decision pending, delivery pending. Passing
the expiry date and cut without a decision moves the option to /waiting for a
decision/. A holder-determined option is not treated as expired for settlement
until the holder has acted. For valuation it is treated as exercised or expired
according to its moneyness. An option past expiry that is neither expired nor
exercised is an invalid state, and is reported.

** Late events

- Booking a trade that has already expired is sometimes needed, for example
  after a novation, and requires authorisation.
- An amendment after maturity or expiry is a post-trade amendment, and is a
  control in its own right.

** Expected behaviour by product

| Product | At expiry |
|---------+-----------|
| European vanilla | Exercise before expiry is refused; exercise at expiry books the exercise trade; expiry books nothing; passing the cut without a decision moves it to waiting |
| European vanilla with a knock-in | Must have knocked in, continuously or at discrete observations, before it can be exercised |
| European vanilla with a knock-out | Exists at expiry only if it has not knocked out |
| American digital paying at maturity | Pays cash at expiry, for delivery at spot from expiry |
| European digital | Pays its rebate from a spot fixing, through fixing management |

Barrier products have expected end states for the triggered and the untriggered
path, with or without a rebate; see [[id:6CAED5F9-21CB-4300-B1B0-89599CFBFC7C][FX Barrier Option]].

** Exchange-traded options

An exchange-traded FX option exercises into a futures contract. The exchange
supplies settlement prices and a status extract at the end of the day, and
exercises options in the money by at least one pip. The system replicates the
exchange's rule so that risk is seen at once, expires before the end of the day,
and reconciles against the exchange's files.
