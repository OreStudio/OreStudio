* Goal

The route is the only consumer record. The ingest reads the routes, and the feed binding is gone from the model, the code, the tables, the screen, the permissions and the widening guard. No migration and no compatibility layer is added: the database is recreated.

* Acceptance

- The ingest reads the consumers from the routes only, and the marketdata service and core tests pass.
- No file under projects/ores.marketdata, projects/ores.shell or projects/ores.web names a feed binding, and the build, the web typecheck and the web tests pass.
- The binding tables, triggers, policies, event mapping, permissions, menu card and generated artefacts are gone from the create and drop scripts, and the widening guard no longer lists a binding policy.
- A recreated database provisions a tenant and still produces ticks from its routes.
