* Goal

The grid's global view answers which tenant is using what, from what the installation observed about its own operation. No policy is widened, no cross-tenant read is added, and no tenant's content leaves its tenant.

* Acceptance

- The compute service writes a usage row per completed result, carrying the tenant, the host, the app version, the duration, the cores and the outcome, at the moment it already acts inside that tenant.
- A usage row carries no tenant content: not the input bundle, the config, the output, the stderr or the report definition.
- The super administrator reads the whole ledger under a permission, and can answer which tenant used what over any window the retention keeps.
- The grid's load and capacity screens draw per-tenant usage from the ledger, and the work counts they state are true rather than a slice.
