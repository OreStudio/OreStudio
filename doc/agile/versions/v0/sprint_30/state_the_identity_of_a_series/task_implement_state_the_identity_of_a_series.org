* Goal

market_series_identity is one wide table of nullable columns with one shape per instrument class, and nothing states which fields a class requires: the requirement lives in the code that parses an oresmd URI, so a new series can be written sparse and nothing refuses it until a consumer far downstream says it has no series. Declare the shape per class as data beside the classification rules, enforce it where identity is written, and name the class and the field on refusal. Note that identity_kind covers both FX and IBOR fixings: the family is asset_class, not key_kind.

* Result

- The shapes are data: =ores_marketdata_series_identity_shapes_tbl= names each
  family by its asset class, series subclass and index family, and
  =ores_marketdata_series_identity_shape_entries_tbl= states each field the
  family requires or forbids. Seeded: =ir.index_fixing.ibor=,
  =ir.index_fixing.swap=, =ir.yield=, =fx.index_fixing.fx=, =fx.spot=,
  =fx.forward= and =fx.volatility=.
- The writer refuses: the identity projector checks every row against the
  shape book (=identity_shape_book::check=) and =check_identity_kind=, and
  the refusal names the series, the family and the field. A family no shape
  states is written and reported, not refused.
- Every FX family requires =ccy= and =unit_ccy=. The IBOR fixing requires
  =ccy=, =index= and =index_name= and forbids =unit_ccy=.
