* Acceptance

- Every pair the ladder carries and the derived source can quote has a surface blueprint: 35 of
  the 39. USD/CLP and USD/COP wait for the derived source to read a second publisher, and
  EUR/CZK and USD/TRY wait for a smile that stays positive in a short window.
- Every blueprint's grid names thirteen expiries, 1W to 10Y, and the ladder publishes a row for
  each of them.
- A fresh tenant provisions through every step.

* Plan

What bounds the surface set today, measured on a freshly provisioned environment:

1. The catalogue. The system tenant holds ten blueprints, seeded by
   =projects/ores.seeder/datasets/fx_vol_surfaces/generate_sql.py= from a fixed =PAIRS= list of
   ten majors. Provisioning copies those ten into each party. Nothing else limits the count.
2. The quotes. The derived source's universe is its vendor inventory, seeded by
   =projects/ores.sql/populate/marketdata/marketdata_derived_catalogue_populate.sql= from the
   same ten pairs and eight expiries (1W to 2Y), so it publishes 10 x 8 x 5 = 400 quotes.
3. The horizon. A surface's grid expiry must be a tenor the forward ladder publishes a row for.
   The ladder's standard tenors end at 5Y, so 5Y is the ceiling today; beyond it the calibration
   refuses the expiry by name, which is the rule the previous task established.
4. The discounting. Not a constraint: the funding curves bootstrap pillars to 3M, 6M, 12M, 2Y,
   5Y, 10Y and 30Y, so a forward is priced at 10Y as readily as at 6M.
5. The forwards. Not a constraint either: 39 pairs already publish a forward set for every party.

Steps:

- Add 3Y, 7Y and 10Y to the ladder's standard tenors, so the grid can reach 10Y. 10Y is where the
  market convention stops (FX volatility is quoted to 10Y); the curve pillars reach 30Y, so the
  forward side is not what would stop a 30Y grid, the quote convention is.
- Extend the derived source's inventory to the same pairs and expiries.
- Extend the seeder's =PAIRS= to the 39 pairs that carry a ladder, each with its own convention's
  short-dated delta type, and its =EXPIRIES= to the ladder's list.
- Regenerate the dataset SQL, recreate the database, provision a tenant, and check that every
  party has a surface for every pair, with the 10Y grid expiry present and the fit converged.

Open question for the pairs whose convention switches delta type at 2Y (USD/JPY, EUR/CZK and the
rest): the blueprint states one delta type for its output strikes while the calibration resolves
the pair's convention per expiry. A grid that reaches past the switch states a strike type the
long expiry does not use. To be settled while implementing.

* What is done

- The ladder publishes 3Y, 5Y, 7Y and 10Y through the same rule, and a test states those dates.
- The blueprint catalogue carries 35 pairs over thirteen expiries from 1W to 10Y; the derived
  source's own expiry list and inventory say the same.
- CAD/JPY and GBP/CAD, which the ladder carried and reference data had no FX option convention
  for, now have one.
- The official adapter's backfill is 3650 days, which is the parser's own maximum and what a
  ten-year expiry's return window needs.

* Result

Every pair the ladder carries and the derived source can quote now has a surface, and every
surface's grid reaches 10Y. A fresh Acme tenant provisions through all 17 steps: each of the five
parties holds 35 blueprints over thirteen expiries, and each business party publishes 29 surfaces.

The other six are held back by the publish gate, which is its job and not a failure: AUD/CHF,
EUR/GBP, GBP/USD, NZD/USD and USD/MXN each have one pillar just past the fit tolerance (0.0010 to
0.0025), and EUR/TRY breaks the butterfly condition at 1W.

Four of the 39 ladder pairs are left out, each for a reason with its own owner:

- USD/CLP and USD/COP have no ECB fixing, and the derived source reads only the ECB. They come
  back when the derived source reads a pair's levels from whichever source quotes it.
- EUR/CZK at 1W and USD/TRY at 3M never read a quote, because the Gram-Charlier smile turns
  negative in the wing; a surface missing one expiry is refused whole. Captured as
  [[id:705E8595-D6FF-45C5-9390-6AAFF53741BA][A short window's Gram-Charlier smile turns negative in the wing]].

The blocker this task first hit, the derived source failing to warm a tenant in time, is gone: the
one-pass derivation (#4750) answers the blueprint step well inside its wait, and the first party
in the profile now gets its blueprints like the other three.

No component changed shape: the change is a catalogue, a dataset, one adapter argument and three
rows of the ladder's tenor list.
