* Decisions

- The trade model declares columns, and the generator emits the filter members. No hand-written filter code.
- Only columns of the trade itself are declared. The identifier, state and book live in child tables and stay in the backlog capture.

* Out of scope

- Search by identifier, filter by state and book, enum column filters and child-table reads. See the backlog capture.

* Result

The trade list filters by counterparty and trade type, and searches and sorts by trade type, through the standard filter record. A repository test covers each, with the total.
