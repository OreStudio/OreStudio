The decide a cut's authorisation, and the apply: live evidence
================================================================

Every command below ran against the development environment `eager_maxwell` on
2026-11-01, with the reporting service built from this branch (commit 6d50987ca6
plus the subject read) and the web service serving the rebuilt BFF. The account
that takes the close is henry.jones; the Finance second person who decides it is
rachel.byrd.


1. The body cannot be frozen here, and why
------------------------------------------

The take raises a cut's authorisation only for a whole body, and the market-data
part of the body is the part this environment cannot fill:

    select count(*) from ores_marketdata_curve_publishes_tbl
      where valid_to = ores_utility_infinity_timestamp_fn();
    -- 0

    select s.oresmd_uri, count(*)
    from ores_marketdata_market_observations_tbl o
    join ores_marketdata_market_series_tbl s on s.id = o.series_id
    where o.valid_to = ores_utility_infinity_timestamp_fn()
    group by 1 order by 2 desc limit 3;
    -- oresmd://fx/USD?...&instrument=fx_spot&quote=rate&ccy=INR | 25
    -- oresmd://fx/NZD?...&instrument=fx_spot&quote=rate&ccy=USD | 25
    -- oresmd://fx/USD?...&instrument=fx_spot&quote=rate&ccy=ZAR | 25

Every observation the store holds is an FX spot, and the one seeded IR curve
bootstrap config (USD-SOFR-FOMC, d0b1e2f3-4a5b-4c6d-8e7f-9a0b1c2d3e4f) belongs to
the system party and points at a series with no observations at all, so neither
`publish_curve` nor `republish_curve` can produce a set. With no published set
there is no whole body, and a take of an incomplete body raises no request. That
is the same wall the take task recorded, and it is why the decide below is proven
against a request the inbox itself raised, and the apply against the rows the
suite wrote.

3. Four eyes refuses the person who asked
-----------------------------------------

    compass shell -l tmp/evidence/decide_four_eyes.sh

    > approval_operations decide-approval 01a19810-f11f-739b-90f8-19c51e4209ad \
        approve "I will sign my own body" __none__ --version 1
    {"result":{"outcome":"conflict","code":"four_eyes",
      "message":"You asked for this request, so someone else decides it.",...}}

The refusal is the inbox's rule and the cut adds nothing to it. (A call with no
`--version` answers `conflict` on the optimistic lock first, which is the other
rule: a decision states the version it read.)

5. The requests as the store holds them
---------------------------------------

    select r.id, r.state_code, a.username as asked_by, u.username as decided_by,
           d.decision_code, d.comment, d.decided_at
    from ores_inbox_approval_requests_tbl r
    join ores_iam_accounts_tbl a on a.id = r.requested_by ...
    left join ores_inbox_approval_decisions_tbl d on d.request_id = r.id ...
    left join ores_iam_accounts_tbl u on u.id = d.decided_by ...

    01a19810-f11f-739b-90f8-19c51e4209ad | approved  | henry.jones | rachel.byrd
      | approve  | The body is the day's close   | 2026-11-01 17:03:41.451335+00
    01a19811-30b3-74ae-8bd6-b7bd7b3e43f9 | refused   | henry.jones | rachel.byrd
      | refuse   | __none__                      | 2026-11-01 17:03:49.760704+00
    01a19811-5ac0-76bb-8db8-e780d007b237 | withdrawn | henry.jones | henry.jones
      | withdraw | The body will be retaken ...  | 2026-11-01 17:04:07.58823+00

The `__none__` is the shell's sentinel for an empty argument, and it is the one
wart in this evidence: the shell's command grammar drops an empty quoted token, so
it cannot state a refusal with no comment. The record's rule that a refusal needs
one is pinned by the suite (`a_refusal_needs_a_comment`, and
`a_refused_cut_authorisation_keeps_the_reason_and_admits_no_second_answer`).

7. The apply, against the store
-------------------------------

The reconciliation runs in the service: on every change to an approval request,
at start, and every sixty seconds.

    journalctl --user -u ores.reporting.service-eager_maxwell | grep reconciler

    [INFO] [ores.reporting.service.app.cut_authorisation_reconciler]
      Cut authorisation reconciliation started. Sweeping every 60s

For the requests above it answers nothing, which is correct — no request names a
body:

    select count(*) from ores_reporting_unapplied_cut_authorisations_fn()
    where tenant_id = '2b146b3a-0317-4711-8f17-275d5ca199fb';
    -- 0

The stamp itself is visible in the same store, written by the same code when a
body existed. The apply's cases run against the development database in a tenant
the suite provisions, so the rows they leave are the apply's own work:

    select c.id, c.approval_request_id, c.approved_by, c.approved_at,
           c.refused_at, d.applied_by, d.applied_at, d.deal_count
    from ores_reporting_cuts_tbl c
    join ores_reporting_cut_approval_details_tbl d on d.cut_id = c.id
    where c.approved_at is not null or c.refused_at is not null;

    b27bff96-... | 01a19800-918e-7fd8-9a1f-03426488915e | cut-decider-15929128
      | 2026-11-01 16:45:36.791996+00 | (null)

The applied instant is the decision's own, and it equals the cut's stamped
instant, so a repeated run cannot move it. The cut's own history shows the act:

    select id, approval_request_id, approved_by, approved_at, modified_by,
           change_commentary, version
    from ores_reporting_cuts_tbl
    where id = 'b27bff96-f5bc-410b-9cff-38e57a420a3c' order by valid_from;

    b27bff96-... | 01a19800-918e-... | (empty)            | (null)
      | cut-taker-15929128  | The day is closed and the reports wait on the body | 1
    b27bff96-... | 01a19800-918e-... | cut-decider-15929128
      | 2026-11-01 16:45:36.791996+00 | cut-decider-15929128

Version 1 is the take, version 2 is the apply, and the commentary names the
request, so the record's History tab shows the decision beside the take.


8. What a reader should conclude
--------------------------------

The decide is proven live, on the cut's own kind, with the inbox's four eyes and
its lifecycle, and through the screens' own reads. The apply is proven against the
store by its own rows and by the service's log, and against the same store by the
suite for the path from a decided request to a stamped cut. What remains unproven
live is the one step the environment cannot host: a take that freezes a whole body
in the demo tenant, which needs a published curve set that no component here can
publish.
