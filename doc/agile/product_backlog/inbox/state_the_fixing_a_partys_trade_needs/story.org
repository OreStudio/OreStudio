* Goal

A trade floats on an index, and its coupon needs the fixing of the day before the
valuation date. The catalogue now names the administrator of every fixing, and the
simulator states the fixings of the administrators that no public adapter serves.

Two gaps remain, and both stop a swap from pricing. The overnight indices whose
administrator also has a public adapter have no producer in a run, because that
adapter is not installed in the release. And a fixing is derived from a party's
spot series by cancelling one base against the other, so a party routed to several
vendors' spots carries several bases, nothing cancels, and every fixing is
dropped.

When the story closes, a party's overnight swap prices from the fixing its index
states, and the reason a fixing is missing is a fact the run states.

* Acceptance

- A provisioned synthetic tenant holds the fixings of ESTR, SONIA and SOFR, each
  under its administrator, or the story records which of them a run does not state
  and why.
- A party routed to more than one vendor's spots holds the fixing of every pair it
  trades.
- An overnight swap prices from the fixing of its index, and a report of the run
  shows a non-zero NPV for it.
- No item has two producers in a run, and the source a party reaches is the one
  its route picks.

* Decisions

- *A fixing is keyed by the index and the pair it belongs to.* The index names
  what is fixed. The party's whole spot book must not decide whether a fixing
  exists.
- *The simulator states an overnight index it stands in for.* The public adapters
  for the Bank of England and the New York Fed are not installed, and a synthetic
  run must price a swap without them. The administrator is unchanged: the value is
  simulated, and its producer kind says so, as
  [[id:142FDA27-36E6-4C0D-95F2-6FAE223FC5E3][Split the publisher of a market
  data value from its producer]] establishes.

* Out of scope

- An adapter that reads the Bank of England or the New York Fed over the network.
  That is its own task, wanted when a run prices from live sources.
- The vocabulary and the producer model, which the story above owns.
