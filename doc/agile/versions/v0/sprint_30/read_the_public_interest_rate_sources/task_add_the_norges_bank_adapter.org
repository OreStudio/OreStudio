* Goal

NOWA is stored as fixings in the system tenant, with its history, read from Norges Bank through the SDMX API, =data.norges-bank.no/api/data/SHORT_RATES/B.NOWA.ON.R=, in CSV. The adapter is specialised: it reads Norges Bank alone, and it supplies only its fetcher to the shared half of the official adapter.

* Acceptance

- The adapter reads Norges Bank alone, and it does not copy the announce, the demand, the backfill or the publish code.
- Norges Bank is a supplier in the catalogue, its series maps to the NOK-NOWA item, and a session and a feed scoped to that item are seeded in the system tenant.
- A fresh environment stores the history as fixings within minutes of provisioning, and the log names the source and the count it stored.
- Each fixing is the decimal of the published percent, with the credit Source: Norges Bank.
- The depth of the history is measured and written into the calibration sources note.
- Unit tests parse a recorded response, and a malformed response gives an error, not a fixing.
