* Acceptance

- The screen lists the visible series and fills the identity form from the chosen row.
- The person can still type an identity.
- An empty list and a refused read each read as what they are, in three languages.

* Plan

Add the series list to the contract and the api. Add a =SeriesPicker= to the evolution page, above the identity form: a
select of the visible series, named by their four words and their fields. A series with no declared shape is listed and
cannot be chosen. Choosing a series fills the form from it and keeps its party, so the read names that series. Typing in
the form clears the pick and the party, so a typed identity asks across the parties the person sees. An empty list, a
cut list, a refused read and a read in progress each have their own words in three languages. Prove it by parsing the
real lists of two parties with the new schema.

* Result

The evolution screen offers a picker. It lists the series the signed-in party can see, each named by its asset class,
scope, instrument type, quote type and the fields that tell it apart. Choosing one fills the identity form and keeps the
series' party, so the snapshot and the evolution read name that series and no other party's. The person can still type an
identity, and typing clears the pick and the party.

A series that declares no shape is listed and cannot be chosen, and its option says "no shape declared". An empty list says
that the party sees no series and that market data seeded by provisioning may not be listed until its identity is
projected. A list cut at the cap says so. A refused read shows the server's words, and a read in progress says so. The
earlier note that no list exists is gone. All messages are in English, French and Portuguese.

Real lists parse with the new schema. As the System Party the picker offers six volatility surfaces. As Acme Corporation
Plc it lists thirteen FX spot series and offers none, because a spot rate has no grid; this is correct and it may look
empty until a person reads the option text.

*What was not done.* The picker has not been looked at in a browser. It is tested with rendered markup and canned lists,
and the real lists were parsed with its schema.
