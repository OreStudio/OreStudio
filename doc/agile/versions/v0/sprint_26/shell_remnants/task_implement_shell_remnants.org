* Acceptance

- The fourteen hand-written trading instrument units are gone, and every verb they answered is answered by a generated unit, or its removal is recorded with its reason.
- accounts sessions answers against the live fleet, with the parse defect fixed at its source.
- bootstrap_smoke.ores runs twice in a row without colliding on the tenants it creates.
- The hand-written accounts verbs that duplicate a capability the generated authorization menu already carries are resolved, and the decision is recorded per verb.
- The iam S01 accepted exception states what the registry now enforces, and check_registry_exceptions.py still passes.

* Plan

Four pieces of work, in the order the standard asks for them: remove what
generation supersedes, fix what is broken, and correct the record.

** 1. Remove the fourteen hand-written trading instrument units

They live in =projects/ores.shell/application/src/app/commands/trading/= and
they are the shell surface of =ores.trading= entities. Measured against that
component's models:

| Unit | Model | Shell facet |
|------+-------+-------------|
| balance_guaranteed_swap_instrument, bond_instrument, callable_swap_instrument, cap_floor_instrument, fra_instrument, inflation_swap_instrument, knock_out_swap_instrument, rpa_instrument, swaption_instrument, trade, vanilla_swap_instrument | present | off |
| commodity_instrument, composite_instrument, credit_instrument, scripted_instrument | absent | n/a |

Their verbs are =get= (a paged list, not the canonical =get <key>=), =add=,
=delete= and =history=, which is a subset of what generation produces and with a
non-canonical spelling. =ore_commands.cpp= is not in this set: it is the ORE
document round trip, it models no entity, and nothing supersedes it.

Eleven of them need =:ores.cpp.shell-command.enabled: true= on their model and a
regeneration; four need a model first. Both are =ores.trading= work, and the
regeneration cannot be narrowed to the shell facet, so it carries that
component's drift. Remove the units only where the generated replacement exists,
and record each removal with the unit that replaces it.

** 2. Fix the three defects, at their source

=accounts sessions= aborts against the live fleet:

#+begin_src text
✗ Command failed: accounts sessions (from_iso8601_utc: missing UTC designator
  (Z, +00:00, or +00) in: 2026-09-27 12:15:14)
#+end_src

The session listing formats a timestamp the service returns in a shape the
parser refuses. Fix the parse or the producer, whichever is wrong, and prove the
verb answers against the live fleet.

=bootstrap_smoke.ores= is not idempotent: it creates the tenants =acme= and
=example=, so a second run collides. The recipe is tangled from
=doc/recipes/shell/provisioning/bootstrap_smoke.org=, so the fix is in the org
source.

A capability duplication, not a name collision. The generated =authorization=
menu already carries =assign-role=, =revoke-role=, =get-account-roles=,
=get-account-permissions= and =suggest-role-commands=, so the hand-written
=accounts= verbs of those names do the same job at a different address. Resolve
each one: retire the hand-written verb and point callers at the generated one,
or keep it and record why the addresses both earn their place.

** 3. Correct the iam record

=component_registry.py= carries an accepted exception on item S01 for =iam=
whose reason reads "registered after the generated aggregator, so some
overlapping verbs are shadowed". #2272 made that impossible: a second
registration throws, and the hand-written unit extends the generated menu. The
reason is rewritten to describe what the registry now enforces and what remains
open. This is a documentation change; no behaviour moves.
