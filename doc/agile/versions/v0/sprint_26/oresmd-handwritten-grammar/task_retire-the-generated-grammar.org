- The oresmd templates (=oresmd_*.mustache= and their literate =.org= sources) and their tests are deleted.
- The twelve oresmd quote-type models and =model.org= are deleted, with the =variant_order=, =parse_order= and =URI order= tables.

* Plan

Delete after the cut-over, so each deletion is proved by a green build. Two
units, one commit each, each ending in a build:

1. *The generated grammar.* The oresmd templates and their literate sources,
   the quote-type models and their manifest, the generated parser,
   projections, resolver, identifier and requirement types and their tests,
   the codegen loader for the =oresmd_quote_type= model type, and
   =check_oresmd_ore_coverage.py= with its tests.

   =ores.ore.series_key_shape= entity with its generated stack and SQL, the
   tenant provisioner's copy of its rows, the test seed, and the split fields
   on =ores.ore='s parsed datum.

* Notes

** What stays, and why

- =pillar_quote_key= stays in =ores.marketdata.core/oresmd/=. It is
  hand-written, and the curve republish service, the curve pillar reader and
  the synthetic IR curve feed use it.
- =asset_class_for_authority= stays; =asset_class_for_identifier= goes. The
  classifier reads the first, and nothing called the second.
- =market_data_urn.org= still describes the old grammar. Rewriting it is
  its own task in this story.

** One behaviour change

The registry refused a market data key with fewer than two segments, and the
refusal aborted the whole file. The file reader now keeps every line it can
tokenise, and the ORE key codec decides what a key names: a key it refuses is
reported and skipped, one row at a time, as every other refused key already
is.

** What moved

=classification_key_of= moved from the import service's anonymous namespace
to =series_classifier.hpp=, so the corpus classification test computes the
classification key the import computes, through the key codec, instead of
splitting keys with the registry.

** What the deletion simplified

- =manifest.py= scanned one level of subdirectories under =modeling/= and
  routed a directory holding =model.org= through it alone. The oresmd specs
  were the only such directory, so the scan now reads direct children only.
- =ores.ore= persists no tables, so =ore_create.sql= and =ore_drop.sql= are
  gone, and its data model diagram says so.

Depends on [[id:FE1151D5-5433-41C1-8F05-835818FEC1F9][Write the ORE index codec]]: after the cut-over, fixings are the
last production consumer of the generated grammar, through =from_index_name=
and =to_index_name= in the import, the export and the classifier.

The template has 2,585 lines and 187 tags; the shape table is a second
description of the same grammar. See [[id:6D60C52C-26B9-416B-A453-478EE5CA33C5][the 2026-10-02 review]], section 3.

* Result

The generated oresmd grammar and the key shape registry are gone, so the key
grammar lives in one place: the hand-written ORE key and index codecs. The
branch deletes 32,076 lines, 28,643 of them the generated grammar with its
templates, models and tests, and the rest the registry, its table and its
seed. The file reader keeps keys verbatim and the codecs decide what they
name; the corpus classification test checks the classification key the
import computes. =ores.ore= persists no tables and its api part is
header-only. Every acceptance item holds: the codegen suite, the drift gate
and the whole-tree build pass, and 90 of 90 test suites pass on a recreated
database.
