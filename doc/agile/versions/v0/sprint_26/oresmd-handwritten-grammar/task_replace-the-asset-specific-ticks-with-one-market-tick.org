* Goal

Every live market data feed, whatever its asset class, publishes and ingests one tick type keyed by the oresmd quote URI, and every feed takes its owner from one source.

* Acceptance

- fx_spot_tick and ir_curve_tick are deleted; one modelled market_tick carries the oresmd quote URI, the value, the observation time and the source.
- Every feed takes tenant, workspace and party from its feed binding; no tick carries an owner.
- The ingest loop reads the URI with oresmd_uri_codec and classifies the datum with classification_key_of; it no longer rebuilds an ORE key from parts or reads a classification off the wire.
- P02: marketdata.v1.tick is declared once, by the market_tick model, and every use of it, the republish subject builder included, references the generated constant.
- The republished subject is =marketdata.v1.tick.<tenant>.<workspace>.<party>.<key>=, where =<key>= is the canonical ORE key of the tick's datum, lower-cased with its slashes turned to dots, written by one builder that both the ingest loop and the client use.
- A tick finds its consumers by =source= through the enabled feed bindings of that source, for every feed; a tick from a source with no enabled binding is dropped with one warning per source.
- =market_tick.value= is decimal text, as stored observations are.
- Tenant provisioning creates bindings for the IR curve generation configs as it does for the FX spot ones.
- The synthetic FX and IR curve feeds publish market_tick, and a live feed run stores observations for both.

* Plan

The wire changes producers and consumers together, so this is one pull request
of commits that each build:

1. *The tick.* Model =market_tick= in =ores.marketdata.operations=, with
   =:subject: marketdata.v1.tick=, and write the one republished-subject builder
   in the api against its generated constant.
2. *The binding.* A binding maps a source to the tenant, workspace and party
   that consume it. Its =oresmd_uri= and =asset_class= go: the tick names its
   own datum, and a curve source publishes many datums across several series.
   Its natural key becomes the party and the source.
3. *The ingest loop.* One path for every tick: find the source's enabled
   bindings, read the URI with =oresmd_uri_codec=, classify with
   =classification_key_of=, store the observation, republish under the builder's
   subject.
4. *The producers.* The synthetic FX and IR curve feeds publish =market_tick=.
   Tenant provisioning creates a binding for every feed, FX and IR alike.
5. *The deletions.* =fx_spot_tick=, =ir_curve_tick=, their JSON I/O, the two
   copies of the subject function, and the FX-only client subscription, which
   becomes a subscription to =market_tick=.
6. *Proof.* The fleet runs both synthetic feeds, and observations from both
   arrive under their datum URIs.
