#+title: A forward ladder can start from today, tomorrow or spot
#+description: Choosing the near date of a ladder's rows, and how forward-forward quotes generalise it.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

Each row of a ladder is a pair of dates, a near date and a value date. Choosing today, tomorrow or spot as the start sets the near date of the rows.

* Detail

The standard ladder starts from spot. A forward-forward quote, such as 3M against 6M, is the general case. Its points are the difference between the two outrights. The rates on a ladder from another start are the same curve read between other dates. This is the design ORE Studio adopts.
