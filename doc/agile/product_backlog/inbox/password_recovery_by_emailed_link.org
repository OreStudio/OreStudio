#+title: Password recovery by emailed link does not exist end to end
#+description: Nobody can recover a forgotten password. There is no request subject and no completion subject (the candidates are iam.v1.accounts.request-password-reset and iam.v1.accounts.complete-password-reset), no table stores a recovery token or its expiry, no screen in ores.web asks for a reset, and no client has a screen that completes one. The product owner set this as the design for Rescue access while accepting its prototype: a tenant administrator sends the link, and the colleague sets a password only they know, so an administrator-typed reset becomes a collapsed fallback rather than the principal action. The journey document journey_rescue_access.org carries the corrected steps, operations table and gaps. The delivery and token machinery this needs is captured separately as the tree cannot send an email; this capture is the flow itself: who may request a reset, what the token guarantees (single use, expiry, invalidation on password change), how a completed reset interacts with the lock state and open sessions, and the two screens (member-requested and administrator-sent) that were left undrawn.
