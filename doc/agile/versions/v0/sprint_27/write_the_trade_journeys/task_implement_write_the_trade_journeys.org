* Notes

** Policy this story follows

A journey is a use case. It names the goal, the actor, the reads and writes it
needs, and what is missing. It does not name a screen. The prototypes that serve
the journeys come in a later story, and they follow the UX cluster
([[id:00BA1689-B060-4BD8-BC46-67034D663B7F][UX]]):

- A trade is an entity, made of several kinds of record. Its screens follow the
  [[id:BD492939-118E-4B55-AE5B-300F7A1C3B41][Entity screen specification]]: one page per trade, one tab per aspect, an
  Edit button on each card, history as a timeline, and a Workflows menu in the
  header.
- Every journey in this story is served by those standard screens. One screen
  serves several journeys. No journey becomes a screen of its own.
- Booking is the one candidate for a guided sequence, because an instrument form
  depends on the trade type. A prototype decides that. It is not decided here.
- The Qt client was entry-centric: a trade list, a detail dialog, one form per
  instrument family. The prototypes keep the entry (the trade) as the subject and
  use the use cases to decide which cards and workflows the page holds.

** I01 to I03: operations, actors and subjects

operation here is system-administrator only. Permission codes are not yet

** I04: sensitive fields

No trade message carries a secret. The grep for password, secret and token in
the trading protocol headers matched only a comment about session tokens.

** I05: how trade records are stored

- The trade anchor is immutable. Party, counterparty, trade type and origin never
  change after booking.
- The booking and the state are temporal components keyed by the trade, so each
  has a history. The state moves by activity, through a finite state machine
  (=activity_type.fsm_transition_id=).
- An activity type says whether it is economic and whether it needs
  confirmation. An amendment is therefore a booked activity, not a bare write to
  the trade. The Amend journey must say so.
- Each instrument table is a one-to-one child of the trade, chosen by the trade
  type. Its child rows (legs, schedules, exercise prices) are owned parts. The
  trade page holds them as cards or panels, and does not give them screens.
- The economic digest folds the instrument and its parts into the trade's
  external version. Two parts are not folded yet: the option exercise prices and
  a composite trade's legs. They are filed as captures. The Amend journey names
  them as a dependency.

Qt mistakes the journeys must not repeat:

- A detail dialog that edited the whole trade at once. The new page edits one
  card at a time, with a reason.
- A hard delete of a trade. A live trade is cancelled by an activity.
- One window per lookup table.
- An instrument form that was missing for a trade type, and the dialog that only
  warned. The model must make a trade type with no card set impossible.

** Gaps found while reading

- No operation writes an activity together with the component versions it
  causes, except =book_trade=. An amendment or a lifecycle event is several
  writes today (activity, then components), with no shared transaction. The
  subject list above is the evidence: the only =ops.= subjects are
  =book_trade=, =export_portfolio= and =export_trades_to_storage=.
- =book_trade= writes the anchor, the booking and the first state. It does not
  write the instrument. A booked trade has no instrument until a second write.
  The Book journey needs one operation, or a defined order with a repair path.
- =trades.list= filters only by trade id, and =trade_activities.list= only by
  activity id. A trade search and a trade's activity history need new filters.
- Trade identifiers, party roles, portfolios, additional fields, links and instrument
  child tables (checked on =bond_leg=) have no list filter at all. A trade page needs
  a by-trade read for each. Only trade bookings, trade states and structure members
  filter by trade id.
- The BFF and the web app have no trading route or screen. Only the generated
  wire types exist in =wire-protocol=. (Checked with a search of =web/src= and
  =bff/src= for "trading": only locale strings, the home page and one inbox test
  match.)
- Option exercise prices and composite legs are outside the digest (two
  captures already filed).
- =BondForward= has no standalone round trip test. It appears only as the
  underlying of a Generic TRS golden file.

* Result

Seven use cases and a hub page exist under =doc/knowledge/journeys/trades/=, and the
group is on the journeys index. The inventory and the Qt dispositions are in Notes.

The reading found gaps that block every screen: no web route for trading, a trade
list that filters only by id, child tables with no by-trade read, no operation that
writes an activity with the versions it causes, and two digest gaps. They are on the
journey pages and in the hub. They are for the owner to plan; none is fixed here.
