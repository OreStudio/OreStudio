* Summary

=ores.storage= is ORE Studio's object storage: a bucket-and-key namespace of
opaque bytes, reachable over HTTP when a caller is moving a body and over NATS
when it is sending a command. It is a composite, and the three parts exist
because the contract, the implementation and the service have different
consumers and different dependencies.

- [[id:8778E5E5-2123-4E40-A16D-B13B16D1B7AD][ores.storage.api]] — the contract: the bucket and key path helpers, and the
  modelled operation surface both interfaces are generated from.
- [[id:9C11A981-EAA8-4AE2-AAEA-7FEE01982CE4][ores.storage.core]] — the implementation: the streaming HTTP client, the
  =tar.gz= archiver, and the atomic and composite transfer helpers.
- [[id:288678EF-EB55-4F89-9005-7B3532671C91][ores.storage.service]] — the process: it binds the operation set's subjects,
  authenticates the caller and answers them.

Storage holds no bucket name of its own, no registry and no allow-list: a
caller names its bucket, and which buckets exist is the caller's concern. The
target is stated in [[id:286B7167-9B8A-4937-BBA5-E9D285AFECE3][Object Storage]].
