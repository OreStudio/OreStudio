This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Find what =ores.compute.service= does while no work is queued, and stop it from
spending CPU then.

* Why

In a six-minute sample of the =brave_hopper= fleet on 2026-10-26, after the
tick runaway was fixed, compute used 3.7 percent of a core with no work queued.
That was the most of any service, ahead of synthetic at 2.1 percent and
marketdata at 0.9 percent. Ten environments on one machine multiply it.

* References

- The fleet sample was taken with systemd's =CPUUsageNSec= per unit.
