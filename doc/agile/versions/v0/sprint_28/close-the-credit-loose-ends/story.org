* Goal

Close the three gaps the credit rework left open. The ORE export keeps every
child element a document requires, so the corpus round trip reads back what it
wrote. The credit and CDS tenor convention resolves on the IMM roll schedule
instead of throwing. A netting set is keyed by the credit limit its analysis
names.

* Acceptance

- The directory round trip over =external/ore/examples/Products/Example_Trades= re-reads every document it writes. The seventeen documents that drop a required child element today, across Commodity, Credit, FX worst-of and Exotic, stop dropping it.
- =resolve_end_date()= resolves an =IMM_ROLL= convention from its roll schedule, and the CDS =tenor_convention_resolution= rows carry the roll-quarter count of each nominal tenor.
- A netting set carries the credit limit that its identity names.
