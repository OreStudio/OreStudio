* Goal

Every store that grows without bound is named, with what produces it, what it holds today, and which existing mechanism, if any, bounds it, so the story can decide one policy model that covers all of them.

* Acceptance

- Each dimension records its size today, the rate it grows, and the artefacts that write it
- The mechanisms already in the tree are inventoried, including Timescale retention policies, service sweeps and compute job folders, and the places that have none
- The concepts a configuration needs are proposed: policy, scope, target store, action, schedule and licence fallback
- The dimensions are ranked by what bounding them would save, so the remaining tasks can be ordered
- The findings are written as an investigation page under this story
