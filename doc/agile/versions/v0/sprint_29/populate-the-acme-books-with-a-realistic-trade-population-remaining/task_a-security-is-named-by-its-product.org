* Acceptance

- A security's name states its product, so a callable bond and a plain bond of one term name two securities and two issues.
- The bond family books, and a full provisioning run writes every family of the population.

* Plan

The name states the product as well as the currency, the anchor and the term.

A security's own rows are what make two products different securities: a
callable bond states the dates its issuer may call it, a convertible states what
it may convert into, and a plain bond of the same term states neither. The
store compares the issue it holds with the issue a second write states, and
refuses the second when they differ — which is the run's =Bond issue … is stored
with other terms=. So the product belongs in the name, and the pool of
securities becomes one per currency, term and product.

Two consequences are stated rather than left to be discovered:

- A term now carries as many securities as the family routes products to it.
  The case that asserted one security per term asserted the fault.
- A security is no longer shared between two books by construction. With an
  even product count and two books, the draw gives each book half the products,
  so no security is shared across books; the name is still a function of the
  security's own facts and of nothing about a book, which is what the case now
  states.
