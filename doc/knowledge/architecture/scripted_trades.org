* Summary

A scripted trade states its payoff as a script ORE interprets, not as one of
ORE's fixed trade elements. In ORE Studio a scripted trade is one of the forms a
product is booked in, and the subsystem around it answers five questions: what
a product offers to be booked as, how it is described, how a script is held and
trusted, how a trade is booked and validated, and how every product is proven to
book and value. Each answer is a page of its own; this page says which to read,
in what order. ORE's side of the module, the script language and where a script
may live, is [[id:2F5B28D9-9AB7-401E-8EF3-612712F626C1][Generic Scripted Products]]. The pages state the target the
[[id:E6359881-7919-4BBC-9B9C-03A8EEDB8A03][Design the scripted trade subsystem]] story builds to, and each names where today's code differs.
