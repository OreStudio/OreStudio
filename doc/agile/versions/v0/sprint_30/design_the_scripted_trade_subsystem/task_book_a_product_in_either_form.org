* Goal

The trading service books a product in any form it offers, and a free-form scripted trade, through book, validate_script and book_scripted; every scripted booking passes the server's validation, and a trade records its product and form.

* Acceptance

- book validates the terms against the schema and builds the native element, or the scripted form through the twin recipe or, for a script-only product, the script's inputs
- validate_script parses with ORE's grammar and checks the variables against the inputs by name and type, booking nothing
- book_scripted books a library script by version or an inline script, for a person permitted to book one
- a trade records its product and the form it was booked in, and reopens in that form
- the twin recipe evaluator lives in ores.trading.api
- validate_script returns the diagnostics as structured data, located by line and column, or by variable for a finding about the trade's variables
- a booking rejected by the server's validation carries the same diagnostics, and a validation that reports only warnings books
- ores.http serves book, book_scripted and validate_script as JSON routes, and a rejected booking returns its diagnostics in the body
- the BFF serves the same under /api/trading/booking, with a unit test of each route, and its booking reads the script pin the store sets

* Plan

Six PRs, each ending in a check:

1. =validate_script=: parse a library script, by name and version as the
   caller's tenant resolves it, or an inline script, with ORE's grammar, and check
   the trade's variables against its inputs; the findings are structured, by line
   and column or by variable, and warnings alone are valid.
2. The twin recipe evaluator moves from the synthetic generator into
   =ores.trading.api=.
3. A trade records its product and the form it was booked in, in a
   =trade_product= row of its own: the anchor is at its ceiling of fields, and
   neither fact follows from the trade (=VanillaBasketOption= is the scripted
   form of three basket options).
4. =book_scripted=: a library script by version or an inline script, refused
   with the validation's findings when they hold an error.
5. =book_product=: a product type, its terms and a form. Decided with the user
   on 2026-11-23: the terms are the product's native instrument rows, as
   =book_trade= takes them, not a generic field-by-field format mapped into each
   of the 115 native builders, which would be a task of its own. The native form
   books the rows as the product's element; the scripted form reads the product's
   twin recipe, or a carried script's, over them. The rows are checked to route to
   the trade type and to state the script's inputs; their values are not checked
   against the schema's enumerations, which hold their values only as text.
6. The =ores.http= and BFF routes under =/api/trading/booking=.

* Result

The trading service books a product in any form it offers, and a free-form
scripted trade, through one set of operations, and both servers serve them:

- =validate_script= parses a library script, as the caller's tenant resolves its
  name and version, or an inline script, with ORE's grammar, and checks the
  trade's variables, with findings located by line and column or by variable
  (PR #5062);
- the twin recipe evaluator lives in =ores.trading.api= (PR #5063);
- a trade records the product it was booked as and its form, in a
  =trade_product= row of its own (PR #5068);
- =book_scripted= books a library script by version or an inline script, refused
  with every finding when the validation holds an error; warnings alone book; an
  inline script needs the permission to write scripts (PR #5070);
- =book_product= books a product type in its native form, or in its scripted
  form through its twin recipe (PR #5071);
- =ores.http= and the BFF serve the operations under =/api/trading/booking=, and
  the form a trade was booked in, with the script version the store pinned (this
  PR).

One acceptance line is met in part, as decided with the user on 2026-11-23: the
terms of =book_product= are the product's native rows, checked to route to the
trade type and to state the script's inputs, not validated field by field
against the schema, whose enumerations hold their values only as text. PR #5072
repaired the web packages #5070 left unbuilt.
