* Goal

* Result

The routes and subscriptions policies hold the own-tenant shape again. A new model property, =rls_service_read_role=, makes the generator write one select-only policy =to :marketdata_service_user using (true)=, as the CRM configs already have by hand. The two temporary exceptions are gone from =check_rls_system_tenant_widenings.py=, which passes. A system-tenant session of any other role now reads no route of another tenant and writes none.

On a fresh database with the services started first and Acme provisioned after, ticks flow: 6890 rows in 130 series (5 parties, 26 pairs), none dated in the future. All pgTAP files pass, including the route visibility test (11 assertions, 5 of them on the policies and the role it names). Two defects came up on the way. The first policy name was longer than 63 characters, and Postgres cut it. The name now leaves the role out. A folder test in the synthetic feeds suite counted folders by name, so it broke once other providers had folders of the same names. It now follows the folder chain of one feed.
