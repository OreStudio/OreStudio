The workflow step that publishes the image assets from a DQ dataset. It is a
trigger, as the reporting and analytics components' publish steps are: the DQ
publisher sends it and reads no reply, so a step states a subject and no
response.

The subject is also the =target_subject= of the DQ artefact type that names it,
in =projects/ores.sql/populate/dq/dq_artefact_types_populate.sql=, and the
handler derives the SQL function it calls from the subject, so it is declared
once here and referenced at the call site.
