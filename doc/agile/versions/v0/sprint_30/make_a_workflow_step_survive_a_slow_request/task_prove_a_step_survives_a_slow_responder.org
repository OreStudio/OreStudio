* Goal

A test drives a provisioning step against a responder that answers after the first deadline and against one that refuses, and proves that the step retries the first, fails at once on the second, reports progress, and names the work in its failure.

* Acceptance

- A responder that misses the first deadline and answers the second time lets the step complete.
- A responder that refuses fails the step at once, with the server's reason.
- The test sees the progress lines the step published.
- The test fails if the retry repeats work that already happened.

* Plan

- Prove the request path at the helper, where the step's four behaviours live:
  a responder that misses the first deadline and answers the second, a
  refusal, the attempt notes, and a repeat that takes what an earlier attempt
  did.
- Prove the step path with a live run: =compass smoke ticks --leave-running=
  provisions =acme_corporation= with the =acme_demo= profile, follows the
  child run, and prints each step and its progress.
- Read the live output for the three lines this story promised: the progress
  line, the attempt line, and the failure line.

* Result

The request path is proven. Nine cases cover the four behaviours: a responder
that misses the first deadline and answers the second, a refusal thrown at
once, the attempt note that reaches the run's record, and a repeat that takes
what an earlier attempt produced and does not do the work twice.

The live run proves part of the step path and stops before the rest. On a
freshly recreated database, `compass smoke ticks --leave-running` provisioned
Acme to 15 of 15 planned steps and printed the heartbeat's progress:

#+begin_src text
  [11/9] give_party_surface_blueprints: in_progress
      progress: Acme Corporation Plc: curves with their quotes 3 of 20
      progress: Acme Corporation Plc: surface blueprints with their quotes 9 of 10
#+end_src

It then failed in =generate_trades=:

#+begin_src text
  [15/13] generate_trades: failed -- Writing bond issue POP5ZCJP1NEQ1 failed: std::bad_alloc
  Workflow failed at step 15 of 13: Writing bond issue POP5ZCJP1NEQ1 failed: std::bad_alloc
#+end_src

No trade was written, and the host had 29 GB available, so this is a fault of
the generator and not the host's memory. It is captured as
[[id:9232CDC9-8444-4856-8D3D-CA7CD4EFA5EC][Generating a bond issue fails with std::bad_alloc]] and is outside this
story, which keeps the trade generator's shape.

Because =generate_trades= failed first, =publish_party_surfaces= never ran in
this run, so the step-level proof of the surface step surviving a slow
calibration is still owed. The task stays open until that fault is cleared and
the run reaches it.
