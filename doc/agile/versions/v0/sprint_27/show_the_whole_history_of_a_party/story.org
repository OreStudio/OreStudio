* Goal

A party's history shows every change to the party and to everything attached to it: its identifiers, contacts, business units, countries, currencies and counterparties, each with who made it and why, and says nothing is missing because nothing is.

* Acceptance

- A history provider finds a row by its whole key, so the history of one party's contact is never another party's.
- A link table has a history read, a history provider and a field mapper from its model, and a closed link is still in its history.
- The party timeline shows the opening and closing of a country, a currency and a counterparty link, and states no gap for them.
- The same holds for any entity with a composite key or a link, not only parties.
