* What

=ores.scheduler.api.tests= fails on main since the =vcpkg= submodule update of
2026-10-05 (=978e1e098d=). The case "cron_expression::from_string rejects
invalid expressions" expects the six-field =* * * * * *= to be refused, and
=from_string= accepts it.

=from_string= prepends a seconds field before it calls =croncpp=, so a
six-field input reaches the library as seven fields. The newer =croncpp=
evidently accepts seven; that the extra field is read as a year is a guess, not
checked against its source.

* Why

A cron expression is five fields in this system. Without a field count of its
own, which inputs pass depends on the vendored library's version. The fix is to
require exactly five fields before the prepend, which keeps the test as it is.
