* Goal

Pricing reads the surface. A report binds to a published surface as it binds to a published curve, and the ORE export writes the published grid as ORE volatility quotes with the =fx_volatility_config= that describes the grid. See [[id:FB8F701C-E56A-49A9-AA90-1B6BD0B02092][A report binds to a published curve]] and [[id:53D14F31-EE32-493E-BBEB-140AA4906BE4][Constructing a volatility surface: from quotes to a published set]].

* Acceptance

- A report's market binding can name a published surface, and cannot name a candidate.
- The export writes each grid point as an ORE volatility quote and writes a matching =fx_volatility_config=.
- ORE loads the exported surface and prices an FX option from it.
- At the strikes of a test trade, ORE's interpolated volatility matches the calibrated surface within a tolerance the test states.
- A report binds only to a surface of its own party.

* Plan

Two parts.

*Part 1, merged in #4577.* The market data export writes a surface only as published: the newest published set at or before the close, each grid point an FX option volatility quote whose strike is a delta label (=DEL/Spot/Put/25= is =25P=, =ATM= stays =ATM=). A candidate is left out. A pair with a published surface takes its option quotes from the surface alone, because the pair's raw quotes share its keys. This also fixed a break: the calibration's points have no ORE key, so before it the export failed for any party with a calibration.

*Part 2.* The matching configuration and the ORE check.
- Each party's curve configuration document gains, for each blueprint with a published surface, an FX volatility curve of dimension Smile and smile type Delta: the blueprint's expiries, its deltas as =10P,25P,ATM,25C,10C=, the pair's ATM and delta types from its FX option convention, and the pair's FX spot and funding curves. The party's today's market names it under the pair.
- A scripted check runs the ORE binary of the ORE build (=App/ore=) on the exported quotes and that configuration with an EUR/USD FX option at a test strike, inverts the NPV to a volatility, and compares it with the SSVI surface at that strike within a stated tolerance. It sits beside =projects/ores.ore/scripts/quantlib_fx_dates=.
