Two operations a workspace serves that are not entity verbs. The resolution
chain of a workspace is computed, not stored: it walks =parent_workspace_id= up
to the Live workspace. The trade scope of a workspace is stored, but in a
whitelist table of its own rather than in the workspace row, so its verbs are
operations rather than writes to the entity.

Both handlers stay hand-written beside this model, because the resolution chain
calls a set-returning SQL function and the trade scope writes a table no entity
model describes. An operation model generates no service handler.

The remaining workspace verbs are the entity's own and generate from
[[id:B09B3A40-C58C-4918-AFD6-22CFF2B4E4C6][ores.workspace.workspace]].

* Physical space

The shell commands are switched off. These messages resolve and set the acting
workspace and its trade scope, which the service answers from the caller's session
rather than from a row, in the way the account menu's sign-in and sign-out change
this client's own state. The shell renders the =workspace= entity's unit.
