* Plan

The inventory lists 22 trading resource routes in four groups. Each group is
one pull request; the first also stands up the part.

| Group | Routes | BFF source |
|-------+--------+------------|
| The part and the trade reads | 3 | =trades.ts= |
| Booking | 5 | =booking.ts= |
| Structures | 8 | =structures.ts= |
| Books and book changes | 6 | =books.ts=, =book-changes.ts= |

The blotter and the book tree are composed views and belong to
[[id:350D91AF-A4E0-43B0-849E-8188345DA443][Serve the trading composed views]].

Decisions made in the first group:

- The part is stood up by the adapter recipe, copied from =ores.http.synthetic=:
  its model, its four CMake files, the composite's parts list, the umbrella
  header, the aggregate, the file lists, the diagram, and the server's link and
  registration. It links =ores.refdata.api= beside =ores.trading.api=, because
  a trade's rows are named by their book and counterparty.
- A page's rows are the summary rows with five members added: the identifier a
  desk quotes, the counterparty's and book's names, and the trade's versions.
  The members are added to the row's JSON, as the BFF spread them into the row,
  so a summary member the BFF passed on is not restated by hand.
- An enrichment read is skipped when the page names nothing for it, where the
  BFF sent an empty one-of list; an empty list's meaning is the service's to
  state, and skipping it reads nothing.
- The structures of a trade, its terms' issue and a structure's reference are
  supplementary: a read of them that is refused answers none, as the BFF's did.
