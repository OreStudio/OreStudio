* Notes

- The prototype is signed off. The product owner's condition: the history, and everything else on the screen, follow the UX standards. The counterparty is an entity made of several kinds of record, so the screen follows the [[id:BD492939-118E-4B55-AE5B-300F7A1C3B41][Entity screen specification]] and the [[id:2BF7F03F-6BF4-45FF-8E26-4D3B4E2D943D][Record screen standard]]:
  - The history tab is the shared history panel, the timeline with the comparison of any two versions and the revert dialog. The screen does not build its own table, as the prototype's does.
  - The tabs are held in the address, so a link reopens a record on its tab. A tab that applies but holds nothing says "None yet."
  - The form, the details and the write read one field table. The Details tab ends with the provenance of the current version.
  - A person who wrote a version or a request is a link with their photo, as everywhere else.
  - The list pages and sorts on the server, and every state (loading, empty, refused) has words.
- The screen waits for three tasks: [[id:A2F9C3A9-63A8-4E49-AD3A-D709A635DA2F][Generate the pending change for the counterparty]], [[id:7C965B73-1358-43C6-B6EB-D6B30415DE08][Raise, preview and apply counterparty changes]] and [[id:1D795DBF-FD05-4E48-A693-25DE97104E1F][Gate the counterparty writes behind the policy]]. They are filed with this change.
- The product owner ruled that the screen is an entity screen like the parties screen, not the onboarding journey. Onboarding is a workflow. The prototype is =keep-a-counterpartys-details-current= under the parties group.
- The server must gate the counterparty first. Only the book is gated today. The counterparty needs the chain the book had: its model marked gated, a pending change, a preview and raise, and an apply. These are tasks of their own, to be filed when the prototype is agreed.
