* Goal

Add trade_activity with its type and participants, the act detail tables, and trade_link with its type, so every component version names its cause and every relation between trades is typed and directed.

* Acceptance

- Every component version carries a foreign key to the trade_activity that caused it; the activity type states the economic, confirmable and real axes and the priority.
- trade_activity_participant names each trade and its role; compression, merge, allocation, runs, bulk book move and import have detail tables keyed by the activity.
- trade_link joins two trades with a type from trade_link_type, which names both roles and the economic effect; successor_trade_id is gone.
