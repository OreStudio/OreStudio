* Goal

Hedgr prototyped five market-data screens for its own terminal. ORE Studio has no instrument search, no rate ladder and no FX spot montage, and reads a curve and a surface only as a candidate or a publish record. This story brings the five screens into doc/prototypes/market_data, adds the journeys they answer, and adds the server operations the screens read, so both clients draw the same facts and neither derives an identity, an axis order or a staleness verdict.

* Decisions

** What each hedgr prototype became

Checked against the hedgr prototypes in =/home/marco/Development/Hedgr/hedgr=
and the market-data prototypes in =doc/prototypes/market_data=, on 2026-11-13.

| hedgr prototype | ORE Studio before | Verdict |
|-----------------+-------------------+---------|
| =explorer_search.html= | No screen searches every instrument kind; a curve, a surface and a pair each have their own list | *Missing*: new prototype =find-an-instrument= and journey |
| =rates_ladder_providers.html= | No rate ladder. =fx-forward-ladder= reads one pair's published forwards; =public-rates= compares public publishers | *Missing*: new prototype =read-a-rate-ladder= and journey |
| =fx_spot_montage.html= | No spot montage. =fx-forward-ladder= is forwards of one pair | *Missing*: new prototype =read-the-fx-montage= and journey |
| =aud_aonia_curve_dialog_prototype.html= | =published-curves= shows a point's value and discount factor and =check-a-curves-fit= checks a candidate's residuals; neither states the curve's conventions, zero rate, implied forward or shift | *Missing as a read*: new prototype =inspect-a-curves-analytics= and journey |
| =vol_surface_smile.html= | =inspect-a-surfaces-slices= reads a *candidate* surface's smiles, mesh and arbitrage; nothing reads a *published* surface | *Missing as a read*: new prototype =read-a-published-surface= and journey |

** Taken wholesale, or improved

- =find-an-instrument=: taken wholesale. The row shape, the kind badges and the
  Enter destinations survived unchanged; only the chrome became ORE Studio's.
- =read-a-rate-ladder=: taken with one deletion and one addition. Hedgr's
  History tab has no read behind it and is not ported; the thin-market list is
  added, because a gap on a ladder is the thing a person explains.
- =read-the-fx-montage=: taken minus the Forwards tab, because the forward
  ladder already has a page and a journey. The changed window is stated as a
  fact, which hedgr left implicit.
- =inspect-a-curves-analytics=: taken with three changes forced by how ORE Studio
  stores a fact. An observation holds one value, so Bid/Ask/Spread become one
  market rate and its provider; the dialog becomes a page; and =shift= is split
  into curve-less-market and the move against the prior close, which hedgr
  conflated under one column.
- =read-a-published-surface=: taken. =model=, =strike convention= and =delta
  type= are promoted into the facts row, because a column called =25D put= is
  not a vol without them.

** What was deliberately not taken

- Hedgr's dialog chrome (collapse, reset, drag, always-on-top) and its per-screen
  acting-party selector. The product draws the party once in its shell.
- Hedgr's client-side derivations (URI parsing, label building, curve grouping,
  tenor ordering, staleness thresholds). Those are the point of the server work
  that follows, and the journeys record each one as a missing read.
