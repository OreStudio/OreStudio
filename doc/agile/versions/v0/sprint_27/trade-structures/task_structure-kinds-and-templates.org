* Result

=structure_kind= states the four rungs of the composition ladder and which of
them confirms as a whole. =structure_template= names its kind from that
catalogue, and =structure_template_role= states each role's leg counts, with
the counts checked and a maximum of zero meaning no upper bound.

The seed carries the four rungs, the four common strategies the composition
page names plus one typed product, and nine role rows. The nine handler
permissions are seeded too, which the previous task's entities were missing.

The task closed on static validation alone, with its nine pgTAP cases written
and never executed, and the seed data verified only by inspection. A follow-up
ran them against a rebuilt database: all nine pass, so the seed and the
template and role constraints are now proven by execution.

No build and no ctest ran on this task. The hourly build and test on main
belongs to swift curie.
