* Acceptance

- Every local check in compass check --all --exclude-heavy passes on the branch, except the sandbox-bound compass suite.
- The macOS and Windows builds no longer fail on from_chars or getenv.

* Plan

Fix each failing gate at its source rather than at the gate. Regenerate
what drifted from its generator (ER diagrams, shell script library, recipe
inventory, backlog index), correct the model and the policy the checks
flag (the IAM profile binding, the counterparty-change party policy,
the synthetic initial rate, the feed-placement writer), and bring the
stale BFF timeline test up to date. Port the two platform breaks through
the shims the estate already uses: =ores.platform::numeric::parse_double=
for the floating-point parse, and =ores.platform::environment= for the
environment read. Prove it with the fast gate set on the branch.
