* Acceptance

- A report over a provisioned Acme, covering every generated trade type, completes through the grid, with every trade in =npv.csv=, a finite value and no Trade errors.
- *Correct is measured against ORE:* for each type, a trade the builder makes with the terms of ORE's sample of that type, on the sample's market, gives the NPV ORE gives for the sample, within a stated tolerance.
- The reference is ORE's =ExpectedOutput/npv.csv= where one exists (25 of the 85 types on 2026-11-09), and otherwise a run of ORE on its own sample of the type.
- A type with no ORE sample is listed with its reason, and is held to a stated plausibility bound, an absolute NPV against its notional.
- A tagged test repeats the comparison, so a change to the generator that moves a value fails.
- Every difference found is fixed at its cause in the builder, the market data or the export. The known lists of the recorded trades end empty, or with a stated reason per entry.

* Plan

Run the report as [[id:0A7DA00D-EA4E-4EA6-B597-6186A76A9F9E][How do I prove a report runs end to end?]] describes, then compare =npv.csv=. Report each type as a row: built, valued, reference NPV, our NPV, difference.
