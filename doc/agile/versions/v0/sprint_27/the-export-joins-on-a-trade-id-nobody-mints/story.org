instrument, so the ORE corpus imports stop resolving a trade onto another
trade's instrument.

* Decisions

- *Mint in the two builders that bypass the planner, and leave the importer
  alone.* The importer leaving ids unset is a contract
  =xml_trade_import_tests= asserts; the planner is what fills them on the
  import path. The roundtrip walk and the exporter tests have no planner, so
  they mint their own, mirroring what it does.

* Out of scope

- The importer's nil-id contract.
- =trade_export_item.anchor.id= being the join key while the batch is keyed by
  =trade_id=. The two are made equal here; whether one should be derived from
  the other is a change of its own.

* Result

Done. =xml::roundtrip()= stamps a minted id onto each instrument, and the
exporter tests share a =make_export_payload= helper with their assertions
unchanged.
