* What

=projects/ores.workflow/core/src/messaging/registrar.cpp= includes the APIs of
=ores.dq=, =ores.ore=, =ores.refdata= and =ores.reporting= and calls their
=register_*_workflow(registry)= functions. The engine therefore cannot build
without four components that should depend on it, not the other way round.

Moving payloads to their owners does not fix this. The engine owns the
=workflow_registry=, so a commissioner registers only when the engine calls it,
and the definitions are C++ lambdas that must compile into the engine's process.
The fix is to make a workflow definition data -- its steps, subjects and
compensations -- which a model of the workflows would produce, so the engine
loads definitions instead of linking them.

* Why

The ores.workflow clean-up recorded this in its P04 detail ("A second problem
the first one hides") as "a finding for its own work item", and closed without
filing one.
