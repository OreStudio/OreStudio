* Result

** Outcome

A feed's scope is its rows in =feed_scopes=: feed, asset class, item (an oresmd
URI) and optionally the vendor instrument that carries it. The four public
feeds declare every mapping of their publisher: 140 rows across ECB, BoC, IMF
and TCMB.

A hand-written trigger refuses an item that already belongs to another feed of
the same publisher, and a vendor instrument that belongs to another publisher.
The same item under a different publisher is allowed, which is what makes the
invariant about one publisher's feeds rather than the whole catalogue.

The entity is wired into the service: handlers, event mapping, history provider
and shell unit, with its permissions. A read returns the scope of a feed by
=feed_id=.
