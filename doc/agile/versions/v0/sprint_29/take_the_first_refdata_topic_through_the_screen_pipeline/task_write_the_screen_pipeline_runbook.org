* Plan

Write the runbook from what the four tasks of the calendars topic did, not
from what was planned: each step names its task shape, the recipe or page it
follows, the document it produces, who signs it off and where the sign off is
recorded, and a section carries what the topic taught that the next must know.
Scaffold the next topic's story, currencies and pairs, with the same four tasks
so the runbook is used before it is forgotten, and wire the runbook into the
catalogue and the story into the sprint's epic.

* Result

The runbook Take a topic through the screen pipeline states the five steps
the calendars topic walked: journeys judged and signed off, prototype drawn
and signed off, screens built through the shared parts with harness checks
and three languages, scenario on a fresh database with the harness and the
sheet and the owner's sign off, and the close that scaffolds the next topic.
It names the page each step follows, the document it produces and where the
sign off is recorded, and carries six lessons from the calendars run. The
runbooks catalogue lists it. The next topic's story, Take the currencies topic
through the screen pipeline, is scaffolded with the same four tasks and wired
into the sprint's epic.
