* Goal

Give each ACME party realistic netting agreements, netting sets, CSAs and
portfolios, built from the ORE samples and improved, and make the base bundle
follow the report definitions each party holds. The result looks like a real
group's data and carries ACME's own catalogue, methodology and licence.

* Acceptance

- A new ACME methodology states how the data is built. It names the ORE
  samples as the starting point and lists every improvement.
- Each party has agreements with banks that fit its jurisdiction, with a
  matching agreement type and governing law.
- The CSAs cover variation margin, initial margin and none. Currencies,
  thresholds, minimum transfer amounts, call frequencies and overnight indices
  are realistic for each party. No CSA uses a retired index.
- Every netting set, counterparty and portfolio that a seeded report definition
  names exists for its party.
- Nothing in the base bundle reads as test or sample data.
- pgTAP and a fresh ACME provision pass.

* Plan

1. Audit what is published today, per party. Find every reference to the
   current dataset codes, in profiles, tests, recipes and the manual.
2. Read the report-needs investigation. List the netting sets, counterparties
   and portfolios each party's report definitions need. The reports that read
   =netting.xml= and =counterparty.xml= (SA-CCR, CVA, exposure) set the demand.
3. Design the data: banks per legal entity, agreement types, CSA archetypes,
   the portfolio and book tree.
4. Write the ACME methodology, datasets and bundle. Wire them into
   =acme_demo= at the step that follows each party's report definitions.
5. Verify with pgTAP and a fresh ACME provision.

* Notes

*Audit, 2026-10-10 (plan step 1).* Read from a freshly provisioned ACME
tenant and from the repository. The database was queried; the report needs are
not yet read (plan step 2).

- *What ACME publishes today, per party.* Four legal entities plus the system
  party. Each entity has a bundle (=acme_group=, =acme_uk=, =acme_us=,
  =acme_hk=) of datasets named =acme.<entity>.<thing>=: business units,
  portfolios, books, accounts and contact information. The =load_staff= step of
  the =acme_demo= profile publishes each bundle to its own party. The realistic
  netting data fits this pattern with no new party machinery: add
  =acme.<entity>.netting_agreements=, =netting_sets= and =csas= datasets to the
  entity's bundle.
- *Counts after provisioning.* Acme Corporation Plc 1 business unit, 22
  portfolios, 22 books. UK 15 business units, HK 6, US 6, each with 20
  portfolios and 22 books. The tenant holds 12,959 GLEIF counterparties, 67
  market series, 155 report definitions and no trades.
- *Mixed portfolios.* Every entity has two official top-level portfolios,
  =Global Portfolio= and =Regulatory Capital=, both aggregating in USD. Acme
  Corporation Plc also holds the sample portfolios =PF1= and =PF2= at the top of
  the same tree, because the =ore_samples= bundle publishes them there. Official
  and sample portfolios are not separated.
- *What the sample netting data is.* Eight banks, 19 netting sets, 7 CSAs and 19
  ORE aliases, published to Acme Corporation Plc only. All 7 CSAs are in EUR and
  every threshold pair is identical. Five use the overnight index EUR-EONIA,
  retired in 2022. The set descriptions read as test data ("pricing test book",
  "wrapped trades").
- *References to the current dataset codes.* The sample netting and portfolio
  dataset scripts, the bundle and bundle member scripts, the =acme_demo=
  profile, =party_provisioning_plan.hpp=, the pgTAP sample netting test, the
  trading envelope names test, the manual chapter on initial setup, and a
  number of plans. The retarget task owns the renaming; this task adds new
  datasets and leaves these in place.
- *Banks are real GLEIF entities.* A counterparty is found by LEI, and the
  GLEIF set holds many affiliates of one bank (BNP Paribas alone has dozens),
  so each agreement must name the right legal entity by LEI.

*Decisions.*

- *Panel.* US Inc faces JPMorgan, Bank of America and Citi under New York law.
  UK plc faces Barclays, HSBC, Deutsche Bank, BNP Paribas and UBS under English
  law. HK Ltd faces HSBC Hong Kong, Standard Chartered and Citi under English
  law. Group Plc faces HSBC and JPMorgan.
- *Hong Kong.* Bank of China (Hong Kong) is not in the loaded GLEIF set. Citi is
  the third bank. Standard Chartered Bank is the covering legal entity for its
  Hong Kong branch.
- *Intragroup.* Group Plc holds an uncollateralised agreement with each
  subsidiary. UK plc and US Inc hold a variation margin agreement in USD. Both
  sides share one reference.
- *CSA archetypes.* Variation margin only, variation and initial margin, and
  uncollateralised. Only live overnight indices are used.
- *Counterparties.* The new dataset =acme.lei_counterparties= makes the ACME
  entities counterparties, so intragroup agreements resolve by LEI.
- *Assumption.* The initial margin regulation codes =ESA=, =CFTC,PRUDENTIAL= and
  =HKMA= are not yet checked against the codes ORE accepts.

* Result

Each ACME legal entity now holds realistic netting agreements, netting sets and
CSAs. They come from the new datasets =acme.<entity>.netting_agreements=,
=netting_sets= and =csas=, with a methodology that names the ORE samples as the
starting point and lists every improvement. The data is in the entity bundles
and is verified on a fresh provision.

*Acceptance.* Met: the methodology; per-jurisdiction agreements with matching
type and law; all three CSA regimes with live indices; no sample wording in the
new data; pgTAP and a fresh provision.

*Not met, and why.*

- The report definitions' needs were not read (plan step 2). It is not verified
  that every netting set, counterparty and portfolio a seeded report definition
  names exists.
- Acme Corporation Plc still carries the sample netting data and the portfolios
  PF1 and PF2. The retarget task removes them.
- No trade corpus exists yet.
- The regulation codes are assumptions.

*Known issues not caused by this task.* The generator is out of step with five
checked-in files (books and photos). The codegen test
=test_trade_type_catalogue= fails in a full run on main. The pgTAP synthetic
scope test hard-codes a DDL user name.
