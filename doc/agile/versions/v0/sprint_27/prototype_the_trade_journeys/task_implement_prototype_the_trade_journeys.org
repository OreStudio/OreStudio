* Result

Four prototypes and a group page are under =doc/prototypes/trades/=: =trade-page=, =book-a-trade=, =ore-portfolio= and =structure-page=. Each has a design note. The group page maps the UX rules to where each can be seen, and lists the decisions the owner must make.

Reading the domain notes while building showed that the journeys written in the previous story were wrong in four places. They are corrected in this branch: the kind of change comes first and bounds the fields; confirmation is the counterparty's, not an internal approval; a novation and a book move are a cancel and rebook with a link; and operational authorisation has no journey. The last is filed as a capture.

The prototypes found three more server gaps: 28 of 121 trade types have no instrument table, so they cannot be booked; booking writes the instrument in a second call, so a half-booked trade is possible; and the ORE upload path of a browser is undefined.
