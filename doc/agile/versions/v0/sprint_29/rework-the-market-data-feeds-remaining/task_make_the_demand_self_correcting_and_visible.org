* Goal

A missed change event delays demand by at most one interval, and an operator sees why a source has no demand without reading logs.

* Acceptance

- The supervisor and the ingest loop refresh on a timer and on events.
- A status operation and a shell command show, for each adapter: routes seen, ids demanded, ids accepted, ticks in the last minute and the age of the last tick.
- A warning is logged once when an adapter is up with routes that name it and has no demand.

* Plan

1. Re-read the catalogue and the routes every minute in the supervisor, and
   re-read them every minute in the ingest loop, so a missed change event
   delays a demand or a route by one interval instead of freezing it.
2. Count the routes each adapter's source has, and keep the count on the
   snapshot the supervisor already reads.
3. Give the adapter status the routes, the ticks the ingest received in the
   last minute and the age of the last tick, and merge the ingest's health
   into the status reply.
4. Print one row per adapter from a shell command.
5. Warn once when an adapter is up, routes name it, and its demand is empty.

* Result

The demand is self-correcting and visible.

- The supervisor re-reads the catalogue and the routes every minute, and the
  ingest loop re-reads them every minute from its status thread. A missed
  change event now delays a demand set by one interval and never loses it.
- The adapter status carries the routes that name the adapter's source, the
  ticks the ingest received from it in the last minute, and the age of its last
  tick. The handler merges the ingest's health into the supervisor's reply.
- =marketdata adapters status= prints one row per adapter. Against the live
  system: =bloomberg-a= serving, 5 routes, 26 demanded and accepted, 298
  ticks/min, last tick 0.0s; =demo-a= and =lseg-a= ready with no routes and no
  demand.
- The supervisor warns once when an adapter is up, routes name its source, and
  the demand set it answers is empty, which is the shape of the 2026-10-23
  failure.
- The old feed screen still reports the controller's run list, which is not the
  demand path. This status is the honest view until the screens task replaces
  it.
