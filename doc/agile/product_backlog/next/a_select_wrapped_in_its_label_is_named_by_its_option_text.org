* What

A select wrapped in its label is named by its option text too. The shared Field wraps its control in the label element, so a select's accessible name is the label's words followed by the chosen option's, such as Rounding type Round to Nearest. A screen reader announces both, and a locator by label finds no select. Seen on 2026-10-26 while driving the currencies scenario. The Field should name the control with aria-labelledby on the label's text alone, or put the control outside the label and point at it with for.

* Why

A screen reader announces the label and the option together, such as Rounding type Round to Nearest, and a locator by label finds no select; the scenario runner had to find selects by their label's text instead. The Field should name the control with aria-labelledby on the label's text alone, or put the control outside the label and point at it with for.
