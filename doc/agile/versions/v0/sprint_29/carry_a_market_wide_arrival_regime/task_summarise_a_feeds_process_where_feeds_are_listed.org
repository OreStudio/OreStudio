- The Provider list, the Market tab's Laws and its Arrivals lanes show each feed's process.
- Opening a feed's process shows its parameters: a mixture's components, or a curve process's named parameters with their values.
- The parameters are read only when a summary is opened, so a long list costs one read.
- The summary links to the feed's Process tab.

* Result

Every list of feeds names each feed's process with a tag: the Provider list,
the Market tab's Laws, and its Arrivals lanes. Opening a tag summarises the
process's parameters (a mixture's components with their weight, mean and
deviation, or a curve model's parameters named by their definitions) and links
to the feed's Process tab. The parameters are read only when a tag is opened,
so a list of a hundred feeds reads no parameters until a person asks; a test
checks that the closed tag makes no query. One summary is open at a time: a
click elsewhere, opening another tag included, or Escape closes it.

At the user's request the Arrivals lanes give the feed, its process and its law
columns of their own, each wide enough to read in full, and the ticks take the
width that is left. The lanes no longer clip their contents, so a summary opened
from a lane shows whole. A Hawkes law's column names its branching and decay.

The feed list's rows carry each feed's engine through the BFF, and the
Provider tab's local engine naming moved into the shared =processName=.

Evidence: web tests pass (221 in marketdata/synthetic), BFF market tests pass
(23), typecheck clean, and the harness passes 15 of 15 live: it opens a feed's
process from Laws, sees its parameters and the link to the Process tab, opens a
second tag and sees one summary, and closes it with Escape. Screenshots of the
Laws summary for an IR and an FX feed and of the lanes were checked.
