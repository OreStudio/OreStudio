* Goal

Build the Trade Blotter: the primary front-office screen from which
traders, sales, and middle office view, manage, and act on the live
book of trades -- booking new deals, amending existing ones, and
routing trades through their lifecycle (expiry, fixing, delivery,
exercise, settlement). See the target-state reference:
[[id:3976695F-F526-4C2A-80ED-89B54854CF6D][Trade Blotter (knowledge doc)]] -- multi-panel architecture, deal grid,
deal actions, STP panel, Expiry/Fixing Manager, Barrier Management,
Delta Jump Screen, Spike Map.

The original design said this story depends on [[id:603F4845-B4A0-41EB-ACF9-B0FC1387E1C4][Workspaces: finish the feature]],
and that a production import needs a holding location, the *Pending
Book*. The design task found that the sandbox model replaced both. The
workspace story is abandoned and the Pending Book is retired. A real
deal books into a real book at once and waits in the operational
authorisation queue (see =* Decisions=).

* Permissioning

Every screen of this story reads and writes =ores.trading=, and the story is where that was discovered: the trade list is the first screen to read trading reference data, and it found that every code it needs is seeded for the handler and held by no human role.

| What the arc needs | Where it stands |
|---|---|
| The list, its identifiers and its books | =trading::trade_summaries:read=, =trading::trade_identifiers:read= and =refdata::books:read= are seeded and held by no human role. The menu entry is therefore hidden rather than opening a screen the route refuses |
| The list's counterparties, the trade page and its tabs | =refdata::counterparties:read= and =trading::trades:read= are held by =ReportRun=; =trading::trade_links:read= and =trading::trade_activities:read= by nobody |
| The writes: amend, book, group, raise and work a request | No human role holds a single =trading::*:write=, and none holds =inbox::approval_requests:*= |

[[id:C3D61F4A-C71A-4884-A35A-2F10ADCD8D44][Grant the Trade Blotter its reads and writes]] settles the role and grants the arc in one change, because the gap is per component and not per screen. No task in this story is delivered until that lands, since a screen nobody may open is not delivered.

* Decisions

- Split from [[id:189E3D42-2702-40B4-A08D-47E37B920ED6][Trade Blotter]] on 2026-10-24 at sprint 28 close. The acceptance above is copied from that story; its done tasks are recorded there.
- A leg is a membership, not a trade. Unlinking removes the membership and leaves the trade booked and groupable, which is what the desk means by unlinking.
- The deal's tree is drawn beside its page and selects the leg being read; the selection is in the address, so it is shareable.
- History is the shared timeline over the record and the parts it holds now. The two-pane compare of =HistoryPanel= is the retired shape and is not used on the trading screens.
- A history read is gated twice: the service must register a provider for the entity type, and the route must allow it. Both were missing for the trade and its parts.
- A screen's party is named from the login session when the read is party-scoped, rather than reading the parties table the trading roles may not see.
- A trade booked from a deal joins it: the deal and the role travel with the form, and the trade is a leg before the form closes.
