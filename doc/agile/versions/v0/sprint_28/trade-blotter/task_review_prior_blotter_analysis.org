* Result

The design is written. It is the investigation

- The Pending Book and the =is_live= marker are retired. The sandbox,
  the =draft= state and the operational authorisation status replace
  them.
- The workspace dependency is gone. The story no longer waits.
- The partial-success part of the May plan is built.
- The open part is narrow: a real import must raise authorisation
  requests, and a row that fails must be reported and not stored.
- The three backlog captures each have a call. Positions and the
  trade-expiry workflow stay out. The dialog split is answered by the
  trade page.
- Seven implementation tasks are in the story, from the authorisation
  kind to the queue screen.
