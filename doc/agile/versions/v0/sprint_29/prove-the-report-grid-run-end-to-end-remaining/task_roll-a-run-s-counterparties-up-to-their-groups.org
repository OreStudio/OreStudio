* Result

The counterparty level states each group above its members.

A counterparty that names a parent carries the parent's name, and a group row
sits above the members that share it. The group's trades, NPV and notional are
the sums of its members, so the two can be checked against each other. Members
are indented under their group and ordered largest first, groups are ordered by
their own figure, and a counterparty with no parent stands on its own.

The run that shows it is =71d08bde-2fd9-4ce5-b455-86766722fc99=, on the NPV
definition of ACME Corporation HK Ltd. Its counterparty level draws fourteen
rows: seven groups and their members, among them *株式会社三菱ＵＦＪフィナンシャル・グループ*
with two members adding to =4,788,523.77= HKD, and *DBS GROUP HOLDINGS LTD* above
*DBS BANK LTD.* at =2,606,275.49= HKD.

Show the trades stays on members, because the filter reads one counterparty's
trades; a group's trades are its members' and are read one by one.

The group names come from the tenant's whole counterparty list rather than from
the trades, because a parent need not trade in the run and would otherwise be
unnamed.
