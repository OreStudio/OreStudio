* Result

Four journeys now stand beside the six synthetic prototypes, which had no
journey at all before this:

- [[id:AAF1D25C-566F-4899-8E88-521DC15EBCDB][Author an FX price feed]] — the pair, the
  process, the mixture of Gaussians and the sample paths, with the
  across-feeds component view the mixture is browsed from.
- [[id:4B62FC8A-169A-4843-A8BF-D5E23FE34852][Author an IR curve feed]] — the currency,
  index and role, the short-rate process and its parameter values, and the
  tenor ladder the curve is bootstrapped from.
- [[id:A829B804-23A5-493C-BDC6-A3B513EBDFEF][Run a synthetic collection]] — the feeds a
  collection owns, what it covers and what is running, and the one
  folder-scoped start.
- [[id:AFABC8A2-61A8-486E-80EC-65856914AA17][Manage the yield curve process types]] —
  the models and their parameters kept as data, so a new model appears in
  the curve editor with no change to it.

Every subject in the four coverage tables was verified against the protocol
headers rather than assumed, and the gaps are stated rather than smoothed
over. The FX feed has no subject that lists the vintages a price source
offers, so the step is marked missing; two IR reads and the process-type
lists carry an =id_one_of= filter where the screen needs the parent or an
order, so those are partial; and the collection's gaps — no coverage read,
an unscoped global running set, skipped feeds returned as counts only — are
named.

The journeys link their prototypes' design notes by id, which is why the
prototype sweep had to land first.
