This page documents a test scenario verifying [[id:F18A305D-FE28-4EAE-BB7F-01787ACDCF2D][Task: Exercise the calibration screens]] in [[id:9FEC9AF7-BF8A-494B-AEF8-B0C41CFD5758][Publish public fixings through an adapter]]. It checks the list, the feed detail with its two tabs, the policy screen and the calibrate action. Record the outcome in each step's =*** Result= block and in =* Results=.

* Before you start

The system must already be in this state before the tester opens the screen:

- The services are rebuilt and running: =compass services status= shows 25 running, none failed.
- The public terms are accepted for ECB, IMF and BoC, and the public sources adapter has backfilled fixings: =select count(*) from ores_marketdata_market_fixings_tbl;= is not zero. Restart =ores.marketdata.official= if it is.
- The system tenant holds the FX feed configs, and the calibration tables exist in the database.

* Steps

** Log in as the system administrator
- Open http://127.0.0.1:20002 and sign in as =super_admin= / =Secure-Password-123=.
- Calibration is a system control: the feeds it lists belong to the system tenant,
  and only the system administrator may change the policy.
*** Result
PENDING

** Open the FX calibration list
- From the shell, open *Market data* → *Calibration*, then the *FX* class.
- The page shows: the asset-class switch, the policy in one line with a link, the counts, the filters, and one row per feed with start, source and state.
- The counts add up to the number of feeds, and at least one feed reads "from a fixing".
*** Result
PENDING

** Read a feed's record
- Click the =EUR/USD= row.
- The detail opens with two tabs. *Feed record* shows the start value, the item URI, the provider and credit, the fixing date and time received, the measured volatility and the mixture scale, the derived candidates, the measures card and the exceptions.
*** Result
PENDING

** Read the flow
- Open the *Flow* tab.
- It shows the policy or defaults into the candidates into the resolver, the resolution trail, what the feed inherits, and the recent calibrations.
- Nothing on the Flow tab can be edited.
*** Result
PENDING

** Read the automatic policy
- Go back with *← All FX feeds*, then open *The policy*.
- With no stored policy it shows the automatic defaults: 90 days, the provider order, inverse allowed, keep the fixed level.
- A tenant administrator can read the same rows but cannot save changes.
*** Result
PENDING

** Customise and restore the policy
- Stay signed in as =super_admin= and open the policy screen.
- Choose *Customise this class*, set the window to 60 days, save.
- The list's policy line and the feeds' windows reflect the change.
- Choose *Return to automatic* and confirm the policy line returns to the automatic defaults.
*** Result
PENDING

** Calibrate all
- On the list, choose *Calibrate all*.
- The list re-resolves every feed of the class and states when it last calibrated and how the counts changed, without leaving the page.
*** Result
PENDING
