#+title: ores.marketdata.fixing_read_operations
#+description: Codegen operation model for reading the durable fixings: the latest on or before a date, and a window of days.
#+type: ores.codegen.operation
#+component: marketdata
#+subcomponent: api
#+entity_singular: fixing_read_operations
#+namespace: ores::marketdata::messaging
#+brief: Reading the durable fixings.
#+filetags: :model:operation:marketdata:
#+created: 2026-10-28
#+updated: 2026-10-28

A consumer, the calibration of the synthetic feeds first, asks for a fixing by its
item and a date, and gets the value with where and when it came from. The item is
the fixing's oresmd URI, which names the publisher, so two providers of one pair are
two items. The values stay decimal text, as they are stored.
