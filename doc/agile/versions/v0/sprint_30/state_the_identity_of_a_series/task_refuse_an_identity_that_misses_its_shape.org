* Goal

Identity is written from an oresmd URI when a series is created. Today a row that misses a field its class needs is stored anyway, and the absence surfaces much later as a consumer that cannot resolve the series. Validate at the writer and refuse, naming the class and the missing or forbidden field.

* Acceptance

- An identity row missing a required field is refused at the writer, naming the class and the field.
- An identity row carrying a field its class forbids is refused the same way.
- No existing fixing series is rejected: all 141 carry the fields their family requires.
