* Acceptance

- A start reports the realised volatility over the window, daily and hourly,
  and the drift annualised from the same returns.
- It reports the daily range, the number of samples against the window, and
  the age of the fixing.
- It reports the jump count above three deviations and the dispersion against
  another accepted provider of the same pair, in basis points.
- The screen says which measure changed the calibration today (the realised
  volatility scales the mixture) and which are recorded for later.
- The measures are information: a missing measure never blocks a start, and a
  feed without a usable fixing reports that it has nothing to measure.
