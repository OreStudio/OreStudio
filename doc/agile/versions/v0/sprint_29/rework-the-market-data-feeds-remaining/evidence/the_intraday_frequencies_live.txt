================================================================================
1. What was applied, and in what order
================================================================================

The seeds state the new cadences, and a development environment is provisioned
once and stays up, so the change reached the database in three steps.

(a) Stage and publish the catalogue. The four ACME definition datasets and the
    Northwind one are staged, and each ACME party is published:

        ./compass.sh sql -- -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 \
            -f .../evidence/apply_the_intraday_frequencies.sql

    The script prints what the publish did:

        dataset           | action  | record_count
        ------------------+---------+--------------
         group definitions | updated |            1
         uk definitions    | updated |            4
         us definitions    | updated |            4
         hk definitions    | updated |            3

    Twelve rows moved, one for each definition whose schedule or description the
    seed changed. The publish writes a new version of the row the party already
    holds and leaves every row whose statement did not move alone: the
    skipped counts are the other definitions of each dataset.

    Northwind's artefacts are staged and not published, because this environment
    holds no Northwind tenant.

(b) The job carries its own copy of the expression. A job is not a view over the
    definition, so the publish does not move a schedule that the scheduler is
    already holding. The UK triple vol spot ladder was the one affected
    definition that held a job, and the reporting service's reconciliation run
    had already closed it, because the job carried no run grant. That is why the
    unschedule step below reports nothing to unschedule.

(c) Schedule the affected definitions as the tenant's administrator, one party
    at a time, which is the operation that writes the job and asks IAM for the
    run grant the runs act under:

        ./compass.sh shell --no-login -l \
            .../evidence/schedule_the_intraday_frequencies.ores

5. The definition the environment carried from the 2026-11-03 publish
================================================================================

The fifteen o'clock pass showed a second triple, under the name the catalogue
used before the ladder family was renamed: "Bump and Reprice Triple Vol Spot
Ladder", which the party still held with a job at the old fifteen-minute
expression. It fired at 15:15 and the catalogue's own triple did not, which is
the two schedules in one environment disagreeing.

That definition is the older statement of the same report, and its job is the
one firing the triple every fifteen minutes, so the job was ended:

    ./compass.sh shell --no-login -l .../evidence/unschedule_the_legacy_triple.ores

    > account_operations switch-party 5b21df84-...
    > report_operations unschedule-report-definitions 0ac857b2-...
    {"success":true,"message":"","unscheduled_count":1,"failed_ids":[]}

The definition itself is left in place with no job, because a definition a person
may run is what the row is, and deleting another catalogue's row is not this
task's to do. The half past pass confirms the result: no triple fired at 15:30.

The same older publish left seven more jobs on three definitions the catalogue
now states under other names: "Bump and Reprice Spot Greeks" in three parties,
its "(EOD)" twin in three parties, and "Bump and Reprice Triple Vol Spot Ladder
(EOD)" in London. Their expressions match the cadences the agreed table states
for the same reports, so they do not contradict it; they do mean that the spot
greeks and the close triple run twice for each firing. That duplication is the
older catalogue's and is left for the work that retires it, and the notes of this
task say so.

================================================================================
6. What this proves, and what it does not
================================================================================

The catalogue states the agreed cadence, each party's definitions state it, and
the scheduler holds a job for each definition with the same expression. The
scheduler fired every definition at the cadence its expression states, with no
person acting, and a reader can see the quarter-hour and the hour come apart.

The reports still meet the engine on inputs the environment does not hold: the
triple's run records the engine's refusal with 235 errors, which is the failure
its page states. The spot ladder and trades since last in ran every phase and
completed. A figure is not what this task set, and the pages say so.
