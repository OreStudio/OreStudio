#+title: Task: Attribute the database's size to a party, and draw it as a pie
#+description: A tenant administrator cannot see what its own parties cost. The housekeeping catalogue is the installation's, and the sizes it states are the installation's, so the tenant's own screen has nothing to say. 373 of the installation's tables carry a party_id, so much of the held data can be attributed to a party; the rest belongs to a tenant, to the system tenant, or to no one. State the sizes per party for one tenant, and draw the split as a pie on that tenant's housekeeping screen.

* Acceptance

- A read states, for one tenant, the size each of its parties holds and the share of the tenant's total
- The tenant's housekeeping screen draws that split as a pie, with the tables a party's bytes come from named on demand
- Rows no party owns are stated as their own slice, so the shares add to the tenant's total
- The attribution is computed where the bytes are, not by pulling every row to the screen
