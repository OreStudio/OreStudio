* Acceptance

- A rung's maturity_date is resolved through the tenor catalog and is after
  the as-of.
- Rungs are ordered by maturity, with ON, TN and SN before 1W and carrying
  their own dates.
- A provider that does not quote a rung is absent from that rung, never
  zero.
- The consensus states its method and how many quotes it rested on.
- Every quote names a series_id that get_series_ticks can read, and
  age_seconds equals as_of minus the quote's as_of.
