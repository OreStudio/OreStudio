* Goal

Barclays Plc has served, until now, as ORE Studio's realistic
demo/test entity for manual walkthroughs and screenshots. Acme
Corporation -- a synthetic four-legal-entity holding group
(Group/UK/US/HK) -- is replacing it. Write a user-manual chapter that
introduces Acme Corporation as the system's reference entity: its
holding-group structure (parent plc plus three operating companies),
the business units, portfolios, and books seeded under each operating
company, its staff/accounts, and the Acme Corp counterparty fixture
(including its pre-populated logo -- see [[id:21C9CA52-3515-4D99-9B95-1F5BA8A05DCD][Attach a logo/image to a
counterparty]]). The chapter should let a new user provision Acme
Corporation (=provision tenant --source acme=) and understand what
they are looking at once it is up.
