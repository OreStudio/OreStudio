This page documents a test scenario verifying [[id:2D3C5B23-E6DB-46D5-8732-ACF54987A33A][Build Book a trade]] in [[id:67C1C30A-5CF6-408A-B73C-99A79A01B19F][Trade Blotter — the remaining work]]. It is filled in with the target screens and checklist of steps before testing starts; record the outcome by hand in =*** Result=.

* Before you start

Ready to test as it stands. The fleet is up on this checkout, and the database
holds tenant =acme_corporation= provisioned from =acme_demo= with its trade
population: 22 structures, each carrying an identifier, and 44 legs over 80
trades.

*Sign out and back in before Step 1 if this browser was signed in before the
database was last provisioned.* A session outlives the data it names, and a
stale one shows you the party it was opened with rather than the one the
provisioned run made.

**What the screen shows is one party's holdings.** The structures list is
party-scoped, as every trading read is: signed in as =diane.kay= you see the
structures of =ACME Corporation UK plc=, which the population grouped as six
or seven deals of two legs each. The database holds 22 across the four legal
entities; the other fifteen belong to parties this session does not work for.

**A rule the data does not yet meet — read this before Step 6.** A structure's
legs are supposed to share one party *and* one counterparty. The store enforces
the party and not the counterparty, so the population's own pairs carry two
different counterparties each. The grouping screen states the rule and refuses a
mixed pair, which means a grouping can only succeed where two ungrouped trades
happen to share a counterparty. If the picker offers no such pair, Step 6 cannot
pass, and that is the data's fault and not the screen's: record it as FAILED with
that note, and see the capture filed for it.
