* Result

** Sprint 28 populated (2026-10-13)

The 41 stories sprint 27 carried into the product backlog — its 21 unfinished
stories and the 20 successors their remainders were split into — moved back
into sprint 28. Each kept its UUID, its task files and its history; each now
names sprint 28 as its parent sprint and carries the =sprint_28= tag instead of
the inbox tags. The sprint's =* Stories= table lists all 41.

Thirteen story folders stay in the inbox: ten carried from sprint 26 and three
older ones. They are out of this sprint's mission, which is the record screens
and the journey remainders carried from sprint 27.

=compass sprint audit= reports nine findings: one story ready to close and the
eight open tasks whose pull requests have all merged, all of them the carried
work sprint 27's review already recorded. They are the planning input.
