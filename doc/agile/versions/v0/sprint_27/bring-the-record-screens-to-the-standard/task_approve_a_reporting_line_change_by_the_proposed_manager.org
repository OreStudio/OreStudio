* Goal

Make a reporting line change a four-eyes decision: one person proposes, the proposed new manager approves or declines, and only an approval writes the line. It follows the role request journey.

* Acceptance

- A proposal names the person, the proposed manager and a reason, and nothing changes until the proposed manager approves.
- The proposed manager sees the proposal in Requests and can approve or decline it with a reason.
- The history shows who proposed, who approved and when.

* Plan

Review item P18 of [[file:task_address_the_people_screens_review.org][Address the review of the people screens]]. Model the change on the role request journey: a proposal is a request, the person it names answers it, and only an approval writes.

1. Read how a role request is stored, routed to its approver and answered, and reuse that shape for a reporting line request.
2. Decide the request's data: the person, the proposed manager, the reason, the state, who proposed and who answered.
3. Server: a propose operation, an answer operation that writes the line on approval, and a read of the requests addressed to a person.
4. Web: a proposal in place of the direct edit, and the proposal in Requests for the proposed manager.
5. Refuse a proposal that would make a loop, as the direct write does today.
6. Tests for propose, approve, decline, and the refused loop.
