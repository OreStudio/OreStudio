* What

Take the conventions topic through the screen pipeline. The nine Qt convention kinds and the lists they draw on: day count fraction, business day convention, floating index, payment frequency, leg type and curve role. Eight journeys exist, from Author a convention for a kind to Publish conventions from data quality. Standard screens per kind plus the import and decision workflows; seven kinds list ids only today, the largest gap in the matrix. Fifth topic in the order the inventory story set.

every screen takes the Qt reference data topics through one pipeline, one topic
at a time. This capture is one topic's turn. The order and the judgement of
each topic are on The screen pipeline topics.
