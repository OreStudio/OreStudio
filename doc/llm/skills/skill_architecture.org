* Summary

A [[id:87D41A08-0B37-41CE-8483-B59B36BA87FA][skill]] is one action applied to one typed target, and the catalogue is
arranged so that an agent can find the right one without reading all of
them. The arrangement rests on three classifications, each useful
without the others.

Artefacts are sorted into [[id:F5CB5A45-6557-410B-9E4F-542957561A16][planes]] by the relation an agent bears to
them: it loads grounding, cites a stance, sequences a method, executes
an action, declares a specification, and convenes a deliberation.
Operations are registered against a target's *type* rather than against
the command that produced the target, which allows the applicable verbs
to be [[id:3018B7FE-2DF7-49E5-A57F-A75C02C4D498][computed]] rather than remembered, and removes the combinatorial
growth a hand-written catalogue suffers. Actors declare a [[id:5365DE9F-E3F9-4B5F-ACC1-B347B5FCE2A9][function]],
whose cybernetic level attenuates how much of the catalogue is in scope,
binding at the audit level and advisory elsewhere.

Work is carried from an unverified beginning to a verified end by a
[[id:E8311826-9900-4932-AFFB-A8AF663414DF][method]], whose phases cite the principles that govern their judgement and
call the actions that touch artefacts. How far that work must climb the
[[id:DEE49C55-1626-470D-BB0F-D58A11D35769][verification]] ladder depends on what it changed, and anything structural
is [[id:D8CB6EED-1F6A-4EE5-840B-2FE28DE17911][generated]] rather than written. An agent can be configured and
[[id:54AD5986-34A0-4B19-9F20-D9A024225036][left running]] against a checkable finish condition.

* Detail

** Entry points

The composition model is prior to any particular way of using it. Types
and verbs compose whether or not something routes them, levels attenuate
whether or not a mode declared them, and the planes classify regardless.
There are consequently three ways in, and each is legitimate.

The most direct is to *invoke an action*, when the operation and its
target are both already known. The second is to *dispatch on a target*
with =compass-act=, when the target is known and the operation is not.
The third is to *enter the mode*, =compass-helm=, which declares a
function, matches the work to a method, and routes to the other two as
the method's phases fire.

The mode is a convenience over methods rather than a precondition for
them, and this is worth stating because the reverse reading would make
the mode a single point of failure for the whole catalogue. A method is
invocable on its own; so is an action.
