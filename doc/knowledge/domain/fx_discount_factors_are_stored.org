#+title: FX forward calculations run on discount factors and not quoted rates
#+description: Why a forward computed from discount factor ratios does not depend on day count.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

Pricing libraries compute an FX forward from a ratio of discount factors. The ratio needs dates and curves, and no day count or compounding convention.

* Detail

Rate quotes carry a compounding and day-count convention. A discount factor does not, because it is the present value of one unit. Two curves on different conventions, such as ACT/360 and ACT/365, therefore combine directly.
