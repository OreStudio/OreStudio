* Goal

IAM already answers every agent grant and key in the caller's parties to a holder of iam::agent_grants:read, and lets a holder of iam::agent_grants:revoke revoke any of them, but no ores.web screen shows them. An administrator sees each grant's person, agent, role, expiry and keys, with when each key was last used, filters by person and state, follows a grant to its agent's sessions, and revokes a grant or key after a warning that it stops the agent at once.

* Acceptance

- An administrator who holds iam::agent_grants:read lists every grant and key in their parties, with the person, agent, role, expiry and state; a person who does not hold it sees no such screen.
- A holder of iam::agent_grants:revoke revokes any grant or key from the screen, after a warning, and the agent's next call is refused.
- The screen never shows a key's secret or its hash.
- Each grant links to its agent's sessions in the sessions window.

* Plan

No HTTP route read every grant: the generated entity reads are NATS-only. A
new operation, =list_every_agent_grant=, answers every grant and key in the
caller's parties to a holder of =iam::agent_grants:read=, never to an agent,
and clears every key's hash, as the person's own list does. Its name avoids
the generated entity protocol's =list_agent_grants_request=. The page lives in
the Operations area beside the sign-ins audit, gated by the declared screen
needs, and joins people and roles by name client-side. Its sessions link opens
the sessions window filtered by =?grant=.

* Result

An administrator who holds =iam::agent_grants:read= opens Agent grants in the
Operations area and sees every grant in their parties: the person, the agent,
the role, the expiry and the live keys, filtered by person and by state. A
holder of =iam::agent_grants:revoke= revokes a grant or a key after a warning
that the agent stops at once. Each grant links to its agent's sessions. IAM's
new =list_every_agent_grant= refuses a reader without the permission and any
agent, and never returns a key's hash. When each key was last used is not
shown; the issue log holds it, and the audit task's activity view is where a
person reads it.
