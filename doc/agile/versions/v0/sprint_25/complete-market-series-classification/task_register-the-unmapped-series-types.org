* Goal

Add series_key_registry entries for the eight ORE series types the corpus carries but the registry does not know, so each type's keys split into one series with many points instead of many single-observation series.

* Acceptance

- The registry carries an entry for each of OI_FUTURE, SHAPE_PROFILE, BOND_OPTION, RATING, INDEX_CDS_TRANCHE, GENERIC-MD, CPR and FIXING, with its qualifier depth and point dimension read off the corpus.
- Every distinct key of those types decomposes into a qualifier and a point, so its observations share one series.
- A test asserts the classifier's type set and the registry's type set are equal, so the two tables cannot drift.
- The corpus census of distinct series is materially lower after the change, and the drop is recorded.
