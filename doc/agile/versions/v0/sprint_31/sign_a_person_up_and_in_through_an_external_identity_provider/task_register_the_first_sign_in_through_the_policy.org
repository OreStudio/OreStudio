* Goal

A provider account whose subject ORE has never seen registers through the same door a password registration uses: the deployment's registration policy decides whether the sign-up is accepted, whether it waits for an approval, which tenant the new account lands in, and which party and role it receives. The callback is idempotent, so a retried or doubled callback creates one account and one link, and the screen shows the waiting state when the policy asks for an approval.

* Acceptance

- A first sign-in follows the deployment's registration policy, and a policy that refuses registration refuses the sign-up with a shape the screen shows.
- A policy that requires approval leaves the account waiting, and the person sees that state rather than a session they do not have.
- The account lands in the policy's tenant with the policy's party and role, and the person can use it as soon as the policy says so.
- A retried or doubled callback creates neither a second account nor a second link, and the suites prove it.
- The account and its link are written as one act, so a failure between them leaves neither.
