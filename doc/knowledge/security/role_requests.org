#+title: Role requests
#+description: How a person gains a role by asking: which roles may be asked for, who decides, how an approval becomes a grant exactly once, and what each step leaves for the audit.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:security:iam:rbac:approval:
#+created: 2026-10-05
#+updated: 2026-10-06

* Summary

A person gains a role by asking for it. The ask is stored as an approval
request of kind =iam.role_grant=, with the role ids in IAM's own detail tables
and the reason on the request. A tenant administrator answers from the queue if
they hold the kind's decide permission, =iam::roles:assign=, and nobody answers
their own request. An approval is not a grant: the IAM service reconciles
approved requests into role assignments exactly once. Both people are told
through in-application notifications.

* Detail

** A request is a record, not a waiting workflow

[[id:0FE6BDD9-EB24-4B07-8B0C-D816AAF1BEBF][Approval requests]] holds the
decision: the workflow engine cannot wait on a person, so a request is its own
record with a state, a raiser, and an answer. The role request is the first
kind, and a kind names the permission that decides it.

** Asking

A member calls =iam.v1.ops.ask_for_roles= with the role ids and a reason. IAM
forwards =inbox.v1.ops.raise_approval= with =kind_code= =iam.role_grant=,
reusing the caller's token so the request is the person's, then writes
=ores_iam_role_grant_requests_tbl= and
=ores_iam_role_grant_request_roles_tbl=. The ask is refused when a role is
unknown, already held, or already asked for in a request that still waits. If
the detail write fails, IAM forwards a withdrawal so the request does not wait
naming no roles.

The roles a person may ask for are the tenant's roles minus what they already
hold, minus the service roles and the role that grants everything. A flag on
the role row would state this directly; today the screens filter, and the ask
operation refuses what it should.

** The queue and the answer

=inbox.v1.ops.list_approval_queue= returns the open requests the caller holds
the decide permission for, and leaves out the ones they raised. The order is
oldest first. =inbox.v1.ops.decide_approval= takes the request id, the
version the row was read at, a decision code, and a comment. A refusal, a hold
and a reversal each need a comment, because they are what the person reads.
An approval needs one only when the kind asks for it. The version is the whole
concurrency control: a second decision on a request that moved is refused.

Four eyes is a rule of the store, not of the screen. The decisions table
refuses a decision by the person who asked, and the queue read already leaves
their own requests out.

** From an approval to a grant

The IAM service does not grant at the moment of the decision. Its
=role_grant_applier= reconciles: =ores_iam_unapplied_role_grants_fn= returns the
approved request roles an account does not hold, the applier grants them as the
person who approved, and marks the detail row applied. The query is idempotent,
so a crash and a retry converge, and a role taken away after it was applied is
not granted again. The grant reaches the member at their next sign-in.

** States

A request is =waiting=, =held=, =approved=, =refused=, =withdrawn= or
=expired=. A refusal is final, and an approval is reversed rather than edited.
The role request kind does not allow a hold, and it carries no expiry.

** Telling the two people

The approval handler raises =inbox.approval_waiting= to the holders of the
kind's decide permission, less the asker, when a request is raised, and
=inbox.approval_decided= to the asker when its state changes. Each notification
carries a kind, the values its sentence names, and a link to the request.

** What a screen cannot read

The self read, =inbox.v1.ops.list_my_approval_requests=, returns the request rows and
not the roles they ask for. The roles live in IAM's detail tables, whose read
codes a plain member does not hold, so a member's own request shows the kind and
the reason. A joined read would close the gap; the store serves no batched read
on =role_grant_request_roles= yet.
