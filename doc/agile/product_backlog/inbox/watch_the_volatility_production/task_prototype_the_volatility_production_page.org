* Acceptance

- The prototype draws the chain as a graph whose nodes are market objects with a state and whose edges state what they carry, and it holds no calibration node.
- Three chain variants are reachable in one prototype: a layered graph above the payload, a flat stepper with the fan-out as a list, and a graph beside the payload. The prototype records which one wins and why.
- The payload panels hold the latent value, each vendor's view and the difference between them as heatmaps on the axes a quote set declares, so a quote set with another pair of axes is a change of sample and not a change of the page.
- The quote ladders and the chain's states sit beside the graph, and the prototype states what it does not show.

* Notes

- Judge the variants on two questions with a ten-second budget: why is this subject empty, and which vendor moved.
