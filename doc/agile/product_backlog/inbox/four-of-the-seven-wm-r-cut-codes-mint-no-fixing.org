* What

=ores_refdata_cut_codes_tbl= holds seven WM/R cut codes. Only three of them name
a fixing item, so only three have an instrument, a mapping and a fixing:
=WMR1000NYC=, =WMR1100WAR= and =WMR1200BUD=. =LON1200=, =LON1600=, =NYC1230= and
=TKY1500= exist in the reference data and nowhere else.

* Why

A catalogue that describes seven cuts and states three of them invites the
reading that four cuts are broken. Either the four cuts get their pairs and
items, or the reference data states only the cuts the store serves.
