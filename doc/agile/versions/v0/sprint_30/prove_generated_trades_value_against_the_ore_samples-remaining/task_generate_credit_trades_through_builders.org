* Acceptance

- convention_lookup answers what each credit builder asks.
- The credit planner draws values and calls the builders; its construct_* functions and *_terms structs are deleted.
- The credit entries leave the recorded trades' known lists, and the recorded files change in the same commit.
- The ORE engine test reads every booked credit type, and builds each one or fails only because the example market has no curve for its reference entity.
- The roundtrip tests read every ORE sample trade of each booked credit type and write back every element ORE needs.

* Plan

- A generated credit trade names its curve by its reference entity: the credit
  spread series of the LEI, senior unsecured, in the trade currency, stored as
  an oresmd address. The market data task gives each LEI its curve; until then
  ORE reads the trade and fails only on the missing curve. The user chose this
  on 2026-11-10.
- =convention_lookup= answers a CDS convention by its id, as ORE names it.
  The planner asks for =CDS-STANDARD-CONVENTIONS=.
- One builder per booked type. CDS and index CDS share a premium leg built
  from the CDS convention. The index option wraps an index CDS. The credit
  linked swap pays a contingent coupon leg and redemption. The RPA wraps a
  vanilla swap built by =vanilla_swap_builder= and pays a protection fee.
- The scope carries the tenant's conventions, read by the population writer,
  so the planner can build trades.
- The credit planner draws terms, calls the builders, and the =construct_*=
  functions, =credit_common_terms= and the plan's =credit_terms= are deleted.
