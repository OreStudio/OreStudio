#+title: Answer a session-layer refusal in the error contract
#+description: The gateway's session refuses with {"error": "..."} while every ported route and the BFF answer {"code", "message"}. The browser parses the second shape, so a refused write on the gateway shows "Request failed with status 403" and loses the words that said why.

#+level: cross

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Make every refusal the HTTP layer itself produces answer the error contract the
client parses: ={"code": "...", "message": "..."}=.

* Why

=ores.http.api/domain/http_response.cpp= holds two shapes. =error(status,
message)= writes ={"error": message}=, and =api_error(status, code, message)=
writes the contract. The session's own refusals use the first:

- the 401 that says a bearer token or a session cookie is required
- the 403 of the CSRF rule, "The Origin does not match this deployment"
- the 400 of a request the route will not take
- the 404 of a route that is not there, "Endpoint not found"
- the 500 of a handler that threw

The browser's =apiErrorSchema= reads the second shape only, so each of these
arrives as =Request failed with status 403= and the words are lost. The BFF
answers the contract throughout (=errors.ts=), which is why the same refusal
reads differently on the two servers while both are live.

Measured on 2026-11-22 against the running gateway, a write from an origin the
allow-list does not name:

#+begin_src
POST /api/market/instruments, Origin: http://evil.example
403 {"error":"The Origin does not match this deployment"}
#+end_src

while the route's own refusal, two calls away, answers the contract:

#+begin_src
400 {"code":"invalid-request","message":"A market read states the body its screen sends."}
#+end_src

* Not the same as

The reason a ported route refuses is worth keeping as it is. This is only about
the shape the *session* uses for its own refusals, which no route writes.
