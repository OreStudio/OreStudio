items a generator starts from, and understands the value it took and the
volatility it measured.

* Acceptance

- The feed page shows the ranked fixing items, and a person adds, removes and
  reorders them, and states each one's policy and window.
- It shows the resolution as of the market clock: the item chosen, its provider,
  the value, the fixing date, the time received, whether it was read as =1/x=,
  and the provider's credit.
- It shows the measured volatility and the factor it applies to the mixture.
- It shows a fixed level only where no item has a fixing, with the reason, and
  it carries no vintage control or field.
