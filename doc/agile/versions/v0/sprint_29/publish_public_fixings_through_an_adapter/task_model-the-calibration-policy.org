* Goal

Make calibration a property of the market, not of every feed: one policy per
asset class, candidates derived from the pair and the fixing catalogue, and
per-feed exceptions that stay visible.

* Acceptance

- Calibration works with no policy: the defaults are the provider catalogue
  order, a 90-day window, an inverse allowed and the fixed level as the
  fallback.
- A policy may exist per asset class, holding the window in days, whether an
  inverse is read as =1/x=, the provider order and the fallback when no
  candidate has a fixing.
- A feed derives its candidate list from its pair and the accepted providers
  in the policy's order, direct before inverted. It stores no list of its own.
- A feed may pin one item, state its own window, or name its items
  explicitly; the record says it overrides, and clearing it returns the feed
  to the policy or the defaults. None of these is required.
- A calibration of a class resolves every feed and records the item, the
  policy and the fallback or refusal for each one.
- The existing per-feed =fx_spot_fixing_item= rows are either left as an
  explicit override or derived when absent; they are never required, and they
  never contradict the policy silently.

* Plan

** Where it lives

The policy is a synthetic concern, so it lives in =ores.synthetic=: the class
that calibrates its generators decides how. The fixing catalogue it reads stays
in =ores.marketdata=.

** The schema

- =calibration_policy= (versioned, system tenant): =asset_class= (the
  refdata code), =window_days=, =allow_inverse=, =fallback= (a lookup:
  =keep_fixed= or =refuse=).
- =calibration_provider_order= (child, versioned with its parent):
  =provider_code= and =rank=. Child rows rather than a JSON list, so the order
  is queryable and the provider is a soft FK.
- =fx_spot_generation_config= gains a nullable =calibration_window_days=: the
  one per-feed exception that has nowhere else to live. The item pin and the
  explicit list already have a home: =fx_spot_fixing_item=.

No policy row means automatic: providers in catalogue display order, 90 days,
inverse allowed, keep the fixed level. A row overrides any of the four.

** Resolution

The synthetic service already resolves a start in =resolve_fixing_start=. The
change is where the candidates come from:

- If the config has =fx_spot_fixing_item= rows, they are the explicit list:
  use them in rank order (today's behaviour).
- Otherwise, derive them: read the class policy (or the defaults), read the
  accepted providers and the fixing series for the pair, order by the policy's
  provider rank then direct before inverse, and filter by the clock.
- A pinned item is one =fx_spot_fixing_item= row; the window is the config's
  override or the policy's.

The derivation reads market data over its existing read subjects; it adds no
new store.

** Recalculation and coverage

=POST /api/synthetic/calibration/{assetClass}/calibrate= resolves every feed of
the class, records one calibration run, and returns the per-feed outcome. A
=GET /api/synthetic/calibration/{assetClass}= returns the policy, the feeds
with their resolved starts, the coverage counts and the run history. The feed
page reads the same resolution for one feed.

** Order of work

1. Entity, child and config column; regenerate the synthetic SQL, C++, TS and
   docs; rebuild.
2. Service reads: policy lookup with defaults, candidate derivation, window
   override; unit tests with the existing fixing fixtures.
3. BFF endpoints and tests.
4. Screens: list, feed detail with two tabs, policy screen (the prototype).
5. Seed one default policy per class? No: the absence of a row is the default.
   Seed a policy only where the market needs one.

** Open choices

- Provider order as child rows (recommended) or a text list.
- Fallback as a lookup table (recommended, consistent with the enums) or a
  check-constrained text.
- Whether the calibration run is its own entity or a field set on the feed's
  start records. Recommended: its own small table, so a class run has an
  identity and an actor.

* Notes

** What has landed

- =calibration_fallback= (simple lookup, system rows) with the two answers.
- =calibration_policy= (one row per asset class; window, inverse, fallback) and
  =calibration_provider_order= (provider and rank), both system-owned and
  readable by tenants.
- =fx_spot_generation_config.calibration_window_days=, nullable, as the one
  per-feed exception.
- The generated SQL, C++ domain, repository, TS and cmake, and the fallback
  seed rows =keep_fixed= and =refuse=.
- The synthetic api, core and service libraries build, and the targeted checks
  pass (model-drift, sql-hygiene, format, cmake-sources).
- =derive_fixing_items= in =feeds/fixing_calibration.hpp=: from a policy and
  the series that cover a pair, it derives the ordered fixing items (provider
  order, direct before inverted, the policy window), ready for the existing
  resolver. Four cases cover the order, the empty order, the inverse rule and a
  pair that no candidate covers; =ores.synthetic.api.tests= passes.

- The feed resolves under the policy: a config with no items derives them from
  the policy and the series that cover its pair; its own
  =calibration_window_days= overrides the policy window; a failed series read
  is reported, not taken for "no series"; a =refuse= fallback stops the feed,
  and both start loops skip it with the reason.
- =read_calibration_policy= is the one policy lookup; no row returns nothing,
  and the caller takes the defaults.
- =synthetic.v1.ops.calibrate_feeds= resolves every feed of a class without
  starting one, and returns the policy and each feed's item, exceptions and
  fallback or refusal. =GET /api/synthetic/calibration/{assetClass}= serves it
  with the coverage counts.

** Decisions

- The derivation applies no terms filter. A provider the adapter may not fetch
  has no fixings, so the resolver skips it; the policy's provider order is the
  explicit filter.
- A calibration is a read. Its response is the record of each feed; runs are
  not stored, because nothing reads a history yet. The calibration screen
  task decides whether it needs one.
- The screens belong to [[id:2159FD0C-714C-45CF-BC8A-9ABFB98D3254][Build the calibration screen]].

* Result

Calibration is a property of the market. One optional policy per asset class
states the window, the inverse rule, the provider order and the fallback; no
row means the defaults (catalogue order, 90 days, inverse allowed, keep the
fixed level).

- A feed with no items derives them from its pair and the policy; it stores
  no list. Explicit items stay an override, and a feed's own window overrides
  the policy's.
- A =refuse= fallback stops the feed with its reason, and the start loops skip
  it.
- =synthetic.v1.ops.calibrate_feeds= and =GET
  /api/synthetic/calibration/{assetClass}= resolve every feed of a class and
  return the policy and each feed's item, exceptions and fallback or refusal,
  with the coverage counts.

A calibration is not stored; the screen task decides whether it needs a
history. The screens are [[id:2159FD0C-714C-45CF-BC8A-9ABFB98D3254][Build the calibration screen]].
