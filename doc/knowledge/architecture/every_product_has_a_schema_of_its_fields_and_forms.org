* Summary

Every product has one schema: its fields, each with a type, scalar or list,
the values it accepts and a description, and the forms it offers
([[id:D18BD092-AF0C-449F-ACDA-F621655BA831][A product is booked in one of the forms it offers]]). A booking form draws itself from the schema, the shell prints
it, import checks a document against it, and the generator draws terms against
it. So no surface states a product's fields again, and a product added to the
catalogue appears on every surface at once.

* Detail

** Where a schema comes from

- A product with a native form takes its fields from its element: the terms its
  builder takes, which are the facts its rows hold.
- A product with only a scripted form takes its fields from its script's inputs
  ([[id:92449D70-09E2-4391-B716-A1001F22B271][A script's free variables are its inputs]]): name, type (=Number=, =Event=, =Index=, =Currency=,
  =DayCounter=), scalar or array, and the rule a value follows.
- Annotations held as data add what neither states: a label, the codes a coded
  input accepts (a =BarrierType= of 1 to 4), a default, and a description from
  ORE's User Guide.

** What a schema states about forms

Each form the product offers, with the script a scripted form runs, the engine
the pricing configuration gives its product tag, and what that costs. For a
product with both forms, the schema is the native one, and the scripted form is
reached through the product's twin recipe ([[id:206312C0-6398-4B9D-86F8-D8AEC9A5CEAD][A twin recipe maps a native product's terms onto its script]]).

** Who reads it

| Reader | Uses the schema to |
|--------+--------------------|
| booking form | draw its fields and the form buttons |
| booking API | validate terms before booking ([[id:3085E65E-413B-4E7A-B60A-5844A124C999][One booking API books a product in either form]]) |
| ores.shell | describe a product ([[id:B01292A4-0394-40E8-A0B4-2DBA3F46C052][The shell parses a script without a login]]) |
| import | check a document's terms |
| generator | draw terms field by field ([[id:8BA8D977-46CC-4DC6-9EFE-3FBE56B76A37][The generator books every product through the booking API]]) |

** Where today's code differs

No schema is served. A native product's fields are its builder's C++ terms, a
script's inputs are rows of [[id:F08D458F-B694-4B2F-A945-E22544A9222F][ores.trading.script_input]] that only the scripted builder
reads, and no surface reads either as a product's fields.
