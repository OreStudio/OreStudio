* Acceptance

- The grouping route completes the structure in one call, and writes no structure or member of its own.
- Two trades grouped together become one structure whose every leg is live and waiting on Operations.
- A refusal from the completion reaches the screen as its own message, and nothing is left behind.

* Plan

One edit, in one place: the grouping route's structure-then-members loop becomes
a single call carrying the anchor and the legs.

The decision the arc left open is taken here — grouping completes. The screen is
called Group trades and its purpose is to make one deal out of trades already
booked; a grouping that left its legs as drafts was the screen doing half its
job. The draft flow keeps its place: booking a trade that waits, or adding a leg
to a structure that is already live.

What the route keeps is its own checks: the session works for a party, every
trade is one this session can read, all legs share one counterparty, the kind
exists, and no trade is already grouped. The completion decides everything that
needs a transaction.
