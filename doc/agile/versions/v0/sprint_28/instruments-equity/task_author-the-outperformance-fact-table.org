* Result

The outperformance option has its own fact table, authored from the ORE
binding. The pair is the subject matter, so both names have a column and
neither is squeezed into the single-underlying shape of
equity_option_instruments.

Every level and rate is an exact decimal: the notional, the two initial
prices, the strike return and the two knock prices. The strike return is a
proportion that multiplies money, and this family holds a rate as an exact
decimal. The knock prices are nullable because the document may omit them.

The two underlyings must differ, and the check says so: an option on an equity
against itself has no relative return.

The table is named equity_outperformance_options rather than
equity_outperformance_option_instruments. The derived name is one character
over the limit the generator allows once the trigger and policy suffixes are
counted, and PostgreSQL truncated it mid-word to ..._instrumen.

Verification. Full build clean, the database recreated, and the whole suite
passes 96 of 96. ores.trading.core.tests passes 1196 assertions in 178 cases.
The table is present in the database after the recreate.

Not done, and named: no mapper writes this table, and EquityOutperformanceOption
still routes to equity_option_instruments. Both move with the mapper migration.
