* Goal

A graceful shutdown closes the sessions the process opened. Seven processes
log in as their service account through =make_service_token_provider=
(=ores.iam.client=): =ores.synthetic=, =ores.ore=, =ores.reporting=,
=ores.scheduler= and =ores.marketdata= services, the http server, and IAM's
own registrar. The provider logs in with =service_login_request= and refreshes
its token, but nothing ever sends =iam.v1.auth.logout=. So every restart
leaves an open session behind until it expires, and the session records
overstate who is signed in. The logout joins the shutdown that the shared
service lifecycle already runs: after the =stopped= event, before
=nats.drain()=.

* Acceptance

- The service token provider can log out: it sends =iam.v1.auth.logout= with
  its current token and forgets the token.
- The shared service lifecycle logs the service out on a graceful shutdown,
  after the =stopped= event and before =nats.drain()=, so no service wires it
  by hand.
- A logout that fails is logged and never blocks the shutdown.
- A test proves a service session is closed after a graceful stop, and the
  session records show no open session for a stopped service.
