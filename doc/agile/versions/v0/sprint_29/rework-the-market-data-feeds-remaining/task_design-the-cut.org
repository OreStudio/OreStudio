* Acceptance

- A journey takes a cut: the kind and timepoint, the assembled body, the review and the authorisation.
- A journey consumes a cut: a valuation names a cut and the run records it.
- A prototype draws the cuts list, the cut record and its body, the take-cut flow, and the run that names the cut.
- The pages are wired, registered, checked, and the review findings closed.
