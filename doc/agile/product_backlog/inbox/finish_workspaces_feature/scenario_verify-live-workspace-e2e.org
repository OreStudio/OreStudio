* Steps

Each step is its own heading — the title should be five to seven
words so it fits on one line in the QA Validation Runner's step list
without wrapping or truncating (e.g. "Edit and save the record", not
a full sentence describing the whole operation). The body below the
title is a bullet-point checklist, not a prose paragraph: give the
tester every piece of context needed to execute that one step without
looking anything up elsewhere — what UI state must already exist,
exactly what to click or type, and exactly what confirms the step
passed. The panel writes each step's PASS/FAIL/PENDING outcome and
notes back as a =*** Result= child heading directly under it.

** Log in as tenant admin

- Start the Qt client with this scenario loaded:
  =compass client start --open-scenario doc/agile/product_backlog/inbox/finish_workspaces_feature/scenario_verify-live-workspace-e2e.org=.
- Log in with username =tenant_admin@acme_corporation= and password
  =Secure-Password-123=.
- Open the Trades list window and confirm its workspace selector
  (bottom bar) shows =Live= and is enabled.

** Create a CDS convention under Live

- Open the CDS Conventions list window (Reference Data menu → CDS
  Conventions).
- Confirm the bottom-bar workspace selector shows =Live=.
- Click New CDS Convention. Fill in: identifier =E2E-CDS-001=,
  calendar (e.g. =GBP=), frequency (e.g. =Q=), day count fraction
  (e.g. =ACT/360=), and settlement days (e.g. =1=).
- Save and confirm =E2E-CDS-001= appears in the list.

** Browse the provisioned portfolio tree

- Open the business unit / portfolio tree for the Acme party.
- Confirm the provisioned Acme business units, portfolios, and books
  are listed (the =acme_group.*= set, including =acme_group.books=).
- Open one book's detail view and confirm it loads its fields.

** Book a trade with an FX forward

- Open the Trades list window (Trading menu → Trades).
- Confirm the workspace selector shows =Live=.
- Click New Trade. In the Instrument tab, select the FX Forward
  instrument type and fill its fields (identifier, underlying currency
  pair, maturity date).
- Fill the trade's required fields and save.
- Confirm the new trade appears in the Trades list.

** Create a temporary workspace

- Open the Workspaces window (Data Transfer menu → Workspaces).
- Click New Workspace, name it =E2E Temp=, and save.
- Confirm =E2E Temp= appears in the workspace list and in the Trades
  window's workspace selector.

** Confirm the temp workspace starts empty

- In the Trades window, switch the workspace selector from =Live= to
  =E2E Temp=.
- Confirm the Trades list is empty: the Live trade does not leak in.
- Open the CDS Conventions list and confirm it is also empty under
  =E2E Temp=.

** Import the ORE sample into the temp workspace

- With the workspace selector still on =E2E Temp=, launch the ORE
  import wizard (Data Transfer menu → Import ORE sample).
- On the "Select ORE Directory" page, choose
  =assets/test_data/domain_roundtrip/Legacy/Example_1= and wait for
  the scan to complete.
- Accept the defaults on the remaining pages and complete the wizard;
  wait for the import to finish.
- Confirm the imported portfolios and trades appear under =E2E Temp=.

** Confirm Live data is untouched

- Switch the Trades workspace selector back to =Live=.
- Confirm the imported trades are absent and the trade booked in step
  4 is still listed.
- Open the CDS Conventions list and confirm =E2E-CDS-001= is still
  listed under =Live=.
