* Summary

The Acme demonstration gives each of its four legal entities one market data
provider, and one simulated source serves that provider. The group and Hong Kong
read =bloomberg= through =bloomberg-a=, London reads =demo= through =demo-a=,
and New York reads =lseg= through =lseg-a=. Every source the parties take is
of kind =simulated=, so the demonstration runs with no vendor connection and no
licence. The provider is the row a subscription names. The source is the row a
route names. The two are never the same row, which is what lets the
demonstration move a party to a live feed by changing one route.

* Detail

** The seven providers

The catalogue names seven providers. Five are data vendors the platform could
connect to, and two are the platform's own.

#+ATTR_HTML: :class hug-leading
| Provider | Kind | What it stands for |
|----------+------+--------------------|
| =bloomberg= | data vendor | Bloomberg L.P., which identifies an instrument by a ticker and a yellow key |
| =lseg= | data vendor | LSEG, known before as Reuters and Refinitiv, which identifies an instrument by a RIC |
| =ice= | data vendor | ICE Data Services, which publishes under its own symbology |
| =tradeweb= | data vendor | Tradeweb, which publishes fixed income and other OTC pricing |
| =ore= | reference | The Open Source Risk Engine; the values of its example market data files are imported under it |
| =demo= | reference | A fictional provider for exercises and demonstrations, whose notation is a slash pair such as EUR/USD |
| =composite= | reference | The internal provider of the items the service derives from the quote series of other providers |

The demonstration uses three of the seven. =ice= and =tradeweb= exist for the
adapter contract and carry no party in the demonstration. =ore= is what the
reference vintage is imported under, and =composite= is what a derived item is
attributed to. [[id:68CE1E1D-7568-4911-BB71-CCF5A9E07BB7][Every source belongs to a provider in the
catalogue]] says why the provider, and not the source, owns the id namespace and
the mapping.

** The six sources

A source is an adapter instance, and its name says which provider and which
instance and never the kind.

#+ATTR_HTML: :class hug-leading
| Source | Provider | Kind | Default | What it serves |
|--------+----------+------+---------+----------------|
| =bloomberg-a= | =bloomberg= | simulated | yes | the values the simulator generates for the ids of Bloomberg |
| =lseg-a= | =lseg= | simulated | yes | the values the simulator generates for the ids of LSEG |
| =demo-a= | =demo= | simulated | yes | the values the simulator generates for the exercises and demonstrations |
| =ore-import= | =ore= | replay | yes | the values of an ORE market data file, as the import and the DQ publish write them |
| =ore-derived= | =ore= | derived | no | the values the service computes from other series, such as a bootstrapped curve |
| =composite-derived= | =composite= | derived | yes | the values the service computes from the inputs of a composite |

The three simulated sources are the ones the demonstration routes to. A source
of kind =derived= is written by the service and never subscribed to by a party.
[[id:C6D75337-0EA6-40F0-8E8B-52737558851D][The source kind]] says what each
kind obliges, and [[id:F64DB34E-084D-4437-B536-392AA26D8B77][No code path may
behave differently because a source is simulated]] says what a simulated source
must not change.

** Which party takes which

#+ATTR_HTML: :class hug-leading
| Party | Provider | Source | Entity of the group |
|-------+----------+--------+---------------------|
| Acme Corporation Plc | =bloomberg= | =bloomberg-a= | the holding company |
| ACME Corporation UK plc | =demo= | =demo-a= | London |
| ACME Corporation US Inc | =lseg= | =lseg-a= | New York |
| ACME Corporation HK Ltd | =bloomberg= | =bloomberg-a= | Hong Kong |

The split is not decoration. A synthetic feeds collection quotes the markets its
own feeds serve, and one series has one running publisher: Bloomberg's feeds
quote the USD spots and the curves every ACME book needs, LSEG's quote the
fourteen cross pairs Bloomberg leaves to the cross-rates matrix, and the demo
provider's quote eight majors and three emerging pairs on one exaggerated
volatility. Giving each office a different provider therefore exercises three
feed collections at once, and each office still marks its trades on a complete
market. [[id:37B27CA8-9F44-4A61-9B02-3EAABD8C3818][A party routes to exactly
one source for each provider]] is the rule that makes the choice a route and not
a filter.

The system party takes =bloomberg= through =bloomberg-a= as well, because a
system collection runs once in the system tenant and a tenant sees it without
owning it. That row is the one subscription in the tenant the demonstration does
not create for a party of its own.

** Why no live source appears

A live adapter needs a vendor connection, credentials and a licence, and the
demonstration must install and run on a laptop. The catalogue still carries the
live providers, because the provider list is what a real installation's
subscription screen offers, and a party on a live feed differs from this one only
inside the adapter. [[id:FA280E9B-7E00-4747-8D29-42B3D4258873][A source switch
changes one route and nothing else]] is the change a person makes to move a
party from a simulated source to a live one.
