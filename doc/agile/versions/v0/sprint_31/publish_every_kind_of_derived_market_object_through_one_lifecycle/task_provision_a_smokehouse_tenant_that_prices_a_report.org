* Goal

A tenant small enough to gate on provisions from an empty database in minutes and
runs the whole lifecycle. A curve set, a forward set and a surface publish, are
superseded and announce. Three trades, one for each book, price into one NPV
report. It replaces the ACME tenant as the end-to-end proof, which takes a
quarter of an hour because it also loads four legal entities, forty-five staff
and eleven hundred trades.

* Plan

1. *The dataset.* One generator, =generate_sql.py=, writes sixteen populate files
   into =projects/ores.sql/populate/smokehouse/=: the catalogue, the dataset, the
   bundle, dependencies, one legal entity and its relationships, a business unit,
   a portfolio, three books and their product types, four counterparties, six
   pricing engines, today's market, the curve configuration, the risk report
   configs, the engine settings and the report definitions.
2. *The profile.* Fifteen steps from =system_provision= to =end_auto_approval=,
   each stating what it needs rather than inheriting the catalogue's whole set: the
   curves, the pair and the three trade types.
3. *The step handler.* The subscription step routes a party by what its provider
   publishes; the curve step publishes in dependency order, because a curve that
   discounts off another waits for it; the surface step takes the pair the curves
   allow.
4. *The report documents.* The template's six documents, cut to the market this
   tenant prices, because a document that names every currency makes the run read
   a market the tenant never publishes.
5. *The two fixes the run needed.* The ORE export carries the FX spots a run prices
   through, and the convention writer names the calendars the way ORE reads them.
6. *The record.* The phase timings, the recipe, and the script tangled from it.
7. *The remainder.* Rebase onto main, re-point the fixing subscription at the
   administrators, and prove the swap with the two fixing tasks.

* Notes

- The commits on this branch cite =Task-ID: 12B71000=, which is the story's other
  task, because this page did not exist when they were written. The rebase that
  lands the branch corrects the trailers to =D3F27A89=.
- Two causes stop the swap, and each has a task. No source states a SONIA fixing in
  a synthetic run, because the Bank of England's adapter is not installed. And the
  fixing the simulator would derive from the party's spots is dropped, because the
  party's book spans two bases and neither cancels.
- The pair is forced by the curves: the population books a pair only when the party
  can price both its currencies, so ESTR, SONIA and LIBOR 3M leave EUR/GBP alone.
  Its spot comes from LSEG, the only EUR/GBP spot source in the release.
- The capture =the-report-market-names-what-ore-cannot-resolve= records the second
  cause with its log line. It needs the first cause before the branch lands.
