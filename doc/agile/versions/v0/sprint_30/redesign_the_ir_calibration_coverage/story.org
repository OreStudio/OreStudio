* Acceptance

- The coverage is one row per curve, and a currency's feed is named once.
- The class policy is a compact card.
- No currency with a feed and a published curve says it has no feed.

* Decisions

- The calibration of a class resolves FX spot feeds only, so the IR coverage
  reads a currency's feed from its IR generation config and where the feed
  started from the run the service holds for it, joined by source name.
- Each curve states its own gap from its own publish; the feed's gap stands
  under the source, once per currency.
- An IR config holds no window of its own, so the coverage shows none.

* Out of scope

- Calibrating IR feeds. The calibration and its apply are built for FX spot
  feeds; an IR feed's start is resolved when it runs.
- CZK states that no series publishes its overnight rate although cnb is in the
  source order; the screen shows the run's own words, and the run is not
  examined here.
