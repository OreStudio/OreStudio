This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The booking check's fixture and =blotter_rail_tests.cpp= each build the same
tenant by hand: an account to write as, a party with its parent, a currency, a
portfolio and a book, with =change_reason_code = "system.test"= on each. One
helper in =ores.testing= should return that tenant, as the database helpers
there already do.

* Why

The two copies have drifted, which is the point. The booking check learned two
rules the store enforces: a portfolio is refused when its currency is a random
code, and a counterparty is its own refdata row and not a party. The blotter
fixture writes a currency but states no aggregation currency, and books its
trades below the booking service, so it never meets the counterparty rule. A
shared fixture is where those rules are stated once and a third suite that
wants a book gets them free.

The review of the booking check raised this and the change did not take it,
because moving a fixture into =ores.testing= reaches a library the trading tests
and the synthetic tests both link, and that is a change of its own.
