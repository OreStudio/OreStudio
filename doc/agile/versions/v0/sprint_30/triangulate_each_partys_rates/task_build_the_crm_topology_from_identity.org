* Goal

A pair is composed from two legs of one publisher that share a base, so the base cancels in the level difference. Today the driver pairs are hand-listed in refdata and the reader hardcodes one publisher. Build the topology from market_series_identity (ccy, unit_ccy) and providers.precedence, make the publisher's pivot a root vertex whose leg is the identity, and refuse an FX level series whose identity is incomplete.

* Acceptance

- The policy chooses the publisher the store ranks first that can anchor a tree, and states its pairs as the star against its own base, so the pivot is the base and a pair whose leg is the pivot needs no series.
- A pair is composed only from one publisher, so no two publishers are mixed inside one rate.
- No currency and no publisher name appears in the topology code: the caller supplies both from data.
- Empty input, a source with no base, and a source that quotes nothing are refused naming the provider.

The wiring is a separate task, [[id:3D29D41E-E006-4404-8D94-7966EB224B29][Serve the CRM the topology the store describes]]: it reads the party's identities and the provider rank into these sources and builds the CRM's engines from them, which is where crm_driver_pair stops being the source of the drivers.

* Result

The policy reads a topology from the identity a source states.
=fixing_source= is one publisher as the store records it: the base all
its series are quoted against, the currencies it quotes, and the rank
resolution reads it at. =topology_from_sources= takes the lowest-ranked
source that quotes at least one currency and states its pairs as the star
against that base, which becomes the pivot, so the base cancels in every
derived pair and two publishers never meet inside one rate.

Measured: the quant suite passes, 1/1 in 15.83 seconds, with nine new
cases. They state that the rank decides and a tie breaks on provider
code, that a source quoting only its own base is passed over, that a
currency named twice appears once, that the same shape holds under names
the code has never met (=XYZ=, =AAA=, =test=), and that the star the
policy states builds a tree reaching every currency it names. The code
was also compiled with the flags the build itself uses before the build
ran, and clang-format is clean.

What it deliberately does not do: it reads no database and nothing calls
it yet. The reader that turns a party's identity rows and the provider
rank into these sources, and the wiring of
=crm_ingest_bridge::refresh()=, are the sibling task, so
=crm_driver_pair= still supplies the CRM's drivers until that lands.
