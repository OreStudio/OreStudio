#+description: The ORE import handler builds 26 put_*_request messages and several copy fewer fields than their write struct declares. This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Give every =put_*_request= the handler builds one constructor that takes the
domain row, as the strike write now does, and make a check fail when a request
leaves a field of its =*_write= struct unset.

* Why

The strike write cost a PR. The handler built =put_instrument_strike_request= in
three places and copied eight of its ten fields in two of them, so a CapFloor or
an index option strike went out with an empty =strike_role= and an indeterminate
=sequence_number=: the service throws on the role, the table refuses it, and
neither product could be imported. Every test passed, because no test crosses
the handler — =ores.ore.service.tests= has no sources.

The same census over the handler and the generated =*_write= structs names the
rest. Distinct partial builders, as of this capture:

| Request | Fields it never sets |
|---------+----------------------|
| =equity_swap_instrument= | =fixed_trigger_levels_json=, =initial_prices_json=, =knock_out_levels_json= |
| =equity_accumulator_instrument= | =range_bounds_json= |
| =trade_identifier= | =issuing_party_id= |
| =swap_leg= | =leg_group= |
| =bond_leg_rate= and =bond_issue_leg_rate= | =fixing_calendar=, =fixing_days=, =index=, =is_in_arrears=, and the stub and rounding fields |
| =instrument_schedule= | =include_duplicate_dates=, and in one builder the dates, the rule and the calendar |

Some are deliberate: a builder may write a header whose dates live in another
table. The rest are silent drops, and the class is the review's finding 5 one
layer up — a field with no producer on the wire rather than in the store.

The equity two belong to the equity rework's owner and should be handed there
rather than fixed here.
