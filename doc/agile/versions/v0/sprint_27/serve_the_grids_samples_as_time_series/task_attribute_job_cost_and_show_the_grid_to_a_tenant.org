* Acceptance

- Every host records a cost rate and each completed result records its cost.
- Idle cost is apportioned to tenants, parties and reports, and the loaded costs plus the unallocated cost equal the host costs.
- A tenant administrator opens the grid screens and sees the tenant's work by party, with no other tenant's rows or counters.
- The grid samples carry the job counters of one tenant in a row of that tenant, so a tenant reads only its own.
