* Goal

Every place that shows a session or a sign-in says how it happened: the password, a named provider, or an agent's key, so a person can tell their own sign-ins from their agents' and can see a sign-in they do not recognise. The session list and the sign-in history read the method the session and the auth event now record, and the agent's sessions stay apart as they already do.

* Acceptance

- The session list names the method of every session, and an agent's sessions stay shown apart from its person's.
- The sign-in history names the provider where one was used, and the address the provider stated.
- A session a person does not recognise can be ended from the screen, and the list says so after it is ended.
- The screens are pinned by tests, and the strings are translated.
