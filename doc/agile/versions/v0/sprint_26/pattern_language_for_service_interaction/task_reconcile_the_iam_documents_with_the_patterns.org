* Result

Four pages now name their patterns. The service-to-service guide opens as the
decision guide of the identity group and its by-layer table has a pattern

impersonation was the pattern the platform chose; it is one use of Token
Exchange. The two plans stay as they are: they are point-in-time records, and
the pattern pages cite them.
