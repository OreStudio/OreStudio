* Notes

- *The screen draws a dash and states the gap, rather than a zero.* The staged
  count needs the coverage operation and the published count needs a provenance
  read, and neither is served. A zero in that column would say the run wrote
  nothing, which is the failure this arc exists to find, so the screen says what
  is absent and why. The note is the one place the screen admits a gap, and the
  task closes it when the operations land.
- *Readiness answers three of its four reasons today.* No dataset row, no
  artefact type and no target subject are all readable from the four
  collections, so =readinessOf= decides them. The fourth, no staged rows, waits
  on the coverage operation; the reasons card names it with its owner so a
  reader is not misled into thinking the metadata check is the whole answer.
- *The area is gated on the four reads it makes*, in the menu entry and in the
  card, so a person without =dq::datasets:read= and its three siblings is not
  offered a screen that would answer 403.
- *The screen is written from the shared parts*, not from the prototype markup:
  =ScreenFrame=, =PageHeader=, =Notice=, =Tag=, =AreaTrail= and the house table
  rows. The prototypes stay the design record; nothing is imported from them.
