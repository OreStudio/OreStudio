* Plan

One area declaration per model, then the generator writes the route unit, its tests, its
recipe and its hurl scripts. The classification rules that decide the paths are repointed by
hand, because the decisions live outside the generator.

The move exposed a collision worth finding before a bulk edit shipped it. The inventory had
classified the generated session collection into =auth=, where the session door already
answers: =POST /api/v1/auth/sessions= signs in and =DELETE= signs out. Two routes cannot own
one path for different reasons. The door keeps it because it mints the credential, and the
collection becomes =/api/v1/access/sessions=, the ledger an administrator reads.

Two models declare nothing. =auth_event= and =role_permission_junction= render no route unit,
so the declaration would have been inert; their only addresses come from operation routes.
