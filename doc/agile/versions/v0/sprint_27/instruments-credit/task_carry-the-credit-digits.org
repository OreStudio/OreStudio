* Goal

Every credit money value is stored and exported as the digits the document wrote, and every credit numeric column has the type its kind needs: money is an exact decimal, a continuous quantity is a binary float, and the column and the domain type agree.

* Acceptance

- UpfrontFee and Strike keep the document's digits, through the reapply script.
- A continuous quantity (FixedRecoveryRate, AttachmentPoint, DetachmentPoint, ParticipationRate) stays a binary float.
- The tranche columns are double precision, so each column agrees with its domain type; recovery_rate stays double precision.
- A mapper test holds the digits of the money member the mapper stores, and the goldens that change are numeric text only.
- The ORE suite passes.

* Plan

1. *Classify.* Sort every credit numeric column and binding member into money
   or continuous, with the exact-money check of the data-oriented design page.
   Money is an exact decimal. A continuous quantity stays a binary float. The
   column and the domain type must agree.
2. *The binding.* Add a table of credit money members to the reapply script:
   the two =UpfrontFee= members and the index option's =Strike=. The script
   patches the struct, the getter, the element info and the serializer of each,
   and it refuses an anchor that is missing or not unique.
3. *The mapper.* The forward mapping already reads a lexical leaf through
   =exact_decimal=. The reverse mapping writes the strike with =set_exact=,
   which moves to the boundary header so both mappers share it.
4. *The tranche columns.* The tranche points are continuous quantities. Their
   columns become =double precision=, so they agree with their =double= domain
   type.
5. *Tests and goldens.* A mapper test holds the strike's digits, a binding test
   holds the strike and the upfront fee, and the goldens that change are
   regenerated.

* Notes

- The first version of this unit made the recovery rate, the tranche points and
  the participation rate lexical leaves and the recovery rate an exact decimal.
  That was wrong. The data-oriented design page lists the recovery rate and the
  tranche fraction as continuous quantities, which stay binary floats, and the
  review criteria the data-model skill walks showed the contradiction. The
  unit was corrected before it was raised: those members are not leaves, the
  recovery rate keeps its type, and the tranche columns follow their domain
  type. The participation rate is treated as continuous like a weight; the
  page does not name it, so the operator can overrule that.
- The design, the ER diagram, the memory and the runbook carried the same
  mistake and are corrected in this unit.
