* Goal

The system administrator sets how the installation's market behaves while it
runs, without restarting the synthetic service: how often the regime turns,
how hard a panic spreads, and how fast the simulated world runs. Today the
regime rates, the heat's jump and decay, and the market speed are fixed in the
service at start.

* Acceptance

- The scheduler takes a command that sets the regime's two transition rates,
  the heat's jump and decay, and the market speed, applied at the clock's now
  and recorded in the trace.
- A set whose heat loop gain is one or more, or a speed that is not positive, is
  refused with the number named.
- A speed change reaches a curve's step when the curve next starts, and the tab
  says so.
- The Market regime tab offers the five numbers to a system session beside the
  switch, and shows them read-only to a tenant.
