The options struct of a library or a process, written from a field group model
that names its header. A model turns it on with
=:ores.cpp.field-group.options_header.enabled: true= and turns the domain
header off with =:ores.cpp.field-group.field_group_header.enabled: nil=.

A field with =:secret: t= is a member but stays out of the wire shape, so the
dump the printer writes never holds it, and the guard counts it. The printer is
declared here and defined by hand, because each one decides what a log line
of its options shows. Declarations the struct needs beside it, such as a
conversion to another library's options, fill the paste point.
