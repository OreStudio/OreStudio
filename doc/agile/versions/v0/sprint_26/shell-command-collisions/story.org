* Goal

The shell has one owner per menu and per verb. A duplicate registration fails loudly at startup instead of being resolved silently by the order of the calls in repl.cpp, and the dead hand-written verbs are migrated or deleted.

* Acceptance

- No two shell command units register the same menu name, and a duplicate would fail at startup rather than be shadowed.
- The eight dead verbs are either migrated to the generated menus or deleted with the reason recorded.
- The shell's command surface is unchanged for every verb that currently answers.
