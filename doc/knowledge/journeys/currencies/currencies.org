#+title: Currencies and FX journeys
#+description: Currencies, the countries and calendars they belong to, currency pairs, and the desk groups that gather them.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:user_journey:refdata:
#+created: 2026-10-04
#+updated: 2026-10-25

A currency is reference data many other things lean on: amounts round by it,
dates settle on its calendars, and desks group it. This group holds the
journeys that keep a currency and the records around it correct, so that the
person corrects a currency on one screen instead of visiting one list per
table.

The three journeys were judged on 2026-10-25 for the
[[id:61C6B0A5-03A5-4424-8EA3-EE7E587629FD][screen pipeline]]: each is a CRUD variation of the standard record screen
with links, the pair's create dialog has two groups and a derived key, the
ORE export is the one own action and waits on the server, and the Qt import of
one file is dropped in favour of the ORE import area. No workflow is needed.
The classifications the pickers offer are kept by
[[id:8D2C0A98-83B9-4C24-89F0-900DF14B2936][Maintain a classification list]].
