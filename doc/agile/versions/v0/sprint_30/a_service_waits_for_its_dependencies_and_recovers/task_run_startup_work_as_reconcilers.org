* Goal

A service registers its startup work with the runner as reconcilers. A
reconciler reads what exists, writes what is missing, and is safe to repeat.
The runner runs each one when the service becomes ready, again each time it
leaves the degraded state, and at a stated interval. Phase 2 of
=service_bootstrap_phases.org= becomes the place a service registers them.
