* Goal

Implement [[id:7AE57098-08D4-4D4A-83D6-918FE520B68E][A pillar names the
market series it reads]]: a curve pillar states the market series its quote
comes from, so the bootstrap no longer derives the series from the ORE
vocabulary. A person can then read a blueprint's inputs, and a curve can be
built from any series the party holds.

The task carries the model change, the reader change, the pillar picker, the
seed, the tests, and the recreation of the demonstration tenant.

* Acceptance

- =ir_curve_bootstrap_pillar= carries =market_series_id=, a soft reference to
  =ores.marketdata.market_series=, required on a new row.
- The Add and Edit pillar dialogs offer the party's series through the standard
  record picker and draw the chosen one by its oresmd URI.
- =curve_pillar_reader= reads the observations of the named series at the as-of,
  and derives the datum's term from the pillar's end tenor as it does today.
- The bootstrap's read path calls neither =make_pillar_quote_key= nor its
  projections, and =curve_republish_service= no longer validates an output datum
  through =ore_key_codec::write=.
- =ir_curve_feed= builds the series it publishes as oresmd datums natively. It
  composes no ORE key.
- =series_classifier= and its seeded rules are keyed by the oresmd identity, and
  the ingest paths in =feed_ingest_loop= and =fixing_ingest= classify by it. This
  is the largest unit: the rules are data, and every series a feed creates passes
  through them.
- =oresmd_uri_codec= accepts a datum with no ORE spelling.
- =ore_key_codec= is called only by =import_service=, =ore_export_service= and
  their tests.
- The test suite proves no runtime path outside the ORE XML boundary calls the
  codec, and a curve builds from a hand-seeded series that no feed publishes.
- The demonstration tenant is recreated and reseeded, and the scenario walks a
  curve to publication against the named series.
