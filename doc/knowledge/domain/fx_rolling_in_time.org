#+title: A spot position is rolled forward with a tom-next swap
#+description: Tom-next rollover of open spot positions and how its swap points carry the interest differential.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

An open spot position is carried to the next value date with a tom-next swap. The swap's points reflect the interest differential between the two currencies.

* Detail

Brokers roll open positions at a daily cut-off, usually 5 pm New York time, and adjust the position with the tom-next points.
