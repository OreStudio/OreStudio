* What

A re-created record continues the deleted one's history, and the deletion itself is not an entry. In the sign off scenario of 2026-10-25, Acme.Scenario was derived, deleted and derived again three times under the same code; the History tab of the last one showed five entries, v1 Created and v2 to v4 as Changed with the reason system.new_record and No changes, one per re-creation, and no entry for any deletion. The page's lead said version 1 while the history said v4, so the record read and the history disagree on the version. Either the deletion is a version of its own that the timeline draws as Deleted, or a re-created key starts its history again; and the version the page shows must be the one the history counts. Found on calendars; the generic history read serves every record kind, so every record screen has it.

* Why

The timeline is the record's story; a deletion that leaves no entry and a re-creation drawn as a change
with no changes mislead the reader, and the version the page shows must be the one the history counts.
