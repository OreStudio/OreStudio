* Acceptance

- The forward publish row and the surface publish row hold the withdrawing account, the instant, the reason and the request, written by one apply for every kind.
- A withdrawal of a set already withdrawn, or of one with an open withdrawal, is refused with a message that names which.
- The withdrawal is reachable from the screen that shows a published set, and the control is live rather than inert.
