This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Activity and volatility as two mutually exciting coordinates: a bivariate Hawkes
whose activity coordinate is the shared heat the platform already carries. The
rank-one kernel =K = diag(n) + m 1'= becomes a 2x2 block of the same shape, and
stability stops being the scalar test =max n + sum m < 1= and becomes a spectral
radius: the largest eigenvalue of the mean kernel must be below one.

* Why

A real panic is self-reinforcing in both directions: a burst of trades raises
volatility, and volatility raises the appetite to trade. One dimension can only
carry half of that, so today's factor can speed a market up or calm it down but
cannot feed on itself the way a crisis does.

It depends on the volatility channel, so it is captured rather than scoped: the
design note states it as out of scope.
