* What

Store the changes that move tick volume as timestamped events beside the tick
counters: a regime pin and its release, a feed start and stop, a change to a
feed's arrival configuration, and a service start. A reader then asks what
changed at a time with one query, instead of searching the journal of several
services.

* Why

On 2026-10-26 the machine went down overnight. Two defects together caused it: the shared Hawkes heat ran the synthetic feeds at about 300 times their configured rate, and the NATS client kept every message it received. Finding both took ad hoc sampling of systemd and /proc, parsing marketdata's once-a-minute INGEST STATUS log line, NATS monitoring endpoints, Postgres row counts and a core dump. The volume rose when the regime was pinned to =active= the night
before, which multiplies arrivals by four. The only record of the pin was a chat
transcript. The journal of the crashed boot was partly lost.
