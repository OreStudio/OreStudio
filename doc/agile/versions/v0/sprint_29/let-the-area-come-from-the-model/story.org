* Acceptance

- An entity that declares #+http_area: renders its routes under that area.
- An entity that declares no area renders under its component, so no existing URL moves.
- A declared area that is not in the published list fails a check.
- A codegen test covers both cases.

* Decisions

- The area is declared per entity, not per component, because section 11.2 lets one component
  publish several areas and ores.iam already needs two.
- The default is the component, so the mechanism lands without a migration in the same diff
  and no client sees a URL change it did not ask for.
- The published areas are the list the inventory is classified against. A second list would
  drift, and a URL is the most expensive thing in the system to change.

* Result

An entity model states the area its routes publish, and the route plan resolves it in the
one place the path is built. Nothing declares an area yet, so the generated routes are
byte-identical to what they were, and the gate that refuses an unpublished area is in place
for the moves that follow.
