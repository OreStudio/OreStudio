* Decisions

- The red continuous jobs are a capacity problem, not a code defect.
  No failing job holds a compile or test error. The one Linux job that
  passed built at a 17.96 % sccache hit rate and took 4h05m. The
  failing Linux clang jobs built at 3.21 %, and the macOS debug job at
  0.00 %.
- A cold build exceeds both budgets: the six-hour job limit and the
  macOS runner's disk.
- The fix belongs in its own story. This story ends at the root cause
  and a named change per job.
