* Result

* Result

The column count does not cause the failure. The widest entity in the build,
=swap_leg= with seventy-three bindings, compiles in the same unity unit that
fails on the trade entity's nineteen, and the compiler already runs with
=/constexpr:depth100000 /constexpr:steps10000000=.

The investigation records the evidence, the working hypothesis -- the
accumulated instantiation context of a sixteen-source unity unit, compounded by
=column_list= nesting a type per column -- and three recommendations: flatten
the generated column list, take the widest entities out of the unity batch, and
do not trim the entity.
