Should role and permission junction tables have change reason support? Analyse
whether the audit trail benefit justifies the additional complexity for these
administrative tables.

* Answered

The audit trail did justify it, and the answer is the whole tail rather than a
reason alone. =ores_iam_role_permissions_tbl= carries =assigned_by=,
=assigned_at=, =change_reason_code= and =change_commentary=, the same four
columns =ores_iam_account_roles_tbl= already had, with the same insert trigger.
A role's bundle can be written, and a write nobody can attribute is worse than
no write, which is what settled it. The rule is recorded in
[[id:08169F5D-29B0-4758-968A-3C1875203A8A][Shape the role catalogue]].
