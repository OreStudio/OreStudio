* Goal

A bond's static data is stored once per security and referenced by trades,
matching ORE's split between the trade and the reference datum.

The collapse (PR 4, decision D14) left two identities where this component
carried three: the trade and the security. The instrument is now the trade,
so what remains is the security. It is still missing: every bond table
hangs off the trade, so a coupon schedule and a future's contract terms
are repeated once per trade instead of held once per security, and
=bond_issue= -- the one entity already shared -- is missing six fields ORE
has and carries =bond_notional=, which ORE holds on the trade.

This ports the abandoned sprint 25 task =0C525D24= onto the collapsed
model, and carries defect 15 from the owner's ruling of 2026-09-28.

* Acceptance

- The shared bond entities are keyed by the ORE reference datum id, which
  is =SecurityId= for a bond and =ContractName= for a bond future. No
  entity needs the words "reference data" in its name: =bond_issue= is
  already the shape to follow.
- =bond_future= keeps only what ORE keeps on the trade -- =ContractName=,
  =ContractNotional=, =LongShort=, =ApplyConversionFactor= and
  =UseFuturePrice= -- and its thirteen contract terms move to a shared
  entity keyed by the contract name.
- =bond_leg= and its rate, amount and amortization tables hang off the
  security rather than the trade.
- =bond_issue= gains the six fields ORE has and we lack: =CreditGroup=,
  =VolatilityCurveId=, =PriceQuoteMethod=, =PriceQuoteBaseValue=,
  =SubType= and =PriceType=. It loses =bond_notional=, which ORE holds on
  the trade.
- The fields duplicated against the leg tables -- currency, coupon rate,
  coupon frequency and day count -- are held once.
- A trade that states its bond inline rather than by reference still
  round-trips: the inline case populates the security rather than
  bypassing it.
- Defect 15: an underlying is an unqualified name. The underlyings the
  ORE documents name become qualified references, and each place the
  document states only a free name is recorded with its evidence.
- The ORE round trip stays at zero loss across the bond products and the
  trade envelope.
- =check_component_drift.py --component trading-cpp --dry-run= reports
  nothing, the whole-tree build exits 0, and the suites pass on a
  recreated database.

* Plan

ORE is the ground truth. The job is to apply data-oriented
principles to ORE's data model, not to invent one.

** The relationship ORE states

=bondReferenceDatum= is exactly =bondData= minus =SecurityId=,
=BondNotional=, =Payer= and =CreditRisk=. Everything else on a bond is
static data about the security, and the four exceptions are the trade's
own: which security, how much, which side, and whether credit risk
applies.

=bondFutureData= says the same in the other direction: v17 cut it to
=ContractName=, =ContractNotional= and =LongShort= plus the new
=ApplyConversionFactor= and =UseFuturePrice=, and moved the thirteen
contract terms into =bondFutureReferenceDatum=.

=ReferenceData= is a list of =ReferenceDatum=, each with an =id=
attribute, an optional =validFrom=, a =Type= element and one payload from
a closed choice. The key is the datum id, so a bond's is its =SecurityId=
and a bond future's is its =ContractName=.

** Where our model disagrees

- =bond_future= holds twelve contract terms that belong to the contract,
  so ten trades on one Bund future repeat them ten times.
- =bond_leg= and its rate, amount and amortization tables hold the coupon
  schedule, currency and day count, which in ORE are =LegData= inside
  =bondReferenceDatum=, so they belong to the security.
- =bond_issue= is the entity already shared and keyed by =security_id=,
  and it is =bondReferenceDatum= under another name. It is missing the
  six fields above, it carries =bond_notional=, and its =currency=,
  =coupon_rate=, =coupon_frequency_code= and =day_count_code= duplicate
  the leg tables.

** Sequence

Shared entities first, because the per-trade tables point at them. Model,
mapper and tests move together; no commit removes a column before its
replacement exists. The corpus round trip runs before the task closes,
as it did for the collapse.
