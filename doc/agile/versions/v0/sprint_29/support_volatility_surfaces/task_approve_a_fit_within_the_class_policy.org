* Goal

A fresh installation runs the fits that pass objective checks without a click. The calibration policy holds an approval mode, manual or auto, and the rules a fit must pass: a minimum of returns, starts that agree, a persistence range, and a variance that reacts to moves. In auto mode each new fit is checked; one that passes is approved through the review verb with the policy, its id and its version as the decider, and the pair's feeds are re-pointed; one that fails stays proposed with the rule it failed as its reason. The review verb records its decider, a person or a policy, so history reads the same either way.

* Acceptance

- The FX policy is seeded in auto mode with the rules: at least 250 returns, starts agree, persistence from 0.80 to 0.995, and an average reaction, alpha plus half of gamma, of at least 0.01. Each class's policy holds its own values.
- An applied run approves each fit that passes and leaves each that fails proposed, naming the failed rule; on the data of 2026-11-03, 13 pairs pass and 11 wait, as =evidence/design_fx_preview_2026-11-03.txt= records.
- An approval's history entry names its decider: the person, or the policy with its id and version.
- A fit approved by the policy can still be rejected, and an exception approved, by a person.

* Plan

The policy owns the rules, so six columns join it: the approval mode and the
five rule values, with CHECKs, regenerated from the codegen model. A pure
function judges a fit's evidence against the rules, so a new fit and a stored
proposal are judged the same way. The applied run sets each fit's reason before
it is stored, and after storing approves a passing fit through the existing
review verb with the policy as the decider. The policy summary carries the rules
to the policy page, and the policy save carries the fields the screen does not
edit.

* Result

The FX policy approves the fits that pass its rules, and a person sees only the
rest. One applied run on a fresh database decided all 28 pairs: 14 approved by
the policy, each with the reason "Approved by the fx calibration policy …
version 1" and the checks it passed; 11 proposed, each naming the rules it
failed, such as "Waits for review: reaction 1.754e-11 below 0.01"; and 3
rejected by the fit for too few returns. A repeat run converged, and the audit
passed 9 of 9.

- The policy holds the approval mode and five rules, seeded in auto mode for FX,
  with CHECKs; pgTAP checks the seed and each refusal (35 of 35).
- approval_failures and approval_evidence judge a fit's evidence; unit tests
  cover each rule (848 api cases).
- The feed's calibration entry names the outcome, for example "calibration
  891711d4 approved by the class policy".
- The policy page shows the mode and the rules; a policy save keeps them.
