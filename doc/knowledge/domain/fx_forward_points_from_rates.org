#+title: Forward points follow from two money market rates
#+description: Covered interest parity on discrete money market rates with separate day-count bases, and its inverse.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

Given spot and one money market rate per currency, the forward has a closed form. The form inverts to give either rate from the points.

* Detail

$$F = S \times \frac{1 + r_q \frac{N}{B_q}}{1 + r_b \frac{N}{B_b}}$$

$r_b$ and $r_q$ are the rates of the base and quote currencies, $N$ the days, and $B_b$ and $B_q$ the day-count bases of each rate. The bases can differ, such as ACT/360 for USD and ACT/365 for JPY. The points are $(F - S)$ divided by the pip factor.

Solving for the quote currency rate:

$$r_q = \frac{B_q}{N} \left[ \frac{F}{S} \left( 1 + r_b \frac{N}{B_b} \right) - 1 \right]$$

The growth factors are inverse discount factors, so this is the discrete form of [[id:49687AD7-396A-4CD8-B96B-B3F6675C9779][Covered Interest Parity and FX Forward Pricing]]. The unit tests of =fx_forward_calculator= hold both directions.
