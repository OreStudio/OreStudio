* What

The deployment's NATS takes the default payload cap: =/varz= answers
=max_payload: 1048576=, and no =max_payload= line sits in
=build/config/nats-brave_hopper.conf=. The upload body carries the image as
base64, so the bytes on the wire grow by a third, and an image over roughly
three quarters of a megabyte exceeds the cap. The transport raises
=TransportError: MAX_PAYLOAD_EXCEEDED=, the BFF answers 503, and the file never
reaches =ores.assets= — so the validator's own =image_too_large= answer, the
one the picker and the validator both state at 2 MB, cannot be reached.

* Why

The photo replace states a rule the transport cannot honour: every image over
roughly three quarters of a megabyte — including the ones the validator would
refuse anyway — dies in the transport before anybody can judge it, and the 503
names no rule the person can act on. The 1 MB cap silently narrows a stated
2 MB limit for every upload path over the fabric, not only the profile picker.
Direction (product owner, 2026-10-05): *an upload larger than 1 MB travels over
HTTP storage, not the message fabric.* Found by the profile journeys end-to-end
run (task [[id:6CDA40DF-EBED-4F62-9804-525EEAB0282C][Build the profile
screen]], 2026-10-05).
