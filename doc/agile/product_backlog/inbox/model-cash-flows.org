* What

Nothing in the tree models cash. Four knowledge pages describe it —
[[id:A281A64C-5A52-4747-BBDD-3CECB7A72F3C][cash]],
[[id:13341B12-0393-4ECF-B392-CD68A4C0CDFB][cash flow]],
[[id:940CB31A-BD50-45D6-98AC-4B0D53973825][cash position]] and
[[id:AAF5F9CB-D854-40A8-AEB5-50D998A9F0C0][linked cash]] — and no
component has a table, a domain class or a link for any of them. This
capture is the analysis that has to happen before one is built.

The question is what represents an amount of cash. Three shapes are on
the table:

- A *cash flow*: a dated amount, in a currency, with a direction against
  the trade or the book that explains it. This is the detail a stream
  decomposes into.
- A *trade that represents cash*. ORE prices a =CashPosition=, which
  carries a currency and an amount, and the knowledge page calls it "the
  trade representation of an amount of cash", stand-alone or as a
  component of a total return swap. A system or dummy trade of this kind
  is what a sweep, a netting or a summarisation run would book.
- *Both*: the flows as detail, and a cash trade as the aggregate the
  engine and the ledger act on.

* Why

Three pieces of work already in flight depend on the answer.

- *Linked cash.* The page says some cash explains a trade and some stands
  alone. The typed links task in flight joins two trades, so it cannot
  carry a trade-to-cash relation, and linked cash stays out of its seed
  until this is settled.
- *Summary cash and orphan cash.* Summarisation strips settled cash into
  summary deals and orphans the children. Nothing holds either today.
- *The processes that move cash.* The funding roll, the spot sweep and
  the profit cleardown all generate cash movements. They wait on the run
  model, and every one of them will need somewhere to put the cash.

* Questions the analysis has to answer

- Is a cash flow an entity of its own, or a projection computed from the
  trades and instruments that produce it?
- Does a cash trade need the whole trade envelope, or is it a record the
  aggregation reports happen to read alongside trades?
- If it is a trade, what does it reference, and does it carry an
  instrument?
- How is summary cash held, and what orphans the children it replaces?
- Does a cash flow point at the trade that explains it, or does the trade
  carry its flows?
- What does ORE need in order to value a cash position? ORE is the ground
  truth for what can be priced, so the answer constrains the model.
