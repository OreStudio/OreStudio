#+title: Decode or refuse a storage put's content encoding
#+description: The storage put handler stores the content as sent and ignores content_encoding, so a base64 put stores the base64 text and reports its length.
#+type: capture
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :storage:correctness:
#+created: 2026-11-02
#+updated: 2026-11-02

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

=objects_handler::put= in =ores.storage.core= stores =req->content= as sent and
never reads =content_encoding=. The model says the field is empty for raw bytes,
or names the encoding the caller applied. The handler must decode a known
encoding, such as =base64=, before it stores the bytes, and refuse an unknown
one with a bad request. =size_bytes= must then report the decoded length.

* Why

Observed on 2026-11-02 in brave_hopper, while proving the storage codec move:
=objects put-objects scratch codec/smoke.txt aGVsbG8gY29kZWM= base64= replied
=size_bytes: 16=, the length of the base64 text, not of the 11 decoded bytes.
A caller that sends base64 because NATS carries text gets the text back, not
its object. The behaviour is on main and does not come from the codec move.
