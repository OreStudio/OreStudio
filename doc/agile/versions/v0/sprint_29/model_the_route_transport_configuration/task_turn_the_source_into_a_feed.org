* Acceptance

- The feed table carries the session, service and provider key.
- The existing official sources are migrated to one session and their feeds.
- The old kind column is removed from the feed.

* Plan

The source entity is reshaped into the feed rather than joined by a second
table. A source was already one adapter instance of a provider; the feed is the
same row with the two facts a source lacked: the session that carries it and the
service it offers.

The model keeps the surrogate id, because routes and ticks already key on it,
and keeps the unique name, because the adapter announces that name and the
screens show it. =provider_code= becomes =publisher_code=, the =kind= column and
its lookup are removed, and =session_code=, =service= and =provider_key= are
added. =is_default= stays: one feed of a publisher is the one a new subscription
routes to.

The rows are migrated in the populate scripts, not by an ALTER: the database is
built from the scripts, and the old sources are seeds. One Frankfurter session
carries the four public feeds (=ecb=, =boc=, =imf=, =tcmb=) with =end_of_day=;
the simulated feeds get the simulator session and =real_time=; the derived and
import feeds get their own internal session.

The runtime follows the rename mechanically: =market_data_source= becomes
=feed=, =source_id= becomes =feed_id=, and the demand calculator, tick
translator, usage planner and ingest loop read the feed. The generated entity
carries the new names; the hand-written service files are renamed with them.

The routes migrate from =source_id= to =feed_id=, keeping their party and
publisher. Their key changes to (party, publisher, class) in the later task.

* Notes

Work in progress. The model is now =ores.marketdata.feed= (same id, new file),
the feed entity is generated, the old =market_data_source= generated files are
removed, and a mechanical rename (=market_data_source= -> =feed=,
=source_id= -> =feed_id=) ran across the marketdata tree and its SQL.

Remaining, in order:

1. Replace both source seeds with a session seed and feed rows: the four public
   feeds through =frankfurter-eu= (=end_of_day=, keys =ECB=, =BOC=, =IMF=,
   =TCMB=), the simulated and internal feeds through =synthetic-local=
   (=real_time=).
2. Rename =provider_code= to =publisher_code= in the feed's consumers and the
   route model; remove the =kind= reads.
3. Regenerate marketdata at every address, then the shell adapter, and compile.
4. Rework the runtime's source assumptions: adapter announce names map to
   feeds, route =feed_id=, usage/billing reads.
5. Delete the source-kind entity and its uses.
6. Tests: pgtap for the feed rows and the default rule; runtime tests.
