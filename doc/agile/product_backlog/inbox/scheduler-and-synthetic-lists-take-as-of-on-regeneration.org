* What

The entities of =ores.scheduler= and =ores.synthetic= have no point-in-time
list read yet. PR 2683 made =as_of= the default for every entity with a
validity window and applied it to the generated files by a three-way merge.
Every merge in those two components conflicted, because their generated files
predate the ordering and filter templates, so both were left out.

* Why

Their clean-up stories, =clean-scheduler= and =clean-synthetic=, regenerate the
files, and the default then brings =as_of= with it. Nothing needs writing by
hand; each clean-up should check that its list protocols carry =as_of= and its
services refuse a malformed one with =as_of_invalid=.
