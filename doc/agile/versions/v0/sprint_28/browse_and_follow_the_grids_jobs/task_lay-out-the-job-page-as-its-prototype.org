* Acceptance

- The job, the runs, the time and the batch's jobs sit in the left column, and the files, the release and the lineage in the right.
- The Find bar takes a batch reference, a job id or a result id and opens the job, or the Jobs tab at the batch, or says nothing matches.
- The link back names the batch and opens the Jobs tab with that batch open.

* Plan

Compare the approved prototype in =doc/prototypes/compute/follow-a-job/= with the built page, panel by panel, and close the gaps that need no new data. The prototype splits the screen in two columns, 3 to 2: the job, the runs, the time and the batch's jobs on the left, the files, the release and the lineage on the right. It has a Find bar for a batch reference, a job id or a result id, and a link back to the batch. The page stacked every panel in one wide column and linked back to the Jobs tab.
