* Summary

This prototype draws the screens of the three currencies journeys,
[[id:9E46C8C2-05FF-4F31-BFC1-5B634963DBE3][Add or correct a currency]], [[id:250CA101-1540-4A42-97F8-E73AA3400DA0][Group currencies for the desk]] and
[[id:FA1D8A3C-70C6-4E35-BEA2-F617851C6B55][Set up a currency pair]], as the [[id:2BF7F03F-6BF4-45FF-8E26-4D3B4E2D943D][Record screen standard]] settled them,
so the review is about the currencies topic and not about the language. It is
the second prototype of the [[id:C0D1A833-3E57-489A-A4D3-8C44266D3D6D][screen pipeline]]: the web already draws the
three screens, and this page says where each moves to meet the standard and
the owner's decision of 2026-10-25, that every kind with an ORE document
exchanges it through the standard /Import/ and /Export/ pair, which the
currencies prove first.

It is throwaway. It reads no service: the fourteen currencies, six desk groups
and sixteen pairs are the Acme seed as the server holds them on 2026-10-25,
cut to a page; the history, the two user changes and the import's file are
invented.

Open [[file:currencies-desk-groups-and-pairs/][the prototype]]. Every state has an address, for example
=?screen=currency&code=EUR&tab=history= or =?screen=import&step=4&file=ok=.

* Detail

** What it asks

Five questions the journeys' judgement left to the prototype:

1. How do the two standard buttons stand, and what do they open? Export and
   Import stand between Refresh and Add on the currencies list (RS-LIST-03,
   RS-ORE-02). Export opens an operation dialog that names the document and
   its count and saves on one press (RS-ORE-04). Import opens the steps at
   their own address, =/refdata/currencies/import=, because a table kind has
   no target to choose and the four steps that remain are a page, not a
   dialog (RS-ORE-05). A reader sees Export and not Import.
2. What kinds are Day basis and ORE currency type? Selects over their lists,
   not free text (RS-WRITE-01): ORE currency type over ORE's own three values,
   Day basis over the day count conventions. Neither list exists on the server
   yet; the build task adds them as classifications. The numbers are number
   controls (RS-WRITE-32).
3. How is the pair's dialog drawn? One dialog of two groups, /The pair/ and
   /Quoting convention/ (RS-WRITE-09); the pair code is derived and shown as a
   line, not asked for (RS-WRITE-07); two equal legs are refused before the
   save, with Add disabled (RS-WRITE-08); the sample rate is a line of help
   under the convention (RS-WRITE-12); and the dialog says what a failed
   second write leaves.
4. What does the currencies list show? Names where the page shows names: the
   monetary nature and the market tier draw their labels as links to their
   classification, not their codes (ES-WORDS-06, RS-PAGE-08).
5. How many members has a desk group? The list and the Details draw the count
   from the members, which waits on a read by group on the server (the
   journey's gap); the prototype draws the intended screen.

** The screens and the rules each follows

- *The lists.* Breadcrumb, plural title and one sentence (RS-LIST-01,
  RS-LIST-02); Refresh with its load time, then Import and Export on the
  currencies, then Add (RS-LIST-03); a search box with Clear (RS-LIST-04);
  sortable columns from the kind's column list, the pager with the count first
  and 15 rows (RS-LIST-17), the state in the address (RS-LIST-20); a click on
  a row opens the page (RS-LIST-10); codes in monospace with their flags, a
  pair's flag as its two codes (RS-ICON-03); the nothing-matches state offers
  /Clear the search/ (RS-LIST-21). Desk groups are in display order with
  their member count; pairs show their legs as links to the currencies.
- *The record pages.* The breadcrumb ends at the key and the title is the
  name with the flag (RS-PAGE-01); the lead reads key and version
  (RS-PAGE-02); the actions stand in this order: Refresh with its load time,
  the access mark, Edit, Delete, each titled by the record's name
  (RS-PAGE-03, RS-FEEDBACK-02). The tabs are Details, the link panels and
  History: Countries, Calendars and Desk groups on a currency; Members on a
  desk group; Calendars on a pair (RS-PAGE-09). The details stand in four
  columns in the field table's order, with the fixed reason under the key, a
  classification as a link to its list, a sample under the format and the
  precision, and the last-changed line at the end (RS-PAGE-05 to RS-PAGE-10,
  ES-REF-01). A pair's Details are two cards, the pair and its convention,
  each with its own last-changed line, because the convention is a part with
  its own version.
- *The links.* Countries, calendars, desk groups and members are link panels:
  key and name, the name a link to the record's page, Remove on each row and
  Add under the table (RS-RELATED-01 to RS-RELATED-03). Add picks a record
  not linked yet; Remove says the linked record itself stays.
- *The dialogs.* Edit is titled /Edit/ and the record's name and says when
  the record was loaded (RS-WRITE-20, RS-WRITE-16); the key is fixed with its
  reason (RS-WRITE-03); the currency's sixteen fields stand in three groups,
  Identity, Display and Market (RS-WRITE-09); one line of help under a
  control (RS-WRITE-12); Why and Commentary close the form; the footer holds
  Cancel then Save or Add (RS-WRITE-14); a create states its reason in a
  note and lists what is still to fill (RS-WRITE-17, RS-WRITE-13); closing
  with changes asks first (RS-WRITE-24). Delete confirms, names the record,
  says what is lost and asks for a reason (RS-WRITE-25); the currency's says
  that nothing checks whether trades, books or pairs still use it.
- *Export.* An operation dialog titled /Export/: "Writes currencyconfig.xml,
  the ORE currency configuration, with the tenant's 14 currencies as they
  stand now", the note that the server writes and the browser saves, and the
  button /Export/. The reply is a notice with the file name and the count
  (RS-ORE-03, RS-ORE-04).
- *Import.* The steps page titled /Import currencies/, with the rail at the
  left as the web's journey pages draw it: /Choose the file/, which accepts
  the =CurrencyConfig= root and refuses another kind naming the root it
  found; /What the file holds/, the counts the server read; /What the system
  does not know/, three lists each drawn only when not empty, a value with
  no row and the choice it needs, an element with no column and why it is
  dropped, a duplicate and which to keep; and /The difference, and the
  review/, removed rows first with /Keep it/ as the default, then changed
  with /Take the file's/, then added, the reach in one line, and Why and
  Commentary before /Import/ (RS-ORE-05, RS-FEEDBACK-06). Nothing is written
  before the last step, and the reply is a notice with what was added,
  changed and kept.
- *History.* The shared timeline of the Entity screen specification
  (ES-HIST-01 to 18): the chart, the rail newest first, the reason and the
  fields that changed, the detail collapsed, Revert on every version but the
  current one for a writer (RS-HIST-02), and the box of what the history
  cannot show. A pair's history is the pair's story: its own versions and
  its convention's, merged and named /Convention/ (RS-HIST-01); the links of
  every kind are named in the box as unversioned.

** What is fake

The history of EUR, XAU, G11 and EUR/USD. The two user currencies' last
changes. The import's file, its counts, its unknowns and its difference. The
member counts, which the server cannot read by group yet. The day basis and
ORE currency type lists. The flags, which are the code in a box. Nothing is
written: Save, Add, Remove, Delete, Export and Import show a notice and go
back.

** Where the code lives

=index.html= is the shell, =prototype.css= the look from the web app's tokens
and the steps rail from the web's journey pages, and =prototype.js= the data
and the screens. The query string is the state; every link changes it. The
script =evidence/render_currencies_prototype.mjs= beside the story renders
every state with Playwright and fails on a script error.

** The verdict

Open. The owner signs it off before the build task starts. The build then
moves the three web screens to this: the Import and Export pair with its
three server operations and the shared =OreExchange=, the pair dialog as the
shared record dialog with two groups, the selects for Day basis and ORE
currency type with their lists, the labels on the currencies list, the
History tabs on the shared timeline with the convention in the pair's story,
and the titled Edit and Delete.

** What it does not settle

- The two writes behind the pair dialog, and the pair code check on the
  server: the journey's capture carries them.
- The member count of a desk group, which needs a read by group.
- The role below the tenant administrator; the reader here is any person
  who may not write.
- Whether the Import steps of a configuration kind, which has a target to
  choose, are the same page with a fifth step; the engine settings topic
  draws it.
