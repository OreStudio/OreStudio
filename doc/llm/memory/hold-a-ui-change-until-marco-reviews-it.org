#+title: Hold a UI change until Marco reviews it
#+description: Do not merge a change that a person sees until Marco has looked at the screen. Open the pull request, show what to open and what to look at, and wait for his word.

* What it means in practice

- A pull request that changes a screen is opened and left open. Marco reviews the
  running screen, then says whether to merge.
- The pull request states where to look and what to look at, so the review is a
  glance rather than a hunt: the address, the account to sign in as, and the one
  thing the change is meant to show.
- This is not a pause on the work. Build the change, prove it, open it, and move
  to the next piece while the review waits.
- A change nobody sees, such as a seed, a service registration or a check, still
  follows the usual rule: prove it and merge it.

* Why

A screen is judged by looking at it. Two faults in one afternoon looked correct
in a unit test and wrong on the page: an identifier read from the field that did
not carry it, and a translation key drawn instead of its value. Neither is
visible in a diff.
