* Goal

describe_product serves every product's schema: its fields, with type, list or scalar, accepted values and description, and its forms, with the script and engine of each; a script-only product's fields are its script's inputs, and annotations are held as data.

* Acceptance

- every product of the catalogue and every script with no element of its own has a schema
- labels, coded values, defaults and descriptions are annotations held as data
- each form states the script it runs and the engine its product tag prices with
- a test fails when a product has no schema
