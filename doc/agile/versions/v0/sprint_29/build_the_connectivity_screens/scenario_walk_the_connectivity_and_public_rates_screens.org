This page documents a test scenario verifying [[id:1994DF76-4830-4510-96BD-3B524E782ED2][Prove the connectivity screens]] in [[id:4D2D0EF9-986A-49F3-91CD-29DF44548590][Build the Connectivity screens]]. It is filled in with the target screen and checklist of steps before testing starts; record the outcome by hand in =* Results=.

* Before you start

The tester opens the client on a ready environment. Do this yourself before handing over.

- The build is green and the services run: =./compass.sh build= then
  =./compass.sh services start=.
- The database is provisioned for =acme_corporation=: =./compass.sh db status=,
  and only if stale =./compass.sh db recreate -y -k= followed by the provisioning
  script =projects/ores.shell/scripts/library/provisioning/how_do_i_provision_the_system_with_acme_corporation_holding_group.ores=.
  The run must finish with no failed steps.
- The catalogue is published: call
  =ores_marketdata_publish_adapters_from_dq_fn= and
  =ores_marketdata_publish_suppliers_from_dq_fn= for the marketdata dataset.
- Terms are accepted for =ecb=, =imf=, =boc= and =tcmb=, so the official adapter
  has demand. Without them the feeds announce and fetch nothing.
- The official adapter announced the four Frankfurter feeds and the service
  accepted them (log: =Adapter 'ecb-frankfurter' ... announced: ready=).
- No live fetch is running against Frankfurter during the walkthrough; the
  download in Step 14 reads stored fixings.

* Steps

Each step is its own heading. Its body is a checklist: what to click, what to
type, and what confirms the step passed. Record the outcome in a =*** Result=
child as PASS, FAIL or PENDING with one line of evidence.

** Log in as tenant_admin

- Open the web client at the address the environment prints.
- Log in as =tenant_admin@acme_corporation= with the password from the
  provisioning run (look the account up with =compass shell= if needed).
- Confirm the shell shows the Market data area and no permission errors.

*** Result

** Open the connectivity area

- Choose Market data, then Connectivity, in the shell.
- Confirm the publisher list is the landing view and the screen title reads
  =Connectivity=.
- Confirm the four public publishers (ECB, IMF, BoC, TCMB) and the simulator
  publishers are listed.

*** Result

** Read the publishers list

- For each row, read the name, publisher code, kind and terms state.
- Confirm ECB, IMF, BoC and TCMB show their terms accepted and their feed
  count is one each.
- Confirm the list shows no invented publisher: only what the catalogue holds.

*** Result

** Open the ECB publisher

- Click the ECB row.
- Confirm the detail shows the publisher code =ecb=, its kind, its supplier
  and its feed =ecb-frankfurter=.
- Confirm the terms panel shows the accepted digest, who accepted it and when.
- Confirm the transport fields (session, environment, endpoint) read
  =frankfurter-eu=, =production=, =https://api.frankfurter.dev=.

*** Result

** Walk the supplier tree

- Open Suppliers.
- Confirm the tree groups suppliers under their publisher and shows
  =frankfurter= and =synthetic=.
- Expand =frankfurter= and confirm the sessions =frankfurter-eu= and the
  feeds that use it.
- Confirm the dashboard counts match the tree (publishers, suppliers,
  sessions, feeds).

*** Result

** Read the adapters list

- Open Adapters.
- Confirm =frankfurter= and =synthetic= are listed with their versions.
- Confirm each row states whether the adapter is installed, and that the
  installed state comes from the module's announcement, not from a seed.

*** Result

** Open the frankfurter adapter

- Click the frankfurter row.
- Confirm the detail shows the adapter code, the module version, and the
  configuration schema (the =url=, =backfill_days= and =poll_minutes= keys).
- Confirm the sessions list shows =frankfurter-eu=.

*** Result

** Open the frankfurter session

- Open the session =frankfurter-eu=.
- Confirm supplier =frankfurter=, adapter =frankfurter=, environment
  =production=, endpoint =https://api.frankfurter.dev=, enabled =true=.
- Confirm the settings panel renders the JSON as fields, not as raw text.

*** Result

** Open the ECB feed

- Open the feed =ecb-frankfurter=.
- Confirm publisher =ecb=, session =frankfurter-eu=, service =end_of_day=,
  provider key =ECB=, default =true=.
- Confirm the scope panel lists the FX items the feed serves and the vendor
  instrument behind each, and that the count matches the availability screen.

*** Result

** Whole catalogues round trip

- On the Connectivity screen, open a publisher, then a supplier, then an
  adapter, then a session, then the feed, using the links between them.
- Confirm every link lands on the record you expect and the back button
  returns to the list without losing the selection.

*** Result

** Switch to Public rates

- Choose Public rates in the shell.
- Confirm the title reads =Public rates= and no screen in this area mentions
  synthetic data.
- Confirm the same four public publishers appear.

*** Result

** Read the publisher freshnes

- For each publisher, read the last fixing date and the number of series it
  serves.
- Confirm ECB, IMF and BoC show a date no older than the last business day,
  and the series counts match the feed scope (ECB 44, IMF 48, BoC 27).
- Confirm TCMB shows its state (a date, or =no fixings yet= when its first
  fetch has not landed).

*** Result

** Read the ECB availability

- Open the ECB publisher.
- Confirm the availability table lists each currency pair the feed serves
  with its first and last fixing date.
- Confirm =EUR/USD= is present and its latest value matches the fixing
  stored for today.

*** Result

** Compare two publishers

- Open Compare, choose ECB and IMF, and select =EUR/USD=.
- Confirm the grid shows both series by date and the values agree with the
  availability pages.
- Confirm a pair only one publisher serves is marked as missing for the
  other, not shown as zero.

*** Result

** Download historical fixings

- Open the Fetch tab.
- Choose publisher =ECB=, pair =EUR/USD= (or all pairs), and a date range of
  the last 30 calendar days.
- Press Fetch. Confirm the UI reports the rows fetched and the file name.
- Download the file and open it.
- Confirm the header names the publisher, pair and date, and that each row
  carries the date and value.
- Spot check three rows against the Public rates availability page; the
  values must match exactly.
- Confirm a second fetch of the same range answers from stored fixings and
  writes no duplicate rows.

*** Result

** Close the walkthrough

- Confirm no screen shows a console error, a raw JSON payload, or a stack
  trace.
- Record the counts you saw (publishers, suppliers, adapters, sessions, feeds,
  fixings downloaded) in the Results section of the task.
