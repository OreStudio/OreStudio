* Result

** Review on 2026-11-08 (day 16 of 7)

Sprint 29 was planned from 2026-10-24 to 2026-10-31. This review runs on
2026-11-08, day 16 and eight days past the planned end. The sprint is still
open. The =* Measurements= section gives the command for each number.

Concerns written down before the data was read:

- The mission repeats the mission of sprint 28, and sprint 28 did not keep it.
- Bearings lists about 36 stories in flight, and the sprint 28 review asked for
  a limit.
- A sprint this size drifts first in its bookkeeping.

*** Goal alignment

The mission has one goal: finish the work carried out of sprint 28 before
drawing anything new from the product backlog.

| Goal | Coverage | Stories (DONE/STARTED/BACKLOG/ABANDONED) | Verdict |
|------+----------+------------------------------------------+---------|
| Finish the carried work | 58 stories, 127 of 332 tasks DONE | 3 / 13 / 41 / 1 | RED |
| Draw nothing new | 68 new stories, 380 of 491 tasks DONE | 32 / 25 / 10 / 1 | RED |

The sprint did the opposite of its mission. It finished 3 of about 58 carried
stories (5%) and 32 of 68 new stories (47%). The carried count is an estimate.
It counts a story as carried when its =#+created:= date is before the sprint or
its title ends "the remaining work". A cross-check supports the size: the
sprint 28 close sent 62 stories to the inbox, 37 whole and 25 split.

Some new work has a clear cause and does not break the mission. Hotfixes and CI
repairs are examples. The =* Hotfixes= table does not show how many: it has 34
rows, and many of them are features, such as "Support volatility surfaces" and
"Hold the session at the door". The table name does not separate unplanned
repairs from new scope.

The HTTP server arc, the volatility surfaces and the IR curves are new
capability that no carried story asked for. The mission was not revised to
admit them. Either the mission was wrong or the sprint ignored it.

Verdict RED.
