* Goal

A person browses the identity shapes as they browse any other record: a paged list of shapes, each saying what its family is for, and a page per shape with its entries and its history.

* Decisions

- An identity shape is a record keyed by a code: the asset class, the series
  subclass and the index family joined by dots. A check holds the code to the
  parts, so a code always names the family the row states.
- A shape's field rules are its entries, keyed by a uuid as a calendar rule is,
  and unique per shape and field. A two-part key would have left the entries with
  no history.
- An entry whose shape was removed speaks for no family. Refusing it would stop
  the projection of every family because of one removal.
- The record registry serves a resource of any component: its permissions take
  the component from its subject.

* Out of scope

- A removal of a shape that takes its entries with it on the server. The screen
  removes the entries first; the shell does not.
