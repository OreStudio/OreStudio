* Goal

Every provisioned party has published volatility surfaces. A step of its own, after the forward curve step, calibrates each party's blueprints from that party's published forward sets and publishes each surface under the tenant's auto-approval. It records each surface published and why it skipped any.

* Acceptance

- Provisioning Acme on a fresh database ends with a published surface for each blueprint of each operational party.
- Each surface's lineage names the party's published forward set.
- A surface that fails its arbitrage or error checks is reported by party and pair, and is not published.
- The step cannot start before the forward curve step has completed.
- A retry publishes no surface twice.
