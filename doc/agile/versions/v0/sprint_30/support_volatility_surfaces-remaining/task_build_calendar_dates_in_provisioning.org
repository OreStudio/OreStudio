* Goal

A freshly provisioned tenant has correct business days. Provisioning loads the holiday rules of every published calendar and builds its dates, so no one has to run Rebuild and a value date never falls on a holiday.

* Acceptance

- Provisioning Acme on a fresh database leaves TARGET, UnitedStates.Settlement and Japan built with their holidays.
- 2026-12-25 reads as closed in TARGET and UnitedStates.Settlement, and the generated holidays come from the QuantLib that ORE prices with.
- EUR/USD on 2026-11-09 has a spot date of 2026-11-12, because 2026-11-11 is a US holiday.
- A second provisioning run writes no duplicate dates.
- Provisioning from ores.shell and from the ores.web wizard leaves the same calendars built.
- Only calendars a currency or a pair convention names are built. Another calendar builds on demand through Rebuild.

* Plan

Sprint 24 transcribed only four calendars by hand. The owner chose to generate the rest. =projects/ores.ore/scripts/quantlib_calendars/generate.sh= compiles a small tool against the QuantLib of the ORE vendor package and writes one exception row for each day a calendar differs from a Monday to Friday week. The services keep no runtime dependency on QuantLib.

The rows reach a tenant through the =refdata.calendar_exceptions= dataset in the =base= bundle. The =build_calendars= step, after =publish_bundle=, builds only the calendars a currency or a pair convention names.

* Result

A freshly provisioned tenant has correct business days, from either client. The =build_calendars= step built 35 calendars for Acme through =ores.shell= and for a second tenant through the web provision route. EUR/USD on 2026-11-09 now has a spot date of 2026-11-12, because 2026-11-11 is a US holiday, and 2026-12-25 is closed in TARGET and UnitedStates.Settlement.

The holidays are generated from the QuantLib that ORE prices with, so dates and pricing agree. QuantLib's lists of lunar holidays stop, so later years in those calendars miss holidays; the capture tracks it.

A repeat of the build writes nothing, as =regenerate_is_extend_only_and_does_not_rewrite_existing_watermark= holds. A second provisioning of one tenant was not run. The two clients share one route to the server, and the web wizard's own screens were not driven.
