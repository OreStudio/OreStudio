* Decisions

- *The credential entity is projected across the whole stack*, exactly as the
  account is. The first cut withdrew every wire facet and left only the SQL
  schema, the domain type, the generator and the repository. That was wrong.
  "No credential column travels on the wire" is what =:no_wire:= is for: it
  keeps the member in C++ and skips it when the struct is serialised, so the
  entity can be projected like any other and still carry nothing. Removing
  the surface sidestepped the mechanism rather than using it. See the task's
  * Not done, and why.
- *The credential's generated half is reads-only.* =:read_only: true=, the
  same flag the account sets, so the generated CRUD service and its write
  verbs are absent and the repository's =write= stays for the hand-written
  service. A generated update is not harmless here: it deserialises a record
  whose =:no_wire:= secrets were skipped and replaces the row whole, which is
  the blanking bug this story exists to kill.
- *The resource carries both codes.* =iam::account_credentials:read= is what
  the generated reads check, so an administrator may ask which accounts hold
  a credential and when it changed, and may not see one.
  =iam::account_credentials:write= gates the administrative password reset,
  deliberately separate from =iam::accounts:reset_password=: a role that may
  edit a profile cannot thereby change what an account signs in with.
- *Writes stay hand-written.* A credential write is a service method over
  =account_credential_repository::write=, because a generated verb cannot
  carry a secret and must not blank one. Change-password and reset-password
  are built; TOTP enrolment, verification and disable are not.
- *A secret that must cross the wire travels on a purpose-built message.*
  TOTP enrolment hands the person a secret to scan, and =:no_wire:= skips the
  member in both directions, so it cannot be a field of the credential
  record. It is its own operation field, as =password= is on
  =save_account_request=. See the task's * Open decision on which namespace
  the write verbs are addressed from.
- *=password_salt= is gone.* The stored hash embeds its own salt, nothing
  read the column, and every writer wrote it empty. It is not carried into
  the new entity.
- *A credential change does not bump the account.* The credential is a child
  of the account in the SQL sense, but its FK is not =:bump_parent_version:=,
  so a password change leaves the profile's history alone and the two rows
  keep independent version streams.
- *A nullable skipped column reads through =get()=.* The generator emitted
  =v.secret_hash.empty()= against an =rfl::Skip=, which does not compile. The
  mapper template now branches on =:no_wire:= and reads the wrapped value.
  =projects/ores.codegen/tests/test_no_wire_columns.py= holds it.

* Out of scope

- =account_type= and =account_status=. They are not secrets, they round-trip
  through the generated domain type, and moving them is a separate story.
