The rebase
----------
Fourteen commits replay onto main. One conflict: the port commit's
regenerated commodity_instrument.hpp against main's hand-written
decomposition. The port's shape wins, which is what its message already
states: the three nested blocks (terms, option, pricing, exotic) become
the flat instrument the nine modelled siblings use. Every other file
applies cleanly, so the tree carries trading, its ores.ore and
ores.shell consumers, and their records, and nothing else.

The services
------------
=./compass.sh services start= ends with 22 of 24 units running. The three
that are not are the environment's own defects:

    ores.reporting.service   subject dq.v1.fsm-states.list is gone      defect 7
    ores.workflow.service    the same subject                           defect 7
    ores.storage.service     its database role does not exist           defects 2 and 3, role side

The command takes 482 seconds, and the wait is the reason: the fleet's
readiness check waits up to 300 seconds for a "Service ready" line and
then retries the failed units for up to 180 seconds. A unit that cannot
ever become ready therefore costs the full window. busctl is not the
cause: =ORES_USE_BUSCTL=1= is in .env and =busctl --user list= works.

ores.trading.service, ores.ore.api, ores.ore.core, ores.ore.service,
