* Result

The staging now carries the Regime 3 finding. Unit 11 retires
=equity_instrument_variant=, =equity_instrument_data=, the equity arm of the
shared =trade_instrument= carrier, and the =if constexpr= branches in
=exporter.cpp= and =trade_mapper.cpp= that select an equity alternative. Its
check is that the family's wire path declares no =std::variant= and the read
carries one typed array per fact table and child.

Before this, E5 was Critical in the findings, named in the mapping as retired,
and the reason Regime 3 scores 1 of 5 — and no unit carried it or checked for
its absence.

The design records the two things that make this unit different from the
others. It lands last, because the typed arrays unit 5 and units 6 to 8
create have to exist before the read can carry them instead of the variant.
And it is the only unit that edits a file the other families also live in:
=trade_instrument= is a single variant over every family, so removing the
equity arm touches the header their arms are declared in. Only the equity arm
goes; the rate, credit, fx, commodity, composite and scripted arms are left
alone, and their own variants are the same defect in their own families.
