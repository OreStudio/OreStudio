#+title: A person cannot reveal a secret a service owns
#+description: The reveal door resolves as the gateway, which registers its own consumer key and holds vault::secrets:resolve, and the vault then refuses because the secret's owner names a service and not the gateway. Proven live: GET /api/v1/vault/secrets/<a service's secret>/value answers 404, 'This secret belongs to another consumer', for the system administrator. The vault's rule is documented (only the owner may resolve), so the question is which of the two yields: the vault, when the caller is the broker whose permission it already checked, or the door, which would have to ask as the owner.
