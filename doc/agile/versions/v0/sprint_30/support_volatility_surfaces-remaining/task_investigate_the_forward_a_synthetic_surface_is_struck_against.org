* Result

The investigation is
[[id:4825DEE0-D3E4-4E38-B46F-0C5EA838F1E0][The forward a synthetic surface is struck against]]. It answers where the
forward comes from -- the run's own latent curves, shared through a market board,
with no curve published -- and it weighs four paths against the approach note's
constraints 1 and 3 and against the three frameworks the surface is judged by.
Two of the four paths are rejected on the ownership decision alone: a
system-published curve, and a party's published forward set.

The result is carried into the pages that own each subject rather than left in
the investigation: the approach note states that the curve is the generator's
state and the quotes are its observations, gains a section on where the forward
comes from, and gains a comparison with Kienitz (2024) and van den Berg (2026a,
2026b); the shared-factors design no longer says "last published" without saying
by whom; the contributor note states that the vendor holds the curve every surface
is struck against; and the party-side construction note says "its own" published
curves and states the bootstrap difference.

The four choices are recorded with the alternatives weighed, a recommendation and
whether each can be changed later, and the owner accepted them on 2026-11-13. The
build is [[id:DDF8585F-75EE-4190-B20B-8A913DC1F883][Build the run's market board and strike every surface against it]].
