#+title: Send the derived FX vol quotes to a party that subscribes late
#+description: The derived FX vol feed sends its quotes once a day, so a party that subscribes after the first tick gets nothing until the market data service restarts, and Acme provisioning stops at give_party_surface_blueprints.
#+type: capture
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :marketdata:synthetic:provisioning:fx:
#+created: 2026-11-17
#+updated: 2026-11-17

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Send a party that subscribes to the =derived= provider the last value of each
derived FX vol quote, even when another party already demanded the same id, so
its series exist when the provisioning step reads them.

* Why

=derived_fx_vol_feed= sends a quote again only for an id that the demand marks
as an =image=. It skips any id whose last sent day is not older than the day of
the fixings. On 2026-11-16, on a fresh database, the feed ticked once at 23:41,
for the system party only. The four Acme parties subscribed at 23:43. For the
next twelve minutes each of them had 0 ticks, and Acme held none of the 35
derived series per party. Provisioning then stopped at
=give_party_surface_blueprints= with "140 surface blueprint(s) were not given".

A restart of =ores.marketdata.service= sent a fresh demand. The Acme parties got
their first tick within a minute, Acme held 140 derived series, and a retry of
the step completed. So the quotes exist. Only the later subscriber is not served.

The probable cause is that the market data service does not put an id into the
=image= of a demand when another party already demands that id. That cause is
not confirmed.
