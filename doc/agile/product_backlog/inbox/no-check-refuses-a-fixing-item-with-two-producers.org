#+title: No check refuses a fixing item with two producers
#+description: The one-producer rule of the architecture is enforced by a silent filter and by the absence of rows, so a second producer of an item would be offered twice and nothing would fail.

This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

The architecture says a fixing item has one producer at a time: the source the
system party routes the item's publisher to. Nothing enforces it. The simulator
offers a source the items its provider's instruments map, and an item mapped by
two providers is offered twice, quietly. The thirteen overnight indices that a
public adapter already serves were removed by deleting their rows, not by a rule
that refuses a second producer.

* Why

The rule is the difference between a store that states one number per fixing and
one that states two, and a reader cannot tell which is official. The failure is
silent: both publishes succeed and the series table shows two series for one
item.

The fix is a check where the catalogue is written, not where the fixings are
offered: refuse a mapping that would give an item a second producer while a route
for its publisher exists. The pgTAP suite already holds checks of this shape, and
the catalogue seed is where a duplicate row would be written.
