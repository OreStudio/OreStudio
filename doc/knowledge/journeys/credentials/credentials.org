#+title: Credentials journeys
#+description: Passwords, sign-in state, and the audit of who signed in.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:user_journey:security:
#+created: 2026-09-23
#+updated: 2026-09-23

Passwords and sign-in state are the part of identity a person worries about,
and the part an administrator is called to fix. One journey belongs to the
member, one to the administrator, and one is the record of what actually
happened.

The sign-in record is an event log, not a versioned entity. Its screen is called
*Audit*, carries no versions, no diff and no revert, and has Refresh only. That
naming is not a preference: it is what the UX language requires.
