-* The other half: the parameters a screen sends

A route also differs from the BFF in the query it takes. ores.http refuses a
parameter a read does not declare, which tells a person their query did nothing,
while the BFF ignored it. That is the better rule, and it breaks a screen whose
call states a parameter the port did not declare: a series' observations refused
the as_of a chosen day states, a report's runs refused a search, and each looked
like a fault in the screen. The sweep therefore compares both: the members an
answer states, and the parameters a route takes, against what the screens send.
