* Goal

Add the preview page to ores.web. A person picks a convention, a calendar and a horizon date. The page calls the batch resolve and lists each tenor with its date or its refusal, in tenor order.

* Acceptance

- The page lists each tenor of the chosen convention with its resolved date, in tenor order.
- A tenor that cannot resolve shows the server's refusal in its row and does not hide the other rows.
- The page has a loading state and a retry, as the entity screen specification requires.
- A test scenario walks the page for a period convention and for an IMM roll convention.
