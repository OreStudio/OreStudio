#+title: Enforce the protocol's 1,000-element bound on generated put-many and delete-many
#+description: The NATS entity protocol says a batch carries at most 1,000 elements and a longer one is invalid, but the generated put-many and delete-many accept any size. Get-many enforces its 1,000-key bound since PR 4739. Find the callers that send larger batches (importers, seeders, derived sources), make them chunk, then refuse an oversized batch in the service template.

* What

The generated put-many and delete-many refuse a batch of more than 1,000 elements as =invalid=, as the protocol states. Callers that send larger batches chunk them first.

* Why

The [[id:C650CBC9-FDED-4C68-981F-7902E296928E][NATS Entity Protocol Specification]] bounds every batch at 1,000 elements so a caller can size its chunks and one request cannot hold the store without limit. Get-many enforces its bound since PR 4739, which restored the trade-id services' refusal for every service. The write paths do not, and importers, seeders and the derived sources may rely on that today, so the callers come first.
