* Result

ores.synthetic is named in COMPONENTS_UNDER_TEST, so check_component_drift
--all covers it. On current main its generated C++ is already byte-identical,
so what regeneration found was its six shell recipes, the tangle and the
inventory that follow them, and one trading service file. All are caught up.

The tangle also absorbed the scripts drifted to __none__, which was the
re-tangle task, so that task is done as a side effect.

Two trunk defects were fixed on the way. The parser behind er-diagram runs
--strict with no reachable-file exemption, and sixteen trading drop scripts had
no \ir entry -- four from the trade link merge and twelve from the structures
work. They are wired and the diagram is regenerated.

Static validation leaves two failures, both trunk and neither this change's:
er-diagram, on six RLS and tenant warnings over marketdata_market_series_
identity, trading_structures, trading_swap_leg_amounts and trading_swap_leg_
rates; and codegen-tests, on test_twenty_one_fleet_processes and a
test_provision_step_words translation case.
