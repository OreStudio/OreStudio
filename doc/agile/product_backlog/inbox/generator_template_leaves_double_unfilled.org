#+description: The C++ generator template writes no value for a double precision field, so synthetic equity position rows carry a zero quantity and a null volatility, and an option underlying a null quantity; commodity_instrument's implied_vol_strike is filled by a path the template does not show.

* What

The C++ generator template emits a fill for a decimal, a date and a text
field, and none for a =double precision= field. The equity position
instrument's =quantity= is a double and its generator leaves it at 0.0. Its
=volatility= and the option underlying's =quantity= became doubles when the
exact-number rule moved them off =numeric(38, 12)=, and their generator
lines went with the regeneration. The commodity instrument's
=implied_vol_strike= has the same model shape and its generator fills it,
so something other than the template writes that line. Make the template
fill a double, and find what writes the commodity line.

* Why

Synthetic rows with a zero quantity and a null volatility do not exercise
the columns they hold, so a test or a demo built on them cannot see a
defect in those columns.
