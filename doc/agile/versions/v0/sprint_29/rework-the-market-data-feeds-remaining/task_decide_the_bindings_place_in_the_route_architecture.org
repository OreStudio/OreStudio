* Goal

One record says which party consumes which source. The task decides whether the feed binding remains as a projection of the routes or retires with its policies, and records the consequence for the ingest, the bindings screen, the seed and the system-tenant policy audit.

* Acceptance

- The decision names one consumer record, rejects the other shape, and cites the evidence in the code and the docs.
- The decision states what happens to the ingest binding read, the bindings screen story, the feed_bindings policies and the two Sprint 29 stories that assume bindings.
- The decision is recorded in the parent story, and the follow-on tasks that it unblocks are listed.

* Plan

1. Collect the evidence. Read the two consumer paths in the ingest, and find
   every writer of the routes, the subscriptions and the feed bindings.
2. Compare the two shapes. Either the binding stays as a projection of the
   routes, or the binding retires and the route is the one consumer record.
3. Judge each shape against the rule that one record says which party consumes
   which source, and against the cost of the migration it needs.
4. Record the decision in the parent story's =* Decisions= and list the tasks
   it unblocks, including the decision for the feed_bindings policy widening.
