* Acceptance

- A blueprint states a currency's grid: its expiries, its terms, its strikes, its volatility type, its shift and its smile model.
- The calibration fits the model to the quotes its pillars read, and records the errors, the arbitrage checks and the pillars no contributor quoted, as the FX calibration records them.
- The publish writes the surface's observations to an output series and raises the approval its kind declares. The withdrawal takes the set out of use.
- The new kind is a row in the kind table with its subject table, its publish kind and its withdrawal kind, and no step of the lifecycle changed to serve it.
- A second calibration at the same as-of converges on one candidate and keeps the earlier fit in history.

* Notes

- The FX calibration record holds the smile model's own columns and an FX forward publish id, so the audit decides whether the subject is parameterised or is a second table.
- The checks differ from the FX ones: a swaption surface is checked for calendar arbitrage across expiries and for a smile that stays positive in the wings, not for a butterfly in delta.
