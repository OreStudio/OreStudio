The reporting component's domain operations, as the
[[id:C650CBC9-FDED-4C68-981F-7902E296928E][NATS entity protocol specification]] § "Domain operations" defines for
entities.

=trigger_report_instance= starts one execution of a report definition. It is
not a write of one entity: it creates a =report_instance=, applies the
definition's concurrency policy, and dispatches the report execution workflow.
It is also not a user action. The scheduler sends it when a job fires, so the
caller is a service rather than a person, and the message names the tenant the
definition belongs to rather than deriving it from the caller's own privilege.

The acting identity is still the caller's. The scheduler presents its own
service account, and the permission check reads that account's roles.

=get_run_health= asks one run's health: the word the screen draws and the
lifecycle state it was derived from. The web asks the same read, so the shell
answers what the web answers. The word is the server's to state, which is why
this operation declares no mapping of its own.

=record_run_pricing= states the date a run priced at, once the packaging step
has settled it. The run document asks for a date and the gathered market data
carries the dates it can price; when the two differ the step prices at the
data's last date, and the run records both the date it used and why it moved.
The fact belongs on the run, so a screen that shows the run does not read it
out of an error message.

=resolve_cut= answers the cut a report's selection resolves to, for the preview
on the report's page and for the run, which resolves the selection before it
gathers anything and records the cut it resolved. Both callers read one
operation, because a preview computed by a second path is a preview of that
path and not of the run.

*** tenant_id
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: boost::uuids::uuid
:END:

The tenant that owns the definition. It is stated rather than derived because
the scheduler runs as one service account in one tenant, while definitions
belong to the tenants that scheduled them. The handler scopes the definition
lookup to this tenant, and the caller's permission check decides whether the
caller may act in it.

*** job_instance_id
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::int64_t
:default: 0
:END:

The scheduler's job instance that caused this trigger. It becomes the trigger's
=schedule_job_run_id=, so a run can be traced back to the firing that asked for
it. Zero when a caller triggers a report by hand, which no firing produced.

*** kind
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:default: "production"
:END:

The kind of instance to create: =production= or =package=. A =package= instance
prepares the engine's input and stops, so a developer can inspect and run the packages
the compute grid would receive. The default is =production=, which is what the scheduler
asks for. The concurrency policy applies to production instances only.

hands to every step builder. It states no subject, because it is not a bus
message: it is serialised into the start message's =request_json=, never sent

:END:

The batch whose results are left unread, so the run's record names them.

** definition_next_fire

One definition's schedule as the screen reads it: the definition, and the
instant the scheduler says its job fires next.

*** definition_id
:PROPERTIES:

The definition the time is of.

*** next_fire_at
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::optional<std::string>
:END:

When the definition's job fires next, in ISO-8601 UTC. Absent when the
definition holds no scheduler job, because it is run on demand, and absent when

** get_next_fire_request
:PROPERTIES:
:subject: reporting.v1.ops.get_next_fire
:response: get_next_fire_response
:END:

Reads when each named definition's schedule fires next.

The answer comes from the scheduler, which is the only thing that states it: the
reporting tables record firings made, not firings due. The reporting service
relays the caller's own session to scheduler.v1.ops.get_scheduler_status, so a
caller needs =reporting::report_definitions:read= to make this read and
=scheduler::job_instances:read= for the scheduler to answer it. =Member= holds
both.

*** definition_ids
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::vector<std::string>
:END:

The definitions to answer for, in any order, in the text form of their ids, as
the schedule and unschedule requests beside this one state them. The party that
scopes the answer is the session's, and never a field of this request.

*** schedules

** resolve_cut_request
:PROPERTIES:
:subject: reporting.v1.ops.resolve_cut
:response: resolve_cut_response
:END:

Answers the cut a report's selection resolves to, its state and the sentence that
would refuse it.

One operation serves the preview and the run. A preview computed by a second code
path is a preview of that path and not of the run, so the report's *Definition*
tab asks this same operation with =preview= set and reads what a run would find.
The covered date is the report's, read from the run settings, because the covered
date says which cut to look for while the cut states its own instant.

A selection of a kind whose instant the run supplies resolves to a body the run
mints, and a run states the four parts of the body it assembled. A part it could
not assemble refuses the run and names the part, so no partial body is minted. A
preview states no body and mints nothing.

*** report_definition_id
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: boost::uuids::uuid
:END:

The definition whose selection is resolved, so the handler reads the selection
from the definition rather than from the caller: a caller that stated the
selection could state one the report does not hold.

*** covered_date
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:END:

The date the report covers, as an ISO 8601 date. It comes from the run settings
and not from the cut, which is why a weekly report prices from the daily cut of
the day it covers.

*** scope_code
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:END:

The book population the cut fixes, as the report's scope names it.

*** business_centre_code
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:END:

The rates centre whose market data the scope's books revalue against. A report
whose books span two centres is refused rather than composed, so the caller
states the one centre the books share.

*** preview
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: bool
:default: false
:END:

Whether this call answers a preview. A preview writes nothing.

*** market_data_snapshot_id
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:default: ""
:END:

The market data snapshot the assembly fixed, in the text form of its id, or empty
where the assembly could not fix one. Read only by a call that mints a body.

*** deal_population_id
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:default: ""
:END:

The copy of the deal population the assembly fixed, in the text form of its id,
or empty.

*** reference_data_version
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:default: ""
:END:

The version of the reference data in force at the instant, or empty.

*** engine_version
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:default: ""
:END:

The version of the risk engine the body was assembled for, or empty.

*** code_version
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:default: ""
:END:

The revision of the code that computed the result, or empty.

** resolve_cut_response
:PROPERTIES:
:END:

*** result
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: ores::utility::domain::result
:END:

Outcome of the operation. A selection that resolves to nothing is a successful

*** cut_id
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:END:

The cut the selection resolved to, in the text form of its id, or empty where it
resolved to nothing.

*** cut_label
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:END:

The label a person quotes for the resolved cut, or empty. Derived from the cut
and its kind, never typed.

*** cut_kind_code
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:END:

The kind of the resolved cut, so a caller filters a run by the occasion it priced

*** cut_timepoint
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:END:

The instant of the resolved cut, in ISO-8601 UTC, or empty.

*** cut_state
:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:END:

The state the resolved cut is in, as the design words it: =draft=, =incomplete=,

:PROPERTIES:
:cpp_type: std::string
:END:

preview's sentence, the report page's sentence and the run's output message are
