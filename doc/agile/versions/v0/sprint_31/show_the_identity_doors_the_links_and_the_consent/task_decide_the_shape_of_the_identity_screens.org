* Goal

Prototype the identity screens before building them: the sign-in door with the providers beside the password, what a first sign-in says the deployment will give it, the section that shows and removes a link, the session list that names how each session was signed in, and the consent screen a client shows. Build two or three variants and put them beside the existing sign-in, security and agent-grant screens, then choose one with evidence from a person using it.

* Acceptance

- Two or three variants are prototyped and shown side by side with the existing sign-in and security screens.
- A person completes each journey on the chosen variant: sign up, sign in, link, unlink, and approve a client.
- The choice is recorded on the story with what the evidence showed, including what the person could not tell apart.
- The prototype is thrown away once the choice is made; the screens are built from the accepted design.
