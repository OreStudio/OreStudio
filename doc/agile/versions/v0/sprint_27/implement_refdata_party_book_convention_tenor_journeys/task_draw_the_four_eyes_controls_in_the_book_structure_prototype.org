* Result

Drew the four-eyes controls into the book structure prototype and the journey
document.

*What the prototype shows.* The review step marks each change as =needs <part>= or
=no approval=, from the decisions in the book controls note: Finance for the
accounting fields and ledger feed, Market Risk for the classification and rates
centre, Operations for status, a move, a rights grant and a new portfolio, the
closing process for a close, and the opening chain for a new book. A description
edit needs no approval. The confirm becomes =Raise request= when any change is
gated. Three new states follow: =waiting= (the raised request and who still has
to answer), =decide= (the checker's screen, which refuses the person who asked and
needs a comment), and =declined= (the book keeps its version). A move under another
portfolio and a new portfolio are drawn, through =?moveto= and the portfolio form.

*What the journey document now says.* Steps 6 to 8, the five side states, the
approval operations in the coverage table, and two new gaps: a gated change has
nowhere to wait and nothing applies it on approval, and the controller and Finance
have no Acme account.

*What is not drawn.* The other four prototypes (counterparty, party, convention,
tenor) do not yet carry these states. They follow the same pattern once this one
is signed off.

*Verification.* Rendered each new state in headless Chrome and read the result:
the review with a move and a status change, the waiting request for a new book
with the four deciders, and the decision screen refusing the maker. =node= parses
the script. No automated test covers a prototype.
