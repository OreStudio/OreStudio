* Goal

Every ACME party (UK, US, HK, holding) receives tick data for the
simulated market, not just the first party whose feeds started. The
sim config lives once, under the system party; each party consumes
the same stream into its own per-party series via per-party feed
bindings bound to the Live workspace. No per-office producers and no
per-office theme publishing.

* Result

Shipped on feature/f15-per-party-feed-consumption.

- The marketdata service keeps one ingest subscription per
  (source_name, tenant, party, workspace) and republishes each tick
  on the per-party realtime subject
  marketdata.v1.tick.<tenant>.<workspace>.<party>.<ore_key>.
- ACME provisioning publishes the two themes and the FX driver
  vintage once, against the system party; creates bindings for the
  system party, holding and every office (workspace = Live sentinel);
  offices publish no theme and start no feeds.
- The factory path resolves "vintage" configs from the config's own
  party series: (source=vintage_source, point_id="SPOT",
  date=vintage_date) for FX, the shortest-tenor DEPOSIT anchor for IR
  curves. market_data_client::find_series filters by party_id, so the
  lookup cannot resolve another party's series.
- The IR curve tenor-combo check now permits the 'FOMC' tenor for RFR
  families (create script + one-shot migration).
- CRM stays per-party; the group/office CRM topology plans are
  unchanged.

Fresh-DB verification (2026-08-12, prime_origin): 34 feeds started, 0
skipped at boot; 65 first-tick fan-outs; every party's series fills
(~10,500 observations per party for AUD/USD); cross-party consistency
holds (0 divergent (source, observation_datetime) rows across all 13
sources); party-scoped CRM reads are non-empty for US and HK; the
per-party republish streams deliver (5/5 messages on
marketdata.v1.tick.<tenant>.Live.<party>.fx.rate.eur.usd). All six
acceptance bullets are met.
