* Acceptance

- The Member role holds compute::workunits:read and compute::results:read in the seed.
- A member of a party reads the workunits and results of a batch of that party and is refused none.
- A member of another party reads none of them.
- The journey states which roles hold the reads.

* Plan

Add =compute::workunits:read= and =compute::results:read= to the Member role in
=iam_roles_populate.sql=. The party policies of the compute tables already cut
the rows, so no policy changes. Record the grant in the RLS exceptions register
and state the roles in the journey. Recreate the database and check three
accounts through the BFF.

* Result

The Member role holds the workunit and result read codes in the seed. After a
database recreate, a member of the batches' own party reads the batches, the
workunits of a batch and the results of a workunit, and sees the host of a
result as hidden. A member of another party reads none of them and gets 404 for
a workunit by id.
