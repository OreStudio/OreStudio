*** What the tree showed

- The shell menus are data (=SHELL_MENUS= in
  =projects/ores.web/packages/web/src/shell/areas.ts=): the system
  administration area holds Home and Tenants only. The deployment's own
  accounts therefore have no entry, and the Qt application kept them on a
  first-class screen. The read exists: =GET /api/accounts= answers the
  session's own tenant accounts, the system tenant for a super
  administrator, with the service accounts among them, and the tenant
  screen's people list already marks them as services.
- No creation path records =full_name=: =ores_iam_create_initial_admin_fn=
  inserts the account without it, and =tenant_provisioning_service.hpp=
  creates the tenant administrator without it. The shell already prefers
  the name (=AppShell=), but the home page greets the raw username.
- The two avatars are not in the repository. The seeds attach the staff
  photos and the party logo; no administrator picture is seeded.
- A stale comment rides on top: =TenantsPage.tsx= (lines 49-51) says the
  system tenant is not on the roster, which =d4525844bc= (2026-10-04)
  contradicted.
