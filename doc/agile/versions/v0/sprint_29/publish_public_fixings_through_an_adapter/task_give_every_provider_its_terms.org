* Acceptance

- The provider has the terms URL, the credit text, whether storage is allowed, a credential reference, and the digest of the terms it accepted, with who accepted them and when. (Amended on 2026-10-28: a digest of the terms rather than a version number, so only a change of the terms themselves clears the acceptance.)
- A change of terms makes a new version and clears the acceptance.
- Values stored under earlier terms stay. (Amended on 2026-10-28: deleting a provider's fixings when new terms withdraw storage is an operator action, moved to the control surface task.)
- The adapter contract refuses a demand for a provider whose terms are not accepted, and a test shows it.

* Plan

1. Add the terms columns to the provider model and regenerate the entity.
2. Write the rules in one place, =ores.marketdata.core/terms/provider_terms=: whether
   a provider has terms, their digest, whether they are accepted, whether its data may
   be fetched, and accepting them.
3. Give the demand catalogue the providers, and refuse a route to a provider whose
   data may not be fetched, with the reason =terms_not_accepted=.

* Result

A provider now carries its terms: the page, the credit, whether storage is
allowed, a credential reference, and the digest of the terms it accepted, with who
and when. The digest is the SHA-256 of the page, the credit and the storage
permission, so a change of any of them clears the acceptance with no further
write, and a change elsewhere, such as the description, does not. A route to a
provider with terms demands nothing until they are accepted and allow storage,
and is refused as =terms_not_accepted=. A provider with no terms page, as every
vendor is today, is demanded as before.

Evidence: =ores.marketdata.core.tests= =[provider_terms]= 4 of 4 and
=ores.marketdata.service.tests= =[demand_calculator]= 13 of 13, including a
subscribed route refused for unaccepted terms and a provider without terms
demanded as before; the web app typechecks against the regenerated type. The
database-backed suites run in the check gate, which recreates the schema.
