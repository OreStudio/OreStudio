* What

The synthetic generators used to start from a vintage: a real observation from a
canned dataset at a fixed past date, such as FRED H10 as of 2026-05-05. The task
[[id:A4165C70-0EAB-483D-8055-C1D8BF543113][Calibrate the synthetic ticks from free public sources]] removes vintage altogether: an FX feed
starts from the newest public fixing, and every other feed from its fixed level.
Find the use cases that still need market data as of a past date, such as a
reproducible demo, a regression run against a known day, or a historical scenario,
and design how to serve them. The stored fixings already keep every date, so one
answer may be to read them as of a date rather than to keep canned vintages.

* Why

The user asked to remove vintage and to have "backdated" data only for specific use
cases, and noted that this needs investigation. Without it, no generator can be
pinned to a known past day.
