states the fixings of its own indices. The query is fixing_coverage_query.sql
beside this file. Both readings are of the same provision, on 2026-11-19: the
database the acme_demo profile built at 00:20, read once before the writer ran
(from commit b586ec39f9, which carries no fixing writer) and once after it ran
(from commit f73b077f00, the writer in place, the synthetic service restarted).

Every one of them is an index no generated curve covers: the seeded curve
configs hold no AOA, BWP, HKD, IDR, MYR, OMR, PLN WIBOR, SAR or THB curve, no
JPY EYTIBOR, no COP IBR at a tenor and no USD SOFR at a tenor. A fixing derived
from the curve of that index and tenor therefore has no curve to come from, and
the curve is the gap, not the fixing.

The series the writer keeps, and where they live:

-------+----------------------------------------+---------------+--------------+--------+---------
 ir    | ffffffff-ffff-ffff-ffff-ffffffffffff   | derived       | system_party |     41 |   21320

The system tenant is the tenant a party's export falls back to for an index it
does not hold itself, which is why the run's fixings are kept there: one series
per index, read by every party.
