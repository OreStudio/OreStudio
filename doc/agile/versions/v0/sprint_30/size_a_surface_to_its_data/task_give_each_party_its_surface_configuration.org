* Goal

The derived source's settings are compiled constants, including a window of 1830 days and a hardcoded method id with no row behind it. A surface is party-level data, so its build configuration is party-level too, copied from the system tenant by the same step that copies the blueprints, and referenced by derivation_config_id on the series it produces.
