* Acceptance

- The active sessions, the session statistics and the failed attempts name the account by username, with the UUID shown beside it in small type.
- The auth events panel keeps the username it already carries and shows the UUID beneath it.
- An account that has never signed in is not listed as a failed attempt, and a zero timestamp never renders as 1970-01-01.

* Result

The audit screen names the account in every row: the username first, the UUID
beneath it in small type. The failed-attempts panel no longer presents an
account that has never signed in as a 1970 event: the zero login_info
sentinels read *Never signed in* and *Never*, and the rows order by what they
have to say. The audit also has a card on =PartyHome=, which had none.

The login_info rows themselves are correct and stay: they are per-account
login state, created with the account because the login path refuses without
them, not a log of events.
