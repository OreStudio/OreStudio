* Result

The Market tab's Laws table has four columns: the feed, its process, its law and
its last minute of ticks. The five number columns (ticks an hour, sensitivity,
branching, decay, stable) moved into the law's tooltip, one to a line, the
Hawkes three only for a Hawkes law. The column of Arrivals tab links is gone,
since the law itself opens the feed's Arrivals tab. The table's lead says so.

Evidence: web tests pass (230 in marketdata/synthetic and i18n), typecheck clean,
and the harness passes 15 of 15 live: it checks the four headers, the law's link
and its tooltip, and that no Arrivals tab link is left. A screenshot of the table
was checked. Held for the user's review of the UI before merge.
