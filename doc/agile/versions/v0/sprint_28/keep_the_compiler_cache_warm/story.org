#+title: Story: Keep the compiler cache warm when a continuous job is cancelled
#+description: The continuous clang jobs ran in about an hour at an 85.72 % sccache hit rate on 2026-10-03, and need more than six hours at the 3 to 10 per cent they see now. A job that hits the six-hour limit is cancelled, and the Save sccache step is gated on !cancelled(), so every object that run compiled is thrown away. The cache can therefore never warm and the job can never finish. Save the cache whenever the CTest step ran and the local cache grew past a floor.
