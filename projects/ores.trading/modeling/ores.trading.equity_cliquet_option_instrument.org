One row per equity cliquet option, keyed by the trade. The cliquet's own
terms live here rather than in =equity_option_instruments=, whose vanilla
shape has no moneyness, no periodic caps and floors and no premium.

The cap and floor pair is the option's own subject matter: the local pair
applies to one period's return and the global pair to the accumulated
return. Every one is money, so every one is an exact numeric.

The moneyness is a ratio and not an amount, so it is a continuous quantity and
stays a binary float.

The schedule and the settlement days state when the option is observed and
settled. The schedule's dates belong to the shared schedule children; this
table keeps the settlement days the document states.
