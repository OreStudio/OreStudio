The calendar adjustment export protocol messages.

=calendar_adjustment= patches an ORE built-in calendar with institution-
or date-specific exceptions. It is assembled server-side from the
=calendar= and =calendar_exception= rows and is never itself persisted, so
no table backs it and the shape is a message payload rather than an
entity.

** calendar_adjustment

#+begin_src cpp :name comment
/**
 * @brief Overrides applied to a named ORE calendar.
 *
 * A calendar adjustment patches a built-in calendar with institution-
 * or date-specific exceptions: additional holidays, and additional
 * business days. Dates are ISO-8601 strings ("YYYY-MM-DD") verbatim
 * from the source file, so a round trip preserves them exactly.
 */
#+end_src
