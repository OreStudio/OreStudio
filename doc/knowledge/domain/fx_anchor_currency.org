#+title: The US dollar is the natural hub for FX forward curves
#+description: The dollar's share of FX turnover makes it the hub of forward curves, and what a second hub would cost.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

The US dollar is on one side of about 89% of FX trades, so most liquid forward points are quoted against it. A system that builds its forwards from USD pairs follows the market's own structure.

* Detail

A cross such as EUR/GBP then follows from its two USD legs. A second hub would give two routes to the same cross, and the routes need not agree. That last step is our reasoning and not a sourced claim.

The dollar's position also shapes the basis. The BIS reports a persistent cross-currency basis for lending dollars against most currencies since 2007.
