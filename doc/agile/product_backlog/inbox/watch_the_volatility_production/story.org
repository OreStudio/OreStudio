* Goal

A person can watch one volatility being produced and see why it looks the way it does.
The page reads the chain in dependency order: the series the generator steps, the latent
surface it holds, each vendor's view of that surface, and the surface a party calibrated,
with the error between them. Each node states its own state, so a surface with no quotes
says which input it lacks.

The chain is the debugger. It is not a quotes board and not a feed page: the internal
values have no party to read them, and no existing screen joins the producer side to the
consumption side.

The page names no asset class. A surface states its own axes -- an expiry and a delta
label for an FX surface, an expiry and a term for a swaption one -- and the page draws the
grid the surface declares. A production whose axes the page cannot read is refused with
the axis it lacks, and is never drawn with a guess.

* Acceptance

- A subject and an as-of answer the whole chain in one read, from the inputs to the
  calibrated surface. The subject is whatever the surface's coordinates name: a pair for
  an FX surface, a currency for a swaption one.
- The page renders the latent surface, each vendor's view and the calibrated surface side
  by side, with the difference between them and the quote ladders beside them, on the axes
  the surface declares.
- A broken chain names the node that failed and the input it lacked, and the node's own
  message log is reachable from it.
- The internal series of the latent surface and of the derived quotes are readable under
  the system tenant, and no non-system party can read them.
- A person can step the latent surface through a day and watch the views follow.
- No part of the page names an asset class. A surface of a class the page has never seen
  renders when it declares its axes, and a class that declares no axes is refused with the
  axis it lacks.

* Decisions

- *The page names no asset class.* The axes and their labels, the quote ladder's shape and
  the model's name come from the surface's own description. When a second class needs
  something the page cannot show, the description gains a field; the page does not gain a
  branch. This is the rule the second class is the test of.
- *The internal values are stored and unentitled, not absent.* A latent surface and the
  derived quotes keep a series so their evolution can be watched; no non-system party may
  read them, and they are never metered.
- *The derived quotes serve one role.* They drive the generation of the simulated surface.
  They are not a quote source for a party.
- *The platform does not manufacture a surface for a party.* A party with no system-level
  quotes for its subject authors its own surface, calibrates it and publishes it.
- *A separate card is not warranted yet.* The page sits under the simulator area until it
  covers more than volatility.
- *The graph is read as heatmaps first.* The two axes a surface declares, for each surface,
  with the difference panel beside them; a 3D surface is a secondary view.
- *The producer of the platform's own arithmetic is =orestudio=.* It is a producer, not a
  publisher, and it replaces the mechanism-named =derived=.

* Out of scope

- An axis a surface cannot express. A surface with three axes is drawn as the slices it
  declares, not as a new page.
