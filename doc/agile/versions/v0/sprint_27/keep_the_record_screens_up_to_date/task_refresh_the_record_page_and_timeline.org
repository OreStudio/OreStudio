* Result

The person page has the Refresh button in its header, beside the access mark. It turns gold when the account, the contact record, a role definition, the sign-in record or the history changed on the server (=iam= =accounts=, =account_contact_informations=, =roles=, =login_info=, =sessions=). One Refresh reads the account, the contact record, the roles, the sign-ins and the history again (=useLoadState= groups the page's reads, so the page is stale when any of them is older than a change). A history entry that arrives with a read is marked for a moment, the same pulse as a changed list row. The page does not reload by itself, apart from react-query's reload when the window regains focus, which lists share.

Finding: granting or taking away a role raises =role_assigned_event= and =role_revoked_event= on the in-process bus only; neither is published to NATS. A person page therefore cannot hear that a role was granted elsewhere. That needs a published event for the grant, which is a service change and is not in this story.

Only the person page is done. The other record pages (currency, calendar, desk group and the rest) use =RecordHeader=, so they take the same Refresh with their own watches and reads; that follows once the person page is accepted.
