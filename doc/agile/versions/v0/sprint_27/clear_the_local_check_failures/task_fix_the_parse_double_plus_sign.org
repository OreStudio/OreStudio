* Acceptance

- ores.platform.numeric.tests and ores.marketdata.core.tests pass.

* Plan

Read the service's contract first: =is_number= is a spelling gate, not a
conversion, so the question is what the grammar accepts and not what a
conversion would. The shim claims =from_chars=' contract, and =from_chars=
takes a minus and not a plus, so the grammar check is the defect and not the
service or its test. Fix it there, where every caller inherits the rule, and
pin the rule in the shim's own test.
