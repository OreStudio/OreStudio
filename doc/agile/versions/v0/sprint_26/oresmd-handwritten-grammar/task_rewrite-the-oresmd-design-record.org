* Acceptance

- The record describes the typed model, the two codecs, the series identity and the tests, and drops "proposed", the defaults and "without per-asset-class variants".
- The record states the strict =type=quote= contract as a rule of the grammar, and states which looser rules (roles, partial requirements, resolution) apply to fixings and curves only.
- The grammar story 12DAAEC7's =* Status= no longer says that no unit started.
- Capture =67A49A94= (case loss) is closed against this story.
- Every other capture the old grammar left is closed or re-pointed.

* Plan

Write the record last, from the code.

1. Take the facts from the headers: the counts in =ore_types.hpp= and
   =schema.hpp=, the families in =market_index.hpp=, and each codec's own
   description.
2. Take every worked example from the codecs themselves: a throwaway test case
   printed the canonical key, the quote URI and the series URI for one key per
   instrument type, and the fixing URI for one name per index family. It ran
   once and was removed before the commit.
3. Close or re-point each capture the old grammar left.

* Result

=market_data_urn.org= now describes the grammar that ships: the typed datum and
index models with their schema tables, the two ORE codecs and what each refuses
on purpose, the URI grammar and its strict contract, and the tests. Every
worked example is the codecs' own output. The record states that roles,
requirements and resolution belong to fixings and curves only, and that no code
implements them today. Story 12DAAEC7 no longer says that no unit started.
Capture 67A49A94 and four other captures that the new grammar resolved are
closed, one is re-pointed, and three that still apply are kept.
