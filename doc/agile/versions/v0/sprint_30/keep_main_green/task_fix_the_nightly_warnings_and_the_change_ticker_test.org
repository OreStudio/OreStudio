* Acceptance

- The gcc release build reports no warnings in random_ast_generator.cpp, catalogue_tools.cpp or anywhere else.
- The change ticker test passes while the machine is loaded and under valgrind.

* Plan

1. Make the four deliberate fallthroughs in =random_ast_generator.cpp=
   explicit, and keep the Switch as it is otherwise.
2. Move =note= and =misread= out of =catalogue_tools::called=. Both are
   closures the coroutine frame holds, which is what gcc reports.
3. Give the change ticker test a deadline instead of a fixed window: run
   the loop until the frame arrives, and fail only when it never does.

* Result

The two warning sites lost their causes rather than their symptoms, and the
ticker test now waits for the behaviour it asserts.

- =random_ast_generator.cpp=: the four cumulative =switch= cases carry
  =[[fallthrough]];=, so the intent is stated and =-Wimplicit-fallthrough==
  has nothing to report.
- =catalogue_tools.cpp=: =record_call= and =misread= are free helpers now, so
  the coroutine frame holds no closure and gcc stops reporting
  =-Wsubobject-linkage= on it.
- =change_ticker_tests.cpp=: the first tick had a fixed hundred milliseconds
  to arrive; under valgrind it can come later. The test runs the loop until
  the frame arrives, with a ten second ceiling.

Verified locally: the release build reports no warnings, the ticker suite and
the MCP tool suite pass, and the ticker test passes under valgrind.

Not verified locally: the gcc compiler, which is where the two warnings were
reported. The nightlies are the check for those.
