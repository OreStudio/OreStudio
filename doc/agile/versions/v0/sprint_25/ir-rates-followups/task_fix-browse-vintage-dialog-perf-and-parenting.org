* Goal

Discovered while manually verifying vintage-aware IR curve seeding
([[id:FC800706-1673-4E48-84C2-22569E836DF5][task]]): clicking *Browse
available…* on either editor's Instrument tab works functionally, but
has two rough edges shared by both:

- *Slow to appear*: =get_market_series_request= (limit 10000) and
  =get_market_observations_by_series_id_request= (limit 10000) have no
  server-side qualifier/series filter, so the handler fetches and
  deserialises up to 10000 rows twice, client-side-filtering down to
  one qualifier afterwards, even when the tenant only actually has a
  handful of relevant series/observations.
- *Detached window*: the resulting picker (=QDialog dialog(self);= in
  both =onBrowseVintageClicked= implementations) doesn't appear rooted
  to the MDI main window the way other app modals are -- it shows as
  a separate top-level window.

Pre-existing in both editors -- this story's IR curve work only
mirrored FX's existing pattern, it didn't introduce either issue.

* Acceptance

- The Browse-available-vintages picker appears promptly regardless of
  how much unrelated market data the tenant holds -- either by adding
  a qualifier-scoped filter to the series/observations lookup
  protocol, or another mechanism that avoids the unfiltered
  10000-row fetches.
- The picker dialog is properly rooted to the MDI main window (parented/
  positioned like other modals in the app), in both =FxSpotRateEditor=
  and =IrCurveEditor=.
- Fix applied to both editors identically -- no divergence between the
  FX and IR pickers.
