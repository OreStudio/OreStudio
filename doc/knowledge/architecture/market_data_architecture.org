* Summary

Market data is one pipeline in ORE Studio. A producer publishes a
=market_tick= on its own subject. The marketdata ingest loop plans that tick
against the feed bindings of its source, writes one observation per binding,
and republishes the tick on the per party stream. A consumer subscribes to
that stream or reads the store.

This page is the structure note for the whole subject. It gists and links
and it carries no content its cluster already carries. It argues a reading
order and says what the reader can do at the end of each section. The detail
lives in exactly one note, and that note is named here.

The subject splits into four sections. The first is the pipeline and its
machinery, which takes a reader from a tick on the wire to a stored
observation and to the consumer that reads it. The second is synthetic
generation, which covers the configs and the producers a person authors and
runs. The third is the operation surface, which covers the verbs and the
window a person drives. The fourth is the vendor and stored data, which is
the same pipeline seen from the real source and from the tables it leaves
behind.

Two boundaries hold. This page does not carry the process mathematics. The
processes a generator steps are collected under
[[id:A8C46639-0639-4587-B07D-B8B006C46618][Stochastic Processes]], and a
reader who wants an SDE reads it there. This page also does not carry the
resolution path from a logical market data requirement to a concrete
identifier, which is
[[id:FD427B5B-BDC5-4803-AFCD-97A59E31FE25][Market Data Requirements and Resolution]].
That path is a different pipeline and it has its own structure note.

** The gap between a binding and the generation config

A =feed_binding= names only its =source_name=. Nothing on the row references
the =market_data_generation_config= or the feed config that produced that
source. No foreign key joins them and no column carries a config id. The link
back is name similarity, and the column comment that says the =source_name=
matches the =source_name= of the feed config is the whole of the
relationship.

The consequence is that a binding cannot show which config authored its
source. Two configs that share a source name are indistinguishable on the
binding row, and a config renamed without the matching binding edited leaves
a binding that names a source nothing is publishing. A reader who wants the
config behind a binding has to match names by hand.

This gap is stated here so a reader sees it rather than inferring a
relationship the schema does not carry. The binding note records it in full.
