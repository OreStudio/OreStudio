* Goal

A caller names a blueprint and an as-of, and the system writes a calibrated surface as a candidate. The =marketdata.v1.surface.calibrate= operation reads the quotes the pillars name and the forwards from the party's published FX forward set. It runs the engine and writes the grid as observations of the output series, with lineage. The recipe checks and the result checks run in the service, so a caller that is not a screen meets the same rules. See [[id:53D14F31-EE32-493E-BBEB-140AA4906BE4][Constructing a volatility surface: from quotes to a published set]].

* Acceptance

- A calibrate of the seeded blueprint writes the grid as observations of its output series at the as-of, and answers the points and the error of each pillar.
- Each point has lineage that names the source series, the contributor weights and the blueprint version.
- The four recipe checks run before any quote is read, and each refusal names what failed.
- The arbitrage and error results are held against the candidate.
- A pillar with no observation is reported, and an expiry left short of its pillars is refused with the series it could not read.
- A missing published curve of either currency refuses the calibrate and names the currency.
- A repeat over the same blueprint and as-of converges on the same points.
- A candidate is not readable as a published surface.
- The forwards come from the party's published forward set, and the lineage names that set.
- The output series declares its expiry and strike axes, so the Evolution page reads the grid as a surface. Today no ingest-made series declares an axis, and the snapshot refuses one without.

* Plan

The calibration follows the curve bootstrap: read the recipe, read the quotes at the as-of, run the engine, write observations of the output series with lineage, and declare the series' axes.

- *Engine.* Grid strikes are a fixed log-moneyness, the ATM or a delta, solved on the fitted smile of each grid expiry in its own market and convention, so the blueprint's =DEL/Spot/Put/25= strikes are honoured.
- *Planner.* =fx_vol_surface_calibration_plan= is pure: the four recipe checks, each expiry's market from the published forward set (the tenor's row, or a log-linear interpolation of the discount factors), each pillar's weighted contributions, and the missing pillars.
- *Service.* =marketdata.v1.ops.calibrate_fx_vol_surface= reads the rows and quotes, calls the planner and the engine, and writes the grid, its lineage, the =expiry= and =strike= axes and the candidate record.
- *Stores.* A new =fx_vol_surface_calibration= record per blueprint and as-of holds the fit and its checks. The lineage gains =source_weights=. A new derivation kind marks the output series.
- *Convention reading* moved to ores.refdata.core first, in Keep quant free of stored codes, so quant takes no stored code.
