* Notes

From the [[file:evidence/hand_written_sql_census.md][hand-written SQL census]] in [[id:B9FE1B6D-08B1-4B06-BD76-5CDD12123568][Find why so much SQL is hand-written and generate what codegen can]].

The 25 tables: 18 with no model (junctions such as =iam_account_roles=,
=iam_role_permissions=, =reporting_risk_report_config_books= and
=_portfolios=; time series such as =iam_auth_events=, =iam_session_samples= and
the telemetry samples; DQ publication logs; =compute_batch_dependencies=,
=scheduler_job_instances=, =workspace_trade_scope= and others) and 7 DQ artefact
staging tables. =mq= and =telemetry= are infrastructure and likely stay
hand-written; =mq_messages= holds the only hard =REFERENCES= in the tree.

** What the two DQ clean-up tasks took off the census

The census is 23 now, not 25. The DQ artefact sharing of it was 7 and is 1.

- [[id:A31257E3-7495-46CD-B36D-10AA6D55CB09][Move the lookup artefact tables onto domain models]] (sprint 28) modelled
  fifteen refdata coded-scheme tables and their DQ artefact tables, which were
  the largest single group.
- [[id:6632B4F0-7005-4A55-BB69-96161952C2EF][Settle the owner of the DQ-only models]] (sprint 28) modelled
  =dq_lei_bic_artefact_create.sql= and moved the report configuration binding
  onto =ores.reporting.report_configuration=, so
  =dq_report_configurations_artefact_create.sql= is generated too.

The one DQ artefact staging table left is =dq_ip2country_artefact_create.sql=,
which waits on its geo store by
