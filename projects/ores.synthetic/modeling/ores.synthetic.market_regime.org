#+title: ores.synthetic.market_regime
#+description: Codegen operation model for the market regime: its read, its pin and configure writes, the scheduler trace and the tick history.
#+type: ores.codegen.operation
#+component: synthetic
#+subcomponent: api
#+entity_singular: market_regime
#+namespace: ores::synthetic::messaging
#+brief: The market regime's read, pin, configure, trace and history operations.
#+filetags: :model:operation:synthetic:
#+created: 2026-11-03
#+updated: 2026-11-03

The market-wide arrival factor the synthetic service runs: a calm or active
state, the shared heat that events add and decay fades, and the pace market
time runs at. A screen reads it, pins it, configures it, and reads the
scheduler's recent decisions and the tick history behind it.

The pin and the configure writes answer with one response, the regime after
the request, so the two share it.

** get_market_regime_response
#+begin_src cpp :name comment
/**
 * @brief The factor in force: its state, its multiplier and whether a scenario pinned it.
 *
 * The heat is the endogenous half, and it is the internal state jump and decay
 * act on: a screen that wants to show the process rather than its outcome reads
 * these readings, which the service takes a few times a second as it fades the
 * heat. The jump and the decay are carried beside them, so a reader can say what
 * the curve means without knowing the configuration.
 */
#+end_src
