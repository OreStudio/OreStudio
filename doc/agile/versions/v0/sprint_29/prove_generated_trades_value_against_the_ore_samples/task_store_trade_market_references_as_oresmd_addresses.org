* Goal

No trade holds an ORE name: every column that names market data holds an oresmd address, and ORE names exist only in the ORE documents we read and write.

* Acceptance

- Every trade column that names market data (indices, curves, underlyings, credit names) holds an oresmd address; the inventory of about 60 columns is in the design.
- Each column is checked by a soft foreign key against the party's series catalogue, as =ores_marketdata_validate_curve_series_fn= checks a curve today.
- The importer converts ORE names to addresses and the exporter converts back, both through =ore_naming=; the corpus roundtrip still loses nothing.
- Stored trades are migrated, family by family.

* Plan

The design is in [[id:63FF92C8-3E18-49BB-93FA-B0862B5FD4B2][Test that every generated trade builds in ORE]], section Design: oresmd references, resolvers and trade builders.
