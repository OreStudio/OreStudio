* Goal

A reader follows the whole GJR-GARCH process from the feed. The feed's History tab reads the pair's variance calibration inputs and calibrations as parts of the feed's story, so a stored input, a proposed fit, a rejection, an approval and a supersession each show as an entry with who, when and why. An applied calibration run's commentary on the feed names its GARCH outcome. When a fit of the pair is proposed, the feed's Calibration tab, the class list and the feed's calibration page say that it waits for review and link to it. The calibration page plots the fitted conditional volatility over the input window against the absolute returns it was fitted from, and the pair's calibrations' alpha, gamma, beta and persistence over time.

* Acceptance

- After an applied run, the feed's History tab shows the stored input and the proposed calibration as entries, with the change reason system.calibration.
- After a review, the history shows the decision, the reviewer and the reason, and on approval the superseded calibration and the re-pointed feed.
- A proposed fit shows as waiting for review on the feed's Calibration tab and on the class list, with a link to the review.
- The conditional volatility plot is computed from the stored points and the stored shape, and a test checks it against the recursion by hand.

* Result

The whole GJR-GARCH process is visible from the feed and the calibration page.

- History. A feed's History tab reads the pair's variance inputs and calibrations
  with the feed's own versions, through three read-only BFF resources over the
  service's generated list and history verbs. Each stored step shows: input
  captured, fit proposed, reviewed, superseded.
- Commentary. The input names what it captured; the calibration names its shape
  and that it waits for review, or why it was rejected; the feed's calibration
  entry names the GJR-GARCH outcome beside the level.
- Waiting for review. The class list counts the fits that wait; the feed's
  Calibration tab and the calibration page say which fit waits and lead to its
  review, which is attached to the stored fit. Approving a fit closes the pair's
  older proposals.
- Charts. The fitted daily volatility over the input, beside the size of each
  move and the long-run level, computed in the browser with the fit's recursion
  and checked against the stored first-day ratio; and the pair's calibrations over
  time as small multiples of α, γ, β and persistence, with a table of states and
  reasons.
