* Goal

A person who opens the market finds one question per tab: what is the market doing now, what moves it, how every feed's ticks arrive, whether each feed follows its law, and what the loop did. The page takes the review's look: a telemetry strip, cards with a titled header and an action on the right, each setting with the number it implies under it, and figures printed without float noise. It keeps the product's tokens, and nothing on it claims a control or a model the service does not have.

* Result

The Synthetic screen's Market tab is now the signed-off prototype: a strip of
seven figures over six tabs, in Gemini's look on the product's tokens.

- *Now*: the pace, stacked as regime and heat, with active spells shaded;
  the market's intensity λ(t) against the ticks seen each second, on one
  axis; the force switch with who holds the state, for how long, the mean
  spells and the active share; and what changed the market.
- *Settings*: four cards. Spells are entered in seconds, each field shows the
  number it implies, the service's rules run as a person types, the change is
  listed before it is applied, and only the edited fields are sent. A tenant
  reads the same numbers.
- *Arrivals*: the last hour folded by the minute, or ten minutes by ten seconds,
  coloured by every feed, a class or the pace; below it the feed lanes, with
  density cells once marks would saturate, curve pillars counted once per
  instant, and asked counted over the stretch the ticks cover.
- *Laws*: read only, each row linking to the feed's Arrivals tab.
- *Fit* and *Trace*: as before, in cards.

The service gained what the screens needed: each heat reading carries λ(t);
an arrival history counts ticks by asset class, an hour by the second and ten
minutes by the tenth (=synthetic.v1.ops.get_market_history=); and the reads
carry a Hawkes law's branching and decay, each feed's id and kind, and the
active multiplier.

Evidence: the quant and synthetic api suites pass under ctest, with tests for
the history's slots, groups and ageing, and for λ at a pinned pace. Web tests
for the market and i18n pass (206), the BFF market tests pass (22), and the
harness passes 13 of 13 live, as a tenant and as the system administrator.
Screenshots of every tab were checked against the live service.

The one-feed fold the prototype offered is not built: the history counts by
class, and a per-feed count would grow with the feeds. The load control was the
prototype's own.
