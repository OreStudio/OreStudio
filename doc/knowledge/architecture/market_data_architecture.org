* Summary

One service, =ores.marketdata=, stands between the sources of market data and the
parties that consume it. Sources are adapters. A live Bloomberg adapter, a replay
adapter and the simulator all meet the service at the same four messages. The
service maps vendor ids to internal items, checks and stores the quote series
for each party, delivers the ticks, and records what each party used so the
platform can share the vendor cost. Nothing past the adapter seam knows whether a
source is live or simulated, and a person can always find out.

This page is the structure note for the whole subject. It gists and links and it
carries no content its cluster already carries. It argues a reading order and says
what the reader can do after each section.

The subject splits into six sections. The first says why the service exists and
what rule keeps it honest. The second gives the model. The third follows a tick.
The fourth compares real and simulated sources in detail. The fifth covers the
governance of usage and cost. The sixth covers the surfaces a person uses and the
stored data.

Two boundaries hold. This page does not carry the process mathematics, which is
in [[id:A8C46639-0639-4587-B07D-B8B006C46618][Stochastic Processes]]. It does not
carry the resolution path from a logical requirement to a concrete identifier,
which is [[id:FD427B5B-BDC5-4803-AFCD-97A59E31FE25][Market Data Requirements and Resolution]].
