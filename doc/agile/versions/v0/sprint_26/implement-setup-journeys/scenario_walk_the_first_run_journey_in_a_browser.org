This page documents a test scenario verifying [[id:68113CC3-55C1-4EDE-AEFF-CEE043A9431C][Build the First run journey]] in [[id:DCF3E855-AEDF-4520-97B6-815A428DB514][Implement the setup journeys in ores.web]]. It is filled in with the target screen and checklist of steps before testing starts; record the outcome by hand in =* Results=.

* Before you start

- The installation must be empty: no administrator and no tenant. Bring it
  there yourself before handing this doc over, because the first step assumes
  bootstrap mode and a page refresh that comes back to the setup screen.
- =compass services stop=, wait until =pg_stat_activity= shows no connection to
  the environment's database, =rm -rf build/nats/<env>/jetstream=, then
  =compass db recreate -y -k= and =compass services start=. The recreate is what
  puts the deployment back into bootstrap mode; the JetStream directory is
  removed because the streams outlive the schema.
- Confirm the state before the tester arrives: =curl -s
  http://127.0.0.1:21402/api/bootstrap= answers ={"isInBootstrapMode":true,...}=.
- The walk needs the demo data the deployment already holds (the GLEIF
  families and the ACME bundle); a recreate seeds them.

* Steps

** Open the setup screen

- Go to =http://127.0.0.1:21402/=.
- The public shell states the client version and the version the deployment
  answered with.
- The welcome screen names the three stages, and the rail on the left lists
  nine steps: Welcome, Create the administrator, Choose a starting point,
  Describe the tenant, Review, Provisioning, Hand off, First sign-in, Ready.
- No sign-in form is offered: the installation has nobody to sign in as yet.

*** Result

** Create the installation administrator

- Press *Get started*.
- The form proposes =super_admin= and =super_admin@system.ores=; the password
  rules listed under the field are the deployment's, read from the server.
- Type a password that satisfies every listed rule and press *Create
  administrator*; the action stays disabled while a rule is unmet.
- Expect the starting points next, which means the deployment left bootstrap
  mode and the journey signed in as the account it just created.

*** Result

** Choose the prefilled demo

- The screen lists the starting points the deployment holds, each stating its
  own audience and counts.
- Choose the demo card and press *Continue*.

*** Result

** Describe the tenant

- The demo profile's tenant name, code, hostname and administrator are already
  filled in; every field stays editable.
- Change one derived field to confirm it can be edited, then change it back.
- Press *Continue*.

*** Result

** Review and create the tenant

- The review states the tenant, its hostname, its administrator and how many
  workflow steps the run orders.
- Press *Create tenant*.
- The provisioning step lists one line per step the run materialised and
  reaches its end without a failed step.
- The rail does not offer a way back over the steps that wrote.

*** Result

** Hand over to the tenant administrator

- Press *Continue as tenant admin*.
- Expect the first sign-in step, with the tenant administrator's principal
  stated.

*** Result

** Choose the party and set a password

- The account works in more than one party: choose *Acme Corporation Plc* from
  the list, which is the party choice the middle of the journey exists for.
- The screen states that the account sets a password of its own; type one that
  satisfies the listed rules, which come from the server's policy read.
- Press *Continue* only once the action is offered; it stays disabled while a
  party is unchosen or a rule is unmet.

*** Result

** Read the Ready screen

- The screen states who is signed in and what to do next.
- Press *Go home*.
- Expect the application home screen, with the tenant and party in the shell
  header, and not the setup journey.

*** Result
