* Goal

Every kind that publishes can take a published set out of use. The withdrawal is part of the kind's declaration: its approval kind, the record columns that say who withdrew the set, when and why, the operation that applies it, and the notice kind its readers are told with.

* Acceptance

- The FX forward set and the FX volatility surface name a withdrawal approval kind, with the permission that raises it, the permission that decides it, the approval count and the auto-approval code.
- A withdrawal of either kind records the withdrawing account, the instant, the reason and the request, and no read by as-of takes the set afterwards.
- A withdrawal notice reaches the party's readers for every kind, seeded under its own code.
- A withdrawal of a set already withdrawn, or of one with an open withdrawal, is refused.
- The withdrawal is reachable from the screens that show a published set.
