One expiry an =fx_vol_surface_feed= quotes. The feed orders its expiries by
length, so the row carries no position. The forward variance of the segment that
ends at this expiry, from the expiry before it, is a Cox-Ingersoll-Ross process
with this row's reversion and volatility.
