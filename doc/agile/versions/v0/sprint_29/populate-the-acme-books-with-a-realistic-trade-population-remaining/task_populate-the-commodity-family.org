ACME's books hold commodity trades across all twenty-five routed types, which no other class can claim: every commodity type the catalogue declares routes to commodity_instrument. The family carries 5% of the population. It is the second planner after bond and it proves the family-neutral plan, so it lands early rather than by weight.
