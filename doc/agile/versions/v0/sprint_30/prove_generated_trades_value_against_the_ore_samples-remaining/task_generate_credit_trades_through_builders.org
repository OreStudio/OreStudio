- The credit entries leave the recorded trades' known lists, and the recorded files change in the same commit.

- The roundtrip tests read every ORE sample trade of each booked credit type and write back every element ORE needs.

* Plan

- A generated credit trade names its curve by its reference entity: the credit
  spread series of the LEI, senior unsecured, in the trade currency, stored as
  an oresmd address. The market data task gives each LEI its curve; until then
  ORE reads the trade and fails only on the missing curve. The user chose this
  on 2026-11-10.
- =convention_lookup= answers a CDS convention by its id, as ORE names it.
  The planner asks for =CDS-STANDARD-CONVENTIONS=.
- One builder per booked type. CDS and index CDS share a premium leg built
  from the CDS convention. The index option wraps an index CDS. The credit
  linked swap pays a contingent coupon leg and redemption. The RPA wraps a
  vanilla swap built by =vanilla_swap_builder= and pays a protection fee.
- The scope carries the tenant's conventions, read by the population writer,
  so the planner can build trades.
- The credit planner draws terms, calls the builders, and the =construct_*=
  functions, =credit_common_terms= and the plan's =credit_terms= are deleted.

* Result

Each booked credit type has a builder in =credit_builders.hpp=:
=credit_default_swap_builder=, =index_credit_default_swap_builder=,
=index_credit_default_swap_option_builder=, =credit_linked_swap_builder= and
=risk_participation_agreement_builder=. Each takes its premium terms from the
CDS convention, which =convention_lookup= now finds by its ORE id. The RPA
builds its underlying with =vanilla_swap_builder=. The credit planner calls the
builders. The population writer reads the tenant's conventions into the scope.
The old credit constructors and the plan's =credit_terms= are deleted.

ORE now reads every booked credit type. Each one fails only on data the
Products example lacks for the generated entities. CDS, credit linked swap and
RPA trades lack the default curve of their LEI. The index trades lack
CreditIndex reference data for it. Those ten lines replace the five read errors
in the known list. They belong to the market data task.

A credit linked swap is a funded note. ORE takes its currency only from the
independent payments, so the funding at the start is one. The builders state
the elements every ORE sample states. Two stay in the known missing paths
because they change the economics. They are the credit linked swap's
=RecoveryPayments= and the RPA's =FixedRecoveryRate=.

The credit corpus roundtrip tests already read every ORE credit sample and
write it back to the same rows, and still pass.
