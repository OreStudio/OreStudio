This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *deferred* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

Allow tenant administrators to self-register. A super admin reviews and approves
the registration via the authorisation queue framework. On approval, the tenant
is created with a system party and the registrant becomes the tenant admin.

**** Acceptance Criteria

- Self-registration form captures tenant details.
- Registration enters authorisation queue for super admin approval.
- Approved registration creates tenant, system party, and admin account.
- Rejected registration logged with reason.

Related: self-registration into a tenant that already exists is settled in
[[id:84706C3E-D48D-4A4B-967A-4AADB3CF44B2][registration and onboarding
defaults]], which keeps
=system.signup_requires_authorization= closing the door until the approval step
this capture describes exists. What the person without a tenant needs is a
different journey again: [[id:CF975012-2E6A-4F9D-821A-30BAE7FCC031][the
role-request journey]] is the neighbouring case, where an account exists and
needs more than its default access.
