* What

Read one calendar's business days for one year in one call. Today
=GET /api/refdata/calendars/{code}/days= pages =list_by_calendar_code= from
offset 0, a thousand days at a time, and stops at the first page past the year,
because =calendar_dates_filter= names only the calendar. Add a date range to
the filter, or a year read on the refdata service, and send the year to it.

* Why

A calendar built from 1900 to 2200 holds about 110,000 days, so a year near the
end costs about 100 round trips. The read also depends on the service answering
in date order, which nothing states: if the order changes, days go missing with
no error. The BFF read the same way, and the C++ route kept its behaviour. The
review of PR #4620 raised it.
