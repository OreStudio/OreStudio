* Goal

A surface should reach as far as its party's market data reaches and no further, and say where it stopped. Today a thirteen-expiry ladder is compiled into the derived source and the seeder, an expiry's length is a constant, and one wholly-unquoted expiry voids the whole surface. This story takes the ladder and the horizon out of the code and into data, and makes the calibration fit what it has.

* Plan

The ladder was stated three times: the C++ array =derived_fx_expiries=, a cross join of
thirteen literals in =marketdata_derived_catalogue_populate.sql=, and =EXPIRIES= in the
blueprint dataset's generator. The blueprint dataset is already a data-quality artefact: each
blueprint's pillars name the expiry and label it reads, and each pillar's source names the
derived contributor. Every derived quote is such a pillar (35 pairs x 65 pillars = 2275, the
universe the adapter announces), so the ladder is published already; the work is to make the
other two read it rather than restate it. No new artefact is added.

- *The catalogue.* The vendor instruments, mappings and scopes move to
  =marketdata_derived_inventory_populate.sql=, which builds them from the pillars the derived
  contributor serves, and runs after the refdata layer stages the blueprints. The provider,
  feed, subscription and route stay where they were, because refdata scripts name the provider.
- *The source.* The array is gone. The feed takes each pair's expiries from the vendor ids in
  its universe, ordered by length; =derive_missing_days= and the live refresh take that ladder.
  An expiry's length is a parse of its tenor (W, M, Y) until "Take an expiry's length from the
  expiry-date rule" replaces it.
- *The generator* keeps =EXPIRIES=: it is the dataset's author, and its methodology now says
  the source's inventory is read from it.

Also on this branch: main added =FxWorstOfBasketSwap=, which asks for a second pair, without
listing it among the products on two pairs, so the GBP book's regression test failed again. It
is listed, and the second pair now refuses a type the list does not name.

* Result

The ladder is stated once, in the blueprint dataset, which is a data-quality artefact: the
derived source's 2275 instruments, mappings and scopes are built from the pillars its blueprints
read, and the source derives each pair at the expiries its vendor ids name. The C++ array and the
SQL cross join are gone. A fresh database holds the same 35 pairs and 13 expiries, and a fresh
Acme setup gave every party its 35 blueprints with their quotes and published 30 of 35 surfaces,
as before. Adding an expiry to the generator's ladder now reaches the source with no code change;
its length comes from its tenor until the next task takes it from the expiry-date rule.

The run also ended generate_trades with a warning that is not this change's: the default
population no longer covers the bond codes main added. It is filed as the capture "The Acme
trade population is too small for the bond codes it cycles".
