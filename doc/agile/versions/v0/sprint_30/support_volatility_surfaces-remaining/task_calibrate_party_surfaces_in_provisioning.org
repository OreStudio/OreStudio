* Goal

Every provisioned party has published volatility surfaces. A step of its own, after the forward curve step, calibrates each party's blueprints from that party's published forward sets and publishes each surface under the tenant's auto-approval. It records each surface published and why it skipped any.

* Acceptance

- Provisioning Acme on a fresh database ends with a published surface for each blueprint of each operational party.
- Each surface's lineage names the party's published forward set.
- A surface that fails its arbitrage or error checks is reported by party and pair, and is not published.
- The step cannot start before the forward curve step has completed.
- A retry publishes no surface twice.

* Plan

A step kind =publish_party_surfaces=, after =publish_party_forwards= and =give_party_surface_blueprints=, with the parties it names as its arguments.

- For each party, the step reads the blueprints the party holds. A party that holds none fails the step.
- For each blueprint, a published surface already found is left alone, so a rerun publishes nothing twice.
- Otherwise the step calibrates at the present instant. A calibration the service refuses fails the step, named with its party.
- A surface with arbitrage, a fit that did not converge or a pillar beyond the tolerance is held back and named. A decider would weigh it, and provisioning has none.
- Otherwise the step raises the publish. Under the tenant's auto-approval it closes and publishes at once. A raise that is not published fails the step.
- The surface publish service refuses to raise a calibration with arbitrage under any rule, as [[id:24950B8E-7FC4-4F0A-8AA4-9603B344F970][A candidate is computed and stored before a publish is raised]] states. Until now a raise a rule closed would have published it.

* Result

The =publish_party_surfaces= step calibrates and publishes every party's surfaces in provisioning, and the surface publish refuses a calibration with arbitrage under any rule. On a fresh Acme provisioning all 40 blueprints of the four parties published, each from its party's own forward set and closed under the =tenant= rule.

The run that proved it left out =generate_trades= in the local database only, because on main that step fails on swap conventions that name one index more than once ([[id:9137921C-B9C9-4C9F-84B6-847A07B61F28][Name GBP LIBOR 3M in one swap convention]]), and a failed run dispatches no later step. The committed profile still orders it.
