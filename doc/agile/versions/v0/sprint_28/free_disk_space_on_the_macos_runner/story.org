* Decisions

- The step mirrors the Linux workflow's, placed as the first step of the
  build job so the space is free before the checkout writes anything.
- The Xcode 16 installation is deliberately kept: the next step selects it.
  Every other Xcode is removed.
- Simulator runtimes and Xcode DerivedData are the bulk of what a ctest run
  never loads, so they are removed first.
- The step prints =df= before and after, so each run records what it freed
  instead of the saving being asserted.

* Out of scope

- The out-of-memory reading of clang's =Killed: 9=. This story fixes the
