* Acceptance

- The export of the ACME tenant takes under 5 seconds.
- The log names each phase of the export with its duration.
- A test shows the reads for every series equal the reads for each series.

* Plan

Find why the export outlasted the 30 second wait of the report run, then remove the cause. The export read each series with three statements. The tenant has 306 series. Each statement paid for planning across the 162 chunks of the observation and tick tables. So the plan is two reads for the whole tenant, the current observations and the as-of snapshot, grouped by series in the service. The log of the export and of each service call names the phase and its duration.

* Notes

The first NPV run of the dev database failed in =gather_market_data= with "NATS request timed out". The marketdata service finished the export after 38 seconds, 8 seconds after the reporting service had stopped waiting. The log of the service said only that the export had started and, much later, that it had written the file.

The log now states the request (valuation date and storage key), each phase of the export with its duration (series, current observations, points at the close, building the data, writing the text), the upload, and the total. The reporting service logs each service call with its duration, as a warning above five seconds, and a failed call states how long it waited.

Seen on the way, and not fixed here:

- After =gather_market_data= fails, =gather_trades= still starts and fails with "cannot move from failed to running".
- The marketdata service cannot write its delivery counts: "Invalid modified_by: ores.marketdata.service. Must be a valid account username or service role". It fails every 40 seconds with 19,910 counts waiting.
- The database fingerprint does not match this checkout. It was built from commit 2a0b529f5a.
