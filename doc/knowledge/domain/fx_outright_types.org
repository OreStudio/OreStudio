#+title: An outright settles before, on or after the spot date
#+description: Value today, value tomorrow, spot and forward delivery, and where the regulatory line falls.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :domain:finance:fx:fx_forward:
#+created: 2026-11-09
#+updated: 2026-11-09

* Summary

An outright is a single exchange of currencies at an agreed rate on one future value date. The value date places it before spot, on spot or after spot.

* Detail

1. *Before spot*: value today or value tomorrow, where tomorrow is not the spot date.
2. *Spot*: T+2 for most pairs and T+1 for pairs such as USD/CAD.
3. *Forward*: any date after spot.

The line between spot and forward has regulatory weight. In the EU a contract delivered within the spot window is excluded from the financial instruments of MiFID II.
