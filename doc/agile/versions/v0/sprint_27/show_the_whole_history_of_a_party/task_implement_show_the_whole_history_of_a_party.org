* Goal

A history provider finds a row by its whole key, so one party's contact never shows another party's history.

Find a row by its whole key in a history provider. A provider reads the newest row that carries the first column of the model's key, and a contact's key is the party and the contact type. Every Acme party has the four contact types, so the history of one party's Legal contact is another party's. The same holds for an identifier read by its value and a business unit read by its code.

Evidence: in the Acme tenant each of the four contact types is held by four parties, and the party timeline drops the versions of every contact that is not the newest row, and says part of the history could not be read.
