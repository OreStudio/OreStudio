* Summary

A scripted trade expresses its payoff as a script that ORE interprets, rather
than as one of ORE's fixed trade elements. Within ORE Studio, the scripted form
is one of the forms in which a product may be booked, and the subsystem that
supports it addresses five questions: which forms a product admits, how a
product is described, how scripts are held and trusted, how trades are booked
and validated, and how the booking and valuation of every product is
demonstrated. Each question is treated in a document of its own; this note
establishes their order. The scripting module as ORE defines it, including the
script language and the two locations a script may occupy, is described in
[[id:2F5B28D9-9AB7-401E-8EF3-612712F626C1][Generic Scripted Products]]. The documents describe the architecture as it stands once
[[id:E6359881-7919-4BBC-9B9C-03A8EEDB8A03][Design the scripted trade subsystem]] is complete.
