* Goal

The ingest of ticks no longer pays a commit, a series read, a notification and two acknowledgements for each tick. A burst of ticks drains at least ten times faster than the 143 rows each second of today.

* Acceptance

- The observation table has no notification trigger and no event mapping.
- One batch writes the rows of one tenant in one transaction, and a series read happens once for each series.
- Producers and the republish use core NATS, and the two tick streams are no longer created.
- A lag gauge shows the age of the newest tick of each source.
- The burst drain rate is measured with the tick injector and recorded in the task.
