* Result

[[id:37D5E28C-A83F-4EDC-891B-4F6F23B2BB42][Session modes and capabilities]] is
the target state, at =doc/knowledge/security/= and listed in the identity and
access hub's reading order.

It fixes the mode as computed by the server and delivered with the session, the
menu's authority as the permission codes the server already answers, and the
protocol change that implies. It marks two things to confirm rather than assume:
the permission =iam.v1.roles.permissions-by-account= demands of its caller, and
whether a member may ask for their own account.

What it does not settle is recorded: how a person comes to hold a privileged
mode, which is [[id:AC2BA866-D2C3-4C8B-AF5A-D573D1317A78][captured]] on its
own, and whether a deployment can switch a journey off for everybody as against
a person not holding its permission.
