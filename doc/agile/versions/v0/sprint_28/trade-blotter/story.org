* Goal

Build the Trade Blotter: the primary front-office screen from which
traders, sales, and middle office view, manage, and act on the live
book of trades -- booking new deals, amending existing ones, and
routing trades through their lifecycle (expiry, fixing, delivery,
exercise, settlement). See the target-state reference:
[[id:3976695F-F526-4C2A-80ED-89B54854CF6D][Trade Blotter (knowledge doc)]] -- multi-panel architecture, deal grid,
deal actions, STP panel, Expiry/Fixing Manager, Barrier Management,
Delta Jump Screen, Spike Map.

The original design said this story depends on [[id:603F4845-B4A0-41EB-ACF9-B0FC1387E1C4][Workspaces: finish the feature]],
and that a production import needs a holding location, the *Pending
Book*. The design task found that the sandbox model replaced both. The
workspace story is abandoned and the Pending Book is retired. A real
deal books into a real book at once and waits in the operational
authorisation queue (see =* Decisions=).

* Decisions

- Closed at sprint 28 close with its 5 done tasks. The 7 open tasks and the original acceptance moved to [[id:67C1C30A-5CF6-408A-B73C-99A79A01B19F][Trade Blotter — the remaining work]] in the product backlog inbox.
The design is in [[id:04CE05B4-CA42-4C40-A119-61C36C69A953][Investigation: the blotter and import design]]. These
decisions come from it.

- The Pending Book is retired. The sandbox, the =draft= state and the
  operational authorisation status replace it. A real deal books into
  a real book at once. It waits in the authorisation queue, which uses
  the inbox. The trade gets no new state.
- The =is_live= marker is not built. Sources and named configurations
  keep imported data out of official use.
- The workspace dependency is gone. The workspace story is abandoned
  and the workspaces are removed.
- The =completed_with_warnings= outcome and the =item_errors= list are
  built. No task remains for them.
- A row that an import cannot book is reported with its source file,
  trade id and message. Nothing is stored. The user corrects the source
  and runs the import again.
- The trade list reads one row for each trade or structure, joined in
  the service. The client does no joining.
- The blotter is a trade list and a trade page. The authorisation queue
  is a second screen for Operations. The Expiry, Fixing, Barrier, Delta
  and Spike screens are separate and stay out of this story.
- Positions and the trade-expiry workflow stay in the backlog. The
  capture on separate trade and instrument dialogs is answered by the
  trade page, which has a Details tab and an Instrument tab. Close the
  capture when the page ships.

* Out of scope

- The Positions domain model and the trade-expiry workflow stay in the
  backlog as separate captures.
- Importing from FpML, CSV or another trading system. Only the ORE
  import exists. Each other source needs its own story.
- The Expiry, Fixing, Barrier, Delta and Spike screens.
