* Result

Both datasets are in the base bundle, so provisioning a tenant publishes the groups and then the members. After I recreated the database and provisioned Acme with the acme source, Acme held the six groups and 37 memberships: G11 11, Scandies 3, Antipodeans 2, commodity 7, Asians 9 and Latams 5. A new pgTAP test, refdata_currency_groups_publish_test.sql, checks the bundle membership, the dependency edge, the artefact rows and a publish into a tenant, 10 of 10 pass. The Desk groups screen at /refdata/desk-groups reads these tables. I did not open the screen in a browser.
