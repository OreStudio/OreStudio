How much the tenant's sessions moved, day by day.

The screen reads this beside the session list: the list says who is
connected, the statistics say how much traffic the tenant's sign-ins carry
and how long they last.

=ores.sql= creates three continuous aggregates over the sessions table, and
they exist only under the Timescale licence. A development database runs the
Apache licence and has none of them, so the read aggregates the sessions
table itself and answers in either edition.
