* Decisions

- *Prototype before implementation.* Each journey is drawn as a
  plain-HTML prototype and reviewed by the product owner before it is
  written or broken into implementation tasks. A screen argues better
  than a paragraph, and the review is cheaper on a prototype than on a
  branch.
- *One prototype per journey, in the journey's own vocabulary.* The five
  are separate documents because a person's goal is separate, even
  though they share the party, book and convention models.
- *The prototypes are throwaway and stay on main.* They are the design
  record of what was agreed, not the product. The product is
  ores.web.
