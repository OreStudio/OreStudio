Delete the guard in generate.py that keeps the codegen graph out of a sub-component project's CMake. Every part's src/CMakeLists.txt and tests/CMakeLists.txt is generated from its model again, and the two files main hand-wrote against their templates are reconciled rather than reverted.

* Acceptance

- The ores.cmake.* guard in generate.py is gone, and the graph renders a part's CMake the way it renders any other component's.
- Every component's link list is model-derived: the api, core and service src templates carry the baseline, and a component that deviates says so in extra_dependencies. No component's links change.
- ores.cmake.component.api_src and .core_src emit component_files.cmake rather than file(GLOB_RECURSE), completing the 2026-07-30 migration that 88 of the 94 checked-in files already follow.
- ores.geo's CMake is regenerated from its model, with the ores.database and ores.telemetry.core links its sources need, and the component builds.
- ores.history/api declares header_only, so its template emits the INTERFACE library it is hand-written as.
- compass check passes, whole-tree build and ctest included.
