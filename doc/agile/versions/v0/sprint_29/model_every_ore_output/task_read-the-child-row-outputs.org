* Acceptance

- =flows=, =stresstest_flows= and =todaysmarketcalibration_cashflows= each have a typed row and a reader.
- A flow row keeps its trade, leg, cashflow number and pay date, and the reader refuses a row that omits one of them.
- A stressed flow states base, scenario and difference for each figure, and the reader refuses a row that states fewer than three.
- The tests read the checked-in sample of each family.

* Plan

A cashflow row is a relation, not a bag: =LegNo= and =CashflowNo= together name the flow, =PayDate= dates it, and the figures describe it. Flattening it to metrics would keep the figures and lose the flow. The stressed family triples every figure, so its row states three values beside each other and the reader checks that they agree in count.
