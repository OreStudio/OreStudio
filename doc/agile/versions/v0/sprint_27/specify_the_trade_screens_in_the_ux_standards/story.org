- The menu page names the Trades area, its screens, its addresses and where each screen belongs.

- The trade list filters through the standard filter record, and the prototypes show the request.

* Decisions

- Trades is its own area. A trade is held by the tenant and is not a shared list, so it is not reference data.
- Book a trade is the Add of the trade list, and booking is one page in tabs. A sequence is allowed only when it makes the task quicker, and it does not here.
- The trade type is a combo box, and the Instrument tab has a tab for each part of the instrument.
- The trade screens use the standard entity protocol. What they need beyond it is a change to the model, and not a new operation.
- Authorisation is out of scope for now.

* Out of scope

- Authorisation, and the capture that waits for it.
- The model changes the trade list needs. They are on the review journey as gaps.
