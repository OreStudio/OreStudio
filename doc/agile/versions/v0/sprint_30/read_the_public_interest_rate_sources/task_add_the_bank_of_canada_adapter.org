* Goal

CORRA is stored as fixings in the system tenant, with its history, read from the Bank of Canada Valet API through series =AVG.INTWO=. The adapter is specialised: it reads the Bank of Canada Valet API alone, and it supplies only its fetcher and its settings to the shared half of [[id:0DB85F8F-4C7D-4428-B514-AF69CE8E5D86][Separate the public source adapter's plumbing from its fetching]].

* Acceptance

- The adapter reads the Bank of Canada Valet API alone, and it does not copy the announce, the demand, the backfill or the publish code.
- The Bank of Canada Valet API is a supplier in the catalogue, its CORRA series maps to the CORRA item of [[id:0D8B2280-F6E1-4198-8372-6712D1E8DFD8][Catalogue the overnight rate items]], and a session and a feed of it are seeded in the system tenant.
- The session's terms are accepted at setup, as the terms of the FX sources are. A session whose terms are not accepted is skipped and reported, and provisioning does not fail.
- A fresh environment stores the CORRA history as fixings within minutes of provisioning, and the log names the source and the count it stored.
- Each fixing is stored as published, with the credit that the terms ask for.
- The depth of the history is measured and written into [[id:3F9D6EAE-9FB8-4A0D-9BAF-1EA3EA93AB4F][the calibration sources note]].
- Unit tests parse a recorded response of the Bank of Canada Valet API, and a malformed response gives an error, not a fixing.

* Plan

- =boc_fetcher= implements =fetcher=: adapter =boc_valet=, scheme =boc_valet=, field =v=. A source's key is a series name, =AVG.INTWO= for CORRA, checked as capitals, digits, dots and underscores before it enters a URL.
- A read is one GET of =/valet/observations/<series>/json= over the window. Each observation holds its date in =d= and the value as text in =<series>.v=, so the decimal is read from the published text with no number in between. An observation without the series or its value is skipped.
- Probed on 2026-11-10: a window with no business day answers an empty =observations= list; an unknown series answers 404, which is an error.
- The seeds add the =boc= supplier, the =boc_valet= adapter, the =boc-valet= session, and the =boc-corra= feed scoped to the CORRA item. The Bank's default feed stays =boc-frankfurter=. The system party already subscribes to =boc=, so it gains only an =interest_rates= route to the feed.

* Result

CORRA is stored as fixings in the system tenant, read from the Bank of Canada's Valet API by a specialised fetcher that the official adapter hosts beside Frankfurter, the New York Fed, the ECB and the Bank of England. A fresh environment stored 272 fixings, the last 400 days, with the log line "Sent 272 fixing(s) of 'boc-corra'". The values are the decimals of the published percents, such as =0.0252= for 2.5200, and the provider =boc= carries the credit "Source: Bank of Canada". The calibration sources note records the 7,305 rates the API serves since 1997-08-12.

Unit tests parse a recorded answer and an empty list, skip an observation without its value, refuse six malformed answers, check the URL of a read, treat a 404 as an error, and refuse a name that is not a series name.

With this task, the four usable overnight rates of the survey are stored: SOFR, €STR, SONIA and CORRA.
