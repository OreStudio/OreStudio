#+title: Books and portfolios journeys
#+description: The tenant's book structure: portfolios that hold books, the classification each book carries, and the rights that decide who may see a portfolio and the books under it.
#+type: knowledge
#+level: cross
#+filetags: :knowledge:user_journey:refdata:
#+created: 2026-10-07
#+updated: 2026-10-07

A portfolio is the folder; a book is the leaf that trades sit in. A book
inherits its place from its portfolio and carries its own classification: a
status, a regulatory book type, a purpose and a ledger feed. Some of those are
the tenant's own rows and some are shared lists the tenant picks from, and the
screen has to keep the two apart.

The structure is also where access is decided. A portfolio right says who may
see a portfolio and the books under it, so shaping the structure and granting
access are the same act from the person's point of view.
