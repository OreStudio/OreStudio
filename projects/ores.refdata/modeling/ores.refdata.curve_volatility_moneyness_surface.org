#+brief: The moneyness grid a MoneynessSurface kind states.
#+created: 2026-10-31
#+updated: 2026-10-31

ORE's =volatilityMoneynessSurfaceConfig= states a grid of moneyness levels
against expiries, how it interpolates each way and how it extrapolates past the
grid, exactly as the strike and delta surfaces do with their own axes. Those are
the kind's own fields: the four terms every kind states and the =priority= are on
=curve_volatility_kind=, and a row of this part exists only when the entry holds
a kind of this type.
