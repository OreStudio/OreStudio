This page is a [[id:671F18E4-E09C-4B3B-BD24-D33DF8AE38A6][capture]] in the *inbox* bucket of the product backlog — a pre-sprint idea, not yet pulled into a sprint as a story.

* What

Let each synthetic generator that writes a closed either-or vary it across its
rows: the forward rate agreement generator writes =Long= unconditionally today.

* Why

The forward rate agreement's mapper ignored the direction its document stated for
as long as the family existed, and every witness agreed with the constant: the
ORE example corpus states Long, and so does the generator. No test, dataset or
screenshot could have shown the difference, which is why the defect survived
review after review.

A fixture that varies a closed set is cheap, and it is the only witness that can
fail. The same habit probably reaches the other either-ors the generators write
— an option's long or short, a leg's payer, a call or a put. Count them before
changing one, so the change lands as a rule rather than as one generator's patch.
